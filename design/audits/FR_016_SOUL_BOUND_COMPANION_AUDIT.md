# FR-016 Soul-Bound Companion Implementation Audit

## Scope

This audit records implementation of FR-016 only. It adds no campaign bond, companion, route, candidate, reunion, populated schema, or unrelated Future Revision.

## Prior Ambiguity

Existing Canon supported Soul bonds, Soul Resonance, Soul Constellations, persistent Relationships, Reincarnation candidate influence, memory continuity, and world-contact routes. No canonical structure distinguished an explicit fate bond with guaranteed eventual reunion from ordinary recurring relationship history.

## Implementation

- Added [Soul-Bound Companion Fate and Reincarnation Continuity](../../docs/soul/SOUL_BOUND_COMPANIONS.md).
- Defined one pair-specific Soul-level Bond and renewable Convergence Intervals.
- Made eventual reunion a qualitative invariant rather than a probability or deadline.
- Preserved candidate validity, world causality, asynchronous lives, current Relationship ownership, memory boundaries, and current-life agency.
- Distinguished the Bond from Soul Constellations, ordinary companionship, control, and social roles.
- Added normalized logical persistence guidance, migration, validation, a GM procedure, worked cases, and a [blank template](../../templates/SOUL_BOUND_COMPANION_TEMPLATE.md).

## Ownership

- Soul/fate owns Bond identity, participants, formation provenance, and active convergence obligation.
- Entities and Life Archive own current and historical incarnation identity.
- Relationships owns current social and emotional state.
- Memory Continuity and Character Knowledge own recall and recognition access.
- Timeline and Campaign History own encounter events.
- World, Gate, travel, and Reincarnation owners retain route and candidate validity.
- Summaries and Context Packets remain Derived or Cache views.

## Schema and Migration Impact

No universal executable campaign schema or populated save exists in this repository. FR-016 therefore requires no mandatory reusable SQL migration. Campaign implementations may normalize Bonds, intervals, manifestations, and interference under the logical contract and adopt them through ordinary backup, audit, merge, validation, and activation.

## Validation Performed

- pair identity and reverse-duplicate prevention;
- guarantee, delay, and meaningful-encounter semantics;
- Reincarnation candidate and cross-world route boundaries;
- asynchronous Final Death and Interlife behavior;
- memory, recognition, Relationship, and agency separation;
- non-transitive multiple bonds;
- unknown route preservation and anti-scripting safeguards;
- Markdown links, navigation, terminology, governance, and repository boundaries.

## Result

FR-016 is complete when repository validation passes and the roadmap records closure. Phase 12 remains active under maintainer authority; completing the last approved objective does not declare release readiness or begin Phase 13.
