# FR-001 and FR-005 Retained Development Implementation Audit

## Scope

This audit covers only FR-001 and FR-005. It does not implement Skill consolidation, conceptual Skill scope, autobiographical memory, Soul Depth visibility, context assembly, or another pending Phase 12 objective.

## Retained-Development Model

Past Lives provide familiarity and bounded redevelopment acceleration rather than current power. Contributions require meaningful source Development, apply only within comparable Historical Territory, and never restore prior attributes or Skill levels.

## Stacking and Bound

All relevant Lives may contribute. Contributions are additive before saturation, overlapping histories have diminishing marginal effect, and the effective result remains below a configured ceiling that preserves meaningful current practice. The qualitative default reaches at most Saturated acceleration, not automatic mastery. Numeric campaigns may use `cap * raw / (cap + raw)` or an equivalent documented monotonic saturation function.

## Embodiment Relevance

Relevance is assessed per capability from anatomy, control, senses, cognition, scale, material, Mana, tools, environment, and historical expression. Direct through Zero and Unknown states avoid Species-name matching and avoid a universal compatibility matrix.

## Attributes and Skills

Attributes use bounded Stat XP Domains and embodied adaptation. Skills additionally distinguish Familiarity Unlock from Development Boost. Zero boost may coexist with familiarity. Current hard prerequisites remain binding.

## Translation and Mana

Stable Skill identity persists across body-specific expressions when bounded function remains continuous. A developed translation can become a bridge for later Lives. Mana can provide an alternate Receiving Route only through established Magic capability, costs, feedback, and practice.

## Life Archive and Ownership

The Life Archive locates source Lives and deeper owner records. Soul continuity owns retained potential; current Development and Skill records own current state; effective acceleration profiles are Derived/Cache records. No current values were moved into archive history.

## Migration

Existing campaigns adopt the framework through Backup, historical audit, justified relevance assessment, provenance construction, bounded derivation, validation, and activation. No populated campaign migration was performed.

## Validation and Future Compatibility

Validation covers source identity, capability and embodiment references, meaningful evidence, unlock/boost separation, zero and Unknown states, bounded stacking, territory, prerequisites, provenance, recalculation, knowledge separation, and FR-012 ownership. Skill expression and predecessor provenance remain available for FR-006 and FR-007; familiarity remains separate from future FR-015 memory.

## Unresolved Issues

No blocking design issue remains. Campaigns that choose numeric rates must define their own capability-bounded contribution scale and cap profile; Eternal Cycle canon deliberately does not impose one universal percentage.
