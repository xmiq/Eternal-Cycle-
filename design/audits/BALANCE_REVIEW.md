# Balance Review

## Scope

This Phase 11 review tests whether the completed systems preserve meaningful alternatives, earned progression, embodiment, agency, counterplay, and causal consequence without relying on a universal power score. It identifies gameplay-validation risks and routes them to the existing [Future Revisions register](../FUTURE_REVISIONS.md). It does not change a rule, tune a formula, declare two paths equal, or treat a speculative concern as a defect.

## Balance Standard

Eternal Cycle uses **contextual balance**. A path is not balanced because it produces the same output as every other path. It is balanced when its strengths arise from established causes, its requirements and limits matter, other actors retain agency, relevant counterplay exists, and no path is universally optimal across bodies, environments, objectives, time horizons, information states, and social conditions.

The review therefore checks six questions:

1. **Acquisition:** Is capability earned through the owning system rather than granted by a neighboring label?
2. **Expression:** Do embodiment, access, practice, source, tools, and context still constrain use?
3. **Cost:** Can meaningful use create strain, opportunity cost, maintenance, exposure, tradeoffs, or consequence?
4. **Counterplay:** Can relevant preparation, incompatibility, environment, information, alliances, interruption, or refusal matter?
5. **Agency:** Do players, NPCs, Souls, Weapon Souls, sources, and affected communities retain their established choices and resistance?
6. **Persistence:** Does retained history support continuity without multiplying current capability automatically?

No single numerical comparison can answer all six.

## Cross-System Stress Review

### Retained Progression and Old-Soul Snowballing

Persistent potential, retained Stat XP, Soul systems, remembered Skills, cross-species translation, Soul Weapon continuity, and accumulated knowledge can reinforce one another. The repository consistently prevents direct multiplication: no retained layer restores current statistics, anatomy, source access, reliability, action economy, authority, or unlimited throughput. The relevant evidence routes are FR-001, FR-004, and FR-005. A new mechanic is not justified without play showing that these independent protections fail together.

### Breadth, Specialization, and Action Economy

Persistent breadth creates options but not simultaneous expression. Embodiment, attention, preparation, tools, Mana, social access, timing, and Practised Reliability still select what can matter in one situation. Specialists may dominate a narrow objective while generalists remain resilient across changing objectives. Neither profile receives universal superiority.

### Optional Systems Becoming Functionally Mandatory

Soul Weapons, Magic, Classes, Professions, institutions, Soul Avatars, and advanced Skills are explicitly non-universal. Formal optionality is insufficient if campaign conditions make one route dominate ordinary play, so validation should compare campaigns and encounters where a route is absent, inaccessible, unsuitable, interrupted, politically costly, or deliberately refused. FR-008 covers the clearest named risk; comparable evidence for another route should create or extend a Future Revision rather than trigger ad hoc tuning.

### Recognition, Authority, and Soft Compulsion

Soul Titles, reputation, social position, institutional office, faction power, divine relationships, and symbolic authority may combine to create pressure. Their owners consistently separate recognition from obedience and preserve observer knowledge, jurisdiction, legitimacy, resistance, and consequence. GMs must not convert several contextual influences into automatic consent or predetermined action.

### Information, Mystery, and Fair Counterplay

Hidden Skills, hidden Evolution Conditions, GM Secrets, Research, uncertain world state, and Soul information preserve discovery only when facts have prior provenance and proportionate discovery routes. Opacity cannot substitute for a rule or retroactively invalidate informed choices. FR-003, FR-007, FR-009, and FR-011 cover the main presentation and operating risks.

### Simulation Fidelity and Play Burden

More records and finer simulation do not automatically create fairer play. Causal Horizons, Simulation Frames, required Read Sets, Affected Sets, Material Exceptions, and validation profiles bound work to the decision at hand. FR-004 and FR-010 through FR-012 appropriately retain this as a gameplay-validation concern rather than a reason to remove continuity safeguards before evidence exists.

## Exploit and Incentive Review

- Repetition, time, suffering, killing, death, consumption, possession, naming, fame, and rarity do not serve as universal progression currencies.
- Deliberate self-harm, engineered failure, trivial repetition, weak-target cycling, and repeated reincarnation cannot establish meaningful Development, Skill formation, Evolution, Soul growth, Awakening, or title identity by themselves.
- Labels from one system do not import capabilities from another. A Class, title, Affinity, species name, weapon form, world event, or stored record must route each effect to its mechanical owner.
- Generated content and encounter framing do not scale rewards or opposition to a universal character level.
- Persistence records preserve justified numbers but cannot invent, smooth, or rebalance them.
- Safeguards reject unsupported rewards while preserving real learning, adaptation, sacrifice, recovery, and creative preparation.

## Future Revisions Disposition

All twelve current Future Revisions remain **Candidate** entries. Their priorities describe the cost of an evidence-supported problem, not proof that the problem exists. This review found no basis to promote, close, reprioritize, or implement any entry and no blocking contradiction requiring a thirteenth entry.

The four non-blocking questions in [Unresolved Questions](../UNRESOLVED_QUESTIONS.md) remain correctly routed through FR-001 through FR-003. They do not block repository standardization because their answers depend on gameplay evidence or campaign presentation.

## Validation Guidance

Long-term playtesting should include unsuitable bodies, different species, low-resource contexts, social and noncombat objectives, no-Soul-Weapon characters, noncasters, unaffiliated practitioners, interrupted plans, failed research, time skips, and campaigns of different record complexity. Evidence should record both successful safeguards and attempted exploits; absence of a problem in one favorable context does not establish universal balance.

Remaining concerns are evidence-dependent calibration, presentation, and operating-load questions already routed to Future Revisions. No gameplay rule or accepted decision changes in this review.
