# Canonical Design Decisions

This file records accepted project decisions. Treat these decisions as authoritative unless the project owner explicitly changes one.

## D-001 — Rules-Only Repository

The repository contains rules, system documentation, templates, design notes, and GM guidance. It must not contain campaign saves or live world state.

## D-002 — Development Over Universal Levels

Independent Development tracks are the primary measure of growth. A universal character level is not authoritative. Local ranks, stages, or levels may exist inside individual systems.

## D-003 — Systemic World Simulation

World events normally emerge from interacting conditions and consequences rather than arbitrary scripted events.

## D-004 — Monster-Focused Reincarnation

Monster reincarnation and branching monster evolution are major focuses of the game.

## D-005 — Separate Progression Trees

Humans and monsters normally possess separate progression trees. Reincarnation may eventually permit carefully limited crossover.

## D-006 — Persistent Soul Progression

Bodies, ordinary statistics, equipment, wealth, social status, and reputation reset between lives. Skills, skill experience, Soul Weapons, Soul Echoes, Soul Resonance, Soul Depth, instincts, and retained Stat XP may persist according to their rules.

## D-007 — Emergent Soul Weapons

Any sufficiently meaningful weapon or tool may awaken a Weapon Soul through extreme shared experience. Soul Weapon formation requires genuine pressure, resonance, and mutual transformation rather than routine use.

## D-008 — Weapon Souls Are Distinct Persons

A Weapon Soul is not merely a copy of its wielder. Intertwining creates a lasting bond without erasing either identity.

## D-009 — Meaningful Death

Reincarnation must not make death trivial. Death ends relationships, positions, plans, and bodily development even when soul progress survives.

## D-010 — Ages May Change Genre

World Ages may alter civilization, technology, magic, species dominance, and genre while preserving the continuity of the protagonist's soul.

## D-011 — Inspirations and Exclusions

Primary inspirations include *Re:Monster* and *So I'm a Spider, So What?* Secondary inspirations include *Mushoku Tensei*, *Frieren*, *The Faraway Paladin*, *Dungeon Meshi*, *Made in Abyss*, *Monster Hunter*, and *Elden Ring*. Avoid harem focus, cannibalism focus, and runaway stat inflation.

## D-012 — Current-Incarnation Agency

The current incarnation is the soul's active self and retains primary agency. A soul has one active incarnation by default. Previous lives, Soul Echoes, Soul Titles, and Soul Constellations may influence it but do not automatically control it, create extra incarnations, or restore former claims over other people.

## D-013 — Persistence Is Not Access

A Soul Imprint may persist without being consciously remembered or immediately usable. Access may require bodily compatibility, present-life development, Soul Depth, Soul Resonance, or a relevant trigger.

## D-014 — Soul Growth Resists Farming and Theft

Death, manufactured danger, killing, or consumption do not automatically create Soul Imprints or transfer another being's soul progression. Permanent growth requires meaningful integration under the rules of the relevant system.

## D-015 — Soul Annihilation Is Exceptional

Soul Strain, Soul Wounds, and extraordinary fragmentation may threaten persistent progression, but total soul annihilation is not a default consequence. Soul harm requires an established cause, fair warning appropriate to the threat, and an opportunity for resistance or response. A campaign must explicitly adopt any rule capable of ending soul continuity.
