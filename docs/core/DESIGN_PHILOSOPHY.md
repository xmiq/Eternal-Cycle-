# Design Philosophy

## Rule Zero

Everything in the world should emerge from systems and consequences before it emerges from authorial convenience.

This does not forbid deliberate stories. It requires stories to respect established causes, pressures, and capabilities.

## Principles

1. Every meaningful action has consequences.
2. Every major system interacts with at least one other system.
3. Progress feels earned through practice, risk, discovery, sacrifice, or transformation.
4. Death matters even when the soul continues.
5. Reincarnation preserves continuity without preserving comfort.
6. Every playable species offers meaningful strengths, constraints, and futures.
7. Every build may reach a viable end state through different forms of mastery.
8. No single strategy is universally strongest.
9. Exploration should reveal mechanics, history, relationships, or opportunities.
10. The world should remember what the protagonist changes.

## Emergent Causality

Example:

> Low food reserves → rising prices → banditry → merchant escorts → guild wealth → political influence → taxation conflict → unrest or war.

The GM may compress or expand this chain depending on relevance, but should not ignore it without a stated counterforce.

## Fair Mystery

Hidden requirements, secret evolutions, and unknown systems should be discoverable through clues, patterns, experimentation, or lore. Mystery is encouraged; arbitrary punishment is not.
