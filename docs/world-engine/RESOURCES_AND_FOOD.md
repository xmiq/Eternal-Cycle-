# Resources and Food

## Purpose

This document defines how Eternal Cycle represents natural resources, produced inputs, food, water, sustenance, extraction, renewal, storage, transport, distribution, depletion, waste, and substitution.

It supplies world conditions to other domains. It does not define prices, trade institutions, population biology, ecology, magical source mechanics, Skills, production mastery, or campaign inventories.

## Core Rule

A **Resource Claim** states that a specified subject can use a particular Stock or Flow for a defined purpose, at a stated place and time, through an actual access and transformation route, with known quality, costs, limits, ownership or agency, and uncertainty.

Nothing is a resource in the abstract. The same substance, site, creature, spell product, or body may be useful, useless, sacred, poisonous, inaccessible, irreplaceable, contested, or alive depending on user, purpose, method, context, and consent.

Presence does not prove availability. Availability does not prove fitness. Fitness does not prove access. Access does not prove sustainable use.

## Resource Dimensions

Every material resource claim identifies only dimensions relevant to the question:

1. **Source.** Where it originates and what sustains it.
2. **User and purpose.** Who can use it and for what process.
3. **Form and quality.** Its current condition, concentration, purity, integrity, potency, freshness, or compatibility.
4. **Location and boundary.** Where it exists and where it can be used.
5. **Access.** Physical, legal, social, ecological, magical, technical, informational, and consent requirements.
6. **Extraction or collection.** What must occur to obtain it and who can perform that work.
7. **Transformation.** Processing, preparation, refinement, purification, preservation, or conversion required before use.
8. **Storage.** Capacity, losses, spoilage, security, maintenance, and time limit.
9. **Transport.** Route, speed, capacity, risk, loss, handling, and interruption.
10. **Distribution.** Who controls, receives, is excluded, waits, pays, or bears risk.
11. **Renewal.** Whether and how the source replenishes under current conditions.
12. **Depletion and damage.** What extraction removes or degrades.
13. **Waste and aftermath.** Residue, pollution, heat, magical Drift, habitat change, disease exposure, or displaced burden.
14. **Substitutes.** Alternatives and the differences, switching costs, and new dependencies they introduce.
15. **Evidence and uncertainty.** How the claim is known and what could materially reverse it.

A precise quantity without these dimensions may be less useful than a qualitative, well-scoped finding.

## Resource Types

### Finite Stocks

A finite Stock exists in a bounded amount at the relevant timescale. Mining, harvesting, burning, consuming, damaging, or dispersing it reduces accessible quantity or quality unless another source replaces it.

Finite does not mean immediately scarce. Abundant does not mean inexhaustible.

### Renewable Flows

A **Renewable Flow** replenishes through a valid process such as growth, reproduction, rainfall, sediment, circulation, seasonal return, Mana Current, maintenance, or deliberate production.

Renewability is conditional. Use can remain sustainable only when extraction, timing, source health, habitat, maintenance, and waste stay within the actual renewal route. A renewable source can collapse, move, refuse, or become inaccessible.

### Recovered and Recycled Inputs

Recovery uses residue, damaged goods, ruins, waste, or prior products as new inputs. It requires collection, knowledge, tools, energy, time, safety, and acceptable loss. Recycling never implies perfect return or costless conversion.

### Produced Resources

Some useful inputs exist only after cultivation, husbandry, crafting, refining, Ritual operation, Enchantment, Alchemical Process, or institutional work. The World Engine tracks their world-side inputs, output, capacity, dependencies, distribution, and waste. Owning systems resolve the capability and magical claims.

### Relational and Service Resources

Transport, care, teaching, translation, protection, labor, trust, legitimacy, and source cooperation may be essential inputs, but persons are not Stocks. Track service availability through willing or compelled actors, capability, time, relationship, authority, and consequence.

Coercion may produce output while changing legitimacy, unrest, health, resistance, and continuity. It never turns a person into inventory.

## Access and Effective Supply

**Effective Supply** is the portion of a resource that can actually reach a defined user and purpose in usable condition during the relevant interval.

Effective Supply may be limited by:

- distance, terrain, weather, season, or hostile territory;
- knowledge of the source or method;
- extraction and processing capacity;
- compatibility, toxicity, contamination, or spoilage;
- law, ownership, monopoly, taboo, oath, or sacred status;
- source agency, consent, refusal, or negotiated conditions;
- transport and storage bottlenecks;
- unequal distribution, discrimination, theft, corruption, or rationing;
- required tools, Skills, Magic, Professions, or maintenance;
- danger to collectors, handlers, consumers, or ecology;
- competing uses and opportunity cost.

Do not subtract all limitations into one universal efficiency percentage unless a campaign has a justified local model for one narrow decision.

## Scarcity, Shortage, and Insecurity

**Scarcity** means accessible supply is limited relative to one or more uses within a stated scope and time. It can exist even when no one currently lacks necessities because allocation, conservation, or substitutes are working.

A **Shortage** is a current failure of Effective Supply to meet a specified demand or obligation. It identifies who lacks what, where, for how long, and why.

**Resource Insecurity** is material exposure to future loss, interruption, control, degradation, or unaffordability of Effective Supply. A population may be supplied today but insecure because one route, source, season, operator, or political agreement has no redundancy.

Scarcity does not directly set price, conflict, innovation, migration, or collapse. Those require Causal Links and their owning domains.

## Resource Bottlenecks

A **Resource Bottleneck** is the narrowest current condition limiting one supply route or use.

The bottleneck may be source quantity, collection, processing, skilled labor, source consent, storage, transport, information, security, distribution, compatible tools, waste capacity, or final access.

Adding more input before the bottleneck may create stockpiles or waste without increasing delivery. Several bottlenecks may operate at different locations or for different groups.

## Depletion and Renewal

**Depletion** is a reduction in accessible quantity, quality, renewal capacity, source health, or future options caused by use, damage, neglect, displacement, or contamination.

Track:

- what was removed or degraded;
- the affected source and users;
- extraction rate and pattern where material;
- renewal process and delay;
- thresholds or irreversible losses established by the owning ecology or magic rules;
- hidden depletion masked by reserves or imports;
- burdens displaced elsewhere;
- recovery requirements and remaining World Legacy.

Ending extraction stops one cause. It does not instantly restore soil, habitat, trust, source consent, aquifers, Mana Fields, or extinct organisms.

## Substitution

**Resource Substitution** replaces one input, source, route, or use with another for a stated purpose.

A substitute must be assessed for:

- functional adequacy;
- compatibility with bodies, tools, methods, culture, and law;
- conversion or retraining needs;
- new source, transport, maintenance, and waste dependencies;
- distribution and control;
- quality loss or changed risk;
- time required to adopt it;
- effects on displaced workers, species, institutions, and places.

No substitute is universally equivalent. A lower-quality food may prevent immediate starvation while causing long-term health problems. Magical transport may bypass a blocked road while creating source dependence and specialized maintenance.

## Reserves and Redundancy

A **Strategic Reserve** is a protected Stock held for defined disruptions, users, or obligations. Its value depends on condition, access, security, release authority, distribution, and replacement.

**Supply Redundancy** exists when materially independent sources, routes, processors, stores, or providers can sustain a function after one fails. Two routes sharing the same bridge, source, operator, or political permission are not fully independent.

Reserves and redundancy reduce some risks. They can also concentrate power, attract attack, decay unused, or create false confidence.

## Food and Sustenance

### Sustenance Profile

A **Sustenance Profile** describes what one body, life stage, species, colony, or embodied process requires to remain viable over a stated interval.

It may include:

- energy and material needs;
- water, minerals, trace substances, Mana, light, heat, hosts, symbionts, memories, emotions, or setting-specific inputs where canon supports them;
- frequency, season, life-stage, and condition;
- preparation, digestion, absorption, or conversion;
- toxicity, allergy, taboo, incompatibility, and excess;
- cultural acceptability and voluntary practice;
- substitutes and consequences of deficiency.

There is no universal food unit across radically different embodiments. Calories, mass, Mana, prey count, sunlight area, or feeding sites may each answer only local questions.

### Food Chain of Delivery

Food security depends on more than production:

> viable source -> cultivation or collection -> harvest -> processing -> preservation -> storage -> transport -> allocation -> preparation -> consumption -> absorption -> health outcome.

Loss can occur at every link. A harvest can be abundant while households starve because storage, transport, authority, money, safety, discrimination, or preparation fails.

### Food Security

**Food Security** is reliable, timely, safe, and socially usable access to adequate sustenance for a defined population and interval.

Assess:

- current Effective Supply;
- nutritional or embodiment-specific adequacy;
- distribution among cohorts and locations;
- seasonal and disruption resilience;
- preparation and care capacity;
- source sustainability;
- cultural, religious, and ethical constraints;
- exposure to monopoly, refusal, conflict, contamination, and route loss.

Food Security is not one global score and does not prove general prosperity, health, legitimacy, or Stability.

### Predation, Sapience, and Personhood

Ecological predation may be ordinary for some species. Sapience, Soul status, culture, relationship, and consent create additional ethical, legal, factional, and spiritual consequences.

The World Engine may track that one being feeds on another. It does not classify persons as morally neutral inventory, waive agency, grant Skills through consumption, or define Soul effects. Existing Skill, Soul, Monster Evolution, and campaign ethics remain authoritative.

## Waste and External Burdens

A **Waste Burden** is residue or displaced harm created by extraction, transformation, transport, use, or disposal that another process, place, population, source, or future time must absorb.

Waste may be:

- spoiled material or unusable by-product;
- contaminated water, soil, air, food, Mana, or habitat;
- heat, noise, light, scent, magical Drift, or predatory attraction;
- injuries, disease exposure, or labor exhaustion;
- disposal sites and maintenance obligations;
- depleted source health or lost future access.

Calling something waste reflects a user's current method. Another process may recover it, but recovery requires its own route and can create further burdens.

## Resource Resolution Procedure

When a resource or food question matters:

1. **Name user and purpose.** Avoid unscoped claims such as "the region has enough."
2. **Identify source and form.** Establish current location, quality, and sustaining process.
3. **Trace the supply chain.** Include collection, transformation, storage, transport, distribution, preparation, and final access as relevant.
4. **Find the bottleneck.** Identify the condition currently limiting delivery.
5. **Apply ownership and agency.** Resolve law, control, consent, refusal, obligations, and contested claims.
6. **Check renewal and depletion.** Compare use with the actual renewal route at the relevant timescale.
7. **Check distribution.** Preserve excluded groups, competing uses, and who bears risk or waste.
8. **Evaluate substitutes and redundancy.** Include switching time, quality, dependencies, and side effects.
9. **Apply uncertainty.** Separate surveys, inventories, forecasts, beliefs, concealment, and world truth.
10. **Update direct variables.** Record Stocks, Flows, Capacity, Constraints, and Legacies once.
11. **Propagate consequences.** Hand changed inputs to population, economy, ecology, faction, war, disease, advancement, dungeon, Magic, or other owners through Causal Event Chains.
12. **Record externally.** Named sources, quantities, reserves, routes, claims, and current conditions are campaign data.

## Cross-System Interfaces

### Populations

[Populations](POPULATIONS.md) supply sustenance demand, cohorts, providers, labor availability, and distribution. Resources supply survival, care, reproduction, settlement, and continuity conditions. Neither automatically resolves the other's outcomes.

### Development and Skills

People need capability to locate, extract, cultivate, preserve, transport, transform, and recover resources. [Development](../progression/README.md) and [Skills](../skills/README.md) own those capabilities. Resource abundance does not grant mastery; mastery does not create absent matter or consent.

### Monster Evolution and Ecology

Resource use changes niches, prey, habitat, competition, toxins, and Evolutionary Pressures. [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) owns Adaptation, Mutation, Evolution, and species consequences. Later ecology rules own ecosystem propagation.

### Human Classes and Professions

Professions and institutions organize production, standards, custody, distribution, and expertise. [Human systems](../human/README.md) own recognition and progression. A job vacancy or material shortage does not create a qualified Profession holder.

### Soul Weapons

Weapon Souls may need maintenance, compatible materials, Mana, relationship, sites, or other support defined by [Soul Weapon](../soul-weapons/README.md) rules. They remain persons and cannot be counted as transferable resources or compelled sources by classification.

### Magic

Magic may locate, create, transform, preserve, transport, contaminate, or destroy resources only through valid Magic rules and a bounded [Magic-World Claim](../magic/WORLD_ENGINE_INTERACTIONS.md). Mana, Divine Sources, spirits, and other persons are not generic fuel. Conservation, source agency, cost, failure, Drift, and maintenance remain active.

## Worked Examples

### Grain Behind a Closed Pass

A valley has full granaries, but an avalanche closes the pass to a mining town. The regional Stock is adequate while the town's Effective Supply falls.

The bottleneck is transport, not production. Pack trails, magical transport, rationing, local substitutes, repair, and evacuation each have different times, users, risks, and dependencies. A regional total cannot settle the local shortage.

### Renewable Forest Under Pressure

A forest regrows harvested wood under ordinary cutting, but demand rises after a city fire. Faster extraction removes seed trees and damages soil while road building fragments habitat.

The forest was renewable under earlier conditions. It is not infinitely renewable. Ecology resolves habitat consequences; economies resolve demand and exchange; the resource record preserves depletion and future recovery needs.

### Abundant but Unsafe Water

A river remains physically abundant after Alchemical waste enters upstream. Water quantity changes little, but quality and fitness for several species decline. Boiling helps against one hazard but concentrates another.

The solution must follow actual contamination, treatment capability, distribution, and waste routes. "Plenty of water" is not a valid access claim.

### Sentient Spring

A local water spirit voluntarily sustains irrigation under a covenant. Officials expand withdrawals without consultation. The spirit reduces cooperation.

The source is not a passive Stock. Magic owns the spirit relation and valid effect. Resource rules update Effective Supply, dependence, alternatives, and affected users. Faction rules later resolve negotiation and legitimacy.

### Monster Sustenance

A monster species can survive on mineral deposits but requires one rare fungus during metamorphosis. Adult food appears abundant while the life-cycle bottleneck declines from cave disturbance.

Population continuity and Evolutionary Pressure change. The resource model does not invent an Evolution, alternative diet, or migration route.

### Perfect-Looking Substitute

An Enchantment preserves meat indefinitely while supplied with Mana. A kingdom replaces local preservation networks with a centralized enchanted store.

Spoilage falls, but dependence on operators, Mana, maintenance, transport, and one facility rises. The substitute improves one function while creating concentration risk and new Waste Burdens when the Enchantment Drifts.

## Safeguards

- Never equate presence, ownership, or total quantity with Effective Supply.
- Never treat renewable as inexhaustible or finite as immediately scarce.
- Never omit quality, access, distribution, renewal, waste, or source agency when they can reverse the result.
- Never treat persons, labor, Souls, Weapon Souls, spirits, gods, or sentient sources as inventory.
- Never grant a Skill, Evolution, magical effect, or Soul benefit because a material was consumed or accumulated.
- Never assume one food unit, diet, resource value, or carrying threshold applies to every embodiment.
- Never make scarcity automatically cause price, innovation, violence, migration, or collapse.
- Never make substitutes perfectly equivalent or instantly adopted.
- Never restore a depleted source merely because extraction stops.
- Never hide unequal distribution inside a regional aggregate.
- Never let reserves or imports erase local dependency and future insecurity.
- Keep current sources, quantities, inventories, routes, harvests, reserves, and shortages outside this repository.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- campaign inventories, loot, prices, currencies, markets, taxes, contracts, or trade outcomes;
- canonical resources, diets, settlements, production values, or regional abundance;
- universal nutrition, extraction, spoilage, renewal, depletion, or carrying-capacity formulas;
- ecology, migration, disease, war, faction, advancement, dungeon, or Stability outcomes;
- magical creation, Alchemical Recipes, Enchantment Functions, Divine obligations, or Mana generation;
- moral permission to consume, own, exploit, or coerce persons.
