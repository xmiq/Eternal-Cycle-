# World Engine Overview

## Purpose

The World Engine models how conditions and decisions create further consequences across time.

## Causal Chains

A causal chain links a condition to plausible reactions:

> Dragon extinction → wyvern expansion → griffin migration → livestock loss → village fortification → military recruitment → taxation pressure.

A chain is not destiny. Counterforces, adaptation, intervention, and chance may redirect it.

## Core Domains

- population and demographics;
- food and natural resources;
- ecology and migration;
- trade and economies;
- factions and institutions;
- war and unrest;
- disease and medicine;
- magic and technology;
- dungeons and supernatural regions;
- climate and disasters;
- culture and religion.

## Simulation Resolution

The GM uses only as much detail as play requires:

- **Immediate:** moments to days, for scenes and crises.
- **Local:** weeks to years, for settlements and factions.
- **Regional:** years to decades, for kingdoms and ecosystems.
- **Epochal:** decades to centuries, for Ages and civilizational change.

The engine should produce playable consequences, not unnecessary bookkeeping.
