# Disease Evolution

## Purpose

This document defines how disease arises, affects embodied beings, spreads or persists through populations and environments, responds to intervention, and changes over time within the [World Engine](README.md).

It provides a qualitative simulation framework rather than a real-world medical reference, universal health statistic, catalogue of diseases, or treatment generator. Diagnosis, medical practice, Skills, bodily Development, Species Traits, Monster Evolution, Magic, Alchemy, Soul harm, and Soul Weapon vessels retain their existing owners.

## Core Rule

> A disease outcome follows an established cause acting through compatible exposure, host, embodiment, transmission, and persistence routes under current protections, interventions, and uncertainty.

Therefore:

- exposure is not infection;
- infection is not symptomatic illness;
- symptoms do not prove the cause or current infectiousness;
- recovery does not guarantee immunity;
- immunity to one process is not universal resistance;
- treatment success in one body does not prove population control;
- a disease does not evolve merely because time passes or treatment exists;
- host suffering does not award bodily, Soul, Skill, or Evolution progress;
- migration, monsters, poverty, Magic, and foreign origin are not diseases;
- death or Reincarnation may end a body-bound condition without ending its world consequences.

## What Counts as Disease

A **Disease Process** is a harmful or materially disruptive change in an embodied being's function, integrity, regulation, development, or relationship with another process, evaluated against that being's actual body, life stage, form, needs, and ordinary variation.

A Disease Process may arise from:

- replicating biological agents;
- parasites, symbionts whose relationship has become harmful, or vectors;
- toxins, deficiencies, contamination, or environmental exposure;
- inherited or developmental conditions;
- immune, regulatory, degenerative, or metabolic dysfunction;
- injuries and secondary complications;
- magical Patterns, Mana Distortion, curses, Divine effects, Alchemical products, or Enchantment failures whose owning rules establish the effect;
- Soul-level harm only when the [Soul Engine](../soul/README.md) explicitly owns the affected layer;
- combinations of several causes.

Not every disease is transmissible, infectious, alive, hostile, visible, curable, or fatal. A harmless difference is not a disease merely because an institution, culture, or observer finds it unfamiliar. Disability, form, species, Mutation, Evolution, magical affinity, possession, moral dissent, and social nonconformity are not disease categories by default.

### Cause and Presentation

**Disease Etiology** is the established or proposed causal account of one Disease Process, including source, route, affected layer, mechanism, enabling conditions, and uncertainty.

**Disease Presentation** is the observable or reported pattern of signs, symptoms, behavior changes, functional limits, stages, and test results in a particular host or cohort.

Similar presentations may have different causes. One cause may present differently across bodies, life stages, forms, Mana conditions, treatments, and coexisting conditions. Naming a syndrome does not prove its Etiology, and knowing an Etiology does not reveal every consequence.

## Disease Agents and Personhood

A **Disease Agent** is a propagating biological, parasitic, magical, spiritual, constructed, or other established cause capable of producing a Disease Process in compatible hosts or systems.

An agent may be:

- non-sapient and replicating;
- one stage of a larger organism's life cycle;
- a colony, swarm, symbiont, or distributed process;
- a magical Pattern or self-maintaining contamination;
- a created construct or Alchemical product;
- a sapient parasite, spirit, curse-bearer, or other person-like source.

Calling something a Disease Agent does not decide its moral status or personhood. Sapient or person-like agents retain agency, communication, interests, consent, and responsibility under their own rules. Protecting hosts can still require separation, containment, treatment, negotiation, or force; those actions must preserve the distinction between hazard control and denial of personhood.

Toxins, deficiencies, inherited conditions, injuries, and non-propagating magical damage may cause Disease Processes without being Disease Agents.

## Host and Embodiment

A **Disease Host** is an embodied being, organism, colony, vessel, or bounded living system in which a specified Disease Process can begin, persist, develop, or be carried.

Host claims are body- and process-specific. Relevant factors may include:

- anatomy, material, metabolism, Channels, Mana relations, and Species Traits;
- life stage, reproductive stage, metamorphosis, dormancy, and current form;
- current health, injury, nutrition, rest, stress, and coexisting conditions;
- prior exposure, acquired protection, inherited features, treatment, and care;
- microbiome, symbioses, parasites, Soul Weapon interfaces, or other embodied relationships;
- behavior, work, housing, travel, culture, and social contact;
- environmental conditions, season, habitat, pollution, Mana weather, and infrastructure;
- access to information, prevention, diagnosis, medicine, healing, and recovery support.

Species labels do not establish uniform susceptibility. Individuals and cohorts may differ materially, and one species may be a host at one life stage but not another.

### Susceptibility and Protection

**Disease Susceptibility** is a host's current, process-specific likelihood of developing a stated Disease State after a defined exposure under present conditions. It is not weakness, moral failure, or a universal vulnerability score.

A **Protection Factor** is one current feature that reduces, redirects, delays, detects, contains, or repairs a specified disease effect or transmission route. Protection can arise from embodiment, prior immune response, behavior, Skill, medicine, Magic, infrastructure, social care, habitat, or policy.

Protection is scoped. A barrier against inhalation may not prevent blood transmission; a treatment that reduces symptoms may not reduce infectiousness; magical cleansing may remove a curse Pattern while leaving tissue injury or social consequences.

## Exposure, Transmission, and Establishment

### Exposure Events

An **Exposure Event** is actual contact between a possible host and a disease cause through a stated route, dose or intensity, timing, duration, and environment.

Potential proximity is not exposure. Exposure is not successful establishment. Resolve:

1. whether the cause is present and viable or active;
2. whether a compatible route reaches the host;
3. the relevant dose, intensity, duration, repetition, and timing;
4. barriers, behavior, Protection Factors, and uncertainty;
5. whether the cause establishes, is cleared, remains latent, or produces another outcome.

### Transmission Routes

A **Transmission Route** is the causal path by which a Disease Agent or disease-causing Pattern moves, reproduces, activates, or is carried from a source or Reservoir to another compatible host or site.

Routes can include:

- direct contact, fluids, breath, spores, shed material, or wounds;
- food, water, soil, surfaces, waste, tools, or constructed infrastructure;
- vectors, intermediate hosts, prey, predators, symbionts, or reproductive stages;
- vertical or developmental transfer;
- magical Channels, shared Ritual structures, Enchantment networks, dreams, names, oaths, or spiritual relationships when a completed Magic rule establishes that route;
- Soul interfaces only under explicit Soul rules with proportionate safeguards.

A **Disease Vector** carries or enables transmission without necessarily suffering the same Disease Process. A **Disease Reservoir** is a population, host, environment, object network, magical structure, or other persistent source in which an agent or Pattern can remain available for later transmission.

Neither Vector nor Reservoir implies guilt, hostility, expendability, or a right to destroy a species or community.

### Transmission Networks

Transmission depends on actual contacts and routes, not population density alone. Social structure, care work, migration, trade, habitat overlap, infrastructure, lifecycle, season, war, housing, Ritual participation, and behavior shape who encounters whom.

Networks are uneven. A healer, water system, pollinator, portal station, nursery, shared spawning pool, or Mana conduit may connect otherwise separate populations. A highly connected route may also support rapid warning, care, and intervention.

## Disease States and Courses

A **Disease State** is one host- and process-specific condition at a given time, such as:

- unexposed;
- exposed but not established;
- established without detectable presentation;
- latent, dormant, contained, or intermittent;
- actively symptomatic;
- transmissible with or without symptoms;
- impaired, stable, worsening, recovering, or relapsing;
- cleared with no lasting protection;
- cleared with scoped acquired protection;
- persistently altered, disabled, scarred, or dependent on support;
- dead or no longer a viable host.

These are descriptive possibilities, not one mandatory sequence. Different processes use different courses, intervals, and states.

### Severity and Outcome

Severity is multidimensional. Record what matters:

- pain, distress, function, integrity, and risk of death;
- duration, recurrence, latency, and recovery burden;
- transmissibility and route timing;
- effects on reproduction, growth, metamorphosis, memory, senses, Magic, or Soul Weapon interfaces;
- treatment needs, side effects, accessibility, and long-term support;
- impact on care networks, households, populations, ecology, institutions, and livelihoods.

A mild presentation can have high transmission consequences. A severe non-transmissible condition can be devastating to one person without creating an outbreak.

## Outbreaks and Persistence

An **Outbreak** is a bounded increase or newly recognized cluster of related Disease Processes, Disease Agents, or transmissions above the expected current pattern for a stated population, place, and interval.

An outbreak can pass through qualitative phases:

1. **introduction or recognition:** a cause arrives, activates, changes, or is newly detected;
2. **establishment:** enough compatible hosts, routes, or Reservoirs sustain further cases;
3. **amplification:** transmission or recognition expands through favorable networks and delays;
4. **response:** behavior, care, policy, treatment, migration, and agent change alter the pattern;
5. **decline, containment, or transformation:** cases fall, routes change, detection changes, or presentation shifts;
6. **elimination, recurrence, or persistence:** local transmission ends, returns from a Reservoir, or becomes maintained.

These are not mandatory stages or numerical thresholds. A recognized increase may reflect better surveillance rather than more disease. An apparent decline may reflect hidden cases, migration, changed definitions, loss of reporting, or true reduction.

### Endemic Persistence

**Endemic Persistence** is continued maintenance of a Disease Agent or Process within a stated population, network, Reservoir, or environment through current transmission, recurrence, reactivation, or replenishment routes.

Endemic does not mean harmless, inevitable, stable, natural, or evenly distributed. A persistent process can change with seasons, institutions, immunity, treatment, conflict, migration, ecology, and agent evolution.

## Disease Evolution

**Disease Evolution** is heritable or self-preserving change across reproducing or propagating Disease Agent lineages or Patterns that alters relevant traits under actual variation, transmission, persistence, and selection.

Disease Evolution requires:

1. a lineage or propagating Pattern with continuity;
2. variation capable of being preserved across propagation;
3. differential persistence, reproduction, transmission, or activation under current conditions;
4. enough events and time for that difference to affect the represented population;
5. costs, tradeoffs, constraints, and uncertainty.

Change may affect:

- host range or tissue access;
- route, timing, persistence, or environmental survival;
- detectability, latency, or presentation;
- interaction with host defenses;
- response to medicine, Magic, Alchemy, or infrastructure;
- virulence or host damage;
- dependence on vectors, Reservoirs, Mana conditions, or social practices.

Evolution is not directed improvement. A variant that spreads under one condition may be less stable elsewhere, more dependent on one host, easier to detect, less damaging, more damaging, or irrelevant after behavior changes.

### Within-Host and Between-Host Change

Change within one host may affect that case without becoming common across transmissions. A trait that helps persistence inside one body may reduce exit or spread. Conversely, a lineage may transmit well while producing little within-host dominance.

Do not infer population-level evolution from one dramatic case. Trace whether the changed lineage actually propagates.

### Disease Selection Pressure

**Disease Selection Pressure** is a current condition that changes which heritable Disease Agent variants or persistent Patterns reproduce, transmit, remain, or activate more successfully.

Possible pressures include:

- host protection and susceptibility patterns;
- treatment, prophylaxis, healing, or magical cleansing;
- surveillance and isolation;
- climate, habitat, Mana, waste, vectors, and Reservoirs;
- behavior, population structure, transport, war, and institutions;
- competition, cooperation, exchange, or recombination among agents;
- changes in host bodies through ordinary biology, Species Development, Mutation, or Evolution.

Pressure does not design a desired counter. Treatment does not command resistance into existence, and failed treatment does not prove evolution. Resistance-like outcomes can also arise from poor diagnosis, insufficient access, wrong application, counterfeit products, interruption, incompatible bodies, protected Reservoirs, or nonheritable host conditions.

### Treatment Pressure and Stewardship

**Treatment Pressure** is Disease Selection Pressure specifically created when interventions affect some agent variants or Pattern states differently from others.

Responsible intervention considers:

- correct cause and target;
- sufficient and safe application;
- distribution and adherence constraints;
- monitoring for failure, side effects, and change;
- alternatives and combination approaches;
- protection of effective tools from avoidable misuse;
- access justice, consent, and the harms of withholding care.

Stewardship does not justify denying necessary treatment merely to preserve an abstract future resource. Present patients and affected populations remain persons with current needs and agency.

## Host Change Is Separately Owned

Hosts may change through several different mechanisms:

- temporary physiological response;
- acquired process-specific protection;
- learned avoidance, care, hygiene, or medical Skill;
- treatment, Magic, equipment, or infrastructure;
- Physical, Species, Professional, Social, or Magical Development;
- Mutation or inherited population change;
- Monster Adaptation or a valid Evolution Route.

These are not interchangeable. Disease exposure can create pressure and evidence, but Disease Evolution does not grant host Evolution. Surviving infection alone does not create hereditary immunity, a Skill, resistance, Mutation, or new Species Potential.

Host and agent populations may change in response to one another over long periods. This **coevolution** remains a causal interaction among separate lineages and systems, not an arms-race ladder that guarantees endless power or symmetrical improvement.

## Diagnosis, Treatment, and Care

### Diagnosis

Diagnosis is an evidence-based professional process that distinguishes plausible causes, states, risks, and interventions under uncertainty. It requires relevant knowledge, observation, tests, interpretation, communication, and revision.

One symptom, spell result, institutional label, species stereotype, divine claim, or recovered memory is not conclusive by itself. Diagnostic evidence has provenance, false positives, false negatives, compatibility limits, and changing validity.

### Intervention Types

Interventions can target different layers:

- prevent or reduce exposure;
- interrupt one Transmission Route;
- reduce susceptibility or support a Protection Factor;
- prevent establishment or activation;
- suppress, remove, contain, negotiate with, or alter an agent;
- reduce symptoms or protect function;
- repair injury and support recovery;
- protect contacts, caregivers, Vectors, or Reservoir interfaces;
- change infrastructure, behavior, housing, waste, food, water, or ecology;
- preserve dignity, communication, pain relief, and quality of life when cure is unavailable.

Success at one layer does not imply success at all others. Symptom relief can improve survival while leaving transmission. A cleansing spell can remove an agent while failing to repair damage. Quarantine can reduce contact while causing material, social, or political harm.

### Medicine, Magic, and Alchemy

[Profession Development](../progression/PROFESSION_DEVELOPMENT.md) owns medical judgment and reliable practice. The [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) owns learned procedures. [Alchemy](../magic/ALCHEMY.md) owns Alchemical Processes and products. Other [Magic](../magic/README.md) rules own their sources, Patterns, effects, costs, and failures.

No magical label creates a universal cure. Healing, cleansing, restoration, purification, resurrection, time alteration, transformation, and divine intervention must state exactly what layer changes. Treatment still requires diagnosis, appropriate targeting, Access, capacity, consent, monitoring, aftercare, and distribution.

### Care and Recovery

Care includes food, water, shelter, hygiene, pain relief, communication, rest, mobility support, mental and social support, rehabilitation, childcare, lifecycle support, and assistance with ordinary needs. It is not lesser than cure and can determine survival and long-term function.

Recovery may produce scars, changed capacity, dependence, disability, altered relationships, lost work, new knowledge, or recurrence risk. It need not restore a prior body or life.

## Public Health and Institutions

**Public Health Capacity** is the function-specific collective ability to observe, prevent, communicate, diagnose, treat, support, contain, and recover from specified Disease Processes within a population under current conditions.

It may depend on:

- trusted surveillance and reporting;
- laboratories, diviners, healers, records, and interpreters;
- accessible care, medicine, Magic, food, water, housing, sanitation, and transport;
- legal authority, legitimacy, funding, staffing, supply, and accountability;
- culturally and species-compatible communication;
- protection for caregivers, disabled people, dependents, migrants, and stigmatized groups;
- cooperation among factions, institutions, gods, spirits, monsters, and neighboring regions;
- contingency plans, reserves, redundancy, and learning.

Public Health Capacity is not one civilization rank. A wealthy magical capital may detect curses well and fail at clean water; a mobile monster society may have strong isolation practices and little surgical capacity.

### Surveillance and Case Definitions

Surveillance observes indicators through specified sources and definitions. Reported cases are not identical to all actual cases. Changes in access, trust, testing, law, stigma, incentives, migration, or definitions can change the record without changing underlying incidence in the same way.

Institutions may be mistaken, captured, secretive, overwhelmed, or politically pressured. World truth, official diagnosis, public belief, and player knowledge remain separate.

### Consent, Coercion, and Burden

Isolation, quarantine, testing, treatment, movement limits, disclosure, culling, magical marking, and contact tracing can protect people while also imposing serious burdens. Adjudication identifies:

- actual evidence and uncertainty;
- authority, legitimacy, necessity, scope, duration, and review;
- informed consent or the exact coercion used;
- who bears material, social, bodily, spiritual, and privacy costs;
- support, appeal, accommodation, alternatives, and accountability;
- whether measures target routes or merely stigmatize identities.

Emergency does not erase agency or make every intervention wise. Refusal does not automatically imply ignorance or malice; it may reflect cost, distrust, prior abuse, incompatible care, misinformation, or different risk.

## Population, Ecology, and Social Consequences

Disease can change:

- mortality, births, maturation, care, migration, composition, and Demographic Momentum;
- labor, production, exchange, debt, prices, medicine access, and inequality;
- faction trust, institutional legitimacy, unrest, war operations, and border policy;
- habitats, vectors, Reservoirs, predation, symbiosis, succession, and waste;
- rituals, funerals, family structures, education, language, memory, and religion;
- magical infrastructure, Divine relationships, Soul Weapon care, and Evolutionary Pressure.

Apply [Populations](POPULATIONS.md), [Resources and Food](RESOURCES_AND_FOOD.md), [Economies](ECONOMIES.md), [Ecology and Migration](ECOLOGY_AND_MIGRATION.md), [Faction Behaviour](FACTION_BEHAVIOUR.md), and [War and Unrest](WAR_AND_UNREST.md) to downstream claims. Disease does not dictate one social response.

Migration can spread, reduce, or merely reveal disease depending on routes and conditions. Migrants are not pathogens. Border closure may interrupt one route while trapping people without care, diverting movement, concealing cases, or leaving ecological Reservoirs untouched.

## Souls, Reincarnation, Monsters, and Soul Weapons

### Souls and Reincarnation

Ordinary Disease Processes affect current embodiment, not the Soul. Final Death ends that body's host state, while contamination, contacts, descendants, institutions, grief, records, and other world consequences persist.

A new incarnation does not automatically inherit the former body's infection, acquired immunity, scars, medical implants, medicine, or Species Traits. It may retain Soul-level memory, Imprints, Echoes, Titles, or Development potential according to their owners, but must learn the new body's susceptibility and rebuild medical Access and expression.

A disease that claims to follow Reincarnation must establish an explicit Soul-level route, affected Soul layer, warning, resistance, limits, and aftermath under Soul-harm safeguards. Narrative labels such as soul plague or karmic illness are insufficient.

### Monsters and Evolution

Monster bodies, life stages, forms, habitats, and social structures create distinct host and transmission relationships. Disease can supply Evolutionary Pressure, but exposure, survival, mortality, or selection among existing bodies does not grant an individual Evolution.

Mutation, Adaptation, Hybridization, and Evolution remain separate. A Disease Agent lineage changing is not a monster's Evolution. If a parasite is itself a monster species, its bodily route still uses Monster Evolution rather than receiving free forms through the disease model.

### Soul Weapons and Biotic Vessels

Weapon Souls are not diseases. A living, symbiotic, colony, prosthetic, or body-linked Vessel may suffer or carry a Disease Process according to its actual material and living interfaces. Vessel illness does not automatically injure the Weapon Soul, Bonded Soul, or Soul Bond.

Treatment must distinguish Vessel health, host health, Weapon Soul consent, Bond communication, magical effects, and repair. Destroying a Vessel can threaten Weapon Soul continuity and is not ordinary equipment disposal.

## Contact Between Previously Separate Worlds

Contact between long-separated ecologies may create unfamiliar exposures, absent protections, novel host opportunities, false diagnoses, or incompatible medicine. It does not guarantee plague, universal susceptibility, or one-way harm.

Before inferring disease consequences, establish:

- an actual contact and Transmission Route;
- compatible hosts, Vectors, Reservoirs, or magical interfaces;
- environmental viability and timing;
- existing protections and cross-reactions;
- detection, communication, care, and movement;
- effects in both directions;
- uncertainty and Counterforces.

[World Gates and World-Contact Events](GATES_AND_WORLD_CONTACT.md) owns how world contact and its channels occur. This document owns disease claims only after a valid contact route exists.

## Disease Legacies

A **Disease Legacy** is a persistent bodily, demographic, ecological, institutional, economic, cultural, magical, or relational condition left after an outbreak or Disease Process changes.

Legacies may include:

- death, disability, chronic illness, altered care needs, grief, and orphaned dependents;
- acquired protection, changed susceptibility, agent Reservoirs, and recurring risk;
- depleted medicine, new production, debt, changed labor, and unequal recovery;
- surveillance systems, emergency powers, medical institutions, records, and distrust;
- stigma, solidarity, ritual change, memorials, and contested narratives;
- changed habitats, vectors, waste systems, migration, and species relationships;
- abandoned wards, Alchemical residue, divine obligations, or magical contamination;
- medical knowledge that remains useful, partial, secret, or dangerously outdated in a later Age.

Eliminating one agent does not erase its legacies. Recovery of population totals or economic output does not imply restored lives, distribution, trust, health, or ecology.

## Simulation Procedure

When disease matters:

1. **Define the Disease Process.** State affected function, host scope, and presentation without assuming cause.
2. **Establish Etiology and uncertainty.** Identify confirmed, inferred, disputed, claimed, and unknown causal facts.
3. **Identify hosts and embodiment.** Separate species, forms, life stages, conditions, and relevant cohorts.
4. **Trace exposure and transmission.** Establish sources, routes, timing, dose or intensity, Vectors, Reservoirs, and barriers.
5. **Resolve host states.** Distinguish exposure, establishment, presentation, infectiousness, recovery, protection, impairment, and death.
6. **Map networks and distribution.** Preserve uneven contact, care, mobility, detection, and vulnerability.
7. **Apply interventions by layer.** State what prevention, treatment, Magic, Alchemy, policy, or care actually changes.
8. **Assess agent change.** Require lineage continuity, heritable or self-preserving variation, differential propagation, and tradeoffs before declaring Disease Evolution.
9. **Trace responses.** Apply faction, institution, economy, war, migration, ecology, and information routes.
10. **Update consequences and uncertainty.** Record direct effects, delayed effects, hidden states, and evidence changes.
11. **Preserve legacies and recurrence routes.** Distinguish decline, elimination, persistence, and recovery.
12. **Store live state externally.** Use only the detail needed for decisions and continuity.

## Worked Examples

### Healing Magic Masks Transmission

A temple spell rapidly closes lung lesions and restores breathing. It does not remove the airborne agent from the host or contaminated dormitory.

Treated patients feel well and return to crowded work, increasing exposure until healers recognize the distinction. The spell remains a real success at damage repair; presenting it as a universal cure created the public-health failure.

### A Disease Pressure Does Not Award Evolution

A burrowing monster population faces a fungal Disease Agent concentrated in wet nesting chambers. Some individuals avoid damp sites, others receive antifungal care, and one lineage has an existing skin trait that reduces establishment.

Survivors do not all gain resistance. Over generations, Disease Selection Pressure may change lineage distribution. A compatible Evolution Route may become relevant only through the Monster Evolution rules; dry architecture may solve the problem without bodily Evolution.

### The Sapient Parasite

A sapient larval people require temporary hosts during one life stage. A new environmental contaminant causes some larvae to damage host organs, turning a formerly negotiated symbiosis into a Disease Process.

The larvae are not pathogens by identity. Physicians investigate the contaminant, hosts negotiate safer care, and factions dispute responsibility. Containment can protect bodies without authorizing extermination or denying larval personhood.

### The False Magical Plague

Residents across a city develop identical nightmares and fatigue after attending several shrines. Authorities assume contagious curse transmission and isolate families.

Investigation finds that each shrine uses pigment from one contaminated Mana source. There is a shared Etiology but no person-to-person route. Quarantine imposes cost without interrupting exposure; closing the source and treating Mana Distortion does.

### The Reincarnated Physician

A physician reincarnates after an Age transition with deep medical Development potential and retained diagnostic habits. The new body has unfamiliar senses, local anatomy includes symbiotic organs, and the dominant Disease Agent changed its presentation under centuries of treatment.

The physician notices patterns quickly but needs current study, tools, colleagues, and practice. Former mastery accelerates relearning; it does not provide automatic diagnosis, current licence, immunity, or a cure.

### Novel World Contact Without Automatic Catastrophe

Two worlds establish a brief material exchange. A spore can survive on imported wood but cannot establish in local animals; a harmless local symbiont becomes damaging inside one visiting construct species.

Disease consequences are asymmetric and specific. Contact creates monitoring, trade delays, negotiation, and new care needs, not an automatic extinction plague. [World Gates and World-Contact Events](GATES_AND_WORLD_CONTACT.md) owns the contact event itself.

## Safeguards

- Do not treat exposure, infection, symptoms, infectiousness, severity, recovery, immunity, and transmission as one state.
- Do not label disability, species, form, Mutation, Evolution, magical affinity, possession, social dissent, unfamiliar behavior, or moral difference as disease without a valid Disease Process.
- Do not infer disease from migrants, monsters, poverty, foreign origin, culture, religion, Soul Titles, Gates, or unusual embodiment.
- Do not make one species uniformly susceptible, immune, contagious, medicinal, or responsible by label.
- Do not make Disease Agents consciously optimize unless they possess established agency and knowledge.
- Do not declare Disease Evolution without continuity, preserved variation, differential propagation, time, and tradeoffs.
- Do not treat failed care as proof of evolved resistance before testing diagnosis, access, application, compatibility, adherence, Reservoirs, and nonheritable causes.
- Do not let healing Magic, purification, Alchemy, divination, divine authority, or resurrection become a universal diagnostic or cure.
- Do not grant immunity, resistance, Development, Skills, Stat XP, Soul growth, Mutation, Evolution, or Soul Weapon awakening merely through exposure, suffering, survival, treatment, or death.
- Do not make Reincarnation a source of inherited bodily immunity or let ordinary disease bypass Soul-harm safeguards.
- Do not erase consent, privacy, support, appeal, personhood, or unequal burden under a public-health emergency.
- Do not use Disease Profiles to provide real-world medical advice or claim universal biomedical accuracy.
- Do not use this document to replace [Technology and Magical Advancement](TECHNOLOGY_AND_MAGICAL_ADVANCEMENT.md), [Dungeon Activity](DUNGEON_ACTIVITY.md), [World Stability](WORLD_STABILITY.md), [Ages and World Resets](AGES_AND_WORLD_RESETS.md), [World Gates and World-Contact Events](GATES_AND_WORLD_CONTACT.md), or [Simulation Abstraction](SIMULATION_ABSTRACTION.md); hand each claim to its canonical owner.
- Do not store named active outbreaks, patients, case counts, current quarantines, treatments, live Reservoirs, or campaign timelines in this repository.

## External Campaign Records

A campaign may maintain a qualitative **Disease Profile** containing only what play requires:

- Disease Process, presentation, host scope, and current Disease States;
- confirmed, inferred, disputed, claimed, and unknown Etiology;
- Disease Agents, non-agent causes, Vectors, Reservoirs, and personhood questions;
- Exposure Events, Transmission Routes, timing, barriers, and networks;
- susceptibility, Protection Factors, severity, course, recovery, and recurrence;
- population distribution, cohorts, reporting, and surveillance limits;
- interventions by layer, access, consent, burdens, failures, and side effects;
- Disease Selection Pressure, variants, lineage evidence, tradeoffs, and uncertainty;
- institutional, factional, economic, ecological, war, migration, magical, and Soul Weapon consequences;
- Pending Consequences, Endemic Persistence, elimination evidence, and Disease Legacies.

This Profile is external campaign state. The repository contains no completed Disease Profile, current patient, or live outbreak.
