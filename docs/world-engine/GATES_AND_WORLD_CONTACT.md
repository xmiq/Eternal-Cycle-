# World Gates and World-Contact Events

## Purpose

This document defines how previously separated parts of the world enter material contact and how an extraordinary interface can carry that contact.

A **World-Contact Event** changes who or what can reach, perceive, influence, or cross between separated domains. A **World Gate** is one possible interface for that contact. Neither term supplies a plot, declares an invasion, compares whole civilizations with one score, or replaces the systems that own the consequences.

Current Gates, endpoints, contacted societies, discoveries, conflicts, and consequences are campaign state and belong in an external Campaign Record.

## Core Rule

A World Gate establishes only the bounded contact routes its Basis, endpoints, channels, conditions, and current operation support.

It does not automatically:

- permit every kind of transit in both directions;
- make either side understandable, habitable, vulnerable, conquerable, or compatible;
- grant ownership or legitimate authority to its discoverer or controller;
- create hostility, friendship, trade, disease, migration, progress, or cultural integration;
- turn a contact event into an Age Transition or World Reset;
- become a Soul Gate, Dungeon Access Route, ordinary portal, or Reincarnation route;
- make the player its cause, mediator, destination, or historically central actor.

Contact creates new possible causal routes. Existing actors and specialist systems determine what actually travels through those routes and what follows.

## Foundational Distinctions

| Phenomenon | Defining function | What it does not imply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ordinary route** | Uses the current world's established geography, transport, communication, or infrastructure | Extraordinary world contact, a World Gate, or a metaphysical transition |
| **Magical transit method** | Produces movement or communication under the [Magic rules](../magic/README.md) | A World Gate merely because it is a portal, teleportation circle, or spatial effect |
| **Dungeon Access Route** | Crosses or navigates a [Dungeon Boundary](DUNGEON_ACTIVITY.md#dungeon-access-route) | Contact between materially separated world domains |
| **Soul Gate** | Permits scoped contact with part of a [Soul Space](../soul/SOUL_SPACE.md#soul-gates-and-visitors) | Physical transit, ordinary possession transfer, or world-side contact |
| **World Gate** | Provides a bounded extraordinary interface between Contact Domains | Automatic Soul access, unrestricted travel, or every consequence associated with first contact |
| **World-Contact Event** | Opens or materially changes consequential exchange between previously separated domains | That a World Gate must exist, remain open, or be publicly known |
| **Age Transition** | Makes a successor historical Age Claim more useful through lasting causal change | That one contact event alone defines the new Age |
| **World Reset** | Reconfigures enough foundational conditions to require broad World Revalidation | That a Gate opening, closure, or breach is automatically a Reset |

The classification follows function and causality, not appearance or local vocabulary. A stone arch may be an ordinary door, a magical portal, a Dungeon Access Route, a World Gate, or merely ceremonial. A society may call all of them gates without making the mechanics interchangeable.

## World-Contact Events

A **World-Contact Event** occurs when a new or restored route permits consequential exchange between **Contact Domains** that were materially separated within the relevant scope and horizon.

A **Contact Domain** is one side of the contact model: a bounded region, realm, reality, population environment, isolated civilization, displaced historical context, or other world-side arrangement with conditions that can be compared at the interface. A Contact Domain need not be:

- a complete universe;
- one polity, culture, species, or faction;
- internally uniform;
- aware that the other domain exists;
- metaphysically independent;
- equal in size, age, population, or power.

Calling one Contact Domain another world, reality, or Age is a scoped claim, not proof of a particular cosmology. An Age label alone does not create time travel, an alternate timeline, a duplicate history, or a second present. Any temporal displacement or concurrent historical domain needs its own prior world-side cause and evidence.

Contact may arise through:

- a World Gate;
- removal of a physical, magical, cosmological, political, or informational barrier;
- rediscovery of an isolated continent, underground society, oceanic civilization, airborne settlement network, or other previously unreachable population;
- return or exposure of a displaced realm or region;
- a new communication route that carries consequential knowledge before bodies or materials can cross;
- another established process that creates a durable exchange route.

An explorer reaching an unfamiliar village by an ordinary navigable road is discovery, not necessarily a World-Contact Event. Reopening a sealed continent after several Ages may be a World-Contact Event even if ordinary ships eventually provide the route. The difference is the material separation and the new consequences, not spectacle.

## World Gate Qualification

A **World Gate** is a bounded world-side interface that creates, restores, or sustains extraordinary contact between two or more Contact Domains that are not otherwise mutually reachable through comparable current routes.

A valid World Gate claim identifies:

1. the Contact Domains being connected;
2. the material separation that made the contact extraordinary;
3. an established Gate Basis capable of creating or sustaining the interface;
4. one or more Gate Endpoints and a Gate Boundary;
5. the channels the interface can actually carry;
6. current direction, throughput, requirements, costs, and failure conditions for each material channel;
7. evidence that the connection exists, even if observers do not understand it;
8. a route by which contact can create or alter world conditions.

No universal distance, duration, energy expenditure, civilization difference, witness count, or visual form qualifies a Gate. A narrow one-way signal breach can qualify. A spectacular portal between nearby rooms may remain an ordinary magical transit method.

### Public Knowledge Is Not Required

A World Gate can exist before anyone discovers it. Hidden operation still needs prior world truth, a valid Basis, and actual effects appropriate to its channels. The GM cannot retroactively declare an unnoticed Gate solely to explain a desired arrival, threat, item, or revelation.

Discovery changes information and actor response. It does not create the Gate unless discovery is part of the established opening mechanism.

## Gate Structure

### Gate Basis

The **Gate Basis** is the established process, structure, relationship, source, law, actor, or combination that creates and sustains a World Gate's current functions.

Possible Bases include:

- a natural cosmological alignment;
- a magical Pattern, Ritual, Enchantment, source response, or Mana condition;
- divine or spiritual action within actual Domain, Jurisdiction, relationship, and consent rules;
- technical infrastructure;
- a living or agentive world process;
- a spatial wound, failed experiment, weapon effect, or other caused breach;
- an Age or Reset legacy whose continuing mechanism survived World Revalidation;
- several interdependent supports.

Calling a Gate natural, artificial, divine, accidental, deliberate, emergent, or weaponized describes provenance or use. It does not decide stability, morality, ownership, destination, compatibility, or consequence.

If the Gate Basis weakens, changes, is contested, or ends, only supported Gate functions change. Destroying a visible arch does not close a Gate anchored elsewhere. Repairing the arch does not restore an ended Basis.

### Gate Endpoint

A **Gate Endpoint** is the bounded world-side location, region, object, condition, or moving interface through which one Contact Domain meets the Gate.

Endpoints may be:

- fixed or mobile;
- concentrated or spatially distributed;
- continuously present or intermittently accessible;
- physically symmetric or radically different on each side;
- known on one side and unknown on another;
- controlled, contested, inaccessible, or unoccupied.

A Gate may have more than two endpoints. Each route between endpoints must still establish its own channels, direction, throughput, and conditions. One working path does not prove that every endpoint can reach every other endpoint.

### Gate Boundary

The **Gate Boundary** is the interface across which Gate-specific transit, transformation, exclusion, detection, or failure rules apply.

The boundary may coincide with a visible threshold, occupy a volume, move with a phenomenon, appear only under certain conditions, or differ by channel. Its scope determines what is exposed to direct Gate effects. Broader political, ecological, economic, and cultural consequences belong to the Gate Footprint rather than the boundary itself.

### Gate Footprint

The **Gate Footprint** is the actual distribution of a World Gate's direct and propagated effects across endpoints, connected routes, populations, environments, institutions, and time.

Its direct portion follows Gate Boundaries and channels. Its wider portion follows specialist causal chains, so it may be uneven, delayed, disputed, or much larger than the visible interface. A Gate Footprint is not proof that the Gate directly caused every change within it.

### Gate Identity

A Gate's identity follows continuity of Basis, endpoints, functions, and causal history rather than its local name or appearance alone.

Closing and reopening the same supported interface may continue one Gate. A new mechanism using an old site may establish another Gate. Several cultures may name one Gate differently, while similar-looking thresholds may be unrelated.

Identity matters for evidence, obligations, technical maintenance, treaties, hazards, and Gate Legacies. It never creates personhood unless another system establishes an actual person associated with the Gate.

## Gate Channels

A **Gate Channel** is one category of transfer or influence a World Gate can carry under stated conditions.

Useful channel categories include:

- **observation:** light, sound, remote sensing, dreams, traces, or other one-sided perception;
- **communication:** signals, language, encoded information, magical messages, or reciprocal awareness;
- **environmental exchange:** atmosphere, temperature, pressure, radiation, weather influence, Mana conditions, or elemental effects;
- **energy and Mana flow:** bounded transfer under the owning physical or [Mana](../magic/MANA.md) rules;
- **material transit:** objects, resources, waste, tools, weapons, vehicles, or infrastructure components;
- **organism transit:** microbes, seeds, spores, animals, monsters, parasites, or other living populations;
- **embodied-person transit:** living or constructed persons crossing with whatever current embodiment and support the interface genuinely permits;
- **causal influence without transit:** a process on one side changing conditions on the other through a supported interface.

These channels are independent. A Gate that carries speech may block matter. A body-sized passage may destroy delicate machines. A Mana flow may be one-way while material transit is reciprocal. A Gate that admits one species may reject another because of size, embodiment, source relation, or boundary conditions.

World Gate classification does not establish a separate soul channel. Soul Gates, Soul Intrusion, Interlife, Reincarnation, and Soul Avatar effects retain their own owners.

### Directionality

**Gate Directionality** records the direction in which one channel currently operates.

A channel may be:

- one-way;
- reciprocal;
- asymmetric, with different requirements or effects in each direction;
- alternating or cyclic;
- conditional on timing, load, actor, permission, source response, or world state;
- uncertain because evidence exists for only one direction.

Directionality is channel-specific. Returning a message does not prove a body can return. An expedition crossing successfully does not establish a safe route home.

### Throughput

**Gate Throughput** is the evidenced amount, frequency, speed, size, or complexity of transfer that one channel can sustain under current conditions.

Throughput may be described qualitatively, such as:

- intermittent signals only;
- individual crossings with long recovery;
- steady foot traffic but no large vehicles;
- seasonal mass migration;
- high material flow with dangerous environmental leakage;
- unknown upper limit because no safe test exists.

Throughput is not one Gate level. It varies by channel, direction, duration, load, maintenance, interference, and Gate condition. Testing it changes world state and can carry risk.

### Transit Conditions and Costs

Every material channel identifies requirements and consequences that matter, such as:

- timing or alignment;
- size, mass, shape, composition, life, identity, or permission;
- compatible equipment or protective preparation;
- Mana supply, source response, technical power, maintenance, or ritual participation;
- travel duration and subjective experience;
- transformation, disorientation, injury, contamination, memory uncertainty, or equipment damage supported by the Gate Basis;
- a safe method to stop, withdraw, or recover where one exists.

Costs arise through established owners. A Gate cannot consume arbitrary health, memory, Soul Depth, Skill, lifespan, or identity merely because passage should feel dramatic.

## Contact Compatibility

**Contact Compatibility** is a purpose-specific assessment of how conditions from one Contact Domain interact with the Gate and the other domain.

Compatibility must be tested by subject, direction, function, and context. Relevant layers include:

| Layer | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| **Spatial and physical** | Do scale, pressure, gravity, geometry, matter, energy, and movement behave sufficiently alike for the intended contact? |
| **Environmental** | Can bodies, tools, crops, structures, and processes tolerate the destination climate, atmosphere, radiation, Mana, or elemental conditions? |
| **Biological** | Can organisms survive, reproduce, transmit disease, consume local resources, or interact with local ecologies? |
| **Magical** | Do Mana relations, sources, affinities, Patterns, authorities, materials, and world laws support the same functions? |
| **Technical** | Are power, materials, tolerances, standards, maintenance, repair, manufacturing, and operator knowledge available? |
| **Sensory and cognitive** | Can participants perceive one another, recognize agency, interpret signals, and reason across different senses or cognition? |
| **Linguistic and informational** | Is communication possible, accurate, reciprocal, authenticated, and protected from false equivalence? |
| **Social and legal** | Who is recognized as a person, visitor, citizen, envoy, owner, refugee, invader, sacred being, hazard, or protected party? |

There is no universal compatibility percentage. A society may communicate precisely while remaining biologically incompatible. A weapon may function while its ammunition, repairs, or operator training cannot be reproduced. A Spell may preserve its Pattern but lack a viable Mana Relation at the destination.

Compatibility can improve through study, adaptation, infrastructure, treaties, translation, protective practice, or technical change. It can worsen through Drift, damaged support, environmental change, sabotage, disease evolution, depleted resources, or political refusal. Each improvement retains its owner, effort, cost, and limits.

## Opening, Operation, and Closure

### Gate Origin and Opening

Gate opening needs a prior causal route. Possible opening patterns include:

- gradual precursor signs followed by a threshold crossing;
- deliberate construction and activation;
- unintended activation of an existing structure;
- erosion or failure of a barrier;
- cyclical alignment;
- an actor opening a route for a stated purpose;
- a catastrophe whose established effects include a breach;
- reactivation of an old Gate whose Basis never fully ended.

Prophecy, narrative timing, player arrival, or the desire for a new theme is not a cause. Precursors are evidence only when an actual Basis produces them.

### Gate Operation

Current operation may be continuous, intermittent, cyclical, conditional, degrading, expanding, narrowing, redirected, or contested. These are scoped findings, not a universal Gate-state ladder.

Actors may affect operation only through valid interfaces. They might maintain a technical array, negotiate with a source, alter a Ritual, block an endpoint, restrict access, change load, sabotage support, or adapt protective equipment. Political control of an endpoint does not necessarily provide technical control of the Gate Basis, and technical control does not prove legitimate authority.

### Gate Closure

**Gate Closure** is the loss, suspension, sealing, redirection, or ending of one or more Gate channels through an established cause.

Closure may be:

- expected or sudden;
- complete or channel-specific;
- temporary, cyclical, conditional, or effectively permanent;
- voluntary, negotiated, accidental, contested, or imposed;
- local to one endpoint or common to the whole interface.

Closing an endpoint does not erase what crossed, undo relationships, refund costs, restore displaced populations, cure disease, reverse ecological transfer, or settle legal claims. A sealing Ritual owns only the magical effect it actually establishes. Military occupation owns only the access it can materially enforce.

### Gate Stranding

**Gate Stranding** occurs when beings, populations, material systems, obligations, or dependencies can no longer use a route on which they relied.

Stranding can create:

- separated families and factions;
- refugees or permanent diasporas;
- lost supply, care, maintenance, authority, or communication;
- abandoned expeditions and isolated settlements;
- stranded hostile forces as well as civilians;
- legal disputes over citizenship, custody, contracts, or property;
- hybrid institutions that can no longer consult either origin;
- ecological populations cut off from migration or reproduction routes.

Stranding is not automatic death, assimilation, return, or narrative removal. Existing resources, relationships, knowledge, embodiment, environment, and actor choices determine what follows.

## World-Contact Process

The following **Contact Phases** are descriptive tools rather than mandatory stages or a countdown:

1. **Precursor:** signs, anomalies, rumors, research, or preparations exist before confirmed contact.
2. **Opening or breach:** a Gate or other route becomes materially available.
3. **Discovery:** one or more actors obtain evidence of the route or other domain.
4. **Containment or uncontrolled contact:** actors attempt to limit access, while contact may instead spread beyond their control.
5. **First exchange:** information, influence, beings, or material crosses with uncertain interpretation.
6. **Escalation or de-escalation:** fear, curiosity, extraction, violence, cooperation, withdrawal, or miscommunication changes contact pressure.
7. **Institutional response:** factions develop policy, logistics, law, diplomacy, research, care, propaganda, or military plans.
8. **Adaptation:** participants, technologies, ecologies, economies, cultures, and institutions adjust unevenly.
9. **Durable outcome:** contact produces continuing integration, coexistence, dependency, domination, separation, collapse, closure, transformation, or several outcomes in different scopes.

Phases may overlap, repeat, reverse, be skipped, or occur differently for each actor. One faction may trade while another still denies the Gate exists. A Gate may close before first exchange. Institutional preparation may precede opening. No phase requires conflict, progress, or permanent contact.

## Discovery, Access, Control, and Authority

These claims remain separate:

- **discovery:** who first obtained credible evidence;
- **reach:** who can physically or informationally approach an endpoint;
- **use:** who can satisfy a channel's current transit conditions;
- **operation:** who can affect the Gate Basis or channel behavior;
- **control:** who can materially regulate access or operation, for which scope and duration;
- **claim:** who asserts ownership, custody, stewardship, jurisdiction, sacred responsibility, or exclusion;
- **authority:** whose decisions are recognized as legitimate by which audience;
- **consent:** which affected persons or sources agree to particular uses.

Discovery grants none of the others automatically. Occupation can create control without legitimacy. A local community may hold legitimate authority but lack the force to exclude an empire. A divine source may control one magical function without owning adjacent land or the people crossing it.

Gate access follows actual routes, infrastructure, schedules, knowledge, permissions, costs, monitoring, corruption, smuggling, and Counterforces. Declaring an endpoint sealed does not make it so. Perfect control is rare and never inferred from a map color or title.

## Information and Communication

World truth, observation, estimate, belief, propaganda, and player knowledge remain independent.

Before and during contact, actors may misunderstand:

- whether the Gate is natural, deliberate, hostile, stable, or unique;
- what crossed and in which direction;
- whether a message represents a government, faction, individual, machine, spirit, or deception;
- whether silence means refusal, inability, distance, delay, death, or an incompatible channel;
- whether unfamiliar behavior is cultural, biological, magical, technical, strategic, or accidental;
- whether either side understands the same map, time scale, personhood, ownership, promise, surrender, or sacred duty.

There is no automatic universal translation. Communication requires shared signals, interpretation, verification, feedback, and time. Magic, Skills, Soul history, technology, institutions, and nonverbal methods may support those functions only through their established rules.

A translation can be fluent yet conceptually wrong. An Archive Record can be relevant yet incomplete. A reincarnated memory can describe a prior society without proving the current contacted society is unchanged. Those specialist effects remain with their owners.

## Contact Asymmetry

**Contact Asymmetry** is a purpose-specific difference between Contact Domains that materially affects one interaction.

Possible asymmetries include:

- battlefield force;
- logistics, transport, repair, medicine, and supply;
- population scale and replacement capacity;
- local terrain and environmental tolerance;
- information, reconnaissance, secrecy, and communication speed;
- magical conditions, source relations, and countermeasures;
- technical production, standardization, and maintenance;
- Soul knowledge and protections;
- disease exposure and care capacity;
- political legitimacy, alliances, law, and institutional reach;
- ecological knowledge and adaptation;
- willingness to absorb losses or sustain long commitments.

No civilization has one authoritative technology level, magic level, progress rank, or power score. Superiority in one context does not transfer automatically to another. A force that wins every direct engagement may still fail to govern, resupply, interpret local politics, survive the environment, or justify continued costs.

Asymmetry is not moral worth, destiny, or proof of inevitable domination. It may change as information spreads, alliances form, infrastructure fails, Counterforces adapt, and objectives shift.

## Autonomous Contact

A World-Contact Event does not create two unified sides.

Each Contact Domain may contain:

- rival states and factions;
- dissenting institutions;
- local communities with different exposure;
- monsters, spirits, gods, Weapon Souls, and other persons with independent interests;
- traders, refugees, smugglers, scholars, missionaries, spies, raiders, diplomats, and opportunists;
- populations that know little about the contact or reject official policy;
- actors who cooperate across the Gate against factions from their own domain.

[Faction Behaviour](FACTION_BEHAVIOUR.md) owns interests, information, decisions, mobilization, cohesion, dissent, and adaptation. The Gate supplies new routes, evidence, incentives, constraints, and risks. It does not decide faction goals.

Off-screen actors continue to investigate, prepare, bargain, migrate, exploit, resist, conceal, adapt, or disengage according to their knowledge and means. Contact consequences continue without the player.

## Cross-System Handoffs

The World Gate document owns the existence and current operation of the contact interface. It hands each consequence to the established specialist owner.

### Populations and Migration

[Populations](POPULATIONS.md) owns demographic change. [Ecology and Migration](ECOLOGY_AND_MIGRATION.md) owns Migration Pressure, routes, displacement, arrival, settlement, return, and ecological movement.

A Gate Channel may create a route. It does not make migration inevitable, safe, voluntary, permanent, or demographically decisive. Throughput, access, destination conditions, policy, family ties, resources, conflict, and closure risk matter.

### Resources, Food, and Economies

[Resources and Food](RESOURCES_AND_FOOD.md) owns supply, access, renewal, depletion, substitution, reserves, Food Security, and Waste Burdens. [Economies](ECONOMIES.md) owns production, allocation, exchange, Price, currencies, obligations, concentration, shocks, and adaptation.

New goods have no universal value or exchange rate. Trade requires compatible use, information, transport, trust, enforcement, payment, and repeatable supply. Extraction may create abundance for one group while concentrating control, depletion, waste, dependency, or conflict elsewhere.

### Ecology and Species

[Ecology and Migration](ECOLOGY_AND_MIGRATION.md) owns establishment, range change, competition, succession, Novel Ecologies, and ecological consequences. [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) owns monster forms, Evolution Routes, adaptations, mutations, and species transitions.

Crossing does not make an organism invasive, dominant, viable, or eligible for Evolution. Habitat, reproduction, resource webs, Counter-Ecology, disease, local adaptation, and actor intervention determine what follows.

### Disease

[Disease Evolution](DISEASE_EVOLUTION.md) owns exposure, transmission, establishment, Disease States, outbreaks, agent change, care, and public-health response.

Gate contact creates no automatic plague, immunity, or one-directional vulnerability. A disease consequence needs a valid organism or process, channel, exposure route, compatible host or environment, timing, and causal response.

### Factions, War, and Unrest

[Faction Behaviour](FACTION_BEHAVIOUR.md) owns collective decisions. [War and Unrest](WAR_AND_UNREST.md) owns contestation, Civil Unrest, Armed Conflict, War, operations, territorial control, cessation, and Conflict Legacies.

First contact is not automatically invasion. Armed entry is not automatically War. A military victory does not grant durable control, legitimacy, supply, or public compliance. Diplomacy, espionage, religious interpretation, colonization, resistance, intervention, and withdrawal emerge through actual actors and conditions.

### Technology and Magical Advancement

[Technology and Magical Advancement](TECHNOLOGY_AND_MAGICAL_ADVANCEMENT.md) owns Discovery, Invention, Validation, Adoption, Diffusion, implementation, maintenance, decline, and recovery. [Magic](../magic/README.md) owns Mana, Affinities, Spells, Rituals, Enchantments, Alchemy, Divine Magic, and restrictions.

Observation, trade, theft, salvage, instruction, or capture does not install capability. Imported systems still need translation, compatible world conditions, materials, infrastructure, Skills, Development, permissions, operators, maintenance, and repair. Neither technology nor Magic has predetermined superiority.

### Dungeons

[Dungeon Activity](DUNGEON_ACTIVITY.md) owns Dungeon classification, boundaries, Access Routes, Sustaining Bases, Topology, activity, inhabitants, resources, and legacies.

A World Gate may become a Dungeon input, overlap a Dungeon, or change one of its routes only through actual boundaries and Bases. A Dungeon entrance or teleportation effect remains distinct unless it separately qualifies as a World Gate.

### World Stability, Ages, and Resets

[World Stability](WORLD_STABILITY.md) owns scoped Stability Referents, supports, strains, couplings, buffers, response, interventions, and forecasts. [Ages and World Resets](AGES_AND_WORLD_RESETS.md) owns Age Claims, transitions, Reset mechanisms, footprints, survivorship, and World Revalidation.

A Gate may support, strain, or transform particular world arrangements through established routes. A sufficiently consequential contact event may contribute to an Age Transition or a Reset Precondition, Trigger, or Mechanism. It is none of those merely by opening or closing.

### Individual Systems

Contact may alter access, teachers, opponents, materials, environments, recognition, institutions, pressures, and opportunities. It cannot directly grant:

- [Development](../progression/README.md), Stat XP, mastery, or reliability;
- a [Skill](../skills/README.md), Skill Evolution, or crossover;
- a Class, Profession, office, certification, or social legitimacy;
- a monster form, Species Trait, Mutation, or Evolution;
- a magical affinity, Spell, source relationship, or authority;
- a Weapon Soul, Awakening, Soul Intertwining, Weapon Evolution, or Weapon Manifestation;
- Soul Depth, Soul Resonance, Soul Echoes, Retained Instincts, a Soul Title, or a Soul Avatar.

Each result requires current-life action and the Owning System's ordinary conditions.

## Soul-System Boundary

World Gates and Soul Gates are different mechanics despite similar names.

A World Gate:

- exists in world-side causality;
- connects Contact Domains through stated channels;
- may carry bodies, organisms, material, information, or environmental influence when supported;
- creates world consequences through Phase 8 systems.

A Soul Gate:

- connects to a Soul Space through Resonance, consent, scope, and Soul rules;
- carries awareness or permitted soul-level contact rather than a physical body or ordinary possessions;
- is governed by Soul Threshold and Soul Intrusion rules;
- does not become a World Gate because its participants come from different places or Ages.

One phenomenon does not inherit the other's channels, protections, permissions, or consequences. A World Gate does not expose Soul Space. A Soul Gate does not establish a trade route.

The next Phase 8 task owns detailed interaction with Reincarnation and Soul Avatars. Until that task is complete, a World Gate creates no special Reincarnation destination, Interlife route, alternate incarnation, divergent Echo, Soul Avatar portal, universal interpreter, or soul-level immunity.

## Gate Legacies

A **Gate Legacy** is a persistent consequence remaining after Gate operation, contact intensity, control, or access changes.

Possible Gate Legacies include:

- mixed or displaced populations;
- new languages, institutions, laws, religions, alliances, prejudices, or identities;
- transferred species, diseases, crops, materials, waste, and ecological relationships;
- technical and magical research traditions;
- infrastructure and settlements built around former endpoints;
- debts, treaties, claims, memorials, grievances, and contested histories;
- stranded expeditions, armies, refugees, families, or Weapon Souls;
- damaged environments and long recovery processes;
- secrecy regimes, myths, false histories, and incomplete records;
- dependencies whose route no longer exists.

Closure does not erase these conditions. Reopening does not restore the former relationship unchanged. Every surviving effect remains with its specialist owner and current evidence.

## World-Contact Procedure

When a possible World Gate or World-Contact Event matters, the GM should:

1. **Define the contact claim.** Identify the Contact Domains, separation, scope, and horizon.
2. **Classify the route.** Distinguish ordinary travel, magical transit, Dungeon Access, Soul Gate contact, World Gate contact, Age transition, and Reset claims by function.
3. **Establish prior causality.** Identify the Gate Basis or other process, precursors, actors, and evidence without retroactive invention.
4. **Map the interface.** Define endpoints, boundary, direct footprint, identity, timing, and any multi-endpoint limits.
5. **Resolve channels separately.** State directionality, throughput, requirements, costs, failure, and uncertainty for each relevant channel.
6. **Test compatibility.** Assess only the physical, environmental, biological, magical, technical, sensory, linguistic, social, or legal layers material to the current question.
7. **Separate information views.** Record world truth, observations, estimates, beliefs, propaganda, hidden facts, and confidence independently.
8. **Identify access and agency.** Distinguish discoverers, users, operators, controllers, claimants, authorities, affected populations, and consenting sources.
9. **Place actors in context.** Give factions and persons their own goals, knowledge, constraints, decision routes, and response delays.
10. **Hand off consequences.** Resolve population, resource, economic, ecological, disease, conflict, advancement, Dungeon, Stability, Age, and other claims through their owners.
11. **Advance causally.** Track branches, Counterforces, feedback, delays, autonomous off-screen action, Gate operation, possible closure, and Pending Consequences.
12. **Preserve continuity.** Record Gate Legacies, stranded dependencies, changed relationships, unresolved uncertainty, and current conditions in the external Campaign Record.

Use only the detail the decision requires. Do not complete a civilization inventory merely because one person crosses a boundary.

## World Gate Profile

A **World Gate Profile** is an external Campaign Record for one World Gate and its associated contact process. It may selectively record:

- Gate identity and evidence;
- Contact Domains and material separation;
- Basis, provenance, opening cause, and sustaining dependencies;
- endpoints, boundary, footprint, channels, directionality, throughput, and transit conditions;
- current operation, expected changes, closure conditions, and unknowns;
- compatibility findings by purpose and direction;
- discovery, knowledge views, communication, misinformation, and confidence;
- access, operation, control, claims, authority, consent, and disputes;
- relevant actors, factions, objectives, decisions, and response delays;
- current Contact Phases by actor or scope;
- direct effects, handoffs, branches, Counterforces, and Pending Consequences;
- closure, stranding, and Gate Legacies.

The Profile is selective, qualitative, dated, and evidence-aware. It is not a universal civilization sheet, encounter table, Gate level, threat score, scripted timeline, or repository template. The reusable Gate-event template remains a later roadmap task.

## Worked Examples

### Industrial Republic and Magical Empire

A technical array opens a stable material channel between an industrial republic and a magical empire. The republic has standardized logistics, long-range artillery, and rapid manufacturing. The empire has local Mana infrastructure, durable wards, trained aerial couriers, and divine treaties that do not function automatically on the republic's side.

Neither side receives a civilization rank. Republic forces dominate one open battle near their endpoint but cannot maintain vehicles beyond imported fuel and parts. Imperial wards stop familiar projectiles around prepared sites but cannot protect every road. Merchants, border communities, priesthoods, military factions, labor organizations, and dissidents make different decisions. Contact produces trade, espionage, labor disputes, translation institutions, and contested intervention rather than one predetermined conquest.

### Incompatible Magical Laws

Two magical civilizations communicate through a Gate whose signal channel is reciprocal but whose Mana channel is sharply asymmetric. Spells from one side preserve recognizable Patterns after crossing, yet the destination lacks the same source relationships and material correspondences.

Study can produce new compatible methods, but no caster gains the other civilization's Spells or affinities by observation. Failed demonstrations create misinformation until researchers separate Pattern, Mana Relation, operator Development, and local world conditions.

### The Returning Continent

A continent displaced behind a long-lived cosmological barrier returns to ordinary geography after several Ages. The barrier's ending creates a World-Contact Event. Once normal sea travel becomes possible, ships use ordinary routes rather than a continuing World Gate.

The returned populations are not relics frozen in the past. They have their own recent history, factions, diseases, ecologies, and interpretations of the separation. The event may contribute to a new Age Claim, but only if lasting consequences make that claim useful.

### Elemental Realm Leakage

A breached World Gate carries heat, pressure, unfamiliar Mana behavior, and small elemental organisms before it can carry embodied people. Its environmental and organism channels are one-way; its signal channel is unclear.

Local actors first treat the breach as a fire disaster. Later evidence reveals a contact process. Ecological establishment, disease-like effects, magical research, evacuation, and sealing attempts go to their specialist owners. Calling the other side an invasion remains an actor belief until agency and intent are established.

### Monsters Enter a World Without Them

A seasonal Gate permits several monster populations to migrate into a domain where those species did not previously exist. Some die because food webs and Mana conditions are incompatible. Some survive only around Gate residue. One forms a mutualistic relationship with local agriculture; another displaces a native predator.

The Gate supplies a route, not Evolution or inevitable ecological dominance. Monster Ecology, reproduction, local societies, hunting policy, trade, and Gate closure determine the long result.

### Superior Soul Knowledge, Weak Battlefield Force

A small society lacks heavy weapons and cannot resist a neighboring contact force in open battle. It possesses sophisticated Soul protections, accurate Reincarnation scholarship, and institutions skilled at identifying coercive soul practices.

Those strengths do not become a combat multiplier. They do expose infiltration methods, protect some negotiators, attract defectors, and give the society diplomatic value to several factions. The invading coalition wins territory but fractures over policy, legitimacy, and dependence on local expertise.

### The Unsustainable Invasion

A force crosses a wide one-way Gate Channel and defeats the endpoint defenders. Its apparent military superiority hides weak intelligence, no local repair base, incompatible medicine, contested supply, divided political authority at home, and no accepted plan for governance.

Resistance, negotiation, disease control, environmental adaptation, and domestic opposition change the campaign. The invasion may remain brutal and destructive without being strategically sustainable. Neither initial victory nor later withdrawal is predetermined.

### Closure After Decades

A reciprocal Gate supports trade, migration, marriages, religious exchange, research, and military treaties for forty years. Its cyclical Basis then ends despite attempts to preserve it.

Closure strands populations on both sides, invalidates supply assumptions, separates institutions, and leaves technology that cannot all be maintained locally. Some communities assimilate, some preserve hybrid identities, some seek another route, and some blame former Gate controllers. The contact era ends; its Gate Legacies do not.

## Safeguards

- Never use **Gate** without clarifying **World Gate** or **Soul Gate** when both meanings are plausible.
- Never classify an ordinary door, road, portal, teleportation effect, Dungeon route, Soul Gate, Age Boundary, or Reset as a World Gate by appearance alone.
- Never open, redirect, expand, or close a Gate without an established Basis and causal route.
- Never assume every channel exists, works both ways, has equal throughput, or shares the same boundary.
- Never make public discovery the retroactive cause of a previously operating Gate.
- Never grant discovery, access, technical operation, control, ownership, authority, legitimacy, and consent as one package.
- Never treat either Contact Domain as one faction, culture, species, policy, or moral alignment.
- Never assume first contact means invasion, trade, peace, plague, progress, colonization, or integration.
- Never create a universal technology level, magic level, compatibility percentage, civilization rank, Gate level, or power score.
- Never give technology or Magic predetermined superiority over the other.
- Never let contact bypass translation, infrastructure, maintenance, embodiment, Skill, Development, source agency, law, ecology, or logistics.
- Never grant a Skill, Development, Stat XP, Evolution, magical affinity, Soul growth, Soul Weapon change, Class, authority, or other progression merely for crossing, discovering, surviving, controlling, closing, or profiting from a Gate.
- Never let World Gate transit expose Soul Space, create a Soul Gate, duplicate a soul, manufacture an Echo, or add an incarnation.
- Never turn a Gate into an Age Transition, World Reset, Dungeon, or War without the receiving system's qualifying route.
- Never erase transferred populations, damage, obligations, ecology, beliefs, institutions, or other Gate Legacies when contact or the Gate ends.
- Never make the player the automatic discoverer, cause, chosen mediator, universal translator, sole solution, or only consequential actor.
- Never store a current Gate, Contact Domain, endpoint, contacted civilization, live contact process, or World Gate Profile in this repository.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- a canonical Gate, contacted world, civilization, geography, cosmology, event, or campaign timeline;
- a universal portal spell, Gate-building technology, cosmological map, or transit formula;
- Gate interaction with Reincarnation, Interlife, Soul Echoes, or Soul Avatars, which is owned by [World Gate Interactions with Reincarnation and Soul Avatars](WORLD_GATE_SOUL_INTERACTIONS.md);
- a Gate-event generator or reusable Gate-event template;
- complete culture, religion, climate, disaster, diplomacy, colonization, or law systems beyond existing owner interfaces;
- long-horizon change-of-resolution rules owned by [Simulation Abstraction](SIMULATION_ABSTRACTION.md);
- a mandatory Gate lifecycle, invasion arc, Age transition, or World Reset;
- campaign state, named actors, current endpoints, live world truth, or playthrough history.
