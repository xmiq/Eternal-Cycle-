# Populations

## Purpose

This document defines how Eternal Cycle represents populations and demographic change. It provides population claims to [Causal Event Chains](CAUSAL_EVENT_CHAINS.md) without reducing persons to interchangeable units or duplicating species, Soul, Development, Skill, Class, Evolution, Magic, or Soul Weapon rules.

Actual populations, members, counts, births, deaths, settlements, and histories are external campaign state.

## Core Rule

A **Population** is a scoped set of beings or living bodies grouped for one demographic question under an explicit membership rule.

A population claim must state:

- who or what qualifies as a member;
- the place, network, institution, habitat, or relation defining scope;
- the relevant time or interval;
- whether membership counts persons, bodies, households, colonies, bonded groups, or another justified unit;
- composition differences that can change the conclusion;
- entries, exits, delays, and uncertainty;
- why the grouping matters to the current decision.

No population is one actor, one Soul, one capability, one culture, or one opinion merely because it can be counted together.

## Population Boundaries

A useful population boundary follows the question rather than a universal census category.

Possible boundaries include:

- members of one species in a habitat;
- residents with effective access to a settlement;
- a migratory population using a route;
- bodies capable of one reproductive process;
- apprentices currently served by an institution;
- adherents participating in one community;
- a monster colony sharing a nest and resource network;
- displaced people originating from one region;
- active Weapon Souls within a legal jurisdiction, when their personhood and consent make the grouping relevant.

Overlapping populations are valid. A healer may belong to a city population, a profession cohort, a species population, a religious community, and a displaced household at the same time. Count each person once within any one stated boundary; do not merge overlapping totals without resolving duplication.

Administrative categories are evidence, not automatic world truth. A census may omit hidden species, unregistered residents, mobile communities, unrecognized persons, dormant beings, hostile territories, or bodies interpreted under the wrong life cycle.

## Demographic Units

### Individual Persons

Use persons when identity, agency, rights, relationships, or Soul continuity matters. One person can change category without becoming a new person.

### Embodied Individuals

Use current bodies when anatomy, maturation, habitat, health, feeding, reproduction, or mortality is the relevant question. Reincarnation can end one embodiment and later begin another without transferring demographic membership automatically.

### Households and Cooperative Units

Households, packs, hives, colonies, broods, bonded groups, and other cooperative units may be tracked when they share care, production, territory, or survival. Their internal agency and distribution remain material.

### Sites and Colonies

Sessile, distributed, fungal, plant-like, swarm, or networked beings may require sites, colonies, identity-bearing nodes, or another setting-specific unit. The chosen unit must respect the owning species and Soul rules.

No unit is universal. A campaign records conversion assumptions when one question compares different units.

## Population Composition

**Population Composition** describes consequential differences among members. Relevant distinctions may include:

- species and viable form;
- life stage, maturation, and expected remaining lifespan;
- reproductive role or route;
- health, impairment, dormancy, injury, or dependency;
- embodied capability and access, without pooling Development;
- occupation, Class, Profession, education, or institutional standing;
- location, mobility, legal status, wealth access, or exclusion;
- cultural, linguistic, religious, or factional affiliation;
- magical compatibility or exposure;
- Reincarnation status only when it is actually known or causally relevant.

Track a distinction only when it can alter a current process, distribution, response, or consequence. Do not invent biologically fixed social traits or assume one species has one culture.

## Demographic Cohorts

A **Demographic Cohort** is a population subset sharing one time-relevant condition, such as birth interval, maturation stage, arrival period, exposure, profession intake, or displacement event.

Cohorts help preserve delay. A large cohort of juveniles may create care needs now, training demand later, and labor or territorial pressure only after maturation. A long-lived cohort may retain knowledge while blocking institutional succession. Different species can require different cohort definitions.

Cohorts are descriptive. They do not grant shared Skills, synchronized choices, or identical futures.

## Demographic Flows

A **Demographic Flow** changes population membership across time.

### Entry

Entry may occur through:

- birth, hatching, budding, division, germination, construction, manifestation, or another valid life process;
- immigration or return;
- maturation into the tracked category;
- conversion of legal or institutional status;
- valid embodiment after Reincarnation;
- discovery or improved observation of members who already existed.

Discovery changes knowledge, not necessarily world truth or actual population size.

### Exit

Exit may occur through:

- death or destruction of the counted body;
- emigration or dispersal;
- maturation out of a category;
- dormancy or inaccessibility when the stated boundary requires activity or access;
- transformation into another valid form or population category;
- loss of status, membership, or effective connection;
- revised evidence correcting a prior estimate.

An embodied death is not automatically Final Death. The [Soul Engine](../soul/README.md) owns persistence and Reincarnation.

### Transfer

Movement between categories may be an exit from one population and entry into another without changing total persons. Record transfers once and preserve their origin, destination, delay, and uncertainty.

Later ecology and migration rules own movement routes and settlement consequences. Population rules own the demographic accounting around those movements.

## Reproduction and Recruitment

Population renewal requires an actual route. Depending on the beings involved, that route may require:

- compatible bodies, partners, colonies, spawning sites, hosts, seeds, vessels, or makers;
- consent where persons participate;
- maturation and recovery time;
- suitable environment, food, Mana, materials, care, safety, or ritual conditions;
- viable offspring, constructed beings, or new bodies;
- social recognition, adoption, teaching, or integration when membership is institutional rather than biological.

Fertility, fecundity, eggs, seeds, births, or created bodies do not equal surviving members. Distinguish conception or initiation, successful development, birth or formation, survival, maturation, and integration.

No species receives exponential growth by premise alone. Repeated reproduction encounters finite time, embodied cost, ecology, resources, predation, disease, care, competition, dispersal, and changing behavior.

## Mortality and Survival

Mortality is scoped by cause, exposure, vulnerability, protection, care, and time. It may differ sharply by cohort and distribution.

Distinguish:

- immediate deaths;
- elevated risk without settled deaths;
- delayed mortality from injury, deprivation, disease, contamination, or lost care;
- bodies missing or unreachable;
- dormant or transformed beings mistakenly counted as dead;
- embodied death followed by Interlife;
- Final Death, which only Soul rules can establish.

Survival does not imply health, access, capability, reproductive viability, social continuity, or recovery.

## Maturation and Life Cycles

Life stage changes what a population needs and can express. A valid population model may track:

- gestation, incubation, formation, or emergence;
- dependent stages;
- physical and cognitive maturation;
- metamorphosis or species-stage transition;
- reproductive windows or cycles;
- senescence, dormancy, renewal, or indefinite maintenance;
- expected transition time and failure routes.

Monster [Species Stages](../monster-evolution/SPECIES_STAGES.md) and Evolution remain under Monster Evolution. Human training, Classes, and Professions remain under their owners. Time passing can enable maturation; it does not automatically grant Development or mastery.

## Population Continuity

**Population Continuity** is the capacity of a scoped population to remain meaningfully present through replacement, care, knowledge transfer, reproduction, recruitment, movement, adaptation, or institutional support.

Continuity may fail even when members survive. A population can lose:

- viable future generations;
- habitat or access;
- caregivers or teachers;
- language, records, or practices;
- reproductive partners or required symbionts;
- trusted leadership or coordination;
- legal recognition or safe movement;
- sources, sites, tools, or infrastructure.

Continuity is not purity. Intermarriage, hybridization, adoption, cultural exchange, species change, and new institutions may transform continuity rather than end it. The relevant community and owning systems determine what remains continuous.

## Demographic Momentum

**Demographic Momentum** is continued population change caused by existing composition and delayed life-cycle structure even after the original pressure changes.

A young population may continue growing after fertility falls because many members have not yet reached reproductive stages. An aging population may continue declining after safety improves because few members can reproduce or train replacements. A brood laid before a winter may emerge after the adult population is gone.

Momentum is neither destiny nor a hidden multiplier. New migration, mortality, care, adaptation, policy, behavior, or environment may redirect it.

## Dependency and Support

Populations contain uneven needs and support relationships. Track who depends on whom for care, food, teaching, defense, reproduction, transport, magical maintenance, interpretation, or social recognition.

A **Support Burden** is the current demand a dependency places on particular providers and systems. It is not a judgment about a person's worth. A dependent juvenile may later become a provider; an elder may require bodily care while preserving irreplaceable knowledge; a powerful monster may depend on a narrow habitat or symbiont.

Do not reduce support to one universal ratio. Different dependencies draw on different capacities and may be shared, contested, reciprocal, or inaccessible.

## Capability Distribution

A population may record the availability and distribution of people with relevant capabilities, but it does not own those capabilities.

Distinguish:

- number or presence of currently capable practitioners;
- reliability and embodied access under completed Development rules;
- geographic and social distribution;
- availability, willingness, authority, and coordination;
- teaching and replacement capacity;
- tools, resources, institutions, and support;
- concentration risk if a few persons carry most of the capability.

Ten novices are not one master. A thousand soldiers do not grant their ruler a pooled combat Skill. A school building does not prove teachers, students, access, or knowledge continuity.

## Hidden and Uncertain Populations

Population truth may be uncertain because members are concealed, mobile, distributed, dormant, inaccessible, transformed, unrecognized, difficult to distinguish, or dangerous to count.

Maintain separate:

- best world-truth range or qualitative finding;
- observation method and coverage;
- official census or institutional claim;
- local belief and rumor;
- deliberate concealment or propaganda;
- consequences of acting on each view.

False counting has real causal effects. Taxes, relief, persecution, representation, defense, and resource allocation may all follow a mistaken estimate.

## Population Change Procedure

When a population may change:

1. **State the question and boundary.** Identify membership, unit, scope, interval, and purpose.
2. **Load composition.** Preserve cohorts and differences that can change the result.
3. **Identify entries and exits.** Separate actual flows from improved observation or reclassification.
4. **Apply timing.** Respect gestation, formation, maturation, travel, recovery, senescence, and institutional delays.
5. **Check embodied requirements.** Establish resources, habitat, care, compatibility, safety, consent, and access where relevant.
6. **Resolve direct causes under their owners.** Do not infer Soul, Skill, Evolution, Class, Weapon, or Magic outcomes.
7. **Apply distribution.** Identify which cohorts and locations receive the cause or Counterforce.
8. **Update composition and continuity.** Record losses, gains, transfers, dependency, and replacement risks.
9. **Preserve uncertainty.** Keep world truth, estimates, records, and beliefs distinct.
10. **Propagate only material consequences.** Hand changed demand, labor availability, habitat use, succession, care, or exposure to the next domain through Causal Event Chains.
11. **Record externally.** Current values, named populations, and events belong in the Campaign Record.

## Cross-System Interfaces

### Souls and Reincarnation

Population rules count current persons or embodiments only under the chosen boundary. Souls are not a reproductive Stock, public resource, or allocable quota. [Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) owns candidate embodiment and Soul continuity.

Past-life mastery, Soul Depth, Resonance, Echoes, Titles, Avatars, and retained progression do not spread across a population through proximity or ancestry unless their owning rules establish a specific route.

### Development and Skills

Population composition can describe access to capable people, teachers, institutions, and practice opportunities. [Development](../progression/README.md) and [Skills](../skills/README.md) own individual capability. Training capacity can change future opportunity without guaranteeing mastery.

### Monster Evolution

Population density, composition, mortality, reproduction, and continuity can create ecological and Evolutionary Pressures. [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) owns species stages, Adaptation, Mutation, Evolution Routes, Hybridization, and extinction adjudication.

### Human Classes and Professions

Classes, Professions, offices, schools, guilds, and traditions can organize demographic roles and replacement. [Human systems](../human/README.md) own recognition and progression. Demographic need does not automatically create qualified practitioners.

### Soul Weapons

Weapon Souls are persons, not equipment units. They may be grouped only for a justified demographic, legal, social, or contact question that preserves individual identity, agency, bond, and consent. [Soul Weapons](../soul-weapons/README.md) own Awakening and continuity.

### Magic

Magic may change fertility, survival, longevity, embodiment conditions, communication, transport, habitat, or care only through a valid [Magic-World Claim](../magic/WORLD_ENGINE_INTERACTIONS.md). Population scale does not create Mana or universal magical access.

## Worked Examples

### Long-Lived Urban Population

A long-lived people experience low birth frequency and low ordinary mortality. Their total number appears stable, but senior officeholders retain positions for centuries while few apprentices gain authority.

Population rules record cohort structure, institutional concentration, replacement delay, and continuity risk. They do not declare social unrest, grant mastery to elders, or assume every member shares one view.

### Brood-Producing Monsters

A monster lays hundreds of eggs after a rare seasonal feeding event. Eggs are not counted as mature hunters. Incubation sites, predation, food, parental behavior, disease, and metamorphosis determine later entries into other cohorts.

Large reproductive output creates possible momentum, not automatic exponential population growth or a guaranteed Evolution.

### Reincarnated Specialist

A renowned healer dies and later reincarnates elsewhere. The former city loses one current practitioner immediately. The continuing soul retains only what Soul and Development rules preserve and may require years to regain embodied expression.

The original population cannot count the future incarnation as local medical capacity, and the new population does not gain a master healer at birth.

### Hidden Settlement

An official census records no monsters in a forest, while several concealed communities live below the canopy. Tax, logging, and military plans rely on the false count.

The hidden population existed before discovery. Discovery changes information and may trigger factional, ecological, or military chains; it does not create the residents retroactively.

### Surviving Without Continuity

After a disaster, many adults survive but the only spawning ground is destroyed and the population's symbiotic caretaker species disappears.

Current abundance may remain substantial while long-term continuity is at risk. Ecology, resources, adaptation, migration, or Hybridization may later supply routes, but population rules do not invent one.

### Distributed Capability

Two towns each list twenty trained defenders. In one, the defenders are healthy, locally trusted, equipped, and coordinated. In the other, most are injured, dispersed, politically divided, or denied access to weapons.

Equal headcounts do not establish equal defensive capacity. The relevant Development, institutional, resource, and faction claims remain separate.

## Safeguards

- Never treat a population as one mind, actor, Soul, culture, alignment, or capability.
- Never infer world truth solely from an official census or visible settlement.
- Never equate births, eggs, seeds, or created bodies with mature surviving members.
- Never grant exponential growth without finite embodied routes and changing constraints.
- Never make time alone grant maturation, mastery, Class, Skill, or Evolution beyond owning rules.
- Never treat current abundance as proof of long-term continuity.
- Never treat survival as health, access, agency, recovery, or reproductive viability.
- Never pool individual Development, Skills, magic, Soul traits, or Weapon Functions.
- Never treat Souls as a resource assigned to fill demographic demand.
- Never make ancestry transfer retained progression or identity by default.
- Never frame dependency as lesser personhood or use demographic categories to erase consent.
- Never convert killing, birth, death, crowd size, or rarity directly into player progression.
- Keep every named population and current value outside this repository.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- canonical population values, species lists, settlements, censuses, or histories;
- resource sufficiency, food production, prices, migration routes, disease transmission, war casualties, or faction policy;
- Monster Evolution, extinction, Hybridization, or species-stage mechanics;
- Soul assignment, Reincarnation candidates, or universal reproductive biology;
- universal growth formulas, mortality rates, age bands, carrying capacities, or population thresholds;
- social worth, biological destiny, cultural uniformity, or mandatory family structures.
