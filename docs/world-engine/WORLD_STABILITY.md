# World Stability

## Purpose

This document defines how Eternal Cycle assesses whether a scoped arrangement of world conditions and relationships can continue, adapt, reorganize, or fail under pressure.

World Stability is a World Engine diagnosis. It is not a campaign meter, moral judgment, prophecy, Reset countdown, or source of personal progression. Current findings belong in an external Campaign Record.

## Core Rule

**World Stability** is the evidenced capacity of a stated world arrangement to preserve specified functions, relationships, or conditions, or to reorganize them without uncontrolled loss, under current and foreseeable strain.

Every valid stability claim must identify:

1. the scope being assessed;
2. the continuity being tested;
3. the conditions and relationships that support it;
4. the strains acting on it;
5. the dependencies through which support or failure may travel;
6. available buffers, alternatives, and response capacity;
7. relevant [State Thresholds](WORLD_STATE_VARIABLES.md#threshold-condition);
8. affected parties and distribution;
9. evidence, uncertainty, and time horizon.

Without those elements, "stable" and "unstable" are impressions rather than canonical World-State Claims.

## What World Stability Is Not

World Stability is not:

- a universal percentage, health bar, resource pool, or countdown;
- the sum or average of all World-State Variables;
- peace, prosperity, justice, legitimacy, safety, uniformity, or technological advancement;
- resistance to every kind of change;
- a requirement that the world return to a former state;
- an invisible force that rewards order or punishes disruption;
- proof that collapse, an Age transition, or a World Reset must occur;
- a permission to erase established causes or consequences;
- a substitute for the specialist rules that produce its inputs.

A world can contain several incompatible stability findings at once. A government may be administratively stable while its food network is brittle, its border ecology is reorganizing, and its magical infrastructure is failing.

## Stability Scope

A **Stability Scope** is the geographic, ecological, social, institutional, technical, magical, metaphysical, or functional boundary within which a stability claim applies.

Useful scopes may include:

- one irrigation network;
- a migratory corridor shared by several species;
- a city's food and sanitation functions;
- a realm's succession system;
- a continent-spanning magical transport network;
- the compatibility of a world's current physical and magical laws;
- an interdependent group of regions or worlds.

The word *world* in World Stability names the owning engine, not a mandatory planetary scale. A local crisis is not automatically a world crisis, and a world-scale finding cannot be inferred by averaging local labels.

When several scopes interact, record their handoffs. Stability in one region may depend on burdens displaced into another.

## Stability Referent

A **Stability Referent** is the exact function, relationship, condition, or identity whose continuity is being assessed.

Examples include:

- reliable access to potable water;
- continued seasonal migration through a corridor;
- an institution's ability to perform succession without organized violence;
- containment of a hazardous magical Field;
- compatibility among the laws that permit embodied life;
- continuity of a community despite relocation and political transformation.

The referent prevents category drift. "The empire is stable" is too broad. "The imperial grain-allocation network can continue supplying three inland regions through the next winter under current route losses" is a usable claim.

Identity and function are not always the same referent. A community may preserve identity while losing a settlement. An institution may preserve its name while no longer performing its former function. State each claim separately.

## Components of a Stability Assessment

### Supporting Conditions

A **Stability Support** is a current condition, relationship, actor, process, reserve, redundancy, or alternative that helps preserve a Stability Referent.

Support may include:

- renewable supply and maintained infrastructure;
- trusted communication and legitimate coordination;
- habitat connectivity and ecological redundancy;
- distributed knowledge and trained maintainers;
- reserves, substitutes, mutual aid, and evacuation routes;
- compatible magical sources and continuing source consent;
- institutions capable of learning from failure;
- communities willing and able to reorganize.

Support must have a causal route. Prestige, prophecy, high Development, abundant Mana, a powerful Soul, or declared importance is not support unless it can affect the referent through an established interface.

### World-System Strain

**World-System Strain** is an established pressure that tests or erodes one or more Stability Supports or increases the burden required to preserve a Stability Referent.

Strain may arise from:

- population and resource mismatch;
- habitat loss, migration blockage, or ecological disturbance;
- unequal allocation, debt, maintenance failure, or supply disruption;
- factional conflict, war, occupation, or institutional fracture;
- disease, disability, caregiving burden, or public-health failure;
- correlated technical failure or dependency concentration;
- Dungeon expansion, collapse, extraction, or hazardous exchange;
- changing Mana laws, source refusal, contamination, or magical Drift;
- climate, disaster, Gate contact, or incompatible world conditions;
- accumulated consequences from several individually survivable changes.

Strain is not a universal penalty and does not accumulate as points. Each strain must identify what it affects, how, where, for whom, and over what time.

### Systemic Coupling

**Systemic Coupling** is a dependency through which a material change in one world function can support, constrain, or destabilize another.

Coupling can be beneficial. A shared transport network can connect food reserves to several regions. It can also transmit failure. If every region depends on one bridge, one source, one lineage of maintainers, or one communication standard, disruption may propagate widely.

Coupling alone proves neither strength nor fragility. Assess:

- direction and strength of dependence;
- substitutes and bypasses;
- delay before consequences arrive;
- whether failures are correlated;
- who controls the connection;
- who receives benefits and burdens;
- whether participants understand the dependency.

### Stability Buffer

A **Stability Buffer** is a bounded reserve, redundancy, slack, substitute, protective relationship, or delayed-use capacity that can absorb a specified strain for a limited scope and duration.

Examples include seed reserves, redundant bridges, decentralized healers, several compatible Mana sources, rotating leadership, habitat refuges, and tested evacuation routes.

A buffer is not free resilience. It may expire, degrade, be inaccessible, protect only some populations, require maintenance, or create another dependency. Consuming it changes future state even when immediate failure is avoided.

### World Response Capacity

**World Response Capacity** is the current collective ability of relevant actors and systems to observe, coordinate, adapt, repair, substitute, retreat, transform, or recover in response to a specified strain.

It is function-specific and distributed. The ability to detect a magical fault does not imply authority to close the network, materials to repair it, trust to coordinate evacuation, or Skill to build an alternative.

Response Capacity may belong to institutions, communities, monsters, ecosystems, spirits, gods, Weapon Souls, technical networks, or combinations of actors. It never pools their individual Development into one civilization score.

## Stability Findings

Stability findings are qualitative descriptions, not ranks or a mandatory progression ladder.

### Robust Continuity

The referent is supported by several effective routes, tolerates relevant variation, and has credible response options. Robustness applies only to the tested strains and horizon.

### Dependent Continuity

The referent currently functions but relies on one or more identifiable conditions whose loss would materially change it. Dependence can be acceptable and openly managed.

### Brittle Stability

**Brittle Stability** exists when a referent appears steady under ordinary conditions but has concentrated dependencies, weak alternatives, hidden maintenance debt, or poor response capacity against a plausible strain.

Brittle does not mean failure is certain. It means apparent continuity should not be mistaken for robust continuity.

### Adaptive Reorganization

The prior configuration is changing, but affected systems are preserving or replacing important functions through new relationships. Reorganization may save some referents while ending others.

### Active Instability

**World Instability** exists when material strains are changing a scoped arrangement faster or more deeply than its current supports and responses can preserve the stated referents.

Instability may be intermittent, uneven, reversible, or transformative. It is not automatically terminal.

### Cascading Failure

**Cascading Failure** occurs when an initial loss travels through established Systemic Couplings and causes further supported losses across functions or scopes.

A cascade requires actual links and insufficient Counterforces. The GM cannot declare one merely because a first failure is dramatic.

### Transformed Continuity

A prior referent no longer continues in its original form, but a successor arrangement preserves identified functions, relationships, or identities. This is neither a hidden restoration nor proof that all loss was acceptable.

Several findings may describe one scope at once because different referents have different supports and strains.

## Stability Is Not Stasis

Change can preserve stability. Migration, institutional reform, technical replacement, ecological succession, decentralization, surrender, and cultural adaptation may prevent more destructive failure.

Resistance to change can create Brittle Stability. A system that suppresses every local adjustment may preserve its appearance while exhausting buffers and concentrating dependency.

No preferred historical configuration is automatically the natural or balanced state. Recovery may return a former arrangement, produce a [Novel Ecology](ECOLOGY_AND_MIGRATION.md#novel-ecologies), preserve only selected functions, or create a viable successor.

## Stability Is Not a Value Judgment

Stability describes continuity under pressure. It does not decide whether the continuing arrangement is good.

A coercive regime may be stable because it controls food, information, and force. A liberation movement may destabilize that regime while improving agency and long-term response capacity. A dam may stabilize irrigation while destroying a migratory ecology. A quarantine may slow transmission while imposing unequal burdens.

Every assessment must therefore separate:

- what is being preserved;
- who benefits;
- who bears cost or risk;
- whose agency is constrained;
- which alternatives are excluded;
- whether a different referent would produce a different finding.

The GM presents those consequences. World Stability does not choose the correct politics, morality, culture, or future for the player.

## Thresholds and Regime Change

World Stability reuses the [State Threshold](WORLD_STATE_VARIABLES.md#threshold-condition). A relevant threshold identifies a local condition at which one process changes behavior or requires another resolution.

Examples include:

- a reserve no longer covering the time needed to reopen a route;
- a maintenance network losing its last qualified repair team;
- a habitat corridor becoming impassable for one life stage;
- a magical containment pattern losing enough anchors that its failure behavior changes;
- an institution losing the participation required to execute succession.

Thresholds are not universal breakpoints. Crossing one does not automatically cause collapse, a Reset, an Evolution, or a reward. It changes the relevant causal situation and may activate alternatives, emergency action, adaptation, or further failure.

Systems can show path dependence. Restoring the input that was lost may not restore the former arrangement after migration, distrust, extinction, technical decay, or changed source relationships have occurred.

## Feedback, Accumulation, and Delay

Stability changes through [Causal Event Chains](CAUSAL_EVENT_CHAINS.md), not direct arithmetic.

- **Stabilizing feedback** reduces a specified strain, restores support, creates alternatives, or improves response.
- **Destabilizing feedback** increases strain, removes support, narrows alternatives, or spreads failure.
- **Mixed feedback** protects one referent while threatening another.

Delays matter. A harvest reserve can conceal soil depletion. A magical ward can conceal rising maintenance debt. Political compliance can conceal collapsing legitimacy. Ecological recovery can begin before human observers recognize it.

When a pressure repeats, update the conditions it actually changes. Repetition alone does not guarantee escalation, immunity, adaptation, or collapse.

## Stabilization and Displacement

A **Stability Intervention** is a deliberate action intended to preserve, restore, replace, or safely end one Stability Referent under stated conditions.

A valid intervention states:

- target referent and scope;
- acting parties and authority;
- mechanism and specialist owners;
- resources, access, consent, and maintenance;
- expected timing and evidence;
- risks, alternatives, and Counterforces;
- affected parties and burden distribution;
- what happens if support ends.

**Stability Displacement** occurs when an intervention preserves one referent by moving material strain, risk, cost, or loss into another population, place, time, domain, or relationship.

Displacement may be chosen as the least harmful available option, concealed for advantage, or produced unintentionally. It must remain visible in the causal model. A protected capital does not prove a stable realm if its food, waste, or magical burden has merely moved elsewhere.

No intervention is automatically successful because it is heroic, magical, divine, ancient, technically advanced, or performed by the player.

## Domain Integration

World Stability receives established conditions from specialist World Engine domains without replacing them.

### Populations, Resources, and Economies

[Populations](POPULATIONS.md), [Resources and Food](RESOURCES_AND_FOOD.md), and [Economies](ECONOMIES.md) establish composition, capability distribution, needs, access, supply, obligations, allocation, and adaptation.

Population size, wealth, trade volume, reserves, or Price alone cannot set World Stability. The stability question is whether stated functions continue through actual access, dependencies, alternatives, and consequences.

### Ecology and Migration

[Ecology and Migration](ECOLOGY_AND_MIGRATION.md) owns ecosystem functions, disturbance, resilience, succession, movement, displacement, and settlement.

Ecological change is not synonymous with instability. A Novel Ecology may preserve some functions while permanently losing species, relationships, or cultural meanings. Migration may be a response that preserves life while destabilizing routes, settlements, or institutions elsewhere.

### Factions, War, and Unrest

[Faction Behaviour](FACTION_BEHAVIOUR.md) and [War and Unrest](WAR_AND_UNREST.md) own collective decisions, cohesion, mobilization, contestation, violence, cessation, and conflict legacies.

Peace is not proof of stability, and conflict is not proof of universal instability. Coercion may conceal Brittle Stability; bounded conflict may create reform, separation, or replacement that preserves other referents.

### Disease

[Disease Evolution](DISEASE_EVOLUTION.md) owns etiology, hosts, transmission, disease states, agent change, care, and public health.

Disease influences stability through actual illness, death, disability, fear, labor, care, trust, ecological, technical, and institutional routes. Infection does not subtract abstract stability, and case decline does not erase Disease Legacies.

### Technology and Magical Advancement

[Technology and Magical Advancement](TECHNOLOGY_AND_MAGICAL_ADVANCEMENT.md) owns collective technical functions, adoption, implementation, maintenance, decline, and recovery.

Technical sophistication is not stability. A high-output network may be brittle because one source, standard, maintainer group, or route supports every user. A low-output arrangement may be robust within its tested environment.

### Dungeons

[Dungeon Activity](DUNGEON_ACTIVITY.md) owns Dungeon boundaries, Sustaining Bases, activity, inhabitants, extraction, collapse, and legacies.

A Dungeon affects stability only through those actual conditions and exchanges. Clearing, sealing, expanding, or losing one Dungeon cannot directly add or remove World Stability.

## Specialist-System Interfaces

### Souls and Reincarnation

[Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) preserves eligible Soul continuity while the world continues. Final Death, Interlife, and a new incarnation do not restore exhausted supports, reverse a cascade, or erase world consequences.

A Soul is not a stability reserve. Soul Depth, Soul Resonance, Soul Titles, Soul Echoes, Retained Instincts, Soul Avatars, sacrifice, suffering, or repeated death cannot directly add or remove World Stability. A Soul may matter through current embodied action and each system's established receiving rules.

### Development, Skills, Classes, and Evolution

[Development](../progression/README.md), [Skills](../skills/README.md), [Human Classes and Professions](../human/README.md), and [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) establish individual and organized capabilities.

Capability can support observation, coordination, repair, adaptation, resistance, or harm. It does not pool into stability, guarantee successful intervention, or produce progression merely because a crisis is severe.

### Soul Weapons

A [Weapon Soul](../soul-weapons/README.md) is a person who may become a consequential actor through consent, refusal, knowledge, relationships, and bounded Functions. It is not equipment infrastructure, a universal anchor, or a stability battery.

No institution may assume a Soul Weapon's indefinite cooperation, transferable Function, or compatibility across every incarnation merely because a current arrangement depends on it.

### Magic

[Magic and the World Engine](../magic/WORLD_ENGINE_INTERACTIONS.md#world-stability) supplies attributable magical causes. Every magical effect retains its Mana Relation, source, access, consent, cost, failure, distribution, and persistence.

No spell, Ritual, artifact, Divine Source, quantity of Mana, sacrifice, or death directly changes World Stability. Magic can affect a Stability Referent only by altering an established support, strain, coupling, buffer, response, threshold, or consequence.

## Ages and World Resets

World Stability can supply evidence that existing arrangements are robust, brittle, reorganizing, or failing. It does not define an Age, select a transition, trigger a World Reset, choose what survives, or authorize a clean slate.

[Ages and World Resets](AGES_AND_WORLD_RESETS.md) owns:

- how Age boundaries are recognized;
- when a transition becomes materially distinct from ordinary change;
- which causes can produce a World Reset;
- how transformation, survival, loss, and continuity are resolved;
- how protected Soul continuity and world-bound consequences coexist.

There is no canonical "zero stability" condition. A Reset requires its own causal route and cannot be justified by a GM assigning a low hidden score.

## Knowledge, Forecasts, and Uncertainty

A **Stability Forecast** is an evidence-based statement about plausible continuity, reorganization, or failure under named assumptions and a stated time horizon.

A forecast separates:

- established world truth;
- observed evidence;
- actor and institutional interpretations;
- assumptions about future behavior;
- unknown dependencies;
- plausible branches and Counterforces;
- confidence and signs that would change the assessment.

The GM may know current world truth without predetermining the future. Forecasts are not prophecy. Unexpected action can redirect a chain when the action has a valid route.

Characters may disagree honestly about stability because they use different referents, evidence, values, or horizons. Propaganda may call domination stable, and an activist may call the same system intolerable; neither claim replaces the underlying conditions.

## World Stability Procedure

When stability matters:

1. **State the question.** Identify the decision or consequence for which stability is being assessed.
2. **Set scope and horizon.** Use the narrowest boundary and period that preserve material causes and affected parties.
3. **Name each referent.** Separate functions, relationships, identities, and conditions that cannot honestly share one finding.
4. **Load current state.** Use established external Campaign Records and current evidence.
5. **Identify owners.** Resolve each population, resource, ecological, economic, factional, conflict, disease, technical, Dungeon, Soul, progression, Weapon Soul, and magical input under its specialist rules.
6. **Map supports and strain.** Record actual routes, not genre expectations or desired outcomes.
7. **Map coupling and distribution.** Identify dependencies, beneficiaries, burdened parties, alternatives, and correlated risks.
8. **Test buffers and response.** Establish access, duration, coordination, maintenance, consent, and failure limits.
9. **Check thresholds and delays.** Use local evidence and preserve Pending Consequences.
10. **Assign qualitative findings.** Describe each referent without aggregating it into a universal score.
11. **Resolve interventions and Counterforces.** Follow actor agency, information, capability, and causal timing.
12. **Propagate material consequences.** Use Causal Event Chains only as far as the current Causal Horizon requires.
13. **Record uncertainty and legacy.** Preserve assumptions, branches, evidence, changed supports, consumed buffers, and durable consequences externally.

## World Stability Profile

A campaign may maintain an external **World Stability Profile** containing only the detail required for current play:

| Field | What it records |
| --- | --- |
| **Question, scope, and horizon** | Why the assessment matters, its boundary, observation time, and forecast period. |
| **Stability Referents** | Functions, relationships, identities, or conditions assessed separately. |
| **Current findings** | Qualitative findings for each referent and the evidence supporting them. |
| **Stability Supports** | Actors, processes, reserves, redundancies, alternatives, and maintained relationships. |
| **World-System Strains** | Pressures, affected supports, intensity or trend where useful, and causal routes. |
| **Systemic Couplings** | Dependencies, directions, delays, correlated risks, control, and substitutes. |
| **Buffers and response** | Available capacity, access, duration, maintenance, consent, and limits. |
| **Thresholds and warning signs** | Local conditions that change behavior and evidence that they are approaching. |
| **Distribution** | Beneficiaries, burdened parties, excluded parties, displaced risks, and unequal access. |
| **Interventions and Counterforces** | Active responses, expected mechanisms, costs, uncertainty, and possible displacement. |
| **Forecast branches** | Plausible outcomes, assumptions, triggers, and evidence that would distinguish them. |
| **Pending Consequences and legacies** | Delayed effects, consumed buffers, irreversible losses, and durable changes. |

This Profile is live campaign state and remains outside this repository.

## Worked Examples

### Prosperous but Brittle Empire

An empire has high trade volume, full warehouses, rapid magical transport, and peaceful borders. Most inland cities depend on one portal standard, one source compact, and a small order of trained maintainers.

Its current distribution is productive, but continuity is brittle against source refusal, standard failure, or loss of maintainers. Prosperity does not settle the stability claim. Diversified routes, local reserves, translation procedures, and new maintenance capacity would change the relevant supports.

### Stable Tyranny and Disruptive Liberation

A dynasty reliably collects grain, suppresses dissent, and transfers command among relatives. Its coercive administration is stable for the referent "continued dynastic control" while many communities face confiscation and violence.

A liberation movement disrupts that control and initially reduces route coordination. World Stability does not declare the movement wrong. The GM separately assesses agency, food distribution, violence, successor coordination, and long-term response capacity.

### Scholar Prevents a Cascade

A physically weak scholar recognizes that several settlements depend on one fungal treatment for seed storage. They persuade local growers to test alternate drying methods before the fungus becomes unavailable.

The intervention succeeds through knowledge, trust, trials, labor, and adoption. The scholar does not add stability points. They reduce one coupling and create buffers through established action.

### Magical Barrier Displaces Strain

A ward diverts seasonal Mana storms away from a capital. Crops inside the barrier recover, but the storms intensify over rural source habitats and the ward requires consent from a spirit whose relationship is deteriorating.

The capital's immediate safety improves while ecological and diplomatic strains increase elsewhere. Calling the realm stable would conceal Stability Displacement and a concentrated dependency.

### Reorganizing Ecology After War

War destroys canals and displaces farmers. Wetlands spread into former fields, migratory monsters establish new routes, and fisheries recover while the old grain system fails.

The former agricultural referent is unstable. The emerging wetland ecology may show Adaptive Reorganization. These findings can coexist with hunger, displacement, species loss, and new livelihoods.

### Soul Avatar as Mediator

A recognized Soul Avatar helps rival factions negotiate access to a failing aqueduct. Its historical credibility improves communication and makes a compact possible.

The Avatar does not directly stabilize the region. Repair Skills, materials, labor, water flow, legitimate representation, enforcement, and continued consent remain necessary. If those supports fail, symbolic authority cannot substitute for them.

### Dungeon Collapse with Bounded Reach

A Dungeon collapse blocks an underground river, displaces inhabitants, contaminates one well network, and closes a trade route. Neighboring regions use independent watersheds and alternative roads.

The collapse creates severe local instability and several causal chains. It is not automatically a world crisis because actual coupling limits its reach. Later choices may widen or contain the effects.

### Crisis Avoided Through Redundancy

A disease removes many operators from a technical heating network during winter. Independent neighborhood furnaces, cross-trained crews, stored fuel, and mutual-aid routes keep essential shelter warm.

The shock is real, buffers are consumed, and care burdens remain. Avoiding collapse demonstrates support against this strain; it does not prove universal preparedness or erase the resulting legacies.

## Safeguards

- Never represent World Stability as one global score, percentage, currency, health bar, or countdown.
- Never infer stability from peace, wealth, output, population, technical sophistication, magical power, order, or player success alone.
- Never make stability a moral verdict or use it to preserve an unjust arrangement by fiat.
- Never treat instability as permission for arbitrary catastrophe, a mandatory plot, an Age transition, or a World Reset.
- Never require return to a prior state when adaptation or transformed continuity has a supported route.
- Never invent a cascade without established Systemic Couplings and insufficient Counterforces.
- Never ignore distribution, displacement, consent, affected parties, or excluded alternatives.
- Never let one intervention, hero, institution, god, Soul, Soul Avatar, or Soul Weapon become a universal stabilizer.
- Never convert Mana, sacrifice, death, suffering, repetition, conflict, or elapsed time into stability points.
- Never grant Skill, Development, Soul growth, Evolution, Class, affinity, Soul Weapon awakening, or any other progression because a stability crisis occurred.
- Never let a Stability Forecast become secret deterministic scripting.
- Never erase strain, consumed buffers, maintenance debt, displacement, or legacies when immediate function recovers.
- Never replace specialist owners or GM judgment with automatic aggregation.
- Never store named live stability findings, thresholds, interventions, forecasts, or World Stability Profiles in this repository.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- a universal formula, scale, threshold, category ladder, update interval, or Reset trigger;
- current stability values, named crises, world conditions, or campaign histories;
- Age boundaries, Age-transition procedures, World Reset causes, or Reset survival rules;
- world-contact Gates or their effects;
- long-horizon change-of-resolution rules owned by [Simulation Abstraction](SIMULATION_ABSTRACTION.md);
- specialist population, resource, economic, ecological, factional, conflict, disease, technical, Dungeon, Soul, progression, Weapon Soul, or Magic effects;
- encounter, quest, catastrophe, timeline, or event generators;
- personal capability, advancement, reward, destiny, or narrative importance.
