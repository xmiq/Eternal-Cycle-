# World Gate Interactions with Reincarnation and Soul Avatars

## Purpose

This document defines the interface between [World Gates and World-Contact Events](GATES_AND_WORLD_CONTACT.md), [Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md), and [Soul Avatars](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md).

It answers two narrow questions:

1. when world contact can affect valid Reincarnation placement across Contact Domains; and
2. how a valid world-contact situation can become relevant to Soul Avatar emergence or Expression.

It does not turn World Gates into Soul Gates, replace the seven-stage Reincarnation transition, expand Avatar Expression into portal magic, or make a reincarnating soul the center of every contact event.

Current souls, Gates, candidates, deaths, incarnations, Avatar Triggers, contact events, and consequences remain external campaign state.

## Core Rules

### World Gates Do Not Carry Disembodied Souls by Default

A World Gate's observation, communication, environmental, energy, material, organism, and embodied-person channels do not include a disembodied-soul channel by implication.

- An embodied person crossing remains the same living incarnation.
- A Soul Gate remains the scoped route into Soul Space.
- Interlife is not a physical location inside a World Gate.
- A World Gate opening does not pull souls toward either endpoint.
- A World Gate closing does not trap a soul inside the interface by default.
- A World Gate controller does not acquire control over Reincarnation candidates.

Any effect that directly transports, confines, redirects, detects, harms, or bargains with a disembodied soul requires a separate established Soul or Reincarnation rule with fair scope, warning, resistance, and consequence.

### Reincarnation Still Uses Seven Stages

World contact never inserts an extra transition stage.

Reincarnation still resolves:

1. Final Death;
2. severance of the incarnation;
3. Life Reconciliation;
4. Interlife;
5. valid candidate generation;
6. Reincarnation Mode;
7. embodiment and awakening.

A Gate can change world state relevant to one or more stages. It cannot skip, reorder, duplicate, reopen, or complete them by itself.

### Soul Avatar Expression Does Not Operate a Gate by Status

A World-Contact Event can be an Avatar Trigger only when actual history, Resonance, present responsibility, and accessible contributors make it relevant.

Soul Avatar status does not:

- open, close, stabilize, redirect, widen, or repair a World Gate;
- create a Gate Channel or improve Gate Throughput;
- exempt the current body from Contact Compatibility;
- send Echoes, Soul Imprints, or Avatar Expressions through the Gate as independent actors;
- translate every language or metaphysical law;
- grant authority over contacted peoples, Gate sources, endpoints, or factions;
- guarantee that the Soul Avatar is needed or recognized.

Any Gate operation still requires an actual technical, magical, divine, living, social, or other supported interface.

## Distinct Soul States at a World Gate

| Soul state | What the World Gate may affect | What it cannot do automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Living embodied incarnation** | current travel, exposure, environment, access, information, and world consequence through supported channels | cause Reincarnation, duplicate the body, or detach the soul |
| **Dying or apparently dead incarnation** | location, hazards, recovery access, witnesses, body movement, and evidence | declare Final Death merely because the boundary was crossed |
| **Final Death and severance** | world-side fate of body, possessions, witnesses, and consequences | transport assets or make the Gate the Interlife destination |
| **Interlife** | changing world state, Gate opening or closure, candidate possibilities, and uncertainty | become a default Gate traveler or gain physical Gate access |
| **Candidate generation** | availability of Contact Domains, species, births, constructions, locations, and causal placement where a valid route exists | add every contacted species or endpoint to the candidate list |
| **New embodiment** | starting environment, compatibility, culture, ecology, and social consequence | remain provisional, split across endpoints, or reopen candidate selection |
| **Avatar Expression** | Trigger relevance, accessible historical context, visibility, pressure, and world response | become a portal, extra body, universal interpreter, or command authority |

Classify the soul's current state before resolving a Gate interaction. A rule that is valid for a living traveler may be invalid during Interlife, and a valid candidate route does not become ordinary embodied travel.

## Embodied Crossing

When a living incarnation crosses a World Gate through a supported embodied-person channel:

1. resolve Gate Directionality, Throughput, transit conditions, costs, and failure;
2. test the body's physical, environmental, biological, magical, technical, sensory, and other relevant Contact Compatibility;
3. resolve any protective equipment, Skills, Magic, allies, and Gate operation through their owners;
4. preserve the soul's singular current embodiment;
5. update location, exposure, knowledge, relationships, and consequences in the external Campaign Record.

The traveler does not die and reincarnate merely because the destination is another world, realm, reality, or Age. The current body remains current until Final Death.

The soul does not leave ordinary possessions behind through Reincarnation rules because Reincarnation has not occurred. Which possessions cross depends on the Gate's material channel and transit conditions. A carried Soul Weapon remains subject to both its vessel rules and the Gate's supported channels.

### Incompatible Destination Conditions

If the destination cannot support the incarnation, resolve actual exposure and countermeasures. Outcomes may include safe withdrawal, inability to cross, injury, equipment failure, forced rescue, transformation under an explicit effect, or Final Death after ordinary recovery options fail.

Incompatibility does not automatically:

- kill the traveler;
- damage the soul;
- trigger beneficial adaptation;
- grant a new body;
- activate Avatar Expression;
- return the traveler to the origin;
- prove that every member of the species is incompatible.

## Death at or Beyond a World Gate

### Final Death Remains a Separate Finding

A body may be lost, divided from rescuers, transported, transformed, or rendered unreachable during a Gate incident. None of those facts proves Final Death until current recovery, survival, revival, substitution, and relevant specialist effects are resolved.

If Final Death occurs:

- it occurs once for the one active incarnation;
- the body and worldly consequences remain where established events leave them;
- Life Reconciliation classifies the life normally;
- Gate exposure grants no death bonus, Soul growth, Echo Presence, or special candidate;
- observers on different sides may hold conflicting beliefs without creating two deaths or two souls.

### Boundary Uncertainty

If evidence cannot establish which side contains the body or where Final Death completed, preserve that uncertainty. Resolve only facts supported by the Gate Basis, channels, timing, observations, and recovery attempts.

The GM must not use spatial ambiguity to:

- create two bodies with one soul;
- form two Echoes from one completed incarnation;
- let revival complete on one side while Reincarnation completes on the other;
- retroactively move possessions or consequences to the more convenient endpoint;
- conceal an arbitrary soul injury.

## Cross-Domain Reincarnation Routes

A **Cross-Domain Reincarnation Route** is an established soul-and-world causal basis that permits valid candidate generation to treat another Contact Domain as reachable for Reincarnation placement.

It is not a Gate Channel. It does not move a living body, ordinary possession, or Interlife soul through the World Gate as physical traffic. It establishes only that the target domain may be considered during normal candidate generation when every other requirement is met.

### When a Route Is Needed

A Cross-Domain Reincarnation Route is required when:

- the target candidate lies across a material Contact Domain boundary;
- that boundary matters to the world's Reincarnation laws or causal placement; and
- no broader established Reincarnation law already includes both domains.

Some settings may have one Reincarnation ecology that already spans several realms or worlds. In that case, a World Gate may affect information, population movement, or world state without defining the soul's placement reach. Record the broader law explicitly rather than pretending the Gate created it.

### Possible Route Bases

A route may be supported by one or more established facts, such as:

- a world-scale Reincarnation law that explicitly spans the Contact Domains;
- a Gate Basis whose defined functions include an interface with candidate placement under the Reincarnation rules;
- an embodied crossing followed by Final Death in the destination domain, combined with a local soul-placement law capable of receiving that continuing soul;
- a divine, spiritual, constructed, or world-system relationship with actual jurisdiction, permission, reach, and placement function;
- a Soul Constellation or other soul-level relationship only where its own rules explicitly permit candidate influence and world causality supports the target;
- a persistent Gate Legacy that established a genuine shared Reincarnation ecology even after physical Gate Closure;
- another explicit setting rule consistent with soul singularity and valid embodiment.

No one item guarantees a route. A death beyond a Gate supplies causal location, not automatic local acceptance. A deity's claim supplies a claim, not jurisdiction. A Constellation supplies relationship, not transport. A Gate Basis supplies only its stated functions.

### Invalid Route Claims

The following do not create a Cross-Domain Reincarnation Route by themselves:

- seeing, hearing about, studying, crossing, owning, guarding, or closing a World Gate;
- physical, material, organism, communication, or Mana channels;
- a map, prophecy, title, blessing, contract, wish, or destination preference;
- similarity between species or souls;
- Soul Resonance without an actual placement owner;
- Soul Avatar status or an Avatar Association;
- death at an endpoint;
- Gate control, political authority, conquest, or legal ownership;
- the existence of a valid body without a causal soul-placement route;
- the existence of a placement route without a valid body.

## Cross-Domain Candidate Resolution

World Knowledge, World Contact, a Travel Route, and current Reincarnation Possibility are separate claims. A World Gate channel can support only the channels it actually owns. Contact may inform candidate adjudication, but it neither guarantees a destination nor turns every previously contacted domain into a permanent menu option.

Player wishes are meaningful adjudication input. Soul state, elapsed Interlife, current world and Gate state, cosmology, valid embodiments, consequences, and Reincarnation rules remain controlling constraints. No numerical destination weighting is implied.

When another Contact Domain may enter candidate generation, test the ordinary Reincarnation requirements plus the cross-domain interface.

### 1. Confirm Final Death and Interlife

Do not reserve or select the cross-domain candidate while the former incarnation can still recover. Complete severance and Life Reconciliation first.

### 2. Establish the Target Domain as Current World Truth

Identify the Contact Domain, its current relation to the Gate, elapsed time, relevant Gate Closure or legacy, and what is known versus merely believed.

The candidate cannot inhabit a remembered, hypothetical, alternate, erased, or purely symbolic version of the destination.

### 3. Establish the Cross-Domain Reincarnation Route

Name the law, relationship, system, or Gate-linked interface that makes placement reachable. Identify its scope, requirements, permissions, limits, and current condition.

### 4. Establish a Valid Embodiment Route

The target must have a plausible birth, hatching, spawning, construction, awakening, transformation, or other species-appropriate entry into life.

Contact with a species does not add it to the candidate pool. Migration, reproduction, settlement, construction, ecology, and current population state may create new embodiment opportunities over time through their own systems.

### 5. Test Soul and Contact Compatibility

Apply Soul Integrity, capacity, bonds, persistent conditions, candidate unlocks, and any destination-specific soul law. Then test the new body's actual environment and world conditions.

Contact Compatibility is evidence, not a universal pass or fail. A body native to the destination may be physically viable while retained magical methods, divine relationships, Soul Weapon forms, or familiar senses remain inaccessible.

### 6. Test Causal Placement

Give the candidate a real history, population, location, timing, and social or ecological context. Gate control does not permit a candidate to appear inside a vault, royal bloodline, extinct species, unique construct, or enemy command merely because that would be useful.

### 7. Apply Reincarnation Mode

Emergent, Constrained Choice, or Directed selection operates only among the valid candidates that remain. A Cross-Domain Reincarnation Route broadens consideration; it does not guarantee that a cross-domain candidate is offered or selected.

### 8. Revalidate Before Embodiment

If the Gate, route Basis, target population, world law, candidate body, or causal placement changes materially before embodiment, revalidate the affected claim. Narrow, replace, delay, or reject an invalid candidate through the ordinary mode and interference rules.

Revalidation is not a free reroll. Once embodiment begins, later Gate Closure does not cancel the incarnation or return the soul to candidate selection.

## Gate Opening, Closure, and Candidate Availability

### Opening

A World Gate opening may:

- reveal Contact Domains that already lay within a broader Reincarnation ecology;
- create a placement interface when the Gate Basis explicitly supports one;
- bring populations across, leading to future local embodiment routes;
- alter religions, institutions, knowledge, and candidate beliefs without changing actual validity;
- produce misleading claims that every newly known species is now selectable.

Opening creates no candidate by category alone.

### Ongoing Contact

Sustained contact may gradually create:

- migrant or mixed populations with real births and life histories;
- constructed bodies supported by new institutions and infrastructure;
- new local species branches under Monster Evolution;
- social recognition or persecution of cross-domain reincarnators;
- research into placement laws;
- shared soul institutions or relationships.

Each development remains with its owner. Time, repetition, and Gate traffic do not automatically make a route safer, broader, or more rewarding.

### Closure

Gate Closure can remove a route only when that route depended on the closed Gate function. It does not:

- end an already embodied incarnation;
- recall a soul or body to its origin;
- erase a broader Reincarnation law;
- erase local populations or embodiment routes established before closure;
- dissolve Soul Resonance, Titles, Constellations, or Archive Records by itself;
- guarantee that an Interlife soul is stranded or destroyed.

Closure may narrow candidate options, extend Interlife, create uncertainty, or force another valid placement when the required route ends before embodiment. Gate Legacies may instead preserve a shared placement ecology after physical transit ends.

## Species, Skills, and World Law

A cross-domain incarnation begins in its actual new body and world.

- Human and monster Skill Trees remain distinct.
- Species Traits, stages, Mutations, and Evolution Routes come from the current form and its owner.
- Retained Skills and Stat XP persist under their established rules but do not arrive as current mastery.
- Retained Instincts may orient attention but do not create foreign anatomy.
- Magic must be revalidated against local Mana, sources, Affinities, Patterns, materials, and world laws.
- Classes, Professions, certifications, titles of office, citizenship, and faction standing do not transfer.
- A Soul Weapon bond may persist while its vessel form, Manifestation, materials, or functions remain inaccessible or incompatible.

World contact may create bounded crossover opportunities. It never merges progression trees, imports infrastructure, or makes foreign embodiment a shortcut around current-life effort.

## World Gates as Avatar Triggers

A World-Contact Event may become an **Avatar Trigger** when the current responsibility materially connects to integrated soul history.

Possible Trigger Bases include:

- contact with a civilization the soul actually lived within;
- a Gate crisis repeating a consequential pattern across several lives;
- conflict among former species perspectives that matters to present contact;
- responsibility for a relationship, institution, ecology, or harm that crosses the Gate;
- a rival Soul Avatar arriving through valid world causality;
- a Gate Legacy exposing consequences the soul helped create in another life;
- current actors recognizing a genuine Soul Title, Association, or prior identity through a valid method.

The event may activate relevant Resonance Links or make dormant history newly important. It does not create the Links, history, Soul Depth, Integrity, or emergence qualifications retroactively.

### Trigger Is Not Compulsion

A Gate breach may produce involuntary onset, memory bleed, urgency, or a partial Echo response when existing rules support it. Once deliberate choice is possible, the Avatar Lead decides whether to stabilize, narrow, reject, or exit Expression.

The GM cannot use world-scale danger to choose the character's speech, allegiance, sacrifice, diplomacy, or Gate crossing. Refusal may have real world consequences without becoming proof of corruption or failure.

## Historical Knowledge at First Contact

A Soul Avatar may contribute knowledge only from actual accessible sources.

Every contribution identifies:

- which incarnation, Echo, Imprint, Archive Record, Title, or other source carries it;
- when and where that source learned it;
- what body, senses, culture, role, and interests shaped it;
- how much time and world change separate the source from current contact;
- what translation, memory, or provenance limits apply;
- which current evidence supports or contradicts it.

A life from the contacted domain may help recognize language, ritual forms, geography, political symbols, ecology, or danger. It cannot prove that names, meanings, laws, factions, species, magical conditions, or relationships remain unchanged.

### No Divergent Echoes

A counterpart, alternate history, copied memory, simulation, prophecy, possible life, or person resembling a prior incarnation does not become a Soul Echo.

Only an actually completed incarnation of the same continuing soul forms its own Echo under Life Reconciliation. Contact between domains does not split the soul's history, create a second present incarnation, or let the Avatar Lead consult lives that never occurred.

## Imperfect Interpretation

A Soul Avatar may act as an interpreter when accessible history and current capability support the task. Interpretation may involve:

- remembered language or nonverbal patterns;
- several species' sensory perspectives;
- institutional, magical, ecological, or religious context;
- warnings about false equivalents;
- comparison of current observations with dated experience;
- explicit uncertainty and requests for verification.

Avatar Expression does not create a universal translator.

It cannot supply:

- vocabulary or concepts the contributing lives never learned;
- perception the current body and available tools cannot receive;
- objective intent behind an ambiguous message;
- authority to speak for either Contact Domain;
- consent from the people being interpreted;
- infallible cultural or moral judgment;
- automatic compatibility between magical laws, Souls, or technologies.

Useful interpretation can still create political pressure. Institutions may recruit, guard, bargain with, surveil, worship, fear, imprison, discredit, or impersonate the Soul Avatar through ordinary faction and social causality.

## Avatar Expression and Gate Transit

An Avatar Lead crosses a World Gate only as the current embodied person through a valid embodied-person channel.

During transit:

- the current body bears the Gate's costs and compatibility limits;
- relevant Echoes may advise or contribute bounded Overlay under their Presence rules;
- Retained Instincts may produce valid cues without selecting deliberate action;
- Avatar Expression may narrow or fail under Strain, incompatible conditions, damaged Links, or insufficient attention;
- the Soul Anchor preserves current identity but is not a navigational beacon or return ticket;
- a willing Weapon Soul remains a distinct person and receives no automatic Gate immunity.

Former incarnations cannot cross separately, scout the destination, hold positions at both endpoints, carry extra equipment, perform parallel diplomacy, or die in place of the Avatar Lead.

If the current incarnation cannot cross, Avatar Expression does not manufacture a compatible body. The Soul Avatar may seek another route, provide counsel to travelers, negotiate through a communication channel, or decline involvement.

## Rival Soul Avatars and Contact Domains

Several Soul Avatars may exist in one or several Contact Domains.

They meet only through valid:

- Gate Channels or ordinary routes;
- Reincarnation placement;
- communication;
- faction decisions;
- relationships;
- world events and timing.

World contact does not attract them cosmically, assign opposed alignments, guarantee recognition, or require a duel. Their histories may support cooperation, rivalry, misunderstanding, indifference, or changing positions.

If two Contact Domains contain similar Soul Avatar traditions, that similarity does not prove a shared office, one chosen role, or duplicate souls. Each claim follows its own history and evidence.

## Other Soul-System Handoffs

### Soul Resonance and Echoes

A World Gate, endpoint, contacted person, place, language, conflict, or Gate Legacy may become a Resonance Target when a genuine Basis exists. Resonance can improve access or reveal Dissonance; it cannot supply missing history or prove the target's nature.

Only relevant Echoes participate. Gate scale does not raise Echo Presence, repair a damaged Echo, compel testimony, or make one perspective objective.

### Soul Titles and Associations

Soul Titles and Avatar Associations may affect recognition, symbolic authority, duties, fear, opportunity, and political pressure. They do not grant Gate access, command over associated beings, citizenship, diplomatic office, or rightful control.

Repeated Gate crossings do not create a Gate, Worlds, Travel, or Mediator Association by themselves. Meaningful integration and consequence remain necessary.

### Soul Constellations

World contact may create meetings through which distinct souls later form or transform a Soul Constellation. One encounter, forced proximity, pursuit across Gates, or institutional matchmaking does not create reciprocal transformation.

Gate Closure does not automatically sever a Constellation, and a Constellation does not automatically reopen contact. A Constellation Commons remains a network of scoped Soul Gates, not a World Gate or transport route.

### Akashic Archive

World contact may create Archive Traces and Records through actual events. A Soul Avatar may read them only through a valid Archive Target, Basis, Interface, scope, permission, capacity, and Reading Mode.

The Archive does not provide a live omniscient view of both domains, guarantee translation, predict the settled outcome, or create a missing Reincarnation route.

### Soul Weapons

A Soul Weapon may recognize unfamiliar materials, former Gate events, metaphysical mismatch, or shared history when its own senses and Weapon Echoes support that recognition. It gains no automatic form, Function, Evolution, Gate compatibility, or independent transit.

The Weapon Soul may advise, consent, refuse, become stranded with its current vessel conditions, or participate in Avatar Expression under the existing bond. It is never absorbed into the Soul Avatar or treated as carried equipment without personhood.

## Contact Continues Without the Soul Avatar

A Soul Avatar may become important to one contact branch without owning the event.

While the Soul Avatar acts or refuses:

- factions pursue independent goals;
- translators and scholars develop other methods;
- trade, migration, disease, ecology, conflict, and advancement continue through their systems;
- rival accounts and misinformation spread;
- Gate operation changes through its actual Basis;
- actors on both sides make decisions without waiting for the player;
- closure, adaptation, or durable contact may occur elsewhere.

Removing the Soul Avatar from a scenario changes only the causal contributions that person actually made. The world does not freeze or invent an identical substitute outcome.

## Interaction Procedure

When Reincarnation or Avatar Expression intersects a World Gate:

1. **Establish the World Gate.** Identify Contact Domains, Basis, endpoints, boundary, footprint, channels, directionality, throughput, transit conditions, compatibility, and current evidence.
2. **Identify the soul state.** Distinguish living embodiment, possible death, Final Death, Interlife, candidate generation, new embodiment, and Avatar Expression.
3. **Preserve singularity and agency.** Identify the one active incarnation and the Avatar Lead where relevant; do not create parallel selves or actions.
4. **Resolve ordinary Gate effects.** Apply travel, exposure, information, access, costs, and failure before inferring Soul effects.
5. **If death is possible, resolve Final Death.** Complete recovery and revival checks, world consequences, severance, and Life Reconciliation once.
6. **If placement crosses domains, establish the route.** Name the Cross-Domain Reincarnation Route and test every ordinary candidate condition.
7. **If Avatar Expression is proposed, establish the Trigger.** Identify the genuine history, Links, contributors, present responsibility, scope, costs, and exit.
8. **Preserve provenance.** Date memories, separate Echo testimony from Archive Records and current evidence, and expose uncertainty.
9. **Check embodiment and compatibility.** Neither retained history nor Avatar Expression waives current body, Skill, Magic, tool, language, environment, or Gate limits.
10. **Resolve choice and other persons.** Protect player agency, Echo willingness, Weapon Soul personhood, source response, and the independent decisions of contacted actors.
11. **Hand off world consequences.** Use the relevant population, ecology, economy, faction, conflict, disease, advancement, Stability, Age, and Gate rules.
12. **Record continuity externally.** Preserve the current incarnation, candidate reasoning, Gate state, knowledge views, Strain, relationships, consequences, and Pending Consequences in the external Campaign Record.

## Worked Examples

### Crossing, Closure, and Later Death

A human reincarnator crosses a reciprocal World Gate and settles in a monster civilization. The Gate closes twenty years later. The incarnation lives another decade and reaches Final Death there.

The closed Gate does not recall the soul. Candidate generation tests the local Reincarnation ecology, valid births, soul compatibility, and causal placement. If local law can receive the soul, a nearby monster birth may qualify without the physical Gate reopening. If no Cross-Domain Reincarnation Route or local receiving law exists, the soul may face longer Interlife or other valid candidates; death at the destination alone does not guarantee local rebirth.

### Death in an Unstable Boundary

A Gate collapses while a traveler is crossing. Observers on each side see part of the body and both believe the traveler died locally.

The GM uses Gate timing, channels, recovery routes, and evidence to resolve one world truth. If Final Death occurred, one Life Reconciliation forms one Echo. Conflicting funerals and legal claims may persist, but they do not create two deaths, bodies with one soul, or simultaneous revival and Reincarnation.

### The Desired Dragon Candidate

A human soul sees dragons beyond a World Gate and dies deliberately near the endpoint hoping to reincarnate as one.

Knowledge and location create no route, no earned access, and no valid dragon birth. The death receives no bonus. Candidate generation includes only bodies supported by world state, the actual placement scope, soul compatibility, unlocks, and causal history.

### Migration Creates Later Possibility

Monster populations cross a seasonal Gate, establish a viable settlement, and reproduce for several generations. A reincarnating soul with an established placement route and compatible history later receives one local monster candidate.

The Gate did not unlock the species at first sight. Population movement, ecology, settlement, reproduction, elapsed time, and valid candidate generation created the later possibility.

### The Imperfect Interpreter

A Soul Avatar once lived in the contacted empire six centuries earlier. Avatar Expression recovers an old court dialect, ritual etiquette, and memories of a border war.

The dialect has become ceremonial, the former enemy is now an allied minority, and one gesture has reversed meaning. The Soul Avatar helps identify the misunderstandings but must verify current usage. Institutions value the Avatar as a witness while disputing whether that history grants any right to negotiate.

### The Familiar City That Is Not the Same

A Gate reveals a city resembling one destroyed in a prior life. Reflexive Resonance activates a relevant Echo, but records show the city was independently built by descendants who deliberately imitated old plans.

The Echo supplies memory of the original city, not knowledge of the present inhabitants. Resemblance creates a question, not a divergent Echo, alternate incarnation, or objective claim that history repeated exactly.

### Rival Soul Avatars Meet

Two Soul Avatars cross from different Contact Domains as members of separate diplomatic missions. One carries an Association with imperial civilization; the other with displaced monsters.

Their Associations create relevant pressure and recognition but no assigned cosmic sides. Gate access, faction orders, personal choices, interpreters, and current evidence bring them into negotiation. They may become rivals, allies, or neither.

### Crisis Trigger Without Gate Mastery

An elemental breach threatens both endpoints. Several prior lives give one Soul Avatar relevant experience with evacuation, elemental ecology, and failed sealing Rituals.

The crisis becomes a valid Avatar Trigger. Expression helps compare options and warn against an old failure, but it cannot close the Gate. Current ritualists, engineers, sources, communities, and the Avatar Lead must choose and act through their actual capabilities.

### Closure Strands the Mediator

A Soul Avatar becomes a trusted interpreter during decades of contact and is on the far side when the World Gate closes.

Avatar status supplies no return ticket. The person remains embodied, politically visible, and subject to local conditions. Past-life experience may help adaptation, while separation, suspicion, obligations, and Gate Legacies continue through world causality.

## Safeguards

- Never use a World Gate as a Soul Gate, Interlife hallway, candidate menu, checkpoint, fast-travel respawn, or source of extra incarnations.
- Never treat crossing as death, Final Death as crossing, or Reincarnation placement as ordinary Gate transit.
- Never add a Contact Domain, endpoint, species, body, bloodline, construct, or Age to candidate generation without an established placement route and ordinary candidate validity.
- Never infer a disembodied-soul channel from physical, organism, communication, environmental, magical, or Mana transit.
- Never let Gate control, ownership, conquest, prophecy, title, resonance, or Soul Avatar status select another soul's incarnation.
- Never preserve revival on one side and complete Reincarnation on the other for the same incarnation.
- Never use boundary uncertainty to duplicate a body, soul, Echo, death, inventory, or consequence.
- Never let Gate Closure cancel an embodied life, erase established populations, or destroy a soul merely because a route ended.
- Never form Soul Echoes from counterparts, possibilities, simulations, copied memories, alternate histories, or lives that did not actually end.
- Never make world contact an automatic Avatar Trigger, emergence event, Association, or Soul growth source.
- Never let Avatar Expression open or operate a Gate, add a channel, increase Throughput, bypass compatibility, or supply a missing body or Skill.
- Never make a Soul Avatar a universal translator, objective historian, rightful diplomat, mandatory sacrifice, destined enemy, sole solution, or only relevant actor.
- Never let Echoes or former incarnations cross as independent bodies, perform parallel actions, carry equipment, or die in place of the Avatar Lead.
- Never award progression for Gate crossing, repeated contact, survival, stranding, historical importance, mediation, or crisis exposure alone.
- Never introduce Gate-caused Soul Strain, Wounds, memory loss, redirection, or annihilation without an explicit owner, cause, warning, resistance, and proportionate aftermath.
- Never store current Gate-soul interactions, candidates, incarnations, triggers, contact histories, or live world facts in this repository.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- a universal cosmology, reincarnation ecology, afterlife map, or soul geography;
- a canonical World Gate, contacted society, soul, incarnation, candidate, or Soul Avatar;
- a universal cross-domain placement formula or probability;
- ordinary Soul Gate, Soul Intrusion, Echo, Avatar, Gate, Skill, Development, Magic, or World Engine mechanics beyond their handoff;
- soul division, alternate incarnations, timeline branches, counterpart identity, or omniversal versions of one soul;
- a Gate-event, Reincarnation, Soul Avatar, encounter, or campaign-state template;
- long-horizon change-of-resolution rules owned by [Simulation Abstraction](SIMULATION_ABSTRACTION.md);
- campaign events, current state, playthrough history, or live rulings.
