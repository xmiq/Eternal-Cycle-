# Physical Development

## Purpose

This document defines how bodies grow, condition, adapt, decline, recover, and express physical capability. It applies to humans, monsters, spirits with bodies, undead vessels, artificial bodies, and other playable embodiments without treating any one anatomy as the default.

## Core Rule

Physical Development is earned adaptation in the current body. It changes what that body can physically perceive, endure, coordinate, recover from, or perform, but it is not a permanent soul-level body score.

Final Death ends the body's developed capacity and Current Condition. Reincarnation may preserve relevant Stat XP and other legitimate Soul Imprints as Persistent Potential, but a new incarnation must still mature, train, calibrate, recover, and adapt through its own embodiment.

No physical dimension determines overall capability or combat victory by itself. Force, speed, endurance, coordination, resilience, senses, body plan, condition, technique, equipment, environment, preparation, and opposition remain distinct.

## Terms

### Physical Development

Earned adaptation in a current body's structure, function, regulation, perception, and movement through meaningful use, training, recovery, and environmental engagement.

### Physical Dimension

A bounded aspect of bodily capability, such as force production, endurance, balance, sensory processing, or recovery. Dimensions interact but do not convert through a universal exchange rate.

### Body Potential

The range and directions of physical adaptation presently possible for a body under its anatomy, species, lifecycle, health, nourishment, environment, and established modifications. Potential is possibility, not current performance.

### Trained Capacity

The physical capability a body has actually developed and retained through adaptation. Capacity may remain above or below what the body can express in its present condition.

### Current Condition

The body's immediate readiness and impairment, including fatigue, injury, illness, pain, hydration, nutrition, sleep, stress, temperature, suppression, and temporary enhancement.

### Physical Calibration

The present-life process of aligning perception, timing, force, balance, and movement with the body's actual proportions, senses, response delays, and available structures.

### Accommodation

A stable method, tool, altered technique, or environmental arrangement that allows a body to act effectively with its actual capabilities. Accommodation is not restoration of anatomy the body does not possess.

## Physical Capability and the Six Layers

Physical capability follows the shared [six-layer model](DEVELOPMENT_SYSTEM.md#the-six-layer-capability-model).

| Layer | Physical question |
| --- | --- |
| **Persistent Potential** | What relevant Stat XP, Skill Imprints, Retained Instincts, and learned principles survived prior lives? |
| **Current Access** | What prior methods, bodily patterns, warnings, or developmental routes can this incarnation presently reach? |
| **Embodied Expression** | What can this anatomy, lifecycle stage, health, and environment physically perform now? |
| **Practised Reliability** | What can this body repeat safely and consistently under pressure, fatigue, variation, or disruption? |
| **Contextual Effectiveness** | Which physical capabilities matter for this objective, terrain, opponent, tool, team, and timescale? |
| **World Recognition** | How do observers, institutions, cultures, or species classify the body's capability, condition, or legitimacy? |

High Persistent Potential does not create adult tissue, mature organs, current stamina, or calibrated movement. High Trained Capacity may be temporarily inaccessible because of injury or exhaustion. Exceptional present performance may come from external magic or equipment without representing lasting Physical Development.

## Distinguishing Physical Qualities

### Body Potential

Body Potential comes from the incarnation's real form. It may be shaped by:

- species and lineage;
- age and lifecycle stage;
- anatomy, size, mass, and material composition;
- sex-linked or form-linked biology where the setting establishes it;
- health, nutrition, sleep, and environment;
- mutation, evolution, magical alteration, or artificial design under their Owning Systems;
- access to suitable movement, load, temperature, pressure, gravity, and other training conditions.

Potential does not guarantee that adaptation occurs. A dragon may possess the structures for tremendous flight endurance while remaining poorly conditioned after confinement. A human may possess fine manual potential while never developing the coordination required by a craft.

### Trained Capacity

Trained Capacity is the body's integrated adaptation to work it has actually performed. Examples include stronger force production, improved oxygen use, more stable joints, refined balance, thicker protective tissue, more efficient locomotion, or better sensory discrimination.

Capacity is not the same as technique. Strong fingers can support lockpicking, surgery, climbing, or music, but each reliable technique belongs to Skill or Profession Development. Physical Development owns the bodily support, not the complete action.

### Current Condition

Current Condition determines how much developed capacity can be expressed now. It can change rapidly through:

- fatigue and recovery;
- acute injury or pain;
- illness, poison, or infection;
- hunger, dehydration, oxygen, pressure, or temperature;
- fear, stress, focus, and altered arousal;
- magical reinforcement or suppression;
- temporary transformation;
- damaged, missing, repaired, or replaced components.

Current Condition is not a moral judgment and should not be mistaken for permanent regression. A rested novice may temporarily outperform an exhausted expert in one task without possessing greater Trained Capacity.

### Technique

Technique is the learned organization of action. It belongs primarily to [Skill Development](SKILL_DEVELOPMENT.md). Physical Development may make a technique possible, cheaper, faster, or more stable, but it does not award the technique merely because the body is capable of it.

Examples in this document do not create a Skill, rank, technique package, or progression-tree unlock beyond the rules in Skill Development.

### Magical Reinforcement

Magical reinforcement is power supplied or directed through a magical effect. It may increase present force, speed, durability, recovery, perception, or environmental tolerance under the magic system's rules.

Using reinforcement can create Physical Development only where the body itself performs and integrates relevant adaptation. Borrowed force is not automatically trained strength, magical pain suppression is not bodily resilience, and repeated healing does not make injury harmless.

### Persistent Stat XP

Stat XP preserves developmental familiarity as Persistent Potential. It is not a direct statistic, body template, starting value, universal physical currency, or proof of current competence.

[Stat XP and Retained Development](STAT_XP_AND_RETAINED_DEVELOPMENT.md) defines its domains, compatibility, transfer loss, access, acceleration, and safeguards. This document owns the rule that physical redevelopment must occur through the current body.

## Physical Dimensions

Physical Dimensions describe different adaptations. They should be recorded only when relevant and should never be summed into one authoritative physical score.

### Force and Strength

Force production includes pushing, pulling, lifting, gripping, striking, bracing, biting, constricting, anchoring, and other anatomy-specific output.

Useful force depends on leverage, position, traction, range of motion, structural tolerance, technique, and the target. Greater raw force does not guarantee a successful strike, escape, grapple, climb, or battle.

### Endurance

Endurance covers sustained work, repeated effort, energy use, heat management, oxygen or mana-supported metabolism, and resistance to performance decline over time.

Endurance is activity-specific. Long-distance flight, submerged pursuit, burrowing, spell-supported movement, and standing guard may rely on different systems and cannot be treated as one interchangeable reserve.

### Speed

Speed describes rate of movement or action within a defined mode and duration. Acceleration, maximum speed, reaction time, repeated speed, turning, stopping, and recovery are different questions.

A fast runner may be slow to change direction. A large predator may accelerate explosively but tire quickly. A small insect may react quickly across its own scale without outrunning a horse over distance.

### Agility, Coordination, and Fine Motor Control

Agility concerns controlled changes of movement and orientation. Coordination organizes several limbs, segments, muscles, tendrils, wings, or mechanisms toward one result. Fine motor control governs precise low-scale manipulation through whatever anatomy is available.

More limbs do not grant automatic coordination. Fewer limbs do not imply poor control. Each body must calibrate its own control problem.

### Flexibility, Mobility, and Balance

Flexibility describes available deformation or range. Mobility describes usable movement through that range. Balance maintains orientation against gravity, current, acceleration, recoil, impact, or an anatomy-specific equivalent.

Extreme flexibility without control can reduce stability. Rigid bodies may compensate through stance, segmented movement, tools, magic, or environmental anchoring.

### Resilience and Recovery

Resilience describes how the body tolerates and remains functional under stress, impact, pressure, temperature, toxins, deprivation, or other defined demands. Recovery describes restoration and adaptation after exertion or harm.

Resistance is source-specific. Tough skin does not imply poison resistance, emotional composure, disease immunity, or rapid healing. Fast regeneration may consume energy, require suitable material, preserve pain, or reproduce a maladapted structure according to its Owning System.

### Pain Tolerance

Pain tolerance concerns functioning while receiving bodily warning signals. It is not immunity to injury, proof of courage, or a reason to ignore damage.

Suppressing pain can increase immediate function while concealing worsening injury. Repeated exposure to pain does not automatically grant safe tolerance and may create fear, sensitization, tissue damage, or maladaptation.

### Sensory Processing

Sensory processing includes discrimination, filtering, integration, localization, tracking, and meaningful response to information the current body can actually detect.

It does not create missing sensory organs or factual interpretation. A dragon may hear a distant movement without knowing its cause. A human with prior echolocation experience needs a present signal source, suitable hearing, calibration, and skill before that principle becomes useful.

### Breath and Internal Regulation

Breath control includes deliberate management of respiration or an equivalent exchange system. Internal regulation includes pacing, pressure control, temperature management, buoyancy, circulation, energy distribution, and other body-specific processes that can be influenced through practice.

Not every body breathes, and not every internal function is voluntarily trainable. Undead, artificial, elemental, and distributed bodies require their own valid equivalents rather than human terminology pasted onto them.

### Bodily Awareness

Bodily awareness is perception of position, tension, damage, internal state, movement, and available response. It supports calibration, rehabilitation, and technique but is fallible and body-dependent.

Prior-life awareness may help notice useful patterns. It cannot guarantee an accurate map of unfamiliar organs, magical channels, distributed consciousness, or replace diagnosis.

## Embodiment Factors

### Age and Maturation

Bodies develop through species-appropriate lifecycles. Growth may involve infancy, hatching, larval stages, metamorphosis, seasonal forms, construction phases, awakening, or other established routes.

Earlier stages may have distinct strengths rather than merely reduced adult values. A juvenile dragon may turn more tightly than an adult but lack its mass and endurance. A larval monster may digest materials its mature form cannot.

Retained progression cannot compress biological maturation into an instant. It may improve choices, safety, coordination, and training quality at an earlier stage, but tissue, organs, shells, frames, or magical structures still need their valid development time and resources.

### Anatomy and Species Limits

Every body has available structures, absent structures, vulnerabilities, and tradeoffs. Species limits describe the current route, not the value of the soul.

Limits may be changed only by a system that owns mutation, evolution, transformation, magical alteration, artificial modification, or another valid bodily change. Training cannot produce wings from ordinary human shoulders or teach an iron construct to metabolize food when it lacks that process.

### Sex-Linked and Form-Linked Biology

Sexual dimorphism or alternate biological forms matter only where a species rule establishes a physical difference relevant to the present task. Such differences may involve size, reproductive burden, seasonal changes, armor, display structures, hormones, migration, or other biology.

They do not establish universal differences in intelligence, worth, courage, skill potential, social role, or authority. Individuals vary, cultures interpret biology differently, and magic, evolution, disability, age, and environment may alter expression.

### Size and Mass

Size and mass affect reach, inertia, pressure, leverage, concealment, resource demand, heat exchange, structural support, transport, and environmental access.

Larger is not universally stronger. A large body may move greater loads while facing slower turning, higher food demand, weaker surfaces, confined spaces, or difficulty shedding heat. A smaller body may exploit acceleration, concealment, relative strength, or access while lacking reach and absolute force.

### Natural Weapons and Defenses

Claws, horns, fangs, mandibles, tails, shells, venom systems, electric organs, breath structures, and similar traits are parts of the current body. Physical Development may condition and coordinate them within species limits.

Possessing a natural weapon does not grant combat technique, safe toxin handling, tactical judgment, or automatic superiority. Damage, regrowth, maintenance, and legal or social consequences follow the relevant body and world rules.

### Locomotion

Walking, climbing, slithering, burrowing, swimming, flying, drifting, rolling, teleport-assisted movement, and modular self-assembly pose different physical problems.

Development is mode-specific while transferable principles may exist. Endurance from swimming may support cardiovascular rebuilding in a human, but it does not provide land balance. A former flier may understand air currents while lacking wings and must use tools or another valid method to act on that knowledge.

### Environmental Adaptation

Bodies adapt only within valid biological, material, magical, or artificial routes. Heat, cold, altitude, pressure, gravity, current, toxins, radiation, mana density, and other conditions can create meaningful adaptation when exposure is survivable, recoverable, and integrated.

Exposure alone does not guarantee resistance. Excessive exposure may injure or kill, and repeated safe exposure eventually produces maintenance or diminishing returns.

## Health, Training, and Recovery

### Training Cycle

Useful physical training normally contains:

1. a defined capability and present baseline;
2. a relevant stimulus that challenges but does not merely destroy the body;
3. feedback about performance and error;
4. adequate recovery, nourishment, sleep, and repair;
5. integration demonstrated by changed capability or reliability;
6. later variation or increased demand once the adaptation stabilizes.

The correct stimulus depends on anatomy and objective. Human exercises cannot be assumed safe or effective for dragons, insects, aquatic beings, undead, or constructed bodies.

### Meaningful Strain

Strain is meaningful when it exposes a current limitation and the body can respond through a valid adaptive process. More pain, danger, weight, repetitions, or duration is not automatically more useful.

Training may be intense and still fail because it targets the wrong function, lacks recovery, uses poor technique, exceeds structural limits, or repeats an already integrated lesson.

### Diminishing Returns and Plateaus

Repeated use of one solved stimulus produces progressively less new adaptation. Further growth may require changed load, movement, environment, feedback, recovery, technique, equipment, or a genuinely different problem.

A Plateau may reflect:

- the current method no longer challenging the intended dimension;
- a supporting dimension becoming the bottleneck;
- inadequate recovery or resources;
- injury, illness, fear, or poor calibration;
- a current anatomical or lifecycle limit;
- a technique problem owned by another Track;
- an absent magical, species, or artificial route.

Greater danger is not a universal breakthrough method. Some limits remain until the body changes through a valid external system or another incarnation offers a different route.

### Overtraining

Overtraining occurs when repeated demand exceeds useful recovery and integration. It may produce fatigue, declining performance, sleep disruption, illness, damaged structures, impaired judgment, fear, or regression.

Overtraining is not a hidden shortcut. Recovery from it restores condition and may teach better regulation, but repeatedly causing the same breakdown cannot be farmed for extra Stat XP or resilience.

### Nutrition and Sleep

Adaptation requires whatever energy, material, maintenance, rest cycle, or magical equivalent the body uses. Food is not universal: constructs may need replacement parts, undead may require a binding source, and elementals may require environmental contact.

Scarcity may shape a different viable body and training plan. It does not make ignored requirements disappear.

## Injury, Disability, Illness, and Scars

### Injury

Injury changes Current Condition and may reduce, redirect, or prevent expression. Severe injury may also cause regression when supporting structures are permanently lost or altered.

Injury grants no automatic Development, resistance, or Stat XP. Rehabilitation can produce legitimate adaptation when it requires new coordination, understanding, accommodation, or integration, but reopening the same wound does not multiply that benefit.

### Disability and Accommodation

Disability describes a body-environment relationship and may be congenital, acquired, fluctuating, species-relative, or created by an environment built for another form.

A disabled character may possess high Development, use accommodations, develop alternate techniques, or outperform a nondisabled character in relevant contexts. Accommodation does not need to imitate an assumed standard body to be legitimate.

Rules should identify the actual capability, barrier, pain, fatigue, access, and support involved rather than reducing disability to a universal penalty or inspirational bonus.

### Illness

Illness may affect energy, coordination, senses, recovery, cognition, tissue, magical channels, or other functions. It can be acute, chronic, recurrent, contagious, environmental, magical, or material.

Surviving illness does not guarantee immunity or growth. Adaptation, immunity, treatment, persistent damage, and recurrence belong to the illness and body rules that establish them.

### Scars

Scars are evidence of repair or alteration. They may affect movement, sensation, flexibility, strength, pain, appearance, recognition, or nothing mechanically significant.

The presence, number, or dramatic appearance of scars does not measure resilience, experience, courage, or power. Spiritual or magical scars require their own established rules and should not be confused with ordinary tissue repair.

## Altered Bodies and External Enhancement

### Mutation

Mutation changes anatomy or function through a species, environmental, magical, corruptive, or other established process. It may open new Body Potential while closing old routes or creating costs.

Mutation is not earned Physical Development merely because it is beneficial. The body must still calibrate, condition, and learn to use the changed structure. Detailed mutation rules belong to later systems.

### Magical Enhancement

Magical enhancement may temporarily or persistently alter physical expression. Track the source, duration, body strain, compatibility, suppression conditions, and what remains after the effect ends.

If the magic does all the work, it produces magical capability rather than trained bodily capacity. If the body adapts around a stable enhancement through meaningful practice, each Owning System records only its own change.

### Artificial Augmentation

Prostheses, implanted tools, replacement organs, armor interfaces, modular limbs, alchemical structures, and constructed components may extend or restore capability.

The augmentation supplies a real embodied route while present. Effective use may require calibration, maintenance, power, compatible anatomy, skill, repair, and social access. Ordinary equipment does not become soul-persistent merely because the body trained with it.

### Temporary Transformation

A temporary form supplies its own anatomy and Current Condition for its duration. Practice in that form can improve calibration and relevant skills when meaningful, but it does not permanently alter the base body's tissues unless the transformation rule says so.

Repeated transformation cannot be used to claim every form's maximum capacity simultaneously. Access, transition cost, duration, recovery, and the active form remain real limits.

## Stat XP and Physical Rebuilding

All legitimately earned Stat XP persists under [Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md). Its physical role is to preserve developmental familiarity, not a former body's result.

Retained Stat XP may support:

- earlier recognition of useful training and harmful error;
- faster initial calibration in a closely compatible body;
- more efficient rebuilding of previously integrated fundamentals;
- better recovery of pacing, balance, coordination, and internal regulation;
- discovery of a body-appropriate analogue when functional correspondence exists;
- reaching a former kind of challenge with fewer wasted attempts.

It cannot:

- assign starting statistics;
- mature a newborn, hatchling, larva, seed body, or unfinished construct instantly;
- create muscle, shell, bone, organs, channels, fuel, or replacement parts without their normal route;
- preserve Current Condition, adult size, prior health, or trained tissue through Final Death;
- turn one Physical Dimension into an unrelated one;
- grant technique, profession, species traits, mutation, evolution, or magical authority;
- reproduce a capability in a body with no functional correspondence;
- multiply itself merely because the soul rebuilds the same capacity in another life.

The exact categories, pacing, transfer loss, and acceleration curves remain owned by the later Stat XP and Retained Development rules. Until then, use qualitative judgments and preserve current-life effort.

## Transfer Between Different Bodies

Prior experience transfers by function rather than appearance.

### Transfer Procedure

1. **Name the former function:** What physical problem did the old body solve?
2. **Name the source structures:** Which limbs, proportions, senses, nerves, organs, materials, or magical structures produced it?
3. **Name the present route:** What current structures could serve a comparable function?
4. **Assess correspondence:** Is the route close, analogical, tool-assisted, magically assisted, or absent?
5. **Recalibrate:** Train perception, timing, force, balance, and recovery in the present body.
6. **Demonstrate reliability:** Test the new expression under meaningful variation and pressure.
7. **Preserve provenance:** Record what came from retained familiarity and what the current body newly developed.

### Limbs and Proportions

Different limb counts, joint arrangements, reach, leverage, and proportions change movement solutions. A four-armed life may leave useful coordination familiarity while a two-armed incarnation must develop a new sequencing pattern. A tail cannot be treated as a spare arm unless its anatomy and training support that function.

### Centres of Gravity and Balance

Stance and movement depend on mass distribution, support points, buoyancy, anchoring, and acceleration. A centaur body, serpent body, winged body, upright human, floating elemental, and wheeled construct require different balance models.

### Senses and Nervous Systems

Signal range, resolution, delay, filtering, integration, and conscious access differ across bodies. Prior expertise may guide attention, but the current nervous or control system must learn its own signals.

A distributed colony, hive mind permitted by another rule, artificial control network, or undead binding may not possess a centralized nervous system. Use its established functional equivalent rather than assuming human reflexes.

### Movement Modes and Natural Weapons

Running expertise may support pacing but not flight control. A former bite fighter may understand commitment distance yet need new physical and skill development to use a spear. Natural weapons require their own conditioning, range judgment, maintenance, and safe recovery.

### Magical Organs

Magical organs or channels are present structures owned jointly by body and magic rules. Prior familiarity may help identify their function, but it cannot create them, set their capacity, repair them, or bypass the magical laws of the current Age.

## Physical Assessment

Assess the body through a scoped Capability Profile rather than one physical rating.

For the present objective, identify:

1. relevant Physical Dimensions;
2. Body Potential that matters now;
3. demonstrated Trained Capacity;
4. Current Condition;
5. movement mode, anatomy, size, mass, and environment;
6. supporting or limiting skills, tools, magic, species traits, and allies;
7. reliability under the expected duration and pressure;
8. uncertainty, hidden features, or unfamiliar biology;
9. consequences of failure or overexertion.

Comparisons should be specific:

> The ogre can produce greater force, but the smaller climber is better calibrated for the narrow, unstable shaft and can sustain the required grip longer.

Avoid claims such as "physically superior" unless the exact dimensions and conditions are named. Even a useful athletic rank or species threat class is an in-world model with limited scope.

## Why Strength Does Not Decide Combat

Combat requires an objective, not merely bodies colliding. Outcomes may turn on:

- range, timing, accuracy, and technique;
- footing, cover, concealment, altitude, water, air, or confined space;
- armor, weapons, traps, medicine, and other tools;
- information, preparation, surprise, and morale;
- species compatibility, toxins, resistances, and vulnerabilities;
- coordination, leadership, allies, and escape routes;
- fatigue, injury, pain, fear, and willingness to continue;
- whether the objective is killing, delaying, rescuing, escaping, capturing, or protecting.

Greater force is decisive only when the situation permits it to be applied. Making agility, speed, regeneration, or intelligence universally decisive instead would repeat the same design error under another name.

## Safeguards

### Exercise Grinding

Safe repetition may maintain capability and refine consistency. Once the body has integrated the available lesson, unchanged repetition produces diminishing new Development. Further growth requires a relevant change in demand, feedback, recovery, environment, or method.

### Injury Farming

Damage does not award adaptation. The body may heal, scar, regress, or die. Rehabilitation may develop a new capability, but repeated self-injury, regeneration loops, and deliberately reopening wounds cannot produce unlimited resilience or Stat XP.

### Death Farming

Final Death ends the body. It does not convert that body's capacity, injuries, unused potential, or dramatic death into bonus Physical Development. Life Reconciliation preserves only progression legitimately earned before death.

### Infinite Training

Every body faces recovery, resources, time, maturation, attention, current limits, and diminishing returns. Soul Space and Interlife do not provide stopped-time physical training. A method that produces no new adaptation cannot be repeated forever for growth.

### Body Bypass

Memories, Echoes, Resonance, Soul Depth, Soul Titles, Avatar Expression, Archive Records, and Stat XP cannot create missing anatomy or waive present maturation and calibration.

### Physical Dominance

No Physical Dimension is a universal victory statistic. Strong capability should matter when causally applicable, while counters, objectives, context, and tradeoffs remain real.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- universal physical attributes or a mandatory statistic list;
- numerical Stat XP rates, transfer coefficients, growth curves, or ceilings;
- exact biology, dimorphism, lifecycle, natural weapons, senses, or limits of every species;
- combat damage, initiative, action resolution, or universal threat ratings;
- skill techniques, profession ranks, spell effects, or social consequences;
- mutations, evolution branches, corruption, purification, or species transformations;
- the complete magic, disease, injury, disability, prosthetic, or artificial-body systems;
- automatic persistence for augmentations, transformations, or equipment.

Later systems may define these details within their domains. They must preserve the separation among Persistent Potential, Current Access, Embodied Expression, Practised Reliability, Contextual Effectiveness, and World Recognition.

## GM Procedure

When resolving physical training, transfer, or performance:

1. name the exact Physical Dimension and objective;
2. identify Body Potential, Trained Capacity, and Current Condition separately;
3. identify the anatomy, lifecycle, environment, resources, and recovery involved;
4. distinguish bodily adaptation from technique, magic, species traits, tools, and reputation;
5. test whether the stimulus creates meaningful adaptation or only repetition, damage, or maintenance;
6. apply fatigue, injury, illness, overtraining, accommodation, and rehabilitation honestly;
7. identify any retained Stat XP without treating it as current statistics;
8. assess functional correspondence before transferring prior experience;
9. require present calibration and demonstrated reliability;
10. describe effectiveness only for the current objective and context;
11. record what changed in the body and what, if anything, qualifies as persistent Stat XP;
12. defer formulas, species evolution, skill unlocks, and magic effects to their Owning Systems.

## Worked Examples

### Human Rebuilding Adult Strength

A former human soldier reincarnates as a human infant with extensive relevant Stat XP. The child may develop coordination early, recognize safe posture, and later respond efficiently to training. It does not possess adult muscle, bone tolerance, reach, cardiovascular capacity, or battlefield reliability.

Years of growth, nourishment, sleep, play, instruction, and progressive training remain necessary. The former life changes the developmental route and reduces wasted effort without making childhood cosmetic.

### Dragon in a Smaller Body

A dragon soul reincarnates as a small humanoid. It remembers bracing against impact and regulating a breath organ, but the new body has different ribs, mass, balance, and no draconic organ.

The old force and breath capacities do not transfer. Physical Calibration may recover useful posture and breath pacing. A future magic or skill rule might translate the principle, but ordinary training cannot create dragon anatomy.

### Insect Coordination

A human craftsperson reincarnates as a six-limbed insect monster. Fine-control potential exists, but two-handed habits initially cause interference because the nervous system coordinates three limb pairs and compound sensory input.

Retained familiarity helps the incarnation recognize sequencing errors. Meaningful multi-limb practice develops new coordination. Extra limbs provide more possible contact points, not automatic extra actions or mastery.

### Aquatic Predator on Land

An aquatic predator reincarnates as a terrestrial humanoid. Its endurance familiarity and current-reading instinct remain Persistent Potential, but buoyancy, propulsion, breathing, and balance are radically different.

Land training develops a new gait and load tolerance. With a boat, sail, or water magic, old environmental judgment may become Contextually Effective. It does not become running capacity merely because both activities involve endurance.

### Undead Vessel

An undead body feels no ordinary fatigue and does not breathe, but its binding weakens when joints crack and necromantic energy is poorly distributed. Pain tolerance is irrelevant; structural monitoring and binding regulation are the valid dimensions.

Endless repetition still produces no automatic growth. The body requires repair material, suitable magic, calibration, and a real adaptive route. Lack of pain can make hidden damage more dangerous rather than granting resilience.

### Artificial Body

A reincarnated athlete awakens in an artificial body with modular limbs. The frame arrives with designed force limits, sensor delays, maintenance requirements, and no biological recovery.

Prior Stat XP helps identify pacing and balance problems, but the soul must calibrate each module and cannot train metal to grow like muscle. Replacing a limb changes Body Potential and Current Condition; software, tools, energy supply, and maintenance remain separate dependencies.

### Strength Is Not the Matchup

A massive subterranean monster can crush a human in open contact. The human cannot become stronger before the encounter, but maps a narrow salt chimney, prepares anchors, uses the monster's poor upward vision, and coordinates two climbers.

The monster remains stronger. The humans become favored for the extraction objective because preparation, tools, terrain, coordination, and route choice prevent that strength from being applied directly.
