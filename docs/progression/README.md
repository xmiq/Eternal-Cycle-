# Progression Rules

This section contains the canonical Development System. It replaces a universal character level with distinct but interacting Development Tracks and keeps retained history separate from present access, embodiment, reliability, context, and recognition.

## Reading Order

1. [Development System](DEVELOPMENT_SYSTEM.md) - establishes the six-layer capability model, earned growth, diminishing returns, mastery boundaries, retained redevelopment, and the prohibition on universal levels or currencies.
2. [Physical Development](PHYSICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md) - defines embodied growth, body potential, trained capacity, current condition, physical calibration, transfer, and current-life rebuilding.
3. [Skill Development](SKILL_DEVELOPMENT.md) - defines Skills, techniques, Skill XP, acquisition, mastery evidence, maintenance, adaptive emergence, and bounded cross-tree translation.
4. [Profession Development](PROFESSION_DEVELOPMENT.md) - defines integrated bodies of practice, professional judgment, tools, standards, credentials, institutions, responsibility, and reincarnational retraining.
5. [Magical Development](MAGICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md) - defines shared principles for magical access, perception, control, capacity, affinity, authority, relearning, and system-specific embodiment without replacing the later Magic phase.
6. [Social and Leadership Development](SOCIAL_AND_LEADERSHIP_DEVELOPMENT.md) - defines communication, cultural calibration, relationships, reputation, authority, legitimacy, coordination, governance, and social agency safeguards.
7. [Species Development](SPECIES_DEVELOPMENT.md) - defines current-form traits, maturation, lifecycle, body-changing routes, ecology, distinct human and monster progression, and bounded reincarnational crossover without implementing evolution trees.
8. [Stat XP and Retained Development](STAT_XP_AND_RETAINED_DEVELOPMENT.md) - defines domain-specific soul-carried familiarity, Compatibility Profiles, Transfer Loss, latent access, bounded relearning acceleration, and anti-farming rules.
9. [Development Interactions](DEVELOPMENT_INTERACTIONS.md) - defines effect ownership, synergy, dependencies, incompatibility, substitution, compensation, bottlenecks, external support, and group capability without merging Tracks.
10. [Capability Assessment](CAPABILITY_ASSESSMENT.md) - provides scoped qualitative profiles, evidence states, uncertainty, observer perspectives, comparative findings, and purpose-bound institutional measures.
11. [Development Safeguards](DEVELOPMENT_SAFEGUARDS.md) - consolidates legitimate-growth tests, anti-exploit rules, causal failure states, proportional consequences, and protections for experimentation and agency.

## Phase 2 Boundaries

Later phases may add local tree schemas, ranks, formulas, content, procedures, and institutions. They must preserve:

- the separation of Persistent Potential, Current Access, Embodied Expression, Practised Reliability, Contextual Effectiveness, and World Recognition;
- one Owning System for each claimed effect and no duplicate gain from overlapping descriptions;
- distinct Physical, Skill, Profession, Magical, Social, Leadership, Species, and retained Development;
- current-life effort, valid present routes, embodiment, compatibility, recovery, and evidence of integrated change;
- separate human and monster progression trees unless a bounded crossover rule explicitly permits a receiving-tree expression;
- qualitative, scoped capability assessment instead of a universal level, combat rating, power score, or progression currency;
- diminishing returns, Plateaus, maintenance, regression, and safeguards against grinding, farming, recursive retained gain, and unrestricted trait stacking.

Phase 3 may now define Skill trees without inventing new foundational Development rules. It may specialize these boundaries but may not silently replace them.
