# Stat XP and Retained Development

## Purpose

This document defines how legitimately earned developmental familiarity persists through Reincarnation without preserving a former body or replacing current-life effort. It is the canonical Development rule for Stat XP, compatibility, transfer loss, and accelerated rebuilding.

Stat XP is **persistent, soul-carried developmental familiarity within a bounded domain**. It records that a soul has previously integrated a kind of adaptation deeply enough to make compatible redevelopment more efficient. It is Persistent Potential, not a current statistic.

The term **XP** does not imply a spendable pool, a universal point economy, or a mandatory numerical interface. A campaign may record Stat XP qualitatively or use local values later, but every representation must preserve the rules in this document.

## Core Rule

Statistics, bodily capacities, active Channels, and Current Condition belong to an incarnation. Stat XP belongs to the continuing soul.

When a current incarnation follows a valid developmental route, compatible Stat XP may help it:

- recognize productive stimuli and avoid familiar mistakes;
- calibrate practice sooner;
- integrate feedback more efficiently;
- regain previously understood foundations with less wasted effort;
- identify a body-appropriate analogue when literal recovery is impossible;
- approach a former depth of adaptation more deliberately.

Stat XP never supplies the adaptation itself. The current body or metaphysical structure must still mature, train, recover, integrate change, and establish Practised Reliability.

## Terms

### Retained Development

The protected developmental history that persists through its proper Owning System. Stat XP, Skill Imprints, Retained Instincts, integrated knowledge, Soul Titles, and other forms of persistence remain distinct; Retained Development is not one pooled resource.

### Developmental Familiarity

Integrated experience with how a particular capability is built, regulated, corrected, and recovered. Familiarity can guide redevelopment even when the former result no longer exists.

### Stat XP Domain

A bounded kind of developmental familiarity with a stated function and provenance, such as sustained aerial exertion, fine multi-limb coordination, low-light sensory discrimination, or stabilizing a particular kind of magical Channel. Domains may overlap functionally, but they do not have a universal exchange rate.

### Compatibility Profile

A qualitative account of how a Stat XP Domain corresponds to the current body, species, lifecycle, metaphysical structures, environment, and developmental route. Compatibility is contextual rather than a permanent percentage attached to a soul.

### Transfer Loss

The familiarity that cannot be expressed as useful acceleration because the current route differs from the original route. Transfer Loss is not deletion: later forms or conditions may make more of the retained familiarity relevant.

### Latent Stat XP

Stat XP that persists but presently has no valid developmental route or functional correspondence. It grants no current output merely because it exists.

### Inaccessible Stat XP

Stat XP for which a potentially compatible route exists but Current Access is blocked or obscured by memory limits, low Resonance, damaged Soul Integrity, suppression, missing instruction, or another established access condition.

### Redevelopment Acceleration

The bounded reduction of rediscovery, calibration, or integration waste when current effort acts through a compatible route. Acceleration changes the rebuilding process; it does not set a starting statistic or guarantee a finishing point.

### Training Efficiency

How effectively valid current effort, feedback, resources, and recovery become useful adaptation. Stat XP may improve efficiency within a compatible domain, but cannot make invalid, trivial, unsafe, or absent training productive.

### Developmental Ceiling

The present limit on a route under the body's anatomy, lifecycle, health, species, materials, environment, magical laws, and established modifications. Stat XP may help an incarnation approach a ceiling; it does not ignore or automatically raise it.

## Stat XP and the Six Layers

Stat XP must be interpreted through the [six-layer capability model](DEVELOPMENT_SYSTEM.md#the-six-layer-capability-model).

| Layer | Stat XP question |
| --- | --- |
| **Persistent Potential** | Which Stat XP Domains and prior developmental routes genuinely persist? |
| **Current Access** | Which retained patterns, warnings, and methods can this incarnation presently reach? |
| **Embodied Expression** | Which compatible adaptations can the current body or metaphysical structure actually build and express? |
| **Practised Reliability** | Which rebuilt capacities have been stabilized through current practice, recovery, and varied use? |
| **Contextual Effectiveness** | Which present capacities matter for this objective, environment, opposition, toolset, and timescale? |
| **World Recognition** | What do observers or institutions believe the incarnation can do, and what evidence do they accept? |

High Stat XP can coexist with low access, an unsuitable body, poor health, immature organs, little recent practice, or no public recognition. None of those layers can be inferred from the retained history alone.

## What Stat XP Is Not

Stat XP is not:

- a direct or permanent body statistic;
- instant strength, endurance, coordination, sensory acuity, mana capacity, or resistance;
- proof of current competence or reliability;
- a preserved body template;
- unrestricted transfer across species, anatomies, materials, or magical systems;
- a universal currency for Skills, traits, magic, professions, Soul systems, or unrelated Stat XP Domains;
- a reward for elapsed time, suffering, danger, injury, killing, or death by themselves;
- an efficient product of trivial repetition;
- a guarantee that a former ceiling remains reachable;
- permission to skip maturation, instruction, equipment, nourishment, recovery, or practice.

## Descriptive Domain Families

The following families help organize Stat XP. They are not a mandatory attribute list and should be divided into more specific domains whenever their routes differ materially.

### Physical Familiarity

Familiarity with adapting force production, resilience, recovery, flexibility, breath, temperature regulation, or another bodily function. "Physical" is too broad to be one interchangeable pool.

### Coordination Familiarity

Familiarity with calibrating timing, balance, proprioception, fine motor control, limb sequencing, movement transitions, or sensorimotor integration. Two-handed sword control and six-limbed web construction may share principles without being the same domain.

### Endurance Familiarity

Familiarity with pacing, sustained output, repeated effort, resource regulation, and recovery in a particular mode. Flight, swimming, burrowing, marching, and maintaining an undead binding are not automatically equivalent.

### Sensory Familiarity

Familiarity with filtering, comparing, and integrating a kind of sensory input. It cannot create an absent eye, echolocation organ, vibration-sensitive web, magical sense, or suitable nervous system.

### Magical-Channel Familiarity

Familiarity with developing, conditioning, regulating, and recovering a particular kind of magical Channel or equivalent embodied structure. It is not spell knowledge, affinity, reserves, authority, or permission to use a different magic system.

### Discipline Familiarity

Familiarity with the bodily or metaphysical regulation that supports sustained practice: attention control, tension release, breath timing, pain-aware pacing, recovery habits, or similar foundations. Discipline Familiarity assists practice; it is not obedience, willpower currency, or a substitute for a Skill.

A useful record names the function, source embodiment, conditions, and evidence instead of writing only "high Physical Stat XP."

## How Stat XP Is Gained

Stat XP records **integrated adaptation**, not raw exposure. A valid gain normally includes the following elements in some form:

1. **Relevant route:** the incarnation possesses a real bodily or metaphysical route capable of adapting.
2. **Meaningful challenge:** the stimulus demands adjustment relative to current capability without requiring arbitrary danger.
3. **Engagement:** the character actively attempts, attends, chooses, regulates, or responds rather than merely being subjected to an event.
4. **Feedback:** success, error, sensation, instruction, observation, or consequence provides information about the attempt.
5. **Adaptation:** the current system changes how it performs, regulates, perceives, or recovers.
6. **Recovery:** the change is repaired, stabilized, consolidated, or otherwise made sustainable for that form.
7. **Integration:** the character demonstrates that the change can inform later meaningful use rather than existing as one accidental output.

No universal count of repetitions, injuries, victories, years, or near-death experiences proves that these conditions occurred.

### Meaningful Strain

Strain can be useful when it tests an appropriate route and remains recoverable. Greater pain or risk does not mean greater Stat XP. Strain beyond the capacity to adapt may cause fatigue, injury, maladaptation, trauma, corruption, or death instead.

### Reflection and Instruction

Reflection can help interpret feedback, distinguish signal from habit, and choose a better next stimulus. Instruction can expose methods and error models. Neither creates tissue, Channels, sensory resolution, or reliable regulation without present use and integration.

### Successful Use and Failure

Successful performance can confirm an adaptation, while partial failure can reveal a productive correction. Either may contribute when real learning and integration occur. Repeating an already solved action with no meaningful variation usually maintains capability rather than producing substantial new Stat XP.

## Diminishing Returns and Plateaus

Stat XP follows the Development System's qualitative diminishing returns.

- Early competent adaptation in a new domain may establish broad familiarity quickly.
- Rebuilding familiar foundations can be efficient but does not award duplicate retained history merely for retracing them.
- Deeper gains require finer feedback, harder variation, better recovery, new constraints, or genuinely extended capability.
- Routine repetition increasingly supports maintenance and reliability rather than unlimited growth.
- Near a current Developmental Ceiling, greater effort may produce little adaptation or cause damage.
- A Plateau calls for diagnosis, altered methods, instruction, rest, environmental change, or acceptance of a current limit; it is not defeated automatically by more suffering.

Stat XP does not recursively multiply itself. Rebuilding a familiar capacity can restore present expression efficiently, but only genuinely new integrated adaptation can deepen the retained domain.

## Compatibility and Transfer

Transfer is evaluated by function, not by label. The GM compares:

- the original structure, movement, sense, Channel, or regulatory system;
- the current structure and its actual developmental route;
- body plan, proportions, scale, centre of gravity, nervous organization, and material composition;
- species traits, lifecycle stage, cognition, and instinct;
- environment, gravity, atmosphere, pressure, available energy, and magical laws;
- whether the former and current tasks solve meaningfully related problems;
- whether current practice supplies feedback capable of translating the familiarity.

Compatibility may be described qualitatively:

- **close:** much of the former route corresponds, though present calibration and training remain necessary;
- **analogical:** principles correspond but must be translated through a materially different route;
- **distant:** only narrow warnings, pacing principles, or problem-recognition transfer;
- **incompatible:** no current route can use the familiarity, so it remains latent.

These labels describe a specific claim, not the whole relationship between two species. A dragon and human may be close in visual tracking, distant in load-bearing posture, and incompatible in wing endurance.

### Transfer Loss

Transfer Loss can arise from:

- missing limbs, organs, senses, Channels, materials, or energy sources;
- different control architecture, response delays, or cognition;
- incompatible lifecycle stage or developmental timing;
- changed gravity, atmosphere, ecology, magic, or physical law;
- old habits that conflict with the present route;
- incomplete Current Access or misleading memory;
- a former method that was narrow, obsolete, or dependent on unavailable support.

Transfer Loss cannot be paid away with unrelated Stat XP. It is addressed, where possible, through current experimentation, accommodations, new Skills, tools, magic, transformations, or a later compatible incarnation.

## Access, Suppression, Injury, and Change

### Latent and Inaccessible Familiarity

Latent Stat XP has no valid present route. Inaccessible Stat XP has a possible route but cannot presently be reached. Neither is erased merely because it is unused.

Soul Resonance, a Soul Echo, instruction, recovery of Soul Integrity, or an appropriate Trigger may improve Current Access. Such access reveals guidance; it does not complete the current adaptation.

### Injury and Suppression

Injury, illness, fatigue, magical suppression, damaged Channels, corrupted interfaces, and Soul Strain can reduce current access, expression, recovery, or reliability. They do not normally erase legitimate Stat XP. They may make former methods unsafe or temporarily unusable.

Recovery and rehabilitation are current-life processes. Prior familiarity can help pace them, but repeated injury is not a shortcut and may permanently lower the body's present ceiling.

### Age and Maturation

An infant, larva, juvenile, unfinished construct, newly raised undead, or immature magical body can use only routes its stage supports. Stat XP may improve safe exploration and later training response, but cannot create adult dimensions, completed metamorphosis, mature organs, or years of structural growth.

Passive aging does not award Stat XP. Maturation changes available routes; meaningful use and adaptation develop them.

### Altered Worlds and Magical Laws

Across Ages, familiar environments, materials, gravity, mana behavior, channel architecture, or metaphysical laws may change. Old Stat XP remains historical potential, but its Compatibility Profile must be reassessed.

Magical-channel familiarity from one Age may offer error recognition or regulation principles in another while its former exercises fail entirely. Developing a new compatible route can create new Stat XP; declaring the systems equivalent cannot.

## Reincarnation and Rebuilding

At Final Death:

- the body's current statistics, Trained Capacity, condition, organs, and species traits end with that incarnation;
- legitimately integrated Stat XP persists as a Soul Imprint;
- provenance, domain, compatibility limits, and ownership persist with it;
- injuries and fatigue do not become permanent soul statistics, though memories and other valid consequences may remain under their own systems.

In a later life, rebuilding still requires:

1. a present compatible route;
2. sufficient Current Access to useful familiarity;
3. age- and form-appropriate stimulus;
4. current effort and feedback;
5. nourishment, materials, energy, rest, or repair appropriate to the embodiment;
6. calibration to the current form;
7. stabilization through varied practice;
8. honest respect for current ceilings and consequences.

A campaign may later use local growth stages or formulas to express this process. Such rules must remain domain-specific, compatibility-sensitive, bounded against recursive acceleration, and incapable of creating a universal character level.

## Interaction with Other Systems

### Physical Development

[Physical Development](PHYSICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md) owns adaptation built in the current body. Stat XP may improve the route by which that adaptation is rebuilt; it does not own current force, speed, endurance, coordination, senses, recovery, or condition.

### Skills

[Skill Development](SKILL_DEVELOPMENT.md) owns learned methods and Skill XP. Stat XP may rebuild supporting regulation or sensorimotor familiarity, while a Skill may make training safer and more precise. The same practice can create both only when there is distinct evidence for bodily adaptation and learned execution; neither receives duplicate credit for the other's result.

### Species Development

[Species Development](SPECIES_DEVELOPMENT.md) owns current traits, maturation, lifecycle, and valid body-changing routes. Stat XP cannot purchase, preserve, or imitate a Species Trait. A new form changes compatibility and may expose or close developmental routes.

### Retained Instincts

[Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) provide preconscious orientation toward patterns. Stat XP records integrated developmental familiarity. An instinct may direct attention toward a useful response without providing the current adaptation; Stat XP may improve rebuilding without supplying an automatic impulse.

### Soul Echoes

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may remember how a former body trained, failed, or recovered and can advise the current incarnation. An Echo cannot lend its old body, grant current statistics, duplicate Stat XP, or make incompatible familiarity usable by assertion.

### Soul Depth

[Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) may support the soul's capacity to bear and integrate a complex developmental history. It does not increase Stat XP values, make a domain compatible, or replace current adaptation.

### Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may improve access to existing Stat XP or reveal a relevant correspondence. It cannot create familiarity that was never earned, convert one domain into another, or supply present bodily output.

### Soul Weapons

A [Soul Weapon](../soul/SOUL_WEAPON_FOUNDATIONS.md) may provide feedback, pacing, resistance, or a stable training relationship. Weapon use can contribute to relevant Skill XP and Stat XP only through the wielder's own integrated development. The weapon's form, powers, and Weapon Soul remain separate owners and are not converted into the wielder's statistics.

## Safeguards

### No Suicide or Reincarnation Farming

Death records no automatic Stat XP. Ending a life early usually destroys opportunities for maturation, depth, reliable practice, relationships, and genuinely new adaptation. Repeated reincarnation does not multiply prior familiarity, refresh easy gains, or count rebuilding the same foundation as new retained progress.

### No Torture or Injury Optimization

Pain, deprivation, repeated wounds, forced healing, and near-death exposure do not produce Stat XP by intensity. They may cause maladaptation, reduced ceilings, trauma, corruption, impaired access, or Final Death. Legitimate resilience or recovery familiarity requires a valid route, agency where possible, successful adaptation, recovery, and meaningful later use.

### No Weak-Body Multiplier

Choosing a weak, immature, damaged, or incompatible body does not multiply gains merely because ordinary tasks feel harder. Challenge is measured against the possibility of productive adaptation, not suffering or relative disadvantage. A weak body may offer distinct experiences, but only genuinely integrated new development persists.

### No Trivial Repetition or Passive Time

Routine actions, elapsed age, sleep loops, effortless exercise, staged danger, and endlessly repeated solved tasks do not generate substantial new familiarity. Maintenance, recovery, or improved reliability may occur when appropriate, but they are not unlimited Stat XP.

### No Exponential Acceleration

Retained familiarity cannot train itself, earn interest, duplicate when recalled by multiple Echoes, or award fresh Stat XP merely because it accelerated rebuilding. Compatibility loss, current ceilings, diminishing returns, recovery, and present practice remain binding in every life.

### No Unrelated Conversion

Flight endurance cannot simply purchase legal judgment; pain regulation cannot become fire affinity; magical-channel familiarity cannot become sword Skill; sensory familiarity cannot buy a Species Trait. Functional analogy may support a bounded new route only through current experimentation and evidence.

## GM Procedure

When resolving a Stat XP claim:

1. name the exact Stat XP Domain and its original provenance;
2. identify the current developmental route and its Owning System;
3. separate Persistent Potential, Current Access, and current expression;
4. compare source and present functions through a Compatibility Profile;
5. identify Transfer Loss, latent portions, and access barriers;
6. verify meaningful present challenge, engagement, feedback, adaptation, recovery, and integration;
7. apply maturation, health, resources, environment, magical laws, and Developmental Ceilings;
8. distinguish Stat XP from Skill XP, traits, knowledge, tools, external power, and reputation;
9. apply diminishing returns and reject duplicate or recursive gains;
10. describe the acceleration qualitatively and record current capability separately;
11. preserve uncertainty where the character or observers lack evidence;
12. defer numerical curves and unrelated system effects to their Owning Systems.

## Worked Examples

### Compatible Human Reincarnation

A human distance runner dies after decades of varied training and reincarnates as a healthy human infant. Relevant pacing, gait-regulation, and endurance familiarity persist, but the infant has no adult heart, lungs, bones, muscles, stride, or nutritional reserves.

During childhood, ordinary play and age-appropriate training produce unusually efficient calibration. After maturation and years of current effort, the runner rebuilds endurance faster than a true novice. Repeating the former training plan does not duplicate decades of Stat XP; only new integrated adaptation beyond the familiar route deepens it.

### Dragon Reborn as a Small Humanoid

A dragon retains familiarity with bracing immense mass, regulating long flight, and stabilizing a breath organ. The humanoid body has neither wings nor that organ. Flight and breath-weapon domains remain latent, and draconic load-bearing transfers poorly.

Narrow principles of posture, breath pacing, and heat awareness may be analogically useful after experimentation. They accelerate body-appropriate training but do not grant dragon strength, fire, scales, or endurance.

### Aquatic Predator on Land

An aquatic predator reincarnates as a terrestrial humanoid. Swimming endurance does not become running endurance: buoyancy, propulsion, respiration, impact, and balance differ. Pacing discipline and sensitivity to flow may transfer distantly, while most locomotor familiarity suffers Transfer Loss.

The incarnation must develop a gait and load tolerance from the beginning. Near water, retained current-reading may become contextually useful through swimming, navigation, or weather observation without becoming a generic Perception statistic.

### Human Reborn as a Six-Limbed Monster

A human artisan with precise two-hand coordination reincarnates as a six-limbed insect monster. Fine-control familiarity helps identify sequencing errors, but old bilateral habits initially interfere with the new nervous organization.

Through current multi-limb practice, feedback, and recovery, the monster builds new coordination. The result is not six copies of human dexterity; it is a new domain shaped by the present body, with some old familiarity used as an analogical guide.

### Mage Across Changed Laws

A mage once conditioned biological mana Channels for a continuous ambient flow. After a Reset, magic arrives in pulses through mineral nodes, and the new artificial body uses etched conduits.

Former Magical-Channel Familiarity warns against overload and supports regulation experiments, but the old exercises do not work. The incarnation must learn node timing, maintain its conduits, and build current reliability. Some old familiarity remains latent; new conduit-specific Stat XP forms through present adaptation.

### Injury Does Not Become a Shortcut

A fighter repeatedly tears a tendon and uses healing magic to resume training immediately. Pain and repair cycles do not grant resilience. Incomplete recovery worsens movement, teaches compensatory habits, and lowers current capacity.

A later rehabilitation program can develop pacing, joint control, and recovery familiarity because the fighter engages with feedback and integrates safer function. The progress comes from rehabilitation, not from the quantity of damage.

### Weak-Body Farming Fails

A soul deliberately selects frail bodies and attempts dangerous labor, expecting every difficult movement to multiply strength Stat XP. The bodies lack the recovery, nourishment, and safe loading needed for productive adaptation. Most effort creates exhaustion, injury, or maintenance rather than new familiarity.

Even when a body adapts legitimately, rebuilding already familiar foundations does not recursively duplicate retained gain. A frail life can still matter through genuinely new methods, accommodations, choices, and consequences, but weakness itself is not a multiplier.

### One Event, Separate Owners

A novice repeatedly practises spear thrusts against moving targets. Better shoulder endurance and balance may create Physical Development and related Stat XP; improved distance judgment and thrust mechanics may create spear Skill XP; a teacher's tactical explanation may add knowledge.

Each gain needs its own evidence. The character does not receive three copies of the same improvement, and Stat XP cannot later be spent as spear mastery or tactical knowledge.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- a mandatory statistic list or character sheet;
- numerical Stat XP awards, transfer coefficients, growth curves, or caps;
- complete physical, magical, species, Skill, profession, or combat formulas;
- automatic evolutionary requirements or cross-tree unlocks;
- a universal measure of development, power, threat, or worth.

Later mechanics may add local measurements only when they preserve domain specificity, current embodiment, present effort, compatibility, diminishing returns, and non-recursive persistence.
