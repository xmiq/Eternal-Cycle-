# Development Interactions

## Purpose

This document defines how independent Development Tracks, Soul systems, tools, groups, and environments combine without merging their ownership or duplicating benefits.

Development is multidimensional because capability emerges from relationships among distinct capacities. A sword technique needs a body that can express it. A ritual may replace personal magical output with preparation and infrastructure. Leadership may coordinate many people without transferring their Skills to the leader. These interactions can be decisive while every contributing system remains separate.

## Core Rule

Every claimed effect has one **Owning System**. Other systems may change its access, available route, cost, scale, reliability, timing, consequence, or Contextual Effectiveness only where their own rules permit.

When several systems describe one event:

1. identify the exact effect being claimed;
2. assign that effect to one owner;
3. identify each supporting contribution and dependency;
4. satisfy every relevant requirement;
5. apply incompatibilities, costs, bottlenecks, and failure states;
6. award Development only for each participant's or Track's distinct integrated change;
7. do not duplicate an action, benefit, mastery, or persistent gain.

Interaction does not imply conversion. Physical capacity does not become Skill XP, a Species Trait does not become a learned technique, and institutional access does not become personal expertise merely because they work together.

## Terms

### Development Interaction

A causal relationship in which one Development Track, system, participant, tool, or condition changes how another owned effect can be accessed, expressed, sustained, or applied.

### Interaction Claim

The exact result being requested from two or more contributors, stated as an effect rather than a list of system names.

### Owning System

The canonical system that defines whether the claimed effect exists and governs its requirements, limits, persistence, and failure states.

### Supporting System

A system that supplies a relevant route, resource, method, access condition, constraint, or consequence without owning the claimed effect.

### Synergy

An interaction in which compatible contributors produce a useful result none could produce as effectively alone. Synergy follows ordinary causality and does not automatically multiply Development or output.

### Dependency

A requirement on which an effect relies. Loss of a required body structure, tool, resource, permission, participant, environment, or supporting capability may reduce or end the effect.

### Incompatibility

A conflict that prevents, narrows, destabilizes, or raises the cost of an intended interaction. Incompatibility can arise from anatomy, timing, materials, methods, instincts, culture, magical laws, permissions, objectives, or competing demands.

### Substitution

The use of a different valid route to satisfy a requirement. A substitution must be explicitly plausible under the Owning System and carries the limits and costs of the substitute.

### Compensation

Support that reduces the practical effect of a limitation without erasing it. A brace may compensate for weak grip; it does not create grip strength. Translation may compensate for a language gap; it does not grant cultural literacy.

### Bottleneck

The contributor that most strongly limits a stated result under current conditions. Bottlenecks are claim-specific and can shift when the objective, environment, resources, or participants change.

### Cascading Failure

A sequence in which failure of one dependency disrupts other contributors. A broken focus may destabilize a spell, which exhausts a caster, which breaks group timing. A cascade is a causal chain, not an automatic punishment.

### Temporary Enhancement

A time-, source-, condition-, or cost-bound increase in access or expression. It remains owned by its source and does not automatically become lasting Development.

### External Support

Capability supplied by another person, tool, institution, environment, contract, infrastructure, or prepared condition. External Support can be decisive while remaining unavailable when the source is absent or unwilling.

### Group Capability

What a particular group can accomplish through member capabilities, coordination, roles, relationships, resources, and conditions. It is not the sum of personal levels and does not belong wholly to a leader or any one member.

## Ownership Model

### Own the Effect, Not the Scene

One scene can contain several distinct effects with different owners.

A warrior crossing a collapsing bridge while carrying another person may involve:

- Physical Development owning current balance, load tolerance, and fatigue;
- Skill Development owning foot placement and rescue technique;
- Species Development owning wings, claws, or adhesive feet that provide routes;
- Magical Development owning a wind barrier;
- Profession Development owning structural judgment;
- Social and Leadership Development owning communication with the rescued person;
- the tool system owning a rope's properties;
- the World Engine owning weather, bridge failure, witnesses, and later consequences.

Each effect is resolved under its owner. The bridge crossing does not become one universal "adventure XP" award.

### Support Cannot Waive Ownership

A Supporting System can help only in the way it actually functions.

- Greater strength can support a heavy-weapon technique but does not grant the technique.
- Excellent technique can reduce wasted force but cannot make an impossible mass safe to wield.
- Magical reinforcement can increase present output but retains magical costs and suppression risks.
- A title can open a court without granting diplomacy or legal authority.
- An Echo can advise without performing the current incarnation's practice.
- a guild can supply tools and legitimacy without creating Professional Judgment.

### One Benefit Is Counted Once

If several systems describe the same improvement, assign the improvement to the narrowest appropriate owner. Supporting contributors may receive their own Development only when evidence shows a distinct change in their capability.

A coordinated spear drill might develop shoulder endurance, spear timing, formation discipline, and leadership communication. It does not award four copies of "combat ability," nor does every participant receive every other participant's gains.

### Persistent and Temporary Sources Stay Separate

Temporary enhancement, borrowed equipment, divine permission, environmental advantage, and group support may create exceptional present performance. They do not become Persistent Potential unless an explicit Owning System records a distinct, legitimately integrated change.

## Interaction Patterns

### Enabling

One contributor provides a route that another effect requires.

- wings enable aerial Skills;
- literacy enables use of written professional knowledge;
- a magical Channel enables a particular casting method;
- institutional membership enables access to restricted laboratories.

Enabling access is not mastery. A winged creature still learns flight control, and an admitted student still learns the Profession.

### Synergy

Contributors reinforce one another without becoming the same capability.

- better technique reduces physical waste, allowing endurance to matter longer;
- professional preparation makes a modest spell effective at the right location;
- cultural literacy makes negotiation Skill relevant to the audience;
- a Species Trait and a learned Skill combine into a body-specific hunting method.

Synergy is demonstrated through the actual interaction. A list of compatible labels does not guarantee it.

### Dependency

An effect may depend on:

- anatomy or a Species Trait;
- a minimum current capacity or condition;
- a Skill, Profession, language, or permission;
- a magical source, Channel, contract, or authority;
- a tool, material, prepared site, team, or institution;
- time, information, weather, terrain, ecology, or world law.

Dependencies should be visible enough that loss, refusal, damage, or substitution has understandable consequences.

### Incompatibility

Incompatibilities may be absolute for one claim or manageable through current adaptation. Examples include:

- a technique designed for joints the body lacks;
- armor that blocks a species' sensory organs;
- spell rhythms that conflict with a transformed Channel;
- two professions using mutually exclusive safety standards;
- an intimidation method that violates the target culture's expected signals;
- a leader's command cadence overwhelming echolocating allies.

The GM should state what conflicts and why. Incompatibility is not a generic penalty for unusual combinations.

### Substitution

A substitution changes the route, not the meaning of every prerequisite.

- a ritual circle may substitute infrastructure and time for personal magical capacity;
- a prosthetic grip may provide a weapon interface where biological fingers are absent;
- a translator may substitute immediate language access for direct conversation;
- maps and instruments may substitute for a missing navigation sense.

Substitution can introduce new dependencies, delays, errors, visibility, maintenance, or consent requirements. It does not automatically award Development in the replaced capability.

### Compensation

Compensation makes a limitation more manageable while keeping its consequences legible.

- a light blade compensates for low force production;
- scaffolding compensates for a climber's poor reach;
- a focus compensates for unstable magical precision;
- clear procedures compensate for a leader's limited improvisation;
- allies compensate for a specialist's narrow coverage.

Compensation may support meaningful agency and experimentation. It should not be treated as evidence that the underlying limitation vanished.

### Bottlenecks

The same character can have different bottlenecks for different objectives.

A powerful caster may be limited by precision during surgery, reserves during a siege, authority in a temple, language during instruction, or recovery during repeated travel. Improving a non-limiting Track may not change the result.

The GM identifies the bottleneck after stating the objective and current conditions. There is no permanent "weakest stat" that controls every scene.

### Cascading Failure

A cascade follows established dependencies:

1. a damaged wing reduces aerial stability;
2. unstable movement disrupts the rider's ritual gestures;
3. the interrupted barrier exposes the formation to wind;
4. broken formation reduces the leader's ability to coordinate retreat.

Each consequence belongs to its system and should allow interruption, adaptation, or recovery where plausible. A single failed roll or mistake does not justify unrelated total collapse.

### Temporary Enhancement

Temporary Enhancement may change Current Access, Embodied Expression, or immediate condition. It must retain:

- its source and owner;
- activation and end conditions;
- costs and recovery;
- compatibility and control requirements;
- suppression and failure states;
- consequences of overuse;
- a distinction from lasting Development.

Practising under enhancement may create real learning when the character remains engaged and can integrate it. Borrowed output itself is not retained capacity.

### External Support

External Support remains attributable and interruptible.

- tools have properties, maintenance, fit, and availability;
- people have agency, consent, fatigue, priorities, and independent Skills;
- institutions have rules, jurisdictions, resources, and political consequences;
- environments have changing conditions rather than permanent bonuses;
- patrons and gods retain the boundaries of their authority and agreements.

The beneficiary may develop Skill in using support without absorbing the support's capability.

### Group Capability

Group Capability depends on:

- member-specific competence and condition;
- role clarity and coverage;
- communication and shared models;
- trust, morale, legitimacy, and willingness;
- leadership, delegation, and adaptation;
- tools, logistics, information, and preparation;
- the objective, opposition, environment, and time available.

Leadership can improve selection, timing, information flow, coordination, and recovery. It does not multiply every member's output by a personal aura, add actions to the leader, or make followers' Development belong to the leader.

## Track Interaction Map

| Track | Common contributions | Common dependencies | Does not automatically provide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Physical** | force, endurance, movement, sensing, recovery, current bodily regulation | anatomy, health, maturation, environment, resources | technique, knowledge, traits, authority |
| **Skill** | method, timing, efficiency, adaptation, reliable execution | supporting body or interface, practice, feedback, tools | body capacity, credentials, species routes |
| **Profession** | preparation, judgment, standards, tool use, responsibility, institutional navigation | knowledge, Skills, tools, cases, authority, access | current physical output, legal permission everywhere |
| **Magical** | perception, control, constructed effects, reinforcement, substitution | source, Channels, law, cost, authority, environment | unrelated Development or permanent embodiment |
| **Social and Leadership** | communication, coordination, trust-building, negotiation, group organization | audience, culture, relationships, information, consent | mind control, personal combat ability, automatic legitimacy |
| **Species** | anatomy, traits, lifecycle routes, instincts, ecological possibilities | current form, maturation, environment, valid change routes | trained capacity, complete Skills, one prescribed culture |
| **Stat XP and Retained Development** | recognition, calibration, relearning efficiency, compatibility clues | provenance, Current Access, present route, practice, recovery | current statistics, unrestricted crossover, recursive gains |

This map is descriptive. It does not establish one required character architecture or resolve specific abilities without their narrower rules.

## Soul and World Support

Soul systems affect Development primarily through persistence, access, interpretation, identity, relationship, and symbolic consequences.

- [Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) routes legitimate persistence and replaces embodiment.
- [Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) supports integration capacity, not Track growth.
- [Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) can align access, not manufacture capability.
- [Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) can advise or demonstrate perspective, not transfer a finished body or Practised Reliability.
- [Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) can orient attention, not supply anatomy or complete Skills.
- [Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) can alter recognition and opportunity, not grant flat bonuses.
- [Soul Avatars](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) can synthesize bounded history, not bypass current embodiment or add actions.
- [Soul Weapons](../soul/SOUL_WEAPON_FOUNDATIONS.md) can cooperate, resist, teach, and provide a changing tool while remaining distinct persons.

The [World Engine](../world-engine/WORLD_ENGINE_OVERVIEW.md) owns external conditions and consequences: teachers, institutions, materials, ecology, logistics, law, war, weather, technology, public response, and adaptation to visible methods. Development does not force the world to supply a missing opportunity, and the World Engine does not grant mastery merely because events occur.

## Resolution Procedure

For any Development Interaction:

1. **State the objective.** Name what success means in the present scene or period.
2. **State the effect.** Describe the exact capability or outcome being claimed.
3. **Choose the owner.** Use the narrowest canonical system that defines that effect.
4. **List contributors.** Name supporting Tracks, Soul systems, participants, tools, institutions, and conditions.
5. **List dependencies.** Identify required routes, resources, permissions, bodies, time, and environments.
6. **Check access and expression.** Apply Persistent Potential, Current Access, Embodied Expression, and Practised Reliability separately.
7. **Test compatibility.** Identify synergy, incompatibility, substitution, compensation, and Transfer Loss.
8. **Find bottlenecks.** Determine what presently constrains the stated objective.
9. **Apply agency and ownership.** Preserve consent, distinct persons, independent actions, and source-specific costs.
10. **Resolve causally.** Apply the effect, costs, failure states, and possible cascades without inventing unrelated consequences.
11. **Award distinct Development.** Record only changes supported by each Track's evidence and reject duplicate benefits.
12. **Route persistence and world response.** Preserve only what the appropriate rules allow and let external consequences continue.

When evidence is incomplete, describe the uncertainty. Do not hide uncertainty inside an overall modifier or power score.

## Worked Examples

### Skilled Swordsman, Unsuitable Weapon

A swordsman has exceptional timing, distance judgment, and one-handed blade control but lacks the force, joint tolerance, and current conditioning to use a massive execution sword safely.

Sword Skill owns the technique; Physical Development owns present capacity; the weapon owns its mass and balance. The Skill reduces waste but cannot make the load harmless. A harness, lighter replica, magical reinforcement, or changed technique might compensate, each with new dependencies. Without them, physical capacity is the bottleneck.

### Powerful Monster Without Combat Skill

A newly matured monster has immense mass, claws, and explosive acceleration. Species Development owns the available traits and Physical Development owns conditioned output. The monster has little experience judging feints, protecting vulnerable joints, pacing a chase, or coordinating attacks.

Against an unprepared target its force is dangerous. Against a trained team, poor Skill and low Practised Reliability create openings. Losing does not erase its physical superiority; winning through one charge does not create combat mastery.

### Weak Mage With Ritual Infrastructure

A mage has precise theory and stable control but low personal reserves. With a surveyed site, charged stones, assistants, a written sequence, and hours of preparation, the mage completes a barrier impossible to improvise alone.

Magical Development owns control of the effect; Profession Development supports planning; tools and site supply resources; the group supplies coordinated roles. The ritual substitutes infrastructure for personal capacity, but becomes visible, slow, interruptible, and dependent on materials. The mage does not gain the stones' reserves as a permanent statistic.

### Leader With Limited Personal Combat Power

A commander is an average fighter but understands the unit's capabilities, keeps information moving, delegates local decisions, maintains morale, and chooses terrain that lets specialists cover one another.

The unit's Group Capability exceeds the commander's personal combat ability. Leadership owns coordination, not the members' attacks or Skills. If trust breaks, communication fails, or the formation scatters, the group's effectiveness changes without reducing the commander's personal Development to zero.

### Reincarnated Artisan Without Tools

A master glassworker reincarnates in a region without suitable furnaces, refined materials, or temperature instruments. Knowledge and Professional Judgment remain valuable, while current tools and local materials are the bottleneck.

The artisan can test clay, teach apprentices, redesign a kiln, bargain for fuel, or pursue magical substitution. Each route creates new costs and Development. Retained expertise does not summon infrastructure or guarantee that old recipes work under different materials and magic.

### Species Trait Enables a Skill

A spider monster possesses spinnerets and vibration-sensitive limbs. Those Species Traits enable web construction and remote tension sensing. Skill Development owns deliberate web patterns, trap design, maintenance, and reliable interpretation.

A human cannot learn the literal spinneret technique through observation. Tools or magic may create a substitution, but the resulting Skill has different dependencies and is not the monster trait copied into a human tree.

### Echo Advice Without Direct Mastery

A current incarnation faces a duelist style once studied by a prior-life Echo. The Echo recognizes a deceptive rhythm and warns that the apparent opening invites a counterattack.

The warning improves information and decision quality. It does not move the current body, grant timing, add an action, or establish Practised Reliability. The current fighter must understand the advice, possess a usable response, and execute it under pressure. Any Development belongs to the current Track evidence, not to repeating the Echo's former mastery.

### Cascading Failure With Recovery

A flying courier team relies on a beast's endurance, a rider's navigation Skill, a magical weather shield, and a leader's timing. A wing injury destabilizes flight; the caster spends more reserve correcting the shield; delayed arrival invalidates the planned route.

The cascade follows real dependencies, but it is not predetermined disaster. The group can land, redistribute cargo, seek shelter, substitute ground navigation, or abandon the original objective. Choices and consequences remain available at each stage.

## Safeguards

- Do not merge contributors into a universal score or shared XP pool.
- Do not let support provide an effect its owner forbids.
- Do not count one action, output, or adaptation several times.
- Do not infer mastery from access to a trait, tool, title, memory, or institution.
- Do not make synergy a free multiplier detached from actual causality.
- Do not erase dependencies after a successful use.
- Do not convert temporary enhancement into lasting Development automatically.
- Do not treat a bottleneck as a permanent weakness across all contexts.
- Do not use cascading failure to remove meaningful choices or impose unrelated punishment.
- Do not assign all group success or Development to a leader.
- Do not let Soul systems replace practice, embodiment, consent, or present action.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- a universal interaction formula, action economy, combat modifier, or power score;
- individual spells, Skills, traits, professions, tools, or species;
- exact group, morale, reputation, logistics, or institutional procedures;
- detailed temporary-enhancement durations or costs;
- the qualitative comparison framework, which is defined by [Capability Assessment](CAPABILITY_ASSESSMENT.md);
- numerical Development awards or conversion rates.

Later rules may refine interactions inside their domains. They must preserve effect ownership, independent contributors, visible dependencies, current embodiment, present effort, and no duplicate benefit.
