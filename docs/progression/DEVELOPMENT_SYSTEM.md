# Development System

## Core Rule

Eternal Cycle does not use one universal character level as the authoritative measure of power. Every domain develops independently.

## Development Domains

Examples include:

- bodily attributes;
- skills;
- professions;
- combat disciplines;
- senses;
- magic;
- species evolution;
- Soul Weapon growth;
- leadership;
- reputation;
- relationships;
- knowledge;
- instincts;
- Soul Resonance;
- Soul Depth.

Local ranks or levels may exist inside a domain, but they do not combine into a single definitive character level.

## Stat XP

Bodily statistics reset with a new incarnation. Stat XP persists and accelerates redevelopment in compatible bodies.

Retained Stat XP does not guarantee identical results. Anatomy, species, health, environment, training methods, and metaphysical limits still matter.

## Earned Growth

Development comes from relevant pressure and adaptation. Repetition without challenge eventually yields diminishing returns. Breakthroughs require refinement, greater difficulty, new understanding, or changed circumstances.

## Assessment

Characters and institutions may use ranks, tests, titles, or estimates, but these are models rather than perfect measurements of total capability.
