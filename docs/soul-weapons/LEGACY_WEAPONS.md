# Legacy Weapons

## Status

This document defines how an awakened Weapon Soul may remain in the world after a defining relationship, purpose, or Age has ended and how later people may relate to it without inheriting ownership of its personhood, memories, capabilities, or bonds.

It extends the established rules for [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md), [Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md), and [Weapon Echoes](WEAPON_ECHOES.md). It does not define detailed compatibility, Weapon Manifestations, unconventional vessel structures, ordinary inheritance law, or a universal relic economy.

Read [Dormant Weapon Souls](DORMANT_WEAPON_SOULS.md), [Awakening Conditions](AWAKENING_CONDITIONS.md), [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md), [Weapon Personalities](WEAPON_PERSONALITIES.md), [Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md), and [Weapon Echoes](WEAPON_ECHOES.md) first.

## Core Rule

A **Legacy Weapon** is an awakened Weapon Soul whose defining earlier relationship, purpose, or historical context has ended or materially changed while the Weapon Soul remains anchored in the world and carries that history into later relationships.

Legacy is a historical and relational condition. It is not:

- a rarity tier;
- a claim of legal ownership;
- proof of a Soul Weapon Bond;
- an inheritance package;
- a heroic or moral designation;
- a source of automatic capability;
- a guarantee that the Weapon Soul wants another wielder;
- a requirement that the Weapon Soul remain trapped in an earlier identity.

A Legacy Weapon remains the same person. It may remember, grieve, refuse, negotiate, change purpose, form new relationships, and pursue a future that its predecessor never imagined.

## Terms

### Legacy Basis

The actual relationship, purpose, event, responsibility, place, institution, or historical consequence that makes an awakened Weapon Soul's earlier life materially relevant to later relationships.

A Legacy Basis is qualitative. Age, fame, market price, bloodshed, number of wielders, and public legend do not create one by themselves.

### Legacy Transition

The causal change through which an awakened Weapon Soul remains world-bound after a defining relationship, purpose, custody, or era closes or becomes inaccessible.

Examples include:

- an Awakening Event in which the user dies before Soul Intertwining can occur;
- a Bonded Soul refusing or losing Reincarnation passage while the Weapon Soul remains in the world;
- Bond Severance followed by continued world-bound existence;
- the collapse of the institution, culture, duty, or place around which the Weapon Soul organized its life;
- a peaceful completion of a long purpose followed by a deliberate choice to remain and begin another.

The transition does not grant power, erase a bond, establish an heir, or force the Weapon Soul to seek a successor.

### Legacy Status

The current relationship between a Legacy Weapon, any Soul Weapon Bond, its physical custody, its available communication, and the people or institutions around it.

Legacy Status is descriptive rather than a rank. A campaign record should state the facts rather than reduce them to one value.

### Legacy Accord

A scoped, revocable agreement between a Legacy Weapon and another person or group concerning matters such as custody, care, travel, use, disclosure, repair, representation, or protection.

A Legacy Accord is not an Intertwining Accord. It creates no Soul Weapon Bond, Reincarnation passage, shared identity, permanent access, or inherited capability.

### Legacy Steward

A person or group whom the Weapon Soul currently accepts in a caretaking, protective, representative, or custodial role under a Legacy Accord.

Stewardship grants only the authority actually agreed. It does not imply permission to wield, command, sell, expose memories, arrange a new bond, or speak for the Weapon Soul.

### Legacy Bearer

A person whom the Weapon Soul currently permits to carry or use its Weapon Vessel within defined circumstances.

A Legacy Bearer is not automatically compatible, skilled, trusted in every matter, morally approved, or eligible for Soul Intertwining.

### Legacy Succession

The social and relational process through which custody, responsibility, cooperation, or public role passes from an earlier person or institution to a later one.

Legacy Succession can produce a steward, bearer, ally, student, caretaker, opponent, or eventual candidate for Soul Intertwining. It cannot transfer a soul relationship by inheritance.

## When Legacy Status Applies

An awakened Weapon Soul qualifies as a Legacy Weapon when all of the following are true:

1. the Weapon Soul is an established person under [Awakening Conditions](AWAKENING_CONDITIONS.md);
2. a genuine Legacy Basis shaped its identity, relationships, duties, choices, or world consequences;
3. the earlier context has ended, become inaccessible, or changed enough that continuation cannot be assumed;
4. the Weapon Soul remains anchored in the world rather than currently accompanying a Bonded Soul through an effective Passage Accord;
5. later custody or relationship must address the Weapon Soul's personhood and prior history rather than treating it as an ordinary unclaimed object.

Legacy Status may be publicly recognized, secretly understood, disputed, or unknown. Public fame is unnecessary. A forgotten blade in a collapsed tunnel can be a Legacy Weapon when its own history and current relationships satisfy the rule.

### What Does Not Qualify by Itself

None of the following independently establishes Legacy Status:

- being old, valuable, enchanted, damaged, buried, or famous;
- having belonged to a dead person;
- appearing in a prophecy, family record, museum, treasury, or royal inventory;
- being called legendary, ancestral, sacred, cursed, or chosen;
- surviving many battles or accumulating many owners;
- being stolen, lost, sealed, sold, or recovered;
- possessing a Dormant Weapon Soul;
- being an awakened Weapon Soul with no meaningful completed earlier context;
- an actively bonded Soul Weapon being temporarily separated from its Bonded Soul.

Temporary loss, theft, imprisonment, or distance does not manufacture a legacy transition. The actual bond and history continue to govern.

## Legacy Status and Bond Status

Legacy Status does not replace the bond states defined in [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md). Resolve bond status first.

| Bond fact | Legacy consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Never intertwined | The awakened Weapon Soul may form a Legacy Basis through an ended relationship or purpose while remaining unbound. |
| Intertwining was proposed but never completed | No bond exists. Gratitude, sacrifice, or an interrupted ritual cannot create one retroactively. |
| Bond is Suspended | The bond still exists. The Weapon Soul may remain world-bound as a Legacy Weapon, but cannot form another standard bond while Suspension continues. |
| Bond is Severed | The former bond has ended. Its history remains, and a later standard bond is possible only through a new fully qualified Soul Intertwining. |
| Bonded Soul is alive elsewhere | Separation alone does not make the weapon unbound or authorize a successor. |
| New standard bond later forms | The new bond governs current passage and cooperation. Earlier legacy history remains real but grants no predecessor or heir authority over it. |

### Passage Refusal and Suspension

If a Weapon Soul refuses Reincarnation passage while preserving the relationship, the Soul Weapon Bond becomes Suspended to the extent defined by the established Accord and circumstances. The weapon remains in the world and may acquire Legacy Status.

During Suspension:

- the original Soul Weapon Bond still exists;
- no later person may replace the Bonded Soul through inheritance, ritual, law, affection, or necessity;
- the Weapon Soul may make bounded Legacy Accords with later stewards or bearers;
- physical use and ordinary cooperation may occur only within current capability, consent, custody, and world causality;
- bond-dependent effects remain unavailable when their access conditions are not met;
- only valid Bond Severance can make a new standard Soul Intertwining possible.

Refusing passage is therefore meaningful. It can create centuries of distance and new world relationships without quietly undoing the original bond.

### Severance and a Later Bond

After valid Bond Severance, neither former participant owns the other's future. A later relationship does not inherit the earlier Accord.

A new Soul Intertwining requires:

- an awakened and unbound Weapon Soul;
- a new genuine shared history and mutual Resonance;
- independent consent from both current participants;
- present compatibility and continuity;
- a new Intertwining Accord;
- all other requirements in [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md).

The earlier bond may shape personality, caution, Weapon Echoes, Soul Titles, reputation, and world response. It cannot count as the new participant's trust, Skill, consent, or relationship experience.

### If the Earlier Soul Returns

When a former Bonded Soul reincarnates and later encounters the Legacy Weapon, determine the present bond state.

- **Bond still active or Suspended:** the same persistent relationship exists, but current access, recognition, embodiment, trust, and boundaries must be renegotiated through the present incarnation.
- **Bond Severed:** the returning soul has no metaphysical claim. A relationship may be rebuilt, but not resumed by assertion.
- **Weapon Soul now has another standard bond:** the former partner cannot displace it. Any new relationship must respect the current bond and the Weapon Soul's agency.

Past intimacy matters without becoming ownership.

## Custody, Law, and Personhood

### Custody Is Not Consent

Custody identifies who currently controls the Weapon Vessel's location, storage, transport, or physical access. It does not establish moral or metaphysical authority over the Weapon Soul.

A person may physically hold, hide, display, damage, or swing a Legacy Weapon without its consent when ordinary causality allows. That coercion is a real world action with ethical, legal, relational, and practical consequences. Personhood does not grant plot armour, teleportation, or an automatic physical escape.

Likewise, a Weapon Soul may lack a current means to communicate refusal or prevent misuse. Silence under incapacity is not agreement.

### Inheritance Transfers No Soul Rights

A will, bloodline, office, victory, purchase, conquest, marriage, court order, temple decree, or family custom may transfer a social or legal claim to the Weapon Vessel. It cannot transfer:

- the Weapon Soul's personhood;
- trust, affection, loyalty, or compatibility;
- a Legacy Accord or Intertwining Accord unless the Weapon Soul independently renews the relevant terms;
- Weapon Echo access or disclosure rights;
- weapon-owned capability;
- the predecessor's Skill or mastery;
- a Soul Weapon Bond or Passage Accord;
- the right to alter, evolve, seal, copy, or destroy the Weapon Soul.

A predecessor may request that the Weapon Soul meet, protect, teach, or consider an heir. The request is testimony and relationship context, not a command that survives the requester.

### Institutions and Public Roles

Kingdoms, clans, guilds, temples, museums, monster societies, and families may treat a Legacy Weapon as:

- a citizen or ancestor;
- a sacred witness;
- a military asset;
- a dangerous captive;
- an office symbol;
- private property;
- a treaty party;
- a fraudulent legend;
- an inconvenient survivor.

These classifications affect law, custody, protection, danger, resources, access, and reputation. They do not determine metaphysical truth. An institution can successfully imprison a person while being wrong that the person is property.

### A Weapon Soul Is Not a Moral Oracle

A Legacy Weapon's acceptance or refusal expresses its own perspective. It does not prove that a bearer is good, destined, innocent, legitimate, or objectively correct.

The Weapon Soul may:

- trust a flawed person;
- reject a worthy person for personal reasons;
- misremember or misunderstand history;
- prefer familiar harm over unfamiliar safety;
- change its judgment;
- care about goals that others consider unjust;
- refuse to arbitrate an inheritance dispute.

Weapon choice creates consequences and testimony, not divine moral certification.

## Establishing a Later Relationship

Legacy Succession should be played as relationship and world causality rather than a selection screen.

### 1. Establish the Source

Determine whether the object contains an awakened Weapon Soul, what evidence supports that conclusion, and what competing sources could explain observed effects.

### 2. Establish Bond and Legacy Facts

Identify:

- the Legacy Basis and transition;
- whether a prior or Suspended Soul Weapon Bond exists;
- the current vessel condition and location;
- known and hidden history;
- existing obligations, stewards, claimants, enemies, and institutions;
- current communication and access limits.

### 3. Address Immediate Custody and Safety

Resolve who physically controls the vessel, what dangers exist, and whether the Weapon Soul can communicate or act. Do not assume that the socially recognized owner is the agreed steward.

### 4. Establish Contact

Contact follows current senses, Resonance, communication channels, vessel condition, language, and source rules. Neither fame nor inheritance grants a private conversation.

### 5. Negotiate Scope

The participants may discuss:

- care and repair;
- safe storage or travel;
- who may carry or use the vessel;
- what purposes are acceptable;
- privacy and Weapon Echo disclosure;
- representation before institutions;
- exit, review, and refusal conditions;
- protection of bystanders and third-party interests.

### 6. Form a Legacy Accord When Appropriate

Record only the scope actually accepted. A Legacy Accord may be narrow, temporary, conditional, renewed through conduct, or refused entirely.

### 7. Let Trust Develop Causally

Reliable conduct, honest disagreement, fulfilled care, shared danger, repair, respect, and meaningful consequence may change the relationship. Gifts, praise, use counts, victories, bloodline claims, and repeated dialogue do not farm trust.

### 8. Resolve Each Capability Through Its Owner

Determine separately:

- what the Legacy Weapon can currently do;
- what the bearer can currently do;
- what cooperation is permitted;
- whether body, Skill, resources, environment, law, and maintenance support the act;
- whether a bond-dependent effect is inaccessible;
- what uncertainty and consequences remain.

### 9. Consider Soul Intertwining Only If Independently Qualified

Legacy Succession never requires Soul Intertwining. If the Weapon Soul is unbound and a later relationship independently satisfies every condition, the participants may consider it. The new bond is earned from their relationship, not inherited from the old one.

## Cooperation Without Intertwining

An unbound or Suspended Legacy Weapon may cooperate with a steward or bearer within current capability and consent. This can include:

- allowing ordinary physical use of the vessel;
- offering source-bounded communication or feedback;
- participating in repair, research, diplomacy, ritual, testimony, teaching, travel, or protection;
- sharing selected Weapon Echo material;
- pursuing its own goals through agreed collaboration;
- refusing uses that violate current boundaries.

Cooperation does not create:

- Reincarnation passage;
- bond-dependent effects;
- automatic manifestation;
- Skill transfer;
- inherited mastery;
- extra actions;
- a Class, Profession, Species Trait, or Evolution Route;
- permanent permission;
- compatibility by declaration.

A Legacy Bearer performs through the bearer's current embodiment, Skills, Development, resources, and circumstances. The Weapon Soul contributes only effects it actually owns and can presently express.

## Legacy Weapons and Weapon Echoes

A Legacy Weapon may use [Weapon Echoes](WEAPON_ECHOES.md) as memory, testimony, relationship context, or evidence within those rules.

### Selective Sharing

The Weapon Soul decides whether to share an accessible Echo, subject to valid channel, capacity, privacy, third-party interests, and coercion. A Legacy Accord may establish standing disclosure boundaries but cannot turn the weapon into an open archive.

### No Inherited Mastery

A bearer who sees an earlier duel through a Weapon Echo may gain information, emotional context, or an instructional lead. The bearer does not receive:

- the predecessor's Skill;
- the Weapon Soul's capability;
- embodied timing or reliable execution;
- a prior Class, Profession, title, or office;
- the right to reproduce private techniques;
- certainty that old conditions still apply.

Current-life learning still requires a valid receiving route, practice, feedback, embodiment, and [Skill Development](../progression/SKILL_DEVELOPMENT.md).

### Testimony Is Not Objective History

Weapon Echoes preserve the Weapon Soul's bounded first-person perspective. A Legacy Weapon may be a uniquely valuable witness and still be incomplete, mistaken, traumatized, biased, deceived, or unable to access a relevant Chapter.

## Legacy Evolution

The standard [Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md) procedure assumes an established Soul Weapon Bond. A Legacy Weapon without an active usable bond may undertake a **self-directed Legacy Evolution Route** only when all normal non-bond requirements remain satisfied and the route can be explained without borrowing an absent partner's soul assets.

### Required Conditions

A valid Legacy Evolution requires:

1. an awakened Weapon Soul and coherent Weapon Vessel;
2. an established Prior Weapon Form or, for a never-intertwined weapon, an equally explicit current vessel-and-capability profile;
3. a genuine Legacy Basis relevant to the proposed change;
4. a valid Weapon Evolution Pressure or self-directed purpose that the present form cannot answer unchanged;
5. route provenance grounded in the Weapon Soul's own identity, history, capabilities, sources, and current relationships;
6. the Weapon Soul's informed consent;
7. consent from any other person whose soul, bond, identity, or independently owned capability would be materially altered;
8. current support, resources, craft, time, vulnerability, and a viable transition;
9. explicit gains, losses, dependencies, limitations, access, failure, and world consequences;
10. compliance with every anti-stacking and source-ownership rule in [Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md).

### What May Support the Route

The route may use:

- the Weapon Soul's own Vessel History, personality, Weapon Echoes, and Development;
- a valid Legacy Basis and current purpose;
- consensual collaboration with stewards, bearers, crafters, cultures, monsters, spirits, or institutions;
- available materials, magic, environments, rituals, and other sources under their own rules;
- consequences and learning produced by actual current-life relationships.

### What Cannot Be Borrowed

An unbound Legacy Weapon cannot treat an absent or former Bonded Soul's:

- Soul Imprints;
- Soul Depth;
- Soul Resonance access;
- Soul Titles;
- Skills or Stat XP;
- Class, Profession, species form, or authority;
- consent, vows, identity, or Reincarnation route

as though those features still belong to the weapon.

Shared history may remain provenance. Separately owned soul assets do not.

### Suspended Bonds

A Legacy Weapon with a Suspended bond may pursue a self-directed change confined to its own body and independently owned capabilities when that change does not rewrite the bond, shared persistent structures, Passage Accord, or the Bonded Soul's identity.

If the route materially alters those shared matters, both bonded participants must consent under [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md) and [Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md), even across distance or Reincarnation. When valid consent cannot be established, that route remains unavailable.

### Legacy Evolution Is Not a Shortcut

Legacy Status does not lower route requirements, convert grief into power, grant a higher tier, make public worship useful fuel, or replace a missing bond with followers. A stable Legacy Weapon may remain unchanged for an Age and still be complete as a person.

## Interactions

### Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may support recognition, communication, Echo Triggers, or a later relationship when a genuine Basis exists. Similarity to a predecessor, inherited emotion, fame, or touching the vessel does not guarantee Resonance or access.

### Soul Echoes

A predecessor's [Soul Echo](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) and the Weapon Soul's Weapon Echo may preserve different accounts of shared history. Neither inherits the other's memories, speaks for the current incarnation, renews consent, or establishes ownership.

### Soul Titles

[Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may influence recognition, symbolic authority, opportunity, fear, or route context. An heir carrying the predecessor's title, or a weapon carrying a title-linked history, gains no automatic Accord, compatibility, bond, or capability.

### Soul Depth

[Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) may support integration of complex identity and relationships for the soul that possesses it. It is not weapon capacity, bearer qualification, succession rank, or a substitute for present communication and consent.

### Reincarnation

[Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) carries a Weapon Soul only through an effective Soul Weapon Bond and Passage Accord. A Legacy Accord with an ordinary bearer creates no passage. The bearer loses worldly custody at Final Death under ordinary reincarnation rules.

### Embodiment and Development

The Weapon Vessel is the Weapon Soul's embodiment. Damage, repair, scale, materials, senses, mobility, and environment remain consequential. A bearer's capability follows [Development](../progression/README.md), current anatomy, health, practice, and access rather than the predecessor's accomplishments.

### Skill Trees and Adaptive Skills

A Legacy Weapon may teach, demonstrate, constrain, or collaborate with [Skills](../skills/README.md). It does not install a Skill Tree or Adaptive Skill. Novel cooperation may contribute experience to a valid [Adaptive Skill](../skills/ADAPTIVE_SKILLS.md), but emergence still requires recurring need, variation, feedback, adjustment, and integration.

### Human Classes and Professions

[Human Classes and Professions](../human/README.md) may teach care, interpretation, diplomacy, craft, historical method, or fighting practice involving Legacy Weapons. No Class, office, licence, hereditary role, or Profession can award a Legacy Weapon's consent or personhood as a credential.

### Monster Evolution

A monster may discover, steward, bear, imprison, study, or relate to a Legacy Weapon. [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) does not make the weapon a species organ or evolution reward, and the weapon does not grant a monster form. Cross-species contact follows actual senses, cognition, anatomy, culture, Resonance, Skill, and consent.

### Akashic Archive

[Archive Records](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) may preserve evidence concerning a Legacy Basis. The Archive does not own the Weapon Soul, grant memory access, renew an Accord, appoint an heir, or replace current relationships.

### World Engine

Legacy Weapons can influence institutions, succession crises, migrations, wars, religions, economies, preservation, and historical knowledge through causal world response. Public legend may grow while the Weapon Soul remains silent, disagrees with the legend, or is physically controlled by others.

## GM Adjudication Procedure

When a Legacy Weapon claim matters:

1. **Identify the person.** Confirm an awakened Weapon Soul rather than a Dormant Weapon Soul, enchantment, spirit, construct, residue, Soul Echo, or ordinary object.
2. **State the Legacy Basis.** Identify the completed or inaccessible earlier context and why it remains meaningful now.
3. **Resolve bond status.** Distinguish never intertwined, active, Suspended, and Severed.
4. **Separate custody from relationship.** Record who physically controls the vessel, who claims legal authority, and whom the Weapon Soul accepts.
5. **Establish current access.** Determine communication, senses, vessel condition, mobility, cooperation, and relevant sources.
6. **State the proposed claim.** Is the issue care, carrying, use, disclosure, repair, evolution, public representation, or Soul Intertwining?
7. **Apply the correct Accord.** Use a Legacy Accord for scoped present cooperation and an Intertwining Accord only for a qualified persistent bond.
8. **Resolve each capability by source.** Keep Weapon Soul effects, bearer Skills, embodiment, materials, magic, institutions, and world causality separate.
9. **Protect third parties.** Consider privacy, prior promises, living persons, public danger, legal interests, and people affected by use.
10. **Apply consequences.** Record trust, refusal, injury, custody, reputation, institutional response, and world effects outside the repository.
11. **Audit inheritance.** Remove any access, mastery, consent, or bond justified only by lineage, office, possession, or predecessor status.
12. **Audit farming.** Reject relationship or evolution progress based only on repeated gifts, uses, battles, owners, ceremonies, suffering, or dramatic claims.

## Worked Examples

### The Spear at the Last Gate

A town guard and an ordinary spear hold a breach long enough for civilians to escape. The spear forms and awakens during the connected struggle, but the guard dies before the newly awakened Weapon Soul can consider intertwining. The town later places the damaged spear above the rebuilt gate.

The Weapon Soul has a Legacy Basis in the shared defence and the consequences that followed. The guard's child inherits a legal claim to the vessel but not the guard's relationship. The spear may accept the child as steward, prefer the masons who repair the gate, ask to remain on watch, or reject public display. The sacrifice creates history, not an inherited bond.

### The Blade That Refused Passage

A Bonded Soul dies after commanding centuries of conquest. The Weapon Soul refuses passage but does not sever the relationship. It remains in the capital while the bond is Suspended.

A later reformer earns a Legacy Accord allowing travel, testimony, and defensive use. The reformer is a Legacy Bearer, not a replacement Bonded Soul. No new standard intertwining is possible while Suspension continues. The weapon's refusal has changed history without making the original bond vanish.

### A New Bond After Severance

An axe and its former Bonded Soul mutually sever after an irreconcilable dispute. Generations later, a forest monster rescues the axe from a flooded shrine, learns to communicate through vibration and scent-associated Resonance, and shares years of difficult stewardship.

The old bond supplies history but no trust credit. If the axe and monster eventually satisfy every intertwining condition, they may form a new bond from their own relationship. The former partner's descendants cannot veto it, and the monster receives none of the former wielder's Skills.

### The Heir and the Groundskeeper

A royal heir performs the succession rite correctly and expects the dynasty's famous sword to endorse the coronation. The sword refuses to communicate with the heir but accepts a Legacy Accord with the elderly groundskeeper who has protected its privacy and maintained the neglected memorial garden.

The sword's decision does not prove the groundskeeper should rule or the heir is evil. It establishes only its own trust and chosen stewardship. Political actors may still treat the refusal as evidence, propaganda, insult, or crisis.

### Property in the Imperial Museum

An empire classifies an awakened bow as a national artifact, seals it in a display, and suppresses its communication. The bow cannot physically escape. It refuses all cooperative effects when soldiers remove it for war, but ordinary hands can still carry and fire the physical vessel within mundane limits.

Personhood has not granted plot armour. The empire's custody is materially real and metaphysically wrong about ownership. Rescue, negotiation, law, sabotage, or institutional reform must occur through world causality.

### The Echo of the Broken Formation

A Legacy Weapon shares a selected Weapon Echo showing how an earlier bearer died when a shield formation collapsed. A new militia learns where communication failed and changes its drills.

The Echo provides testimony and an instructional lead. It grants no Formation Skill, embodied timing, or automatic success. The militia still needs teaching, practice, equipment, trust, and experience.

### The Bell Chooses a New Purpose

An awakened warning bell outlives the mining settlement it protected. Centuries later, refugees use the ruined valley as a sanctuary. The bell decides that remaining unchanged cannot serve people who communicate through ground vibration rather than sound.

With its own Legacy Basis, current collaborators, compatible craft, resources, consent, and a bounded transition, it follows a self-directed Legacy Evolution Route into a resonant warning instrument. It cannot borrow its dead keeper's Soul Title or Skills, and the new form carries material costs and a narrower range in open air.

### The Returned Founder

A founder reincarnates centuries after valid Bond Severance and finds that the former Soul Weapon has formed a new bond with someone else. The founder remembers intimacy and claims that history makes the weapon theirs.

The claim fails. The old relationship remains meaningful, but Severance ended it and the current bond has its own agency. The three people may negotiate contact, grief, testimony, or reconciliation; no one is reassigned as equipment.

### Theft Does Not Create Legacy

Thieves steal an actively bonded dagger and hide it for ten years. They call it a Legacy Weapon and demand that it choose a new master.

No Legacy Transition occurred merely through theft and delay. The existing bond remains unless changed through its own rules. Physical separation may limit access and create consequences, but it does not open a vacant bond slot.

### The Carpenter's Plane

An awakened plane remains in a coastal workshop after its Bonded Soul peacefully severs the bond and retires from craft. The plane accepts a cooperative workshop as stewards, helps evaluate repairs through touch and vibration, and refuses to be used for weapons.

No battle, dynasty, or prophecy is required. Decades of rebuilding homes supply the Legacy Basis, while each craftsperson still develops their own Profession and Skills.

## Safeguards

- **No ownership by inheritance:** lineage, office, law, purchase, victory, prophecy, or possession cannot transfer personhood, consent, memory, access, capability, or bond.
- **No vacant bond slot:** an active or Suspended standard bond prevents another standard bond until valid Severance.
- **No retroactive intertwining:** death, sacrifice, an interrupted ritual, or a predecessor's wish cannot complete an absent Accord.
- **No successor package:** Legacy Succession grants no Skill, Class, Profession, species trait, title, statistic, mastery, form, or predecessor capability.
- **No mandatory successor:** a Legacy Weapon may choose stewardship, solitude, non-use, several bounded collaborators, or no later relationship.
- **No moral oracle:** acceptance and refusal express one person's judgment rather than objective goodness, legitimacy, destiny, or truth.
- **No weapon plot armour:** personhood does not guarantee mobility, rescue, immunity to custody, indestructibility, or the power to enforce refusal physically.
- **No silent consent:** incapacity, suppression, damage, sleep, distance, uncertainty, and lack of a communication channel do not establish agreement.
- **No bond through a Legacy Accord:** scoped cooperation does not create Reincarnation passage, persistent access, or Soul Intertwining.
- **No inherited Echo library:** the Weapon Soul controls ordinary Weapon Echo access and disclosure; heirs and institutions have no automatic right.
- **No inherited mastery:** memory, advice, demonstration, or former user history does not replace current embodiment, Skill Development, and practice.
- **No absent-partner laundering:** Legacy Evolution cannot use a former or distant partner's Soul Imprints, Depth, Titles, Skills, authority, or consent as weapon-owned resources.
- **No follower-powered evolution:** worship, fame, bearers, heirs, battles, ceremonies, grief, and years do not accumulate into automatic route readiness.
- **No infinite evolution:** every Legacy Evolution still requires fresh provenance, pressure, support, transition, costs, tradeoffs, and consequences.
- **No compatibility assumption:** trust, affection, usefulness, Skill, lineage, and Resonance do not settle detailed compatibility before its dedicated rules.
- **No manifestation assumption:** an awakened or evolved Legacy Weapon remains physically constrained unless the later Weapon Manifestation rules establish another expression.
- **No repository-bound campaign state:** particular weapons, bonds, bearers, stewards, locations, Echoes, Accords, custody disputes, and histories remain external Campaign Records.

## Scope Boundaries

This document owns Legacy Status, Legacy Transitions, succession, scoped Legacy Accords, custody-and-personhood separation, later relationships, and the bounded self-directed evolution exception for Legacy Weapons.

It does not define:

- detailed Soul Weapon compatibility;
- Weapon Manifestations or summoning;
- unconventional vessel structures;
- ordinary property law for every culture;
- a universal heir-selection procedure;
- equipment statistics or treasure generation;
- a campaign's actual Legacy Weapons or succession disputes.
