# Soul Weapons

Soul Weapons are persistent partnerships between a continuing soul and a distinct Weapon Soul. They are not equipment tiers, inventory rewards, Skill packages, Classes, species traits, or substitutes for current-life Development.

Read the [Soul Weapon Foundations](../soul/SOUL_WEAPON_FOUNDATIONS.md) before this section. The foundations establish emergence through meaningful shared history, Weapon Soul personhood, the separation between awakening and intertwining, Reincarnation persistence, and the safeguards every detailed rule must preserve.

## Canonical Reading Order

1. [Dormant Weapon Souls](DORMANT_WEAPON_SOULS.md) defines how a distinct Weapon Soul may coalesce within a meaningful weapon or tool, what dormancy means, how Vessel Continuity works, and why dormancy grants no active capability.
2. [Awakening Conditions](AWAKENING_CONDITIONS.md) defines the transition into Weapon Soul personhood through genuine shared pressure, mutual Resonance, Reciprocal Imprinting, interpretive necessity, and lasting consequence.
3. [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md) defines the mutual Accord that creates a persistent Soul Weapon Bond, its consent and identity boundaries, standard dyadic structure, Reincarnation passage, Suspension, and Severance.
4. [Weapon Personalities](WEAPON_PERSONALITIES.md) defines how Vessel History, Vessel Perspective, Awakening Interpretation, relationships, and autonomous choice shape an evolving person without predetermining morality, loyalty, or capability.
5. [Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md) defines qualitative form change through causal routes, pressure, consent, transition, tradeoffs, branching, persistence, and strict anti-stacking safeguards.
6. [Weapon Echoes](WEAPON_ECHOES.md) defines bounded first-person memory structures formed from reconciled Weapon Chapters, their access and sharing, and why they are neither past personalities nor stored capabilities.
7. [Legacy Weapons](LEGACY_WEAPONS.md) defines world-bound historical continuity, bond-status separation, custody, stewardship, succession, scoped Legacy Accords, later relationships, and bounded self-directed Legacy Evolution.
8. [Soul Weapon Compatibility](SOUL_WEAPON_COMPATIBILITY.md) defines claim-specific compatibility layers, qualitative findings, supports, drift, testing, cross-species interaction, and strict separation from consent, Skill, Resonance, and bond status.
9. [Weapon Manifestations](WEAPON_MANIFESTATIONS.md) defines Perceptual, Vessel, and Embodied expression through explicit sources and Anchors, including consent, action limits, Reincarnation access, failure, recovery, and anti-duplication safeguards.
10. [Unconventional Soul Weapons](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md) defines Vessel Topology, personhood, continuity, Agency Routing, and source ownership for composite, paired, distributed, modular, biotic, body-linked, record-bearing, mutable, and site-bound vessels.

The documents are cumulative. A later document adds a distinct question or procedure without retroactively turning formation, Awakening, Soul Intertwining, capability, access, or cooperation into one automatic progression track.

## State Model

Soul Weapon rules distinguish several facts that may coexist but never prove one another.

| Fact | Canonical meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Ordinary vessel** | No Weapon Soul is established. Importance, age, enchantment, ownership, and danger do not change that by themselves. |
| **Dormant Weapon Soul** | A distinct identity has formed through coherent Vessel History but has not awakened into active personhood or capability. |
| **Awakened Weapon Soul** | The Weapon Soul is an active person with perspective and agency. Awakening creates no bond, loyalty, Skill, Evolution, or Manifestation by implication. |
| **Soul Weapon Bond** | An awakened Weapon Soul and one continuing soul have formed a voluntary persistent relationship through Soul Intertwining. The bond is not ownership or permanent consent. |
| **Bond state** | Relationship history records whether a bond is active, Suspended, or ended through Severance. Severance is not a continuing bond, and Legacy Status does not replace this fact. |
| **Legacy Status** | Earlier relationship, purpose, or historical context materially shapes a world-bound Weapon Soul's later life. It is neither a vacant bond nor an inheritance package. |
| **Weapon Soul personality** | The Weapon Soul's current interpretation, values, tensions, relationships, and choices. Personality is not capability or compatibility. |
| **Soul Weapon Form** | The current soul-bound organisation of the vessel and weapon-owned capabilities. Current physical availability remains a separate embodiment question. |
| **Weapon Echoes** | Bounded first-person memory structures formed from reconciled Weapon Chapters. They are neither extra persons nor stored abilities. |
| **Compatibility** | A purpose-specific assessment across relevant layers. It is not consent, destiny, merit, or one total affinity score. |
| **Manifestation access** | One established route for bounded external expression under current sources, Anchors, costs, consent, and conditions. It is optional. |
| **Vessel Topology** | The current boundary, Components, soul arrangement, continuity, perspective, and Agency Routing of an unusual vessel. Topology is not rank. |

These facts do not form a compulsory ladder. A dormant soul may never awaken. An awakened Weapon Soul may never intertwine. A bonded pair may never pursue Evolution or Manifestation. A Legacy Weapon may remain unbound, and a simple vessel may matter as much as an unconventional one.

| Claim | Primary owner |
| --- | --- |
| Whether a Weapon Soul exists, persists, awakens, bonds, changes, or acts | Soul Weapon rules |
| Whether the wielder can perform a learned method | [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) |

| Whether an institution recognizes, teaches, controls access to, or claims ownership over a weapon | [Human Classes and Professions](../human/README.md), society, and world causality |
| Whether a monster form changes | [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) |
| Whether a magical effect, enchantment, spirit, or construct exists | Its magical or other source, including the later Magic rules |
| Current custody, location, damage, reputation, relationships, and world consequences | External Campaign Record and the World Engine |

One event may affect several owners. Record each effect once. A Weapon Soul can support a wielder without its capability becoming the wielder's personal Development, and a wielder can develop excellent Skill without changing the Weapon Soul.

## Soul Weapon Claim Resolution

Use this sequence whenever a claim combines a vessel, Weapon Soul, bond, ability, memory, form, manifestation, or Reincarnation. Open only the specialist documents relevant to the claim.

1. **State the exact claim.** Name the proposed person, vessel, relationship, action, source, objective, observer, and consequence rather than asking whether a weapon is simply "powerful."
2. **Identify existing persons and sources.** Separate a Weapon Soul from spirits, constructs, enchantments, living persons, Soul Echoes, users, and other Weapon Souls before assigning any effect.
3. **Establish the vessel.** For an ordinary vessel, verify Vessel Continuity. For an unusual vessel, complete the Vessel Topology audit, including boundary, Components, soul count, perspective, Agency Routing, maintenance, and failure.
4. **Determine formation and state.** Establish whether no Weapon Soul, a Dormant Weapon Soul, or an awakened Weapon Soul is supported by current evidence. Do not infer a later state from importance or spectacle.
5. **Separate relationship facts.** Determine bond status, current consent, trust, loyalty, compatibility, custody, legal ownership, reputation, and institutional claims independently.
6. **Assign capability ownership.** Identify what the Weapon Soul can do, what another actor can do through Skills and Development, what the vessel materially permits, and what Magic or another source supplies. Record each capability once.
7. **Test present embodiment and access.** Check body, senses, scale, handling, environment, interfaces, materials, damage, maintenance, communication, and Practised Reliability. Persistence is not present expression.
8. **Resolve memory and identity.** Distinguish Vessel History, current memory, Weapon Echoes, Soul Echoes, Archive evidence, public legend, and mistaken belief. None substitutes for another.
9. **Resolve change through its owner.** Use Weapon Evolution for lasting qualitative Soul Weapon reorganisation, Development for changing personal capability, Monster Evolution for anatomy, and Human or world systems for social change.
10. **Resolve external expression separately.** A Weapon Manifestation needs its own route, source, Anchor, consent, costs, action limits, interruption, and termination. Bond, compatibility, or appearance cannot imply one.
11. **Resolve Reincarnation passage and current access separately.** Apply the standing Passage Accord, then reassess embodiment, expression, compatibility, memory access, and worldly consequences in the new life.
12. **Propagate consequences once.** Apply injury, repair, witness knowledge, custody, law, relationships, institutions, ecology, reputation, and future opportunity through world causality. Store every live result outside this repository.
13. **Run the safeguards.** Reject compulsory bonds, automatic Awakening, inherited mastery, extra actors, copied souls, unrestricted Manifestation, infinite Evolution, universal compatibility, source laundering, title farming, and inventory-like Reincarnation.

## Phase Guarantees

- Weapon Souls are distinct persons rather than projections, loot properties, or institutional resources.
- A Soul Weapon remains optional; no character requires one for viable progression.
- Persistence never guarantees present access, manifestation, compatibility, cooperation, or mastery.
- Awakening, intertwining, evolution, and manifestation remain distinct claims.
- Current embodiment, current-life practice, consent, cost, and world consequence continue to matter.
- Human Classes, monster forms, Skills, and Soul Weapons retain separate ownership.
- No weapon category, material, rarity, age, violence count, or reputation guarantees a Weapon Soul.
- Component count, vessel scale, multiple bonds, Echo count, form count, or public legend grants no extra actor, action economy, inventory, or pooled capability.
- Compatibility, Resonance, trust, consent, bond status, passage, physical use, Manifestation, and cooperation remain separate claims.
- Evolution reorganises through bounded causal routes and tradeoffs; it neither stacks every former form nor creates an infinite ladder.
- Unconventional vessels retain bounded personhood, topology, source ownership, failure, and Reincarnation limits rather than becoming exception containers.
- No live Soul Weapon, wielder, inventory, or campaign history belongs in this repository.

## Authority and Boundary

Files in this section contain playable canonical rules. Accepted governance remains in [Design Decisions](../../design/DECISIONS.md); neither source silently overrides the other. Resolve any conflict before treating the affected rule as internally consistent.

Particular weapons, Weapon Souls, wielders, forms, bonds, locations, histories, and current conditions are campaign state and belong outside this repository under the [Game Master Framework](../gm/GAME_MASTER_FRAMEWORK.md).
