# Weapon Echoes

## Status

This document defines how an awakened Weapon Soul may preserve a bounded first-person structure from a meaningfully completed chapter of its own history, how that structure differs from a Soul Echo, how it can be recalled or shared, and why it grants neither extra agency nor inherited capability.

It does not define Legacy Weapons, the detailed Profiles owned by [Soul Weapon Compatibility](SOUL_WEAPON_COMPATIBILITY.md), [Weapon Manifestations](WEAPON_MANIFESTATIONS.md), or topology under [Unconventional Soul Weapons](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md).

Read [Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md), [Weapon Personalities](WEAPON_PERSONALITIES.md), and [Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md) first.

## Core Rule

A **Weapon Echo** is a persistent, bounded memory structure formed when an awakened Weapon Soul meaningfully reconciles a completed Weapon Chapter.

It preserves how the Weapon Soul experienced, interpreted, and participated in that chapter through its Vessel Perspective. It is part of the continuing Weapon Soul, not:

- a separate soul or person;
- a former incarnation;
- an autonomous personality;
- a copy of a user or Bonded Soul;
- an objective history;
- a stored Skill, ability, Soul Weapon Form, or action.

The Weapon Soul remains the only current person represented by its Weapon Echoes. It may recall, interpret, share, withhold, or reconsider them under current access and consent. An Echo cannot speak, choose, vote, possess, manifest, or act independently.

Weapon Echoes let a long-lived partner remember distinct lives, forms, relationships, Ages, and consequences without treating perfect memory as automatic or turning every past event into power.

## Terms

### Weapon Echo

A persistent, bounded memory structure formed when an awakened Weapon Soul meaningfully reconciles a completed Weapon Chapter. Use **Weapon Echo** on first reference and **Echo** thereafter only where it cannot be confused with a Soul Echo.

### Weapon Chapter

A bounded span of a Weapon Soul's lived history organised around one meaningful form, relationship, purpose, place, conflict, Age, responsibility, or transition. A Chapter is a way the Weapon Soul integrates history, not a universal calendar unit.

### Chapter Reconciliation

The qualitative process through which an awakened Weapon Soul recognizes that a meaningful Weapon Chapter has closed, distinguishes what it actually experienced from later interpretation, and integrates a bounded Weapon Echo without duplicating identity or capability.

### Weapon Echo Core

The source-rooted Vessel Perspective, memories, interpretations, relationships, emotions, choices, capability context, and unresolved meaning preserved from one reconciled Weapon Chapter.

### Weapon Echo Trigger

A current person, place, body, form, material, technique, sensation, symbol, threat, relationship, or consequence that creates valid Resonance with a particular Weapon Echo.

### Weapon Echo Access

The current degree to which the Weapon Soul can approach and interpret a Weapon Echo: Latent, Stirring, Accessible, Articulate, or Integrated. Access describes recall and coherence, not power or independent Presence.

### Weapon Echo Sharing

A consensual, source-bounded disclosure through which the Weapon Soul communicates part of a Weapon Echo to another person by an established channel.

### Weapon Recall Alignment

A cooperative state in which the Weapon Soul uses an accessible Weapon Echo to inform its own current attention, feedback, timing, or judgment while another actor performs through present embodiment and capability. It is not Soul Echo Overlay, Delegation, or Skill transfer.

## Why Weapon Echoes Differ From Soul Echoes

A [Soul Echo](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) forms because one incarnation ends at Final Death while the continuing soul begins another life. It preserves the bounded perspective of a former active incarnation and can develop Presence within the same continuing soul.

A Weapon Soul normally remains the same active person across the end of a user's life, a change of Age, a bond transition, or a Soul Weapon Evolution. It therefore needs memory organisation, not another former self with agency.

| Question | Soul Echo | Weapon Echo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Source** | One completed incarnation after Final Death and Life Reconciliation. | One meaningfully completed Weapon Chapter after Chapter Reconciliation. |
| **Identity** | A bounded former-incarnation perspective within the continuing soul. | A bounded memory structure belonging to the currently continuing Weapon Soul. |
| **Personhood** | Part of one soul and capable of bounded Presence, advice, and consent. | Not a person, actor, advisor, or separate perspective with present agency. |
| **Formation default** | Every completed incarnation normally forms one. | No event, user, life, form, or Age automatically forms one. |
| **Current learning** | May receive later context through explicit channels. | The current Weapon Soul interprets the Echo; the Echo itself does not learn. |
| **Control** | May qualify for consent-based Overlay or Delegation. | Cannot control a body, vessel, Weapon Soul, Bonded Soul, or action. |
| **Capability** | Provides perspective and context without restoring bodily mastery. | Provides vessel-history context without restoring Prior Weapon Forms or abilities. |
| **Voice** | May communicate as a coherent former perspective at sufficient Presence. | Any apparent voice is the current Weapon Soul recalling or presenting the memory. |

The same event may appear in both a Soul Echo and a Weapon Echo. Each preserves its own source's first-person account. Neither automatically verifies the other.

## Weapon Chapters

### What Can Define a Chapter

A Weapon Chapter may be organised around:

- one substantial shared incarnation with a Bonded Soul;
- a period before, during, or after a Soul Weapon Bond;
- one Prior Weapon Form and the way it was lived;
- service within a community, institution, monster society, household, craft, or conflict;
- a recurring purpose such as protection, healing, construction, teaching, exploration, or execution;
- an Age, migration, exile, imprisonment, recovery, or World Reset;
- a relationship with a maker, user, caretaker, rival, victim, student, or source;
- a prolonged effort to answer one Weapon Evolution Pressure;
- a period in which the Weapon Soul's identity or Vessel Perspective changed substantially.

Chapter boundaries come from meaning and integration rather than elapsed time. A single night may become a Chapter when it contains sustained transformative participation. A thousand quiet years in storage may form one Chapter, several Chapters, or none depending on what the Weapon Soul actually experienced and integrated.

### Closure

A Chapter may close through:

- the Bonded Soul's Final Death and a genuine end to that shared-life context;
- Reincarnation into a body or world where the relationship changes materially;
- Bond Suspension or Severance;
- completion or abandonment of a long responsibility;
- Soul Weapon Evolution that closes a Prior Weapon Form;
- destruction, reconstruction, recovery, exile, liberation, or transfer that changes lived context;
- death or departure of a central relationship;
- the end of a war, institution, species, settlement, Age, or world order;
- a self-recognized decision that an earlier role no longer defines the present.

Closure is not automatically loss or trauma. A peaceful apprenticeship, fulfilled promise, completed restoration, or mutually chosen separation can close a Chapter.

### No Mandatory Segmentation

The GM should not create a Weapon Chapter for every:

- battle, kill, repair, user, owner, year, level, title, or named technique;
- Final Death the Weapon Soul did not meaningfully share;
- routine transition between scenes or locations;
- temporary form, equipment configuration, or change in public reputation;
- attempt to farm more memories, bonuses, voices, or narrative importance.

Several connected events may belong to one Chapter. One long era may contain several when the Weapon Soul's lived context genuinely changes. The test is whether a bounded first-person structure preserves a meaningfully distinct part of the Weapon Soul's history without arbitrary fragmentation.

## Formation Through Chapter Reconciliation

Weapon Echo formation is not automatic at the instant a Chapter closes.

### 1. Confirm an Awakened Weapon Soul

Only an awakened Weapon Soul can form a Weapon Echo because formation requires self-originated interpretation. Dormant Vessel History may later inform Awakening, but it does not retroactively become a collection of Weapon Echoes.

### 2. Establish a Bounded Chapter

Define:

- what span of history is being reconciled;
- what relationship, form, purpose, or context makes it coherent;
- why the Chapter is now closed or meaningfully distinct;
- where it overlaps other history without duplicating it;
- what remains ongoing and therefore belongs to the current person rather than the Echo.

### 3. Verify First-Person Provenance

The proposed Weapon Echo Core must come from what the Weapon Soul could actually experience through:

- Vessel Perspective;
- established communication;
- its own choices and emotions;
- disclosed information;
- source-valid Soul Resonance;
- its participation in a bond, form, capability, or consequence.

Public legend, later research, another person's memory, Archive evidence, and retrospective theory may help interpret the Chapter but do not become first-person memory.

### 4. Identify Meaningful Closure

The Weapon Soul must have something substantive to reconcile:

- a changed understanding of self;
- a relationship that ended or changed form;
- a responsibility fulfilled, failed, rejected, or inherited;
- a Prior Weapon Form no longer lived in the same way;
- a consequence that recontextualizes what the soul believed it was doing;
- an Age or society whose disappearance materially changes the meaning of the history;
- an unresolved question important enough to preserve distinctly.

Closure alone is insufficient. A user dying after one routine use does not force a Weapon Echo.

### 5. Preserve, Do Not Duplicate

Chapter Reconciliation organises existing history. It does not create:

- another Weapon Soul;
- a copy of the Bonded Soul;
- a Soul Echo belonging to the Weapon Soul;
- a second version of a memory already recorded under another label;
- an extra Skill, Soul Imprint, Soul Title, or Soul Weapon Form;
- objective knowledge the Weapon Soul lacked.

One event can contribute to several systems from their own perspectives, but each effect has one owner.

### 6. Establish the Weapon Echo Core

Record:

- the Chapter's source and boundaries;
- the Weapon Soul's Vessel Perspective at the time;
- relevant memories, relationships, choices, emotions, and beliefs;
- the Prior Weapon Form and capability context without storing the capability;
- what the Weapon Soul knew, inferred, misunderstood, or could not perceive;
- unresolved meaning and likely Triggers;
- any damage, sealing, Dissonance, or uncertainty affecting access.

### 7. Resolve Current Access Separately

Formation establishes persistence, not immediate clarity. Determine Weapon Echo Access through current Resonance, Integrity, Personality, bond state, vessel condition, Soul Space, and Trigger.

### 8. Apply Consequences

Chapter Reconciliation may affect the Weapon Soul's current personality, bond conversations, grief, goals, research, evolution evidence, or world relationships through actual interpretation. It grants no completion bonus.

## What a Weapon Echo Preserves

A Weapon Echo Core may preserve:

- first-person vessel sensations and spatial relationships;
- the Weapon Soul's historical Self-Presentation and communication;
- memories of users, maintainers, opponents, victims, students, and witnesses as experienced;
- shared decisions, arguments, promises, refusals, and misunderstandings;
- how one Soul Weapon Form felt and was interpreted from within;
- contextual knowledge surrounding weapon-owned capabilities;
- timing, pressure, balance, damage, repair, and environmental patterns;
- emotional associations and Core Concerns during the Chapter;
- the meaning assigned to a Bonded Soul's incarnation, Soul Title, role, or death;
- knowledge learned through valid communication or instruction;
- unresolved fears, hopes, duties, errors, and questions;
- perceptions distorted by injury, manipulation, incomplete access, or false belief.

A Weapon Echo does not automatically preserve:

- perfect sensory recording or a complete timeline;
- thoughts, intentions, feelings, memories, or Soul Echoes of a user;
- facts occurring outside the Weapon Soul's senses and communication;
- objective truth about disputed events;
- events after the Chapter's reconciled boundary;
- a Prior Weapon Form as a selectable configuration;
- a capability, enchantment, material property, Skill, statistic, or resource;
- legal ownership, institutional office, social authority, or succession rights;
- every person who touched or saw the vessel;
- knowledge merely because an inscription, book, Archive Record, or speaker was nearby.

## Memory, Interpretation, and Truth

### Historical Memory

The Weapon Echo Core preserves the Chapter as the Weapon Soul understood it during reconciliation. It may include uncertainty and later context available by that point, but must preserve provenance.

### Current Interpretation

The continuing Weapon Soul may revisit the Echo and decide that its earlier judgment was mistaken, incomplete, cruel, naive, or still correct. That current interpretation belongs to the present Weapon Soul. It does not rewrite what the Chapter-self knew or felt.

### External Evidence

Archive Records, Soul Echo testimony, documents, witnesses, institutions, archaeology, and later research may corroborate or challenge a Weapon Echo. They do not merge into it automatically.

Track separately:

- Weapon Echo memory;
- the current Weapon Soul's interpretation;
- the Bonded Soul's memory or Soul Echo;
- current character knowledge;
- public history and myth;
- external evidence;
- world truth;
- unresolved uncertainty.

### Mistakes and Deception

A Weapon Echo can preserve a mistaken historical belief because the Weapon Soul was mistaken. The current Weapon Soul may also selectively present, frame, or conceal what it recalls.

The memory structure itself does not deliberately lie because it has no agency. Apparent contradiction may come from:

- different moments within the Chapter;
- damage or incomplete access;
- current selective sharing;
- later reinterpretation;
- two genuine perspectives on the same event;
- an enchantment, spirit, projection, or false record impersonating an Echo.

The GM cannot invent arbitrary memory falsity solely to invalidate reasonable player investigation. Falsehood needs source, evidence, and fair discoverability.

## Weapon Echo Access

Access describes the current Weapon Soul's relationship to one Echo. It is not a power ladder, truth rating, value judgment, or independent Presence.

| Access | Available recall | Default limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Latent** | The Echo persists without reliable deliberate recall. | It produces no independent signal or passive benefit. |
| **Stirring** | A Trigger evokes sensations, emotions, images, balance, pressure, or memory fragments. | Fragments may lack context and cannot act. |
| **Accessible** | The Weapon Soul can deliberately approach bounded memories and distinguish much of their provenance. | Recall may remain incomplete, taxing, or emotionally difficult. |
| **Articulate** | The Weapon Soul can reconstruct the Chapter coherently enough to explain relationships, choices, and capability context. | Coherence does not prove objective truth or restore capability. |
| **Integrated** | The Weapon Soul can hold the Chapter's meaning in present identity without severe loss of provenance or destabilization. | Integration grants no bonus and does not require approval of the past. |

Access is conditional. An Integrated Echo may become difficult to reach after vessel damage, Soul Strain, Dissonance, passage disruption, or changed Resonance. A powerful Trigger may briefly make a Latent Echo vivid without establishing stable Articulate access.

No fixed number of years, recalls, battles, conversations, lives, or Depth events advances access. Repeated identical retrieval does not create infinite clarity.

### Sealed and Damaged Echoes

An Echo at any access state may be sealed, distorted, fragmented in recall, or linked to Soul Strain through a valid cause.

Sealing may be:

- voluntarily established for privacy, stability, or recovery;
- part of a Bond Boundary;
- caused by vessel damage, Soul Wound, hostile magic, or intrusion;
- imposed by an institution or captor through an explicit contested effect;
- a result of incompatible current access rather than literal damage.

The cause determines recovery. Silence does not prove erasure.

## Recall and Sharing

### Private Recall

The Weapon Soul owns its Weapon Echoes and may decide when to revisit them. An Intertwining Accord does not grant the Bonded Soul unrestricted access.

Private recall may occur through:

- ordinary Weapon Soul memory;
- a Weapon Echo Trigger;
- Resonance attunement;
- an accessible Soul Space interface;
- reflection, investigation, repair, or contact with relevant history;
- an established magical or spiritual method.

Recall consumes appropriate attention and may produce distress, Dissonance, Soul Strain, or temporary confusion where the history is difficult. It does not create stopped time or parallel processing.

### Weapon Echo Sharing

Sharing requires:

1. an accessible Weapon Echo;
2. the Weapon Soul's informed consent;
3. a willing recipient or an explicit hostile disclosure rule;
4. a valid communication, Resonance, Soul Space, magical, or other channel;
5. a defined scope and purpose;
6. enough current Integrity and compatibility for the attempted detail;
7. preservation of provenance and uncertainty;
8. limits on duration, repetition, privacy, and downstream use.

The Weapon Soul may share:

- a warning sensation;
- one memory scene;
- a pattern of pressure or movement;
- an account of a former relationship;
- the felt limitations of a Prior Weapon Form;
- a sequence of choices and consequences;
- an explicit statement of uncertainty.

Sharing does not transfer ownership of the Echo, create the recipient's memory as lived experience, or guarantee identical interpretation.

### Consent and Privacy

A Bonded Soul, maker, former user, heir, institution, god, Archive interface, or legal owner cannot compel ordinary Weapon Echo disclosure.

Weapon Echoes may contain memories involving other people. The Weapon Soul owns its own experience but not every private fact about those people. The GM should preserve:

- consent where present disclosure affects living persons;
- current safety and legitimate accountability;
- the difference between testimony and invasive extraction;
- uncertainty about facts never known directly;
- consequences for secrecy, disclosure, evidence, and misuse.

Privacy is not immunity from investigation or accountability. It also is not a reason for the GM to hide clearly available information arbitrarily.

## Weapon Recall Alignment

Weapon Recall Alignment lets the current Weapon Soul use one accessible Echo as context for its own present cooperation.

It may support:

- recognizing a familiar stress, material, opponent behavior, or environmental pattern;
- recalling how a former Soul Weapon Form failed;
- giving source-bounded timing or balance feedback;
- distinguishing a current situation from a misleadingly similar past event;
- explaining a prior shared method to the current incarnation;
- identifying what present practice or adaptation is missing.

It does not:

- make the Weapon Echo an actor;
- grant an extra action, turn, reaction, or autonomous defense;
- restore a Prior Weapon Form or retired capability;
- install the Bonded Soul's former Skill or current proficiency;
- replace perception, practice, resources, anatomy, consent, or action;
- guarantee that the present situation matches the remembered one;
- allow the Weapon Soul to control the Bonded Soul.

The Weapon Soul performs only through capabilities it currently owns. The Bonded Soul or another user performs through their current body and Skills. Alignment may improve information and coordination while remaining a dependent support.

## Multiple Weapon Echoes

A long-lived Weapon Soul may accumulate many Weapon Echoes. This creates history, not parallel actors.

Several Echoes may be compared when:

- a pattern recurs across Ages or incarnations;
- different Soul Weapon Forms addressed similar problems;
- the Weapon Soul investigates a long-term change in its personality;
- several users remembered the same institution differently;
- a current route needs evidence about repeated failure or tradeoffs;
- public legend conflicts with multiple first-person Chapters.

Comparison requires current attention, access, interpretation, and time. It does not create:

- a Weapon Echo Chorus;
- majority vote among former selves;
- passive research or planning;
- simultaneous simulations;
- copied capabilities from several forms;
- extra Soul Depth, Resonance, intelligence, or actions;
- automatic objective truth.

The GM should activate only Echoes relevant to the present situation. An endless Weapon Soul history should deepen choices rather than require exhaustive memory management.

## Personality and Identity

A Weapon Echo preserves historical personality context without becoming a historical personality that can overrule the current Weapon Soul.

The current Weapon Soul may:

- recognize that it once valued something differently;
- feel affection, shame, grief, pride, anger, or distance toward the Chapter;
- preserve an old boundary or deliberately revise it;
- use the Echo to explain why a current Relational Posture exists;
- disagree with its own earlier interpretation;
- integrate the Chapter without accepting every choice;
- keep part of the history private.

A sharp difference between past and present does not create another person or prove corruption. It becomes a Personality Tension only when it remains meaningfully unresolved in the current Weapon Soul.

Chapter Reconciliation may contribute to personality change through reflection and consequence. It cannot be farmed as a shortcut to a desired trait.

## Persistence, Damage, and Erasure

Weapon Echoes normally persist with the Weapon Soul through:

- Soul Weapon Evolution;
- Reincarnation passage with a Bonded Soul;
- Bond Suspension or Severance;
- remaining in the world when passage is refused;
- repair, reforging, and vessel change that preserve Vessel Continuity;
- long time skips, Ages, and World Resets.

Persistence does not guarantee current access or sharing.

### Damage

An Echo may become inaccessible, distorted, or fragmented by a valid source affecting memory, Vessel Continuity, Soul Integrity, Resonance, or relevant channels.

Damage must identify:

- the affected Echo or access route;
- the source and mechanism;
- warning and evidence;
- resistance or prevention where meaningful;
- current effect and uncertainty;
- possible recovery routes and costs;
- consequences if recovery fails.

### Erasure

Permanent erasure is an exceptional loss of Weapon Soul structure. It requires explicit soul-harm authority, fair warning, meaningful resistance, and consequence proportionate to destroying part of a person's history.

Ordinary forgetting, inaccessible recall, disagreement, personality change, Evolution, bond change, repair, or a new user does not erase a Weapon Echo. Fragmented recall does not duplicate Echoes or create extra souls.

## Reincarnation and Shared History

When a Weapon Soul accompanies a Bonded Soul through Reincarnation, both may preserve different structures from one shared life:

- the continuing soul normally forms a Soul Echo of its completed incarnation during Life Reconciliation;
- the Weapon Soul may later form a Weapon Echo if that shared history constitutes a meaningful closed Chapter;
- each record comes from its own first-person perspective;
- neither record copies the other person's private memory;
- neither gains authority over the current incarnation merely by remembering more.

A Weapon Soul may tell a new incarnation, "Last time we fought a dragon, you stood too close." That statement may be sincere and useful. It remains:

- the weapon's memory of what it perceived;
- limited by its prior Vessel Perspective;
- subject to changed bodies, dragons, environments, and tactics;
- advice rather than control;
- no substitute for current perception or Skill.

If the Weapon Soul stays in the world, its Chapters continue through actual experience. It does not freeze while the Bonded Soul reincarnates elsewhere.

## Evolution, Legacy, and Related Soul Weapon Rules

### Soul Weapon Evolution

A Soul Weapon Evolution may close a Chapter centered on a Prior Weapon Form. The resulting Weapon Echo can preserve:

- how that form felt from within;
- its capability context, limitations, maintenance, and failure;
- the choices that led to transition;
- relationships and consequences tied to the form.

It cannot restore the form, satisfy a future Weapon Evolution Route, provide a Stable Multiplicity configuration, or grant a retired capability. It may serve as honest route evidence only through what was genuinely learned.

### Legacy Weapons

A [Legacy Weapon](LEGACY_WEAPONS.md) may share selected Weapon Echoes with later users under its own agency and access. No user inherits mastery, ownership, bond status, or every prior relationship through disclosure. Legacy Weapon rules own succession and later relationships.

### Compatibility

Weapon Echoes may reveal prior cooperation, conflict, body mismatch, or communication methods. Historical compatibility is evidence, not a guarantee about another person, body, Age, purpose, or current form. Assess the present claim through [Soul Weapon Compatibility](SOUL_WEAPON_COMPATIBILITY.md).

### Weapon Manifestations

A Weapon Echo cannot independently manifest, take physical form, wield the vessel, or become a Weapon Manifestation. Replaying an image in Soul Space is memory presentation rather than external Manifestation.

## System Interactions

### Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) owns the Link, Basis, Trigger, compatibility, State, and Dissonance used to access or share a Weapon Echo. Resonance does not make the memory objective, complete, or independently conscious.

### Soul Depth and Soul Integrity

Soul Depth can support integration of many complex Chapters. Soul Integrity constrains current recall, sharing, and resistance to damage. Neither is an Echo count, memory capacity score, truth rating, or permission to access another person's history.

### Soul Echoes

A Soul Echo and Weapon Echo may preserve different first-person accounts of the same event. The Soul Echo retains bounded Presence as part of its continuing soul; the Weapon Echo remains a non-agent memory structure of the Weapon Soul. They cannot merge, copy one another, or consent for one another.

### Soul Titles

A [Soul Title](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may help identify a meaningful Chapter or create a Trigger. It does not form, unlock, verify, own, or compel disclosure of a Weapon Echo.

### Soul Space, Constellations, and Soul Avatars

[Soul Space](../soul/SOUL_SPACE.md) may present an Echo as a memory scene, archive, scar, former workshop, or other symbol. It creates no physical item or stopped time. [Soul Constellations](../soul/SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md) may connect participants remembered in a Chapter without proving their current identity or consent. A [Soul Avatar](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) may receive willing Weapon Soul testimony but cannot turn the Echo into an Avatar contributor or extra actor.

### Reincarnation and Retained Instincts

[Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) owns the Bonded Soul's Life Reconciliation and Soul Echo formation. [Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) preserve body-shaped attention in the reincarnating soul; Weapon Echoes preserve the Weapon Soul's vessel-shaped memory. Neither grants the other's source or capability.

### Skills, Adaptive Skills, and Development

Weapon Echoes can provide instruction, examples, warnings, and capability context. The [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) and [Development System](../progression/README.md) own learning and reliable performance. [Adaptive Skills](../skills/ADAPTIVE_SKILLS.md) still require a valid Receiving Route, meaningful practice, feedback, and current embodiment.

### Human Classes and Professions

A Class, Profession, tradition, school, archive, court, or institution may seek Weapon Echo testimony. It cannot classify the Echo as institutional property, make memory disclosure compulsory by metaphysical authority, or convert testimony into automatic certification and mastery.

### Monster Evolution

A Weapon Echo may remember cooperation with a former monster form. It cannot restore that form, grant Species Traits, unlock an Evolution Route, or prove compatibility with the current monster. Monster Evolution remains separately owned.

### Akashic Archive and World Engine

The [Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) may preserve external Records related to the same Chapter. A Weapon Echo remains personal testimony. The World Engine owns surviving evidence, institutions, law, witnesses, reputations, ruins, and consequences that corroborate or contest it.

## GM Procedure

When forming or accessing a Weapon Echo:

1. **Confirm the source person.** Identify the awakened Weapon Soul and separate Soul Echoes, enchantments, spirits, constructs, copied memories, and projections.
2. **Define the Weapon Chapter.** State its meaningful history, boundaries, organizing relationship or form, and closure.
3. **Verify first-person provenance.** Apply Vessel Perspective, communication, participation, knowledge, mistakes, and blind spots.
4. **Test meaningful reconciliation.** Identify what changed, ended, remained unresolved, or became distinct enough to preserve.
5. **Prevent duplication.** Confirm the same history is not being split into several Echoes or used to copy another person's memory, Skill, or form.
6. **Establish the Weapon Echo Core.** Record memory, interpretation, relationships, capability context, uncertainty, and likely Triggers.
7. **Set current access.** Apply Resonance, Integrity, Personality, bond state, vessel condition, and any sealing or damage.
8. **Obtain consent for sharing.** Define recipient, channel, scope, privacy, cost, and interpretation limits.
9. **Resolve present use.** Apply current attention, embodiment, Skills, resources, and Weapon Recall Alignment without adding actions.
10. **Separate evidence layers.** Keep Weapon memory, Soul Echo testimony, Archive Records, public history, current belief, and world truth distinct.
11. **Apply consequences.** Record investigation, trust, disclosure, institutions, danger, and world response externally.
12. **Audit farming.** Reject formation or access based only on event counts, users, deaths, battles, recalls, or dramatic labels.

## Worked Examples

### The Dragon Warning

A spear accompanies its Bonded Soul into a new human incarnation. A dragon's wingbeat creates a Weapon Echo Trigger linked to a former Chapter. The Weapon Soul recalls that its prior wielder stood inside the sweep of a similar tail and warns, "Last time we fought a dragon, you stood too close."

The warning is the current Weapon Soul presenting its memory, not an Echo speaking independently. The dragon may differ, the new body may move differently, and the character still chooses and acts through current perception and Skill.

### Two Accounts of One Betrayal

A former monarch Soul Echo remembers being betrayed by a bodyguard. The bodyguard's shield preserves a Weapon Echo of the same scene and recalls the monarch ordering an unlawful execution before the bodyguard intervened.

Both accounts are sincere first-person testimony. Neither is objective truth. Surviving documents, current witnesses, Archive Records, and causal investigation may clarify what each could not know.

### The Retired Greatsword Form

A Soul Weapon evolves from a heavy greatsword into a lighter repairable form. Chapter Reconciliation preserves an Echo of the greatsword era: impact patterns, maintenance strain, shared siege work, and the Weapon Soul's pride and exhaustion.

The Echo can explain why the old form failed. It cannot summon the greatsword, restore its gate-breaking capability, or make that form a free Stable Multiplicity option.

### The Unwitnessed Vigil

An awakened lantern remains alone in a sealed passage for decades, maintaining a warning light through an established source while a dangerous seal weakens. No public witness knows.

When the passage finally opens and the duty ends, the Weapon Soul may reconcile a Chapter of solitude, fear, endurance, and changing belief. Public knowledge is unnecessary. The Echo preserves the lantern's experience, not facts beyond its senses.

### The Thousand Practice Cuts

A swordsman performs one thousand identical practice cuts with an awakened blade and asks for one Weapon Echo per hundred repetitions.

The repetitions may support Skill Development, relationship context, or one meaningful Chapter if understanding changes. Counting and relabeling them creates no Echoes, power, or clearer access.

### The Heir Demands Proof

An heir claims a Legacy Weapon and demands every Weapon Echo involving the royal family. The Weapon Soul remembers intimate disputes and one concealed crime but refuses unrestricted disclosure.

Inheritance creates no memory ownership. Investigation and accountability may continue through valid means, and the refusal has consequences, but no Echo can be extracted merely because the heir has legal custody.

### Familiar Timing, New Body

A Weapon Recall Alignment gives an intertwined bow the memory of coordinating with a former avian incarnation during a falling shot. The current incarnation is a ground-bound monster with different balance and senses.

The bow can explain the timing and identify mismatches. It cannot provide flight, former anatomy, or the old Skill. Any translated method requires current Adaptive Skill Development.

### The False Echo

A sword appears to replay a scene in which its maker commands eternal obedience. Investigation shows that an enchantment projects the maker's recorded image whenever the weapon resists an heir.

The recording is not a Weapon Echo and has no authority over the Weapon Soul. Source separation prevents a planted message from becoming personality, consent, or history.

### Peaceful Craft Chapter

An awakened chisel and a sculptor spend forty years restoring damaged memorials from opposing sides of a war. The sculptor dies peacefully after their final joint work, and the chisel later reconciles a Chapter about repair, contested memory, and the cost of making symbols public.

No battle is required. The Echo may inform future testimony and personality without granting artistic Skill or a restorative weapon power.

### Damaged Recall

A hostile ritual fractures access to one Weapon Echo. The Weapon Soul experiences disconnected pressure, smoke, and a name without context. The GM has already established the ritual's source, evidence, and recovery possibilities.

Repeated recall does not fix the damage by effort alone or create new fragments as separate Echoes. Recovery must address the actual injury and may remain incomplete.

## Safeguards

- **No second person:** a Weapon Echo is memory structure within the current Weapon Soul, not an autonomous former self, advisor, or soul.
- **No automatic Chapter:** users, deaths, battles, repairs, Ages, Evolutions, and elapsed time do not force Echo formation.
- **No event splitting:** one history cannot be relabeled into many Echoes to gain importance, access, or power.
- **No dormant retroactivity:** pre-awakening Vessel History informs identity but does not become a library of retroactive Weapon Echoes.
- **No copied user:** a Weapon Echo cannot contain another person's thoughts, Soul Echo, Skills, statistics, Titles, or private memory merely through contact.
- **No objective record:** first-person vessel memory remains incomplete, interpreted, and distinct from world truth or the Akashic Archive.
- **No stored form:** a Weapon Echo cannot restore a Prior Weapon Form, retired capability, material, enchantment, resource, or configuration.
- **No inherited mastery:** sharing memory or capability context does not grant a Skill, Class, Profession, monster trait, or reliable performance.
- **No extra actions:** Echo access, comparison, sharing, and Weapon Recall Alignment provide no additional actor, turn, reaction, or parallel process.
- **No unrestricted access:** the Bonded Soul, owner, heir, institution, god, or Archive interface has no automatic right to recall or disclosure.
- **No perfect warning system:** familiar memories do not guarantee present similarity, danger detection, truth, or successful response.
- **No echo chorus:** several Weapon Echoes cannot vote, speak independently, fuse into a superior personality, or run simultaneous analysis.
- **No personality overwrite:** an old Chapter may pressure current identity without becoming a compulsory former personality.
- **No access grind:** repeated identical recall, conversation, danger, or Resonance does not advance access indefinitely.
- **No erasure by convenience:** forgetting, silence, repair, Evolution, bond conflict, and new users do not destroy persistent history.
- **No false-source collapse:** recordings, enchantments, spirits, constructs, projections, Soul Echoes, and Archive Records retain their own provenance.
- **No campaign canon:** particular Weapon Chapters, Echo Cores, Triggers, access states, disclosures, and injuries remain external campaign state.
