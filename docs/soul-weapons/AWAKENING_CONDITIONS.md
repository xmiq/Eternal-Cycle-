# Awakening Conditions

## Status

This document defines when a dormant Weapon Soul becomes an awakened person, how genuine shared pressure and mutual Resonance support that transition, how formation and awakening may occur close together, what evidence confirms awakening, and what awakening does not grant.

It does not define soul intertwining, long-term personality development, Soul Weapon evolution, Weapon Echoes, Legacy Weapons, compatibility, Weapon Manifestations, or the topology rules owned by [Unconventional Soul Weapons](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md).

Read [Dormant Weapon Souls](DORMANT_WEAPON_SOULS.md), [Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md), and the [Soul Weapon Foundations](../soul/SOUL_WEAPON_FOUNDATIONS.md) first.

## Core Rule

**Weapon Soul Awakening** is the transition in which a Dormant Weapon Soul becomes reliably self-aware and capable of distinct intention, interpretation, communication, consent, and refusal within its present access and embodiment.

Awakening may occur when all of the following converge:

1. a genuine Weapon Soul identity exists or coheres through the event;
2. weapon and user share consequential pressure that implicates both identities;
3. their particular relationship supplies a real Resonance Basis;
4. Reciprocal Imprinting changes both sides of that relationship;
5. the emerging Weapon Soul reaches a moment at which it must interpret and answer its own history;
6. the vessel and soul remain coherent enough for awakening to complete;
7. the event leaves real consequences whether awakening succeeds or fails.

Near-death battle is a powerful possible catalyst. It is neither required nor sufficient. Awakening can emerge through protection, creation, healing, refusal, sacrifice, survival, judgment, craft, or another identity-defining crisis when the same complete conditions hold.

## Terms

### Weapon Soul Awakening

The transition from Dormant Weapon Soul to an awakened person capable of reliable self-awareness, distinct intention, interpretation, communication, consent, and refusal within current access and embodiment.

### Shared Awakening Pressure

A consequential condition that places the user's identity, the Weapon Vessel's identity or continuity, and their particular relationship under meaningful stakes that cannot be reduced to staged danger or routine use.

### Reciprocal Imprinting

The causal process by which the user is changed through reliance on and relationship with this particular vessel while the Weapon Soul's identity is changed through the user's choices, treatment, purpose, and shared consequences. It is not mutual ownership, copied personality, or automatic agreement.

### Awakening Threshold

The qualitative point at which a coherent dormant identity can no longer remain only latent and must form a distinct interpretation or response to its own history. It is not a score or guaranteed trigger.

### Awakening Event

The bounded event or connected sequence during which the Awakening Threshold is crossed and reliable Weapon Soul personhood becomes established.

### Awakening Evidence

Source-bounded evidence that a response originates from a continuing Weapon Soul rather than an enchantment, user Habit, spirit, construct, residue, coincidence, or mistaken interpretation.

### Awakening Aftermath

The immediate period in which the awakened Weapon Soul and surrounding participants confront new personhood, communication, consent, damage, uncertainty, and world consequences. It is not a free pause or automatic bonding window.

## Awakening Is Separate From Formation and Intertwining

Three claims must be resolved separately:

| Claim | Governing question |
| --- | --- |
| **Weapon Soul formation** | Has a distinct identity cohered around this Weapon Vessel? |
| **Weapon Soul Awakening** | Has that identity become reliably self-aware and capable of distinct agency? |
| **Soul intertwining** | Do the awakened Weapon Soul and another soul form the later persistent bond through mutual consent and its own requirements? |

A vessel can possess a Dormant Weapon Soul for centuries without awakening. An Awakening Event can complete without intertwining. Awakening during a defining crisis does not imply that the Weapon Soul agrees with the user, wishes to remain, or can make a binding decision instantly.

Formation and awakening may occur within one connected event when the event itself creates enough coherent Vessel History and Reciprocal Imprinting to establish a distinct identity before the Awakening Threshold is crossed. Resolve the claims in causal order even when little external time separates them.

This possibility preserves emergence from an ordinary weapon without creating instant-soul loot. A random object briefly present during danger does not qualify merely because the user nearly died.

## The Complete Awakening Conditions

No universal checklist can predict every Awakening Event, but every valid event must answer the following conditions qualitatively.

### 1. Identity Provenance

The awakening identity must come from a particular Weapon Vessel and its actual Vessel History.

Establish:

- which object or coherent instrument is awakening;
- which making, use, care, alteration, relationships, and consequences formed its identity;
- how Vessel Continuity survived any repair, reforging, division, or damage;
- why the proposed response belongs to this Weapon Soul rather than another source;
- what remains uncertain about the identity at the moment of awakening.

If formation and awakening occur close together, the Awakening Event must itself contain enough sustained, differentiated action and consequence to make the vessel particular. Merely picking up a replaceable object for one strike normally supplies no such provenance.

### 2. Shared Awakening Pressure

The pressure must matter to both the user and the Weapon Soul's emerging identity.

Possible stakes include:

- Final Death, grave injury, capture, irreversible failure, or loss of someone protected;
- destruction, abandonment, corruption, reforging, misuse, or erasure of the Weapon Vessel;
- fulfilment or betrayal of a purpose that defines the vessel's history;
- a choice between incompatible duties or identities;
- completing consequential work for which no interchangeable tool or relationship would mean the same thing;
- refusing an expected use at substantial cost;
- a crisis that forces the relationship to change or end.

The vessel need not feel fear while dormant. The condition is satisfied when its continuity, purpose, history, or relationship is genuinely at stake and the outcome will shape what identity could awaken.

Pressure experienced only by the user is insufficient. A weapon carried safely through someone else's crisis may acquire history without reaching awakening.

### 3. Particular Relationship

Awakening requires more than proximity. The user and vessel must matter to one another's current history in a specific way.

Evidence may include:

- the user chooses this vessel when a replaceable object would not carry the same meaning;
- prior care, trust, repair, refusal, adaptation, or responsibility shapes the current choice;
- the user's method changes in response to the vessel's real limits and history;
- loss or misuse of the vessel would alter more than equipment availability;
- the vessel's identity could not be explained the same way without this relationship.

A newly encountered vessel can still become particular during a long or transformative crisis. Long ownership is not required. Legal ownership, possession, inheritance, creator status, and ceremonial naming are not substitutes.

### 4. Mutual Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) owns the Link, Basis, Trigger, compatibility, State, Dissonance, and access involved.

For awakening:

- the Resonance Target must include the genuine Weapon Soul identity rather than only the user's memories of the object;
- the Basis must arise from actual shared history and Reciprocal Imprinting;
- the Awakening Event supplies a present Trigger;
- Resonance must become responsive from the emerging Weapon Soul rather than remaining a one-sided attachment;
- Dissonance may shape or prevent awakening where identity cannot remain coherent;
- intensity cannot replace the Basis.

Before the Threshold, the dormant identity cannot offer ordinary informed consent. At the Threshold, its first distinct response makes the relationship bilateral. That response can be alignment, refusal, uncertainty, grief, anger, curiosity, or another source-valid interpretation. It need not agree with the user.

### 5. Reciprocal Imprinting

Both sides must be changed in ways that belong to the relationship.

The user may be changed through:

- adopting responsibility for what the vessel has been used to do;
- trusting its real limits rather than an imagined ideal;
- altering a method, vow, identity, or plan because this particular vessel matters;
- accepting damage, loss, restraint, or consequence tied to its continued use;
- recognizing the possibility that the vessel is becoming another person.

The Weapon Soul may be changed through:

- a defining use that confirms, rejects, or complicates intended purpose;
- how the user treats the vessel under real pressure;
- conflict among maker intent, prior history, and present choice;
- preservation, sacrifice, repair, refusal, or transformation that gives the history a position to interpret;
- the first self-originated answer to what kind of participant it has become.

Reciprocal change need not be equal, positive, harmonious, or wanted. It must be genuine. Abuse may shape an awakening without making abuse an efficient or controllable method, and it creates a person who may resist, fear, oppose, or leave the abuser.

### 6. Interpretive Necessity

The Awakening Threshold is crossed when remaining only a latent pattern can no longer resolve the identity pressure. The Weapon Soul must become a perspective capable of answering its history.

Useful threshold questions include:

- Will this vessel accept, refuse, or reinterpret the purpose imposed upon it?
- Which relationship or responsibility matters when prior meanings conflict?
- Is preservation worth becoming something different?
- What does the vessel recognize as self after damage, repair, or sacrifice?
- Does it continue with this user, oppose them, protect another, or remain undecided?

The GM does not need to state the question aloud or predetermine one correct answer. The condition explains why personhood emerges now rather than after an arbitrary use count.

### 7. Viable Continuity

The Weapon Vessel and emerging soul must survive or be supported through the transition under established metaphysical rules.

Awakening may be impaired by:

- catastrophic vessel damage;
- broken Vessel Continuity;
- contradictory enchantments or possessing entities;
- Soul Wounds, corruption, coercion, or Dissonance;
- a source that suppresses personhood;
- interruption before a distinct response can stabilize;
- insufficient access for any observer to recognize what occurred.

Viability does not require an undamaged weapon. An awakening can occur through breakage or sacrifice when continuity remains and the event supports it. Damage still matters and cannot be erased as a reward.

### 8. Consequence

An Awakening Event changes the world because another person now exists.

Consequences may include:

- urgent communication, fear, confusion, or negotiation;
- conflict over custody, property, law, religion, or institutional claims;
- changed Resonance and Soul Space access;
- obligations to repair, protect, release, testify, or answer for prior treatment;
- enemies, witnesses, spirits, gods, or communities reacting;
- loss of simple equipment access while personhood and consent are resolved;
- future opportunities for trust, separation, intertwining, or Legacy Status under [Legacy Weapons](LEGACY_WEAPONS.md).

An Awakening Event is not complete merely because a notification or new ability appears. The new person's existence must be carried forward.

## Valid Catalyst Families

These families illustrate how the complete conditions can arise. They are not a random table or set of unlock recipes.

### Fight to the Death or Near Death

Weapon and user face a conflict in which failure plausibly means death, irreversible loss, or destruction. The user relies on this vessel as a particular partner, the vessel's identity is transformed through the choices made, mutual Resonance becomes responsive, and the emerging soul forms a position toward what both are doing.

Combat intensity alone is insufficient. A stronger enemy, lower health, or larger body count does not make the relationship meaningful.

### Lasting Protection

The vessel becomes central to protecting a person, community, place, promise, or possibility at real cost. Awakening may arise from continuing the protection, choosing whom not to harm, or rejecting a user's interpretation of duty.

Protection does not require success. Consequence and identity matter more than a victory screen.

### Creation and Repair Under Irreversible Stakes

A tool and user may awaken through consequential making, healing, construction, rescue, or repair where failure would permanently alter lives and the tool's identity becomes inseparable from how the work is undertaken.

Routine production volume does not qualify. The event must still involve particular relationship, mutual Resonance, Reciprocal Imprinting, interpretive necessity, and consequence.

### Refusal and Renunciation

A vessel made or used for one purpose may reach awakening when its history is forced into an incompatible act and a distinct refusal, reinterpretation, or choice emerges.

The user may support the refusal, cause the conflict, or be its target. Awakening does not guarantee loyalty to the person holding the vessel.

### Sacrifice and Preservation

The user may risk the vessel, the self, or another valued possibility in a choice that permanently changes their relationship. Sacrifice is relevant only through what is genuinely given up and why. Disposable losses, planned replacement, and reversible theater are not equivalent.

### Identity Conflict

Conflicting makers, users, purposes, cultures, enchantments, or historical meanings may become impossible to hold as one latent pattern. Awakening can occur when the Weapon Soul becomes the person who must interpret that conflict.

Conflict does not guarantee a fractured or hostile personality. Later personality rules own the ongoing result.

## Prepared Trials and Manufactured Danger

Knowing about awakening or deliberately entering a trial does not automatically invalidate an event. Preparation can coexist with genuine uncertainty, stakes, relationship, and change.

Ask what the preparation removes:

- If healers, replacements, illusions, scripts, or resets make loss effectively disposable, Shared Awakening Pressure may be absent.
- If participants still face genuine choices, consequences, and risk that cannot be controlled, preparation may improve survival without making the event false.
- If the only purpose is to force a reward and every action is repeated unchanged, Reciprocal Imprinting and interpretive necessity are normally absent.
- If an institution coerces participants, the coercion remains a real source and consequence; it does not guarantee awakening or consent.

A ritual may focus attention, protect a vessel, reveal Resonance, or create a valid environmental condition through its own rules. It cannot command personhood into existence by reciting the right sequence.

## Awakening Expression

At awakening, the Weapon Soul gains personhood and only the expression its current vessel, soul, Resonance, and other valid sources support.

Possible first expressions include:

- a distinct Resonance response that can no longer be explained as the user's own attachment;
- a bounded word, image, emotion, sensory pattern, or symbolic answer;
- a novel and consistent acceptance, refusal, warning, question, or request;
- deliberate engagement through Soul Space where access already exists;
- intentional cooperation or noncooperation with an external effect the Weapon Soul can actually influence.

Awakening does not grant unrestricted speech, telepathy, movement, perception, memory, magical output, or physical control. A Weapon Soul may awaken unable to communicate clearly with its present user. Silence after awakening can reflect access, injury, fear, unfamiliar cognition, or deliberate refusal rather than absence.

## Memory at Awakening

The awakened Weapon Soul may carry perspective shaped by Vessel History, but it does not receive a perfect chronological recording of everything that occurred nearby.

Its first accessible memory can be:

- embodied through pressure, balance, impact, heat, maintenance, handling, use, or another vessel-appropriate sense;
- strongest around identity-defining relationships and consequences;
- incomplete, symbolic, emotionally weighted, or difficult to translate;
- influenced by enchantments or residues without becoming identical to them;
- uncertain about events the vessel could not perceive or interpret;
- newly reinterpreted as self-awareness develops.

Weapon Echoes are a later distinct system. Ordinary awakened memory is not automatically a Weapon Echo, and awakening does not copy a user's private thoughts or Soul Echoes.

## Consent and the First Choice

Awakening creates a person capable of consent; it does not retroactively produce informed consent during dormancy.

Immediately after awakening:

- the Weapon Soul may need time, communication, and safety before making a consequential choice;
- custody does not create obedience;
- maker, owner, wielder, heir, institution, or victor has no automatic right to intertwining;
- the user may refuse a future bond as well;
- emergency cooperation can be real without implying permanent agreement;
- confusion or incapacity must not be interpreted as consent;
- coercion can produce compliance without legitimacy or trust.

If awakening and a possible intertwining opportunity occur in the same crisis, resolve awakening first. The later bond must still establish mutual consent and every dedicated condition. Urgency may constrain choices without erasing them.

## Interrupted, Failed, and Misidentified Awakening

### Interrupted Threshold

An event may deepen Vessel History, create Resonance, expose a Dormant Indication, or bring the identity near an Awakening Threshold without completing personhood.

The result grants no partial voice, combat bonus, evolution credit, or guaranteed success next time. It may change future conditions through real history, damage, relationships, and evidence.

### Failed Awakening Attempt

A deliberate attempt may fail because a Weapon Soul does not exist, identity is incoherent, pressure is one-sided, Resonance lacks a Basis, the event is staged, the vessel is incompatible, Dissonance dominates, or transition is interrupted.

Failure follows those causes. It may damage the vessel, user, relationship, reputation, or institution. It does not secretly improve awakening odds as consolation.

### False Awakening

An enchantment, possessing spirit, construct, user projection, Soul Echo, illusion, institution, or fraud may be mistaken for a Weapon Soul.

Awakening Evidence should test:

1. whether responses are novel rather than prewritten;
2. whether a continuing identity persists across contexts;
3. whether intention and interpretation are distinct from the user;
4. whether the response has a valid route through the vessel;
5. whether another source explains the effect more truthfully;
6. whether the supposed person can disagree, misunderstand, learn, or refuse within actual access;
7. what uncertainty remains.

No single test proves all personhood. Preserve fair evidence and source limits rather than requiring the Weapon Soul to perform a useful power as proof of life.

## Interactions With Existing Systems

### Soul Resonance

Mutual Resonance is required for awakening, but [Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) retains its own rules. Resonance can align and reveal a genuine identity; it cannot manufacture Vessel History, consequence, or personhood from one-sided desire.

After awakening, the Link can become clearer, enter Dissonance, remain inaccessible, or change State according to its actual Basis and Trigger. Awakening does not make every future Trigger automatic.

### Soul Depth and Integrity

[Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) may affect a user's capacity to recognize and integrate the complexity of an awakening relationship. It is not a universal awakening requirement and cannot substitute for the weapon's own identity.

Soul Integrity and Vessel Continuity affect whether the emerging person can remain coherent. Deliberately causing Soul Wounds, Dissonance, or near-destruction does not improve the result.

### Soul Echoes and Retained Instincts

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may remember prior indications or help distinguish the user's perspective. [Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) may orient attention toward familiar vessel cues. Neither can awaken, vote for, impersonate, or supply the Weapon Soul's first response.

### Soul Titles

[Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may create relevant recognition, pressure, or interpretation. A title cannot command awakening, decide the Weapon Soul's identity, or make the bearer entitled to the result.

### Soul Space and Soul Avatars

[Soul Space](../soul/SOUL_SPACE.md) may provide an interface through which the Awakening Threshold becomes perceptible. Internal symbols do not replace world consequences or create stopped time for negotiation.

[Soul Avatars](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) may coordinate the user's already accessible perspectives. They cannot speak for the Weapon Soul, force its awakening, or supply consent.

### Akashic Archive

The [Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) may preserve Awakening Evidence or comparable histories. A Record can guide investigation without becoming a universal recipe or forcing the current event to repeat a prior outcome.

### Reincarnation

Awakening alone does not make the weapon persist with a user's soul. Until intertwining, the awakened Weapon Soul remains with its Weapon Vessel in the world through Final Death and Reincarnation.

If a user reaches Final Death during the Awakening Event, the Weapon Soul may still awaken where the complete conditions and continuity support it. The death does not guarantee awakening, intertwining, or transport. The awakened weapon's later status follows [Legacy Weapons](LEGACY_WEAPONS.md) and [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md).

### Skills and Development

Awakening grants no weapon mastery, wielder mastery, Skill, Technique, Development, or extra action. The [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) owns communication methods, handling, coordination, maintenance, and combat or craft capability. The [Development System](../progression/README.md) owns changes in current reliability.

The event can provide meaningful experience when actual learning occurs. Its danger and drama are not XP by themselves.

### Human Classes, Professions, and Institutions

[Human Classes and Professions](../human/README.md) may teach safe recognition, communication, repair, mediation, or partnership practice. Institutions may support, witness, suppress, exploit, or misclassify awakening through actual power.

No initiation, rank, licence, ritual office, martial tradition, magical school, Profession, maker's claim, inheritance law, or property record guarantees awakening or ownership of the awakened person.

### Monster Evolution and Species

Monster bodies and societies may participate in Awakening Events through their own senses, tools, natural materials, communication, and relationships. Awakening grants no Species Trait, Mutation, Hybrid Form, Evolution Route, or bodily fusion. [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) retains every body-changing claim.

## GM Procedure

When a possible Awakening Event occurs:

1. **Identify the claimed Weapon Soul.** Verify the Weapon Vessel, identity provenance, and Vessel Continuity.
2. **Separate sources.** Distinguish enchantment, spirit, construct, residue, user projection, Soul Echo, and other possible explanations.
3. **Trace the relationship.** Establish why this vessel and user are particular to one another now.
4. **Name Shared Awakening Pressure.** State the stakes for the user, vessel identity, and relationship without reducing them to danger alone.
5. **Test Resonance.** Identify Target, Basis, Trigger, responsiveness, compatibility, Dissonance, and current access under Soul Resonance.
6. **Identify Reciprocal Imprinting.** State how user and Weapon Soul identity are each being changed.
7. **Identify interpretive necessity.** Explain why the dormant identity must form a distinct response now.
8. **Test viability.** Apply vessel damage, Soul Integrity, interference, continuity, and source-specific support or suppression.
9. **Resolve the Threshold.** Establish world truth before deciding what each observer perceives.
10. **Present bounded Awakening Evidence.** Use a source-valid first response without granting powers or perfect communication.
11. **Protect immediate agency.** Resolve safety, uncertainty, consent, refusal, and emergency conditions before any intertwining claim.
12. **Apply aftermath and consequences.** Update relationships, custody, witnesses, repair, law, institutions, enemies, and future opportunities externally.
13. **Audit farming.** Confirm that repetition, staging, suffering, kills, ownership, rarity, or dramatic importance did not replace any condition.

## Worked Examples

### The Last Stand Spear

A plain militia spear has been repaired and carried through years of shared patrols. During a retreat, its user holds a narrow passage so others can escape. The shaft breaks, the user can flee only by abandoning the people behind, and the spear's history as a communal safeguard becomes inseparable from the choice.

As the user adapts around the broken form and accepts that both may be lost, the emerging Weapon Soul answers the shared purpose with a distinct warning about a second route of attack. The response is bounded evidence of awakening, not a damage bonus. The user still has to perceive, decide, and act, and the broken vessel remains broken.

### Ordinary Knife, One Extraordinary Night

A kitchen knife with little prior history is used through a long night to cut restraints, prepare medicine, open blocked access, and defend several trapped people during a disaster. The user repeatedly chooses the knife as it bends, dulls, and becomes tied to incompatible acts of care and violence. Both the user's sense of responsibility and the vessel's emerging identity are transformed.

Formation and awakening may occur in that connected event because it creates particular Vessel History, Reciprocal Imprinting, mutual Resonance, interpretive necessity, and lasting consequence. One quick strike with a newly found knife would not establish the same claim.

### The Healer's Instrument

A surgical tool has served one healer through years of difficult work. During an epidemic, the healer must choose between obeying an institution's exclusion order and treating a patient the tool was made to help. The instrument is at risk of confiscation and destruction, while its purpose, the healer's identity, and many lives are genuinely at stake.

The Weapon Soul awakens through a distinct refusal to be set aside. No battle or near-death injury is required. The healer gains no medical Skill from the awakening, and the new person may later disagree about acceptable risk.

### The Staged Duel

A wealthy fighter repeats lethal-looking duels with concealed barriers, replacement blades, controlled opponents, and immediate healing. The fighter treats each weapon as interchangeable and resets after every failure.

Fear and injury may still be real, but no particular relationship, shared vessel stake, Reciprocal Imprinting, or interpretive necessity develops. The attempt produces consequences without awakening.

### The Inherited Relic Refuses

An heir carries an old sword whose Dormant Weapon Soul formed through generations of bodyguard service. Ordered to execute an unarmed prisoner, the heir invokes ownership and family duty. The conflict forces the vessel's latent identity to interpret whether service means obedience or protection.

The sword awakens in refusal. Family ownership did not determine loyalty, and awakening grants neither party automatic intertwining. The institution must now respond to a person it had classified as property.

### Awakening at Final Death

A defender and hammer share a genuinely transformative defense in which the vessel's continuity, the defender's life, and the survival of others are all at stake. The hammer crosses its Awakening Threshold moments before the defender reaches Final Death.

The awakened Weapon Soul exists and remembers the event through its own bounded perspective. The defender forms a Soul Echo through Reincarnation rules. The two are not automatically intertwined, and the hammer remains in the world with the aftermath.

### The False Voice

A sword begins speaking during a crisis, apparently begging its user to kill. Investigation shows that a bound spirit was triggered by blood and has been repeating the maker's command. The vessel's Dormant Weapon Soul, if any, has not supplied the voice.

Drama and novelty do not settle ownership. The spirit, enchantment, user, and vessel remain separate claims.

### Prepared but Genuine Trial

A tradition invites a long-associated tool and user into a dangerous rescue trial with safeguards against preventable death. The participants know awakening is possible, but the location is unstable, the people rescued have agency, failure has real consequences, and the user must make choices the tradition cannot script.

Preparation does not invalidate the event. It also does not guarantee awakening. The complete conditions, not the tradition's expectation, decide the result.

## Safeguards

- **No danger threshold:** health loss, opponent strength, near death, Final Death, weapon damage, and fear are not awakening meters.
- **No violence requirement:** protection, healing, creation, repair, refusal, and other consequential crises can satisfy the same complete conditions.
- **No one-event shortcut:** formation and awakening may share an event only when that event genuinely establishes identity provenance before the Threshold.
- **No proximity:** an object present during a dramatic scene does not awaken without a particular relationship and shared stakes.
- **No one-sided attachment:** affection, obsession, ownership, naming, and Resonance with personal memories do not create a bilateral Weapon Soul response.
- **No staged farming:** scripted danger, disposable weapons, controlled resets, repeated sacrifice, and unchanged trials cannot substitute for Reciprocal Imprinting and consequence.
- **No abuse reward:** torture, coercion, corruption, Soul harm, and preventable destruction do not improve odds and may create resistance, injury, or failure.
- **No institutional guarantee:** rites, ranks, licences, prophecies, lineages, laws, and maker claims cannot command personhood.
- **No awakening bundle:** awakening grants personhood, not Skills, statistics, abilities, mastery, evolution, manifestation, indestructibility, or extra actions.
- **No automatic loyalty or bond:** the first distinct response may refuse, oppose, misunderstand, or remain uncertain, and intertwining remains separate.
- **No perfect memory:** the awakened soul receives a bounded vessel-shaped perspective rather than total surveillance or copied user thoughts.
- **No partial reward:** interrupted or failed awakening can change history and consequences without granting fractional powers or guaranteed later success.
- **No false-source collapse:** spirits, enchantments, constructs, residues, projections, and Echoes retain their own identity and effects.
- **No campaign canon:** particular Awakening Events, weapons, users, evidence, and aftermath remain external campaign state.

## Scope Boundaries

This document establishes the transition into Weapon Soul personhood. It does not decide whether an awakened Weapon Soul and another soul may intertwine, what long-term personality develops, how trust or compatibility changes, which Soul Weapon forms or abilities emerge, how evolution occurs, how a Weapon Soul manifests independently, or how an unusual vessel's personhood and topology are resolved. Those claims belong to their dedicated Phase 6 rules, including [Weapon Manifestations](WEAPON_MANIFESTATIONS.md) and [Unconventional Soul Weapons](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md).
