# Soul Intertwining

## Status

This document defines how an awakened Weapon Soul and another continuing soul may form, maintain, suspend, and end the persistent relationship that makes the partnership a Soul Weapon.

It does not define long-term Weapon Soul personality, Soul Weapon evolution, Weapon Echoes, Legacy Weapons, detailed [Soul Weapon Compatibility](SOUL_WEAPON_COMPATIBILITY.md), the external-expression procedure owned by [Weapon Manifestations](WEAPON_MANIFESTATIONS.md), or the topology and multi-person arrangements owned by [Unconventional Soul Weapons](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md).

Read [Dormant Weapon Souls](DORMANT_WEAPON_SOULS.md), [Awakening Conditions](AWAKENING_CONDITIONS.md), [Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md), and [Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) first.

## Core Rule

**Soul Intertwining** is a voluntary, persistent relationship in which an awakened Weapon Soul and one continuing soul establish a mutual Soul Weapon Bond without merging identities.

A valid intertwining requires:

1. two distinct and sufficiently coherent souls;
2. an awakened Weapon Soul capable of consent and refusal;
3. genuine shared history and mutual Soul Resonance;
4. an informed Intertwining Accord accepted by both participants;
5. reciprocal trust or recognition sufficient for the bond being attempted;
6. enough present Soul Integrity and relational compatibility to sustain the connection;
7. a viable Weapon Vessel and continuity route;
8. acceptance of the bond's responsibilities, risks, and world consequences.

Successful intertwining establishes a durable relationship and a standing route by which the Weapon Soul may accompany the Bonded Soul through Reincarnation under their Accord. It does not grant a Skill, statistic, Class, species trait, evolution, manifestation, mastery, perfect communication, obedience, or an additional action.

The bond is persistent, not inescapable. Either person may refuse a proposed intertwining, set boundaries, renegotiate continued passage, suspend cooperation, or seek severance under the rules below.

## Terms

### Soul Intertwining

The voluntary transition by which an awakened Weapon Soul and one continuing soul establish a persistent Soul Weapon Bond while remaining distinct persons.

### Bonded Soul

The continuing soul partnered with the Weapon Soul through Soul Intertwining. The term does not imply current ownership, physical possession, social rank, or ability to wield the Weapon Vessel.

### Intertwining Accord

The mutual, informed agreement that establishes the intended bond, its immediate boundaries, its standing Reincarnation passage terms, and the participants' right to renegotiate, suspend, or end it.

The Accord may be communicated through words, emotion, action, ritual, imagery, Soul Space, or another reliable channel. The form matters less than whether both participants can understand the material commitment and answer as themselves.

### Soul Weapon Bond

The persistent relationship created by successful Soul Intertwining. It can support target-specific Resonance, continuity across incarnations, bounded communication, cooperative expression, and later Soul Weapon development when their separate rules permit.

### Passage Accord

The part of the Intertwining Accord that states whether and under what standing conditions the Weapon Soul accompanies the Bonded Soul through Final Death, Life Reconciliation, Interlife, and Reincarnation. It does not itself decide the Weapon Vessel's physical manifestation.

### Bond Boundary

A mutually recognized limit on access, use, disclosure, communication, memory sharing, manifestation, custody, passage, or other cooperation within the Soul Weapon Bond.

### Bond Suspension

A state in which the Soul Weapon Bond persists but one or more forms of access, cooperation, expression, or passage are closed. Suspension does not erase shared history, personhood, or the right to refuse.

### Bond Severance

The exceptional ending of a Soul Weapon Bond by mutual decision, valid unilateral withdrawal, or an explicit hostile effect that satisfies the protections for soul harm. Severance does not erase either person's identity, memories, consequences, or independently owned Development.

## What Intertwining Is Not

### Not Soul Fusion

The participants do not become one soul.

- each retains its own identity, Soul Integrity, Soul Imprints, Soul Depth, Soul Titles, memories, perspective, and agency;
- neither becomes a component, subordinate mind, internal character, or emotional organ of the other;
- injury to one does not automatically duplicate the same injury in the other;
- disagreement does not prove corruption or bond failure;
- ending the bond does not destroy either person by default.

[Skill Fusion](../skills/SKILL_FUSION.md) remains a Skill Engine procedure and cannot fuse the participants, their souls, or their capability histories.

### Not Ownership

Making, buying, finding, inheriting, carrying, repairing, naming, commanding, or legally owning a Weapon Vessel does not establish authority over its Weapon Soul.

A culture may call a Bonded Soul a master, bearer, keeper, chosen wielder, thief, saint, or criminal. Those are social interpretations. They do not alter the metaphysical relationship or remove either participant's agency.

### Not a Capability Package

Intertwining does not install:

- weapon proficiency;
- the Weapon Soul's prior capabilities;
- the Bonded Soul's Skills in the Weapon Soul;
- missing anatomy, senses, mana, resources, or technique;
- a new Human Class or Profession;
- a monster Evolution Route;
- passive statistics, durability, damage, luck, or immunity;
- automatic evolution, form change, summoning, or manifestation.

The [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md), [Development System](../progression/README.md), current embodiment, and later Soul Weapon rules own those claims.

### Not Permanent Consent to Everything

Agreeing to intertwine does not waive future consent. The Accord can authorize standing cooperation in named circumstances, but it cannot make every future use, intrusion, disclosure, alteration, or passage acceptable by default.

Likewise, one disagreement does not erase the bond. Relationships can contain conflict, repair, distance, changed boundaries, and incompatible judgments without becoming false.

### Not a Soul Constellation

A Soul Weapon Bond may contribute history to a [Soul Constellation](../soul/SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md), but intertwining alone does not establish recurring reciprocal transformation across lives. Constellation membership remains separately qualified.

## Preconditions

### 1. Distinct Persons

The proposed participants must be two distinct souls.

The Weapon Soul must already have crossed the Awakening Threshold and demonstrated enough self-awareness, interpretation, consent, and refusal to participate. A Dormant Weapon Soul cannot consent. A user's projection, enchantment, bound spirit, Soul Echo, construct, or copied personality cannot be substituted for the Weapon Soul.

Intertwining may occur soon after awakening only when the new person has enough clarity and freedom to understand the commitment. Urgency, gratitude, injury, dependence, and a user's expectations are reasons for caution, not shortcuts.

### 2. Shared Basis

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) must establish a genuine Basis between these particular souls.

Useful evidence includes:

- shared choices that changed both participants;
- reliance tested against the vessel's real limits;
- care, repair, refusal, adaptation, protection, or sacrifice that shaped the relationship;
- conflict that produced recognition rather than mere domination;
- a shared purpose understood from both perspectives;
- consequences for which both accept some responsibility.

Intensity cannot replace history. A spectacular awakening may begin the Basis without making a permanent bond inevitable.

### 3. Reciprocal Recognition

Each participant must recognize the other as a person whose choices matter to the proposed relationship.

They need not like, obey, forgive, or fully understand one another. They must be able to distinguish partnership from possession, coercion, rescue debt, institutional duty, or assumed destiny.

A bond built around honest rivalry, difficult duty, or guarded cooperation can qualify. A relation in which one participant is treated only as a tool cannot.

### 4. Informed Accord

Both participants must understand the material commitments currently knowable, including:

- that their identities remain separate;
- that the bond is intended to persist beyond the current body and ordinary custody;
- what communication or internal contact is presently proposed;
- what uses or requests require fresh cooperation;
- whether a standing Passage Accord is being established;
- known risks from Soul Strain, Dissonance, vessel damage, hostile attention, or incompatible embodiment;
- how either participant may pause, renegotiate, or end cooperation;
- which facts remain uncertain because later Soul Weapon rules or world conditions are unknown.

No participant must possess perfect cosmological knowledge. Informed consent requires honest disclosure of material known facts and uncertainty, not omniscience.

### 5. Free Choice

Consent must originate from each participant rather than from:

- ownership, law, rank, creator authority, inheritance, or prophecy;
- magical compulsion, Soul intrusion, possession, threat, torture, or hostage pressure;
- an Echo, Avatar contributor, institution, god, spirit, family, or prior wielder speaking on a participant's behalf;
- silence, incapacity, confusion, gratitude, emergency dependence, or assumed loyalty;
- a command embedded in an enchantment or awakening tradition.

External danger does not automatically invalidate choice. Two persons may make a real commitment during a crisis. The GM must establish that each can answer the relationship rather than merely submit to the threat or to the other participant.

### 6. Integrity and Capacity

Both souls and the Weapon Vessel must be coherent enough to sustain the proposed bond.

[Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) can support more complex integration, but it cannot manufacture trust, consent, identity, or compatibility. Low Depth is not a universal prohibition, and high Depth is not automatic qualification.

Relevant constraints may include:

- active Soul Strain or Soul Wounds;
- unstable Vessel Continuity;
- severe Resonance Dissonance;
- incompatible passage requirements;
- interference from a spirit, enchantment, curse, institution, or world law;
- inability to distinguish either participant from another source;
- a proposed scope too broad for current Integrity and access.

The result may be delay, a narrower Accord, Bond Suspension, or failure. It is not a percentage chance or bond level.

### 7. Viable Continuity

The Weapon Soul must remain anchored to a coherent Weapon Vessel under [Vessel Continuity](DORMANT_WEAPON_SOULS.md). Intertwining does not repair a broken vessel, settle disputed identity, duplicate a divided Weapon Soul, or create a physical form from nothing.

If physical and metaphysical continuity are uncertain, resolve that uncertainty before granting a passage route. An Accord can express intent, but intention alone cannot make transport possible.

### 8. Accepted Consequence

The participants must accept that intertwining enters the world as a relationship, not a private reward flag.

Possible consequences include:

- duties to one another;
- changed custody, inheritance, law, religious interpretation, or institutional interest;
- enemies targeting the vessel or relationship;
- practical constraints imposed by future bodies;
- exposure to one another's distress or disagreement through established channels;
- the need to negotiate repair, use, secrecy, travel, and passage;
- grief, responsibility, or conflict when one participant changes.

The exact consequences are campaign state. The rule requires causality, not a prescribed punishment.

## The Intertwining Process

Intertwining is qualitative. It has no universal ritual, duration, resource cost, ceremony, bond meter, or probability.

### 1. Establish World Truth

Confirm:

- the Weapon Soul is awakened and distinct;
- the Weapon Vessel has coherent continuity;
- both participants are present through a valid contact route;
- no other source is impersonating, compelling, or replacing either person;
- the relevant Resonance Basis and current Trigger are genuine.

### 2. State the Proposed Relationship

The participants identify what they are agreeing to now. At minimum, the proposal must distinguish:

- persistent identity and recognition;
- current communication or contact;
- ordinary cooperation and use;
- access to Soul Space or private memories, if any;
- standing passage through Reincarnation;
- boundaries and conditions for later renegotiation.

An Accord can be narrow. The participants do not need to pre-authorize every future evolution, manifestation, use, or form.

### 3. Test Mutual Understanding

Each participant must have a reliable opportunity to interpret the commitment and answer.

When communication is limited, use demonstrated choices, repeated confirmation, source-valid Resonance, and observable boundaries. Do not use ambiguity to manufacture consent. If a material term cannot be understood, narrow or delay the Accord.

### 4. Test Consent and Dissonance

Obtain a separate answer from each participant. Agreement may include uncertainty or conditions. A refusal, withdrawal, or request for time ends the current attempt without treating the participant as defective.

Resonance Dissonance may reveal contradictions such as:

- one participant expects ownership while the other expects equality;
- one seeks permanent passage while the other agrees only to current cooperation;
- one relies on a false account of shared history;
- a Soul Title, institution, god, Echo, or former user is being mistaken for authority;
- the proposed bond would require identity erasure or inaccessible capability.

Dissonance can prompt clarification or a narrower Accord. It cannot be overwhelmed by intensity.

### 5. Form the Bond

When the complete conditions hold, mutual Resonance establishes a persistent target-specific Soul Weapon Bond.

The bond records:

- the identities of its two participants;
- the genuine Basis and shared provenance;
- the agreed current scope and Bond Boundaries;
- the Passage Accord;
- any known constraints, Dissonance, or unresolved uncertainty;
- the fact of mutual establishment without erasing either perspective.

The bond is a relationship, not an item property. It persists as Soul-level continuity while its Current Access and Embodied Expression may change.

### 6. Resolve Immediate Access

After formation, determine what is actually accessible now.

Possible immediate outcomes include:

- recognition of the other's presence;
- a bounded communication channel;
- improved coordination based on mutual feedback;
- a consensual Soul Space Region or Gate representing the relationship;
- awareness of serious Dissonance or distress;
- no new outward capability at all.

Every effect must have its own source, access, embodiment, cost, and permission. Intertwining itself supplies continuity and relationship, not an unrestricted power set.

### 7. Apply World Consequences

Record current custody, witnesses, law, institutions, relationships, obligations, danger, vessel condition, and reputation outside the canonical repository. Observers may misunderstand or dispute what occurred.

## Standard Bond Structure

Standard Soul Intertwining is dyadic: one awakened Weapon Soul and one Bonded Soul.

A Weapon Soul can sustain only one standard Soul Weapon Bond at a time because its standing passage route cannot simultaneously follow independent reincarnation continuities. Bond Suspension does not make the Weapon Soul unbound. Only Severance makes another standard intertwining possible, and the new relationship must satisfy every requirement independently.

A continuing soul is not assigned a universal Soul Weapon slot. It may form more than one separate standard bond only when every relationship independently satisfies the complete rules and the soul can sustain the resulting complexity. Multiple bonds do not:

- pool identities, memories, capabilities, or evolution;
- create a group mind or shared inventory;
- provide extra actions or simultaneous forms;
- waive current embodiment, carrying, access, maintenance, or cooperation;
- make Depth a collection capacity score;
- permit one Weapon Soul to consent for another.

Configurations outside this standard structure remain unsupported until an explicit later rule defines them.

## Persistence and Reincarnation

### Persistent Relationship, Variable Access

The Soul Weapon Bond persists across lives as a Soul-level relationship. The following may change with each incarnation:

- recognition and memory access;
- communication medium and clarity;
- whether the Weapon Vessel can appear, be located, or be physically used;
- size, shape, sensory, cultural, legal, and environmental compatibility;
- available Skills, resources, and Practised Reliability;
- trust, boundaries, goals, and willingness to cooperate;
- how the bond appears in Soul Space;
- public knowledge and world response.

Persistence proves neither immediate access nor present capability.

### Standing Passage

An established Soul Weapon Bond includes an explicit Passage Accord. At Final Death and Life Reconciliation:

1. confirm the bond remains established rather than Severed;
2. establish the current Passage Accord and any valid prior renegotiation;
3. distinguish each participant's choice from coercion, suppression, impersonation, or incapacity;
4. determine whether the Soul Weapon Bond and Weapon Soul accompany the Bonded Soul;
5. preserve world consequences and any physical-manifestation limits;
6. establish access in the next incarnation separately.

If both participants have maintained standing consent, the established bond and Weapon Soul accompany the reincarnating soul as stated in [Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md). The weapon is not inventory, and its physical expression follows [Weapon Manifestations](WEAPON_MANIFESTATIONS.md).

### Refusing Passage

Either participant may reject a future passage when able to make a valid choice. A Weapon Soul is not cargo, and the Bonded Soul cannot use Final Death to compel transport.

A valid refusal before Life Reconciliation places the passage route into Bond Suspension or proceeds to Bond Severance according to the expressed choice. The Weapon Soul remains anchored to its Weapon Vessel in the world unless another completed rule establishes a different result. The relationship's history remains real.

When sudden Final Death prevents a new discussion, the last valid Passage Accord governs. Silence at death does not create new consent or cancel prior informed standing consent. Evidence of magical suppression, possession, falsified communication, or coercion must be resolved before treating an apparent refusal or agreement as valid.

The future status of an awakened Weapon Soul left in the world follows [Legacy Weapons](LEGACY_WEAPONS.md). Legacy Status does not undo the bond state or transfer it to a later bearer.

### A New Body Does Not Renew Ownership

The new incarnation begins as the same continuing soul but a new embodied person with present agency, cognition, culture, needs, and constraints. The bond persists, while expectations must be translated into the new life.

The Weapon Soul may remember more shared history than the current incarnation can access. It cannot use that asymmetry to speak for the current person, force a former role, or demand exact recreation of a prior relationship. The current incarnation likewise cannot treat persistence as ownership or inherited mastery.

## Boundaries, Conflict, and Change

### Ongoing Cooperation

An Intertwining Accord may authorize routine cooperation within clear boundaries. A fresh explicit conversation is not required before every familiar act when both have established standing expectations and remain able to object.

Fresh cooperation is required when an action materially exceeds those expectations, such as:

- exposing private memories;
- altering the Weapon Vessel or Soul Weapon identity;
- entering a guarded Soul Space Region;
- invoking an unfamiliar or dangerous form;
- using the weapon for a purpose the Weapon Soul has opposed;
- transferring custody under coercive conditions;
- changing the Passage Accord;
- accepting severe soul-level risk.

Physical custody can allow a person to move or misuse the vessel through ordinary causality. It does not compel the Weapon Soul to provide cooperation or powers it actually controls. Refusal does not make the object immovable or indestructible unless a separate established capability causes that effect.

### Conflict

Conflict may produce:

- negotiation or changed Bond Boundaries;
- reduced communication or cooperation;
- Resonance Dissonance;
- Bond Suspension;
- development of either participant through meaningful reflection and action;
- social, legal, emotional, or tactical consequences;
- eventual repair or severance.

The GM must not use disagreement as automatic punishment, corruption, obedience failure, or proof that one participant secretly lacks agency.

### Suspension

Bond Suspension can be mutual or unilateral where a valid boundary is invoked. It should name what is suspended:

- communication;
- internal contact;
- use of a form or capability;
- physical cooperation;
- passage;
- all active expression while the persistent relationship remains.

Suspension is not stasis. The world continues, the vessel may remain vulnerable, relationships may change, and consequences accumulate. Reopening access requires a valid route, renewed cooperation, and resolution of relevant constraints.

### Severance

Voluntary Bond Severance requires a clear decision by one or both participants and a viable way to end the persistent relationship without using disappearance as retroactive coercion. It may involve grief, obligations, custody disputes, altered Resonance, or world consequences, but it is not metaphysical punishment for withdrawal.

Hostile severance is soul harm. It requires an explicit source, fair warning, meaningful resistance, proportionate consequence, and the safeguards in [Soul Fundamentals](../soul/SOUL_FUNDAMENTALS.md) and [Soul Engine Safeguards](../soul/SOUL_ENGINE_SAFEGUARDS.md). A spell, god, institution, owner, or GM cannot cut the bond merely for narrative convenience.

After severance:

- both persons remain themselves unless an independent injury says otherwise;
- shared history and world consequences remain;
- neither gains or loses unrelated personal Development;
- the Weapon Soul remains anchored according to valid vessel and world conditions;
- re-intertwining is not automatic and must satisfy the complete rules again;
- another bond is not inherited, transferred, or awarded by custody.

## Knowledge and Privacy

Intertwining creates no unrestricted mind reading or shared memory.

Track separately:

- what each participant personally experienced;
- what each remembers or can currently access;
- what one deliberately communicates;
- what bounded emotions, impressions, or warnings a valid channel carries;
- what Soul Resonance indicates without explaining;
- what an Echo, Archive Record, observer, institution, or myth claims;
- what remains hidden, mistaken, sealed, or unknown.

A Bonded Soul does not inherit the Weapon Soul's entire Vessel History. A Weapon Soul does not inherit every incarnation, Echo, Skill, Title, private thought, or moral judgment of the Bonded Soul.

A consensual [Soul Space](../soul/SOUL_SPACE.md) Region can represent the relationship. It is an interface, not proof that the Weapon Soul lives inside, belongs to, or is a fragment of the Bonded Soul. Gates, Thresholds, disclosure, and withdrawal remain scoped.

## System Interactions

### Soul Resonance

Soul Resonance owns the Basis, Target, Link, Trigger, access State, Dissonance, and alignment of the Resonance itself. [Soul Weapon Compatibility](SOUL_WEAPON_COMPATIBILITY.md) owns the wider layer-by-layer assessment of a proposed interaction. Intertwining establishes a durable target-specific relationship but does not turn either system into a universal bond score or guarantee alignment.

### Soul Depth and Soul Integrity

Soul Depth can support the complexity of one or several persistent bonds. Soul Integrity governs current coherence and vulnerability. Neither replaces consent, relationship, compatibility, or current access, and neither determines a universal number of Soul Weapons.

### Soul Echoes

A [Soul Echo](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may remember a past relationship, advise the current incarnation, or disagree about a proposed bond. It cannot consent, refuse, renegotiate, or sever on behalf of the continuing soul or Weapon Soul. Intertwining does not turn the Weapon Soul into an Echo.

### Soul Titles

A [Soul Title](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may recognize identity shaped through a Soul Weapon relationship and influence reactions or symbolic authority. It cannot command a Weapon Soul, prove compatibility, transfer a bond, or make a claimed partnership true.

### Soul Constellations

Repeated reciprocal transformation may separately qualify the participants for a Soul Constellation. The Constellation cannot compel contact, passage, cooperation, or reunion, and bond persistence alone does not satisfy its formation rules.

### Soul Space and Soul Avatars

Soul Space may provide a consensual communication interface. A [Soul Avatar](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) may coordinate with a willing Weapon Soul under each system's rules. Neither system absorbs the Weapon Soul, bypasses Bond Boundaries, grants manifestation, or provides an extra actor.

### Reincarnation and Retained Instincts

Reincarnation owns Final Death, Life Reconciliation, Interlife, candidates, and the new body. The Soul Weapon Bond owns standing passage eligibility. [Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) may preserve body-shaped attention relevant to prior use but do not preserve the weapon, the bond, proficiency, anatomy, or consent.

### Development and Skills

Development owns current capability change. Skills own learned capabilities and performance methods. Shared practice may let both participants develop relevant but separately owned capabilities. Intertwining transfers none automatically and cannot replace current-life practice, feedback, maintenance, or embodiment.

### Human Classes and Professions

A [Human Class, Profession, or Institution](../human/README.md) may teach use, recognize a role, regulate custody, facilitate communication, or contest personhood. It cannot grant, own, inherit, rank, certify, or revoke a Soul Weapon Bond by social authority alone.

### Monster Evolution

A monster's current form may make a Weapon Vessel easy, difficult, dangerous, or impossible to use. [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) can alter anatomy and access through its own causal routes but does not grant intertwining or make the weapon evolve. A later human or monster life must establish current expression separately.

### Akashic Archive and World Engine

The [Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) may preserve evidence of a bond or shared event. A Record cannot provide present consent, perfect private truth, or ownership. The World Engine owns external consequences, witnesses, law, institutions, custody, distance, damage, and historical change.

## Adjudication Procedure

When a Soul Intertwining claim arises:

1. **Identify both souls.** Confirm Weapon Soul awakening, distinct identity, current Integrity, and valid contact.
2. **Verify the vessel.** Apply Vessel Continuity and separate spirits, enchantments, constructs, and projections.
3. **Name the Basis.** State the actual shared history and mutual Resonance rather than intensity or possession.
4. **Test reciprocal recognition.** Establish that each treats the other as a person relevant to the proposed relationship.
5. **State the Accord.** Name current cooperation, access, Bond Boundaries, uncertainty, and Passage Accord.
6. **Test understanding.** Confirm each participant can interpret the material commitment through a reliable channel.
7. **Test free consent separately.** Remove ownership, prophecy, rank, coercion, emergency dependence, and assumed gratitude from the decision.
8. **Apply Integrity, Dissonance, and continuity.** Narrow, delay, suspend, or fail the attempt where current conditions require.
9. **Establish the bond.** Record the persistent relationship without merging identity or granting a capability package.
10. **Resolve current access.** Name only effects with valid source, permission, embodiment, cost, and current capability.
11. **Apply world consequences.** Keep custody, observers, institutions, danger, obligations, and current vessel state in campaign records.
12. **Audit exploits.** Confirm that no ownership, ritual, sacrifice, danger, bond count, death, or prior life replaced the complete requirements.

## Worked Examples

### The Last Stand Does Not Decide for Them

A militia spear awakens while it and its defender hold a passage long enough for refugees to escape. Their mutual Resonance is intense, but the new Weapon Soul is frightened, injured, and uncertain whether it wants another battle.

The defender asks for permanent partnership immediately. The Weapon Soul asks for time. Awakening succeeds; intertwining does not yet occur. Gratitude and shared danger are not consent. Months later, after repair, disagreement, and deliberate cooperation, they form a narrow Accord that permits communication and standing passage while requiring fresh agreement for lethal use.

### Intertwining During a Crisis

A surgical instrument awakened years earlier and has worked beside a healer through an epidemic. When an institution attempts to destroy it as forbidden property, both clearly understand the risk, affirm that neither owns the other, and choose continuing partnership before the seizure.

The crisis supplies a Trigger, not the consent by itself. Their prior Basis, reliable communication, informed Passage Accord, mutual choice, and accepted consequences make intertwining valid. The bond grants no healing Skill and does not make the damaged tool usable without repair.

### The Coerced Rite

A royal order chains an awakened sword inside a ritual circle. Its legal owner offers a blood sacrifice and declares that tradition binds the sword to the royal soul forever. The sword refuses.

The ritual may create an enchantment, injury, false signal, or hostile tether if another system permits those effects. It cannot create a Soul Weapon Bond. Rank, ownership, sacrifice, and metaphysical force do not substitute for the Weapon Soul's answer.

### The Faithful Weapon Disagrees

An intertwined spear and Bonded Soul have crossed three incarnations together. In the current life, the soul becomes a ruler and orders an execution that the spear considers a betrayal of their shared history.

The spear closes its cooperative combat channel and invokes a boundary against the execution. The bond persists in Suspension while they argue. The spear does not automatically become corrupted or disloyal, and the ruler can still physically carry it if ordinary circumstances allow. Neither receives an extra action or supernatural compulsion.

### Refusal at Final Death

Before a dangerous final campaign, a Weapon Soul states that it will not leave the civilization it helped build. The Bonded Soul accepts the choice, and they suspend standing passage while preserving communication and affection.

When the Bonded Soul later reaches Final Death, the Weapon Soul remains with its vessel in the world. Reincarnation does not treat it as abandoned inventory or force it to follow. Their shared history remains real, and [Legacy Weapons](LEGACY_WEAPONS.md) determines any additional Legacy Status without erasing the existing bond state.

### The Unsuitable New Body

A greatsword accompanies its Bonded Soul into a life as a small burrowing monster. The bond, recognition, and a faint communication channel persist. The current body cannot hold, summon, or safely move the vessel's established form.

Intertwining does not resize the sword, grant hands, or install Swordsmanship. The pair may pursue communication, environmental tools, translated Skill development, later evolution, or another valid route. Until then, the partnership is real while outward use remains inaccessible.

### Two Soul Weapon Bonds

An old soul has separately intertwined with a shield devoted to protection and a calligrapher's brush devoted to truthful record. Each Weapon Soul has its own Accord, boundaries, history, and consent.

The two bonds do not merge into a set, share memory, grant simultaneous actions, or turn Soul Depth into equipment capacity. In a body unable to carry the shield but able to hold the brush, current access differs. Conflict between the two Weapon Souls is a relationship problem, not an automatic fusion or bonus.

### The Echo Cannot Renew Consent

A current incarnation cannot hear an intertwined knife, but a former assassin Echo remembers that the knife once accepted a violent mission. The Echo urges the current soul to assume the old permission still applies.

The bond's established boundaries and current Weapon Soul consent govern. The Echo can provide its own testimony but cannot speak for either participant. If communication remains unavailable and the action exceeds standing terms, the current incarnation must delay or act without Weapon Soul cooperation through ordinary causality.

### Peaceful Partnership

A carpenter and an awakened plane spend decades restoring homes after disasters. Their relationship is shaped by exacting craft, arguments about reuse, care during repeated repairs, and responsibility to communities that will outlive them.

They intertwine through a deliberate Accord at the completion of a generational rebuilding project. No battle or near-death event is required. The plane becomes a Soul Weapon because of the persistent partnership, not because every Soul Weapon must be designed to kill.

## Safeguards

- **No dormant consent:** a Dormant Weapon Soul cannot agree through user attachment, ritual interpretation, or predicted future wishes.
- **No ownership bond:** making, possession, purchase, inheritance, law, rank, prophecy, and creator intent grant no authority over intertwining.
- **No crisis shortcut:** danger and awakening may supply context, but the newly awakened person must still understand and freely answer the bond.
- **No soul merger:** identities, Integrity, Depth, Imprints, Titles, Echoes, memories, and agency remain distinct.
- **No capability transfer:** intertwining grants no Skills, statistics, Classes, species traits, anatomy, resources, evolution, mastery, or automatic abilities.
- **No permanent blanket consent:** ordinary standing cooperation may exist, while material boundary changes require fresh agreement.
- **No perfect channel:** the bond grants no unrestricted telepathy, emotion reading, memory access, location sense, or truth detection.
- **No forced passage:** either participant may validly reject future Reincarnation passage; prior standing consent governs only while not withdrawn.
- **No reincarnation inventory:** a persistent relationship does not make the Weapon Vessel ordinary starting equipment or guarantee manifestation in a new body.
- **No universal slot:** a Bonded Soul has no metaphysical weapon slot, while every separate bond must satisfy the full requirements and current complexity limits.
- **No passage fork:** one Weapon Soul cannot maintain several active standard passage bonds to independent reincarnation continuities.
- **No extra actors:** multiple bonds, Soul Space contact, Echo advice, or Avatar Expression do not grant additional turns or autonomous weapon actions.
- **No punishment for refusal:** delay, boundaries, Suspension, and Severance are valid expressions of agency rather than moral or mechanical failure.
- **No convenient severance:** hostile bond destruction requires an owned source, fair warning, meaningful resistance, and proportionate soul-harm consequences.
- **No retroactive bond:** a later revelation, Title, Archive Record, ritual, or famous history cannot declare that earlier possession was intertwining.
- **No campaign canon:** particular bonds, participants, Accords, boundaries, vessel conditions, and consequences remain external campaign state.

## Scope Boundaries

This document establishes the persistent relationship that makes an awakened Weapon Soul and its Bonded Soul a Soul Weapon partnership. It does not define what personality the Weapon Soul develops, which forms or capabilities a Soul Weapon may acquire, how evolution occurs, whether old vessel experiences become Weapon Echoes, how an unaccompanied awakened weapon gains Legacy Status under [Legacy Weapons](LEGACY_WEAPONS.md), the detailed Profiles defined by [Soul Weapon Compatibility](SOUL_WEAPON_COMPATIBILITY.md), the routes defined by [Weapon Manifestations](WEAPON_MANIFESTATIONS.md), or the vessel and assembly structures defined by [Unconventional Soul Weapons](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md).
