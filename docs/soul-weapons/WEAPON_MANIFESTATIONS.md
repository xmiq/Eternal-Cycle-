# Weapon Manifestations

## Status

This document defines how an awakened Weapon Soul may become externally perceivable or embodied through a bounded, temporary expression.

It does not define Weapon Soul formation, awakening, Soul Intertwining, personality, Soul Weapon Evolution, Weapon Echoes, Legacy status, compatibility, ordinary physical custody, Soul Space appearance, Soul Avatar Expression, magical summoning, or the vessel topology owned by [Unconventional Soul Weapons](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md).

Read [Soul Weapon Compatibility](SOUL_WEAPON_COMPATIBILITY.md), [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md), [Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md), [Soul Space](../soul/SOUL_SPACE.md), and [Soul Avatars](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) first.

## Core Rule

A **Weapon Manifestation** is a bounded external expression through which one awakened Weapon Soul becomes independently perceivable, physically present, or temporarily embodied by means of a valid Manifestation Route and Manifestation Anchor.

Every valid Weapon Manifestation must establish:

1. the awakened Weapon Soul that is expressing itself;
2. the exact Manifestation Mode being attempted;
3. a capability source that can produce that mode;
4. a valid Manifestation Anchor and location;
5. the present Compatibility Claims and supports that matter;
6. the consent and standing boundaries of affected persons;
7. the expression's form, senses, movement, interaction, and unavailable claims;
8. onset, maintenance, range, duration, and termination conditions;
9. costs, vulnerabilities, interruption, recovery, and consequences;
10. how one Weapon Soul retains one coherent center of weapon-owned physical agency.

Awakening, a Soul Weapon Bond, strong Soul Resonance, a dramatic crisis, a Soul Title, long history, Weapon Evolution, public worship, or Reincarnation passage cannot supply any missing element by itself.

Manifestation is optional. A Weapon Soul can remain a complete person, companion, and Soul Weapon partner without ever manifesting.

## Terms

### Weapon Manifestation

A bounded external expression through which one awakened Weapon Soul becomes independently perceivable, physically present, or temporarily embodied through a valid Manifestation Route and Anchor.

### Manifestation Mode

The kind of external expression a route supports: Perceptual, Vessel, or Embodied. Modes describe function rather than rank, power, maturity, or a mandatory progression sequence.

### Manifestation Route

The causally supported method by which a particular Weapon Soul can produce one defined Manifestation Mode under stated sources, anchors, access, consent, limits, costs, vulnerabilities, and termination conditions.

### Manifestation Source

The actual capability that produces and sustains a Manifestation Route. It may belong to the Weapon Soul, a Soul Weapon Form, an established bond structure, a valid external effect, or another owning system. Manifestation itself is not a free source.

### Manifestation Anchor

The present person, Weapon Vessel, Soul Weapon Bond, object, place, structure, or source-defined focus that locates and stabilizes a Weapon Manifestation. An Anchor does not establish ownership or consent.

### Manifestation Form

The temporary sensory, physical, or embodied configuration produced by a Manifestation Route. It is distinct from the persistent [Soul Weapon Form](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md), even when it resembles or expresses part of that form.

### Manifestation Profile

A qualitative campaign record of one established Manifestation Route and its current expression, including mode, source, anchor, form, consent, capabilities, unavailable claims, compatibility, onset, range, duration, costs, vulnerabilities, termination, recovery, and consequences.

The Profile belongs in an external Campaign Record. It is not a statistic block, inventory entry, Class feature, or universal template.

## What Manifestation Is Not

### Not Ordinary Vessel Presence

A Weapon Vessel resting in a room, being carried, used as a tool, or wielded through a person's present Skill is physically present without being manifested.

The vessel's own established movement, vibration, heat, light, or other capability also remains ordinary capability unless a Manifestation Route specifically externalizes the Weapon Soul as a presence. Calling every weapon-owned effect a manifestation would erase useful ownership boundaries.

### Not Communication Alone

A voice, image, emotion, pressure, movement, or other signal through an established communication channel is not automatically a Weapon Manifestation. Communication becomes Perceptual Manifestation only when a route creates an independently perceivable external presence with defined observers, location, and limits.

### Not a Soul Space Appearance

A Weapon Soul may appear within [Soul Space](../soul/SOUL_SPACE.md) through a Soul Construct, Region, image, or other valid interface. That appearance is internal and symbolic. It grants no external body, location, reach, action, or witness.

### Not a Weapon Echo

A [Weapon Echo](WEAPON_ECHOES.md) is a bounded first-person memory structure owned by the continuing Weapon Soul. Replaying or sharing one does not cause the earlier form, personality, user, or event to manifest.

### Not a Soul Avatar

A [Soul Avatar](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) is a whole-soul synthesis led by the current incarnation. A Weapon Manifestation expresses one distinct Weapon Soul. Neither term grants the rules, identity, history, or capabilities of the other.

The unqualified term **Avatar** is not used for a Weapon Manifestation.

### Not Evolution or Form Selection

[Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md) changes the persistent Soul Weapon Form. Manifestation temporarily expresses a form through a current route. Evolving does not grant a route, and manifesting does not restore a Prior Weapon Form or cause Evolution.

### Not Summoning, Recall, or Teleportation by Default

Relationship, need, ownership, command, longing, danger, and distance do not pull a Weapon Vessel across the world. If a route moves matter, reconstitutes a vessel, or creates a temporary external vessel, it must say which occurs, identify the source, and preserve one coherent continuity.

### Not an Extra Soul or Free Action

A Manifestation does not create a duplicate Weapon Soul, copy its consciousness, split its capability history, or add actions to its Bonded Soul. Any independent physical action requires an Embodied or otherwise explicitly physical route with its own senses, locomotion, interaction, cost, and opportunity.

### Not Proof of Truth or Authority

A manifested Weapon Soul remains a bounded person. It can be mistaken, biased, frightened, deceived, private, conflicted, or unwilling. Visibility does not make it an oracle, judge, rightful ruler, moral certificate, or owner of the person carrying its vessel.

## Manifestation Modes

The three standard modes are not a ladder. A route may support one mode, several separately bounded modes, or none. Access to one does not imply access to the others.

### Perceptual Manifestation

A **Perceptual Manifestation** creates an externally located presence that specified observers can perceive.

It may include a bounded combination of:

- voice, music, vibration, scent, light, shadow, texture, temperature, or pressure;
- an image, silhouette, symbolic figure, remembered shape, or abstract pattern;
- directionally located communication;
- source-defined perception returned to the Weapon Soul;
- different appearances or channels for different observers when the route explains why.

Perceptual Manifestation has no mass, reach, collision, locomotion, manipulation, damage, restraint, bodily protection, or physical action unless a separate physical mode and source establish them. Looking like a person does not create anatomy. Looking like a weapon does not create a usable vessel.

### Vessel Manifestation

A **Vessel Manifestation** produces an externally physical expression of the Weapon Soul's current Soul Weapon Form or a bounded configuration derived from it.

The route must state whether the expression is:

- the continuing Weapon Vessel translated through an established source;
- a reconstituted current vessel whose continuity replaces an earlier material state;
- a temporary physical expression anchored to the Weapon Soul or Soul Weapon Bond;
- a new material vessel accepted through a separate continuity procedure;
- another explicitly defined source-owned result.

Vessel Manifestation never assumes free transport or duplication. A temporary expression cannot be sold, stored, inherited, harvested for permanent material, or counted as an additional inventory copy. If it ends, source-defined residual matter and consequences remain only when the route says so.

A physical vessel does not wield itself merely because it manifested. Self-movement, perception, redirection, transformation, or other weapon-owned action requires an established capability and must obey the one-center rule below.

### Embodied Manifestation

An **Embodied Manifestation** creates a temporary external body or interactive presence through which the Weapon Soul can perceive, communicate, move, or manipulate within its route.

The body may be humanoid, monstrous, animal-like, geometric, elemental, object-like, environmental, or culturally symbolic. Appearance does not supply:

- human or monster anatomy not explicitly present;
- biological needs, immunities, reproduction, or species membership;
- a Human Class, Profession, monster form, Evolution Route, or Species Trait;
- the Bonded Soul's Skills, statistics, Titles, authority, or knowledge;
- every affordance suggested by the body's image;
- unrestricted speech, locomotion, touch, combat, or tool use.

The route must define the body's actual senses, movement, interaction, resilience, vulnerabilities, and action capacity. A humanoid-looking sword spirit may be able to speak and walk but lack taste, blood, leverage for combat, or the dexterity to use tools. A large spectral animal may have an imposing outline but no mass.

Embodied Manifestation makes independent action possible only within those definitions. It is not mandatory companionship, a free combat unit, or a second body for the Bonded Soul.

## Building a Manifestation Route

### 1. Identify the Person and Purpose

Name the awakened Weapon Soul and the specific purpose of the proposed route. A route for speaking with public witnesses is not automatically a route for combat, travel, vessel recall, disguise, repair, or carrying another person.

Purpose constrains interpretation. It does not compel the Weapon Soul to use the route whenever that purpose appears.

### 2. Establish Provenance

Identify why this Weapon Soul can create this expression.

Valid provenance may include:

- a weapon-owned capability established after Awakening through meaningful development under its owning rules;
- an explicit feature of the current Soul Weapon Form acquired through valid Weapon Evolution;
- an independently established bounded capability that uses the Soul Weapon Bond and is accepted within the Intertwining Accord;
- a ritual, relic, site, material, spirit, god, enchantment, or other external source under its owning rules;
- a Legacy context that provides a real anchor or source rather than fame alone;
- several compatible sources whose separate costs and limits remain visible.

The source must already exist or be established through its own rules. This document does not implement Magic, divine authority, enchantment, spirit binding, or world-contact mechanics. Until those systems are complete, their contribution remains canonical only where another existing rule already supports it; otherwise it requires a properly recorded Provisional Rule under the [Provisional Rulings](../gm/PROVISIONAL_RULINGS.md).

### 3. Select One Mode and Form

State whether the route produces Perceptual, Vessel, or Embodied Manifestation and describe what actually appears.

An appearance can express personality, Vessel History, Weapon Echo imagery, Soul Titles, culture, relationship, or current interpretation. Symbolism is not capability. A crown-shaped aura does not command subjects, dragon wings do not grant flight, and an executioner's silhouette does not compel confession.

### 4. Establish the Anchor

Every expression must be located by a current Anchor.

Common Anchors include:

- the present Weapon Vessel;
- the Soul Weapon Bond, when the Intertwining Accord permits that use;
- the Bonded Soul's current body, with that person's consent and Embodiment compatibility;
- a prepared object, repair, sheath, shrine, forge, grave, battlefield, or other meaningful site;
- a source-owned ritual focus or environmental condition;
- a Legacy Weapon's continuing vessel or accepted place of stewardship.

The Anchor defines where onset can occur, how far the expression can move or be perceived, and what ends or destabilizes it. An Anchor does not become an owner, master, battery, prisoner, or permanent home merely by serving this function.

No route may silently hop among Anchors. Changing Anchor requires a defined handoff, a new onset, or another valid movement rule.

### 5. Assess Current Compatibility

Manifestation is a [Soul Weapon Compatibility Claim](SOUL_WEAPON_COMPATIBILITY.md). Assess only the layers relevant to this route.

Questions may include:

- can the Weapon Soul access and sustain the source now;
- can the Anchor bear the proposed expression;
- can the current body host a bond-anchored route without harm or suppression;
- can intended observers perceive the chosen channel;
- does the environment disrupt the source or form;
- can the Weapon Soul coordinate the expression with its current vessel and partner;
- do current Skills or accommodations support safe use without being treated as the source itself.

A favourable Compatibility Finding removes no cost and grants no route. Incompatibility creates only ordinary or source-defined failure, not generic soul rejection.

### 6. Establish Consent and Boundaries

The Weapon Soul must consent to voluntary manifestation. Any person whose body, Soul Space, bond, private senses, or property is used as an Anchor must consent within the material scope of that use.

An Intertwining Accord or Legacy Accord may contain standing terms for routine manifestations. Standing consent must remain:

- specific enough to understand;
- revocable when the participant can communicate;
- limited by stated triggers, places, purposes, observers, privacy, and duration;
- subject to renegotiation when the body, form, source, risk, or relationship changes.

A Bonded Soul cannot manifest the Weapon Soul as an owned effect. A Weapon Soul cannot occupy, expose, or use a person's body as an Anchor merely because a bond persists.

### 7. Define Capability and Unavailable Claims

Record what the Manifestation can perceive and do, then state important unavailable claims.

At minimum, define:

- who can perceive it and through which senses;
- whether it has mass, collision, reach, or manipulators;
- its locomotion and range from the Anchor;
- its communication channels;
- any weapon-owned capabilities it can currently express;
- whether another person can wield or interact with the Weapon Vessel at the same time;
- how concentration, divided attention, or cooperation affect use;
- what it categorically cannot do.

Capability follows sources rather than imagery or narrative importance.

### 8. Define Onset and Maintenance

Specify how the expression begins and what keeps it coherent.

Possible onset requirements include:

- deliberate attention by the Weapon Soul;
- a mutually agreed call or gesture;
- contact with the Anchor;
- a prepared environment or external participant;
- access to an established source;
- a narrow emergency trigger accepted in advance;
- recovery from prior use.

Possible maintenance demands include attention, proximity, a stable vessel, current bond access, environmental conditions, materials, an external ritual, or another source-owned cost. There is no universal mana cost, timer, use count, or manifestation rank.

### 9. Define Vulnerability and Termination

Every route needs meaningful ways to end, fail, or be interrupted.

Termination may follow:

- voluntary withdrawal by the Weapon Soul;
- withdrawal of required consent;
- loss, movement, damage, or suppression of the Anchor;
- range or duration limits;
- source exhaustion or incompatibility;
- disruption of attention or coordination;
- ordinary damage to a physical expression;
- completion of the route's stated purpose;
- an established hostile countermeasure.

The route must state what termination does to the expression, Weapon Vessel, Weapon Soul, bond, and participants. Dispersing a temporary body is not Final Death, bond Severance, vessel destruction, or a Soul Wound by default. Those consequences require their own proportionate causes and protections.

### 10. Record Recovery and Consequence

After manifestation, resolve source-owned recovery and world consequences.

Possible consequences include:

- bodily or attentional fatigue;
- temporary communication loss;
- a damaged Anchor or vessel;
- reduced compatibility until repair or rest;
- privacy breaches, fear, reverence, legal attention, or religious claims;
- evidence that changes a Legacy dispute;
- enemies learning the route's conditions;
- altered boundaries within an Accord;
- obligations arising from promises made by the Weapon Soul itself.

There is no automatic fatigue, Soul Strain, or soul damage. Apply each only when its established source and severity support it.

## One Soul, One Center of Weapon-Owned Physical Agency

One Weapon Soul remains one person across its vessel, bond communication, and manifestations.

Under the standard rule:

- the Weapon Soul cannot create independent copies of itself;
- only one manifestation from that Weapon Soul may exercise weapon-owned physical agency at a time;
- a Perceptual Manifestation may coexist with ordinary vessel use because it has no physical action;
- a separate wielder remains free to act through that wielder's body and Skill, but does not receive the Weapon Soul's action as a bonus;
- if an Embodied Manifestation acts physically, the Weapon Soul cannot also make a separate self-directed physical action through its vessel during the same opportunity;
- sustaining several sensory images for several observers does not create several actors;
- divided, distributed, swarm, composite, or multi-vessel agency is not granted by this document and follows [Unconventional Soul Weapons](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md).

The GM should evaluate actual time, attention, distance, communication, and coordination rather than treating the relationship as one initiative slot or granting a universal action formula.

## Reincarnation and Vessel Continuity

### What Passes

An effective Passage Accord can carry the Weapon Soul and persistent Soul Weapon Bond through Reincarnation. The current Soul Weapon Form may remain part of the Weapon Soul's continuity where its established rules permit.

Passage does not itself carry ordinary worldly matter, place a physical weapon in the new body's hands, or keep a Manifestation active through Interlife.

### What Happens to the Prior Vessel

Before Final Death or passage, the established route must distinguish among:

- a material vessel that remains in the world;
- matter translated by a valid source;
- a vessel whose material configuration ends while its coherent Soul Weapon Form persists;
- a temporary manifested vessel that terminates;
- unresolved custody or damage outside the passage route.

The campaign records the actual outcome. No interpretation may leave two complete originals carrying one Weapon Soul.

If the Weapon Soul remains world-bound rather than accompanying the Bonded Soul, relationship or distance does not permit remote manifestation into the next life.

### Access in the New Life

A new incarnation must reassess:

- current bond and communication access;
- the Manifestation Source and Anchor;
- embodiment, environment, and source compatibility;
- the form the current body can safely perceive, carry, wield, or support;
- current consent and Accord boundaries;
- learned methods needed for reliable coordination.

A valid Vessel Manifestation Route may produce a temporary vessel, reconstitute the continuing vessel, or use a new material Anchor according to its explicit provenance. It is not an inventory retrieval command. A newborn, bodiless spirit, tiny monster, aquatic form, or body without suitable senses may retain the bond while lacking safe physical access for years or for the entire life.

### World Resets

A World Reset may change available materials, laws, magic, environments, cultures, and institutions. The Weapon Soul and a valid bond may persist while a route becomes inaccessible, altered, dangerous, or misunderstood. Persistence protects continuity, not identical expression in every Age.

## Involuntary and Hostile Manifestation

### Agreed Reflexive Onset

A route may contain a narrow reflexive trigger accepted in advance, such as producing a warning image when the bearer becomes unconscious near a known threat.

The trigger must define:

- the condition it can actually detect;
- the permitted mode and observers;
- the maximum response;
- who may end it;
- what happens when evidence is ambiguous;
- how standing consent can be changed.

An emergency does not expand the trigger by implication.

### Involuntary Onset

Damage, Dissonance, source instability, hostile interference, or a changed body may cause partial, distorted, or uncontrolled onset only when an established cause supports it.

Involuntary onset grants no increased power or guaranteed useful warning. The Weapon Soul remains a person experiencing loss of control, not a malfunctioning item. Resolution should prioritize grounding, source repair, consent restoration, and causal treatment rather than punishment for disobedience.

### Hostile Forced Manifestation

A hostile effect may attempt to expose, bind, distort, relocate, or sustain a Weapon Manifestation against the Weapon Soul's will only through an explicit source that provides:

- a valid Target and Anchor;
- fair warning or discoverable evidence;
- meaningful resistance and interruption;
- a bounded effect and duration;
- consequences for the aggressor and victims;
- protections proportionate to threatened soul harm.

Institutions, gods, creators, legal owners, Bonded Souls, and former wielders receive no default authority to force manifestation. A compelled expression does not become cooperation, consent, a new Accord, Evolution, or compatibility progress.

## Damage, Dispersal, and Suppression

Manifestation routes must keep four claims separate:

1. damage to the temporary Manifestation Form;
2. damage to the current Weapon Vessel;
3. disruption of the Manifestation Source or Anchor;
4. injury to the Weapon Soul itself.

One may affect another only through an established causal link.

A broken projected arm may merely impair the temporary body's reach. A shattered reconstituted vessel may create real Vessel Continuity questions. A suppressed ritual focus may end the expression while leaving the Weapon Soul unharmed. Direct soul injury requires the [Soul Engine Safeguards](../soul/SOUL_ENGINE_SAFEGUARDS.md), not a dramatic description alone.

Suppression can block a route without erasing the capability permanently. Repeated dispersal, deliberate damage, or forced re-formation does not train the route, produce Evolution Pressure by itself, deepen Resonance automatically, or farm Soul Depth.

## System Interactions

### Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may help a Weapon Soul locate a valid Target, coordinate with a willing Anchor, preserve meaning across an expression, or notice Dissonance. Resonance cannot become the Manifestation Source, force consent, create range, or supply missing capability by itself.

### Soul Echoes

A [Soul Echo](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may advise the current incarnation about earlier contact with a Weapon Soul. It cannot manifest the Weapon Soul, act through the expression, or consent for either participant.

### Soul Titles

[Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may shape appearance, recognition, symbolic pressure, observer expectations, or access to a social opportunity. They do not create a route, command the Weapon Soul, determine truth, or add capabilities matching the title's name.

### Soul Depth and Soul Integrity

[Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) and Soul Integrity may support complex consent, sustained coordination, or resistance to pressure. They do not guarantee manifestation, increase its mode, remove embodiment, or supply the source.

### Soul Space

[Soul Space](../soul/SOUL_SPACE.md) may support preparation, communication, rehearsal, agreement, and symbolic design. Internal time, imagery, and Constructs do not cross into the world unless a Manifestation Route establishes how.

### Soul Avatars

[Soul Avatar](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) Expression may coordinate a willing Weapon Soul as a distinct contributor. It cannot absorb, copy, force, or substitute for the Weapon Soul and grants no Manifestation Route.

### Soul Intertwining and Reincarnation

A [Soul Weapon Bond](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md) may serve as a source component or Anchor only within its Accord. Neither intertwining nor [Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) grants physical access, remote recall, obedience, or automatic expression.

### Weapon Personalities and Weapon Echoes

[Weapon Personality](WEAPON_PERSONALITIES.md) shapes choices, boundaries, and presentation. [Weapon Echoes](WEAPON_ECHOES.md) may inform imagery or testimony. Neither becomes a capability source, and no manifestation creates a separate personality.

### Weapon Evolution

[Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md) may add, alter, narrow, or retire a Manifestation Route only when the resulting Soul Weapon Form explicitly contains that source-owned change with costs and tradeoffs. Evolution and manifestation remain separate transitions.

### Compatibility

[Soul Weapon Compatibility](SOUL_WEAPON_COMPATIBILITY.md) assesses whether a proposed route can operate under current conditions. It does not create the route or override refusal. A support remains useful only while its own setup, maintenance, and limits continue.

### Skills and Development

[Skills](../skills/README.md) may govern learned manifestation control, safe coordination, anchor preparation, observation, concealment, interruption, or fighting alongside an Embodied Manifestation. A Skill cannot manufacture Weapon Soul personhood, duplicate an actor, or turn another person's capability into the user's own.

[Development](../progression/README.md) determines current access, embodied expression, practised reliability, and contextual effectiveness. Retained progression may accelerate relearning but cannot bypass a new body's limits or restore a route whose source is absent.

### Human Classes and Professions

[Human Classes and Professions](../human/README.md) may teach methods, recognize roles, regulate public manifestations, maintain Anchors, or interpret Weapon Souls through local doctrine. Membership, certification, rank, office, and ownership cannot grant a route or authority over the Weapon Soul.

### Monster Evolution

[Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) may create anatomy, senses, environments, or Species Traits that support or obstruct an Anchor. A monster form does not install manifestation, while a manifestation that resembles a monster does not become that species or enter its Evolution Tree.

### Legacy Weapons

A [Legacy Weapon](LEGACY_WEAPONS.md) may possess a route anchored to its continuing vessel, accepted site, or current relationship. Age, fame, inheritance, worship, and public expectation do not create a manifestation or force the Weapon Soul to testify.

### World Engine

A Weapon Manifestation enters causal world state. Witnesses can report it; institutions can regulate or counterfeit it; cultures can misinterpret it; enemies can study its Anchor; damaged property remains damaged; and promises create relationships and obligations. The [World Engine](../world-engine/WORLD_ENGINE_OVERVIEW.md) resolves those consequences without making every manifestation historically important.

## Anti-Exploitation Safeguards

- No awakening, bond, Compatibility Finding, Soul Title, Resonance state, dramatic need, or Reincarnation count automatically grants manifestation.
- No manifestation is mandatory for a viable Soul Weapon or character path.
- No route provides unrestricted duration, range, form, observers, movement, interaction, or action.
- No Weapon Soul may duplicate itself, its vessel, its capability history, or its physical agency through standard manifestation.
- No Vessel Manifestation creates permanent harvestable matter or a free inventory copy unless another explicit source owns that consequence.
- No Embodied Manifestation inherits the Bonded Soul's Skills, Class, Profession, statistics, Titles, authority, or species capabilities.
- No wielder receives free actions, mastery, protection, damage, or tactical certainty from the Weapon Soul's personhood.
- No Weapon Soul becomes a compulsory moral guide, automatic rescuer, quest answer, or plot-immunity source.
- No crisis, injury, repeated dispersal, forced onset, manifestation count, performance, worship, or public witness farms Evolution, Depth, Resonance, compatibility, or capability.
- No institution, creator, owner, god, ancestor, or Bonded Soul receives default control of the expression.
- No manifestation bypasses current embodiment, source access, Compatibility Claims, learned methods, consent, action economy, or world consequence.
- No image, metaphor, title, or dramatic description grants an unrecorded ability.
- No route remains automatically usable after Reincarnation, Evolution, severe damage, source loss, or World Reset.
- No dispersal or suppression becomes Final Death, Severance, Soul Wound, corruption, or permanent loss without an explicit proportionate cause.

## GM Procedure

When a Weapon Manifestation is attempted:

1. identify the awakened Weapon Soul and exact proposed expression;
2. classify the mode as Perceptual, Vessel, or Embodied;
3. verify the established Manifestation Route and capability source;
4. locate the Anchor and determine its current condition;
5. establish the necessary consent and standing Accord boundaries;
6. assess only the relevant Compatibility Claims;
7. state actual senses, movement, interaction, capabilities, and unavailable claims;
8. establish onset, range, duration, maintenance, action, and attention limits;
9. identify interruption, termination, damage links, and recovery before resolving severe stakes;
10. resolve world reactions and record the current Profile externally.

If any required element is missing, narrow the expression, seek a valid support, treat an incomplete later-system contribution as Provisional under the Provisional Rulings, or rule that manifestation is presently unavailable. Do not reward ambiguity with the broadest interpretation.

The GM may conceal unknown route conditions when fair mystery requires it, but observable evidence must remain consistent. The player should be able to investigate Anchors, compatibility, costs, disruptions, and source behavior through meaningful action.

## Worked Examples

### The Voice Beside the Campfire

An awakened sword has long communicated privately with its Bonded Soul through emotion and Soul Space imagery. A later Weapon Evolution gives it a route for projecting a visible, audible figure within several steps of its current scabbard.

The figure can speak to people the sword intentionally includes and can see only through the sword's established senses. It has no mass, cannot lift a cup, and cannot stand watch beyond the scabbard's range. The first public appearance causes political and religious attention, but does not make the figure a human, a ghost, or an oracle.

### The Reborn Bearer Without a Blade

A Bonded Soul reincarnates as a small aquatic monster. The Weapon Soul and bond pass, but the prior iron vessel remains in the former world because no source translated its matter.

The pair possess a Vessel Manifestation Route that can create a temporary current-form blade when the new body contacts a mineral Anchor and both consent. The monster cannot grip or swing the blade and lacks its former sword Skill access. It instead spends years developing a fitted jaw harness and a new handling Skill. Persistence preserved the partnership; embodiment and current-life effort still decide useful expression.

### The Humanoid Shape With Narrow Hands

A hammer's Embodied Manifestation resembles a broad-shouldered smith. Its route grants speech, sight, walking, and enough manipulation to inspect cracks or hold small tools. It does not grant human biology, a blacksmith Profession, the former smith's Skill, or combat statistics.

While the body acts physically, the Weapon Soul cannot also animate the hammer for a separate attack. Another person may carry the ordinary hammer, but its weapon-owned effects remain unavailable until the manifestation ends or the route transfers its physical agency back to the vessel.

### The Bell That Testifies

A Legacy bell remains fixed in a ruined civic tower. Its Perceptual Manifestation can reproduce its own voice and selected Weapon Echo imagery within the tower when it consents and the intact foundation serves as Anchor.

An heir asks it to appear in a distant court. Lineage and urgency do not move the Anchor. The bell may share testimony through a messenger or seek a separately valid route, but cannot teleport, project across the continent, or prove the heir's legal claim by appearing.

### The Agreed Warning

A dagger and its bearer add a standing term permitting a brief shadow-image when the bearer is unconscious and the dagger perceives a specific poison scent it has learned to identify. The image can alert nearby chosen allies but cannot diagnose other poisons, attack the source, expose unrelated memories, or remain after the bearer wakes.

When smoke produces a similar odor, the warning appears incorrectly. The dagger's route is working; its evidence is fallible. The group revises the trigger rather than treating the error as betrayal or corruption.

### Refusal at the Execution

A ruler orders a Bonded axe to manifest its historical executioner image to frighten prisoners. The route exists and the current setting is compatible, but the Weapon Soul refuses because the use violates its present boundaries.

The ruler's legal ownership and the Bonded Soul's command do not replace consent. The refusal may carry legal, relational, or political consequences, but it does not suspend the bond or prove the axe incapable.

### The Broken Anchor

A spear's Embodied Manifestation is stabilized by inscriptions in its current shaft. An enemy breaks the inscriptions after fair evidence reveals their role.

The external body loses cohesion and terminates. The Weapon Soul survives, the spearhead remains, and the bond persists. Repairing the Anchor may restore access after source-defined recovery; repeated forced attempts do not deepen the route or guarantee Evolution.

### An Illusion Wearing the Weapon's Face

A court magician creates an image resembling a famous Weapon Soul and claims it has endorsed a treaty. The image comes from the magician's effect, not the Weapon Soul's Manifestation Route.

Observers may be deceived, but source evidence, inconsistent knowledge, the absent Anchor, and the real Weapon Soul's later testimony can expose the fraud. Resemblance does not transfer personhood or consent.

### The Distant Vessel

A Weapon Soul has awakened but never intertwined. Its physical vessel is stolen and carried across an ocean. A former bearer can still feel emotional Resonance with their shared history.

Resonance does not create an Anchor at the bearer's location. The weapon cannot manifest there, recall itself, or accompany a later Reincarnation without an established source and route. Recovery must follow travel, allies, negotiation, tracking, or other world causality.

### Two Weapons, Not an Army

A veteran has separate relationships with two awakened weapons. One can project a Perceptual Manifestation; the other has a narrow Embodied route for manipulating its own repair tools.

Each Weapon Soul remains one person with its own consent, source, Anchor, and limits. The veteran gains no command hierarchy, shared compatibility, pooled actions, or ability to multiply either expression. Coordinating all three persons under pressure is a learned and context-sensitive challenge.
