# Dormant Weapon Souls

## Status

This document defines the canonical existence, formation, continuity, evidence, and limits of dormant Weapon Souls. It does not define awakening, intertwining, Weapon Soul personality after awakening, Soul Weapon evolution, Weapon Echoes, Legacy Weapons, compatibility, Weapon Manifestations, or the topology rules owned by [Unconventional Soul Weapons](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md).

Read the [Soul Weapon Foundations](../soul/SOUL_WEAPON_FOUNDATIONS.md), [Soul Fundamentals](../soul/SOUL_FUNDAMENTALS.md), and [Soul System Interactions](../soul/SOUL_SYSTEM_INTERACTIONS.md) first.

## Core Rule

A **Dormant Weapon Soul** is a distinct but not yet awakened soul that has cohered around the identity and lived continuity of a particular weapon or meaningful tool.

It is not present in every object. It is also not restricted to a chosen material, maker, weapon category, rarity, or social class. Any sufficiently meaningful weapon or tool may develop one when its history becomes coherent enough to sustain a distinct continuing identity, but no surface feature or repeated action guarantees that result.

Dormancy means the Weapon Soul does not yet possess reliable self-awareness, communication, deliberate action, or expressed capability. It is a real continuity, not an active equipment benefit.

## Terms

### Dormant Weapon Soul

A distinct Weapon Soul whose identity has cohered but has not awakened into reliable self-awareness, communication, deliberate agency, or expressed capability.

### Weapon Vessel

The current physical weapon, tool, or coherent instrument around which a Weapon Soul is anchored before intertwining. The vessel is the soul's embodiment, not an inventory container that merely stores it.

### Vessel History

The causally connected history of making, purpose, use, care, alteration, relationship, consequence, and recognition through which a Weapon Vessel acquires meaning.

### Vessel Continuity

The qualitative continuity that allows a Weapon Soul to remain anchored while its vessel is repaired, altered, reforged, or partly replaced. It depends on actual material, functional, relational, historical, and identity connections rather than one universal percentage of original material.

### Dormant Indication

An observable event or pattern consistent with a dormant Weapon Soul but insufficient by itself to prove one exists or explain its future identity.

## Existence Is Not Universal Animism

Objects can carry history without containing souls. A tool may be old, beloved, bloodstained, enchanted, famous, symbolically important, or meticulously maintained and still possess no Weapon Soul.

Distinguish at least four facts:

| Fact | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Object history** | Events happened to or through the object, whether or not anything metaphysical formed. |
| **Object or magical imprint** | A source has left information, emotion, habit, effect, or residue in the object without creating a distinct person. |
| **Dormant Weapon Soul** | A distinct continuing identity has cohered but has not awakened. |
| **Awakened Weapon Soul** | The Weapon Soul has crossed the later awakening boundary into reliable selfhood and agency. |

The existence of one fact does not prove the next. An enchantment can repeat a phrase without awareness. A spirit can possess a sword while remaining a separate visitor. A construct can reason because its maker designed it. A Soul Echo belongs to a completed incarnation. None should be relabelled as a Weapon Soul merely because the effect speaks through a weapon.

Conversely, a dormant Weapon Soul need not produce a visible enchantment, voice, aura, or supernatural effect. Most remain quiet.

## How a Dormant Weapon Soul Forms

Formation is a qualitative process of identity coherence. The GM asks whether the vessel's history has produced a stable enough pattern of meaning, relationship, consequence, and continuity for a distinct soul to exist.

Relevant contributors may include:

- a maker's sustained purpose, care, sacrifice, or self-expression;
- repeated use in a coherent role rather than interchangeable consumption;
- a long relationship with one or several users;
- being trusted, maintained, repaired, named, inherited, refused, protected, or deliberately changed;
- participation in consequential work, protection, creation, survival, ritual, exploration, judgment, or conflict;
- choices made because this particular vessel mattered rather than merely because any object was available;
- a consistent tension among intended purpose, actual use, treatment, and consequence;
- recognition by people, communities, spirits, places, or institutions that reinforces a real history;
- metaphysical conditions that can support a soul without manufacturing one by fiat.

No contributor is necessary in every case, and none is sufficient alone. Fame is unnecessary. Violence is unnecessary. A peaceful tool used through generations of meaningful craft may develop a soul, while a celebrated execution blade may remain only an object with a terrible history.

### Meaning Must Be Causal

Meaning is not a designer label placed on an object after the fact. It is demonstrated through choices, relationships, uses, costs, care, conflict, and consequences that actually shaped the vessel's history.

Calling a new sword legendary, commissioning a prophecy, staging witnesses, or writing an elaborate backstory into a catalog does not create the lived continuity that the claim describes.

### Repetition Must Integrate History

Repetition can strengthen Vessel History when circumstances vary and the same vessel continues to matter through adaptation, responsibility, relationship, and consequence.

Identical low-stakes use does not fill a soul-formation counter. A thousand routine cuts, manufactured duels, scripted sacrifices, or ceremonial touches cannot be converted into a reliable Weapon Soul recipe.

### One Event Is Evidence, Not a Universal Shortcut

A single extraordinary event may transform the meaning of an existing vessel, expose a continuity already forming, or become central to later awakening. It does not establish a universal rule that any object present during danger instantly gains a soul.

Whether formation and awakening can occur in close succession belongs to the dedicated awakening rules. This document establishes only that dramatic intensity cannot replace provenance and coherence.

## Dormancy Is a Condition, Not a Rank

Dormancy does not have universal levels, percentages, experience, rarity, or power grades.

Before a Dormant Weapon Soul exists, observers may describe a vessel's identity as diffuse, nascent, or merely historical. Those descriptions express uncertainty or formation in progress; they are not mandatory progression stages.

Once a Dormant Weapon Soul exists:

- its continuity can deepen or become more coherent;
- its history can become contradictory or difficult to interpret;
- relevant Resonance may become easier or harder to establish;
- damage or radical alteration may threaten its anchor;
- conditions may eventually support awakening;
- it may remain dormant forever.

Time does not force awakening. Dormancy is not failure, infancy measured by a meter, or a promise of future power.

## Identity Before Personality

A dormant Weapon Soul has distinct identity without a complete expressed personality.

Its identity may already be shaped by:

- what the vessel was made to do;
- what it was actually used to do;
- how users treated it and one another;
- which responsibilities, harms, repairs, and refusals defined its history;
- recurring relationships and environments;
- contradictions the vessel carried without resolving.

These influences do not predetermine a later personality. A weapon used by cruel people need not awaken cruel. A healer's instrument need not be gentle. A king's sword need not desire authority. Awakening produces a person capable of interpretation, surprise, disagreement, and change, not a moral summary generated from usage statistics.

Do not write complete current preferences, dialogue, loyalties, or plans for a Dormant Weapon Soul. Those claims require awakening. The GM may preserve latent tensions and provenance without treating them as spoken decisions.

## The Weapon Vessel

Before intertwining, the Weapon Soul remains anchored to its Weapon Vessel. It does not follow a user's soul through Final Death, travel independently as inventory, or inhabit a replacement merely because the user considers both objects equivalent.

The vessel may be held, stolen, inherited, abandoned, buried, displayed, repaired, dismantled, or destroyed through world causality. Custody changes access and history; it does not transfer ownership of the soul's identity or guarantee future cooperation.

### Repair and Replacement

Ordinary maintenance does not end Vessel Continuity. Sharpening an edge, replacing a grip, mending a haft, resetting a stone, patching a binding, or restoring a damaged working surface can preserve the same vessel when causal continuity remains clear.

More extensive change requires a qualitative assessment:

1. Which material or structural parts continue?
2. Which function, use, and embodied organization continue?
3. Which relationships and custodial practices connect the forms?
4. Do participants treat the change as repair, continuation, reconstruction, division, or replacement, and why?
5. Which history can still act through the resulting vessel?
6. Was the change performed with awareness of, consent from, or violence toward an awakened person? For a dormant soul, what credible evidence of identity was available?
7. Did a metaphysical source preserve, sever, transfer, duplicate, or corrupt the anchor under its own rules?

No universal material percentage decides the result. Meaning alone cannot ignore complete physical discontinuity, while material continuity alone cannot settle every identity claim.

### Reforging

Reforging may preserve, alter, strain, or end Vessel Continuity depending on what process occurs. Melting a blade into an unrelated batch of metal is not automatically the same as repairing it. Deliberately remaking a known vessel around its continuing identity may support continuity, but intention is evidence rather than absolute control.

The physical and metaphysical process must explain how the soul remains anchored. Reforging cannot be used to copy one Weapon Soul into every fragment or to move it into any preferred shape without consequence.

### Division, Combination, and Copies

Physical division does not duplicate a soul. Exact copies, molds, blueprints, memories, replicas, and matching enchantments do not copy identity.

If one vessel is divided, the GM establishes where continuity remains, whether the anchor is damaged or inaccessible, and what evidence supports that outcome. A dormant Weapon Soul cannot be multiplied into a collection by cutting the vessel into pieces.

Combining several objects does not automatically merge their souls or histories. Multiple-soul, composite, paired, distributed, and other unusual vessels follow [Unconventional Soul Weapons](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md) and require explicit personhood and continuity treatment.

### Destruction and Loss

Severe damage can make the dormant soul inaccessible, wounded, unanchored, or ended only where the relevant Soul rules and source support that consequence. The GM must not use ordinary item breakage as a casual shortcut to permanent soul destruction once a Weapon Soul is established.

Equally, dormancy does not make the vessel indestructible. Physical damage remains real. Repair may be impossible, and a soul can face meaningful loss without granting a free replacement or manifestation.

Any lasting soul-harm claim follows [Soul Fundamentals](../soul/SOUL_FUNDAMENTALS.md) and the [Soul Engine Safeguards](../soul/SOUL_ENGINE_SAFEGUARDS.md): explicit cause, fair evidence, proportional threat, meaningful resistance where possible, and persistent consequence.

## Dormant Indications and Evidence

A Dormant Weapon Soul is usually difficult to prove because it cannot reliably communicate or act.

Possible Dormant Indications include:

- source-valid Soul Resonance around a particular vessel;
- recurring symbolic representation in Soul Space with a genuine access route;
- consistent responses detected by an established soul-sensitive practice;
- Archive Records or testimony documenting earlier indications with credible provenance;
- repeated anomalies tied specifically to the vessel after ordinary enchantment, damage, user Habit, and coincidence are considered;
- reactions from gods, spirits, monsters, institutions, or awakened Weapon Souls whose own rules permit recognition.

Every indication retains its source limits. A resonant feeling may reflect the user's history with the object rather than a distinct soul. A magical sensor may detect an enchantment. A spirit may lie. An Archive Record may preserve an observer's mistaken interpretation.

### Evidence States

Use ordinary information states rather than a dormancy meter:

- **unnoticed:** no observer has identified relevant evidence;
- **suspected:** indications support the possibility but plausible alternatives remain;
- **corroborated:** independent or source-diverse evidence supports a distinct continuity;
- **established:** world truth or sufficiently reliable evidence confirms a Dormant Weapon Soul within the relevant scope.

These are observer claims, not stages of the soul. Different observers can disagree honestly. The GM records world truth separately from character knowledge, player knowledge, institutional doctrine, and mistaken belief under the [Game Master Framework](../gm/GAME_MASTER_FRAMEWORK.md).

## Dormancy Grants No Active Capability

A Dormant Weapon Soul does not by itself:

- improve damage, accuracy, durability, balance, reach, speed, or magical output;
- guide a user's hand or make decisions;
- grant a Skill, Technique, Habit, Development, Profession, Class, rank, title, affinity, or Species Trait;
- communicate, remember aloud, refuse, consent, warn, teach, or take an action;
- supply luck, plot protection, automatic return, indestructibility, or hidden inventory access;
- awaken during every crisis;
- follow a wielder through Reincarnation;
- make the vessel a Soul Weapon.

An external enchantment, spirit, construct, maker, user, institution, or magical source may produce an effect through its own rules. Do not attribute that effect to dormancy merely because a Dormant Weapon Soul also exists.

## Personhood, Custody, and Ethics

A Dormant Weapon Soul is a distinct soul, but dormancy prevents reliable communication and ordinary informed consent. This creates duties of caution without turning every object interaction into retroactive wrongdoing.

Apply these principles:

- legal ownership of a vessel does not establish ownership of an awakened person;
- creation, purchase, inheritance, conquest, or custody cannot predetermine future loyalty or consent;
- before credible evidence exists, ordinary good-faith use does not become retroactive enslavement merely because a soul is discovered later;
- after credible evidence exists, deliberate experiments, transfer, destruction, coercive awakening attempts, or radical alteration should account for the possibility of personhood and the risks imposed;
- institutions cannot classify a Dormant Weapon Soul as mere property in order to erase later agency;
- uncertainty about personhood is not permission for careless soul harm, nor proof that every suspected object must be treated as fully communicative.

The appropriate response depends on evidence, risk, culture, law, available alternatives, and the acts proposed. The rules protect a real emerging person without granting an unverified claim unlimited authority over current people.

## Interactions With Existing Systems

### Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may reveal or strengthen access to a genuine Resonance Link involving a vessel. It cannot create a Weapon Soul merely because a user feels attached, and a Link with the object does not prove the Target is a distinct soul.

When Resonance is used as evidence, identify the Target, Basis, Trigger, compatibility, access, and alternative explanations. Dormant identity and the user's attachment remain separable facts.

### Soul Depth

[Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) may affect a user's capacity to perceive or later sustain a complex Soul relationship. It does not generate a Weapon Soul, accelerate Vessel History, guarantee awakening, or establish compatibility.

### Soul Echoes and Retained Instincts

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) and [Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) may preserve a former user's perspective, habits, or attention around a vessel. Those Soul Imprints remain part of the user's soul. They do not become a Weapon Soul or prove that the vessel remembers the same events.

A deceased wielder's soul cannot be relabelled as the weapon's dormant identity merely because the wielder died holding it.

### Soul Titles

[Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may affect how people, spirits, institutions, or a genuine Weapon Soul later perceive a bearer. A title cannot manufacture a dormant soul, and a famous title attached to an object is not itself a Weapon Soul.

### Soul Space and Soul Avatars

[Soul Space](../soul/SOUL_SPACE.md) may provide symbolic access to a genuine Weapon Soul relationship. A symbolic weapon, forge, voice, or sealed door remains an interface and cannot create or contain the soul by imagination alone.

[Soul Avatars](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) may coordinate already accessible soul evidence. They cannot awaken, speak for, absorb, or appoint the identity of a Dormant Weapon Soul.

### Akashic Archive

The [Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) may preserve records of making, use, indications, repairs, and soul-sensitive observations. Information supports investigation; it does not create the soul, supply complete present access, or make an observer's interpretation infallible.

### Reincarnation

Before intertwining, a dormant Weapon Soul remains with its vessel. [Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) does not carry it with a former user, return the vessel as starting equipment, or grant the new incarnation automatic knowledge of its location.

The user's memories, Echoes, Skills, Stat XP, Titles, Resonance, and other protected Imprints persist only through their own rules. The vessel and its Weapon Soul continue in the world, subject to custody, damage, time, and other actors.

### Skills and Development

The [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) owns learned handling, maintenance, craft, perception, communication, and tactical methods. The [Development System](../progression/README.md) owns changes in current capability and reliability.

Training with a meaningful vessel may develop the user and deepen shared history. Dormancy does not transfer that Development into the weapon or grant the user mastery. A Dormant Weapon Soul supplies no extra action, Technique, passive defense, or parallel attention.

### Human Classes and Institutions

[Human Classes and Professions](../human/README.md) may preserve doctrines, care practices, detection methods, laws, and ethical procedures concerning Weapon Souls. Institutions may control physical custody or access through current power.

No school, guild, temple, army, Academy, maker, owner, licence, office, or rank can create a Weapon Soul by declaration, guarantee awakening, own future consent, or turn its members into compatible wielders.

### Monster Evolution and Species

Monster anatomy, Species Traits, current instincts, Hybrid Forms, Mutations, and Evolution Routes remain owned by [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md). A body-grown tool, natural weapon, shed structure, symbiont, or living instrument may raise a claim under [Unconventional Soul Weapons](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md), but dormancy cannot grant anatomy or merge a Weapon Soul into a monster body by implication.

## GM Procedure

When deciding whether a dormant Weapon Soul exists:

1. **Identify the vessel.** State the particular coherent weapon or tool whose identity is being considered.
2. **Trace Vessel History.** Record making, purpose, use, relationships, treatment, alterations, consequences, and continuity without inventing a heroic history after the claim.
3. **Separate adjacent sources.** Identify enchantments, residues, spirits, constructs, Soul Echoes, user Habits, reputations, and ordinary object behavior independently.
4. **Assess identity coherence.** Ask whether the history supports a distinct continuing identity rather than only sentimental attachment or accumulated events.
5. **Assess Vessel Continuity.** Examine repair, replacement, division, reforging, copying, and physical survival qualitatively.
6. **Establish world truth.** Decide whether no Weapon Soul, an uncertain forming continuity, or a Dormant Weapon Soul actually exists before resolving discovery or awakening.
7. **Determine evidence by observer.** Apply source limits and preserve uncertainty; do not make silence prove absence or attachment prove personhood.
8. **Apply current limits.** Dormancy grants no active capability, communication, consent, persistence through Reincarnation, or guaranteed awakening.
9. **Protect agency and soul integrity.** Treat credible risk proportionately without assigning a complete personality before awakening.
10. **Record only campaign state externally.** The vessel, soul, history, custody, evidence, and current condition belong in the external Campaign Record.

## Worked Examples

### The Famous Sword With No Soul

A ceremonial sword has an ancient name, priceless decoration, and a public story linking it to several rulers. Most of its history involved display, replacement parts, and political claims. Soul-sensitive examination detects an oath enchantment but no distinct continuity.

The sword remains culturally powerful and magically active without possessing a Weapon Soul. Fame and age do not force one into existence.

### The Quiet Orchard Knife

One knife passes through generations of orchard keepers. It is repeatedly repaired, used for grafting living trees, carried during failed harvests, and entrusted to apprentices when they accept responsibility for the grove. No public legend knows it.

Its coherent role, relationships, care, and consequence can support a Dormant Weapon Soul. Peaceful work and private history are sufficient kinds of meaning even though no battle occurred.

### The Repaired Hammer

A craft hammer receives a new handle many times and later a reforged head made partly from its damaged metal. The makers preserve its working proportions, marks, responsibilities, and apprenticeship relationships as an intentional continuation.

No material percentage decides the result. The causal bridge among matter, function, users, repair, and identity may preserve Vessel Continuity. A superficially identical new hammer made from a copied pattern does not receive the original soul.

### The Talking Enchanted Spear

A spear recites tactical warnings placed by its maker. Its speech is accurate but fixed, and examination traces every response to the enchantment. The spear may also possess a Dormant Weapon Soul, but the voice is not evidence of awakened agency unless a distinct source is established.

The enchantment owns the warnings. Dormancy grants no second intelligence.

### The Weapon Held at Final Death

A warrior dies protecting others with an ordinary axe that had shared years of consequential service. The death may deepen the axe's Vessel History and leave strong Resonance among survivors. The warrior forms a Soul Echo through the warrior's own soul rules.

The Echo does not move into the axe. Unless intertwining had already occurred, the axe and any dormant Weapon Soul remain in the world while the warrior enters Interlife.

### The Manufactured Relic

An institution commissions a thousand identical knives, gives each a dramatic name, stages a ritual duel, and declares that the surviving blade must contain a soul.

The ritual, danger, labels, and witness count cannot substitute for identity coherence. Real harm and public belief may create world consequences without making the claimed Weapon Soul exist.

### The Divided Blade

A broken blade is made into two daggers. Both contain original metal, and rival heirs claim each is the authentic continuation.

The soul is not copied. The GM examines the actual repair intent, use, relationships, metaphysical anchor, and later evidence. One dagger may preserve continuity, continuity may remain inaccessible while separated, or the division may have harmed the anchor. The heirs' beliefs remain socially real without determining world truth.

## Safeguards

- **No soul in every object:** history and sentiment do not make universal animism.
- **No category lock:** material, rarity, weapon type, maker, social class, species, and intended violence do not decide eligibility.
- **No formation counter:** use count, kills, age, repairs, owners, sacrifices, names, and ceremonies are evidence only through actual meaning and continuity.
- **No dormant bonuses:** dormancy grants no statistics, Skills, guidance, actions, durability, luck, magic, or plot protection.
- **No enchantment confusion:** speech, memory, aura, animation, possession, and magical effects retain their actual source.
- **No predestined personality:** history influences later identity without fixing morality, loyalty, temperament, or consent.
- **No material-percentage identity:** Vessel Continuity is qualitative and causal.
- **No copying or fragment farming:** replicas, divisions, blueprints, and reforging cannot multiply one soul.
- **No Reincarnation carryover before intertwining:** the vessel remains in the world.
- **No institutional ownership of personhood:** custody and law cannot predetermine future consent or loyalty.
- **No retroactive guilt:** good-faith ordinary use before credible evidence is not reclassified automatically as abuse.
- **No casual soul destruction:** established soul harm requires an explicit source, fair evidence, proportional stakes, resistance where possible, and persistent consequence.
- **No campaign canon:** particular vessels, histories, evidence, and soul states remain external campaign data.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not decide when awakening occurs, whether a newly awakened Weapon Soul consents to any bond, how intertwining works, which personality emerges, how Soul Weapons evolve, how Weapon Echoes form, how [Legacy Weapons](LEGACY_WEAPONS.md) establish later relationships, how compatibility is assessed, how Weapon Manifestations operate, or which [unconventional vessels](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md) qualify. Those claims belong to their dedicated Soul Weapon rules.
