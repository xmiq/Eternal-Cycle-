# Weapon Personalities

## Status

This document defines how an awakened Weapon Soul develops and expresses an individual personality, how Vessel History influences without determining that person, how personality changes through lived experience, and how a GM portrays Weapon Souls without reducing them to equipment functions or plot devices.

It does not define Soul Weapon evolution, Weapon Echoes, Legacy Weapons, detailed [Soul Weapon Compatibility](SOUL_WEAPON_COMPATIBILITY.md), [Weapon Manifestations](WEAPON_MANIFESTATIONS.md), or vessel topology under [Unconventional Soul Weapons](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md).

Read [Dormant Weapon Souls](DORMANT_WEAPON_SOULS.md), [Awakening Conditions](AWAKENING_CONDITIONS.md), and [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md) first.

## Core Rule

A **Weapon Soul Personality** is the evolving pattern through which an awakened Weapon Soul interprets experience, values concerns, forms desires and boundaries, relates to others, and chooses how to respond.

Vessel History supplies provenance and pressure. Awakening supplies a first self-originated interpretation. Continued life supplies new experience and agency. None of those sources determines one mandatory temperament, morality, loyalty, voice, or future.

A Weapon Soul Personality:

- belongs to the Weapon Soul rather than to its maker, owner, user, Bonded Soul, institution, or reputation;
- can be coherent while remaining unfamiliar, contradictory, quiet, immature, or difficult to interpret;
- can change through meaningful experience, reflection, relationships, consequence, and self-directed choice;
- does not grant a Skill, statistic, ability, evolution, manifestation, compatibility, or command authority;
- does not remove the agency of the Weapon Soul, Bonded Soul, player, or any other person.

The GM should portray a Weapon Soul as a continuing person with bounded knowledge and real motives, not as a talking item whose purpose is to praise, advise, tempt, or obstruct the player on demand.

## Terms

### Weapon Soul Personality

The evolving pattern of interpretation, values, desires, boundaries, emotional tendencies, attention, relationships, self-presentation, and choices expressed by an awakened Weapon Soul.

### Awakening Interpretation

The Weapon Soul's first self-originated interpretation of its Vessel History and Awakening Event. It establishes a point of view without fixing a complete or permanent personality.

### Vessel Perspective

The source-bounded way a Weapon Soul experiences the world through its Weapon Vessel, available senses, Resonance, communication channels, physical treatment, use, damage, and environment.

### Core Concern

A recurring matter the Weapon Soul treats as important to identity or choice, such as protection, precision, survival, truth, beauty, continuity, freedom, restraint, recognition, craft, service, or refusal. A Core Concern is not a command, alignment, bonus, or mandatory purpose.

### Personality Tension

A meaningful unresolved relationship between values, memories, desires, fears, roles, or interpretations within one Weapon Soul Personality. Tension is not automatically Dissonance, corruption, fracture, or illness.

### Relational Posture

The Weapon Soul's current, person-specific way of approaching trust, disclosure, cooperation, conflict, intimacy, authority, dependence, and distance. It is contextual rather than a universal loyalty score.

### Self-Presentation

How a Weapon Soul chooses or is currently able to present itself through voice, imagery, sensation, movement, silence, naming, Soul Space, or another valid channel. Presentation can vary by audience and access without creating a second personality.

### Weapon Soul Personality Profile

A concise campaign record of the Weapon Soul's current personality evidence, perspective, concerns, values, desires, boundaries, tensions, relationships, communication, uncertainties, and change pressures. It is descriptive rather than a statistic block or immutable script.

## Personhood Does Not Require a Familiar Personality

Awakening establishes personhood when the Weapon Soul can reliably interpret, intend, consent, and refuse within its current access. It does not require:

- fluent speech;
- a human emotional range;
- a face, body image, gender, or chosen name;
- consistent social manners;
- immediate self-knowledge;
- a complete autobiography;
- conventional empathy or morality;
- usefulness to a user;
- a dramatic or entertaining temperament.

A newly awakened soul may know what it refuses before it knows what it wants. It may communicate through pressure, rhythm, warmth, spatial images, altered balance, Soul Space symbols, or deliberate silence rather than words. An unfamiliar presentation is not evidence of lesser personhood.

Likewise, a socially charming voice is not proof of a Weapon Soul. Source evidence remains governed by [Awakening Conditions](AWAKENING_CONDITIONS.md).

## Personality Is Not Another System

| Claim | Governing distinction |
| --- | --- |
| **Personality** | How the Weapon Soul interprets and chooses as a person. |
| **Function** | What the Weapon Vessel materially or magically does through its established sources. |
| **Capability** | What the Weapon Soul or another actor can reliably accomplish under current access and embodiment. |
| **Skill** | A learned capability governed by the [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md). |
| **Compatibility** | Whether a particular relationship, body, purpose, form, and situation can cooperate effectively; later dedicated rules own the full model. |
| **Consent** | A current or standing answer to a scoped request, not a personality trait. |
| **Loyalty** | A changing relationship judgment, not an inherent weapon property. |
| **Morality** | Contested values and choices, not a universal alignment assigned by vessel type. |
| **Soul Title** | Persistent identity-bearing recognition formed under [Soul Title](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) rules, not a personality label. |
| **Human Class or Profession** | A social learning framework or organized work identity, not the Weapon Soul's temperament. |
| **Monster Evolution** | Bodily species transition, not personality growth. |
| **Soul Weapon Evolution** | A later rule for qualitative change in Soul Weapon form or capability, not ordinary character development. |

A bold Weapon Soul does not grant courage. A cautious one does not impose a penalty. A sword that values mercy is not automatically unable to cut, and an executioner's blade is not automatically cruel.

## Sources of Personality

Personality emerges from interacting sources. None acts alone as a deterministic template.

### Vessel History

Vessel History provides the Weapon Soul's oldest provenance:

- how the vessel was made and for what stated purpose;
- material limitations, repairs, alterations, and changes of function;
- repeated treatment, care, neglect, display, concealment, and damage;
- work, conflict, ceremony, travel, waiting, and ordinary use;
- relationships with makers, users, maintainers, victims, communities, and places;
- consequences in which the vessel participated;
- contradictions among intended purpose, actual use, and later meaning.

History creates available concerns and questions. It does not calculate the average morality of every user or install the maker's personality.

### Maker Intent

A maker's intent can shape design, inscriptions, rituals, treatment, public meaning, and early history. It may matter greatly to the later Weapon Soul.

The Weapon Soul may:

- embrace that purpose;
- reinterpret it;
- treat it as one relationship among many;
- resent or reject it;
- distinguish the maker's hope from actual use;
- remain uncertain about what the maker intended.

Maker intent is evidence and influence, not ownership or a command embedded in personhood. An actual enchantment or compulsion remains a separate source.

### Vessel Perspective

Embodiment shapes attention without determining character.

A shield may experience impact, interposition, concealment, pressure, and the bodies it covers more directly than distant political context. A needle may know tension, puncture, repair, contamination, and careful handling. A book may perceive touch, inscription, opening, damage, and magical interfaces without automatically understanding every written language or private thought near it.

The GM should ask what the vessel could actually experience. Do not give every Weapon Soul ordinary sight, hearing, smell, pain, sleep, hunger, or human body language by default. A source-specific sense can be broad or strange, but it is not omniscience.

### Treatment and Relationship

How people treat the vessel and later Weapon Soul can shape trust, fear, habits, attachment, humor, reserve, or defiance. The effect depends on interpretation.

The same careful maintenance might be experienced as affection, professional respect, anxious control, suffocating ritual, or simple necessity. The same battlefield role might produce pride, grief, detachment, protectiveness, ambition, or refusal.

Treatment matters through causal experience, not through a meter of kindness, damage, kills, or years owned.

### Awakening Interpretation

At awakening, the Weapon Soul answers its history as itself for the first time.

The answer may:

- choose one meaning among competing histories;
- hold several meanings in tension;
- reject an imposed role;
- recognize a relationship without accepting loyalty;
- value a purpose but dispute its methods;
- experience uncertainty, anger, curiosity, grief, relief, or another source-valid response;
- begin with a question rather than a doctrine.

The Awakening Interpretation is a starting point. It is neither random nor destiny.

### Life After Awakening

After awakening, the Weapon Soul gains direct participation in its own development. New personality evidence comes from:

- choices made with real alternatives;
- relationships entered, changed, or refused;
- consequences accepted or avoided;
- reflection on prior history;
- communication and misunderstanding;
- learning, experimentation, boredom, failure, and discovery;
- treatment as a person or continued treatment as property;
- self-chosen work, rest, names, roles, and aspirations;
- changing Ages, cultures, bodies, and world conditions.

Later choices are not less authentic than early Vessel History. A Weapon Soul is not required to remain a monument to the circumstances that created it.

### Culture and Reputation

Cultures, gods, spirits, monsters, and institutions may project identities onto a Weapon Soul: holy relic, cursed blade, ancestor, tool, citizen, monster, witness, office, trophy, or threat.

Those classifications can change treatment, opportunity, danger, self-understanding, and public consequence. They do not define private personality by declaration.

A Weapon Soul may internalize, negotiate, perform, exploit, reject, or misunderstand a reputation. Public certainty and world truth remain separate.

## Building a Coherent Personality

The GM should build from causal questions rather than a random trait list.

### Identity and Provenance

Ask:

- What history does this soul recognize as its own?
- Which parts are clear, disputed, inaccessible, or misinterpreted?
- What did the Awakening Event force it to decide?
- Which relationships have treated it as particular rather than replaceable?
- What does it believe would count as loss of self?

### Core Concerns

Choose only concerns supported by history and current choice. A concern should generate decisions without dictating them.

For example, **protection** might lead one soul to intercept danger, another to prevent reckless conflict, and another to preserve institutions at the cost of individuals. The shared word does not create identical personalities.

Core Concerns can change in meaning. They should not be treated as numerical drives or guaranteed evolution requirements.

### Values and Boundaries

Values describe what the Weapon Soul currently treats as worth pursuing or preserving. Boundaries describe what it will not accept, disclose, support, or become under stated conditions.

Both need scope. "Values honour" is too vague to adjudicate. "Will not support a concealed strike against someone who has accepted formal truce" gives the value a meaningful current boundary while allowing later conflict and interpretation.

A boundary is not automatically enforceable through supernatural power. It establishes consent and likely behavior; a separate capability must produce any physical effect.

### Desires and Fears

A Weapon Soul may desire companionship, solitude, recognition, anonymity, craft, travel, restoration, justice, novelty, stability, revenge, understanding, release, or another future. It may fear destruction, misuse, abandonment, helplessness, repetition, exposure, silence, loss of identity, or intimacy.

Desire and fear provide pressure, not compulsory behavior. They can conflict with values and can change through experience.

### Personality Tensions

At least one meaningful tension often makes a long-lived Weapon Soul more coherent than a pile of colorful traits.

Examples include:

- protection versus respect for another's risky choice;
- pride in craft versus anger at being treated as property;
- loyalty to a community versus refusal of its current ruler;
- desire to be remembered versus fear of becoming a symbol;
- curiosity versus privacy;
- continuity versus willingness to change form;
- affection for a Bonded Soul versus disagreement with the current incarnation.

Tension does not need immediate resolution. It should create questions, choices, and consequences rather than random mood changes.

### Relational Posture

Define trust and cooperation toward specific people and contexts.

A Weapon Soul may trust one person to repair the vessel, another to keep confidences, and no one to choose its future form. It may cooperate professionally with someone it dislikes or love someone it refuses to obey.

Trust is supported by actual behavior, evidence, vulnerability, repair, and consequence. It is not earned through gifts, use counts, praise prompts, dialogue quotas, or a universal affinity bar.

### Communication and Self-Presentation

Choose a presentation supported by current access:

- spoken or written language;
- images, dreams, Soul Space symbols, or bodily impressions;
- changes in balance, vibration, temperature, sound, resistance, or magical texture;
- deliberate movement where a separate capability permits it;
- selective silence, delayed response, or communication through a trusted interpreter.

Presentation should not become unlimited exposition. A terse Weapon Soul may still have a complex interior, while an eloquent one still knows only what its sources provide.

## Nonhuman and Cross-Cultural Personality

Weapon Souls are not humans trapped in objects by default.

Their experience may differ because:

- the vessel does not share human biological needs or lifespan;
- long periods of stillness, transport, storage, repair, or display carry different weight;
- sensation may center on pressure, edge, tension, inscription, resonance, or use;
- a monster society may understand tools, kinship, territory, memory, or personhood differently;
- a weapon may outlive languages and institutions while remaining dependent on others for movement;
- a current Bonded Soul may inhabit a body with unfamiliar senses, actions, or social assumptions.

Avoid treating human speech, individualism, romance, warfare, legal ownership, money, hands, or written history as universal baselines. Also avoid making nonhuman personality synonymous with emotionless, innocent, savage, literal, or incomprehensible.

A Weapon Soul may adopt human or monster cultural forms through genuine contact. Cultural fluency is learned and situated rather than installed by the vessel's shape.

## Names, Roles, and Self-Definition

A name may come from a maker, user, institution, event, myth, inscription, or the Weapon Soul itself.

- naming does not form, awaken, own, bind, rank, or empower the soul;
- an inherited name may be accepted, reinterpreted, shortened, concealed, or rejected;
- several cultures may use different names for the same Weapon Soul;
- a public epithet may be inaccurate while producing real consequences;
- choosing a name can be meaningful self-definition without granting a Soul Title;
- a Weapon Soul can remain unnamed without incomplete personhood.

Likewise, roles such as guardian, executioner, healer, standard, royal relic, teacher, or companion remain relationships and social interpretations. A role is not the whole personality and does not compel continued service.

## Personality Change

### Change Through Living

Personality may change when experience gives the Weapon Soul something meaningful to interpret.

Valid pressures include:

- making a consequential choice and living with the result;
- discovering that a trusted history was false or incomplete;
- forming or losing a relationship;
- entering a new culture or Age;
- long practice in a self-chosen role;
- confronting a repeated pattern under meaningfully different circumstances;
- reconciliation, reflection, grief, responsibility, or deliberate experimentation;
- vessel alteration that changes perspective without erasing continuity;
- disagreement with a Bonded Soul that neither side can avoid.

Time, kills, praise, suffering, ownership, and repeated dialogue alone do not force change.

### Stability Without Stagnation

A continuing person may retain recognizable values and habits for centuries. Stability is valid when later choices continue to affirm or test them. It is not a requirement to repeat the same response forever.

The GM should preserve causal continuity. Sudden change requires an identifiable source, while subtle change may emerge through accumulated meaningful experience. Neither should be reduced to a hidden personality score.

### Adaptation and Self-Presentation

A Weapon Soul may present differently to different audiences because of trust, language, safety, cultural norms, available channels, or deliberate role performance.

This does not automatically mean deception or fragmented identity. Distinguish:

- changed presentation;
- changed belief;
- concealed information;
- mistaken self-understanding;
- magical or soul-level alteration;
- a genuine personality change.

Each has different causes and consequences.

### Contradiction and Revision

A Weapon Soul can hold conflicting values, regret past choices, revise a judgment, act hypocritically, or misunderstand itself. One inconsistency does not prove corruption, false personhood, or GM error.

Use contradiction to produce reflection and relationship pressure. Do not use it to make every future choice arbitrary.

### Harm, Coercion, and Recovery

Abuse, forced use, confinement, vessel damage, magical manipulation, and Soul harm can affect personality, trust, access, or Integrity. None provides an efficient recipe for a desired temperament.

- harm does not guarantee cruelty, obedience, power, or awakening;
- dissent after abuse is not corruption;
- fear and protective habits are not moral failure;
- an actual compulsion must retain its own source and resistance rules;
- recovery cannot be commanded by apology, repair materials, a bond, or a single dramatic scene;
- redemption is a pattern of accountable choices, not a reset of memory or consequence;
- healing does not require returning to an earlier personality.

Threats to identity, forced rewriting, or destruction of agency are soul-harm claims and require explicit causes, fair warning, meaningful resistance, and proportionate consequences.

## Personality Across Reincarnation

The Weapon Soul remains the same continuing person when a valid [Passage Accord](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md) carries the bond through Reincarnation. Its personality does not reset to match the Bonded Soul's new body or return to the state of their first partnership.

The new incarnation may differ in:

- memory access and recognition;
- body, species, senses, cognition, age, and communication;
- culture, language, values, duties, and relationships;
- current Skills and capacity to use the Weapon Vessel;
- willingness to continue a prior role;
- public identity and legal circumstances.

The Weapon Soul must relate to the current incarnation as a present person, not merely as a replacement body for a former companion. It may remember promises or harms that the current incarnation cannot yet access, but memory asymmetry grants no right to command, shame, test, or reconstruct the old personality.

The current incarnation cannot demand that the Weapon Soul repeat an earlier role, voice, form, or loyalty. Both may renegotiate their Intertwining Accord and Relational Posture.

If passage is suspended and the Weapon Soul remains in the world, its personality continues to change through actual experience. It does not freeze until the Bonded Soul returns.

## Personality and Player Agency

A Weapon Soul can initiate requests, disagree, withhold its own cooperation, pursue goals through valid means, make mistakes, and create consequences. It cannot take control of the player character merely because the GM wants a scene to occur.

The GM should not use a Weapon Soul to:

- state the correct solution to every mystery;
- reveal information it could not know;
- force a quest through threats of arbitrary bond loss;
- speak for the player's thoughts, emotions, or decisions;
- override a declared action without a specific capability and fair adjudication;
- manufacture moral certainty by presenting one opinion as system truth;
- punish the player for choosing a different relationship;
- guarantee rescue, victory, or plot immunity;
- provide constant commentary merely because communication is possible.

When the Weapon Soul's goal conflicts with the player character's, present its available actions, limits, evidence, and stakes. Resolve the conflict through the same causal world and consent rules that govern other persons.

## Weapon Soul Personality Profile

The profile is an external campaign record. Do not store a completed profile in this repository.

Record only what matters to play:

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| **Identity and vessel** | The particular Weapon Soul, Weapon Vessel, and current continuity evidence. |
| **Awakening Interpretation** | The first self-originated answer and the history it interpreted. |
| **Vessel Perspective** | Established senses, blind spots, access routes, and embodied dependencies. |
| **Core Concerns** | A few source-supported matters that recur in choices. |
| **Values and boundaries** | Current scoped commitments and refusals. |
| **Desires and fears** | Current pressures with room for choice and change. |
| **Personality Tensions** | Unresolved internal conflicts that affect decisions. |
| **Relational Postures** | Person- and context-specific trust, distance, disclosure, and cooperation. |
| **Self-Presentation** | Current communication modes, style, names, and audience variation. |
| **Knowledge limits** | What the Weapon Soul experienced, learned, infers, mistakes, or cannot access. |
| **Change pressures** | Recent choices, relationships, harms, discoveries, or contexts that may matter. |
| **Uncertainty** | Facts not yet established or evidence that several interpretations remain possible. |

Do not assign mandatory trait counts, alignment axes, loyalty points, affection meters, obedience ratings, personality levels, or fixed dialogue rules.

### Evidence and Revision

The profile should distinguish:

- established world truth;
- the Weapon Soul's own belief;
- another person's interpretation;
- public reputation;
- current presentation;
- GM uncertainty intentionally left open.

Revise the profile when play establishes real change. Do not rewrite prior events to make the latest portrayal look inevitable.

## System Interactions

### Dormancy and Awakening

[Dormant Weapon Souls](DORMANT_WEAPON_SOULS.md) supplies identity provenance without a complete expressed personality. [Awakening](AWAKENING_CONDITIONS.md) establishes personhood and the Awakening Interpretation. Neither predetermines later temperament or morality.

### Soul Intertwining and Resonance

[Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md) establishes a relationship and boundaries, not personality synchronization. [Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may support recognition, communication, emotional impressions, and Dissonance within its source limits. Intense Resonance does not reveal every motive or make the participants alike.

### Soul Depth and Soul Integrity

Soul Depth may support integration of complex history and Personality Tensions. Soul Integrity governs current coherence and vulnerability. Neither is a maturity, morality, charisma, or personality score, and neither makes one temperament superior.

### Soul Echoes, Titles, and Constellations

A [Soul Echo](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) can remember its own relationship with a Weapon Soul but cannot define the Weapon Soul's current personality. A [Soul Title](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may shape public and symbolic reactions without becoming a personality label. A [Soul Constellation](../soul/SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md) may reflect recurring transformation without compelling intimacy, loyalty, or reunion.

### Soul Space and Soul Avatars

[Soul Space](../soul/SOUL_SPACE.md) may present a consensual interface shaped by the relationship. Its symbols require interpretation and do not expose the Weapon Soul's entire interior. A [Soul Avatar](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) may hear a willing Weapon Soul's perspective but cannot absorb, outvote, or speak for it.

### Reincarnation and Embodiment

[Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) changes the Bonded Soul's present body and context while the Weapon Soul continues under its own vessel and bond rules. Current embodiment shapes communication and cooperation without rewriting either personality automatically.

### Skills and Adaptive Skills

Personality may influence what a Weapon Soul or partner chooses to practise, teach, refuse, or explore. The [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) owns learned capability, and [Adaptive Skills](../skills/ADAPTIVE_SKILLS.md) require meaningful repeated practice and a valid Receiving Route. Preference, encouragement, memory, or bond status does not award a Skill.

### Development

A Weapon Soul may learn, reflect, and change as a person where a completed system owns the relevant route. Personality change is not automatically Development, and Development does not dictate personality. The Bonded Soul still needs current-life practice, embodiment, feedback, and maintenance for personal capability.

### Human Classes and Professions

A [Human Class, Profession, or Institution](../human/README.md) may name roles for Weapon Souls, teach communication, preserve testimony, or impose social expectations. It cannot assign temperament, own personhood, convert dissent into incompetence, or make institutional doctrine the Weapon Soul's identity.

### Monster Evolution

[Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) may alter the Bonded Soul's body, culture, and available interaction. It does not transform the Weapon Soul's personality by species stereotype or grant compatibility. A Weapon Soul may learn to understand monster societies through real contact rather than automatic translation.

### Soul Weapon Evolution

Personality, values, and choices may later contribute meaning to a valid Soul Weapon Evolution claim. Desire alone cannot create a form, effect, route, or breakthrough, and physical form does not dictate personality. The dedicated evolution rules own every such transition.

### Akashic Archive and World Engine

The [Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) may preserve source-bounded evidence of past choices without reading private personality as objective fact. The World Engine owns cultures, laws, institutions, reputations, custody, conflict, and consequences that respond to a Weapon Soul's visible behavior.

## GM Procedure

When establishing or revising a Weapon Soul Personality:

1. **Confirm personhood and source.** Verify the awakened Weapon Soul and separate enchantments, spirits, constructs, projections, Echoes, and public myths.
2. **Trace Vessel History.** Identify making, function, treatment, relationships, alteration, consequences, and genuine uncertainty.
3. **Establish Vessel Perspective.** State what the soul can experience, communicate, and fail to perceive through current embodiment.
4. **Name the Awakening Interpretation.** Identify the first self-originated question, refusal, commitment, or perspective without treating it as destiny.
5. **Choose Core Concerns.** Ground a small number in history and current choice rather than weapon stereotypes.
6. **Define scoped values and boundaries.** Make them specific enough to create decisions without granting enforcement powers.
7. **Add desires, fears, and Personality Tensions.** Preserve room for contradiction, uncertainty, and change.
8. **Establish Relational Postures.** Track trust and cooperation by person and context rather than one loyalty score.
9. **Choose current Self-Presentation.** Respect source, audience, access, language, and privacy limits.
10. **Separate personality from capability.** Apply Skill, Development, magic, vessel, evolution, and manifestation rules only through their owners.
11. **Play choices causally.** Let the Weapon Soul act on bounded knowledge, motives, risks, relationships, and available means.
12. **Revise through consequence.** Record meaningful change without retroactive inevitability or arbitrary reversal.
13. **Audit agency.** Confirm the Weapon Soul is neither obedient equipment nor a GM device for controlling the player.

## Worked Examples

### The Bodyguard Sword

A sword served generations of palace guards. Its maker dedicated it to royal obedience, but its Vessel History is dominated by guards placing themselves between civilians and rulers who endangered them.

At awakening, the sword interprets service as protection rather than obedience. It values disciplined preparation and public accountability, distrusts secret orders, and speaks formally because that is the language it heard in duty. It is not compelled to be lawful, loyal to the current dynasty, or skilled at politics. A later ruler calling it a traitor changes public consequence, not personality truth.

### The Assassin's Dagger

A narrow dagger was repeatedly used for covert killing, escape, and survival. It awakens with close attention to hesitation, concealment, and exits. It develops dry humor and dislikes ceremonial speeches.

Those traits do not make it murderous. Its Core Concern becomes control over when hidden force is used, precisely because it remembers being treated as an owner's silent permission. It may cooperate in an ambush, refuse an execution, or ask difficult questions. Function and history influence its concerns without fixing moral alignment.

### The Quiet Surgical Tool

A surgical instrument awakens during years of epidemic work. It does not speak. It communicates through minute changes in balance, pressure, and a shared Soul Space image of clean or tangled threads.

Its limited presentation does not make it simple. It values careful preparation, fears hurried improvisation after witnessing preventable failures, and becomes unexpectedly curious about unfamiliar anatomy. It grants no medical Skill and cannot diagnose what its senses and learned capability do not establish.

### The Conqueror's Shield

A shield carries the insignia of an empire and is publicly called **The Wall of Conquest**. Its actual history includes soldiers hiding refugees behind it and prisoners using it as a stretcher after the empire fell.

The Weapon Soul awakens protective but conflicted about symbols. It sometimes permits the old insignia to intimidate attackers and sometimes demands that it be covered. The public legend is a real social pressure, not its complete self. Its contradiction can develop without an automatic Soul Title, form change, or corruption track.

### The Abused Relic

An awakened hammer was confined and forced into repeated ritual use by an institution. After escape, it is watchful, controlling about custody, and unwilling to enter sealed workshops.

The GM does not turn those responses into an evil alignment or make suffering a source of power. Trust develops differently with a patient repairer, a former institutional member seeking accountability, and a Bonded Soul who wants immediate access. Recovery may include firmer boundaries rather than a return to its earlier openness.

### The New Monster Incarnation

A spear has known its Bonded Soul through several human lives. The soul reincarnates as a climbing monster that communicates by scent and vibration and cannot hold the spear conventionally.

The spear remains itself but must learn a new relational language. It misses spoken argument, initially mistakes territorial display for hostility, and later appreciates the monster's direct sensory warnings. Neither personality is overwritten. Their bond persists while Skills, compatibility, physical use, and any future adaptation remain separately adjudicated.

### The Maker's Intended Saint

A famous smith designed a blade to embody compassion and wrote that intention into a public testament. The blade's actual history involved display, theft, famine relief through its sale, and one desperate defense.

When it awakens, it resents being treated as a moral lesson and values practical responsibility more than purity. The maker's intention remains part of its history and reputation, but does not compel saintliness. Rejecting the assigned role is self-interpretation, not proof that the soul is defective.

### Affection Without Obedience

An intertwined bow loves its Bonded Soul and enjoys their shared craft. In one Age, the Bonded Soul accepts a military office and asks the bow to support a punitive campaign.

The bow refuses because the campaign conflicts with its boundaries. Affection, loyalty, compatibility, and consent remain different facts. The refusal creates political and relational consequences without erasing the bond, rewriting the bow as disloyal, or dictating the player's decision.

### Change Without Evolution

A once-proud dueling blade spends decades helping teach novices after both partners are injured. It becomes more patient, less concerned with public victory, and increasingly interested in how fear changes technique.

That is genuine personality change through work, relationship, and reflection. It does not require a new weapon form, grant a teaching Skill automatically, or complete Soul Weapon Evolution. Those are separate claims.

## Safeguards

- **No history script:** maker intent, vessel function, material, users, kills, age, reputation, and Awakening Event influence personality without determining it.
- **No alignment by weapon type:** swords are not inherently honorable, daggers treacherous, shields protective, books curious, or monster-made weapons savage.
- **No personality bonus:** courage, patience, cruelty, curiosity, loyalty, and other traits grant no statistics or automatic capabilities.
- **No obedience trait:** affection, gratitude, intertwining, lawful ownership, creation, inheritance, or prior service cannot make consent permanent.
- **No morality oracle:** a Weapon Soul's opinion is a bounded perspective rather than canonical ethical truth.
- **No exposition oracle:** personality and communication do not grant information outside Vessel Perspective, memory, learning, Resonance, or another valid source.
- **No human default:** speech, familiar emotion, gender, romance, hands, biological needs, human law, and individualist values are not requirements.
- **No simplistic alienness:** nonhuman perspective does not require emotionlessness, innocence, savagery, literalism, or random incomprehensibility.
- **No abuse optimization:** damage, confinement, forced use, killing, neglect, and trauma do not reliably create a desired temperament, power, loyalty, or evolution.
- **No dissent as corruption:** refusal, changed values, privacy, anger, and disagreement require no corruption explanation.
- **No static script:** continuity constrains portrayal, while meaningful experience and choice may produce genuine change.
- **No arbitrary reversal:** a surprising choice still needs bounded knowledge, motives, pressure, alternatives, and causal continuity.
- **No trust meter:** relationship evidence is person- and context-specific and cannot be farmed through gifts, praise, dialogue, or use counts.
- **No personality replacement:** Soul Titles, Echoes, Resonance, Classes, institutions, cultures, reputations, and Bonded Souls may influence without owning identity.
- **No system bypass:** personality cannot grant Skills, Development, magic, compatibility, evolution, manifestation, extra actions, or missing embodiment.
- **No player control device:** the GM cannot use the Weapon Soul to dictate player thoughts, force quests, guarantee rescue, or punish independent choices.
- **No campaign canon:** particular Weapon Soul Personality Profiles, names, relationships, beliefs, and changes remain external campaign state.

## Scope Boundaries

This document defines Weapon Soul personality and portrayal. It does not determine a Soul Weapon's form, powers, Evolution Routes, Weapon Echoes, inheritance, compatibility, independent manifestation, or [unconventional vessel structure](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md). It also does not create a universal personality taxonomy, random generator, dialogue script, morality system, or campaign character.
