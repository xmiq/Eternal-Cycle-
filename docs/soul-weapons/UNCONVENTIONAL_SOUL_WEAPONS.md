# Unconventional Soul Weapons

## Status

This document defines how the Soul Weapon rules apply to vessels whose function, scale, material, topology, embodiment, or mode of use differs from a singular inanimate handheld instrument.

It does not create a superior weapon category, a universal list of qualifying objects, a new soul type, group Soul Intertwining, unrestricted transformation, distributed free actions, magical matter, living-species rules, or a way to turn people into equipment.

Read [Dormant Weapon Souls](DORMANT_WEAPON_SOULS.md), [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md), [Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md), [Soul Weapon Compatibility](SOUL_WEAPON_COMPATIBILITY.md), and [Weapon Manifestations](WEAPON_MANIFESTATIONS.md) first.

## Core Rule

An **Unconventional Soul Weapon** is a rules-facing description for a Soul Weapon whose Weapon Vessel needs explicit treatment beyond the default singular physical instrument.

Every unconventional claim must still establish:

1. a coherent weapon, meaningful tool, or instrument rather than a label, institution, person, or abstract capability;
2. the exact Vessel Topology and Vessel Boundary;
3. every existing soul or person involved before a new Weapon Soul is considered;
4. whether the structure supports one Weapon Soul, several distinct Weapon Souls, or no Weapon Soul;
5. the identity-bearing structures, components, interfaces, users, and consumables;
6. Vessel History and Vessel Continuity across use, growth, repair, replacement, separation, and recombination;
7. the Weapon Soul's actual Vessel Perspective and Agency Routing;
8. current compatibility, handling, maintenance, access, costs, vulnerabilities, and failure;
9. separate qualification for Awakening, Soul Intertwining, Evolution, Manifestation, Reincarnation passage, and Legacy status;
10. world consequences and an external campaign record.

Unusual appearance, size, life, complexity, magic, rarity, danger, distributed parts, or cultural importance does not relax any requirement.

## Unconventional Is Not a Metaphysical Rank

What one culture calls an impossible living arsenal may be an ordinary craft tradition elsewhere. A human sword may be more alien to a colony organism than a coordinated needle cloud. The term **unconventional** helps the rules notice additional ownership and continuity questions; it does not define objective normality.

An unconventional vessel is not automatically:

- older, rarer, more intelligent, or more powerful;
- harder or easier to awaken;
- better suited to Soul Intertwining;
- more compatible with monsters or humans;
- entitled to extra capabilities, components, actions, or forms;
- exempt from damage, maintenance, custody, law, or embodiment;
- an advanced stage of Soul Weapon Evolution;
- more important to the story.

The same rules apply whether the vessel is a pair of kitchen knives, a living surgical instrument, a fortress gate, a cloud of linked needles, a spellbook, a prosthetic shield, or a conventional spear.

## Terms

### Unconventional Soul Weapon

A rules-facing description for a Soul Weapon whose vessel function, scale, material, topology, embodiment, or use requires explicit treatment beyond a singular physical instrument. It is not a rank or separate soul species.

### Vessel Topology

The actual arrangement by which one or more components, structures, spaces, interfaces, and relationships form a proposed Weapon Vessel.

### Vessel Boundary

The qualitative distinction between what belongs to the Weapon Vessel and what merely uses, carries, supplies, inhabits, repairs, surrounds, or interacts with it.

### Identity-Bearing Structure

A material, functional, relational, historical, or source-supported part of a vessel whose continuity materially contributes to the Weapon Soul remaining the same person.

No single Identity-Bearing Structure is a universal soul container. Different structures can matter differently, and continuity remains qualitative.

### Vessel Component

A bounded part of a composite, paired, distributed, modular, body-linked, or site-bound vessel whose role in identity, function, access, and continuity must be established rather than assumed.

Within this document, **Component** is shorthand for Vessel Component.

### Composite Vessel

A Weapon Vessel made of several integrated components that function and accumulate history as one coherent instrument.

### Paired Vessel

A Weapon Vessel whose established identity and ordinary function require two or another small bounded set of coordinated counterparts.

### Distributed Vessel

A Weapon Vessel whose identity-bearing or functional components can occupy distinct locations while remaining connected through an established topology and one coherent continuity.

### Modular Vessel

A Weapon Vessel with a bounded set of replaceable, exchangeable, attachable, or reconfigurable components under an established Vessel Envelope.

### Vessel Envelope

The documented range of components, replacements, configurations, interfaces, and operating conditions that count as ordinary continuity for one Modular or otherwise variable Vessel rather than a new Soul Weapon Form.

### Biotic Vessel

A grown, living, regenerative, metabolic, or organically maintained instrument whose personhood, species, host, and soul arrangement have been resolved before Weapon Soul formation is considered.

### Body-Linked Vessel

A distinct tool, prosthesis, implant, grown instrument, or external organ-like structure physically integrated with a person's current body without becoming that person's anatomy or soul by implication.

### Site-Bound Vessel

A coherent structure, installation, or bounded place-function that serves as a weapon or meaningful tool while remaining physically anchored to a particular site. Vehicles use the same topology audit but are not site-bound merely because they are large.

### Agency Routing

The established way one Weapon Soul directs attention, perception, communication, and weapon-owned physical agency through the components of its Vessel Topology without becoming several actors.

### Concordant Assembly

A cooperative configuration containing two or more already distinct Weapon Souls that retain separate personhood, vessels, consent, bonds, memory, and agency. It is not one merged Soul Weapon.

### Vessel Topology Profile

A qualitative campaign record of a vessel's boundary, components, soul arrangement, identity-bearing structures, interfaces, topology, continuity, perspective, Agency Routing, compatibility, maintenance, failure, and Reincarnation implications.

The Profile belongs outside this repository. It is not an equipment sheet or a source of capability.

## The Vessel Topology Audit

Before adjudicating formation, awakening, bonding, evolution, or manifestation, establish the vessel itself.

### 1. Name the Instrument

Describe what the proposed vessel actually does and how people or other beings use it.

Useful questions include:

- what choices can be enacted through this instrument;
- what work, protection, harm, creation, movement, perception, or communication it enables;
- whether users treat it as one continuing instrument or an interchangeable collection;
- which consequences depend on this particular vessel;
- whether the same claim would still be made if its dramatic appearance were removed.

The term **weapon** in Soul Weapon names the system's history, not a combat requirement. A loom, surgical tool, ship, shield, musical instrument, cooking implement, surveying device, or rescue apparatus can qualify when its own history does. An abstract profession, technique, organization, or virtue cannot.

### 2. Find Existing Persons First

List every soul, spirit, sapient construct, host, symbiont, inhabitant, user, bound entity, and other possible person already involved.

Do not create a Weapon Soul to explain an effect already owned by:

- a living person's soul;
- a monster or human body;
- a spirit possessing or inhabiting an object;
- an enchantment, curse, god, construct, familiar, or artificial intelligence;
- a Soul Echo, Weapon Echo, Soul Avatar, or Soul Space Construct;
- an institution acting through members;
- several existing Weapon Souls cooperating.

Uncertainty about personhood is a reason to investigate and protect agency, not permission to classify a being as equipment.

### 3. Draw the Vessel Boundary

Identify what belongs to the vessel and what remains external.

Possible external elements include:

- wielders, pilots, crews, patients, audiences, riders, inhabitants, and operators;
- fuel, ammunition, ingredients, ink, food, blood, replacement stock, and other consumables;
- sheaths, racks, docks, rooms, harnesses, mounts, tools, and repair facilities;
- temporary enchantments, summoned matter, weather, terrain, and external rituals;
- institutions, offices, legal ownership, traditions, reputations, and communities;
- outputs such as arrows, shed plates, woven cloth, written copies, sparks, notes, or harvested material.

An external element may be essential to use without being part of the Weapon Soul. A ship needs a crew; the crew does not become its anatomy. A spellbook needs a reader and magic; neither becomes a page.

### 4. Classify Components

For each element near the proposed Vessel Boundary, record its current role.

| Element role | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Identity-bearing** | Its material, function, relationship, history, or source materially supports the same Vessel continuity. |
| **Structurally essential** | The current vessel cannot remain organized or embodied without it, though replacement may or may not preserve continuity. |
| **Functionally essential** | A named use depends on it, while the Weapon Soul may remain coherent if that function is lost. |
| **Replaceable module** | The Vessel Envelope permits bounded replacement or exchange without treating every module as a new soul. |
| **Consumable or output** | It is spent, released, generated, copied, shed, or replenished without carrying the Weapon Soul by default. |
| **Interface or support** | It connects vessel and user or supplies operation while retaining separate ownership and continuity. |
| **External person** | It remains a distinct agent and can never be reduced to a component by utility or law. |

These roles can change through causal history. No role is a rarity tier, material percentage, or hidden soul slot.

### 5. Determine the Soul Arrangement

Use actual formation history and existing personhood rather than visual count.

Possible arrangements include:

- one coherent vessel with one Weapon Soul;
- one paired, composite, modular, or distributed vessel with one Weapon Soul across its topology;
- several separate vessels with several separate Weapon Souls;
- a Concordant Assembly of several Weapon Souls cooperating without merger;
- a coherent instrument with no Weapon Soul;
- an object occupied by another kind of soul or effect;
- an unresolved case where evidence does not yet support a conclusion.

One object can contain several mechanisms without containing several souls. Several objects can form one vessel without multiplying personhood. Conversely, assembling several already ensouled objects cannot erase or merge their persons.

### 6. Establish Continuity and Failure

Describe how the topology persists through ordinary use and what threatens it.

Address:

- maintenance, growth, healing, repair, replacement, and reconfiguration;
- permitted separation, range, latency, and reconnection;
- loss or destruction of identity-bearing and functional components;
- missing modules, inactive configurations, and degraded function;
- whether one Anchor or several relationships sustain continuity;
- what evidence distinguishes damage, inaccessibility, division, reconstruction, and replacement;
- how the Weapon Soul survives, suffers, becomes inaccessible, or faces true soul harm under established rules.

Vessel Continuity has no universal core, quorum, percentage, radius, or replacement count. The Profile states what matters for this vessel and why.

### 7. Define Perspective and Agency Routing

State what the Weapon Soul can actually perceive, attend to, communicate through, and direct.

Several components do not imply:

- sight or hearing from every part;
- perfect simultaneous awareness;
- unlimited range or communication;
- one action per component;
- immunity to distraction, interference, latency, or contradictory input;
- knowledge possessed by every user or inhabitant;
- several personalities or Weapon Echoes.

Agency Routing must identify its source, bandwidth, coordination demands, unavailable claims, and failure modes. A distributed body may support parallel motion as one bounded capability, but component count cannot become an action multiplier.

### 8. Apply the Existing Soul Weapon Sequence

After the topology is coherent, resolve each later claim through its owner:

1. [Dormant Weapon Soul](DORMANT_WEAPON_SOULS.md) formation;
2. [Awakening](AWAKENING_CONDITIONS.md);
3. [Weapon Personality](WEAPON_PERSONALITIES.md);
4. [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md);
5. [Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md);
6. [Weapon Echoes](WEAPON_ECHOES.md);
7. [Legacy status](LEGACY_WEAPONS.md);
8. [Soul Weapon Compatibility](SOUL_WEAPON_COMPATIBILITY.md);
9. [Weapon Manifestation](WEAPON_MANIFESTATIONS.md);
10. Reincarnation access and world consequences.

Topology answers none of these by itself.

## Single-Soul Composite Vessels

A Composite Vessel may support one Weapon Soul when its components were made, used, maintained, recognized, and changed as one coherent instrument.

Examples may include:

- a surgical kit whose tools have long served as one deliberately maintained practice set;
- a shield assembled from interlocking plates whose repair history belongs to the whole;
- a musical instrument with many separately replaceable parts;
- a siege engine or vehicle whose subsystems share one continuing function and history;
- a transforming tool whose bounded configurations were established before soul formation.

The claim requires more than storage in one box or use by one owner. Components accumulated opportunistically, replaced without identity, or used independently may remain an ordinary collection.

### Component Loss

Losing one Component does not automatically:

- move the entire Weapon Soul into that part;
- copy the Weapon Soul into what remains;
- kill or split the soul;
- erase all function;
- preserve full capability;
- create a replacement component.

Resolve what identity, function, perspective, and source actually depended on the missing part. The vessel may become impaired, inaccessible, unstable, reconfigured, or genuinely threatened without changing the number of souls.

### Component Replacement

Replacement follows [Vessel Continuity](DORMANT_WEAPON_SOULS.md). A Vessel Envelope can make ordinary renewal legible, but it cannot convert unlimited upgrades into free continuity.

A replacement must retain its own:

- material and craft source;
- installation time and Skill;
- compatibility and maintenance;
- altered function and risk;
- prior soul or enchantment, if any;
- consequences for the Weapon Soul's identity and consent.

Adding a powerful component does not make its capability soul-owned. Removing it does not erase another system's capability from history.

## Paired Vessels

A Paired Vessel has one soul only when the pair's formation and history support one instrument-level identity.

Two matching blades may be:

- one Paired Vessel with one Weapon Soul;
- two vessels with two Weapon Souls;
- one ensouled blade and one ordinary counterpart;
- two ordinary objects;
- a temporary fighting configuration with no shared vessel identity.

Symmetry, maker intent, one sheath, a paired name, or simultaneous use does not settle the answer.

If one Weapon Soul spans the pair:

- separation does not duplicate it;
- one component may become the stronger current Anchor without becoming the whole by declaration;
- each component can have different damage, custody, compatibility, and handling;
- separate wielders act through their own bodies and Skills;
- the Weapon Soul retains one Agency Routing structure and one set of personhood protections;
- loss of one counterpart may impair, reshape, or threaten continuity without producing a second soul.

Restoring a missing counterpart requires actual craft, material, relationship, consent, and continuity. It is not an automatic respawn.

## Multiple-Soul Assemblies

When components already possess distinct Weapon Souls, they remain distinct persons.

A **Concordant Assembly** may let them cooperate as one larger instrument through:

- scoped mutual consent;
- compatible physical and metaphysical interfaces;
- separate capability sources;
- learned coordination;
- explicit decision and refusal routes;
- component-level damage and withdrawal consequences;
- a bounded shared purpose.

The assembly does not create:

- a new overarching Weapon Soul;
- group consciousness or fused identity;
- one Intertwining Accord covering every Weapon Soul;
- pooled Soul Depth, Resonance, Titles, Weapon Echoes, Skills, or capabilities;
- majority ownership over a dissenting member;
- a single Reincarnation route for the group;
- free actions or universal compatibility.

Each Weapon Soul forms, awakens, bonds, manifests, evolves, remembers, and passes through Reincarnation under its own rules. Cooperation can be meaningful without pretending the participants are one person.

## Distributed Vessels

A Distributed Vessel can sustain one Weapon Soul across separated components only when real continuity connects the topology.

Possible supports include:

- physical links such as chains, threads, roots, channels, frames, or circulating components;
- repeated coordinated return to one maintained configuration;
- a source-defined communication or control network;
- coherent manufacturing, repair, naming, use, and responsibility across the whole;
- stable relationships that treat the components as one instrument for causal reasons;
- a Weapon Evolution Route that deliberately reorganizes one prior vessel into a distributed form.

Mere similarity, common ownership, storage, remote command, serial numbers, shared enchantment, or mass production is insufficient.

### Range and Disconnection

Every Distributed Vessel must define:

- normal component range and connection;
- what information can cross that range;
- delay, interference, environmental limits, and suppression;
- what the Weapon Soul perceives from each component;
- how components are recovered, repaired, or replaced;
- what happens when a part crosses the supported boundary;
- how temporary disconnection differs from loss or division.

A disconnected component does not acquire a copied soul. It may become inert, limited, unreachable, physically usable by someone else, or dangerous according to its actual sources.

### Distributed Action

One Weapon Soul may direct several components as one coordinated physical pattern only when an established capability supports it.

That pattern retains:

- one person making decisions;
- finite attention and sensory fidelity;
- source-defined speed, force, range, and precision;
- interference, obstruction, and counterplay;
- coordination demands and Practised Reliability;
- meaningful tradeoffs between breadth and precision.

Component count cannot produce one full attack, spell, defense, reaction, or independent plan per part. Separate users can act with separate components through their own bodies and Skills, but their actions do not become weapon-owned duplicates.

## Modular and Reconfigurable Vessels

A Modular Vessel uses a bounded Vessel Envelope to distinguish ordinary configuration from qualitative Soul Weapon Evolution.

The Profile should state:

- which modules are identity-bearing, essential, replaceable, consumable, or external;
- which configurations are ordinary uses of the same vessel;
- what transition, tools, time, and Skill reconfiguration requires;
- which sources own module capabilities;
- what happens when a module is missing or incompatible;
- whether a module can be used independently and what that means for continuity;
- when change exceeds the Vessel Envelope and requires repair, reconstruction, or Weapon Evolution.

An established folding spear does not evolve every time it folds. A weapon that qualitatively reorganizes into a new persistent topology may require [Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md). [Stable Multiplicity](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md) remains a bounded evolved configuration set, not an excuse to declare every attachment a permanent form.

Blueprints, molds, recipes, copied inscriptions, and interchangeable modules do not copy the Weapon Soul. A module with its own soul remains a person rather than being absorbed.

## Biotic and Living Vessels

### Existing Life Comes First

A living being does not become a Weapon Vessel because someone uses, breeds, commands, rides, wears, or describes that being as a weapon.

If a creature, plant, colony, symbiont, familiar, construct, organ, or other living structure already has a soul or personhood, that person remains governed by its own embodiment and species rules. This system does not add a second Weapon Soul inside it, convert consent into ownership, or classify its body as equipment.

A willing person can fight with natural weapons, carry another Weapon Soul, cooperate with a Weapon Soul, or enter a Soul Constellation. The person does not become a Soul Weapon.

### When a Biotic Vessel Can Qualify

A Biotic Vessel may support Weapon Soul formation only when:

1. it is a coherent grown or living instrument rather than an existing person relabelled as a tool;
2. its soul arrangement is established and does not duplicate a host, species soul, spirit, or construct mind;
3. its metabolism, growth, healing, reproduction, and dependencies are defined by their actual source;
4. its Vessel Boundary distinguishes host, users, symbionts, fuel, offspring, shed outputs, and environment;
5. Vessel History and continuity belong to this particular instrument;
6. damage, care, consent, and agency receive the same protections as any other Weapon Soul.

Once a Biotic Vessel's Weapon Soul awakens, it is a person. Terms such as cultivated, bred, owned, harvested, or programmed create social claims, not metaphysical authority.

### Growth and Healing

Growth and healing do not provide free upgrades or immortality.

The Biotic Vessel must define:

- resource and environmental needs;
- maturation or renewal patterns;
- scars, disease, toxicity, dormancy, and death risks;
- which changes are ordinary growth;
- which changes belong to Monster Evolution, Mutation, Magic, craft, or Weapon Evolution;
- whether removed material is dead matter, living output, a functional component, or another person.

Regeneration cannot duplicate the Weapon Soul. Repeatedly cutting and regrowing parts does not farm components, Echoes, Soul Depth, Evolution, or capability.

### Offspring and Propagation

Seeds, spores, cuttings, buds, eggs, copies, clones, shed fragments, and offspring do not inherit or copy the Weapon Soul.

They may become:

- ordinary biological outputs;
- separate organisms under species rules;
- new instruments with their own future histories;
- components retained by the original Vessel Topology;
- failed or harmful growths under an established source.

Creating a new soul, person, species lineage, or inherited Weapon Soul requires its own rules and cannot be inferred from resemblance.

## Body-Linked and Body-Grown Vessels

Natural claws, fangs, horns, tails, venom organs, shells, magical organs, and similar structures belong to the current body and [Species Development](../progression/SPECIES_DEVELOPMENT.md). They are not separate Weapon Vessels by default.

A Body-Linked Vessel can qualify when a distinct tool identity and continuity exist across an actual boundary, such as:

- a prosthetic limb used as an instrument;
- an implanted shield or blade with its own maintained structure;
- a detachable organ-like tool deliberately grown for repeated work;
- a non-person symbiotic instrument with separate continuity;
- a body-mounted modular apparatus;
- a shed structure that begins a separate history after detachment.

The claim must resolve:

- whether the structure is anatomy, equipment, prosthesis, graft, symbiont, Mutation, Hybridization, or Weapon Vessel;
- whether removal harms the host and who can consent;
- which person controls movement, sensation, maintenance, and disclosure;
- whether bodily growth replaces Vessel Components and at what cost;
- how host death, Reincarnation, healing, metamorphosis, and Monster Evolution affect the boundary;
- whether the Weapon Soul can refuse use without being treated as bodily malfunction;
- how compatibility and Skill make joint action reliable.

A host cannot manufacture a second soul by declaring part of their own body separate. A detached claw, bone, feather, shell, or horn does not carry a copy of the donor's soul. It may become an ordinary tool and accumulate a new Vessel History over time.

## Wearable, Defensive, and Prosthetic Vessels

Armour, shields, clothing, masks, gauntlets, harnesses, prostheses, mobility devices, and protective tools may qualify when their own Vessel History does.

Wearing or integrating one does not:

- merge souls or bodies;
- add passive statistics;
- grant movement, senses, pain tolerance, protection, or technique beyond actual sources;
- make removal harmless;
- make every worn component part of the Weapon Vessel;
- create a Human Class, Profession, Species Trait, or monster form;
- authorize the Weapon Soul to control the wearer's body.

These vessels often require especially clear consent, privacy, sensory, medical, and emergency boundaries. A life-supporting Soul Weapon may be necessary to one person's survival without becoming mandatory for the game or owned by that person.

## Record-Bearing and Performative Vessels

Books, maps, decks, instruments, masks, scripts, brushes, tablets, coded devices, and other record-bearing or performative tools can develop Weapon Souls.

Keep medium, content, user knowledge, and external effects separate.

- a spellbook's pages and bindings may form the Vessel, while its spells remain owned by Magic and learned use by Skills;
- a copied text does not copy the Weapon Soul;
- a song performed through an instrument is not automatically part of the vessel;
- notation can preserve information without preserving personhood;
- replacing pages may preserve or threaten continuity according to actual history;
- memorizing the contents does not transfer the soul into the reader;
- burning the final physical copy can matter without making information itself the Weapon Soul.

A record can be one Identity-Bearing Structure among several. Pure information is not enough by itself.

## Site-Bound Vessels and Vehicles

A vehicle, gate, bridge, forge, workshop, tower, defensive wall, mill, observatory, or similar structure may qualify only when it functions as a coherent weapon or meaningful tool with a defensible Vessel Boundary.

Scale does not grant power. A Site-Bound Vessel may be unable to move, perceive occupants, operate its mechanisms, defend itself, communicate beyond one room, or survive loss of a foundation.

The Profile must distinguish:

- structure from surrounding land and ecosystem;
- instrument from institution, city, crew, inhabitants, owners, and users;
- integrated mechanisms from replaceable equipment and supplies;
- the Weapon Soul from place spirits, gods, wards, constructs, and accumulated public memory;
- ordinary operation from weapon-owned action;
- local custody from personhood and consent;
- physical continuity from a name transferred to a replacement structure.

An entire kingdom, army, guild, tradition, road network, language, or civilization is not a Weapon Vessel. A place whose identity is primarily ecological, ancestral, divine, or communal may belong to another soul or spirit framework rather than the Soul Weapon system.

## Consumables, Projectiles, and Generated Outputs

A Weapon Vessel may use, fire, grow, release, spend, or transform materials without placing its soul in every output.

Examples include:

- arrows, bolts, bullets, sling stones, thrown blades, and needles;
- ink, paper, pigments, reagents, fuel, food, water, blood, mana, and medicine;
- seeds, spores, silk, resin, venom, shed plates, feathers, and teeth;
- sound, light, heat, smoke, sparks, force, and temporary magical matter;
- printed pages, copied maps, forged products, woven cloth, prepared meals, and repaired objects.

An output is part of the continuing Weapon Vessel only when the established topology says so. Recoverable projectiles may be Components; ordinary ammunition may be consumable; a particular returned arrow may develop its own Vessel History; a generated copy does not inherit the source Weapon Soul.

No production loop may duplicate personhood, harvest soul-bound material without consequence, or turn regeneration into infinite wealth.

## Mutable, Fluid, and Source-Dependent Vessels

A vessel may be liquid, gaseous, particulate, energetic, magical, shadow-like, dream-linked, or otherwise non-solid only when an existing source establishes a coherent continuing instrument.

The source must define:

- what carries continuity when shape changes;
- how the vessel remains bounded and distinguishable from its environment;
- what supplies matter, energy, stability, and return;
- what counts as loss, leakage, dispersal, contamination, and repair;
- which configurations are ordinary, which are Stable Multiplicity, and which require Evolution or Manifestation;
- how containment, suppression, environment, and countermeasures work;
- why ending one expression does or does not threaten the Weapon Soul.

This document does not create magical matter or a free source for such vessels. Magical contributions must qualify under the relevant [Magic](../magic/README.md) owner; a genuinely missing narrow mechanic may be handled only as Provisional under the [Alpha Playtest Rules](../gm/ALPHA_PLAYTEST_RULES.md).

## Conceptual-Only Claims Do Not Qualify

A Skill, technique, oath, law, office, Class, Profession, institution, reputation, story, title, language, spell, recipe, song, combat style, relationship, or abstract concept is not a Weapon Vessel by itself.

Such things may:

- shape Vessel History and personality;
- provide meaning or social recognition;
- be recorded in a physical instrument;
- become a Target or bounded relationship for Concept-Bearing Reorganisation;
- guide a Weapon Evolution Route;
- affect a Soul Title, Resonance Link, Class, Skill, or magical effect through its own rules.

They cannot remove the need for a coherent vessel and current embodiment. A Soul Weapon may become concept-bearing without becoming a disembodied metaphor that can appear in any object, user, copy, or Age.

## Awakening and Personality

Unconventional topology does not change the [Awakening Conditions](AWAKENING_CONDITIONS.md).

The GM must establish:

- one coherent Vessel History for each proposed Weapon Soul;
- Shared Awakening Pressure that affects the actual topology;
- Reciprocal Imprinting rather than component count or spectacle;
- one self-originated Awakening Interpretation per newly awakened Weapon Soul;
- evidence that distinguishes the Weapon Soul from existing persons and sources.

A distributed Weapon Soul has one current [Weapon Personality](WEAPON_PERSONALITIES.md), not one mood or voice per component. Its Vessel Perspective may contain varied sensory and functional viewpoints, but those are experiences of one person.

Several Weapon Souls in a Concordant Assembly retain several personalities. Agreement cannot be presumed, and conflict does not prove malfunction.

## Soul Intertwining

One Weapon Soul, however complex its vessel, forms one standard [Soul Weapon Bond](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md) with one Bonded Soul through one fully qualified Intertwining Accord.

Components do not form separate bonds. A Paired or Distributed Vessel with one Weapon Soul does not gain one passage route per part.

A Concordant Assembly cannot form a group bond. Each Weapon Soul must separately establish:

- distinct consent and capacity;
- a genuine Resonance Basis;
- its own Intertwining Accord and Bond Boundaries;
- compatible passage terms;
- current complexity the proposed Bonded Soul can sustain.

One continuing soul may have several Soul Weapon Bonds only as allowed by the existing complexity safeguards. Assembly convenience does not waive them.

## Weapon Evolution

Unconventional topology may exist from formation or result from a valid [Weapon Evolution Route](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md).

A lasting change requires Weapon Evolution when it qualitatively reorganizes:

- the Vessel Boundary;
- number or roles of identity-bearing Components;
- Agency Routing;
- ordinary configuration set;
- continuity and failure structure;
- biotic growth or body linkage as a soul-bound form;
- source-owned capabilities;
- relationship between vessel and physical expression.

Routine folding, assembly, healing, growth, component exchange, loading, docking, or maintenance within an established Vessel Envelope is not Evolution.

Combining powerful objects does not guarantee a route. Existing souls cannot be fused or sacrificed as components through this system. Every route retains consent, sources, transition, tradeoffs, costs, closure, and anti-stacking safeguards.

## Weapon Echoes

One Weapon Soul preserves its own [Weapon Echoes](WEAPON_ECHOES.md) across the whole Vessel Perspective.

A Weapon Chapter may center on:

- loss or recovery of a major Component;
- a long separation across distributed parts;
- a change in Vessel Envelope;
- service by a crew or community;
- migration from one host body to another valid Body-Linked arrangement;
- transformation from singular to composite form;
- survival after a site or culture ends.

The Chapter remains one bounded first-person history. Each component does not generate a separate Echo, and several simultaneous sensory inputs do not create several past personalities.

In a Concordant Assembly, each Weapon Soul owns its own Echoes, privacy, recall, and testimony. Similar memories do not merge into objective truth.

## Compatibility

Apply [Soul Weapon Compatibility](SOUL_WEAPON_COMPATIBILITY.md) to one proposed interaction at a time.

An unconventional claim may require separate findings for:

- each user or operator;
- each relevant Component or configuration;
- whole-vessel soul interface;
- handling, attachment, piloting, wearing, or bodily integration;
- communication across range or several participants;
- source access and environmental support;
- maintenance and repair methods;
- Reincarnation passage and later physical expression.

Do not average component findings. One operator may safely use a shield plate but not the full armour. A monster may carry a distributed vessel while lacking the senses to coordinate it. A ship may trust a captain while remaining incompatible with the captain's navigation method.

Accommodations remain material and causal. Unconventional anatomy can inspire better interfaces without producing universal compatibility.

## Weapon Manifestations

[Weapon Manifestations](WEAPON_MANIFESTATIONS.md) remains the owner of temporary external expression.

For an unconventional vessel, the Manifestation Route must additionally state:

- which Components physically appear and which remain external;
- whether the route expresses one vessel, a temporary subset, or one configuration;
- how Component continuity avoids duplication;
- how Agency Routing operates through manifested and unmanifested parts;
- which material, biotic, site-bound, or source dependencies remain;
- how the manifestation terminates without leaving harvestable copies;
- whether a Concordant Assembly member is manifesting separately.

A distributed or composite topology does not repeal the one-center rule. One Weapon Soul may perform a bounded coordinated pattern through several parts only through established capability, finite attention, and real counterplay. A Manifestation cannot mint an army of Components.

## Reincarnation

An effective Passage Accord may carry one Weapon Soul, one bond, and the persistent Soul Weapon Form through Reincarnation under the existing rules.

It does not automatically carry:

- material Components, site, vehicle, crew, inhabitants, host tissue, fuel, ammunition, stock, outputs, or land;
- other Weapon Souls in a Concordant Assembly;
- another living person's soul or body;
- every module or configuration ever used;
- compatible anatomy, Skill, magic, maintenance, or Manifestation access.

After Reincarnation, establish:

1. which soul and bond actually passed;
2. what happened to each prior Component and external person;
3. whether the current Soul Weapon Form remains coherent but inaccessible;
4. which current source can anchor or express the topology;
5. how the new body can perceive, carry, wear, pilot, supply, or coordinate it;
6. what craft, growth, relationship, and training are needed to rebuild access;
7. which world-bound consequences remain behind.

A Site-Bound Vessel does not fit into a newborn's inventory. A Biotic Vessel does not tear host anatomy through Interlife. A Distributed Vessel does not copy every component beside the new body. Persistence protects the person and valid form, not worldly mass or convenience.

## Legacy and World Consequences

Unconventional vessels can become [Legacy Weapons](LEGACY_WEAPONS.md) under the same historical rules.

Their topology can create distinctive consequences:

- several custodians dispute different Components;
- a city depends on a Site-Bound Weapon Soul while denying its personhood;
- a Biotic Vessel requires habitat, food, care, or seasonal migration;
- a ship's crew changes while the vessel remembers earlier commands;
- a distributed component becomes a relic while the main vessel survives elsewhere;
- a Concordant Assembly fractures over succession or purpose;
- an institution controls maintenance knowledge without owning the Weapon Soul;
- cultures disagree whether a prosthetic or living instrument is property, kin, citizen, sacred office, monster, or threat.

Law and custom can create real access and power without rewriting metaphysical personhood. The [World Engine](../world-engine/WORLD_ENGINE_OVERVIEW.md) preserves material, ecological, political, and historical consequences.

## System Ownership

### Soul Systems

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may connect Targets across a topology but cannot create Components, range, personhood, or Agency Routing. [Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) may support complexity without becoming a soul-count or vessel-size allowance. [Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may shape recognition and pressure without defining the Vessel Boundary.

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) belong to the continuing incarnating soul, while Weapon Echoes belong to the Weapon Soul. [Soul Space](../soul/SOUL_SPACE.md) can represent topology symbolically without creating physical Components. [Soul Avatars](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) cannot absorb a Weapon Soul or operate every part as extra actors.

### Skills and Development

[Skills](../skills/README.md) own learned wielding, piloting, wearing, maintenance, medical integration, component coordination, formation tactics, performance, and safe handling. Adaptive Skills may emerge from real practice without changing personhood or topology by themselves.

[Development](../progression/README.md) owns current access, embodiment, reliability, and effectiveness. Retained progression can accelerate relearning without supplying modules, anatomy, source access, or years of rebuilding.

### Human Classes and Professions

[Human Classes and Professions](../human/README.md) may teach, certify, regulate, build, repair, crew, study, steward, or exploit unconventional vessels. A Class cannot install compatibility or a Soul Weapon, while an institution cannot make several persons one property unit by doctrine.

### Monster Evolution

[Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) owns anatomy, natural weapons, Species Traits, body growth, Mutations, Hybridization, symbiosis, lifecycle, and form change. A Weapon Soul does not turn a prosthesis into inherited anatomy, and a monster Evolution does not awaken an attached tool.

The same event may affect both systems. Record the bodily change under Monster Evolution and the vessel change under Soul Weapon rules without merging their trees.

### Magic and Other Sources

Magic, divine effects, spirits, curses, constructs, technology, and materials retain ownership of every capability they provide. Calling a vessel living, conceptual, distributed, or soul-bound cannot launder those effects into permanent generic Soul power.

## Anti-Exploitation Safeguards

- No unconventional vessel category is a rarity, power, morality, or endgame tier.
- No object, body, structure, collection, site, or idea qualifies solely because it is unusual or important.
- No existing person can be relabelled as a Weapon Vessel to bypass consent, agency, species, or soul protections.
- No natural weapon or body part becomes a separate Weapon Soul without a genuinely distinct vessel identity and history.
- No copy, clone, offspring, cutting, shed part, generated output, projectile, module, or component duplicates a Weapon Soul.
- No composite or paired arrangement merges existing souls, bonds, Skills, Titles, Echoes, Depth, Resonance, or capabilities.
- No distributed topology grants omniscience, unlimited range, one action per component, or immunity to interference.
- No modular system acquires every attached capability permanently or uses replacement as infinite Evolution.
- No Biotic Vessel gains free healing, reproduction, upgrades, resources, or immunity from ordinary needs and harm.
- No Site-Bound Vessel controls every occupant, mechanism, institution, or surrounding place by implication.
- No pure concept, Class, Skill, spell, oath, title, institution, or story becomes a disembodied Soul Weapon.
- No Soul Weapon is mandatory for a viable character, species, Class, Profession, Skill, or progression route.
- No unusual topology bypasses Awakening, Soul Intertwining, compatibility, manifestation, embodiment, current-life effort, consent, cost, or world consequence.
- No component farming, deliberate breakage, regrowth, copying, repeated assembly, distributed attack count, or rapid Reincarnation guarantees formation, awakening, Echoes, Resonance, Depth, Evolution, or capability.
- No repository file may record a campaign's actual vessel, Component map, current damage, inventory, custody, bond, or Manifestation state.

## GM Procedure

When an unconventional Soul Weapon claim arises:

1. state the exact claim and why the proposed structure is a weapon, tool, or coherent instrument;
2. identify every existing person, soul, spirit, construct, host, symbiont, user, and effect before assigning a Weapon Soul;
3. draw the Vessel Boundary and classify Components, interfaces, consumables, outputs, users, and supports;
4. establish the Vessel Topology, Identity-Bearing Structures, and qualitative continuity;
5. determine whether the case contains one Weapon Soul, several distinct souls, no Weapon Soul, or unresolved evidence;
6. define Vessel Perspective, Agency Routing, range, attention, action ownership, maintenance, damage, and failure;
7. apply formation, Awakening, personality, Intertwining, Evolution, Echo, Legacy, compatibility, and Manifestation rules separately;
8. preserve Skill, Development, Class, Profession, species, Magic, and external-source ownership;
9. resolve Reincarnation passage without moving matter, bodies, Components, or other souls by implication;
10. record the Profile and all live consequences in the external Campaign Record.

If the topology cannot explain identity, soul count, continuity, and agency without relying on desired powers, the claim is not ready. Narrow it, investigate it, or keep it Provisional rather than granting the most advantageous interpretation.

The GM may conceal unknown topology facts for mystery, but observable component behavior, damage, range, soul evidence, and source limits must remain consistent and discoverable.

## Worked Examples

### Twin Knives With One Soul

Two kitchen knives were made, stored, used, sharpened, and repaired as one pair through generations of communal cooking and disaster relief. Neither has a separate history, and the pair's distinct roles continually depend on one another.

The evidence can support one Paired Vessel and one Weapon Soul. When one knife is stolen, the soul is not copied. The remaining knife becomes the stronger accessible Anchor, the Weapon Soul's perspective is impaired, and several cooperative uses become unavailable. Replacing the lost knife requires real craft and relationship; buying a matching blade does not restore continuity by appearance.

### Two Swords in a Concordant Assembly

Two old swords awaken separately before a smith joins their hilts into a transforming staff. Each already has a personality, memories, and Vessel History.

The staff is a Concordant Assembly, not a new merged soul. Each sword may consent to particular configurations and refuse others. The user must learn the assembly's mechanics and coordinate two persons. One agreement, bond, or favourable compatibility result cannot speak for both.

### The Needle Cloud

A healer's set of reusable needles has been maintained as one numbered clinical instrument across many crises. A valid source links the needles within a limited room and lets one awakened Weapon Soul sense coarse tension through the set.

The Distributed Vessel can position several needles as one carefully coordinated procedure. It does not gain one independent action or perfect viewpoint per needle. Walls, distance, missing numbers, interference, patient movement, and divided attention matter. Disposable replacement needles remain external until individually integrated through continuity.

### The Spider-Grown Shuttle

A spider monster grows a detachable chitin shuttle for weaving load-bearing rescue silk. The shuttle is maintained, repaired with later growth, and used as a distinct tool through years of dangerous construction.

The spider's web glands and natural silk remain Species Traits. The shuttle may develop its own Weapon Soul only after a separate coherent history; it does not copy the spider's soul. If the pair intertwine and the spider later reincarnates as a human, the Weapon Soul may persist while the human lacks the organ, material, and Skill needed to rebuild or use the shuttle.

### The Living Seed-Staff

A culture cultivates living walking staffs from a vine strain whose ordinary growth has no distinct soul or personhood under the setting's established evidence. One particular staff is pruned, fed, carried, and relied upon through decades of migration before forming and awakening a Weapon Soul.

Its Biotic Vessel needs water, light, soil contact, and treatment for disease. Cuttings do not copy the soul, rapid growth does not grant Evolution, and the culture's claim of ownership cannot command the awakened person. A blight can threaten the vessel without automatically becoming soul corruption.

### The Prosthetic Shield

A warrior uses an integrated forearm prosthesis as a shield, climbing aid, and craft brace. The device develops a distinct Vessel History and later awakens.

The prosthesis remains a Body-Linked Vessel rather than the warrior's anatomy. The Weapon Soul cannot move the warrior's shoulder without an established interface and consent. Removal may cause medical harm, but dependence does not establish ownership. Skill and Physical Development still govern use.

### The Rescue Ship

A rescue ship has one continuing hull-and-keel identity, repeatedly replaced rigging, a changing crew, and a century of consequential voyages. Evidence supports a vehicle-scale Weapon Soul anchored to the ship.

The crew are not Components, and the Weapon Soul does not know every conversation below deck. It may sense strain through established structures but cannot sail itself unless a capability and Agency Routing support that action. If it intertwines with a captain, Reincarnation does not place the entire ship beside the captain's next body.

### The Copied Spellbook

A spellbook's bindings, annotations, repairs, and use support a Weapon Soul. Scribes produce exact copies of every page.

The copies preserve information, not personhood. The original Weapon Soul owns no reader's magical Skill and cannot cast every recorded spell. Rebinding the original may preserve continuity; copying its text into a new volume does not settle transfer without an actual continuity route.

### The Returning Arrow

A bow uses ordinary arrows as consumables. One arrow is repeatedly recovered, repaired, named, and chosen for consequential shots until it develops a history distinct from the ammunition stock.

The bow's Weapon Soul, if any, does not inhabit every arrow. The returning arrow may eventually support its own Weapon Soul, creating two possible persons and compatibility questions rather than a free extension of the bow.

### The Fortress Gate

A fortress gate has been rebuilt around the same hinges, counterweight pattern, public duty, and repair lineage while defending and admitting generations of inhabitants.

It may qualify as a Site-Bound Vessel. The walls, soldiers, city, laws, and nearby land remain external. The Gate Soul cannot command the army or appear in another Age as a complete fortress. Its legal classification as state property conflicts with, but does not settle, awakened personhood.

### The Oath Without a Vessel

A royal order claims that its founding oath is a Soul Weapon carried by every sworn knight.

The oath may be a tradition, Soul Title influence, Class doctrine, Resonance Target, or recorded history. It is not a Weapon Vessel. A particular ceremonial chain, book, bell, or blade could develop a Weapon Soul through its own continuity, but the abstract oath cannot inhabit every copy or initiate.

### The Fluid Chain

A Soul Weapon Evolution Route reorganizes a metal chain into a source-dependent fluid form with three bounded configurations. The source defines cohesion, containment, mass, transition, and vulnerability to heat and contamination.

This is one Mutable and Modular Vessel with Stable Multiplicity, not unlimited shapeshifting. Lost droplets do not become small Weapon Souls, added metal does not integrate automatically, and each configuration still requires handling Skill and current compatibility.
