# Soul Weapon Compatibility

## Status

This document defines how to assess whether a Weapon Soul, another person, a current body, a Soul Weapon Form, and a proposed interaction can function together under particular conditions.

Compatibility constrains access and expression. It does not create personhood, trust, consent, Soul Resonance, Skill, Soul Intertwining, Weapon Evolution, Weapon Manifestation, or capability.

Read [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md), [Weapon Personalities](WEAPON_PERSONALITIES.md), [Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md), [Weapon Echoes](WEAPON_ECHOES.md), [Legacy Weapons](LEGACY_WEAPONS.md), and [Capability Assessment](../progression/CAPABILITY_ASSESSMENT.md) first.

## Core Rule

**Soul Weapon Compatibility** is a qualitative, purpose-specific assessment of whether named participants and sources can sustain one proposed interaction in the current body, form, relationship, environment, and circumstances.

Compatibility is always assessed for a claim such as:

- carry this Weapon Vessel safely;
- communicate through this channel;
- perform this technique with this Soul Weapon Form;
- sustain this cooperative weapon-owned effect;
- establish this proposed Soul Weapon Bond;
- preserve this Passage Accord through a particular transition;
- share this Weapon Echo through this interface;
- complete this repair or evolution transition;
- use this form in this environment against this target.

There is no universal compatibility score between two people. A pair may be compatible for one interaction and incompatible for another, and the answer may change across bodies, forms, purposes, Ages, injuries, environments, and relationships.

## Terms

### Soul Weapon Compatibility Claim

The exact proposed interaction being assessed, including participants, current body, Weapon Soul, Soul Weapon Form or Weapon Vessel, purpose, method, source, environment, duration, and intended effect.

Without a defined claim, “compatible” is too broad to be a playable conclusion.

### Soul Weapon Compatibility Profile

A qualitative campaign record of the compatibility layers, evidence, conditions, constraints, supports, unknowns, consent boundaries, and current finding for one or more related Soul Weapon Compatibility Claims.

The Profile belongs outside this repository. It is not a percentage, affinity score, destiny rating, relationship meter, or permanent judgment of either person.

### Compatibility Layer

One distinct kind of fit relevant to a claim, such as soul interface, relationship, communication, embodiment, method, source, environment, or passage. Layers are assessed separately because success in one cannot silently substitute for another.

### Compatibility Finding

A qualitative conclusion for one layer of one claim under stated conditions.

Use the following findings when helpful:

- **Unknown:** available evidence does not settle the layer.
- **Conflicted:** a present incompatibility prevents, destabilizes, or makes the proposed interaction unsafe.
- **Conditional:** the interaction is possible only through named support, limits, cost, preparation, or circumstances.
- **Workable:** current requirements are sufficiently aligned for an attempt under the stated conditions.
- **Established:** repeated relevant evidence supports reliable operation under the stated conditions.

These are not ranks. Established compatibility in a narrow task may be less useful than Conditional compatibility in a broader one. No finding transfers automatically to another claim.

### Compatibility Support

A specific adaptation, tool, technique, agreement, treatment, resource, environment, communication method, or other condition that addresses a named incompatibility.

Compatibility Support preserves its owner, cost, maintenance, limits, and failure modes. It is not a universal bridge.

### Compatibility Drift

A meaningful change in compatibility caused by a new body, injury, healing, Soul Weapon Evolution, practice, relationship change, source change, environmental change, World Reset, or other causal development.

Drift is not moral decline or automatic bond damage.

## What Compatibility Is Not

### Not Consent

Two participants may be highly compatible and still refuse an interaction. Consent is a current act of agency. Compatibility cannot give it, infer it from silence, or make refusal irrational.

Likewise, consenting to an attempt does not make the attempt compatible or safe.

### Not Trust, Affection, or Loyalty

People who love and trust one another may have incompatible bodies, methods, sources, or goals. Rivals who dislike one another may coordinate effectively for one bounded purpose.

Trust can support communication and cooperation. It cannot replace anatomy, Skill, a viable channel, or current consent.

### Not Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) owns the Basis, Target, Link, Trigger, State, and Dissonance of a genuine connection. Resonance may reveal or support compatibility for a named interaction, but intensity does not prove complete compatibility.

Two souls can resonate strongly while their bodies, methods, values, or passage requirements conflict. Weak or inaccessible Resonance does not prove that ordinary physical cooperation is impossible.

### Not Skill or Mastery

A skilled swordsman may handle the physical vessel expertly while remaining incompatible with its communication channel, bond, purpose, or weapon-owned effect. A willing compatible partner may still lack the Skill to use the form safely.

Compatibility permits or constrains expression. [Skills](../skills/README.md) own learned capability and reliable performance.

### Not Personality Similarity

Shared temperament, morality, humour, aggression, culture, or preferences may make some relationships easier. Similarity is neither required nor sufficient.

Complementary differences may support cooperation. Identical values may intensify conflict over authority or method. Personality is evidence, not a matching key.

### Not Ownership or Qualification

Possession, inheritance, purchase, office, certification, prophecy, bloodline, Class, Profession, Soul Title, reputation, and public acclaim do not establish compatibility.

Institutions may accurately test limited claims. Their recognition remains evidence scoped to those tests rather than metaphysical authority.

### Not Power

Greater Soul Depth, statistics, rank, magic, evolution, fame, age, or combat ability does not make a person universally more compatible. Power may support capacity or overwhelm resistance, but coercion is not compatibility.

### Not Destiny

Compatibility can emerge, change, or disappear through causal life. No weapon category has one predestined species, bloodline, personality, gender, Class, or moral alignment.

## Compatibility Layers

Assess only the layers relevant to the current claim. Do not average them.

### 1. Identity and Soul Interface

This layer asks whether the involved souls and persistent structures can safely sustain the proposed soul-level contact.

Relevant facts may include:

- distinct and coherent personhood;
- Soul Integrity and active Soul Wounds;
- a genuine Resonance Basis and current Link State;
- scope of the proposed contact;
- existing Soul Weapon Bonds and Bond Boundaries;
- interference from Echoes, spirits, curses, enchantments, gods, or world law;
- whether the contact demands more integration than present Soul Depth can support;
- whether identities and sources remain distinguishable.

Identity-interface compatibility does not establish consent or a bond. High Soul Depth cannot force it, and low Soul Depth is not a universal disqualification for simple contact.

### 2. Relational and Purpose Compatibility

This layer asks whether the participants can willingly cooperate for the named purpose under current boundaries.

Relevant facts include:

- current consent;
- trust appropriate to the risk;
- honest understanding of the purpose;
- compatible or negotiable goals;
- authority and decision-making expectations;
- willingness to accept costs and consequences;
- unresolved betrayal, coercion, dependency, or conflicting duties;
- effects on third parties.

Agreement about one purpose does not authorize another. A weapon may support rescue and refuse execution, or accept travel while refusing public display.

### 3. Communication Compatibility

This layer asks whether information can pass with enough fidelity, timing, and privacy for the proposed interaction.

Relevant facts include:

- available senses and Vessel Perspective;
- language, imagery, emotion, vibration, movement, Soul Space, or other channels;
- speed and precision required;
- noise, distance, suppression, injury, or divided attention;
- cultural interpretation and learned conventions;
- privacy and consent boundaries;
- whether feedback can be distinguished from enchantment, instinct, or projection.

Communication need not resemble speech. Clear combat timing and deep conversation may require different channels and produce different findings.

### 4. Embodiment and Handling Compatibility

This layer asks whether the current body and Weapon Vessel or Soul Weapon Form can physically interact as intended.

Relevant facts include:

- scale, mass, balance, grip, attachment, reach, leverage, recoil, and locomotion;
- available limbs, mouths, tails, tendrils, surfaces, senses, and posture;
- strength, endurance, coordination, pain, injury, and maturation;
- material hazards, temperature, toxicity, sharpness, resonance, or magical exposure;
- whether the body can carry, deploy, recover, maintain, or store the form;
- whether an adapter, harness, mount, assistant, or environment supplies missing support.

A persistent bond does not create hands, resize a weapon, remove weight, or grant safe handling. Disability and unconventional anatomy do not imply failure when real accommodations and training support the claim.

### 5. Method and Skill Compatibility

This layer asks whether the actor's current Skills and methods can use the form or cooperative effect for the intended task.

Relevant facts include:

- the specific Skill and its current access;
- whether the body supplies a valid receiving route;
- differences between prior and current weapon forms;
- timing, stance, sensory, resource, and control requirements;
- Practised Reliability under present conditions;
- whether retained Skill history requires translation;
- whether the Weapon Soul can provide feedback it actually owns and can communicate.

Weapon familiarity accelerates learning only through established retained-progression rules. A Weapon Echo can provide context without transferring mastery.

### 6. Source and Channel Compatibility

This layer asks whether every required effect source and channel can operate together without contradiction.

Relevant facts include:

- mana, stamina, material, charge, divine permission, spirit cooperation, or other required resources;
- magical affinities and channels under their owning rules;
- enchantment provenance and maintenance;
- whether several effects compete for the same structure or resource;
- target eligibility and resistance;
- counters, suppression, contamination, and world law;
- whether the weapon-owned effect remains accessible in the current bond state.

Soul Weapon rules cannot invent missing magic to solve a source conflict. Phase 7 and other owning systems define those sources when completed.

### 7. Environmental and Operational Compatibility

This layer asks whether the interaction can function in the current place, time, duration, and operational context.

Relevant facts include:

- terrain, space, gravity, weather, pressure, temperature, light, and concealment;
- local law, custody, social consequences, and available support;
- maintenance, ammunition, repair, transport, and recovery;
- allies, opponents, bystanders, and action economy;
- duration, fatigue, repeated use, and failure consequences;
- whether the current target or problem matches the form's actual affordances.

A pair compatible in a training hall may be incompatible in a flooded tunnel, vacuum, crowded court, or anti-magic region.

### 8. Passage Compatibility

This layer applies only to Reincarnation passage and asks whether the established Soul Weapon Bond, Passage Accord, Weapon Soul, vessel continuity, Bonded Soul, and transition conditions can sustain the intended passage.

It does not ask whether the next body can physically wield the weapon. Passage compatibility and post-Reincarnation handling compatibility are separate claims.

The [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md) rules remain authoritative for agreement, Suspension, Severance, and continuity.

## Building a Compatibility Profile

A useful Soul Weapon Compatibility Profile records:

1. **Claim:** the exact interaction, purpose, duration, and intended effect.
2. **Participants:** the Weapon Soul, bearer or Bonded Soul, other affected persons, and current bond status.
3. **Embodiments:** the current body, Weapon Vessel, Soul Weapon Form, injuries, scale, and relevant conditions.
4. **Required layers:** only the Compatibility Layers that materially affect the claim.
5. **Evidence:** observations, prior attempts, Skill demonstrations, communication, Resonance, records, and source limits.
6. **Findings:** Unknown, Conflicted, Conditional, Workable, or Established for each relevant layer.
7. **Supports:** named tools, accommodations, agreements, training, resources, environments, or participants.
8. **Constraints:** exact conflicts, risks, costs, maintenance, and unavailable claims.
9. **Consent boundaries:** what each participant currently allows and what requires fresh agreement.
10. **Uncertainty:** hidden information, disputed causes, observer limits, and what evidence could change the finding.
11. **Drift triggers:** changes that require reassessment, such as Reincarnation, Evolution, injury, betrayal, World Reset, or source loss.

Do not record a total score, star rating, percentage, “perfect match,” or strongest-wielder rank.

## Compatibility and Soul Intertwining

Compatibility is one part of Soul Intertwining rather than a substitute for it.

### Before Intertwining

The proposed bond needs enough:

- identity-interface compatibility to preserve distinct persons;
- relational and purpose compatibility to support an honest Accord;
- communication compatibility for informed consent;
- Passage compatibility for the standing continuity being proposed;
- viable Vessel Continuity and current Soul Integrity.

Physical handling and combat Skill need not be excellent. A person can form a meaningful Soul Weapon Bond with a tool they do not fight with, a form their current body cannot wield, or a Weapon Soul whose main relationship is craft, testimony, care, or shared duty.

### After Intertwining

The bond persists while layer-specific compatibility changes.

- a new body may block physical use while preserving communication and affection;
- an evolved weapon form may require new technique;
- a relationship dispute may close one purpose while preserving passage;
- injury may make a formerly Established interaction Conditional;
- a World Reset may disrupt an effect source while leaving the persons and bond intact.

Incompatibility does not automatically suspend or sever a bond. Apply Suspension or Severance only through [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md).

## Compatibility Supports and Change

### Accommodation

Tools, harnesses, grips, mounts, frames, prostheses, environmental structures, assistants, and altered procedures may answer an embodiment mismatch.

An accommodation must state:

- which incompatibility it addresses;
- who or what owns it;
- what it enables;
- its limits, cost, maintenance, setup, and failure modes;
- what remains incompatible.

Accommodation does not prove lesser mastery or lesser personhood. It also does not erase the equipment and context that make the interaction possible.

### Practice and Skill Development

Training can improve Method and Skill Compatibility, communication conventions, coordination, reliability, and use of accommodations. It cannot train away a missing source, override refusal, or guarantee soul-level compatibility.

Repetition without feedback, variation, integration, or meaningful use does not create indefinite compatibility progress.

### Relationship Work

Honesty, repair, boundaries, shared consequence, negotiated authority, and reliable conduct may improve relational compatibility. Gifts, flattery, victories, obedience, possession, suffering, and scripted dialogue do not fill a relationship meter.

Reconciliation may fail. Participants may become better understood and still decide not to cooperate.

### Weapon Evolution

[Weapon Evolution](WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md) may reorganize a Soul Weapon Form in response to a genuine incompatibility when a valid route, consent, transition, tradeoff, and source structure exist.

The bearer cannot demand bodily change from the Weapon Soul as an accommodation. The Weapon Soul's consent governs its body, while changes to shared bond structures require every affected participant's consent.

Evolution should answer a meaningful route problem, not grant universal fit. Solving one mismatch may create another.

### Adaptive Skills

[Adaptive Skills](../skills/ADAPTIVE_SKILLS.md) may arise from sustained efforts to coordinate an unusual body, method, and weapon when all Skill requirements are met. They belong to the learner and do not alter the Weapon Soul's personhood or guarantee compatibility outside their trained scope.

### Healing and Repair

Healing a body, restoring Soul Integrity, repairing Vessel Continuity, or treating a communication injury may restore previously available compatibility. Repair does not recreate consent, trust, a severed bond, or retired capabilities by itself.

## Discovery, Testing, and Uncertainty

Compatibility is often partly unknown until interaction produces evidence.

### Fair Testing

A fair compatibility test should identify:

- the claim being tested;
- which layers it can actually observe;
- the current conditions and supports;
- who consents and what risks they accept;
- safe stopping conditions;
- possible false positives and false negatives;
- what the result does not prove.

Useful methods may include:

- conversation through an established channel;
- low-risk handling and movement;
- controlled Skill drills;
- observation of Resonance or Dissonance;
- craft, repair, or maintenance collaboration;
- bounded source activation;
- simulation, scholarship, or comparison with prior evidence;
- gradual trials in varied environments.

### No Omniscient Match Test

A ritual, examiner, institution, god, Soul Title, archive, machine, or magical display may assess only claims its source can observe. No default procedure reveals a complete compatibility destiny.

False beliefs can persist. A culture may mistake physical familiarity for soul approval, or interpret refusal as impurity. The GM keeps world truth, observer evidence, doctrine, and propaganda separate.

### Failure Produces Evidence, Not Automatic Progress

A failed attempt may reveal a constraint, motivate practice, damage participants, alter trust, or change the world. Failure does not automatically improve compatibility, awaken a hidden power, advance Soul Depth, or make the next attempt more likely.

## Reincarnation and Compatibility Drift

A Soul Weapon Bond and its history may persist while almost every current compatibility layer changes.

On Reincarnation, reassess:

- the new body's anatomy, scale, senses, maturation, health, and environment;
- current memory and recognition;
- communication channels;
- Skill access and Receiving Routes;
- physical handling and maintenance;
- source and magical conditions;
- social and legal context;
- current-incarnation consent, goals, and boundaries;
- whether the Passage Accord operated as intended.

Do not reassess the existence of an established bond merely because expression is difficult. Do not assume old compatibility transfers merely because the same continuing soul has returned.

Retained Instincts, Stat XP, Skill Imprints, Soul Echoes, Weapon Echoes, Soul Titles, and Soul Resonance may provide evidence or accelerate relearning through their own rules. None bypasses the current body, present practice, or the Weapon Soul's current agency.

## Cross-Species Compatibility

No species is universally compatible or incompatible with a weapon category.

A monster and a Weapon Soul may develop effective cooperation through:

- nonhuman communication;
- mouth, tail, horn, limb, shell, tendril, swarm, or environmental handling where the actual anatomy supports it;
- harnesses, mounts, crafted interfaces, or allies;
- monster-native Skills;
- bounded Reincarnation Skill crossover;
- a consensual Weapon Evolution Route;
- purposes other than conventional wielding.

Human weapon conventions are evidence about design, not laws of soul compatibility. Likewise, a weapon born in monster society does not install monster instincts in a human bearer.

Species difference may create real cognitive, sensory, scale, material, lifecycle, cultural, or source conflicts. Those conflicts remain specific and may be impossible to solve in the current form. Respecting difference does not require guaranteeing every pairing every expression.

## System Interactions

### Soul Depth and Integrity

[Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) may support complex sustained integration, while Soul Integrity governs current coherence. Neither produces trust, Skill, anatomy, source access, or a favourable result.

### Soul Resonance

Resonance provides target-specific connection and may reveal alignment or Dissonance. Compatibility determines whether a proposed interaction can use that connection under present conditions; it does not replace the Resonance Basis or State.

### Soul Echoes and Weapon Echoes

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) and [Weapon Echoes](WEAPON_ECHOES.md) may supply prior perspectives, warnings, methods, and evidence. Old success does not prove current compatibility, and no Echo consents for a current participant.

### Soul Titles

[Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may shape recognition, opportunity, symbolic authority, and Resonance. A title such as *Dragon Friend*, *King Killer*, or *Bearer of the Last Blade* does not certify compatibility.

### Legacy Weapons

[Legacy Weapons](LEGACY_WEAPONS.md) may maintain different Compatibility Profiles for different stewards, bearers, purposes, and contexts. Compatibility does not transfer through succession and cannot open another standard bond while a prior bond remains Suspended.

### Skills and Development

[Development](../progression/README.md) owns current capability change, while the [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) owns learned methods. Compatibility identifies where those capabilities can interact with the Weapon Soul and form; it grants neither.

### Human Classes and Professions

[Human Classes and Professions](../human/README.md) may provide training, tests, accommodations, craft, vocabulary, and social recognition. Their certifications can support a scoped finding but cannot certify personhood, consent, bond eligibility, or universal compatibility.

### Monster Evolution

[Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) may change anatomy and therefore future compatibility layers. It cannot be selected solely as a weapon-fit upgrade without a valid Evolution Route, and the weapon does not become a free species trait.

### Weapon Evolution

Weapon Evolution may change form-owned compatibility and create Compatibility Drift. It does not automatically update the bearer's Skills, body, source access, or consent.

### Weapon Manifestations

Every [Weapon Manifestation](WEAPON_MANIFESTATIONS.md) is a purpose-specific Compatibility Claim. A favourable Compatibility Profile may support a route under current conditions but does not itself summon, embody, move, or project the Weapon Soul.

### World Engine

Compatibility claims can affect training traditions, weapon design, disability access, law, inheritance, monster diplomacy, military doctrine, trade, prejudice, and institutional power. Social beliefs may respond to real patterns without becoming metaphysical truth.

## GM Adjudication Procedure

When Soul Weapon compatibility matters:

1. **Name the claim.** State the exact interaction, purpose, participants, form, body, context, duration, and intended effect.
2. **Identify ownership.** Separate Weapon Soul capability, bearer Skill, body, bond, magic, equipment, institution, and environment.
3. **Confirm current consent.** Record what each person permits; do not infer agreement from compatibility.
4. **Select relevant layers.** Ignore layers that do not affect the claim and avoid a total score.
5. **Gather evidence.** Use source-bounded observation, history, tests, Echoes, Resonance, and current conditions.
6. **Assign findings.** Mark each relevant layer Unknown, Conflicted, Conditional, Workable, or Established with reasons.
7. **Name supports and constraints.** State exact accommodations, resources, costs, maintenance, risks, and unavailable claims.
8. **Resolve the attempt.** Use current capability, environment, opposition, uncertainty, and consequence rather than compatibility alone.
9. **Apply failure honestly.** Distinguish refusal, lack of Skill, source failure, body mismatch, environmental conflict, and ordinary bad luck.
10. **Record Drift.** Update the external Profile only when causal facts change.
11. **Reveal fair evidence.** Communicate observable limits and uncertainty without exposing hidden world truth automatically.
12. **Audit shortcuts.** Remove results justified only by lineage, weapon category, affinity scores, repetition, suffering, or narrative chosenness.

## Worked Examples

### The Greatsword and the Burrowing Body

A Soul Weapon Bond persists when the Bonded Soul reincarnates as a small burrowing monster. Identity-interface and relational compatibility remain Workable, and vibration through the bond permits Conditional communication. Embodiment and handling compatibility for conventional sword use is Conflicted: the body has no hands and cannot move the blade safely.

The pair can preserve their relationship, seek a tunnel-mounted defensive frame, develop new communication conventions, or later pursue a consensual Evolution Route. The bond does not resize the sword or grant limbs.

### The Expert the Sword Refuses

A celebrated sword master can balance and use a Legacy Weapon's physical blade with exceptional Skill. Method and handling compatibility are Established. The Weapon Soul refuses participation in the master's campaign of conquest, making relational-purpose compatibility Conflicted.

The master may still wield the physical vessel where ordinary custody allows, but receives no cooperative weapon-owned effects. Mastery does not become consent.

### The Loving but Untrained Heir

A Legacy Weapon genuinely trusts the child of its former bearer and accepts them as steward. Relationship and communication are Workable. The heir has never trained with a spear, so Method and Skill Compatibility is Conflicted for combat and Unknown for ceremonial coordination.

Affection creates no inherited mastery. Training may change the combat finding while the Legacy Accord remains independent.

### Rivals at the Floodgate

A hammer's Weapon Soul and an engineer despise one another's politics but agree that a collapsing floodgate must be sealed. Their broad relationship remains tense, while purpose compatibility for the repair is Workable. The engineer's Profession, a lifting frame, and the hammer's feedback make the specific operation Conditional but viable.

Success does not prove friendship, bond eligibility, or compatibility for combat.

### The Tail Harness

A reptilian monster cannot use a human sabre grip. A crafted tail harness changes leverage and secures the vessel, while months of practice produce a monster-native cutting Skill. Handling and Method Compatibility become Established for broad sweeps in open ground but remain Conflicted in narrow corridors.

The accommodation remains material, maintainable, breakable, and context-dependent. It does not humanize the monster's Skill Tree or alter the Weapon Soul's body.

### The Form That Outgrew the Technique

A bonded rapier evolves into a flexible segmented form through a valid route. The Weapon Soul's identity and bond persist, but the bearer's former thrusting Skill no longer maps cleanly to the new balance and timing.

Method Compatibility drifts from Established to Conditional. Prior mastery accelerates learning where transfer is valid, but the evolution does not update the bearer automatically.

### Several Bearers, No New Bond

A Suspended Legacy Weapon permits one steward to transport it, a historian to receive selected Echo testimony, and a monster defender to use its vessel during attacks. Each has a different scoped Compatibility Profile.

None becomes the new Bonded Soul. Compatibility with several people does not multiply standard bonds, merge permissions, or create a group mind.

### The Dynasty's Perfect Match

A royal test declares that every firstborn heir is perfectly compatible with the dynasty's blade. The test measures hand geometry, a hereditary magical channel, and performance of one ritual stance. It cannot observe the Weapon Soul's consent, private boundaries, wider purposes, or future bodies.

The result may accurately establish Conditional compatibility for the ritual technique. Calling it destiny is institutional doctrine, not world truth.

### The Silent but Workable Partnership

A shield's Weapon Soul and a deaf warrior lack a shared language and cannot access Soul Space. Communication Compatibility is initially Conflicted for tactical detail. Through pressure taps, stance cues, and visual signals, they establish a narrow but reliable channel.

The finding becomes Established for familiar defensive formations while remaining Unknown for abstract conversation. Speech was never a requirement for personhood.

### Magic After a World Reset

A Soul Weapon's form persists into an Age whose world laws no longer support the enchantment source that once projected a protective field. Bond, relationship, physical handling, and Skill remain Workable. Source Compatibility for the field is Conflicted.

The old effect does not operate by nostalgia. Research may discover a translated source, but neither Resonance nor compatibility can invent missing magic.

## Safeguards

- **No universal score:** never combine layers into one percentage, rank, affinity, destiny, or “best wielder” value.
- **No compatibility by category:** weapon type, material, species, Class, gender, morality, lineage, title, or personality archetype is not a universal match key.
- **No consent substitution:** compatibility, trust, Resonance, Skill, bond history, crisis, and institutional authority cannot consent for a person.
- **No mastery transfer:** favourable compatibility grants no Skill, Development, statistics, Class, Profession, species trait, or Practised Reliability.
- **No bond shortcut:** compatibility is necessary only where a specific intertwining claim requires it and cannot replace history, Resonance, consent, Accord, Integrity, or continuity.
- **No mandatory Soul Weapon:** ordinary weapons, tools, allies, accommodations, magic, strategy, and nonweapon paths remain viable.
- **No body bypass:** a persistent soul or bond does not provide missing anatomy, scale, strength, senses, resources, or maturation.
- **No forced evolution:** a bearer cannot require the Weapon Soul to change form for convenience; every evolution retains consent, route, transition, and tradeoffs.
- **No universal accommodation:** each support answers named constraints and retains cost, maintenance, limits, and failure.
- **No rejection damage by default:** incompatibility may cause ordinary failure or named source consequences, but generic magical backlash requires an established cause.
- **No incompatibility farming:** repeated mismatch, Dissonance, injury, refusal, tests, or failure grants no automatic compatibility, evolution, Depth, Resonance, or hidden ability.
- **No permanent typecasting:** a current finding may drift through causal change without rewriting history or guaranteeing improvement.
- **No moral judgment:** incompatibility is not impurity, betrayal, weakness, disability, or proof that either person is wrong.
- **No perfect prediction:** tests and observers remain limited by source, scope, evidence, hidden information, and changing conditions.
- **No manifestation implication:** compatibility never creates an independent Weapon Manifestation.
- **No campaign canon:** particular Profiles, findings, bodies, forms, supports, injuries, and relationships remain external Campaign Records.

## Scope Boundaries

This document owns Soul Weapon Compatibility Claims, Profiles, Layers, Findings, Supports, Drift, testing, and compatibility adjudication.

It does not define:

- the source or capability being tested;
- consent, trust, or personality;
- Soul Resonance Links or States;
- Skill acquisition or Development;
- Soul Intertwining procedure;
- Soul Weapon Evolution routes;
- the source, route, and procedure for [Weapon Manifestations](WEAPON_MANIFESTATIONS.md);
- the Vessel Topology and component procedures owned by [Unconventional Soul Weapons](UNCONVENTIONAL_SOUL_WEAPONS.md);
- live compatibility records for a campaign.
