# Weapon Evolution

## Status

This document defines how an established Soul Weapon may undergo a lasting qualitative change in its soul-bound form, vessel expression, owned capabilities, or cooperative relationship without becoming an equipment tier, Skill upgrade, Class feature, or monster Evolution.

It does not define Weapon Echoes, Legacy Weapons, detailed compatibility, Weapon Manifestations, or unconventional Soul Weapon categories.

Read [Dormant Weapon Souls](DORMANT_WEAPON_SOULS.md), [Awakening Conditions](AWAKENING_CONDITIONS.md), [Soul Intertwining](SOUL_INTERTWINING.md), and [Weapon Personalities](WEAPON_PERSONALITIES.md) first.

## Core Rule

A **Soul Weapon Evolution** is a lasting qualitative reorganisation of an established Soul Weapon's coherent form or weapon-owned capability through a valid Weapon Evolution Route.

A standard Soul Weapon Evolution requires:

1. an awakened Weapon Soul and coherent Weapon Vessel;
2. an established Soul Weapon Bond;
3. a defined Prior Weapon Form with real capabilities, limits, and continuity;
4. route provenance grounded in that weapon's history, identity, sources, and possible forms;
5. meaningful Evolutionary Pressure or purpose that the prior form cannot answer unchanged;
6. relevant shared or self-directed Development, experimentation, use, reflection, and consequence;
7. consent from every person whose identity or bond is materially altered;
8. a viable transition with time, resources, vulnerability, failure, and cost;
9. a qualitatively distinct resulting form with explicit gains, losses, dependencies, and limits;
10. enough integration and evidence for the new form to remain stable beyond one dramatic moment.

Evolution reorganises what the Soul Weapon is capable of being. It does not automatically improve every function, preserve every prior feature, scale to the Bonded Soul, or make the new form usable in the current body.

No kill count, rarity, age, sacrifice total, material value, bond duration, praise, Soul Depth, Resonance intensity, title, Skill rank, monster stage, Class rank, or number of reincarnations can replace this structure.

## Terms

### Soul Weapon Evolution

A lasting qualitative reorganisation of an established Soul Weapon's coherent soul-bound form or weapon-owned capability through a valid Weapon Evolution Route.

### Soul Weapon Form

The coherent configuration of a Soul Weapon's vessel identity, structure, affordances, source-owned capabilities, dependencies, limitations, and relationship to physical expression. It is not itself a manifestation event or inventory item.

### Prior Weapon Form

The Soul Weapon Form from which an Evolution begins. It may remain partly available, become conditional, be reorganised, close, or survive only through a separate valid route; it is not automatically preserved as a selectable loadout.

### Weapon Evolution Route

A causally supported pathway from one Prior Weapon Form to a qualitatively distinct Soul Weapon Form under defined provenance, pressures, participation, transition, costs, tradeoffs, and consequences.

### Weapon Evolution Pressure

A sustained or meaningfully recurring problem, responsibility, opportunity, incompatibility, or identity tension that makes changing the Prior Weapon Form relevant without granting the change by itself.

### Weapon Route Basis

The actual history, capabilities, relationships, materials, soul structures, sources, choices, and consequences that explain why one Weapon Evolution Route is possible for this Soul Weapon.

### Weapon Route Readiness

A qualitative assessment that one specific route has sufficient provenance, evidence, consent, current support, opportunity, and transition viability to begin. It is not a meter or guarantee of completion.

### Weapon Evolution Transition

The bounded process that reorganises the Prior Weapon Form into the resulting Soul Weapon Form. It has an onset, method, duration, requirements, vulnerability, interruption conditions, and outcome.

### Evolved Weapon Form

The coherent Soul Weapon Form after a completed Weapon Evolution Transition. It has its own capabilities, dependencies, limitations, access conditions, costs, and later possibilities.

### Weapon Evolution Cost

A meaningful loss, demand, dependency, incompatibility, risk, maintenance burden, route closure, opportunity cost, or world consequence created by access to, transition through, or continued use of an Evolved Weapon Form.

### Weapon Form Stability

The current ability of an Evolved Weapon Form to remain coherent under its vessel condition, Soul Integrity, resources, access, maintenance, bond state, environment, and source dependencies.

### Weapon Evolution Profile

A scoped campaign record of one proposed or completed Weapon Evolution, including prior form, route provenance, pressure, evidence, participants, consent, transition, result, tradeoffs, access, stability, world consequences, and uncertainty.

## Evolution Ownership

Soul Weapon rules own changes to the Weapon Soul's vessel-linked form and weapon-owned Soul effects. Adjacent systems retain their domains.

| Claim | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Learned use, coordination, technique, and reliable performance | [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) |
| Current bodily ability to hold, move, sense, channel, or survive the form | [Development System](../progression/README.md) and present embodiment |
| Biological species form, Species Traits, organs, and Monster Evolution Routes | [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) |
| Human Class, Profession, tradition, school, rank, licence, and institution | [Human Classes and Professions](../human/README.md) |
| Mana, spell sources, enchanting, rituals, divine effects, and magical law | Existing Magical Development and later Magic rules |
| Weapon Soul identity, bond, personality, form, and weapon-owned Soul effects | Soul Weapon rules |
| Current materials, repair, custody, law, witnesses, resources, and consequences | World causality and the external Campaign Record |

One transition may involve several owners. Record each effect once. A ritual may provide the transition method, a smith may provide present expertise, a rare material may change the vessel, and a Soul Weapon Route may integrate the result. None silently absorbs the others.

## What Soul Weapon Evolution Is Not

| Change | Distinction |
| --- | --- |
| **Repair** | Restores or stabilises established vessel function and continuity. A profound repair may contribute route evidence without automatically creating a new form. |
| **Reforging or alteration** | Changes material construction through craft or magic. It becomes Soul Weapon Evolution only when a valid route integrates a qualitative soul-bound form change. |
| **Equipment upgrade** | Adds, replaces, or improves an item component through an external source. The component retains its source and does not become Soul Evolution by being valuable. |
| **Enchantment** | Adds or changes a magic-owned effect. It remains an enchantment unless a later valid Evolution Route integrates it without losing provenance or cost. |
| **Skill improvement** | Improves how a person uses or coordinates with the weapon. The Soul Weapon may remain unchanged. |
| **Skill Evolution** | Reorganises a learned capability under [Skill Evolution](../skills/SKILL_EVOLUTION.md), not the Weapon Soul or vessel-linked form. |
| **Monster Evolution** | Reorganises a monster's species form and Species Potential, not the Soul Weapon. |
| **Personality change** | Changes how the Weapon Soul interprets and chooses. Personality may matter to a route without itself changing form or capability. |
| **Bond change** | Alters trust, boundaries, passage, or cooperation under Soul Intertwining. It is not an evolution rank. |
| **Weapon Manifestation** | Later rules govern whether and how a Soul Weapon appears or acts independently. Evolution does not automatically grant manifestation. |
| **Renaming** | Changes a label, legend, translation, or self-description without a qualitative form change. |
| **Temporary empowerment** | Provides a bounded effect through a spell, environment, title interaction, Avatar Expression, borrowed source, or crisis. It remains temporary unless a valid route integrates a stable result. |

## Prior Weapon Form

Every Evolution begins from the Soul Weapon that actually exists now.

Define the Prior Weapon Form through:

- Weapon Vessel identity and Vessel Continuity;
- current material and metaphysical structure;
- established shape, scale, function, and affordances;
- actual weapon-owned capabilities and their sources;
- known limits, vulnerabilities, dependencies, and maintenance;
- current bond scope and cooperation;
- Weapon Soul Personality and self-understanding;
- present access, Embodied Expression, and Practised Reliability of each participant;
- prior Evolutions and forms that were lost, reorganised, or made conditional;
- current world circumstances and evidence.

Do not begin from the weapon's public legend, ideal future, strongest former moment, or a decorative name. If the prior form is damaged or inaccessible, that condition is part of the transition problem rather than something evolution ignores.

## Route Provenance

Every Weapon Evolution Route needs a causal reason it can exist.

Possible provenance includes:

- coherent possibilities latent in the Weapon Soul's Vessel History;
- an established soul-bound capability capable of qualitative reorganisation;
- repeated interaction between the bond and a materially different embodiment;
- a valid repair, reforging, craft, ritual, magical, spiritual, or conceptual source;
- integration of a source-compatible material or effect without erasing its provenance;
- a genuine Soul Title, Resonance Link, Soul Space structure, or Archive-derived method that another system permits to contribute;
- a self-directed Weapon Soul project sustained through meaningful Development and consequence;
- a long relationship in which both participants develop a new cooperative function;
- a world change that makes a previously impossible route causally available.

Provenance creates possibility, not entitlement. A dragon scale does not grant a dragon form. A legendary forge does not create a route for every weapon. A former wielder's Skill does not become the Weapon Soul's capability. A poetic resemblance does not establish a conceptual effect.

Unknown routes may exist, but the GM must establish world truth before commitment and provide fair evidence proportionate to risk. Hidden conditions cannot be invented after the player chooses.

## Weapon Evolution Pressure

Pressure explains why the Prior Weapon Form is being challenged.

Possible pressures include:

- a recurring body-form incompatibility across one or several incarnations;
- a role the current form performs unreliably despite genuine shared practice;
- conflict between the Weapon Soul's identity and the uses its form makes easy;
- a new environment, enemy, material, magical law, or Age;
- repeated damage caused by a known structural limitation;
- a self-chosen craft or purpose the prior form cannot sustain;
- tension between concealment and public symbolic identity;
- a relationship that requires new communication or coordination;
- preserving continuity through an unavoidable alteration;
- a meaningful choice to abandon a former advantage in favor of another way of living.

Pressure does not choose the answer. The participants may respond through repair, Skills, tools, tactics, social change, refusal, different equipment, magic, Monster Evolution, or no change. Evolution is one possible response when a valid route exists.

A single crisis may expose a route or begin a transition. Intensity does not replace recurrence, integration, provenance, or stability.

## Weapon Route Basis and Readiness

Assess one route at a time.

### Identity Fit

Establish how the proposed form relates to the Weapon Soul's continuing identity.

Identity fit does not mean personality conformity. A merciful blade may choose an intimidating form, and a proud spear may choose to become less visible. The question is whether the Weapon Soul can integrate the change as itself rather than whether the outcome matches a stereotype.

### Capability Basis

Name the established capabilities, materials, structures, and relationships from which the new form can develop.

A route may extend, reorganise, specialise, combine, suppress, or replace existing features. It cannot invent an unrelated effect merely because the new name sounds appropriate.

### Evidence

Relevant evidence may include:

- meaningful use under varied conditions;
- failed and corrected attempts to answer the pressure;
- deliberate experimentation by the Weapon Soul and partner;
- current-life craft, research, instruction, or practice;
- sustained changes in cooperation and communication;
- source-compatible repair, materials, magic, or ritual;
- consequences that force an integrated choice rather than repeat a script;
- demonstrated ability to manage the Prior Weapon Form's relevant capabilities;
- fair evidence of hidden route conditions.

Evidence explains causality. It is not evolution currency.

### Current Support

Identify what the transition needs now:

- a coherent vessel and sufficient Weapon Form Stability;
- Soul Integrity and manageable Strain;
- current access to the relevant bond, source, material, environment, or site;
- participants with the necessary Skills or Development;
- time, protection, recovery, and maintenance;
- consent and authority for any affected person or resource;
- a physical, magical, spiritual, or Soul route that can actually perform the change.

Persistent potential cannot replace missing present support.

### Tradeoff Visibility

Before irreversible commitment, communicate known likely:

- gains and new affordances;
- losses, exclusions, and prior features at risk;
- dependencies and maintenance;
- body and Skill recalibration;
- bond or personality tensions;
- transition vulnerability and failure;
- social, legal, ecological, and institutional consequences;
- uncertainty that remains genuinely unknown.

Fair uncertainty does not require a complete menu. It does require enough causal evidence for meaningful agency.

## Consent and Bodily Autonomy

The Weapon Vessel is the Weapon Soul's embodied continuity. Voluntary form change therefore requires the Weapon Soul's informed consent.

The Bonded Soul's consent is also required when the proposed route would:

- rewrite the Soul Weapon Bond or Passage Accord;
- draw on the Bonded Soul's Soul Imprints, body, Development, Titles, Echoes, or other owned structures;
- impose a new persistent channel, obligation, risk, or access condition on the Bonded Soul;
- alter a shared capability that exists only through mutual participation;
- expose the Bonded Soul to material transition harm.

The Bonded Soul does not receive a general ownership veto over a self-directed vessel change that affects only the Weapon Soul's body and capabilities. The Weapon Soul likewise cannot unilaterally rewrite the partner's identity or bond obligations.

A disagreement may delay a shared route, produce a self-directed alternative, change the bond, or lead to Suspension or Severance. The GM does not resolve it by declaring one participant the true owner.

### Involuntary Change

A curse, hostile ritual, divine act, environmental catastrophe, forced reforging, or other external source may alter a Weapon Vessel without consent. Resolve the source, resistance, injury, continuity, and consequences first.

An imposed alteration is not automatically Soul Weapon Evolution. It becomes part of an Evolved Weapon Form only if a valid route produces stable integration rather than temporary transformation, unresolved damage, possession, enchantment, or coercive control. Calling harm an evolution does not make it beneficial or earned.

## Weapon Evolution Routes

Routes are descriptive causal possibilities rather than an upgrade shop.

### Route Definition

Every route records:

1. **origin:** the Prior Weapon Form or forms that can enter;
2. **provenance:** why this form change exists;
3. **pressure or purpose:** what recurring problem or chosen future makes it relevant;
4. **participants:** whose action, expertise, resources, identity, or consent matters;
5. **conditions:** evidence, compatibility, sources, opportunity, and current support;
6. **transition:** onset, process, duration, vulnerability, interruption, and completion;
7. **result:** coherent form, capabilities, affordances, and current access;
8. **costs:** losses, dependencies, maintenance, route closure, and failure states;
9. **consequences:** relationship, social, legal, historical, material, and world effects;
10. **knowledge:** what is known, hidden, disputed, or misinterpreted;
11. **ownership:** where every adjacent capability remains defined.

### Branching

A Prior Weapon Form may support several routes. Branches can reflect:

- different responses to the same pressure;
- distinct materials or transition sources;
- the Weapon Soul's choices and values;
- different Bonded Soul embodiments or cooperative methods;
- specialisation versus broader adaptability;
- preservation versus abandonment of a prior feature;
- competing cultural, spiritual, or technical traditions;
- a hidden route established by unusual but valid history.

Routes do not need to be equally powerful, equally known, or simultaneously available. They do need meaningful distinctions and viable reasons to choose among them.

### Branch Commitment

Entering or completing one route may:

- consume or replace structures required by another;
- change Vessel Continuity assumptions;
- commit a scarce material or one-time relationship opportunity;
- reorganise a capability that cannot coexist with its former form;
- establish maintenance incompatible with another route;
- change identity or bond boundaries enough to close an alternative;
- leave another route theoretically possible but materially harder.

Do not hide irreversible branch closure when the characters have a fair way to know it.

### Convergence and Divergence

Different weapons may develop similar functions through distinct routes while retaining different provenance, expression, limits, and personality. Similar appearance does not make them the same form.

One shared origin may diverge according to history and choice. The classic chains:

> Iron Sword -> Knight Blade -> Hero Sword -> Living Blade -> Astral Edge -> Conceptual Blade

and:

> Iron Sword -> Executioner's Edge -> Blood Fang -> Tyrant's Judgment

are illustrative route stories, not universal stages, rarity tiers, morality ladders, guaranteed sequences, or proof that later names are stronger. Each arrow would require its own complete route, and some labels may be only public names unless a qualitative change is established.

### Reversal and Reorientation

An Evolved Weapon Form does not freely toggle back to every Prior Weapon Form.

Reversal, recovery, simplification, or reorientation requires its own valid process when it materially changes form. A route may deliberately abandon costly complexity, narrow a function, restore an earlier architecture under new constraints, or translate to a less powerful but more sustainable form.

Loss of scale or spectacle can be valid Evolution when it creates a coherent new way of being. It is not automatically regression or failure.

## Evolution Patterns

Patterns describe how a form changed. They may overlap and are not stages.

### Refinement

The form reorganises for finer control, feedback, precision, repairability, or stability. Better degree alone is ordinary improvement; Evolution requires a qualitative change in structure or operation.

### Specialisation

The form becomes unusually suited to a bounded role, material, environment, opponent, method, or relationship while accepting narrower transfer or new vulnerabilities.

### Broadening

The form integrates several genuinely practised contexts into a more adaptable configuration. It cannot claim contexts that were never developed, and breadth may reduce peak performance or increase maintenance.

### Form Translation

The Soul Weapon reorganises around a materially different Bonded Soul embodiment, scale, sense, movement, or environment. Translation does not grant anatomy or a matching Skill and need not reproduce the prior weapon shape literally.

### Cooperative Reorganisation

The weapon-owned form changes around a genuinely developed method of partnership, communication, timing, or shared responsibility. The Soul Weapon owns its contribution; the Bonded Soul's learned coordination remains a Skill.

### Source Integration

A material, enchantment, spirit relationship, ritual, environment, or other established source becomes structurally integrated into the form. The source retains provenance, dependencies, permissions, and counters rather than becoming generic Soul power.

### Protective or Constructive Reorientation

A form previously centered on harm may reorganise around protection, repair, craft, medicine, navigation, communication, or another purpose, or the reverse. Purpose does not determine morality, and noncombat routes are not lesser Evolutions.

### Compression

Several established form operations become one coherent weapon-owned function. Compression may reduce transition delay or coordination burden while making the form less modular, harder to repair, or more vulnerable to one failure.

### Stable Multiplicity

A route may support a small set of explicitly defined configurations when the transition mechanism, identity, costs, and access rules make that multiplicity part of one coherent form. It is not unrestricted shapeshifting, a menu of every Prior Form, or Weapon Manifestation.

### Concept-Bearing Reorganisation

A rare route may organise a form around a bounded conceptual relationship supported by valid Soul, Skill, magical, symbolic, or world-law sources. The route must define exactly what relationship is expressed, what it cannot affect, what access and interpretation are required, and what costs or counters remain.

The name **Conceptual Blade** does not grant the ability to cut fate, distance, souls, laws, immortality, or anything else by metaphor. Each effect needs explicit ownership and a specific completed rule.

## Weapon Evolution Transition

Every transition occurs through the world rather than through an automatic menu.

### Onset

State what begins the transition:

- voluntary entry into a forge, ritual, repair, meditation, migration, or shared practice;
- an established threshold reached through integrated work;
- a world event that opens a temporary opportunity;
- a self-directed Weapon Soul act through an existing capability;
- Final Death or Reincarnation only where another completed rule explicitly permits that timing.

Reincarnation itself does not provide a free evolution window.

### Process

Define:

- where the Weapon Soul and vessel are;
- who acts and through which capability;
- which materials, sources, relationships, permissions, and environments are required;
- how long meaningful external time passes;
- what becomes vulnerable or unavailable during the change;
- which decisions remain open;
- what can interrupt, redirect, or fail;
- how participants can recognize progress without a universal meter.

Soul Space may support reflection, consent, or an interface. It is not a free forge, material source, stopped-time workshop, or substitute for world causality.

### Completion

A transition completes when:

- identity and Vessel Continuity remain coherent;
- the qualitative change is physically or metaphysically established through its source;
- gains, losses, dependencies, and boundaries are real;
- the Evolved Weapon Form can persist beyond the Trigger;
- immediate access is assessed separately;
- consequences enter the world.

One spectacular use can demonstrate completion if the transition was already integrated. It cannot create all missing integration retroactively.

### Interruption and Failure

Failure may produce:

- no lasting change;
- a damaged but still coherent Prior Weapon Form;
- unstable partial alteration requiring repair or renewed work;
- source-owned enchantment, mutation, curse, or transformation without Evolution;
- changed route evidence or a closed opportunity;
- Soul Strain, vessel damage, relationship conflict, lost material, or public consequence;
- a different valid form only when that outcome had prior provenance and fair evidence.

Failure does not grant evolution credit, a guaranteed later success, or a consolation ability. It should preserve actual learning and consequences without manufacturing power.

## The Evolved Weapon Form

Record the result as a coherent replacement or reorganisation, not a stack of bonuses.

### Form Identity

Name what makes the form qualitatively distinct:

- structure, scale, balance, material organization, or mode of use;
- source-owned capability or affordance;
- relationship between vessel and Weapon Soul;
- role, purpose, or environment for which it is organised;
- dependencies, vulnerabilities, and failure behavior;
- relationship to Prior Weapon Forms and later routes.

Names are optional. A dramatic name does not substitute for this definition.

### Capabilities and Affordances

Every new capability states:

- source and provenance;
- actor and owner;
- Target or affected domain;
- current access conditions;
- bodily and Skill requirements for use;
- resource, attention, time, consent, or maintenance cost;
- scope, limits, counters, and failure states;
- what changed from the prior form;
- what remains unavailable.

The Weapon Soul may own a capability while the Bonded Soul cannot currently invoke or exploit it. Cooperation never transfers ownership.

### Losses and Tradeoffs

Possible costs include:

- greater mass, fragility, visibility, or maintenance;
- dependence on a material, environment, magical source, relationship, or ritual;
- loss of a Prior Form or broad utility;
- reduced concealment, reach, speed, precision, or repairability;
- increased Soul Strain or sensitivity to Dissonance;
- new social, legal, religious, or ecological attention;
- narrower compatibility with bodies, Skills, or purposes;
- a closed route or difficult reversal;
- obligations to a source, craft tradition, community, or consenting participant.

An Evolution Cost must matter in the contexts where the form matters. Decorative disadvantages do not balance unrestricted capability.

### Access and Reliability

Completion does not guarantee mastery.

Assess separately:

- whether the Weapon Soul understands and can control its new form;
- whether the Bonded Soul can perceive, hold, move, channel, or cooperate with it;
- whether relevant Skills have been adapted;
- whether the form can physically manifest under current rules;
- whether resources and maintenance are available;
- whether the bond currently permits the intended use;
- how reliably the form operates under stress and variation.

An evolved form may begin powerful in potential but clumsy, inaccessible, dangerous, or narrow in current use.

## Persistence Across Reincarnation

A completed Soul Weapon Evolution persists with the Weapon Soul and established bond as part of Soul Weapon continuity. It does not reset to a base weapon merely because the Bonded Soul receives a new body.

Each new incarnation separately determines:

- recognition and memory access;
- whether the Soul Weapon can currently appear under later Manifestation rules;
- physical, sensory, magical, cultural, and legal compatibility;
- usable Skills and Practised Reliability;
- resources, maintenance, and source availability;
- cooperation and Bond Boundaries;
- whether a Form Translation route becomes possible or desirable.

An unsuitable body does not automatically resize, simplify, or re-evolve the weapon. A valid translation route may address the incompatibility through present-life work and real tradeoffs.

### No Reincarnation Snowball

Persistent Evolution does not mean additive accumulation.

- each new form reorganises capabilities rather than adding a permanent upgrade layer;
- features not retained by the result are unavailable unless another valid route restores them;
- old materials, forms, users, victories, and Titles do not become free passive effects;
- one life cannot repeatedly evolve through trivial branches merely to collect options;
- inaccessible forms do not supply background statistics;
- maintaining several valid configurations carries explicit scope, transition, and maintenance limits;
- current-life Skill and embodiment remain necessary after every Reincarnation.

There is no universal evolution cap, but there is also no infinite ladder. Every later route needs new provenance, unresolved pressure, meaningful development, a viable transition, real tradeoffs, and coherent identity. A stable form may have no currently valid successor.

## Repair, Materials, Craft, and Magic

### Repair

Repair preserves or restores Vessel Continuity. It may reveal limitations, deepen relationships, and contribute evidence. Repair is not lesser than Evolution and should not be withheld to force an upgrade.

### Reforging and Craft

A smith, artificer, monster craft tradition, living workshop, or other valid practitioner may perform physical work required by a route. Their expertise remains their capability. The Weapon Soul's consent remains necessary, and the result depends on materials, access, method, and consequences.

The crafter does not own the Evolved Weapon Form or inherit the bond by contribution.

### Materials

Rare or meaningful materials can supply structure, properties, dependencies, symbolism, and world consequences. They do not grant a themed Evolution automatically.

Integrating material must address:

- compatibility with the vessel and Weapon Soul;
- provenance and lawful or ethical acquisition;
- what physical or magical property the material actually has;
- how the transition incorporates it;
- repair and replacement consequences;
- whether Vessel Continuity remains coherent;
- which effect belongs to material, craft, magic, or Soul Weapon Evolution.

### Magic and Ritual

Magic may reveal, enable, stabilize, alter, or threaten a route through a completed source. Ritual may coordinate participants and conditions. Neither can waive personhood, consent, route provenance, cost, or ownership.

Until complete Magic rules exist, any campaign-specific magical transition method remains Provisional under the [Alpha Playtest Rules](../gm/ALPHA_PLAYTEST_RULES.md).

## System Interactions

### Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may reveal route relevance, align participants, support a transition, or expose Dissonance. It does not function as evolution XP, guarantee a branch, or make an unsupported effect possible.

### Soul Depth and Soul Integrity

Soul Depth can support integration of complex forms and histories. Soul Integrity and Weapon Form Stability constrain current transition safety. Neither is a weapon level, evolution currency, route count, or permission to ignore consent and embodiment.

### Soul Echoes and Weapon Personalities

A [Soul Echo](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may remember prior use and offer bounded testimony. It cannot evolve the weapon, provide current consent, or perform another participant's Development. [Weapon Personality](WEAPON_PERSONALITIES.md) shapes choice and interpretation without granting the route or result.

### Soul Titles

A [Soul Title](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may create symbolic pressure, recognition, or a genuine route relationship where its Basis is directly relevant. It does not upgrade the Soul Weapon, transfer authority, or make a dramatic name factual.

### Soul Space, Constellations, and Soul Avatars

[Soul Space](../soul/SOUL_SPACE.md) may provide a bounded interface for planning, consent, and integration but not materials or stopped time. [Soul Constellations](../soul/SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md) may bring relevant relationships without forcing participation. A [Soul Avatar](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) may coordinate willing contributors without manufacturing a route, extra actions, or automatic completion.

### Reincarnation and Retained Instincts

Reincarnation preserves a completed Evolved Weapon Form through the established bond while resetting the Bonded Soul's body and current access. [Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) may support attention relevant to prior coordination but do not grant the weapon form, anatomy, or adapted Skill.

### Skill Trees and Adaptive Skills

The [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) owns learned use. A form change may invalidate assumptions, open a new Receiving Route, or create pressure for [Adaptive Skills](../skills/ADAPTIVE_SKILLS.md). The Bonded Soul and Weapon Soul must still develop, calibrate, and maintain any learned method through current practice.

Skill Evolution cannot be relabelled as Soul Weapon Evolution, and Soul Weapon Evolution cannot install a finished Skill.

### Human Classes and Professions

Classes, Professions, Martial Traditions, Magical Schools, and Institutions may preserve route knowledge, teach coordination, perform craft, recognize an Evolved Weapon Form, or regulate access. They cannot award an Evolution by rank, rite, licence, ownership, or curriculum completion.

### Monster Evolution

Monster Evolution and Soul Weapon Evolution may respond to the same environment or relationship, but neither synchronizes the other. A monster may evolve a new body while the weapon remains unchanged, and a weapon may evolve while the current body remains incompatible.

When both change, resolve each transition separately, then develop present coordination. No paired form, organ, Monster Skill, or Evolution Route is granted by thematic resemblance.

### Akashic Archive and World Engine

The [Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) may preserve route evidence or methods without granting Current Access, consent, resources, or completion. The World Engine owns materials, sites, experts, institutions, law, ecology, witnesses, scarcity, conflict, and consequences.

## Adjudication Procedure

When a Soul Weapon Evolution is proposed:

1. **Define the Prior Weapon Form.** Record actual continuity, structure, capabilities, limits, access, damage, and dependencies.
2. **State the exact change.** Distinguish form, capability, Skill, magic, body, personality, bond, and manifestation claims.
3. **Identify route provenance.** Explain why this weapon can possibly undergo this particular change.
4. **Name Weapon Evolution Pressure or purpose.** Establish the recurring problem, responsibility, opportunity, incompatibility, or chosen future.
5. **Build the Weapon Route Basis.** Trace meaningful use, experimentation, source, relationship, Development, failure, correction, and consequence.
6. **Assign ownership.** Preserve material, craft, magic, Skill, monster, class, Soul, and world effects under their systems.
7. **Test identity and consent.** Obtain Weapon Soul consent and every additional consent required by shared alteration.
8. **Assess Weapon Route Readiness.** Check current Integrity, vessel, support, resources, access, opportunity, and fair knowledge.
9. **Define the transition.** State onset, process, duration, vulnerability, interruption, failure, and completion.
10. **Define the Evolved Weapon Form.** Record qualitative distinction, capabilities, losses, dependencies, maintenance, stability, and later routes.
11. **Resolve current access.** Apply manifestation, embodiment, Skills, cooperation, resources, and Practised Reliability separately.
12. **Apply world consequences.** Record observers, institutions, law, scarcity, reputation, repair, and history externally.
13. **Audit accumulation.** Confirm that no kills, levels, materials, names, sacrifice, suffering, reincarnations, or prior forms became free stacked power.

## Worked Examples

### Bastion Blade

An ordinary sword awakened and intertwined through years of bodyguard work. Its narrow guard and forward balance repeatedly make interposition difficult, while both partners develop a method centered on controlling approach lanes rather than pursuing strikes.

A valid route uses deliberate reforging, current craft expertise, shared practice, the Weapon Soul's consent, and repeated evidence. The Evolved Weapon Form becomes broader near the guard, changes balance, and supports clearer source-bounded pressure feedback when an agreed protected Target crosses its line.

The form is better for interception, heavier in recovery, harder to conceal, and awkward for prior dueling methods. The feedback is weapon-owned, not danger omniscience. The Bonded Soul must adapt relevant Skills, and the form grants no durability statistic or automatic block.

### Duel-Line Edge

Another sword of similar origin develops through precise formal duels, extensive failure analysis, and a Weapon Soul that values explicit commitments. Its route narrows the blade, increases sensitivity to edge alignment through an established Resonance channel, and sacrifices resilience against lateral force.

This branch is not morally superior or universally stronger than Bastion Blade. The two forms diverge because their histories, purposes, tradeoffs, and supporting practice differ.

### The Carpenter's Plane

An awakened plane and Bonded Soul spend decades restoring structures made from incompatible woods after a World Reset. Replacing blades and using ordinary attachments solves part of the problem but creates repeated calibration failures.

Through current craft, source-compatible materials, and meaningful cooperative experimentation, the Soul Weapon evolves a stable interface for a small set of deliberately prepared cutting inserts. Stable Multiplicity belongs to the defined form; it is not unrestricted shapeshifting or free material creation. Each insert still needs making, maintenance, transport, and learned use.

### Translation for a Climbing Monster

A spear persists into a Bonded Soul's life as a climbing monster. The weapon does not automatically shrink or become claws. For years the pair develops harness tools, tension-based communication, and a Skill using anchored thrusts while the monster's society provides a valid living-fiber craft.

A Form Translation route eventually reorganises the spear into an articulated climbing lance that can be anchored and drawn along the body. The Weapon Soul consents to a major change in Vessel Perspective. The route preserves reach while sacrificing quick freehand redirection. It grants no limbs, no Monster Skill, and no automatic competence.

### The Dragon Scale Upgrade Fails

A ruler orders a dragon scale hammered into an intertwined sword because legends claim it will become a Dragon Blade. The scale has heat resistance, but the smith lacks a compatible method, the Weapon Soul refuses the alteration, and no Weapon Route Basis connects dragon biology to the Soul Weapon's form.

The attempted work may damage the vessel or create a removable material layer. It does not produce Soul Weapon Evolution, dragon powers, or improved future odds. Rarity and theme are not provenance.

### The Conceptual Name

After killing a tyrant, a blade receives the public name **Crown-Cutter** and its Bonded Soul gains a relevant Soul Title. The player proposes an evolution that can cut all authority and royal magic.

The name and Title create public reaction and may provide symbolic route evidence in a specific future context. They do not define a Target, source, mechanism, limit, counter, transition, or cost. No Concept-Bearing Reorganisation exists yet. A later route would need complete provenance and a bounded claim rather than metaphor made literal.

### Choosing a Narrower Future

A massive war hammer can break fortified gates but requires scarce magical repair and repeatedly leaves its Weapon Soul unable to communicate after impact. The Weapon Soul chooses a route that reduces mass and abandons the gate-breaking structure in favor of stability, repairability, and sustained communication.

The resulting form has lower siege effectiveness but is a valid Evolution because it creates a coherent new way of living with real gains and losses. Evolution is not a one-direction power ladder.

### The Partner Disagrees

An intertwined knife wants a self-directed vessel change that would make it safer to handle and visibly mark it as a person. The Bonded Soul objects because concealment has protected them both.

The change does not rewrite the bond or draw on the Bonded Soul's structures, so the Bonded Soul has no ownership veto. The Weapon Soul still needs a valid route, craft, resources, and transition. If it proceeds, the relationship and world consequences may be severe. Agency does not guarantee convenience.

### Curse Is Not Evolution

A hostile god causes a spear to grow barbs that drink blood and compels painful use. The effect has an external source, duration, resistance, and injury. It is a curse, not a Soul Weapon Evolution merely because the shape changed.

If the Weapon Soul later chooses to integrate, remove, or transform part of that history, a new route must establish consent, provenance, present work, tradeoffs, and stable identity. Suffering supplies no automatic upgrade.

### Evolution Without Immediate Use

A Soul Weapon completes an Evolved Weapon Form during a long repair project. Its Bonded Soul currently inhabits an aquatic body unable to hold the resulting physical configuration, and Manifestation rules provide no present external access.

The Evolution can still be complete for the Weapon Soul while Embodied Expression remains unavailable to the partner. It supplies no passive statistics. Future access requires compatible embodiment, tools, Skills, another valid translation, or changed circumstances.

### A Stalled Transition

A bow begins a route requiring a rare resonant wood and a season of controlled tension. War interrupts the work, the source material dries incorrectly, and the bow must choose between preserving the stable Prior Weapon Form or risking damage.

Choosing to stop preserves personhood and meaningful learning. The bow gains no partial evolution ability or guaranteed success. The Weapon Route Basis may remain stronger if future conditions genuinely support another attempt.

## Safeguards

- **No rarity ladder:** materials, fame, age, maker, location, and names do not establish stages or universal quality tiers.
- **No automatic awakening package:** awakening and intertwining grant no Evolution or preselected route.
- **No kill or use counter:** repetition matters only through integrated evidence, changed pressure, learning, choice, and consequence.
- **No sacrifice currency:** pain, damage, death, blood, consumed items, and lost allies do not buy evolution.
- **No desire unlock:** personality, vows, moral certainty, dramatic speeches, and mutual affection cannot create unsupported forms.
- **No owner command:** legal ownership, creator intent, Class rank, institution, god, or prophecy cannot compel voluntary Evolution.
- **No bodily violation by bond:** shared routes require consent from every person whose identity or persistent structures are altered.
- **No universal stronger form:** every Evolved Weapon Form has scope, tradeoffs, dependencies, counters, and contexts where another option remains preferable.
- **No infinite stacking:** new forms reorganise capabilities; every retained prior feature must be explicit, compatible, and paid for.
- **No free loadouts:** Prior Weapon Forms do not become instantly selectable configurations without a dedicated Stable Multiplicity route and real transition limits.
- **No unrestricted shapeshifting:** Form Translation and Stable Multiplicity define bounded configurations and do not grant Manifestation.
- **No Skill transfer:** the Bonded Soul and Weapon Soul need current learning, adaptation, and reliability for every method they perform.
- **No Class replacement:** Evolution does not grant a martial tradition, Profession, magical school, rank, office, or institution.
- **No monster-tree merger:** weapon and monster Evolutions remain separate even when they respond to the same history.
- **No magic laundering:** enchantments, rituals, materials, spirits, divine effects, and curses retain source, permissions, dependencies, and counters.
- **No concept by metaphor:** conceptual names and symbolism cannot produce unlimited effects or bypass specific ownership.
- **No free reincarnation scaling:** persistence does not resize, translate, strengthen, or make the form accessible in each new body.
- **No failure reward:** interrupted transitions preserve real learning and consequences without fractional powers or guaranteed later success.
- **No convenient soul harm:** imposed alteration, identity rewriting, or destructive transition requires explicit cause, warning, resistance, and consequence.
- **No campaign canon:** particular routes, forms, materials, transitions, profiles, and outcomes remain external campaign state.

## Scope Boundaries

This document defines Soul Weapon Evolution for standard established Soul Weapon Bonds. [Legacy Weapons](LEGACY_WEAPONS.md) defines the bounded self-directed exception for an unbound or Suspended Legacy Weapon without changing this standard procedure. [Weapon Echoes](WEAPON_ECHOES.md) defines prior-experience memory. Detailed compatibility, physical manifestation, and living, distributed, composite, body-grown, conceptual-only, and other unconventional vessels remain with later Phase 6 tasks.
