# Authority Governance

## Governed Changes

G01: Narration/session/state/history/play evidence may propose higher review, never enact it. Change a higher layer only through its own authority process.

G02: Available repository updates do not automatically change active profiles, prior valid outcomes, records, causality, capabilities, Provisional Rules without review or retcon permission. Compare versions; decide adoption/conversion; migrate affected records; preserve historical interpretation; validate through Migration and Versioning. Routine profile constraints remain in E03.

G03: Retcon means deliberate Campaign-Canon-authorized correction/replacement, not discovery of false belief. Record grantor, exact prior claim, replacement, reason/effective scope, affected history/state, knowledge/relationship/progression/consequence dependencies, and discussion/remedy for material player choices. Preserve traceably superseded old records, never silently delete.
