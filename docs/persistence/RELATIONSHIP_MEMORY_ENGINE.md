# Relationship Memory Engine

## Purpose

This document defines how Eternal Cycle preserves and updates relationships across interactions, sessions, separation, death, Reincarnation, Time Skips, Age changes, and migration.

Relationships are evolving causal histories among identifiable participants. They are not one approval score, a permanent personality script, or a reward track.

## Core Rule

A material relationship never silently resets.

Every relationship change requires stable participant identities, an established Relationship Event or ongoing process, participant-specific information and interpretation, affected Relationship Dimensions, a causal update, preserved prior history, and current unresolved issues and dependencies.

A known character is never treated as a stranger without an in-world explanation supported by identity, recognition, memory, information, or relationship rules.

## What a Relationship Is

A relationship is the persistent structure produced by interactions, identities, expectations, commitments, institutions, kinship, communication, shared circumstances, and each participant's interpretation.

It may include:

- acquaintance and recognition;
- friendship, affection, respect, or rivalry;
- trust and distrust;
- hostility, fear, or grievance;
- family and kinship;
- promises and obligations;
- debts, gifts, custody, and exchange;
- authority, representation, employment, patronage, or membership;
- communication channels and secrecy;
- shared discoveries and projects;
- practical or emotional dependencies;
- betrayal, repair, forgiveness, estrangement, or grief;
- cultural, legal, religious, magical, or Soul-related interpretation.

No one dimension defines the whole relationship.

## What a Relationship Is Not

A Relationship Record is not:

- a universal friendship meter;
- a charisma difficulty;
- proof of consent;
- control over another person's choices;
- a prediction of loyalty;
- a transfer of Knowledge;
- proof that each participant interprets events identically;
- a substitute for faction, institution, family, legal, or Soul-system rules;
- automatic recognition across forms or lives;
- immunity from change, conflict, or consequence.

## Relationship Identity

Every material relationship has a stable **Relationship ID** distinct from its participants.

The record identifies:

- participant Record Identities;
- the capacity in which each participates;
- whether the relationship is pairwise, group, organizational, familial, mediated, or networked;
- start and closure status;
- relevant Incarnation, office, faction Version, or body scope;
- continuity claims across changed identities or roles;
- duplicate and merge history.

The same two people may have several overlapping relationships. A ruler may be another person's sibling, creditor, commander, and research collaborator. These aspects may share one coordinated record or several linked records, but their rights and expectations do not collapse.

## Relationship Record

A **Relationship Record** contains the following logical sections.

### Identity

- Relationship ID;
- participant IDs and aliases;
- relationship form or forms;
- inception source;
- active, changed, closed, disputed, or unknown status;
- relevant persistence and visibility classifications.

### First Meeting

- event and Timeline reference;
- each participant's identity and embodiment at that time;
- location and circumstances;
- what each participant perceived;
- first commitments, impressions, errors, or consequences;
- uncertainty or Record Gaps.

The first meeting is not rewritten by later opinion. A mistaken identity may be corrected while preserving what participants believed then.

### Latest Meeting

- most recent established direct or mediated interaction;
- time, location, and channel;
- participant identities and forms;
- material outcomes;
- current open follow-up;
- whether newer indirect communication exists.

Latest meeting differs from latest Relationship Event. A letter, betrayal discovered in absence, family death, faction order, or shared consequence may update the relationship without a meeting.

### Important Encounters

The record references Relationship Events significant enough to explain current dimensions, commitments, recognition, or unresolved issues.

Importance follows causal effect, not spectacle. A quiet promise may matter more than a shared battle.

### Relationship Dimensions

Each dimension records direction, evidence, current qualitative state, trajectory, uncertainty, and review conditions.

Common dimensions include:

- familiarity;
- recognition;
- trust;
- hostility;
- fear;
- affection;
- respect;
- resentment;
- obligation;
- dependence;
- authority or deference;
- communication quality;
- reliability expectations;
- vulnerability and boundaries;
- shared purpose;
- ideological alignment or conflict.

Not every relationship needs every dimension.

### Commitments and Exchanges

- promises;
- oaths and contracts;
- debts;
- gifts;
- favours;
- custody and entrusted items;
- duties;
- conditions, deadlines, witnesses, and status;
- interpretations and disputes.

### Kinship and Organizations

- biological, adoptive, social, legal, ritual, chosen, or disputed family ties;
- memberships, offices, employment, patronage, representation, alliance, subjecthood, or other organizational relations;
- relevant institutions or faction Versions;
- rights, duties, audiences, and jurisdiction;
- cultural interpretation and uncertainty.

### Shared Knowledge and Work

- shared discoveries;
- jointly witnessed events;
- research collaboration;
- Projects;
- secrets entrusted;
- techniques or records communicated;
- disagreements about interpretation;
- information no longer mutually held.

### Dependencies

- material support;
- access, transport, medicine, shelter, training, translation, or protection;
- emotional or social reliance where established;
- institutional dependency;
- asymmetric alternatives and risks;
- conditions under which dependency changes.

Dependency creates pressure and consequence, not automatic obedience or ownership.

### Unresolved Issues

- unkept or disputed promises;
- unanswered questions;
- concealed facts;
- unresolved grief or injury;
- boundaries requiring negotiation;
- suspected betrayal;
- uncollected debt;
- conflicting duties;
- missing communication;
- identity uncertainty;
- planned conversations or Review Points.

## Direction and Perspective

Relationship facts and participant perspectives remain separate.

For participants A and B, the record may preserve shared objective facts, A's Knowledge and interpretation, B's Knowledge and interpretation, what each believes the other knows, GM-only hidden facts, disputed meaning, and audience or institutional interpretations.

A may trust B while B distrusts A. Both may believe the trust is mutual. A third party may falsely believe they are enemies. These claims coexist in different [Truth Layers](TRUTH_LAYERS.md).

The GM does not average perspectives into one canonical relationship mood.

## Trust

Trust is target- and function-specific willingness to rely on another participant under uncertainty.

A person may trust another to keep a secret but not repay money, in battle but not politics, as a researcher but not a guardian, or under ordinary pressure but not when family is threatened.

Trust records direction, trusted scope, evidence, current confidence, accepted vulnerabilities, breaches, repairs, Trust Trajectory, and change conditions.

## Trust Trajectory

A **Trust Trajectory** describes supported direction over a stated interval without becoming a numerical prediction.

Useful findings include:

- `Forming`;
- `Strengthening`;
- `Stable`;
- `Conditional`;
- `Strained`;
- `Declining`;
- `Broken`;
- `Repairing`;
- `Volatile`;
- `Unknown`.

Trajectory follows evidence and events. It does not guarantee the next choice or imply every trust scope changes together.

## Hostility

Hostility records adverse intent, disposition, grievance, threat perception, or willingness to oppose within a stated scope.

It distinguishes private anger from planned action, fear from hatred, institutional opposition from personal hostility, strategic rivalry from intent to kill, temporary conflict from enduring grievance, and perceived hostility from established intent.

Hostility requires a source and does not become arbitrary aggression. It may coexist with respect, affection, debt, family, or cooperation.

## Communication

Communication records channels, languages, access, frequency, reliability, security, interruption, misunderstandings, intermediaries, and last confirmed exchange.

Silence may result from refusal, distance, delay, censorship, injury, death, lost routes, competing duty, fear, or absence of opportunity. Participants react according to what they can know.

Communication changes Character Knowledge through valid routes. Relationship records reference those changes and do not pool information automatically.

## Promises

A **Promise Record** identifies:

- promisor;
- recipient or beneficiary;
- exact commitment;
- conditions and limits;
- time or trigger;
- witnesses and evidence;
- participant interpretations;
- authority and capacity;
- current status;
- attempts, fulfilment, breach, release, renegotiation, impossibility, or dispute;
- consequences and unresolved issues.

A promise creates an expectation and possible obligation, not guaranteed performance.

## Betrayal

Betrayal is a participant or audience interpretation of a material breach of trust, loyalty, confidence, expectation, or obligation.

The record separates the factual action or omission, the expected commitment, whether it was communicated, actor intent and Knowledge, participant interpretations, witnesses, consequences, denial, justification, remorse, repair, and escalation.

One participant may experience betrayal while another denies that any promise existed. The emotional and social consequence can be real while the obligation remains disputed.

## Debts and Gifts

A debt records source, parties, object or obligation, valuation where relevant, terms, transferability, repayment, dispute, enforcement, forgiveness, and cultural context.

A gift records giver, recipient, object or act, custody and ownership transfer, stated meaning, acceptance, conditions if any, witnesses, later use, return, loss, and participant interpretation.

A gift does not create an unstated debt. A debt does not grant control. Inventory, economy, law, culture, and relationship owners retain their claims.

## Family

Family relationships identify biological, adoptive, chosen, legal, ritual, marital, lineage, reincarnational, disputed, or mistaken bases.

Family may create rights, duties, recognition, inheritance, stigma, affection, conflict, or no particular emotional bond according to culture and history.

Family does not force love, trust, obedience, forgiveness, ownership, memory, or recognition across Reincarnation.

A [Soul-Bound Companion Bond](../soul/SOUL_BOUND_COMPANIONS.md) may guarantee that two Souls eventually meet again, but it does not restore any Relationship dimension or compel continued contact. Relationships owns what the current incarnations become to one another after reunion.

## Organizations

Relationships with factions and institutions distinguish member, office holder, employee, representative, patron, client, ally, dependent, subject, audience, creditor, debtor, coerced participant, and former participant.

No organizational relationship grants full Knowledge, consent, loyalty, authority, ownership, or representation automatically. Current faction Version and institution records own collective context.

## Shared Discoveries

A shared discovery records participants, the event or evidence, what each perceived, interpretation differences, secrecy or publication agreements, resulting Knowledge, credit or access consequences, and later confirmation, dispute, or loss.

Presence does not guarantee identical understanding. A discovery may deepen trust, create rivalry, expose deception, or produce no relationship change without causal support.

## Relationship Events

A **Relationship Event** materially changes, confirms, tests, or reveals at least one dimension, commitment, recognition state, dependency, or unresolved issue.

Examples include first contact, meaningful assistance or refusal, a promise, trusted disclosure, betrayal, shared danger, repeated reliable work, meaningful absence, family change, death, Reincarnation, institutional decision, repair attempt, boundary violation, or discovery of deception.

Killing, gifts, praise, time, proximity, and repeated dialogue do not guarantee relationship change by themselves. Meaning depends on participants, expectations, interpretation, and consequence.

## Update Procedure

After a material Relationship Event:

1. **Resolve identities.** Confirm participant, Incarnation, office, faction Version, disguise, and alias references.
2. **Load prior state.** Read the Relationship Record, important encounters, unresolved issues, each participant's Knowledge, and current context.
3. **Record the event.** Append Historical and Timeline references before changing summaries.
4. **Separate perspectives.** Record what each participant observed, believed, intended, concealed, and misunderstood.
5. **Identify affected dimensions.** Do not update every dimension by default.
6. **Update commitments.** Add or change promises, debts, gifts, duties, boundaries, or dependencies with provenance.
7. **Assess trajectory.** Update trajectories only when evidence supports direction.
8. **Route Knowledge.** Apply communication and discovery through valid Knowledge routes.
9. **Update latest contact.** Change Latest Meeting only for an actual meeting.
10. **Preserve unresolved issues.** Close only what the event resolves.
11. **Route consequences.** Send social, faction, Soul, or world effects to their owners.
12. **Validate.** Check identities, chronology, references, promises, perspectives, and protected information.

## Relationship Change Over Time

Elapsed time alone does not add or subtract trust.

Time permits messages, due promises, grief, repeated support or absence, changed dependency, Rumour spread, institutional pressure, reflection, new priorities, and changed memory access. The GM resolves those causes under [Simulation Abstraction](../world-engine/SIMULATION_ABSTRACTION.md) and updates only supported dimensions.

## Recognition

**Recognition State** is observer-specific and separates known identity, familiar signs, suspected identity, recognized role, remembered history, emotional familiarity, mistaken identity, nonrecognition, concealment, and unknown.

A stored relationship does not guarantee current recognition. Recognition may depend on embodiment, senses, disguise, memory, Soul Resonance, Retained Instincts, Soul Titles, cultural signs, records, or testimony.

When recognition fails, the Relationship Record still exists. Current Knowledge and recognition state explain why it cannot yet be expressed.

## Death and Reincarnation

Final Death changes relationships without deleting them. Records preserve grief, relief, succession, obligations, unfinished issues, Knowledge of death, worldly consequences, and Soul-level links only where established.

A new incarnation does not regain former social standing, family role, marriage, debt, authority, consent, or recognition automatically. Former relationships may create evidence, Resonance, symbolic pressure, or opportunity according to their owners. Current participants choose what develops now.

## Soul and Weapon Relationships

[Soul Constellations](../soul/SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md) and Soul Resonance may preserve or expose relationships among souls but do not replace current Relationship Records.

A Soul-level connection can coexist with no current recognition, hostility, changed consent, different family roles, mistaken interpretation, or separation by world or Age.

Weapon Souls, monsters, spirits, gods, collective persons, and other nonhuman actors use the same identity, perspective, agency, and causal-update safeguards with embodiment-appropriate communication.

A Soul Weapon Bond is not a complete relationship record. Consent, conflict, friendship, dependency, promises, privacy, and repair may evolve without changing bond validity or form.

## Repair, Forgiveness, and Closure

Repair may involve acknowledgment, evidence, apology, restitution, changed behavior, boundaries, accountability, mediation, and acceptance or refusal. It is a process, not a reset command.

Forgiveness does not erase history, restore trust automatically, cancel consequences, or require renewed contact. Redemption in one audience does not force another's interpretation.

A relationship may become active, distant, estranged, suspended, dormant, transformed, closed, ended by death, or disputed in continuity. Closure records why active expectations ended and what obligations or consequences remain.

## Migration and Identity Repair

Relationship migration preserves:

- Relationship ID;
- participant IDs and roles;
- first and latest meeting;
- important encounters;
- directional perspectives;
- dimensions and trajectories;
- promises, betrayals, debts, gifts, family, organizations, discoveries, dependencies, and unresolved issues;
- chronology;
- visibility and Secrets boundaries;
- Incarnation and faction Version scope;
- closure and supersession.

Names alone never merge people or relationships. Duplicate candidates remain separate until an identity audit supports merge. A merge preserves aliases, former IDs, sources, and affected references.

## Worked Examples

### Known NPC Omitted From a Summary

An NPC last appeared ten sessions ago, and the recent recap omits them. Actor and Relationship records show a first meeting, two promises, and unresolved distrust.

The GM loads those records. The NPC does not introduce themselves as a stranger unless disguise, memory loss, changed embodiment, deception, or another valid cause supports it.

### Trust and Hostility Together

A rebel trusts a royal officer to honor prisoner exchanges but hates the regime and expects manipulation.

Trust is `Stable` for one function; hostility remains active elsewhere. A successful exchange may reinforce reliability without producing friendship or ideological agreement.

### Gift Without Debt

A monster community gives a traveller a carved token after shared mourning. The gift transfers custody and carries recognition, but no repayment was requested.

A merchant later claiming the token proves a debt does not make that claim true.

### Betrayal Without Shared Premise

A patron believes a scout betrayed them by refusing an illegal order. The scout never accepted unconditional obedience and acted to protect civilians.

The refusal is factual. Betrayal is the patron's interpretation, while disputed expectation, authority, witnesses, and consequences remain separate.

### Reincarnated Parent

A former parent reincarnates in another species. Their adult child knows the parent died and has no evidence of the new identity.

Family history persists, but recognition, legal kinship, trust, and desired relationship do not restore automatically. Discovery may create a profound new Relationship Event while both retain agency.

### Shared Research Credit

Two researchers discover a cure, but one publishes alone. Research records contributions and evidence. Knowledge records who knew what. Relationship memory records secrecy agreements, credit expectations, publication, perceived betrayal, institutional response, and open repair.

No one score represents the resulting trust, respect, hostility, dependence, and shared purpose.

## Safeguards

- Relationships are multidimensional, directional, and evidence-based.
- Trust, hostility, affection, recognition, obligation, and authority never collapse into one score.
- Change requires an event or process; elapsed time alone applies no drift.
- Known characters and prior ties never reset silently.
- Recognition failure requires an in-world identity, memory, perception, or information route.
- Family and organizations grant no automatic love, trust, obedience, consent, Knowledge, or authority.
- Promises create expectations rather than guaranteed action.
- Betrayal preserves factual action and participant interpretation separately.
- Gifts and debts remain distinct from each other and control.
- Dependency creates stakes without ownership.
- Shared discovery does not pool interpretation or Knowledge.
- Reincarnation preserves history without restoring worldly roles or claims.
- Soul links do not replace current interpersonal history or agency.
- Repair and forgiveness do not erase history.
- Names do not merge identities.
- No populated Relationship Record belongs in this repository.

## Scope Boundaries

This document defines relationship records, dimensions, perspectives, events, trust trajectories, recognition, commitments, change, Reincarnation, closure, and migration. It does not define general social Development, faction behavior, legal systems, Soul Constellation mechanics, Research progression, or save integration.
