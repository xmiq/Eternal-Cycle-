# Research Engine

## Purpose

This document defines how in-world inquiry persists from initial observation through competing explanation, experiment, evidence, theory, confirmation, loss, and rediscovery.

Research is gameplay because methods, access, uncertainty, collaboration, error, secrecy, infrastructure, and consequences shape what actors can responsibly claim and do.

## Core Rule

Research progresses through an iterative evidence cycle:

> Observation -> Hypothesis -> Experiment -> Evidence -> Theory -> Confirmed Knowledge

The sequence is not a guaranteed ladder. Research may return to an earlier stage, branch into competing theories, stall for lack of access, reveal that the question was malformed, become obsolete when the world changes, be disproved, be forgotten, or require rediscovery.

Research becomes Campaign Canon only through [In-World Confirmation](TRUTH_LAYERS.md#in-world-confirmation) sufficient for the exact claim and accepted by the relevant factual and specialist owners.

## Research Is Not

Research is not:

- a universal point currency;
- passive progress from elapsed time;
- automatic Development or Skill XP;
- a guaranteed invention pipeline;
- one global technology tree;
- proof that an institution is correct;
- permission to reveal GM Secrets;
- a substitute for Character Knowledge;
- a way to bypass magical, species, Soul, ethical, or access requirements;
- a guarantee that experiments are safe, repeatable, or meaningful;
- universal truth merely because a confidence label says `Confirmed`.

## Research Record

Every material Research undertaking has a stable Research ID and records:

- Research Question;
- scope and definitions;
- investigators, collaborators, subjects, sponsors, and affected parties;
- current Project and location references;
- methods, instruments, Skills, Development, access, and infrastructure;
- observations;
- active and retired hypotheses;
- experiments;
- evidence;
- theories and competing theories;
- confidence for each exact claim;
- known limitations, biases, risks, and alternatives;
- secrecy, publication, ownership, and communication;
- confirmed facts and their factual-owner references;
- obsolete, forgotten, disproved, or rediscovered states;
- unresolved questions and next Review Points;
- Timeline, History, and migration provenance.

One Research record may contain several claims with different confidence. A broad question should be split when its parts have different methods, scopes, or owners.

## Research Question

A **Research Question** states the exact unknown or disputed claim under inquiry.

It identifies:

- subject;
- scope in time, place, species, form, population, Mana context, source, or conditions;
- relevant definitions;
- current Knowledge and uncertainty;
- why the question matters;
- possible affected parties;
- canonical owners;
- what evidence could change the inquiry;
- what remains outside scope.

Questions such as “How does magic work?” or “What is the strongest monster?” are too broad until decomposed into answerable, owner-specific claims.

## Observation

An **Observation Record** preserves what an observer, instrument, spell, archive, experiment, or other established source detected under stated conditions.

It records:

- observer or instrument identity;
- time and location;
- target and conditions;
- senses, interfaces, calibration, and access;
- raw result where recoverable;
- interpretation kept separately;
- uncertainty, detection limits, and possible interference;
- custody and integrity of samples or records;
- who has Character Knowledge of the observation;
- replication or contradiction references.

Observation is not perfect perception. Expertise can improve methods and interpretation without removing embodiment, bias, deception, magical interference, distance, or unknown variables.

Repeated observations matter when they add variation, independent provenance, changed conditions, improved precision, or meaningful contradiction. Identical repetition with no new information does not farm progress.

## Hypothesis

A **Hypothesis** is a provisional explanatory or predictive claim that can be compared against evidence within a stated scope.

It records:

- exact claim;
- originating observations or prior theory;
- assumptions;
- predicted findings;
- plausible alternatives;
- disconfirming evidence that would matter;
- test conditions;
- scope limits;
- Research Confidence;
- proponents, critics, and access boundaries.

A hypothesis may be imaginative, culturally shaped, mistaken, or partly correct. Creating one grants no fact, Skill, magical access, or Development by itself.

Untestable claims may remain beliefs, philosophies, Player Theories, Rumours, or open Mysteries. They are not forced into an experimental model where no valid test exists.

## Experiment

An **Experiment Record** describes a deliberate attempt to distinguish hypotheses, measure a process, reproduce an effect, or test a prediction.

It records:

- objective and exact tested claim;
- protocol and sequence;
- participants and roles;
- subject consent and welfare where persons are involved;
- location, time, environment, and Mana context;
- materials, samples, tools, spells, Rituals, infrastructure, and sources;
- controls, comparisons, baselines, or reasons they are impossible;
- expected observations;
- risks, stopping conditions, and containment;
- execution deviations;
- direct outcomes and failures;
- generated Evidence Records;
- costs, damage, traces, and world consequences;
- repeatability conditions.

An experiment is an in-world event. Its effects follow Magic, Skills, Development, Monster Evolution, Soul, item, actor, and World Engine owners. Research intent does not waive consent, danger, resource, law, or causality.

## Evidence

An **Evidence Record** is a source-preserving observation, measurement, sample, testimony, trace, result, or comparison used to support, weaken, distinguish, or disprove an exact claim.

Evidence records:

- source and custody;
- method;
- scope;
- relevance to claims;
- independence or shared dependencies;
- integrity and possible contamination;
- reproducibility;
- uncertainty;
- supporting and contradicting relationships;
- access and disclosure;
- current interpretation;
- later correction or invalidation.

Evidence does not belong only to the theory that first cited it. Competing theories may explain the same observation differently.

Quantity is not independence. A hundred copied reports may trace to one mistaken witness. A prestigious archive may preserve an authentic record with a false conclusion. An ancient Soul Echo may offer valuable testimony while retaining its memory and access limits.

## Theory

A **Theory Record** is an integrated explanatory model connecting multiple observations and evidence within a defined scope and producing testable expectations or useful predictions.

It records:

- exact claims and scope;
- constituent hypotheses;
- supporting and contradicting evidence;
- assumptions and mechanisms;
- predictions;
- unresolved anomalies;
- alternatives and Competing Theories;
- Research Confidence by claim;
- domain and Age compatibility;
- practical uses and risks;
- known failure conditions;
- history of revision;
- advocates, critics, institutions, and access.

A theory may be useful without being true in every detail. It may predict well in one region and fail where Mana law, species embodiment, climate, technology, or divine Jurisdiction differs.

## Confirmed Knowledge

**Confirmed Knowledge** is a scoped research claim that has passed In-World Confirmation and now has a corresponding Campaign Canon fact owned by the relevant system.

Confirmation requires:

- precise claim and scope;
- sufficient evidence provenance;
- valid methods and access;
- material alternatives addressed;
- contradictions investigated or bounded;
- appropriate replication or independent support where feasible;
- specialist-system adjudication;
- uncertainty honestly retained;
- a recorded confirmation event or finding;
- Character Knowledge updates only for actors who receive it.

The Research record remains. It preserves how the claim developed, what evidence supports it, where it may fail, and who knows it.

`Confirmed` never means omniscient, eternal, universal, immune to later evidence, or automatically known by the world.

## Research Confidence

Every material Research claim uses one of these qualitative confidence values:

| Confidence | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | In-World Confirmation supports the exact scoped claim through its factual owner |
| **Strong** | Several credible, materially independent lines of evidence support the claim and serious alternatives are constrained, but confirmation is incomplete |
| **Supported** | Relevant evidence favors the claim while meaningful alternatives or gaps remain |
| **Tentative** | Some evidence is consistent with the claim, but provenance, sample, method, or alternatives remain weak |
| **Speculative** | The claim is a plausible inquiry direction with little direct evidence |
| **Rumour** | The claim presently rests on circulating testimony or inherited assertion without adequate research provenance |
| **Disproved** | Evidence defeats the claim within its stated scope or shows its required assumptions fail |

Confidence is claim-specific, qualitative, and revisable. It is not a percentage, tier of researcher power, or automatic probability of success.

Different communities may assign different confidence based on the evidence they can access. The Research record preserves who holds each assessment and why.

## Competing Theories

**Competing Theories** offer materially different explanations, mechanisms, scopes, or predictions for overlapping evidence.

The engine preserves:

- shared evidence;
- unique evidence;
- differences in assumptions;
- predictions that distinguish them;
- proponents and information access;
- institutional or political pressures;
- compatibility or partial overlap;
- confidence by claim;
- unresolved tests.

Research does not select one winner for narrative simplicity. Several theories may remain viable, each may explain a different scope, or all may be wrong.

## Obsolete, Forgotten, and Disproved Theories

These states are distinct.

### Obsolete Theory

An **Obsolete Theory** was useful or valid in a former context but no longer describes current conditions, available practice, or the best model.

Examples include a spell model invalid after a World Reset, a disease theory superseded by better mechanisms, or a navigation method made irrelevant by a closed World Gate.

Obsolescence does not imply the theory was always false.

### Forgotten Theory

A **Forgotten Theory** no longer has accessible living practitioners, readable records, institutional continuity, or current Character Knowledge sufficient to use or evaluate it.

The theory may still be true, false, obsolete, or unknown. Forgetting is an access condition, not a truth value.

### Disproved Theory

A **Disproved Theory** has evidence that defeats its exact claim or required assumptions within stated scope.

Disproof does not erase:

- why the theory was believed;
- evidence it explained correctly;
- consequences of acting on it;
- institutions or relationships built around it;
- parts not actually tested;
- future mistaken revival.

## Rediscovery

**Rediscovery** is a new in-world Research process that recovers, reconstructs, reinterprets, or independently reaches lost or inaccessible knowledge.

Rediscovery requires current:

- observations or sources;
- access and translation;
- methods and Skills;
- compatibility with present embodiment and world law;
- verification;
- provenance linking prior material where found;
- current Character Knowledge.

An ancient record, Soul Echo, Akashic Archive Trace, Soul Weapon memory, ruin, or inherited Skill may accelerate inquiry without automatically restoring complete context, authority, infrastructure, confidence, or practical mastery.

## Research Lifecycle

The Research cycle normally follows:

1. **Frame the question.** Define scope, owners, knowns, unknowns, stakes, and affected parties.
2. **Collect observations.** Preserve raw source, conditions, access, limits, and interpretations separately.
3. **Form hypotheses.** Record alternatives and evidence that could distinguish them.
4. **Design inquiry.** Choose experiment, comparison, field observation, archival work, testimony, modelling, or another valid method.
5. **Check ethics and risk.** Identify persons, consent, hazards, restrictions, containment, costs, and stopping conditions.
6. **Execute.** Resolve the actual in-world process through its canonical owners.
7. **Record evidence.** Preserve success, failure, deviation, trace, and custody without forcing a preferred conclusion.
8. **Evaluate.** Compare evidence with all relevant claims and update confidence by scope.
9. **Replicate or challenge.** Seek independent support, changed conditions, alternatives, and anomalies where material.
10. **Build or revise theory.** Integrate only what the evidence supports.
11. **Confirm where justified.** Route exact claims through In-World Confirmation and factual owners.
12. **Communicate.** Update Character Knowledge through actual publication, instruction, secrecy, or other routes.
13. **Persist consequences.** Update Projects, Relationships, institutions, infrastructure, Magic, Species, world conditions, and Timeline only where caused.
14. **Set Review Points.** Preserve unresolved questions, next tests, maintenance, and conditions that may obsolete the result.

The cycle can branch or stop at any stage. A negative result may close one hypothesis while opening another. A destroyed laboratory may halt progress without erasing prior evidence.

## Failed Experiments

A failed experiment distinguishes:

- failure to execute the method;
- valid execution with a null result;
- contradictory result;
- contaminated or unusable evidence;
- unsafe interruption;
- subject refusal;
- equipment or infrastructure failure;
- incorrect assumptions;
- unknown cause.

Failure may produce evidence when the observation is interpretable. It does not guarantee insight, Development, Skill growth, a discovery, or narrative compensation.

Repeated failure without changed method, feedback, or information has diminishing research value and may consume resources or create harm.

## Research, Development, and Skills

Research requires current-life effort.

- Skills own learned techniques used to observe, design, measure, experiment, reason, communicate, and build.
- Development owns capability, reliability, judgment, and current expression.
- Professions and institutions organize roles, standards, access, accountability, and transmission.
- Research owns inquiry records and evidence states.
- Confirmed facts do not grant the Skill or equipment needed to use them.

Retained progression may accelerate relearning or interpretation through its canonical routes. It does not provide current samples, instruments, collaborators, world compatibility, or confirmation.

## Research Projects and Infrastructure

A Research Project references:

- objective and question;
- participants and authority;
- methods;
- current work state;
- samples and Inventory;
- sites and Infrastructure;
- funding or supply;
- permissions and restrictions;
- risks and containment;
- Timeline;
- expected Review Points;
- outputs and communication.

Elapsed time alone produces no fixed Research yield. Off-screen progress follows actual effort, inputs, feedback, interruptions, and [Simulation Abstraction](../world-engine/SIMULATION_ABSTRACTION.md).

## Ethics, Consent, and Affected Parties

Research involving persons, Souls, Weapon Souls, sapient monsters, spirits, gods, communities, bodies, memories, dangerous magic, disease, or ecological intervention preserves:

- personhood;
- informed consent where applicable;
- refusal and withdrawal;
- authority and jurisdiction;
- risk distribution;
- protected interests;
- containment;
- care and aftermath;
- externalities;
- accountability.

Scientific interest, emergency, sponsorship, secrecy, or possible benefit does not erase agency or ordinary safeguards.

## Fraud, Bias, and Error

Research records may include:

- fabricated or altered evidence;
- selective reporting;
- mistaken calibration;
- biased samples;
- institutional pressure;
- conflicts of interest;
- translation error;
- magical interference;
- memory distortion;
- genuine disagreement.

Fraud is an actor choice and event, not an automatic twist. Its discovery updates Evidence integrity, Character Knowledge, relationships, institutions, and Historical consequences through valid routes.

An incorrect researcher need not be foolish or malicious. A correct researcher need not possess enough evidence to justify certainty.

## Publication, Secrecy, and Knowledge

Research findings reach actors only through communication and access.

The record distinguishes:

- private notes;
- shared team records;
- restricted institutional knowledge;
- public publication;
- oral transmission;
- stolen or leaked material;
- deliberately false publication;
- unread or inaccessible archives;
- GM Secrets;
- Player Theories.

Publication creates an information route, not universal comprehension or acceptance. Secrecy protects access, not truth authority.

## Cross-System Research

### Species and Monster Evolution

Research may establish scoped anatomy, lifecycle, ecology, behavior, Mutation, or Evolution evidence. Monster Evolution and Species Development own the actual traits and routes. Observing one individual does not define a species universally.

### Magic

Research may examine Mana, Affinity, spells, Rituals, Enchantments, Alchemy, Divine Magic, or restrictions. Each magic system owns function, cost, source, failure, and compatibility. A Recipe or spell procedure still requires present access and practice.

### Souls and the Akashic Archive

Soul memories, Echoes, Retained Instincts, Soul Weapons, and Archive Records may supply evidence. Their provenance, access, interpretation, consent, damage, and truth limits remain in force.

### Technology and Institutions

Confirmed knowledge may support invention, validation, adoption, standards, and infrastructure through [Technology and Magical Advancement](../world-engine/TECHNOLOGY_AND_MAGICAL_ADVANCEMENT.md). Research confirmation does not create production capacity, social adoption, maintenance, or equitable access.

### World Engine

Experiments consume resources, create waste, alter ecology, spread information, affect factions, and generate opportunities or hazards. The World Engine receives only established direct changes and preserves autonomous response.

## Reincarnation, Ages, and World Resets

At Final Death:

- research records, laboratories, institutions, publications, samples, and social credit remain world-bound;
- eligible Skills, knowledge Imprints, memories, or instincts persist only through their Soul owners;
- current access and practical reliability end with embodiment where applicable;
- collaborators and rivals continue acting;
- unfinished Projects remain subject to succession, abandonment, or continuation.

After Reincarnation, retained history may accelerate inquiry but does not restore authority, proof, facilities, or universal confidence.

Age Transitions and World Resets may change the validity or scope of theories. Revalidate current law, species, Mana, source, infrastructure, and Contact Domain assumptions rather than deleting the Research history.

## Migration

Research migration preserves:

- Research and claim IDs;
- questions and scopes;
- observations and source provenance;
- hypotheses and predictions;
- experiment protocols and deviations;
- Evidence custody and integrity;
- theories, alternatives, and confidence by claim;
- Confirmed Knowledge links;
- obsolete, forgotten, disproved, and rediscovered states;
- participants, institutions, access, and Secrets;
- Timeline and History;
- unresolved questions and Review Points.

A migration cannot promote confidence, discard contradictions, merge independent evidence, expose secrets, or infer missing results.

## Worked Examples

### Competing Spider Venom Theories

One group argues a spider monster's venom changes with diet. Another argues maturation stage is the cause. Observations overlap, and early samples confound age and diet.

Both theories remain `Supported`. A controlled study across stages and diets produces Evidence that separates the effects. Species records receive only the scoped Confirmed findings; neither theory wins by institutional prestige.

### A Failed Resurrection Experiment

A ritual fails to return a body after Final Death. The failure may show that one procedure, timing, source, or condition was insufficient. It does not prove all revival impossible, trigger Reincarnation twice, or grant Soul knowledge.

Costs, consent, damage, traces, grief, and magical consequences remain real even if the Evidence is inconclusive.

### Forgotten Gate Mathematics

An archive contains damaged formulae from a former Age. A reincarnated scholar recognizes some notation through retained history but lacks the former instruments and Mana law has changed.

The team enters Rediscovery: reconstructing sources, testing translations, and revalidating assumptions. The old theory is neither fully restored nor dismissed.

### False Theory With Real Infrastructure

A kingdom builds quarantine towers around the wrong disease theory. Later Research disproves the mechanism.

The theory becomes Disproved, but towers, laws, careers, trauma, relationships, and any accidental benefits remain Campaign Canon and Historical consequences.

### Secret Divine Research

A temple studies when petitions receive responses. Its evidence is restricted, some sources are person-like, and silence may reflect refusal rather than failed mechanics.

Research tracks Domain, Jurisdiction, relationship, consent, doctrine, and alternative explanations. Publication choices alter faction and faith relationships but do not compel a Divine Source to respond.

## Safeguards

- The six-stage cycle is iterative, not a guaranteed progression ladder.
- Research uses no universal points, tiers, or passive yield.
- Confidence is qualitative, claim-specific, and source-backed.
- Research becomes Campaign Canon only through In-World Confirmation.
- Confirmed Knowledge remains scoped and observer-specific in access.
- Competing theories remain until evidence distinguishes them.
- Obsolete, Forgotten, and Disproved are distinct states.
- Failed experiments grant no automatic insight, Skill, Development, or compensation.
- Repetition without new information does not farm progress.
- Evidence quantity does not imply independence.
- Prestige, divinity, antiquity, secrecy, and correct guesses grant no truth authority.
- Research does not waive consent, safety, law, or affected-party agency.
- Research records do not grant practical capability or infrastructure.
- Reincarnation preserves only eligible Soul history, not world-bound research assets.
- Migration preserves contradictions, provenance, confidence, and Secrets.
- No populated Research record belongs in this repository.

## Scope Boundaries

This document defines Research records, lifecycle, confidence, competing and retired theories, confirmation, ethics, system interfaces, and migration. It does not define individual scientific disciplines, universal experimental formulas, invention adoption, Research templates, save integration, or campaign content.
