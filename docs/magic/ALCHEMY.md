# Alchemy

## Purpose

This document defines alchemy as the controlled magical transformation, separation, combination, stabilization, and application of matter and its source-bound properties. It establishes reagents, process conditions, recipes, batches, yield, byproducts, exposure, quality, degradation, failure, ethics, and safeguards without becoming a potion catalog.

Alchemy integrates [Mana](MANA.md), [Magical Affinities](MAGICAL_AFFINITIES.md), [Spell Formation](SPELL_FORMATION.md), [Rituals](RITUALS.md), [Enchanting](ENCHANTING.md), [Magical Development](../progression/MAGICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md), the [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md), Professions, embodiment, ecology, medicine, crafting, and Monster Evolution. It does not replace those systems.

## Core Rule

**Alchemy is a controlled material process that uses established physical and magical relationships to produce a bounded transformation with traceable inputs, conditions, outputs, byproducts, and consequences.**

An alchemical result comes from what actually happens to matter, Mana, sources, bodies, and environments. A recipe name cannot create absent properties, and a rare ingredient cannot waive an invalid process.

Alchemy is not:

- a universal potion list;
- a way to bottle any spell, Skill, affinity, Species Trait, Soul, Title, memory, or divine power;
- a consumable experience or evolution currency;
- proof that eating, drinking, injecting, wearing, or absorbing something copies what it came from;
- a replacement for medicine, cooking, chemistry, craft, enchanting, ritual, or species development;
- a guarantee that one formula works across every body, species, Age, source, and environment;
- automatically permanent, safe, pure, reproducible, scalable, legal, or ethical;
- an inventory carried through Reincarnation.

## What Counts as Alchemy

A process counts as alchemy when:

1. material or embodied inputs undergo a controlled transformation, separation, combination, or stabilization;
2. at least one relevant relationship is magical under current world law;
3. source, Mana Relation, process conditions, and material provenance can be identified;
4. outputs and byproducts follow from those inputs and conditions;
5. quality, variation, failure, degradation, and application can be adjudicated;
6. learned technique and professional responsibility remain distinct from the product.

Ordinary cooking, metallurgy, fermentation, medicine, and chemistry remain ordinary unless magic materially participates. A magical heat source does not make every meal alchemical if the food changes only through ordinary cooking. Conversely, a noncaster may perform alchemy through prepared materials, tools, or infrastructure when current Access permits.

## Canonical Terms

### Alchemy

The controlled magical transformation, separation, combination, stabilization, or application of matter and its source-bound properties through traceable inputs, conditions, outputs, byproducts, and consequences.

### Alchemical Objective

The bounded material or embodied change an Alchemical Process is intended to produce, including intended use, exclusions, quality criteria, and acceptable failure.

### Alchemical Process

The causal sequence or dependency structure through which Reagents, Mana, tools, environment, timing, and skilled actions produce an alchemical Batch.

### Alchemical Recipe

A documented or remembered model for attempting an Alchemical Process, including Reagents, provenance assumptions, preparation, stages, Process Windows, tools, Mana Relations, controls, expected outputs, byproducts, tests, and safety.

### Reagent

A material, substance, organism-derived product, magical medium, environmental sample, or other bounded input whose current properties perform one identified function in an Alchemical Process.

### Reagent Provenance

The source, history, species, environment, collection method, age, storage, processing, custody, and uncertainty relevant to a Reagent's current properties.

### Reagent Property

One physical, chemical, biological, magical, relational, or source-bound characteristic of a Reagent that matters to a specified Alchemical Process.

### Reaction Medium

The material, magical, environmental, or mixed context in which Reagents interact and through which heat, Mana, pressure, information, or other process state is transferred.

### Alchemical Catalyst

A material, tool, organism, source, spell, Enchantment, or other contributor that changes a reaction route or Process Window without becoming generic free energy or necessarily remaining unchanged.

### Process Stage

One causally distinct preparation, transformation, separation, stabilization, testing, or finishing part of an Alchemical Process.

### Process Window

The bounded range of time, temperature, pressure, concentration, Mana State, sequence, biological state, celestial condition, or other circumstance in which one Process Stage can produce its intended relation.

### Alchemical Batch

One actual set of outputs and byproducts produced through a particular Alchemical Process, Reagent provenance, equipment state, environment, and operator history.

### Alchemical Yield

The usable output obtained for one stated objective relative to the actual inputs, losses, byproducts, time, and quality of a Batch. Yield is process-specific rather than a universal efficiency score.

### Alchemical Byproduct

Any material, magical, biological, environmental, informational, or relational output not included in the Batch's intended usable product.

### Batch Stability

The degree to which a Batch preserves its relevant properties under stated storage, handling, time, environment, and use conditions.

### Alchemical Exposure

Contact between an alchemical product or byproduct and a body, material, environment, source, or system through a defined route, amount, timing, and duration.

### Effective Dose

The amount and delivery pattern of an alchemical product that reaches the relevant receiving system strongly enough to produce one intended effect under current conditions.

### Alchemical Toxicity

The source-specific harmful effect an alchemical product, impurity, concentration, interaction, route, or byproduct can produce in a stated receiver and context.

### Alchemical Profile

A qualitative external Campaign Record describing one Alchemical Process, Recipe, Batch, or application without treating products as universal consumable statistics.

## Alchemical Objective

An objective states what material or embodied result is sought.

Examples include:

- separate a toxic magical salt from water;
- stabilize a volatile resin for transport;
- produce an adhesive that cures under one Mana Field;
- extract a pigment that reveals a bounded Mana Trace;
- prepare a medicine that reduces one Channel inflammation;
- convert dangerous Ritual Residue into inert glass and recoverable waste;
- create an ink compatible with one Enchantment Host;
- preserve monster silk without destroying its tension response;
- neutralize a particular venom in one species;
- catalyze a controlled Mutation route under its own safeguards.

An objective also states:

- intended receiver or application;
- target property and acceptable range;
- exclusions and contraindications;
- expected duration;
- quality and evidence;
- acceptable byproducts and disposal;
- failure thresholds;
- whether the result is treatment, tool, Reagent, food, poison, structural material, or another category.

**Healing potion**, **dragon essence**, **strength elixir**, and **universal solvent** are incomplete objectives.

## Reagents and Provenance

A Reagent contributes only properties it currently possesses and the Process can use.

Relevant provenance may include:

- species, organ, tissue, secretion, mineral, plant, fungus, spirit-produced material, or constructed origin;
- habitat, diet, lifecycle, disease, stress, and season;
- Mana Field, source, affinity, Ritual, Enchantment, divine relationship, or contamination;
- collection method, consent, injury, death, decomposition, and preservation;
- storage temperature, light, pressure, container, time, and transport;
- washing, drying, grinding, fermentation, distillation, purification, blending, or prior use;
- substitutions, adulteration, fraud, and chain of custody;
- observer uncertainty and local naming.

Two materials with the same common name may differ enough to require different processes. Two different materials may substitute for one function while changing yield, byproducts, safety, or storage.

### No Essence Assumption

A Reagent does not automatically contain the identity or complete capability of its source.

Dragon blood may contain heat-stable proteins, a source-bound Mana pattern, disease, and political significance. It does not contain **dragonhood**, flight Skill, Soul Titles, memories, authority, Evolution Routes, or a generic fire bonus unless separate rules establish a bounded property.

## Properties and Ownership

Every claimed Reagent Property has an owner and evidence.

Possible owners include:

- ordinary material composition;
- current biological structure;
- Species Trait or Magical Organ;
- Mana State or source relation;
- Enchantment Layer;
- Ritual Residue;
- divine or spirit effect;
- environmental contamination;
- Soul-related trace under an explicit rule;
- observer belief or fraud rather than world truth.

Alchemy may transform or transfer only what the current receiving rule permits. It cannot relabel an adjacent property as a free alchemical ingredient.

When processing destroys the structure that carried a property, the property may vanish. Grinding a rune-bearing crystal can increase surface area while destroying its spatial Pattern. Preserving one property may require sacrificing another.

## Process Design

An Alchemical Process identifies:

### Inputs

Reagents, Mana, water, air, fuel, catalysts, containers, filters, tools, information, labor, and environment.

### Preparation

Cleaning, sorting, measuring, cutting, grinding, culturing, drying, dissolving, stabilizing, tempering, attuning, or other operations needed before interaction.

### Process Stages

The sequence, dependency graph, parallel operations, and state transitions through which the objective is attempted.

### Process Windows

Conditions that must remain within useful bounds. A Window may be narrow, shifting, observable only through an instrument, or dependent on biological and magical timing.

### Controls and Feedback

How operators sense process state, distinguish desired transition from failure, adjust inputs, stop safely, and verify assumptions.

### Separation and Finishing

How useful product is isolated, stabilized, packaged, labeled, tested, and protected from contamination or degradation.

### Outputs and Byproducts

Expected product, waste, emissions, residues, spent catalysts, altered tools, contaminated water, source debt, and uncertain fractions.

### Application and Disposal

How the result is used, who may be affected, what competence is required, and how remaining material is contained or returned to ecology.

## Recipes Are Models

An Alchemical Recipe records a process model. It is not the process itself.

A useful Recipe states:

- objective and intended receiver;
- Reagent identities, functions, provenance ranges, and substitutions;
- preparation and equipment;
- Mana Relations and source authority;
- Process Stages and Windows;
- sensory or instrument evidence;
- intervention and Abort Routes;
- expected Yield, quality, and variation;
- impurities and Byproducts;
- storage and Batch Stability;
- application, Effective Dose, contraindications, and disposal;
- known failures, unknowns, and revision history.

Following words exactly cannot compensate for different water, species, altitude, vessel, source, ingredient maturity, or world law. Skilled practice includes recognizing when the Recipe no longer applies.

### Substitution

A substitution claim identifies which Reagent function is being replaced and compares:

- relevant properties;
- compatible Process Windows;
- source and Mana Relation;
- quantity and concentration;
- new impurities and Byproducts;
- equipment and operator changes;
- Yield and quality;
- application safety;
- environmental and social consequence.

Shared color, folklore, price, elemental label, or broad effect does not establish substitution.

## Process Lifecycle

### 1. Define Objective and Receiver

State desired material change, intended application, exclusions, and success evidence.

### 2. Verify Reagents

Assess identity, provenance, condition, contamination, ethics, storage, and uncertainty.

### 3. Establish Process Conditions

Confirm tools, environment, Mana Relations, sources, authority, safety, and operator capability.

### 4. Prepare Inputs

Perform the actual physical, magical, biological, or ritual work needed for each Reagent and Catalyst.

### 5. Run Stages

Maintain Process Windows, monitor feedback, manage transfers, and intervene when evidence supports it.

### 6. Separate and Stabilize

Isolate useful product, prevent unwanted continuation, and preserve required properties.

### 7. Test the Batch

Evaluate identity, concentration, Function, impurities, Stability, application, and uncertainty without consuming the entire Batch or exposing unprotected subjects.

### 8. Package and Label

Record provenance, date, conditions, dose, storage, hazards, ownership, and who validated it.

### 9. Apply or Store

Use through a defined route with current receiver assessment, or maintain under conditions that preserve Stability.

### 10. Handle Byproducts and Review

Contain, reuse, neutralize, return, or dispose through real ecology and law. Compare Recipe, observations, and outcome for future learning.

## Catalysts

A Catalyst changes a process route or Window. It may:

- lower required temperature or pressure;
- direct one product over another;
- synchronize a magical transition;
- provide a temporary interface;
- carry information or orientation;
- regulate Mana flow;
- bind an impurity;
- support a biological stage;
- enable a source to participate.

A Catalyst may be consumed, poisoned, transformed, degraded, contaminated, or merely difficult to recover. The word catalyst does not guarantee it remains unchanged.

Spells, Enchantments, Rituals, living organisms, spirits, and Soul Weapons can catalyze only through their actual Functions, agency, and consent. They do not become ingredients owned by the alchemist.

## Batch Variation, Yield, and Quality

Every Batch is an event under particular conditions.

Variation may come from:

- Reagent provenance;
- measurement uncertainty;
- operator timing and Skill;
- equipment wear and calibration;
- Mana State and source behavior;
- environment and contamination;
- Process Window drift;
- scale and mixing;
- biological activity;
- storage and sampling;
- fraud or incomplete records.

Yield is scoped to one objective. More volume may mean lower concentration, poorer Stability, greater toxicity, or less recoverable catalyst. A low-volume Batch can be more useful when purity and safety matter.

Quality statements identify the measured or observed properties relevant to intended use. **High grade** and **masterwork potion** have no universal effect.

## Scaling

Scaling an Alchemical Process changes more than quantity.

Larger or smaller Batches may alter:

- heat and Mana transfer;
- mixing and concentration gradients;
- reaction speed;
- pressure and containment;
- Process Windows;
- biological viability;
- operator reach and sensing;
- equipment stress;
- separation and stabilization;
- source throughput;
- waste, ecology, labor, and cost.

A Recipe validated in a cup does not automatically work in a reservoir. Industrial infrastructure can outperform a personal alchemist while remaining dependent on supply, maintenance, institutions, and environmental capacity.

## Application to Bodies

Alchemy can affect a body only through a valid exposure and receiving route.

Possible routes include:

- ingestion;
- inhalation;
- injection;
- skin or shell contact;
- wound application;
- implanted depot;
- eye, gill, root, or other species interface;
- magical Channel;
- environmental immersion;
- symbiotic delivery.

For each application assess:

- receiver species, body plan, age, mass, health, pregnancy, lifecycle, diet, microbiome, Magical Organs, Channels, and current condition;
- Effective Dose and timing;
- absorption, distribution, transformation, elimination, and accumulation;
- intended target and off-target effects;
- interactions with food, medicine, venom, Mana, Enchantments, Mutations, and other products;
- tolerance, sensitivity, allergy, dependence, withdrawal, and toxicity where established;
- ability to consent and stop;
- aftercare, monitoring, and reversibility.

One dose does not fit every species. A product harmless to a slime may desiccate a human; a human sedative may stimulate a distributed fungal mind.

### Temporary and Lasting Effects

An alchemical product may temporarily alter condition, supply material for healing, suppress a process, trigger a bounded change, or support reconstruction.

It does not create lasting Development merely because performance improves while the product is active. Permanent bodily change belongs to physical, medical, Species, Mutation, Hybridization, or Evolution rules as appropriate.

Repeated use may create real physiological dependence, injury, adaptation, or environmental exposure. Those changes follow their owners rather than becoming an alchemy level.

## Poison, Medicine, Food, and Tool Boundaries

The same substance can be food, medicine, poison, Reagent, solvent, fuel, or waste depending on receiver, dose, route, timing, purpose, and context.

Alchemy owns magical transformation and product properties. Other owners remain distinct:

- medicine owns diagnosis, treatment planning, care, monitoring, and patient responsibility;
- cooking owns food preparation and culinary practice;
- poison use owns delivery, tactics, law, and intent through relevant Skills and social rules;
- ordinary chemistry and craft own nonmagical transformations;
- Skills own learned handling;
- Professions own standards, accountability, and integrated work;
- bodies and species own response;
- law and institutions own regulation and access.

A healing effect does not make the maker a physician. A poison label does not make a substance universally harmful.

## Skills and Professions

Alchemy may require Skills in:

- identification and provenance assessment;
- harvesting and ethical collection;
- measurement and preparation;
- process control;
- magical sensing and regulation;
- equipment use and maintenance;
- Recipe interpretation and adaptation;
- substitution analysis;
- Batch testing;
- toxicology and application;
- containment and disposal;
- documentation and fraud detection;
- research and scale-up.

Profession Development owns integrated judgment, standards, responsibility, clients, patients, workers, affected communities, supply, and consequences.

Possessing a Recipe does not grant technique. Producing one successful Batch does not establish reliability. Consuming a product does not grant the maker's Skill.

## Ecology, Harvesting, and Supply

Reagent demand can change ecosystems and societies.

Consider:

- harvest rate and regeneration;
- lifecycle and breeding;
- habitat damage;
- domestication and cultivation;
- bycatch and non-target harm;
- disease and contamination;
- invasive species;
- waste and water use;
- Mana source depletion;
- labor, land, trade, monopoly, smuggling, and war;
- personhood and consent of source beings;
- cultural, sacred, legal, and territorial claims;
- substitutes and recycling.

A rare product is not inherently more potent. Scarcity may reflect slow growth, dangerous access, monopoly, fashion, habitat loss, or poor knowledge.

Alchemical Byproducts remain in the world. Pouring spent magical solvent into a river can alter ecology, food webs, settlements, and future Reagent quality.

## Persons, Remains, and Ethical Sources

Material taken from a person or sapient species retains provenance, consent, custody, mourning, law, and social consequence.

Identify:

- whether collection harms, kills, coerces, or exploits;
- whether material is shed, donated, cultivated, purchased, stolen, scavenged, or taken after death;
- whether consent is informed and current;
- who has custody without assuming ownership of personhood;
- what properties actually remain after collection;
- disease and identity risk;
- cultural and political meaning;
- alternatives and necessity claims.

The fact that a cruel process works does not make cruelty a privileged source of power. Harm may damage the desired property, create resistance, attract opposition, or remain a moral wrong without compensating strength.

## Souls and Identity

Alchemy does not treat Souls as substances by default.

Blood, bone, memory-bearing tissue, Soul Weapon fragments, grave soil, Ritual Residue, Mana Traces, and Archive evidence may be alchemically relevant only through properties their owning systems actually establish.

No Recipe can consume, distill, bottle, split, copy, transfer, merge, erase, or manufacture a Soul, Soul Imprint, Soul Depth, Soul Title, Echo, Resonance Link, Constellation, Avatar, or Reincarnation route merely by naming it as an ingredient.

Any exceptional Soul interaction requires an explicit high-consequence receiving rule consistent with [Soul Engine Safeguards](../soul/SOUL_ENGINE_SAFEGUARDS.md), agency, resistance, provenance, and non-duplication.

## Species, Mutation, Hybridization, and Evolution

Alchemy may:

- supply materials for healing or growth;
- suppress or induce one bounded physiological process;
- create an Artificial Modification;
- support a Mutation procedure;
- prepare a compatible graft or hybrid transition;
- alter an environmental pressure;
- stabilize a dangerous transition under an explicit Evolution Route.

It does not:

- copy consumed Species Traits;
- create a lineage from unrelated ingredients;
- replace Source Lineage or body compatibility;
- grant monster Skill Trees;
- turn temporary enhancement into an Evolved Form;
- satisfy hidden conditions through a shopping list alone;
- make repeated consumption Evolution currency.

[Mutations](../monster-evolution/MUTATIONS.md), [Hybridization](../monster-evolution/HYBRIDIZATION.md), and [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/MONSTER_EVOLUTION.md) retain ownership of bodily transition, compatibility, agency, failure, continuity, and consequence.

## Spell, Ritual, Enchantment, and Affinity Interactions

### Spells

[Spell Formation](SPELL_FORMATION.md) may heat, cool, separate, contain, sense, transform, or regulate one Process Stage. The spell remains a Spell Instance with its own source and failure.

### Rituals

[Rituals](RITUALS.md) may coordinate large Batches, roles, timing, source authority, rare Windows, or dangerous containment. The Ritual does not grant every participant alchemical Skill.

### Enchantments

[Enchanting](ENCHANTING.md) may supply controlled vessels, sensors, filters, heating, storage, or interfaces. The Enchantment remains a Host-bound dependency and can Drift or fail.

An alchemical product may be a Reagent for Enchanting. It does not install itself or grant the resulting Function to its maker or consumer.

### Affinities

[Magical Affinity](MAGICAL_AFFINITIES.md) may affect one Reagent, source, method, environment, or sensing route through an exact Expression. It does not identify every ingredient, guarantee Recipe success, neutralize toxicity, or transfer through consumption.

## Soul-System Interactions

### Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may align a soul with a meaningful Reagent, craft, place, source, maker, or event within its Basis and Target. A receiving rule may support recognition or interpretation without changing material composition.

### Soul Echoes and Retained Instincts

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may remember Recipes, process cues, former Batches, and consequences. [Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) may orient attention toward familiar smells, textures, timing, or danger.

They do not supply Reagents, tools, current senses, valid measurements, or Practised Reliability.

### Soul Titles

A [Soul Title](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may influence source response, institutional access, witness, or social meaning where a receiving rule recognizes its Basis. It does not change Recipe chemistry or make the bearer's remains uniquely potent by default.

### Soul Depth and Soul Avatars

[Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) may support integration of complex craft history without becoming alchemical Capacity. A [Soul Avatar](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) may synthesize accessible perspectives but cannot perform parallel laboratory actions or replace current embodiment.

### Akashic Archive

[Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) Records may preserve Recipe versions, Batch evidence, ecological history, and failures. They do not provide Reagents, instruments, source access, current-law validity, or guaranteed truth.

## Soul Weapons

A Weapon Soul is a person, not a Reagent.

Alchemy may treat a Weapon Vessel through cleaning, alloy work, adhesive, cooling, stabilization, corrosion removal, or another material process while keeping separate:

- Weapon Soul personhood and consent;
- Vessel Continuity;
- ordinary material repair;
- Alchemical Process and product;
- Enchantment Layers;
- external sources;
- Soul Weapon Form and Weapon Evolution.

Alchemy does not Awaken, bind, consume, distill, copy, evolve, manifest, transfer, or command a Weapon Soul. Fragments of a vessel do not contain fractions of its soul by default.

An alchemical material may support a [Weapon Evolution Route](../soul-weapons/WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md) only while Source Integration preserves provenance, permission, maintenance, tradeoffs, counters, and consequences. Ingredient rarity and dramatic forging do not buy Evolution.

## Reincarnation

At Final Death:

- products, Reagents, recipes as physical records, laboratories, tools, sources, stores, licences, patients, suppliers, and current Batches remain in the world;
- active alchemical effects on the former body do not transfer unless a separate Soul rule explicitly owns an effect;
- legitimate conceptual and process knowledge may persist through Magical and Professional Imprints;
- bounded laboratory, diagnostic, application, and craft Skills may preserve Skill Imprints;
- Soul Echoes and Archive Records may preserve experience and evidence;
- Practised Reliability, current senses, embodiment, material access, and authority must be rebuilt.

A reincarnated alchemist must retest old Recipes against current species, body, Reagents, measurement systems, tools, sources, environment, and world law. Retained expertise accelerates investigation without conjuring inventory or making obsolete formulas safe.

## Storage, Stability, and Degradation

Batch Stability depends on stated conditions such as:

- temperature, pressure, light, oxygen, humidity, motion, and container;
- Mana Field and source proximity;
- time since preparation;
- contamination and repeated opening;
- biological activity;
- separation, settling, crystallization, evaporation, and leakage;
- Enchantment maintenance;
- user handling and transport;
- World Reset or altered law.

Degradation may reduce intended Function, increase toxicity, change dose, create gas or pressure, attract creatures, damage containers, or produce useful new Reagents.

Expiration labels are evidence based on tested storage assumptions. They are not universal moments at which a product becomes inert.

## Failure

### Identification Failure

A Reagent, receiver, impurity, source, or product is misidentified.

### Provenance Failure

Relevant history, collection, storage, treatment, adulteration, or uncertainty is wrong or missing.

### Preparation Failure

Inputs are cleaned, measured, shaped, cultured, or stabilized incorrectly.

### Window Failure

Temperature, pressure, time, concentration, Mana State, biological state, or sequence leaves the valid range.

### Process Failure

The intended transformation, separation, combination, or stabilization does not occur or takes a competing route.

### Equipment or Containment Failure

Tools, vessels, sensors, filters, Enchantments, or safety boundaries break or misreport.

### Separation Failure

Useful product remains mixed with impurities, catalysts, solvents, or dangerous fractions.

### Stability Failure

A Batch changes during finishing, storage, transport, or application.

### Application Failure

Receiver, route, Effective Dose, timing, interaction, or diagnosis is wrong.

### Scale Failure

A Process changes qualitatively when Batch size, geometry, throughput, labor, or environment changes.

Failure may waste materials, injure people, poison ecology, create unstable products, spread disease, reveal secrets, cause source debt, or produce no useful Yield. It does not award Development, resistance, affinity, Mutation, Evolution, or Recipe mastery automatically.

## Safety, Testing, and Counterplay

Responsible alchemy may require:

- provenance records and quarantine;
- small controlled Batches;
- reference samples and independent tests;
- calibrated instruments;
- ventilation and containment;
- protected operators and affected-party consent;
- clean separation and labeled storage;
- species-specific application review;
- antidotes, decontamination, and emergency disposal;
- environmental monitoring;
- Batch recall and adverse-event reporting;
- peer review and reproducibility checks;
- refusal when failure cannot be bounded.

Counterplay may exploit Reagent supply, contamination, substitution, Process Windows, tools, source access, storage, transport, dose, delivery, metabolism, false labels, antidotes, ordinary material barriers, or ecological dependencies.

Knowing a Recipe does not neutralize a Batch. A nonmagical investigator may trace procurement, test containers, interrupt cold storage, expose adulteration, or protect a receiver from delivery.

## Information and Secrecy

Keep separate:

- actual Reagents and process state;
- operator model and Recipe;
- proprietary names and public labels;
- provenance records and chain of custody;
- observed effects and causal explanation;
- Batch tests and sampling limits;
- fraud, adulteration, substitution, and contamination;
- player knowledge and character knowledge;
- unknown long-term or species-specific effects.

Secrecy cannot make an impossible Recipe work or erase material evidence. It can create monopoly, unsafe imitation, information asymmetry, dependence, and political conflict.

## Alchemical Claim Procedure

When alchemy matters:

1. state the Alchemical Objective, intended receiver, and relevant quality;
2. identify each Reagent, function, provenance, property owner, condition, and uncertainty;
3. establish Mana Relations, catalysts, tools, environment, authority, and consent;
4. map Process Stages, Windows, controls, separation, stabilization, and Abort Routes;
5. distinguish Recipe knowledge, operator Skills, professional judgment, and current reliability;
6. resolve Batch variation, Yield, Byproducts, testing, and Stability;
7. assess application route, Effective Dose, receiver embodiment, interaction, and toxicity;
8. separate spells, Rituals, Enchantments, medicine, species, Mutation, Evolution, Soul, and Weapon effects by owner;
9. resolve failure and counterplay at the correct layer;
10. propagate harvesting, labor, waste, ecology, trade, law, and social consequences;
11. award Development only for capability meaningfully practised and integrated;
12. apply Reincarnation according to knowledge, Skill, body, material, source, and world ownership;
13. store current Recipes, Batches, inventory, patients, and profiles outside canon.

## Safeguards

### No Potion Catalog

Products are defined by process, properties, receiver, dose, context, and evidence rather than universal consumable names, colors, rarities, or tiers.

### No Trait Copying Through Consumption

Eating, drinking, injecting, absorbing, wearing, or processing a being or material does not copy its Species Traits, Skills, affinity, Soul Imprints, Titles, memories, authority, Mutation, Evolution, or Weapon Soul.

### No Recipe-to-Skill Transfer

Reading, memorizing, stealing, inheriting, or receiving a Recipe does not grant identification, process control, judgment, tools, Access, or Practised Reliability.

### No Ingredient Shopping-List Progression

Possessing rare Reagents does not satisfy missing Process, body, source, authority, compatibility, Evolution Route, or Development requirements.

### No Universal Essence

Labels such as dragon, holy, monster, life, death, fire, or void do not provide generic transferable properties. Every Reagent Property requires evidence and a valid receiving process.

### No Infinite Yield

Scaling, recycling, catalysis, ambient Mana, self-replication, or industrial infrastructure retains matter, source, rate, loss, waste, ecology, labor, maintenance, and consequence.

### No Harm Dividend

Poisoning, overdose, addiction, pain, failed experiments, dangerous exposure, sacrifice, and survival do not automatically create resistance, Development, affinity, Mutation, or stronger products.

### No Medical Bypass

A product's magical effect does not replace diagnosis, informed consent, species compatibility, dose, interactions, monitoring, care, and professional responsibility.

### No Evolution Bypass

Alchemy may support a valid Mutation, Hybridization, or Evolution Route but cannot purchase bodily transformation or make temporary enhancement into a species form.

### No Soul Substance

Souls and Soul structures are not alchemical Reagents or currencies by default. Exceptional interactions require explicit Soul-safe rules and cannot duplicate personhood.

### No Weapon Soul Ingredient

A Weapon Soul and its vessel cannot be consumed, fractioned, copied, evolved, or transferred through alchemy. Material treatment preserves Weapon Soul consent and Vessel Continuity.

### No Reincarnation Inventory

Products, Reagents, laboratories, tools, records, suppliers, and active bodily effects remain world-bound. Only eligible knowledge and Skills persist through their owners.

### No Campaign Data in Canon

Current Recipes, Batches, ingredient inventories, suppliers, patients, doses, laboratory state, discovered substitutions, and populated Alchemical Profiles belong in external Campaign Records.

## Worked Examples

### Channel-Inflammation Medicine

An alchemist prepares a suspension that binds one irritating Mana salt in human respiratory Channels. A physician diagnoses the condition and selects dose; the alchemist controls extraction and purity; the patient consents and follows monitoring.

The product does not restore severed Channels or grant magic. A spider monster with different Channels cannot use the human dose safely, and repeated overdose damages ordinary lung tissue rather than building resistance.

### Adhesive Alchemy

A rescue team uses a two-part adhesive whose resin cures when exposed to a low-pressure earth Field. It closes a wound mechanically until surgery is possible.

The closure is not healing Skill, regeneration, or permanent physical development. Outside the Field the resin cures slowly, while too much Mana makes it brittle. The useful effect depends on chemistry, magic, application Skill, and aftercare.

### Dragon-Blood Fraud

A merchant sells **dragon essence** said to grant fire affinity. Testing shows animal blood, volcanic salt, and a warming Enchantment on the bottle.

The mixture causes flushing but grants no affinity. Even genuine dragon blood would require property-specific evidence; dragon identity and capability are not bottled by name.

### Slime Solvent Process

A slime community cultivates shed gel that selectively dissolves one mine residue. The Reagent's useful property depends on diet and colony water chemistry.

Industrial harvesting changes the ponds, lowers gel quality, and threatens the community's food supply. The slimes are suppliers and political actors, not raw nodes. A negotiated cultivation plan produces less solvent but remains sustainable.

### Mutation Support Without Evolution Purchase

A beetle monster with an established Mutation route receives an alchemical mineral carrier that reaches developing shell tissue during molting. The carrier enables material delivery but does not choose the Mutation or guarantee survival.

Compatibility, body state, consent, process, aftercare, and Mutation rules still apply. Feeding the mineral to unrelated monsters copies nothing.

### Soul Weapon Vessel Treatment

A Weapon Soul consents to an alchemical bath that removes corrosive residue from its metal vessel. The process preserves inscriptions and monitors Vessel Continuity.

The bath repairs no Soul Wound, changes no personality, and grants no Weapon Evolution. A recovered rare alloy remains an external material fact unless a later valid Source Integration route uses it.

### Reincarnated Alchemist

A former alchemist remembers a precise frost-antidote Recipe but Reincarnates as a warm-blooded desert monster in an Age without the original herb. Its senses cannot distinguish the old aromatic cue.

Retained knowledge reveals the likely binding function. The alchemist learns new instruments, studies a local fungus, tests substitutions, and rebuilds the process. The successor Recipe is real current-life work rather than automatic restoration.

### Industrial Scale Failure

A village Recipe safely neutralizes ten jars of Ritual waste. A city copies it into a massive vat without accounting for heat and Mana gradients.

The center leaves its Process Window, producing a stable toxic fraction that the small Batch never formed. The error is scale failure, not proof that the Recipe was fraudulent. Fixing it requires mixing, cooling, sampling, and disposal infrastructure.

### Expired Night-Vision Draught

A draught temporarily increases sensitivity in one nocturnal species by supplying a light-reactive compound. Months of warm storage separate the compound into a stronger top layer and toxic sediment.

The bottle is not uniformly **half effective**. Shaking it creates a dangerous dose, while careful testing may recover a usable fraction. Storage history and Batch state matter more than an abstract expiration timer.
