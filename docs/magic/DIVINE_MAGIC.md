# Divine Magic

## Purpose

This document defines divine magic as magic whose source, permission, or governing relationship is established through a Divine Source or divine law. It establishes domains, jurisdictions, relationships, access, mandates, covenants, petitions, responses, blessings, miracles, rites, intermediaries, signs, withdrawal, burdens, failure, counterplay, and safeguards without defining a universal pantheon.

Divine magic integrates [Mana](MANA.md), [Magical Affinities](MAGICAL_AFFINITIES.md), [Spell Formation](SPELL_FORMATION.md), [Rituals](RITUALS.md), [Enchanting](ENCHANTING.md), [Alchemy](ALCHEMY.md), [Magical Development](../progression/MAGICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md), the [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md), Human Classes, Monster Evolution, Souls, Soul Weapons, institutions, religion, ecology, and world law. It does not replace those systems.

## Core Rule

**Divine magic is a bounded magical interaction in which an established Divine Source or divine law provides source, permission, judgment, mediation, or effect through a valid present relationship and route.**

A divine label does not create a Divine Source. Faith does not compel one. A god's power does not make every doctrine true, every priest capable, every act moral, or every outcome inevitable.

Divine magic is not:

- all religion, prayer, ritual, sacred art, doctrine, morality, or social office;
- a universal power system shared by every god and Age;
- proof that a claimant speaks for a god;
- proof that a god is omniscient, omnipotent, benevolent, truthful, or entitled to obedience;
- a favor meter purchased with worship, sacrifice, suffering, or kills;
- a replacement for Skills, Magical Development, Classes, Professions, medicine, leadership, law, or personal judgment;
- unrestricted intervention that ignores embodiment, Mana, domain, jurisdiction, relationship, consent, resistance, cost, or consequence;
- automatically inherited through bloodline, office, doctrine, Soul Title, Soul Weapon, or Reincarnation.

## What Counts as Divine Magic

An effect counts as divine magic when:

1. a Divine Source or divine law is established in world truth rather than merely claimed;
2. that source has a relevant Divine Domain and present Divine Jurisdiction;
3. a valid route connects source, effect, and receiver;
4. Divine Access or a Divine Mandate permits the attempted interaction;
5. Mana, authority, embodiment, method, and target requirements remain satisfied;
6. source agency, affected-party agency, resistance, cost, failure, and consequence remain real;
7. evidence can distinguish at least some source involvement from doctrine, fraud, ordinary magic, and coincidence.

A priest using an ordinary fire spell is using a spell, not necessarily divine magic. A god teaching that spell does not permanently make the procedure divine. A Divine Source directly supplying or authorizing its present Formation can make that Spell Instance divine-sourced while [Spell Formation](SPELL_FORMATION.md) still owns the procedure.

## Canonical Terms

### Divine Source

A being, collective, structure, or world-law authority whose established nature permits divine magical relationships within a bounded Domain and Jurisdiction. A person-like Divine Source retains agency, interests, consent, refusal, and vulnerability.

### Divine Domain

The bounded subjects, relationships, processes, places, identities, or responsibilities through which a Divine Source can meaningfully act. A Domain establishes relevance, not ownership, morality, or unlimited control.

### Divine Jurisdiction

The present scope in which a Divine Source's authority can operate, such as a place, covenant, community, lineage, office, condition, relationship, Age, or world law. Jurisdiction is distinct from Domain and may change.

### Divine Relationship

The actual current connection among a Divine Source and one or more persons, communities, places, offices, objects, species, or other participants, including recognition, history, expectations, consent, obligations, and uncertainty.

### Divine Access

The valid present route through which a participant may request, receive, mediate, invoke, or express one bounded divine effect. Divine Access is not ownership of the source or proof of general approval.

### Divine Mandate

A scoped grant of Magical Authority from a Divine Source or divine law to perform, mediate, protect, judge, or maintain defined effects under current terms. A Mandate may be conditional, shared, contested, suspended, or withdrawn.

### Divine Covenant

An established relationship whose participants recognize defined permissions, obligations, protections, exchanges, limits, remedies, and exit or breach consequences. A Covenant creates only what its terms and valid world law support.

### Divine Petition

A directed request for a Divine Source to perceive, consider, authorize, answer, or act. A Petition is neither a command nor proof that the source received or accepted it.

### Divine Response

An identifiable action, permission, refusal, communication, change, or effect produced by a Divine Source in relation to a Petition, Covenant, Mandate, event, or self-chosen purpose.

### Divine Blessing

A bounded beneficial or protective effect established by a Divine Source through a defined recipient, scope, source, conditions, duration, limits, and withdrawal behavior. The label does not prove moral endorsement or permanent favor.

### Miracle

An exceptional Divine Response whose occurrence, scale, timing, or form exceeds an ordinary established divine procedure while retaining source, Domain, Jurisdiction, receiver, cost, evidence, and consequence. A Miracle does not create a reusable Skill or universal precedent by itself.

### Divine Rite

A [Ritual](RITUALS.md) whose Structure includes a Divine Source, divine law, Mandate, Covenant, Petition, or Response as an actual dependency. Sacred ceremony without magical divine participation is not a Divine Rite under this definition.

### Divine Intermediary

A person, spirit, construct, institution, office, place, object, or other valid participant that carries, translates, witnesses, filters, administers, or contests one part of a divine relationship without becoming the Divine Source by implication.

### Divine Sign

Evidence reasonably attributed to a Divine Source under a stated method and uncertainty. A Sign may support a bounded inference but does not reveal complete intent, doctrine, morality, future action, or world truth.

### Divine Silence

The absence of an identifiable Divine Response within the relevant observation window. Silence alone proves neither approval, rejection, absence, incapacity, ignorance, nor hidden action.

### Divine Withdrawal

An identifiable reduction, suspension, alteration, or termination of Divine Access, Mandate, Blessing, protection, communication, or support by its owning source or law.

### Divine Burden

A cost, obligation, exposure, dependency, conflict, responsibility, risk, or consequence created by participation in a divine relationship. A Burden is not a universal payment currency or proof of worth.

### Divine Profile

A qualitative external Campaign Record describing one Divine Source relationship or claim, including source evidence, Domain, Jurisdiction, participants, Access, Mandates, Covenants, methods, responses, signs, burdens, uncertainty, and current state.

## Divine Status and Source Truth

Campaigns may contain gods, divine collectives, divine offices, impersonal divine laws, world-scale custodians, ascended beings, ancestral powers, or entities falsely called divine. This document does not prescribe one cosmology.

For any claimed Divine Source, keep separate:

- what the source actually is;
- what it claims to be;
- what worshippers and opponents believe;
- what world law recognizes;
- what powers and vulnerabilities evidence supports;
- what Domain and Jurisdiction currently apply;
- whether the source is a person, collective, office, process, or impersonal law;
- whether intermediaries speak accurately;
- what remains unknown.

Worship does not create divinity by declaration. Power does not prove divinity. Longevity, prophecy, resurrection, Soul perception, world-scale influence, or control of an institution may be evidence without settling every metaphysical claim.

When a source is person-like, all ordinary agency safeguards apply. When a source is impersonal, do not invent consent or personality; establish the law, conditions, responses, and limits that actually exist.

## Domain and Jurisdiction

Domain answers **what kinds of relationship or change are relevant to this source?** Jurisdiction answers **where, for whom, and under what present conditions may its authority operate?**

A sea god may have a Domain concerning currents, voyages, drowned memory, and maritime obligations. Its Jurisdiction might apply only within one connected ocean, to vessels under a Covenant, or during a named Age. It does not therefore own all water, command every aquatic species, know every sailor, or control rain inland.

Domain may be:

- material, such as rivers, iron, seeds, flame, or storms;
- relational, such as hospitality, oaths, mourning, kinship, or guardianship;
- procedural, such as judgment, healing, passage, transformation, or remembrance;
- territorial, such as one mountain, city, road, grave field, or world layer;
- identity-linked, such as one lineage, species, profession, office, or community;
- historical, such as a Covenant, catastrophe, founding, victory, betrayal, or unresolved duty;
- cosmological, such as one lawful transition, boundary, cycle, or world function.

Domain and Jurisdiction can overlap among several sources. Overlap creates negotiation, conflict, divided authority, interference, joint action, or uncertainty rather than an automatic hierarchy.

Neither category is a power rating. A locally narrow source may act decisively within one Covenant while a vast source has no route into the same situation.

## Divine Relationships

A Divine Relationship can arise through:

- direct encounter and mutual recognition;
- Covenant or contract;
- accepted service, guardianship, kinship, mentorship, adoption, or alliance;
- office or institution recognized by the source;
- place, species, lineage, or lifecycle connection under explicit law;
- a Divine Response that establishes continuing terms;
- inheritance of responsibility where the source and successor both recognize it;
- rescue, opposition, debt, betrayal, litigation, rivalry, or other non-devotional relations;
- Reincarnation recognition under a valid soul-sensitive route.

Relationships are not always worshipful, friendly, exclusive, voluntary, or symmetrical. A character may bargain with, oppose, investigate, serve, refuse, love, fear, distrust, or outgrow a Divine Source.

Track separately:

- recognition;
- communication routes;
- trust and distrust;
- permissions and prohibitions;
- obligations and unresolved claims;
- source interests;
- participant interests;
- affected third parties;
- power imbalance and coercion;
- exit, breach, appeal, and remedy;
- public interpretation;
- actual current Access.

No relationship meter converts these into one score.

## Access, Mandates, and Covenants

Divine Access is effect-specific. A person may be able to hear one source but not channel it, carry one blessing but not petition, perform one rite but not interpret signs, or invoke protection only within one sanctuary.

A Divine Mandate states:

- granting source or law;
- recipient or office;
- authorized acts and excluded acts;
- Domain and Jurisdiction;
- route and method;
- affected parties and consent boundaries;
- resources or Mana Relations;
- obligations and reporting;
- duration, renewal, transfer, and succession;
- suspension, withdrawal, appeal, and breach;
- evidence and uncertainty.

A Covenant may establish mutual terms, but unequal power does not erase agency or make every term ethical. Coerced compliance remains coercion. A valid magical effect can still be abusive, illegal, resisted, or morally wrong.

Office does not guarantee a Mandate, and Mandate does not grant technical Skill. An unordained outsider may receive one direct permission while a senior priest retains doctrine and social authority but lacks current Divine Access.

## Faith, Worship, Doctrine, and Religion

Faith is personal trust, commitment, interpretation, or belief. Worship is a practice of reverence, devotion, service, relationship, or community under a culture's meaning. Doctrine is an articulated interpretation. Religion is a social and cultural system.

These may influence attention, practice, identity, relationships, institutions, decisions, and willingness. They do not automatically:

- create a Divine Source;
- compel a source to respond;
- grant affinity, Access, Mandate, Skill, or power;
- prove doctrine true;
- make disbelief magically resistant;
- make believers morally good or nonbelievers morally deficient;
- establish consent for sacrifice or divine intervention;
- preserve a relationship through Reincarnation.

A nonbeliever can receive a Divine Response. A devoted believer can receive silence. A society can maintain accurate procedures alongside false doctrine, or profound theology without any magical divine effect.

Religion, temple organization, office, law, and public legitimacy remain owned by [Institutions and Academies](../human/INSTITUTIONS_AND_ACADEMIES.md), [Social Advancement](../human/SOCIAL_ADVANCEMENT.md), and the relevant culture. Divine magic owns only the magical source relationship and effects.

## Petition, Response, and Silence

A Petition identifies:

- intended source;
- petitioner and represented parties;
- requested perception, permission, communication, or action;
- Domain and Jurisdiction basis;
- method and route;
- urgency and duration;
- offered information or contribution;
- consent and affected parties;
- uncertainty and acceptable outcomes.

Possible outcomes include:

- no identifiable response;
- acknowledgement without action;
- request for clarification;
- permission;
- refusal;
- counteroffer;
- warning;
- partial or delayed action;
- action different from the request;
- response through an intermediary;
- response to another participant;
- apparent response later shown to have another source.

Divine Silence is ambiguous. The source may not perceive the Petition, may lack Jurisdiction, may be unable or unwilling to act, may consider no response appropriate, may be communicating through an unrecognized channel, or may not exist as believed. The GM should preserve uncertainty until evidence resolves it.

Repeated petitions do not build a guaranteed success chance. Persistence may deepen practice or relationship only through meaningful feedback and consequence; harassment, spam, self-harm, and escalating sacrifice do not force attention.

## Blessings

A Divine Blessing defines:

- source and recipient;
- intended Function;
- Domain and Jurisdiction;
- receiving route and embodiment;
- activation or continuing conditions;
- source and Mana Relations;
- duration and persistence;
- limits and excluded uses;
- interaction with resistance and counterplay;
- maintenance, renewal, withdrawal, and recovery;
- evidence, visibility, and social interpretation;
- Burdens and consequences.

A blessing can protect, permit, warn, connect, heal, reveal, mark, stabilize, or support only as defined. It does not establish general holiness, moral purity, Soul Title, class, Skill, immunity, or permanent divine favor.

An unwanted blessing may be refused when the receiving route allows refusal, resisted when imposed, or treated as a hostile effect when it violates agency. Beneficial intent does not waive consent or bodily autonomy.

## Miracles

A Miracle is exceptional because the response exceeds ordinary established procedure, not because it ignores causality.

For a Miracle identify:

- Divine Source and evidence;
- relevant Domain and Jurisdiction;
- who initiated or received it;
- why a route existed;
- what changed and what did not;
- source, Mana, authority, embodiment, and resistance;
- cost, Burden, aftermath, and world consequence;
- whether the event is repeatable, petitionable, or unique;
- uncertainty and rival explanations.

One Miracle does not create a spell formula, priestly entitlement, transferable blessing, doctrine, class feature, or promise of repetition. Studying it may produce knowledge, evidence, or Skill only through meaningful work and valid feedback.

Miracles can fail, be partial, arrive too late, impose difficult choices, or protect one value while allowing another loss. They must not function as arbitrary rescue from consequences or as GM permission to override player decisions.

## Divine Rites

A Divine Rite is a magical [Ritual](RITUALS.md) with a genuine divine dependency. Ritual rules continue to own:

- Objective and Structure;
- roles and contributions;
- components and site;
- sequence and transfers;
- integrity, interruption, failure, and aftermath.

Divine magic additionally owns:

- Divine Source;
- Domain and Jurisdiction;
- Petition, Mandate, Covenant, or divine law;
- source agency and response;
- Divine Access and Withdrawal;
- signs, Burdens, and divine consequence.

A rite can be technically perfect and receive refusal. A source can respond despite a flawed rite through a separate Miracle. Neither fact erases the ritual's actual Skills, errors, or consequences.

Sacred ceremony without magical effect remains socially meaningful but is not a Divine Rite under these rules.

## Intermediaries

An Intermediary may:

- translate communication;
- verify identity or office;
- carry a Petition;
- administer a Mandate;
- filter dangerous contact;
- witness terms;
- maintain a site or interface;
- distribute a bounded blessing;
- interpret Signs;
- contest, appeal, or terminate one route.

Intermediaries retain their own knowledge, bias, authority, incentives, vulnerability, and agency. They may misunderstand, deceive, disagree, become captured by institutions, lose Access, or act faithfully within narrow instructions.

An intermediary cannot grant more authority than it possesses. Destroying one does not necessarily harm the source; obeying one does not prove obedience to the source; replacing one does not preserve the same relationship automatically.

## Signs, Evidence, and Attribution

Divine evidence may include:

- source-specific Mana Traces;
- responses through authenticated channels;
- effects constrained by Domain or Covenant;
- repeatable answers under known conditions;
- direct perception through a valid sense;
- independent witnesses;
- records with provenance;
- an Intermediary whose authority can be tested;
- predictions or knowledge not otherwise accessible;
- consequences tied to breach or withdrawal.

Keep separate:

- world truth;
- source claim;
- intermediary statement;
- doctrine;
- institutional ruling;
- public belief;
- observed effect;
- inferred motive;
- player knowledge;
- character knowledge.

Signs may be forged, imitated, intercepted, mistranslated, displaced, outdated, or produced by another magical source. A genuine Sign can still be misunderstood. A spectacular display is not stronger evidence of moral truth than a quiet authenticated response.

## Source Agency, Consent, and Ethics

Person-like Divine Sources may choose, refuse, negotiate, change priorities, make mistakes, deceive, disagree, suffer, or be constrained. Power does not remove personhood.

Affected people also retain agency. A god cannot consent on behalf of a patient, sacrifice, community, Weapon Soul, monster, child, prisoner, deceased person, or future incarnation merely by claiming Domain or ownership.

Divine relationships can contain coercion because of unequal power, dependency, threats, doctrine, or control of essential resources. The rules must expose that coercion rather than translating it into voluntary devotion.

A source demanding blood, pain, death, humiliation, forced conversion, memory loss, Soul access, or harm to others gains no privileged efficiency. The demand remains a choice with victims and consequences; cruelty is not a superior magical fuel.

Characters may reject, leave, renegotiate, expose, resist, or oppose a Divine Source where causally possible. Consequences may be severe without removing that agency.

## Costs, Burdens, and Consequences

Divine magic may depend on:

- Mana supplied by source, petitioner, environment, Ritual, Host, or another valid relation;
- time, attention, travel, preparation, tools, or site;
- duty, service, guardianship, testimony, restitution, or stewardship;
- observance needed to preserve a Covenant route;
- vulnerability to source perception or enemies;
- public expectations and institutional scrutiny;
- conflict among Divine Sources or communities;
- ecological effects of intervention;
- debt, responsibility, or promises;
- limits on use rather than payment for use.

No universal favor currency converts prayer, worship, offerings, sacrifice, pain, service, or obedience into power. A source may value particular acts, but the relationship remains qualitative, specific, and agentive.

Burden does not prove authenticity or worth. An abusive demand can be burdensome and false; a generous source can provide help without hidden payment while still facing real limits and consequences.

## Embodiment, Species, and Monsters

Divine Access does not bypass embodiment.

Assess:

- senses and channels through which contact occurs;
- organs, body plan, lifecycle, and physical tolerances;
- language, cognition, memory, and communication;
- species-native ritual and social forms;
- environmental requirements;
- whether the effect targets body, Soul, territory, relationship, or source;
- vulnerability, overload, recovery, and adaptation;
- whether current form can perform the authorized action.

A god recognizing a former human priest now incarnated as a slime does not create lungs for hymns, hands for gestures, or human social office. The Divine Relationship may persist or be renewed while expression must be rebuilt through the slime's actual body.

Monsters may be worshippers, priests, divine rivals, protected species, sacred ancestors, Intermediaries, or Divine Sources under valid local rules. Human religious structure is not the default template.

Divine Alteration may influence a body only through [Species Development](../progression/SPECIES_DEVELOPMENT.md), [Mutations](../monster-evolution/MUTATIONS.md), [Hybridization](../monster-evolution/HYBRIDIZATION.md), or [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/MONSTER_EVOLUTION.md) as appropriate. Blessing, repeated worship, and divine ancestry do not purchase an Evolved Form.

## Skills, Classes, and Professions

Relevant Skills may include:

- doctrine and source history;
- Petition composition;
- communication and interpretation;
- Divine Rite roles;
- source-specific magical control;
- sign authentication;
- mediation and negotiation;
- pastoral care;
- medicine or protection using divine support;
- Covenant analysis;
- ethical review and affected-party advocacy;
- resistance, containment, and safe withdrawal;
- investigation of fraud and hostile attribution.

Skill cannot compel a Divine Response. Access cannot install Skill. A miracle cannot grant professional judgment.

A priest, oracle, saint, divine knight, shrine keeper, healer, covenant advocate, monster shaman, or similar label may be a Class, Profession, office, reputation, Soul Title, or cultural role. Each owner remains separate.

Classes organize current role. Professions own sustained responsible practice. Institutions own office and recognition. Divine magic owns source relationship and effect. One does not prove the others.

## Interactions with Other Magic

### Mana

[Mana](MANA.md) owns source, state, movement, storage, conversion, depletion, and ecology. Divine provision remains a Mana Source with rate, Access, Capacity, loss, renewal, competition, and consequence as established. A god is not an infinite reservoir by label.

### Magical Affinities

[Magical Affinity](MAGICAL_AFFINITIES.md) may affect compatibility with one Divine Source, Domain, law, method, or effect through an exact Basis and Expression. Affinity neither proves divine approval nor compels consent, response, or restored Mandate.

### Spells

A divine-sourced [Spell](SPELL_FORMATION.md) remains a bounded spell procedure. Divine magic owns source and authority; Spell Formation owns Pattern, Targeting Relation, formation lifecycle, sustainment, termination, and spell failure.

### Rituals

[Rituals](RITUALS.md) own Divine Rite Structure and performance. Sacred symbolism does not replace a valid source relation, and source response does not give every participant the Ritual's Skills.

### Enchanting

A Divine Source may supply, authorize, inspect, protect, or alter one [Enchantment](ENCHANTING.md). The Enchantment remains Host-bound with Pattern, Anchor, Interface, Supply, maintenance, Drift, and failure. Consecration by name does not create a permanent divine Layer.

### Alchemy

[Alchemy](ALCHEMY.md) may process materials produced, altered, or authorized by a Divine Source only through actual Reagent Properties and provenance. Holy labels do not bottle authority, personhood, blessing, Domain, or favor.

## Soul-System Interactions

### Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may align a soul with a Divine Source, Domain, sacred event, Covenant, place, or relationship under an exact Basis and Target. Resonance does not grant Mandate, compel response, prove doctrine, or make the source's power internal.

### Soul Echoes and Retained Instincts

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may remember former relationships, rites, doctrine, signs, mistakes, and divine encounters. [Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) may orient attention toward familiar forms of contact or danger.

They cannot recreate current Access, source consent, office, embodiment, or Practised Reliability. An Echo claiming to speak for a god remains an Echo unless a valid present source channel is independently established.

### Soul Titles

A [Soul Title](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may be recognized by a Divine Source where its Title Basis is relevant. That recognition may shape reactions, opportunities, symbolic authority, or a new relationship; it does not create a blessing, priestly office, Mandate, or obedience.

Divine declaration alone cannot manufacture a Soul Title. A title such as **Beloved of the Dawn** may record identity-forming deeds even after the dawn god withdraws support.

### Soul Depth and Soul Avatars

[Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) may support integration of complex divine relationships and moral conflict without becoming divine Capacity or worth. A [Soul Avatar](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) may synthesize accessible religious perspectives but cannot vote away current agency, speak as a Divine Source, or bypass its consent.

### Soul Constellations

[Soul Constellations](../soul/SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md) may include repeated relationships among worshippers, rivals, Intermediaries, or a reincarnating Divine Source where their own rules permit. A Constellation does not create destiny, ownership, Covenant, or compulsory reunion.

### Akashic Archive

[Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) Records may preserve signs, covenants, miracles, doctrines, breaches, and source history. Records can remain incomplete, biased, forged, or misread. Archive access does not compel a source, restore a Mandate, or make one doctrine canonical.

## Soul Weapons

A Weapon Soul is a person and may hold its own Divine Relationship.

Divine magic may:

- recognize or communicate with a Weapon Soul;
- bless or burden a Weapon Vessel through a bounded effect;
- supply a source to one Soul Weapon Function;
- participate in Awakening circumstances without causing automatic Awakening;
- support a compatible Weapon Evolution Route;
- witness or negotiate a Passage Accord;
- heal or harm through an explicit Soul-safe route.

It does not:

- own the Weapon Soul;
- consent for it or its Bonded Wielder;
- manufacture Soul Intertwining;
- install personality, loyalty, Echoes, or inherited mastery;
- turn an external blessing into Weapon Evolution by repetition;
- make a holy weapon automatically a Soul Weapon;
- command manifestation without an established Manifestation Route.

Divine Source Integration into [Weapon Evolution](../soul-weapons/WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md) retains source, permission, maintenance, limits, withdrawal, counters, and consequence. The Weapon Soul may refuse even when the wielder or institution accepts.

## Reincarnation and World Resets

At Final Death:

- divine offices, institutions, property, relics, sites, legal authority, public reputation, and current body effects remain in the world;
- Mandates, Covenants, Blessings, protections, obligations, and relationships persist only according to their own terms and source recognition;
- no god is compelled to identify, contact, accept, punish, or empower the next incarnation;
- legitimate doctrine, source knowledge, ethical judgment, and bounded Skills may persist through their established Imprints;
- Soul Echoes and Archive Records may preserve encounters and evidence;
- Practised Reliability, present communication, embodiment, and social role must be rebuilt.

A source may recognize a reincarnated soul through an actual soul-sensitive route. Recognition is not restoration. The source and incarnation may renew, renegotiate, reject, grieve, oppose, or redefine the relationship.

World Resets may change divine law, Jurisdiction, available Domains, access routes, source condition, pantheons, institutions, memories, and ecology. A former god may be absent, transformed, divided, forgotten, diminished, replaced, or newly constrained without erasing the soul's history.

## Conflict and Counterplay

Divine magic can be opposed through its actual dependencies, including:

- denying or leaving Jurisdiction;
- contesting Mandate or Covenant interpretation;
- protecting or removing a target relation;
- interrupting a Divine Rite;
- blocking communication or exposing a false Intermediary;
- disrupting Mana Supply or a Host;
- invoking another valid authority;
- appealing to the same source through a recognized route;
- presenting evidence, fulfilling remedy, or renegotiating terms;
- resisting the effect through body, Soul, affinity, Skill, environment, or countermeasure;
- exposing doctrine, fraud, coercion, or institutional capture;
- accepting a cost the source expected to deter action;
- persuading the source or affected community.

Counterplay is not always combat. Law, theology, diplomacy, migration, secrecy, care, repair, evidence, collective action, and refusal can matter.

No mortal automatically loses because an effect is divine. No god automatically loses because a skeptic disbelieves. Resolve the actual relation.

## Failure

### Source Misidentification

The claimed god, law, Intermediary, Sign, or response has another source or none.

### Domain Failure

The source lacks the relevant Domain, or the attempted effect assumes authority the Domain does not provide.

### Jurisdiction Failure

The relationship does not apply to this place, participant, condition, Age, or circumstance.

### Access Failure

The current route is absent, damaged, misunderstood, revoked, or incompatible with embodiment.

### Mandate Failure

Authority is exceeded, expired, suspended, contested, forged, or applied outside its terms.

### Petition Failure

The intended source does not receive, understand, accept, or answer the request as expected.

### Interpretation Failure

A Sign, doctrine, dream, intermediary statement, or silence is misunderstood.

### Formation Failure

The divine source relation is valid, but the Spell, Ritual, Enchantment, Alchemical Process, target, or bodily expression fails under its owner.

### Relationship Failure

Trust, consent, Covenant, obligation, or communication breaks down.

### Withdrawal

The source or law reduces or ends a permission, effect, protection, or route.

### Overreach

The recipient attempts scale, duration, precision, targets, or control beyond current Capacity, source agreement, or Practised Reliability.

Failure may create injury, debt, conflict, doctrinal crisis, public harm, source retaliation, lost access, ecological consequence, false accusation, or no effect. It does not award affinity, favor, Skill, Development, resistance, Title, or renewed Mandate automatically.

## Divine Claim Procedure

When divine magic matters:

1. identify the claimed Divine Source and distinguish world truth from belief;
2. establish personhood, agency, evidence, Domain, Jurisdiction, and uncertainty;
3. identify participants, affected parties, intermediaries, institutions, and consent;
4. define the Divine Relationship, Access route, Mandate, Covenant, or law;
5. identify the exact requested or initiated effect and its owning procedure;
6. resolve Mana, source, authority, embodiment, targeting, resistance, cost, and Capacity;
7. preserve Spell, Ritual, Enchantment, Alchemy, Skill, Profession, Species, Soul, and Weapon ownership;
8. distinguish Petition, Response, Sign, Silence, doctrine, and interpretation;
9. resolve failure, counterplay, withdrawal, Burden, and aftermath at the correct layer;
10. propagate institutional, cultural, ecological, political, and historical consequences;
11. award Development only for capability meaningfully practised and integrated;
12. apply Final Death, Reincarnation, and World Reset according to each owner's persistence;
13. store current gods, relationships, mandates, signs, blessings, and profiles outside canon.

## Safeguards

### No Worship Currency

Prayer, praise, service, offerings, sacrifice, pain, loyalty, and follower counts do not convert through a universal favor meter or guarantee response.

### No Divine Omnipotence by Label

A Divine Source acts only through established Domain, Jurisdiction, routes, Capacity, agency, and world law. Divine status is not unlimited capability or knowledge.

### No Moral Proof

A genuine miracle, blessing, mandate, or god does not make every doctrine, institution, intermediary, command, or action true or good.

### No Compulsory Faith

Nonbelief is viable, worship is not required for every Divine Relationship, and no character must accept a god, Covenant, blessing, or religious identity to remain playable.

### No Automatic Response

Petition, ritual precision, sacrifice, desperation, seniority, ancestry, affinity, Title, and repeated use do not compel a Divine Source.

### No Skill Replacement

Access, Mandate, Blessing, and Miracle do not grant magical control, rite performance, medicine, leadership, interpretation, or professional judgment.

### No Soul Ownership

Divine Domain, creation claims, worship, Covenant, rescue, punishment, prophecy, and power do not grant ownership of a Soul or consent to Soul Intrusion.

### No Agency Override

Gods, institutions, doctrine, intermediaries, and prophecies cannot choose deliberate player action, consent for affected persons, or force identity without an explicit hostile control effect and its safeguards.

### No Divine Evolution Purchase

Blessing, bloodline, worship, sacrifice, and divine favor do not bypass Species Development, Mutation, Hybridization, Monster Evolution, or Weapon Evolution.

### No Infinite Provision

Divine Mana and support retain source rate, Access, Capacity, competition, recovery, withdrawal, ecology, and consequence.

### No Harm Dividend

Martyrdom, punishment, self-injury, sacrifice, persecution, and survival do not automatically grant favor, affinity, Development, resistance, Title, or stronger miracles.

### No Reincarnated Entitlement

Former office, Mandate, relationship, blessing, service, and miracle do not compel recognition or restored Access in a new life.

### No Campaign Pantheon in Canon

Current gods, doctrines, temples, mandates, covenants, blessings, signs, conflicts, and populated Divine Profiles belong in external Campaign Records.

## Worked Examples

### The Healer Whose Mandate Was Revoked

A temple healer retains medical Skill, doctrine, and experience but loses a Mandate after publicly opposing the deity's demand to deny care to outsiders.

The healer can still diagnose, organize care, use non-divine medicine, petition, appeal, leave, or seek another route. Revocation removes defined divine Access; it does not erase competence, force repentance, or prove the deity morally right.

### A Miracle Without a Priest

A skeptical ferryman turns back during a storm to rescue strangers. A river god stills one current long enough for the boat to pass because guardianship of travelers lies within its Domain and it chooses to act.

The ferryman gains no priest Class, water affinity, reusable spell, or obligation by automatic rule. The event can begin a relationship, public dispute, or investigation if both parties later engage.

### Two Gods with Overlapping Domains

A city asks a harvest god to divert floodwater from fields while a river god protects the floodplain's spawning cycle. Both have relevant Domain; neither automatically outranks the other.

The resulting negotiation considers Jurisdiction, Covenants, ecology, affected communities, and available routes. A compromise protects seed stores while leaving part of the floodplain intact. Divine conflict remains causal rather than a contest of abstract god levels.

### The False Intermediary

An oracle orders costly offerings and claims silence proves the god's anger. Independent petition through an old authenticated shrine receives a warning that the oracle's Mandate ended years ago.

The oracle's social power and magical tricks remain real, but source authority does not. Evidence can expose institutional capture without making every former teaching false.

### A Monster Divine Rite

A distributed fungal colony petitions a subterranean growth source through synchronized chemical pulses and root contact. No spoken prayer, humanoid gesture, temple, or individual priest exists.

Ritual rules own synchronization and roles; divine rules own source response and Jurisdiction; species embodiment owns the colony's interfaces. Human religious form is irrelevant.

### A Blessing That Is Also a Burden

A guardian deity grants a scout the ability to sense breaches in one sanctuary boundary. The sense is painful near severe damage and makes concealment from the deity difficult.

The blessing is useful without becoming a stat bonus. It grants no general danger sense, combat mastery, moral authority, or permanent Soul trait. The scout may negotiate limits or reject renewal.

### Reincarnated Divine Champion

A former champion Reincarnates as a juvenile burrowing monster. A Soul Echo remembers battlefield prayers, and a Soul Title causes an old god to recognize the soul.

Recognition opens communication, not restoration. The new body cannot wield the old relic, the former Mandate ended with its office, and the god must negotiate with the current person. New Skills and embodiment-specific expression require current-life work.

### A Holy Sword Without a Weapon Soul

A temple sword bears a divine Enchantment that harms one hostile spirit under a Covenant. It shows no evidence of a dormant Weapon Soul.

The sword is a divine enchanted object, not automatically a Soul Weapon. If a Weapon Soul later Awakens, it remains a person whose consent and relationship with the god must be established separately.

### Divine Silence

A village petitions its storm goddess during a drought and receives no identifiable response. One priest calls it punishment, another suspects the old shrine lost Jurisdiction after a river changed course, and a farmer doubts the goddess exists.

Silence settles none of those claims. Investigation of Mana Traces, Covenant boundaries, ecology, records, and alternative water plans can continue without forcing a theological answer merely to move play forward.

### Refusing Rescue

A god offers to stop a lethal curse by permanently binding the victim to temple service. The victim refuses.

The refusal stands. Allies may seek medicine, Ritual counterplay, negotiation, another source, or accept the risk. The GM does not make acceptance inevitable because the offer is powerful or benevolent by the god's standards.
