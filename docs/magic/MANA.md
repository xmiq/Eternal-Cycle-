# Mana

## Purpose

This document defines Mana as the shared natural foundation through which magical change occurs in Eternal Cycle. It explains what Mana is, how local Mana systems may differ, how Mana moves and changes, how bodies and sources mediate it, and how GMs preserve causal costs without turning Mana into generic MP.

Mana is one part of a magical claim. It does not replace [Magical Development](../progression/MAGICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md), [Skills](../skills/README.md), embodiment, authority, sources, or the world conditions that make a particular effect possible.

## Core Rule

**Mana is the world-side capacity and medium through which magic can produce change under the laws of a particular world, Age, source, and environment.**

Mana is natural even when it is rare, divine, dangerous, poorly understood, or accessible only through extraordinary conditions. Natural does not mean harmless, morally neutral in every use, universally measurable, or available to everyone.

Every magical effect must have an explicit **Mana Relation**. The effect may draw, generate, redirect, pattern, store, convert, release, dissipate, or indirectly invoke Mana through a source that performs the magical work. The practitioner need not personally hold or spend a reserve, but the effect still requires a causal route through Mana.

Mana is not:

- a Soul or material made from Souls;
- experience, Development, Skill XP, Soul Depth, or Soul Resonance;
- health, stamina, emotion, attention, or willpower, although a magical route may burden those things;
- spell slots, a second health bar, or a universal action budget;
- a replacement for Skills, Soul development, Human Classes, Monster Evolution, or Soul Weapons;
- a universal liquid, particle, element, or numerical pool;
- automatically interchangeable across worlds, sources, species, or magic systems;
- proof of Magical Access, control, affinity, authority, or reliable spell use;
- an explanation that waives source, embodiment, consent, cost, or consequence.

## Canonical Terms

### Mana

The world-side capacity and medium through which magical change can occur under local world laws. Mana is a shared canonical category, not a claim that every local system uses one substance, vocabulary, measurement, or conversion rule.

### Mana Relation

The specific way a magical effect encounters Mana, including its source, present state, route, transformation, burden, resulting state, and relevant consequences.

### Mana State

A qualitative description of Mana in a bounded context. A useful Mana State may distinguish availability, distribution, flow, pattern, stability, provenance, compatibility, renewal, load, and observable traces without combining them into one score.

### Mana Source

A being, process, place, body, object, relationship, or world condition that supplies, replenishes, concentrates, transforms, or makes a defined form of Mana available under stated rules.

### Mana Field

The local distribution and behavior of Mana across a bounded place, body, object, or other established domain.

### Mana Current

A sustained directional movement of Mana through a field, Channel, environment, network, or other valid route.

### Mana Reservoir

A bounded being, organ, object, place, structure, contract, or arrangement that can retain a defined form of Mana under local storage, capacity, leakage, access, and release rules.

### Mana Sink

A being, process, place, object, or condition that draws in, transforms, binds, disperses, or otherwise removes Mana from a defined local state.

### Mana Trace

Source-bounded evidence left by a magical process in Mana, matter, a body, a place, or another valid medium. A trace may support investigation without revealing complete identity, intent, method, or truth.

### Mana Conversion

The transformation of Mana from one established state, source relationship, or usable form into another through a valid route. Conversion retains provenance, requirements, loss, byproducts, limits, and incompatibilities.

### Mana Depletion

A meaningful reduction in accessible Mana, supply rate, renewal, or usable distribution within a bounded context. Depletion is not always total absence and does not erase unrelated sources.

### Mana Saturation

A condition in which a body, place, object, Channel, or environment approaches or exceeds its safe ability to receive, retain, process, or disperse the Mana currently present.

### Mana Distortion

A source-caused change in Mana behavior, pattern, compatibility, flow, or interaction. Distortion is descriptive rather than automatically corrupt, evil, contagious, permanent, or beneficial.

### Mana Context Profile

A qualitative campaign record for one bounded Mana context, including laws, source, state, routes, compatibility, renewal, burdens, evidence, uncertainty, and consequences. Live profiles belong outside this repository.

## One Category, Many Local Systems

**Mana** is a canonical umbrella term. It lets the rules discuss the world-side foundation of magic without declaring that all worlds possess the same substance or obey the same formula.

Different local systems may treat Mana as:

- an ambient field shaped by weather and geology;
- a substance produced or filtered by living organs;
- a current moving through roots, rivers, stars, or constructed networks;
- a pattern sustained by language, geometry, memory, relationship, or collective action;
- a resource made available by a god, spirit, territory, contract, or other source;
- a state change in matter or space that cannot be stored in a body;
- several incompatible phenomena that one culture has grouped under one word.

Two cultures calling a phenomenon **Mana** does not establish sameness. Two cultures using different words does not establish difference. Interoperability requires a scoped causal test, not vocabulary.

Local magic rules must state which Mana facts matter. They may use local measures, thresholds, diagrams, colors, sensations, or institutional classifications, but those descriptions remain purpose-bound. No local notation becomes a universal Mana scale.

## The Mana Relation

Whenever an effect is described as magical, establish the following facts.

1. **Effect:** What exact change is being attempted or maintained?
2. **World law:** Which current rule permits Mana to participate in that change?
3. **Source and provenance:** What supplies, renews, concentrates, authorizes access to, or otherwise makes the relevant Mana available?
4. **Present state:** What is known about availability, distribution, flow, pattern, stability, compatibility, and load?
5. **Route:** Through what Field, Current, Channel, organ, tool, contract, site, participant, or other path does Mana reach the process?
6. **Access and authority:** Who or what may use the route now, and which permission or consent is required?
7. **Embodied expression:** What can the present body, vessel, structure, or arrangement safely receive, regulate, and express?
8. **Patterning method:** Which learned, innate, constructed, negotiated, or source-owned process organizes Mana toward the effect?
9. **Burden and cost:** What is drawn down, strained, displaced, consumed, risked, owed, exposed, or made unavailable?
10. **Resulting state:** How are the source, Mana State, participants, tools, target, and environment changed afterward?
11. **Trace and evidence:** What could relevant observers detect, misread, conceal, or investigate?
12. **Failure and recovery:** What can interrupt the process, what remains afterward, and how can readiness or environment recover?

An unknown entry may remain unknown. The GM should preserve uncertainty rather than invent a false answer, but a missing fact cannot be treated as a free bypass.

## Qualitative Mana State

A Mana State is multidimensional. Use only the dimensions relevant to the claim.

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Availability** | Is relevant Mana presently available, and to whom or what? |
| **Distribution** | Where is it concentrated, diffuse, isolated, layered, or absent? |
| **Flow** | Is it still, circulating, directional, intermittent, turbulent, or blocked? |
| **Pattern** | What structure, rhythm, relation, or organization does it presently hold? |
| **Stability** | How readily does the current state persist through disturbance? |
| **Provenance** | Which sources, transformations, and prior uses shaped it? |
| **Compatibility** | Which routes, bodies, materials, methods, targets, and laws can presently interact with it? |
| **Renewal** | By what process, under what conditions, and at what practical pace can it be restored or replaced? |
| **Load** | What current pressure does it place on the receiving body, vessel, route, or environment? |
| **Evidence** | What signs exist, who can perceive them, and how certain is the interpretation? |

These dimensions do not aggregate. A Field may be abundant but inaccessible, stable but incompatible, renewable but slow, or easy to draw yet difficult to shape. A small stable Reservoir may be more useful for one precise task than a vast turbulent Current.

## Mana, Reserve, Capacity, and Access

These facts answer different questions.

| Fact | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Mana** | What world-side magical capacity or medium is involved? |
| **Magical Access** | Does this being or arrangement have a valid present route to participate? |
| **Magical Reserve** | What usable resource is presently available to this being, tool, place, contract, or group under one system? |
| **Magical Capacity** | What amount, rate, complexity, or pressure can the present route safely receive, hold, process, or express? |
| **Magical Control** | Can the practitioner regulate the process toward an intended state? |
| **Magical Authority** | Does a relevant source, law, person, territory, or contract permit the defined interaction? |

A Field can contain abundant Mana while a person has no Access. A body can have Access and no personal Reservoir. A Reservoir can be full while its damaged Channel has little safe Capacity. A god can grant authority without granting control. A trained practitioner can retain control principles while current Mana is unavailable.

Do not turn these distinctions into adjacent bars on one character sheet. Record only what the current magical claim needs.

## Sources, Renewal, and Agency

Mana does not appear because a character needs it. Every source has an established process and scope.

Possible sources include:

- world processes such as celestial cycles, weather, geology, tides, seasons, or dimensional boundaries;
- living bodies, colonies, ecosystems, symbioses, or species organs;
- prepared materials, constructed engines, reservoirs, or maintained networks;
- territories, sacred sites, ruins, dungeons, Gates, or other established environments;
- gods, spirits, Weapon Souls, collectives, ancestors, or other person-like beings;
- contracts, offices, oaths, relationships, or authorities whose owning rules provide a real route;
- transformations of another valid Mana state.

For each source, establish:

- what the source actually supplies;
- whether it creates, gathers, releases, converts, or merely permits access;
- its domain and boundaries;
- its renewal process and practical timescale;
- its dependencies, vulnerabilities, and competing demands;
- whether it has agency, interests, consent, or authority;
- what happens when demand exceeds supply or recovery.

A person-like source is not a fuel deposit. Consent, refusal, negotiation, obligation, coercion, deception, and withdrawal remain real. Magical Authority may permit one act without granting ownership of the source or its future choices.

## Fields, Currents, Reservoirs, and Sinks

These structures describe where Mana is and what it is doing. They are neither power tiers nor a mandatory taxonomy for every culture.

### Fields

A Mana Field may overlap other Fields, vary across space and time, and contain several patterns or provenances. A Field can support life, suppress another route, interfere with tools, carry traces, or remain inaccessible to most beings.

Boundaries may be gradual, moving, disputed, layered, or maintained. A map is evidence from a time and method, not permanent world truth.

### Currents

A Mana Current has direction and continuity. Drawing from it may alter downstream availability, create turbulence, expose the user to its provenance, or change the relationship between connected places.

Blocking or redirecting a Current is a world event, not a private resource adjustment. Effects may propagate beyond the immediate scene.

### Reservoirs

A Mana Reservoir must define:

- what form of Mana it holds;
- its boundary and storage mechanism;
- how Mana enters and leaves;
- safe Capacity and signs of Saturation;
- leakage, decay, contamination, or transformation where relevant;
- who has Access and authority;
- what happens when the vessel is damaged, moved, copied, or destroyed.

Storage does not erase provenance or create compatibility. A crystal containing storm Mana is not automatically usable by every caster, spell, Soul Weapon, or machine.

### Sinks

A Mana Sink may be protective, ecological, industrial, predatory, accidental, or destructive. It may disperse Mana harmlessly, bind it, transform it, route it elsewhere, feed a living process, or leave dangerous byproducts.

Calling something a Sink does not imply that Mana ceases to exist universally. State the bounded result and downstream effect.

## Transfer and Conversion

Mana can move or change only through an established route.

A transfer or conversion claim must identify:

- origin and destination;
- source provenance;
- compatible states and routes;
- the mechanism performing the work;
- rate, Capacity, and stability limits;
- losses, waste, delay, and byproducts;
- effects on source and destination;
- authority, consent, custody, and risk;
- what evidence survives the process.

Conversion is not a universal exchange desk. Fire-associated Mana, divine provision, biological reserve, stored ritual charge, a Soul Weapon's external source, and an ambient spatial Field may have no direct correspondence.

Where conversion is possible, it can still be asymmetric, lossy, narrow, slow, unstable, environmentally restricted, or dependent on specialized Skills and tools. Repeated conversion does not teach a body to produce the source or make the converted state permanently internal.

## Causal Accounting Without One Conservation Formula

Eternal Cycle does not impose one numerical conservation equation on every Age. Local laws may include creation, renewal, dissipation, conversion, cyclic return, divine supply, or effects that cultures cannot measure completely.

The shared rule is **causal accounting**:

- a magical change must have a valid source and Mana Relation;
- the route must be present and compatible;
- the actual burden must be carried by its owner;
- storage, conversion, and release cannot silently duplicate supply;
- a result must leave the source, route, participants, target, or environment in a causally consistent state;
- uncertainty in measurement does not mean unlimited availability;
- apparent creation requires an established source or world law rather than narrative convenience.

An effect may cost no personal reserve while consuming prepared material, drawing from a Current, using a god's provision, burdening a tool, displacing ambient Mana, requiring recovery, creating debt, or changing the environment. **No personal cost** is not **no cost**.

## Embodiment and Species

Bodies mediate Mana through their actual structures.

A current body may:

- sense only particular Mana patterns;
- produce or replenish one local Mana form;
- store Mana in a Magical Organ;
- channel ambient Mana without storing it;
- filter, convert, or disperse Mana;
- require a symbiont, colony, territory, lifecycle stage, diet, emotion, temperature, or other condition;
- lack direct Access while still supporting tool use, ritual participation, research, or resistance.

[Species Development](../progression/SPECIES_DEVELOPMENT.md) and [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) own anatomy, Species Traits, Mutations, and evolved forms. Mana may make a route possible, relevant, costly, or ecologically valuable, but exposure alone does not manufacture organs or complete Evolution.

Current health, maturation, injury, suppression, exhaustion, environment, and calibration govern expression. A Soul with extensive magical history cannot use a body as though its former Channels were still present.

## Perception, Measurement, and Traces

Mana can be observed only through a valid sense, Skill, instrument, source relation, or other route.

Distinguish:

- **detection:** noticing that a relevant signal exists;
- **discrimination:** separating patterns, sources, directions, or changes;
- **interpretation:** inferring what the evidence means;
- **attribution:** linking evidence to a source, method, event, or actor;
- **prediction:** estimating what the current state may do next.

Success at one does not guarantee the next. A Mana Trace may show that heat-associated shaping occurred without identifying the caster. A divine mark may prove a source acted without proving the recipient's motives. An old trace can be overwritten, mixed, forged, displaced, misunderstood, or changed by the environment.

Mana perception does not automatically reveal Souls, Soul Resonance, emotions, truth, morality, exact reserves, hidden Skills, or future actions. Those claims require their own owning rules.

## Depletion, Saturation, and Distortion

These are causal Mana States, not progression rewards or generic status effects.

### Depletion

Depletion may reduce available supply, slow recovery, fragment a Field, expose a dependency, force migration, alter magical ecology, or make one route inaccessible while another remains.

Recovery requires the actual renewal process. Waiting is sufficient only where time and current conditions genuinely restore the source.

### Saturation

Saturation occurs relative to the receiving system's current Capacity. Possible consequences include leakage, instability, interference, tissue or vessel strain, unwanted activation, ecological change, attraction of dependent species, or forced dissipation.

High ambient availability is not automatically beneficial. A saturated environment may be unusable or dangerous to a route designed for fine low-pressure work.

### Distortion

Distortion names changed Mana behavior or compatibility and must retain provenance. It may arise from interacting Fields, failed conversion, damaged infrastructure, a curse, a divine act, Monster Evolution, pollution, World Reset, or another established cause.

Do not assume Distortion is corruption, sentience, evil, improvement, mutation, or permanent damage. Resolve each claimed consequence through its owner.

Being depleted, saturated, distorted, overloaded, or injured does not grant Magical Development, affinity, resistance, Soul Depth, or Evolution merely because the event was intense.

## Mana and Ecology

Mana participates in the world rather than existing only for casters.

It may influence:

- habitats and migration;
- feeding, reproduction, dormancy, metamorphosis, and lifecycle timing;
- plants, monsters, spirits, fungi, constructed ecologies, and nonliving processes;
- territory, competition, symbiosis, predation, and disease;
- materials, weather, water, soil, ruins, dungeons, and settlement viability;
- which magical practices are sustainable in a place;
- which groups control, protect, study, extract, or contest a source.

Drawing Mana can affect other users and ecological functions. Protecting a source can also preserve harmful dependencies or unequal control. Mana abundance does not guarantee a healthy ecology, and depletion does not imply that every nonmagical organism fails.

Detailed population, resource, economy, migration, faction, and long-timescale simulation belongs to the later World Engine tasks. This document establishes only that Mana changes must enter world causality and cannot be reset after a scene for convenience.

## Souls and Reincarnation

Souls can interact with Mana without being made of Mana or functioning as permanent Mana reservoirs.

### Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may align a soul with a genuine source, place, pattern, method, relationship, or prior Mana context within a valid Basis and Target. It can support recognition, interpretation, or an existing Access route. It does not supply Mana, create affinity, restore a Reserve, or force a source to respond.

### Soul Echoes and Retained Instincts

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may remember Mana signs, hazards, methods, and former laws. [Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) may orient attention toward familiar cues. Neither performs present sensing, opens a Channel, transfers Mana, or guarantees that the old interpretation remains correct.

### Soul Titles

[Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may influence a Mana source or magical route only where that source or route has a specific receiving rule for the Title's identity and history. Symbolic recognition does not become generic fuel, affinity, authority, or control.

### Reincarnation

[Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) may preserve Magical Imprints, Skill Imprints, memory, and bounded source relationships under their own rules. It does not carry:

- personal or external Mana Reserves;
- active Fields, Currents, charges, wards, or prepared effects;
- former Magical Organs or Channels;
- sites, materials, tools, contracts, environmental supply, or authority;
- automatic compatibility with the new world's Mana;
- the former body's recovery or Capacity.

A new incarnation must establish a present Mana Relation. Retained history may accelerate safe inquiry and relearning, but it cannot make an incompatible world obey an old formula.

## Soul Weapons

A [Soul Weapon](../soul-weapons/README.md) may interact with Mana as a distinct person and vessel. A particular Soul Weapon may:

- possess a body or form that conducts, stores, filters, senses, or resists a defined Mana state;
- use an established magical source through its own Access and Skills;
- cooperate as a focus, participant, stabilizer, or observer;
- carry an enchantment or other source-owned effect once that system establishes it;
- provide a valid Manifestation Source only where the [Weapon Manifestation](../soul-weapons/WEAPON_MANIFESTATIONS.md) rules and source both permit it.

Awakening, Soul Intertwining, possession, symbolism, or Reincarnation does not make a Soul Weapon a generic Mana battery. The Weapon Soul's consent, the vessel's Capacity, the source's provenance, the wielder's Skills, and every external cost remain separate.

Mana cannot replace Vessel Continuity for non-solid or source-dependent Soul Weapons. Loss of a sustaining source may suppress expression or threaten a vessel according to its actual topology without automatically killing, duplicating, or converting the Weapon Soul into energy.

## Skills, Classes, and Professions

### Skills

The [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) owns learned capabilities used to sense, gather, channel, shape, convert, release, contain, diagnose, interrupt, or recover from Mana processes.

A Skill does not create Mana, Access, authority, anatomy, a source, or compatibility unless an owning Magic rule establishes that exact effect. [Adaptive Skills](../skills/ADAPTIVE_SKILLS.md) may emerge through repeated meaningful problem-solving with Mana, but not through passive exposure, reserve depletion, or a dramatic accident alone.

### Human Classes and Magical Schools

[Human Classes and Professions](../human/README.md) own social learning frameworks, work, institutions, recognition, responsibility, and authority established through those structures.

A [Magical School](../human/MAGICAL_SCHOOLS.md) may model Mana accurately, partially, locally, or incorrectly. Its curriculum may improve observation and practice, but school membership cannot grant a Mana source, Reserve, affinity, Access, or control.

### Monster Learning and Evolution

[Monster Skill Trees](../skills/MONSTER_SKILL_TREES.md) own learned monster-native methods. [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) owns whole-form reorganization and magical anatomy. Mana ecology may create pressure, opportunity, or route relevance without awarding a Skill or Evolution.

## Nonmagical Capability and Counterplay

Mana does not make magic universally superior.

Magical routes can be limited by:

- unavailable or incompatible Mana;
- narrow Access or authority;
- preparation, timing, attention, and recovery;
- bodily, tool, source, or environmental dependencies;
- visible traces and social consequences;
- suppression, insulation, grounding, interruption, distance, deception, or terrain;
- targets for which the effect is poorly suited;
- skilled nonmagical action before, during, or after magical expression.

A nonmagical path remains viable. Tools, alliances, tactics, law, ecology, stealth, craft, and ordinary physical action can exploit magical dependencies or accomplish objectives without magic.

No species, Class, Profession, Skill Tree, Soul Weapon, or protagonist is required to use Mana directly.

## Failure and Recovery

Mana-related failure should identify its causal layer.

Possible failures include:

- no relevant Mana is available;
- Access, authority, or consent is absent or revoked;
- the route is blocked, damaged, overloaded, or incompatible;
- the body or vessel lacks safe Capacity;
- the practitioner misreads a Field or Trace;
- a Current changes or a Reservoir leaks;
- conversion produces waste, delay, instability, or the wrong state;
- several Fields interfere;
- a source refuses, weakens, moves, or serves a competing demand;
- Saturation forces discharge or harms a receiving system;
- Depletion changes ecology or recovery;
- Distortion makes a formerly reliable method unsafe;
- the local world law differs from the practitioner's model.

Recovery may require rest, repair, renewed supply, environmental restoration, migration, source consent, contract renewal, medical care, recalibration, material replacement, dissipation, containment, or acceptance that the route no longer functions.

Failure does not automatically grant resistance, insight, affinity, a stronger Reserve, a new Skill, Monster Evolution, Soul Depth, Soul Resonance, or permission to repeat the attempt without consequence.

## Safeguards

### No Generic MP

Do not collapse Mana, Reserve, Capacity, Access, control, recovery, health, attention, or authority into one bar. Local measures may exist only for a stated system and purpose.

### No Unlimited Mana

Uncertain measurement, ambient abundance, divine provision, a large Field, a Soul Weapon, a ritual site, or a renewable source does not mean infinite usable supply. Rate, Access, Capacity, compatibility, competing demand, recovery, and consequence still matter.

### No Soul Fuel by Default

A Soul, Echo, Title, Bond, Soul Weapon, or Soul Depth cannot be spent or converted into Mana unless a later explicit high-consequence rule establishes a narrow effect consistent with Soul safeguards. Descriptive sacrifice is not sufficient.

### No Source Laundering

Drawing through a god, spirit, artifact, environment, contract, institution, participant, Soul Weapon, or other source does not make its Mana, authority, consent, or capability internal and permanent.

### No Universal Conversion

Shared names, similar effects, common colors, school doctrine, or a converter label do not make Mana states interchangeable. Every conversion requires a receiving rule and retains loss, byproducts, limits, and provenance.

### No Exposure Farming

Living near Mana, repeatedly emptying a Reserve, enduring Saturation, suffering Distortion, surviving overload, consuming magical material, or witnessing powerful effects does not automatically create Development, affinity, resistance, Skills, Evolution, or Soul growth.

### No Automatic Refill

Recovery follows the source's real renewal process. A new scene, rest period, level-up, Reincarnation, or GM convenience does not restore Mana by default.

### No Mandatory Magic

Magic is one family of paths among many. Encounters, societies, and progression must preserve meaningful nonmagical routes, counters, costs, and opportunities.

### No Omniscient Detection

Mana perception and traces remain scoped evidence. They do not reveal all magic, Souls, identity, intent, morality, truth, exact capability, or future action.

### No Campaign Data in Canon

Named current Fields, live Reservoir amounts, active depletion, discovered traces, current sources, campaign contracts, character reserves, and changing Mana ecology belong in external Campaign Records.

## GM Procedure

When Mana matters:

1. state the exact magical effect or Mana claim;
2. identify the current world, Age, local laws, and relevant system;
3. establish the Mana Relation and source provenance;
4. describe only the relevant Mana State dimensions;
5. separate Mana, Access, Reserve, Capacity, control, affinity, and authority;
6. identify embodiment, Channels, organs, tools, sites, participants, and environmental conditions;
7. establish person-like source agency, consent, obligations, and refusal where relevant;
8. apply learned methods through the Skill Engine and social support through its actual owner;
9. account for draw, transfer, conversion, waste, strain, displacement, debt, and other burdens once;
10. resolve interference, Depletion, Saturation, Distortion, traces, and failure at the correct layer;
11. propagate effects into ecology, society, institutions, and later world causality at an appropriate scale;
12. preserve retained knowledge without carrying Reserves, anatomy, sites, or world laws through Reincarnation;
13. communicate evidence and uncertainty rather than a universal Mana score;
14. store all live state in the external Campaign Record.

## Worked Examples

### Breath-Channel Practitioner

A human tradition draws a thin ambient Current through breath-shaped Channels. The practitioner has no large internal Reservoir; breath rhythm regulates rate and pressure while the surrounding Field supplies the Mana.

Holding the breath interrupts Access. Smoke reduces control. A calm expert can produce a precise effect with little draw, while a panicked novice Saturates the airway and leaks Mana into nearby metal. Skill, ambient availability, body Capacity, and result remain separate facts.

### Rune-Web Spider

A spider monster's spinnerets produce silk that temporarily holds spatially patterned Mana. The surrounding Field supplies most of the Mana, the silk acts as a short-lived Reservoir, and the creature's vibration sense provides feedback.

Cutting anchor strands changes the pattern and releases the stored Mana irregularly. A human can study the geometry but cannot reproduce the biological filtering without another valid route. Exposure to the web does not grant spinnerets, affinity, or a monster Skill Tree.

### River Compact

A community has authority to redirect a river's Mana Current during floods under a compact with a person-like river spirit. The spirit supplies permission and guidance; surveyed stones shape the route; trained participants maintain boundaries.

The effect does not come from each participant's personal Reserve. Drawing too much can deprive downstream spawning grounds and cause the spirit to withdraw consent. The compact, participants, infrastructure, ecology, and Mana Current all retain separate ownership.

### Soul Weapon as Channel, Not Battery

An awakened spear has an established form that conducts one storm-associated Mana state safely. Its bonded wielder has learned timing and targeting, while the Weapon Soul controls whether it opens the conducting route.

The spear does not produce storms, store unlimited charge, or grant the wielder affinity. Clear weather removes the usual source. A prepared external Reservoir might support a narrow use only if compatible, and the Weapon Soul may refuse unsafe Saturation.

### Reborn Storm Adept

A former storm adept reincarnates in an underground species whose body senses mineral Fields but lacks the former atmospheric Channels. The soul remembers pressure signs, conversion errors, and storm-pattern theory.

Those Imprints help the incarnation investigate mineral currents and reject dangerous analogies. They do not carry storm Mana, create an internal Reserve, or make rock currents interchangeable with weather. A new practice may eventually emerge through current-life Access and training.

### Overdrawn Spring

A settlement's healing craft depends on a spring where Mana renewal follows seasonal groundwater and a cooperative root network. Repeated extraction exceeds renewal.

The spring still contains water, but its usable Mana becomes depleted and uneven. Healing work grows unreliable, root-feeding monsters migrate, and institutions dispute access. Restoring the source requires ecological repair and time, not a night's rest or a caster refilling a bar.

### Artificial Converter

An artificial body uses a core that converts geothermal Mana into a form its constructed Channels can regulate. Conversion creates waste heat and mineral residue, and the core loses stability when moved away from compatible geology.

Installing a larger core increases possible throughput but not the user's perception, control, or judgment. Copying the shell without the conversion structure produces no usable Mana route.

### Nonmagical Counterplay

A Mana-dependent aerial predator tracks active Fields but has poor ordinary night vision. A nonmagical hunter avoids enchanted gear, studies the creature's feeding route, uses terrain to break its dive, and damages the mineral node that stabilizes its wing Channels.

The hunter wins through knowledge, preparation, tools, and matchup. The result does not prove universal superiority over the predator, and it requires no hidden anti-magic statistic.
