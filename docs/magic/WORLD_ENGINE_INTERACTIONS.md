# Magic and the World Engine

## Purpose

This document defines how established magical causes enter the [World Engine](../world-engine/WORLD_ENGINE_OVERVIEW.md) as persistent, bounded consequences. It connects the completed Magic frameworks to world simulation without defining Phase 8's detailed variables, domain procedures, campaign state, or event generators.

Magic is part of the world. A spell, Ritual, Enchantment, Alchemical Process, Divine Response, Mana change, affinity pattern, restriction, or magical institution can affect ecology and society only through its actual source, route, scale, duration, distribution, dependencies, responses, and aftermath.

## Core Rule

Every magical world effect begins with a valid specialist Magic claim and enters ordinary causality.

> Established magical cause -> direct change -> affected beings, structures, and relationships -> responses and counterforces -> delayed and distributed consequences -> recovery, adaptation, persistence, or further change.

Magic does not skip this chain because it is powerful, mysterious, divine, ancient, forbidden, or narratively important. It cannot create unbounded resources, erase affected parties, guarantee adoption, solve every domain, or disappear after a scene without a causal reason.

The World Engine does not create magical capability. It receives established magical facts, simulates their consequences at the necessary resolution, and returns changed world conditions to the owning systems.

## Canonical Terms

### Magic-World Claim

A bounded causal claim connecting one established magical change to possible world consequences under a stated place, population, timescale, resolution, evidence base, and uncertainty.

### Magic-World Profile

A qualitative external Campaign Record for one Magic-World Claim, including its cause, direct effects, footprint, distribution, dependencies, responses, delays, feedback, persistence, recovery, evidence, and uncertainty.

### Magical Footprint

The complete set of inputs, routes, sites, labor, authority, maintenance, traces, burdens, waste, obligations, and affected relationships required or left by a bounded magical arrangement.

### Magical Dependency

A condition in which a world function relies on continued magical supply, access, relationship, infrastructure, knowledge, labor, permission, or maintenance.

### Magical Infrastructure

A durable world-bound arrangement of sources, sites, tools, Enchantments, Ritual Structures, Alchemical Processes, institutions, trained roles, records, and ordinary material support used to provide a magical function.

### Magical Adoption

The causal process through which a magical practice or service becomes repeated, distributed, taught, supplied, trusted, governed, and integrated into ordinary activity.

### Magical Displacement

A shift of activity, cost, risk, labor, extraction, pollution, conflict, or dependence from one being, group, place, method, or time to another rather than a true removal of that burden.

### Magical Lock-In

A condition in which sustained reliance on a magical arrangement weakens alternatives, concentrates expertise or authority, raises switching costs, or makes disruption unusually consequential.

### Magical Externality

A benefit, burden, risk, trace, obligation, or lasting change borne by beings, places, sources, or future participants outside the actors who chose the magical action.

### Recovery Horizon

The qualitative sequence of conditions, actions, and time required for an affected world function to recover, reorganize, or reach a different viable state. It is not a universal countdown.

### Magical Legacy Condition

A world-bound effect that remains after the active magical operation ends, such as altered ecology, residue, infrastructure, law, debt, knowledge, dependency, damaged trust, changed habitat, or an ongoing source relationship.

## The Magic-World Chain

### 1. Establish the Magical Cause

Name the exact event or continuing arrangement and apply its specialist rules first.

Examples include:

- sustained draw from one Mana Current;
- operation of an irrigation Enchantment network;
- repeated use of a healing Spell Procedure;
- a seasonal weather Ritual;
- industrial demand for one Reagent;
- withdrawal by a Divine Source;
- enforcement of one Magic Restriction;
- a change in the available magically trained workforce.

**Magic increased**, **the gods intervened**, **a great ritual occurred**, and **forbidden energy spread** are not complete causes.

### 2. Identify the Direct Change

State what the magic changes before inferring broader consequences.

A healing spell may reduce one kind of injury when delivered in time. It does not directly increase population. An irrigation Enchantment may move water under defined Supply conditions. It does not directly create food security. A restriction may increase the legal risk of a procedure. It does not directly remove the underlying hazard or erase demand.

### 3. Account for the Footprint

Identify relevant:

- Mana Source, route, state, renewal, and byproducts;
- bodies, Channels, Skills, Development, and recovery;
- tools, Hosts, Reagents, sites, transport, and ordinary materials;
- labor, instruction, coordination, records, and maintenance;
- authority, consent, contracts, Jurisdictions, and source agency;
- waste, Residue, traces, Drift, degradation, and failure;
- opportunity costs and displaced alternatives.

An effect with no personal Reserve cost may still require a river compact, weather, a living source, infrastructure, political permission, repair crews, and downstream ecological capacity.

### 4. Name Affected Parties and Distribution

Ask who receives the intended benefit, who can reach it, who controls it, who supplies it, who bears its risks, who is excluded, and who may respond.

Distribution may differ by:

- geography and transport;
- species and embodiment;
- affinity and source compatibility;
- wealth and social position;
- institutional membership or legal status;
- time, season, lifecycle, and emergency conditions;
- language, knowledge, trust, doctrine, or secrecy;
- source consent and political control.

An average benefit cannot erase concentrated harm. A prosperous capital may depend on depleted rural sources, coerced spirits, dangerous Reagent extraction, or neglected nonmagical systems.

### 5. Resolve Responses and Counterforces

People, monsters, sources, institutions, ecosystems, and opponents react according to their information, goals, capabilities, relationships, and constraints.

Possible responses include:

- adoption, refusal, imitation, regulation, sabotage, theft, or reform;
- migration, adaptation, dormancy, substitution, or predation;
- price changes, new work, unemployment, hoarding, smuggling, or monopoly;
- alliance, conflict, negotiation, worship, protest, or enforcement;
- defensive Skills, counterspells, changed tactics, and nonmagical alternatives;
- source refusal, altered terms, withdrawal, retaliation, or cooperation.

The first-order effect is not the final outcome.

### 6. Propagate Through Time

Consider:

- **delay**, where consequences appear after the cause;
- **accumulation**, where individually small effects combine;
- **threshold**, where a relationship changes after enough pressure;
- **feedback**, where a consequence strengthens or weakens its cause;
- **seasonality**, where timing changes effect and recovery;
- **path dependence**, where earlier choices shape later options;
- **substitution**, where another method or source takes over;
- **rebound**, where efficiency or abundance increases total use;
- **legacy**, where consequences outlive active operation.

These are causal patterns, not universal numerical mechanics. The relevant Phase 8 domain will later define any detailed state variables or procedures.

### 7. Determine Persistence and Recovery

When the magical operation stops, resolve what stops with it and what remains.

Possible outcomes include:

- immediate cessation;
- gradual dissipation;
- maintenance-dependent continuation;
- ecological or institutional recovery;
- migration or replacement;
- persistent damage, residue, law, obligation, or memory;
- reorganization into a new viable state;
- irreversible loss within the relevant timescale.

Removal of the cause does not guarantee restoration of the prior world. Recovery requires the actual routes, resources, actors, and time appropriate to each affected domain.

## Scale, Resolution, and Aggregation

Use the [World Engine's resolution](../world-engine/WORLD_ENGINE_OVERVIEW.md#simulation-resolution) appropriate to current decisions:

| Resolution | Magic-world focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Immediate** | direct effect, exposure, interruption, failure, witnesses, traces, and urgent response over moments to days; |
| **Local** | service access, maintenance, site ecology, settlement behavior, institutions, prices, and nearby migration over weeks to years; |
| **Regional** | networks, source competition, trade, population movement, warfare, ecological adaptation, and political change over years to decades; |
| **Epochal** | magical infrastructure, institutional dependence, species distributions, knowledge systems, source relationships, and Age transitions over decades to centuries. |

Resolution changes detail, not causality. Do not simulate every lamp in a city, but preserve what makes the lighting network possible and what happens if its supply, maintenance, authority, or adoption changes.

Repeated similar actions may be aggregated when their sources, users, conditions, and effects are sufficiently alike. Preserve rare failures, unequal distribution, source agency, bottlenecks, and threshold behavior that could change decisions. Averages cannot silently erase a vulnerable district, minority species, seasonal collapse, or catastrophic tail risk.

## Adoption and Scale

A working magical effect does not automatically become a world system.

Magical Adoption may require:

- repeatable Access and compatible users;
- teachable Skills and sufficient Development;
- stable sources, components, sites, tools, and transport;
- safe operation, maintenance, repair, and failure response;
- records, standards, verification, and trusted institutions;
- source consent, legal permission, or negotiated authority;
- economic demand and an acceptable distribution of costs;
- cultural meaning and public confidence;
- compatibility with existing infrastructure and alternatives.

Adoption can be partial, contested, geographically limited, seasonal, species-specific, underground, or reversed. One prodigy, artifact, miracle, Soul Weapon, secret Recipe, or successful demonstration does not establish scalable access.

### Infrastructure and Maintenance

Magical Infrastructure is a collective world arrangement rather than a personal power.

Its performance may depend on:

- source throughput and renewal;
- trained operators and noncasting roles;
- Host condition, Enchantment Drift, and spare materials;
- Ritual schedules, offices, sites, and succession;
- Reagent harvest, storage, testing, and waste handling;
- contracts, public trust, law, and institutional continuity;
- ordinary roads, food, water, shelter, tools, and labor;
- emergency procedures and nonmagical fallback capacity.

Infrastructure may outperform every individual involved while remaining fragile to correlated failure. Reincarnation does not carry it as inventory, and the death of one expert need not destroy it when knowledge and roles are genuinely distributed.

### Dependency, Lock-In, and Alternatives

Magical Dependency is not inherently good or bad. It becomes important when interruption changes survival, production, authority, mobility, defense, or identity.

Magical Lock-In may develop when:

- older methods are abandoned;
- source control concentrates;
- compatible bodies or affinities receive structural privilege;
- institutions suppress alternatives;
- training becomes too specialized to transfer;
- settlement placement assumes continued magic;
- ecological systems reorganize around magical supply;
- switching requires time, scarce knowledge, or dangerous transition.

Preserve alternative magical and nonmagical routes where causally supported. Nonmagical methods are not obsolete merely because magic exists, and magical methods are not inferior merely because they create dependencies.

## Distribution, Power, and Agency

Magic changes social power only through actual control, access, belief, enforcement, relationships, and response.

Ask:

- who owns or claims the source, site, Host, Recipe, Pattern, record, or office;
- whose consent is required and whose consent is ignored;
- who verifies safety and whose evidence is trusted;
- who can withdraw labor, access, recognition, or cooperation;
- who receives protection and who is exposed;
- whether affected beings can appeal, negotiate, migrate, resist, or create alternatives;
- whether a claimed public benefit depends on exploitation or secrecy;
- how misinformation, doctrine, fear, and unequal knowledge alter behavior.

Access to magic does not prove legitimacy. Social recognition does not prove capability. Legal authority does not prove moral correctness. Source agency cannot be converted into ownership through infrastructure, worship, contract language, or emergency need.

## Specialist Magic Interfaces

### Mana

[Mana](MANA.md) supplies world-side source and state facts.

World-relevant changes include:

- altered availability, distribution, flow, renewal, or accessibility;
- depletion, saturation, distortion, leakage, storage, or changed conversion;
- competition among users and ecological functions;
- new or broken Currents, Reservoirs, Sinks, Channels, and source relations;
- Mana Traces and long-lived byproducts;
- source agency, refusal, or changing terms.

Mana abundance does not automatically create prosperity, life, magical talent, or political stability. Scarcity does not affect every being equally. A change must enter the actual habitats, bodies, technologies, institutions, and relationships that can receive it.

### Magical Affinities

[Magical Affinities](MAGICAL_AFFINITIES.md) may affect which beings, materials, methods, environments, and sources interact readily or adversely.

At world scale, affinity patterns may influence:

- habitat and migration;
- species viability and reproduction;
- labor specialization and occupational access;
- source relations and settlement placement;
- material supply and craft location;
- medicine, exposure, and environmental risk;
- culture, discrimination, inheritance claims, and institutions.

Do not infer a population-wide affinity from prominent individuals, a species label, ancestry, or social doctrine. Distribution, inheritance, expression, and stability require evidence from the affinity and species owners.

### Spell Formation

[Spell Formation](SPELL_FORMATION.md) supplies bounded repeatable procedures and actual Spell Instances.

One spell changes the world through its direct effect and aftermath. Repeated spell use may become a service, tactic, craft, or institution only when operators, sources, training, validation, supply, demand, and failure management support adoption.

Spells may affect labor, communication, transport, construction, medicine, surveillance, agriculture, war, crime, and disaster response. They also create costs, traces, counters, vulnerabilities, error, and unequal access. Shared visual effects or school labels do not establish interoperability or scalable replacement of ordinary work.

### Rituals

[Rituals](RITUALS.md) may coordinate effects too distributed, place-bound, negotiated, or infrastructure-heavy for one actor.

World consequences may arise through:

- recurring calendars and seasonal services;
- sites, offices, trained roles, records, and succession;
- collective obligations and source relationships;
- maintenance of wards, weather arrangements, boundaries, or civic functions;
- Ritual Residue, altered sites, interrupted obligations, and failed Transfers;
- political authority over participation, access, or sacrifice.

Collective scale belongs to the whole arrangement. It cannot be attributed to one leader, copied into every participant, or assumed to persist after roles and dependencies fail.

### Enchanting

[Enchanting](ENCHANTING.md) may turn bounded magical Functions into durable tools and infrastructure.

World consequences depend on:

- Host supply and material life;
- Pattern and Anchor reproducibility;
- source and Supply continuity;
- installation, validation, user interfaces, and safe Triggers;
- maintenance, Drift, repair, removal, and replacement;
- interaction among Layers and wider networks;
- control of manufacture, standards, and knowledge;
- disposal, salvage, and residual hazards.

Installed Enchantments are not permanent productivity bonuses. Networks can create efficiency, specialization, public goods, monopoly, lock-in, synchronized failure, and large maintenance professions.

### Alchemy

[Alchemy](ALCHEMY.md) links magical processes to matter, bodies, supply chains, and waste.

World consequences may arise through:

- Reagent cultivation, harvest, mining, hunting, trade, and theft;
- land, water, energy, Mana, tool, and labor demand;
- medicine, poison, food, agriculture, materials, sanitation, and industry;
- Batch variation, fraud, standards, storage, transport, and degradation;
- toxicity, exposure, Byproducts, contamination, and disposal;
- substitution, scarcity, monopoly, and ecological pressure;
- receiver-specific effects and changing disease environments.

A successful Recipe does not guarantee scalable supply, uniform Batches, safe application, or sustainable extraction. Consuming organisms does not transfer their complete species, Soul, Skill, affinity, or Evolution.

### Divine Magic

[Divine Magic](DIVINE_MAGIC.md) enters world causality through bounded Divine Sources, Domains, Jurisdictions, relationships, institutions, responses, and withdrawals.

World consequences may include:

- services, protection, healing, information, judgment, or environmental effects within a Domain;
- temples, offices, Covenants, law, pilgrimage, doctrine, and redistribution;
- legitimacy claims, schism, diplomacy, conflict, and reform;
- source cooperation, silence, refusal, competing interpretations, or withdrawal;
- dependence on a relationship no institution fully controls;
- changing ecology or politics within a source's actual reach.

A Miracle is causal but non-precedential. It does not prove future supply, unlimited capacity, doctrinal truth, moral approval, or the institution's ownership of the source.

### Forbidden Magic

[Forbidden Magic](FORBIDDEN_MAGIC.md) contributes both the underlying magical mechanism and the world effects of restriction.

Keep distinct:

- actual hazard and affected interests;
- legal, institutional, cultural, divine, contractual, ecological, or world-law classification;
- enforcement capacity and legitimacy;
- public knowledge, secrecy, stigma, and misinformation;
- controlled practice, defensive access, review, and reform;
- displacement into another place, method, market, or population.

A ban may reduce use, drive it underground, concentrate it among authorities, change research, create black markets, displace harm, or provoke resistance. Permission may improve oversight or expand exposure. Neither classification automatically changes the underlying magic or proves its morality.

### Magical Development and Knowledge

[Magical Development](../progression/MAGICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md) influences the availability and reliability of particular magical roles.

World-scale capability depends on:

- compatible bodies and present Access;
- education, apprenticeship, practice, feedback, and recovery;
- instructors, records, instruments, sites, and institutions;
- specialization, maintenance, rust, injury, and succession;
- migration, exclusion, secrecy, labor conditions, and professional responsibility;
- transfer limits across species, sources, traditions, and Ages.

Knowledge can spread faster than embodied reliability. Infrastructure can automate work while concentrating design and repair expertise. Retained progression may accelerate individual rebuilding but cannot instantly staff an institution, restore a supply chain, or carry world-bound authority through Reincarnation.

| **Ecology and migration** | Which niches, habitats, senses, affinities, predators, prey, symbioses, diseases, and routes change; who adapts, moves, declines, or replaces whom? |
| **Trade and economies** | What inputs, skills, maintenance, scarcity, ownership, risk, trust, standards, prices, substitutes, and distribution shape adoption and exchange? |
| **Factions and institutions** | Who controls access, knowledge, sites, offices, enforcement, and legitimacy; what goals, dependencies, succession problems, and opposition follow? |
| **War and unrest** | How do offense, defense, intelligence, logistics, medicine, fortification, source control, counters, doctrine, and civilian exposure change for each side? |
| **Disease and medicine** | Which hosts, vectors, environments, diagnosis routes, treatments, resistance patterns, access inequalities, toxicities, and care systems change? |

| **Climate and disasters** | What bounded atmospheric, geological, hydrological, ecological, or source relation changes; where are burdens displaced and how does recovery proceed? |
| **Culture and religion** | How do experience, doctrine, symbolism, fear, hope, identity, taboo, evidence, source relations, and institutions change without making belief equal world truth? |

### Cross-Domain Effects

One magical change often crosses several domains.

Improved healing may reduce deaths, increase demand for food and housing, alter inheritance, expand elder expertise, change warfare, reshape family structure, create medical institutions, and shift disease selection. None of those outcomes follows from **healing exists** alone. Access, distribution, capacity, disease response, social choice, and time determine the chain.

Track only domains that can plausibly change the current or future situation. Breadth is not permission to declare every magical event globally important.

## Ecology, Species, and Monster Evolution

Magic can alter environmental conditions and evolutionary pressures without directly granting Evolution.

Possible routes include:

- changed Mana availability or compatibility;
- new resources, toxins, habitats, barriers, or predators;
- Alchemical harvesting or Enchantment waste;
- Ritual weather and seasonal disruption;
- divine protection or withdrawal;
- spell-based hunting, agriculture, travel, or warfare;
- restricted practices shifting pressure to other populations;
- magical infrastructure creating novel niches.

[Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) decides how actual populations, bodies, Mutations, adaptations, and Evolution Routes respond. Exposure is not a reward, environmental pressure is not XP, and an individual transformation does not prove population success.

Ecological response may be faster, slower, indirect, or contrary to social expectations. Removing an apex magical predator can expand prey, competitors, disease vectors, or replacement predators before any equilibrium appears.

## Soul Systems and World Consequences

Soul systems enter world causality only through their established expressions and receiving rules.

- [Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may affect a genuine relationship or recognition without becoming environmental Mana.
- [Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may contribute testimony and retained perspective without supplying infrastructure or perfect history.
- [Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may influence symbolic authority, affinity, reaction, or narrative pressure only where a source, culture, institution, species, law, or other receiving rule recognizes the Title.
- [Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) supports integration without becoming a world resource or stability score.
- [Soul Constellations](../soul/SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md) may sustain relationships without controlling the choices of connected Souls.
- [Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) preserves eligible soul-carried history while bodies, property, office, infrastructure, law, and world state continue independently.

Recognition of a returning Soul can create religion, fear, diplomacy, opportunity, conflict, or mistaken identity. It does not make prophecy deterministic or grant the incarnation its former worldly resources.

## Soul Weapons in World Simulation

A [Weapon Soul](../soul-weapons/README.md) is a person and world actor, not equipment infrastructure.

Its world consequences may involve:

- consent, refusal, alliance, witness, testimony, and negotiation;
- continuity across a Bonded Soul's lives;
- public recognition, myth, law, custody, persecution, or diplomacy;
- source relations and bounded magical Functions;
- vessel materials, repair, Enchantments, transport, and Manifestation dependencies;
- strategic or cultural importance that provokes other actors.

A Weapon Soul cannot be multiplied to scale a service, compelled into infrastructure by ownership, treated as a transferable technology, or assumed to support every incarnation equally. Its actions follow current agency, capability, form, source, bond, and circumstance.

## World Stability

Magic can stabilize, destabilize, or reorganize world conditions only through documented causal effects.

Examples include:

- repairing or exhausting a source;
- reducing one disaster while displacing another burden;
- maintaining a boundary that prevents contact;
- creating a brittle infrastructure dependency;
- changing species viability or institutional legitimacy;
- producing widespread Drift, contamination, conflict, or recovery.

No spell, Ritual, artifact, Divine Source, Soul, sacrifice, death, or quantity of Mana automatically adds or removes World Stability. [World Stability](../world-engine/WORLD_STABILITY.md) owns its scoped definition and adjudication. This document supplies only attributable magical inputs and consequences.

## Ages and World Resets

At an [Age Transition or World Reset](../world-engine/AGES_AND_WORLD_RESETS.md), revalidate every Magic-World Claim affected by changed assumptions against the new world.

Check:

- which Mana laws, sources, Fields, Currents, and conversion routes exist;
- which affinities still have valid Targets and Bases;
- which Spell Patterns and Formation Grammars remain operable;
- which Ritual sites, roles, calendars, authorities, and relationships survive;
- which Enchantments retain compatible Hosts, Supply, Anchors, and Functions;
- which Reagents, Process Windows, Batches, and storage conditions remain valid;
- which Divine Sources, Domains, Jurisdictions, institutions, and relationships persist;
- which hazards, restrictions, evidence, and enforcement assumptions remain current;
- which infrastructure, ecology, knowledge, and social memory survives or becomes legacy.

A Reset is not a universal purge or free restoration. Some active magic may fail immediately, some may Drift, some may remain stable, and some former impossibilities may become possible. Each outcome requires its own persistence rule and new-world evidence.

Soul-carried knowledge and Imprints may help investigation and rebuilding. They do not preserve world-bound sources, inventories, institutions, settlements, or infrastructure.

## Uncertainty and Information

Separate:

- **world truth**, including causes and relationships that actually exist;
- **observed evidence**, including traces, records, tests, outcomes, and source testimony;
- **character knowledge**, which may be incomplete or mistaken;
- **player knowledge**, which may differ from character knowledge;
- **institutional claims**, doctrine, law, propaganda, and classification;
- **GM uncertainty**, where the outcome remains genuinely unresolved.

Magic makes neither evidence nor interpretation perfect. A successful harvest may hide depletion. A failed Ritual may be blamed on heresy when a component degraded. A god may know its own motive but not a distant ecological consequence. A World Engine projection is a reasoned possibility, not prophecy.

Communicate uncertainty in terms relevant to action:

- what is known and how;
- what is inferred;
- credible alternatives;
- signs that would distinguish them;
- likely direction and timescale where supportable;
- what remains unknowable without further change or evidence.

## Off-Screen Simulation

Magic does not pause outside the player's view.

At the chosen resolution, continue:

- source renewal, depletion, refusal, and changed relationships;
- maintenance, Drift, decay, storage loss, and infrastructure repair;
- Reagent growth, harvest, trade, substitution, and contamination;
- teaching, migration, institutional succession, and knowledge loss;
- ecological adaptation, predation, reproduction, and disease;
- faction plans, regulation, enforcement, resistance, and alternatives;
- recovery, accumulation, feedback, and delayed consequences.

Simulate only what can plausibly affect play, but do not freeze an established dependency merely because it is off-screen. NPCs and sources act from their own information and goals rather than waiting to deliver a planned plot.

## Magic-World Claim Procedure

When magic may alter world conditions:

1. state the world question, scope, timescale, and required simulation resolution;
2. establish the exact magical cause under its specialist owner;
3. identify source, Mana Relation, Access, embodiment, authority, agency, and current evidence;
4. state the direct change before broader consequences;
5. account for inputs, footprint, maintenance, failure, traces, and opportunity costs;
6. identify affected parties, distribution, exclusion, and externalities;
7. identify relevant World Engine domains without inventing their future variables;
8. resolve responses by sources, people, monsters, institutions, ecosystems, and opponents;
9. consider adoption, displacement, substitution, dependency, lock-in, and alternatives;
10. consider delay, accumulation, thresholds, feedback, seasonality, and path dependence;
11. determine what stops, persists, recovers, reorganizes, or becomes irreversible;
12. preserve uncertainty and identify evidence that could change the finding;
13. record only decision-relevant live state in an external Magic-World Profile;
14. propagate later changes back through the relevant specialist and World Engine owners.

A campaign may record only the fields useful to current decisions:

| **Direct change** | What does that cause change before response and inference? |

## Safeguards

- A magical effect requires an established source, route, owner, footprint, and consequence.
- Mana is not a universal world-development currency, prosperity score, or substitute for World Stability.
- Magic does not replace Skills, Development, Professions, institutions, bodies, ecology, technology, ordinary labor, or source agency.
- One successful effect does not prove repeatability, adoption, scalability, sustainability, safety, or universal access.
- Magical scale requires real supply, training, infrastructure, maintenance, authority, and distribution.
- Efficiency does not erase increased use, displaced burdens, waste, maintenance, or source depletion.
- Benefits and harms remain distributed; aggregate improvement cannot silently erase affected parties.
- Person-like sources, gods, spirits, Weapon Souls, and other persons retain agency; animals, populations, and ecosystems respond through their own causal behavior.
- Magical Infrastructure does not become a person's Development or Reincarnation inventory.
- Reincarnation does not restore world-bound offices, property, networks, supply chains, laws, or services.
- World Resets require revalidation and do not automatically clear residue, debt, ecology, knowledge, or consequence.
- Prohibition does not erase hazard, and permission does not erase risk.
- Magical catastrophe does not automatically grant affinity, resistance, Skill, Development, Evolution, Soul growth, or compensating prosperity.
- No magical path is universally superior to preparation, tools, alliances, ecology, politics, or nonmagical alternatives.
- A World Engine outcome cannot retroactively justify a magical effect that specialist canon prohibits.
- Live world state and current Magic-World Profiles remain outside the canonical repository.

## Worked Examples

### Healing Network and Demographic Change

A regional network uses one validated healing Spell Procedure, local clinics, trained diagnosticians, a seasonal Mana Source, and ordinary aftercare. It reduces deaths from a narrow class of wound where patients arrive quickly.

The direct change is improved survival for reachable treated injuries. Over years, possible consequences include more disabled survivors needing support, older skilled workers, changed inheritance, population growth in well-served towns, pressure on food and housing, military demand for clinics, and political conflict over rural access.

The network does not cure unrelated disease, create practitioners instantly, or guarantee growth. Source depletion, transport, training, price, public trust, and unequal distribution remain causal.

### Enchanted Irrigation and Lock-In

A valley replaces gravity channels with linked pump Enchantments powered by a deep Mana Current. Crop reliability rises and farms expand onto land that cannot be watered otherwise.

Over a generation, old channels decay, repair expertise concentrates, settlement and debt patterns assume continued pumps, and downstream organisms adapt to reduced flow. The valley has gained productivity and Magical Lock-In.

When the Current shifts, the crisis follows dependencies rather than an arbitrary curse. Repair, rationing, source negotiation, restored channels, migration, crop change, and ecological recovery become competing responses.

### Reagent Boom and Ecological Displacement

An Alchemical Process turns the glands of one cave predator into an effective antitoxin. Demand raises incomes and reduces deaths from a local venom.

Heavy harvest lowers predator numbers. Its prey expands, damages fungal farms, and carries another disease toward settlements. Hunters move deeper underground, exposing new habitats and provoking conflict with an intelligent monster society.

Regulation, cultivation, synthetic substitution, different treatment, habitat protection, and black-market extraction create different chains. The antitoxin remains useful; its benefit does not erase supply and ecological consequences.

### Divine Withdrawal

A harbor's safe-passage service depends on a Covenant with a tide spirit, trained petitioners, maintained markers, and yearly habitat restoration. A new government claims ownership of the spirit's Domain and stops the restoration work.

The spirit withdraws cooperation under the established relationship. Shipping risk rises, insurers and merchants change routes, dock labor declines, smugglers exploit unsafe channels, temples dispute doctrine, and the government blames sabotage.

The withdrawal is source agency, not a generic divine disaster. Negotiation, political reform, nonmagical navigation, a different source relationship, or costly harbor reconstruction may respond.

### Restriction and Displacement

A city forbids a memory-dulling spell after evidence of coercive use. Public clinics stop using it, enforcement seizes records, and legitimate research becomes difficult.

Use declines in inspected institutions but moves into private clubs and a neighboring Jurisdiction. Some survivors gain protection; others lose a controlled treatment for severe trauma. Forged consent records, defensive diagnosis, political reform, underground harm, and cross-border enforcement emerge.

The restriction changes behavior and distribution. It does not make the spell more powerful, prove every use immoral, or remove the underlying hazard.

### Seasonal Weather Ritual

A coalition performs a Ritual that redirects one storm front toward drought-struck fields. The direct effect is bounded rainfall under current atmospheric and source conditions.

Reservoirs refill and one harvest improves. The diverted front brings less rain elsewhere, flood risk rises where soil cannot absorb the sudden water, participating spirits expect agreed habitat repair, and neighboring factions demand a role in future decisions.

Repeated use may become infrastructure only if sites, roles, forecasts, source consent, maintenance, and regional governance persist. The Ritual does not create water from narrative need or guarantee the same result next year.

### Monster Adaptation Around a Mana Current

An industrial Enchantment network redirects a forest Mana Current. One affinity-dependent pollinator loses breeding habitat, a resistant burrowing species expands, and predators follow it toward farms.

The World Engine records altered pressure and ecology. [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) determines whether actual populations adapt, migrate, decline, mutate, or access an Evolution Route. The Current does not award Evolution points.

### A Weapon Soul as a Public Actor

A site-bound Weapon Soul maintains a defensive boundary through a source-bound Function and a long Accord with nearby settlements. Its testimony and consent become central to regional diplomacy.

The settlements cannot copy the Function, transfer the Weapon Soul as property, or assume permanent agreement. Maintenance, vessel safety, political promises, source conditions, and the Weapon Soul's changing choices shape the arrangement.

Its importance may create law, worship, fear, alliance, or attack. None of those reactions replaces its personhood or determines its decision.

### World Reset and Obsolete Infrastructure

An Age relies on ambient Mana for lighting Enchantments, public wards, and Alchemical refrigeration. A World Reset changes Mana movement so the old ambient-supply route no longer functions reliably.

Some devices fail, some Drift, and some continue near surviving Sources. Food spoils, night work contracts, warded roads become dangerous, repair guilds gain influence, and nonmagical fuels return. Reincarnated experts may recognize principles while lacking compatible Patterns, materials, and institutions.

The Reset changes world conditions rather than deleting history. Salvage, migration, retraining, new infrastructure, inequality, and cultural reinterpretation follow through ordinary causality.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- live world state or campaign events;
- Phase 8's detailed domain variables, formulas, update cadence, or simulation procedures;
- universal measures for magical output, adoption, externality, dependency, recovery, prosperity, danger, or stability;
- a catalog of magical industries, resources, institutions, laws, settlements, gods, species, or disasters;
- a guarantee that every magical act has regional or epochal significance;
- automatic relationships among Mana, population, economy, ecology, disease, war, technology, dungeons, or World Stability;
- World Reset triggers or Age-transition procedures;
- content generators or encounter scaling;
- a method for converting world consequences into personal Development, Soul growth, Skill XP, affinity, Evolution, or Soul Weapon advancement.

Later Phase 8 documents may define domain-specific state and procedures inside these boundaries. They must preserve specialist Magic ownership, source agency, distribution, causal accounting, uncertainty, recovery, and the repository's campaign-data boundary.
