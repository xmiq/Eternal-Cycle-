# Spell Formation

## Purpose

This document defines how a bounded magical effect is specified, formed, expressed, sustained, ended, tested, and recorded as a spell. It supplies a shared framework without imposing one universal spell language, element chart, casting sequence, tier list, or list of known spells on every Age and world.

Spell Formation integrates [Mana](MANA.md), [Magical Affinities](MAGICAL_AFFINITIES.md), [Magical Development](../progression/MAGICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md), the [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md), embodiment, authority, sources, and current world law. It does not replace any of them.

## Core Rule

**A spell is a bounded, repeatable magical procedure that organizes a valid source and route toward a specified effect under explicit formation, targeting, cost, control, termination, and failure conditions.**

A spell is repeatable when its relevant relationships can be identified and attempted again. Repeatability does not guarantee identical results, universal compatibility, automatic success, or reliable use by every observer.

A spell is not:

- every magical event;
- a Skill, even when casting depends on one;
- a name, incantation, diagram, gesture, formula, or visual effect by itself;
- a spell slot, generic action, universal unit of power, or item in a permanent list;
- proof of Magical Access, affinity, authority, source consent, sufficient Mana, or present Capacity;
- a replacement for ritual, enchanting, alchemy, divine, species, Soul, or Soul Weapon rules;
- a package that transfers intact through Reincarnation;
- an effect made valid merely because a practitioner intends or imagines it.

## What Counts as a Spell

A magical procedure counts as a spell when it has enough stable structure to answer:

1. what change is being attempted;
2. which source and Mana Relation support it;
3. which current route, authority, and embodiment can form it;
4. how the procedure represents and constrains the intended effect;
5. how target, boundary, timing, and persistence are established;
6. what must be supplied, controlled, and paid;
7. how it releases, sustains, terminates, or fails;
8. what evidence permits learning, testing, interruption, and counterplay.

Not every magical expression needs to be a spell. A dragon's breath organ may release magic as a Species Trait, a spirit may alter its own domain through authority, a Weapon Soul may express an established Soul Weapon Form, and a natural Mana Current may ignite exposed crystal without anyone forming a spell.

Calling any of those events a spell socially does not transfer ownership. Use the smallest truthful representation.

## Canonical Terms

### Spell

A bounded, repeatable magical procedure that organizes a valid source and route toward a specified effect under explicit formation, targeting, cost, control, termination, and failure conditions.

### Spell Pattern

The organized magical relationships that make one spell procedure capable of producing its intended effect. A Pattern may be embodied, symbolic, spatial, verbal, conceptual, material, relational, environmental, or distributed.

### Formation Grammar

A local system of meaningful relationships and constraints through which Spell Patterns can be expressed, combined, interpreted, and rejected. A grammar is not necessarily a spoken or written language.

### Formation Component

One functionally relevant part of a Spell Pattern, such as a breath cycle, gesture, symbol, material, spatial relation, rhythm, concept, bodily configuration, source response, or tool state.

### Effect Specification

The bounded description of what a spell is intended to change, preserve, reveal, move, create, transform, constrain, or prevent, including exclusions and limits.

### Targeting Relation

The valid causal relationship by which a Spell Pattern identifies what its effect may act upon. Visibility, a name, proximity, resemblance, ownership, contact, sympathy, coordinates, consent, and authority matter only where current rules recognize them.

### Spell Boundary

The spatial, temporal, material, conceptual, relational, or jurisdictional limit that constrains where and upon what a spell may act.

### Casting Trigger

The event or satisfied condition that causes a formed spell to begin, release, change state, or attempt its effect.

### Sustainment

The ongoing relationships, attention, source flow, authority, structure, environment, or maintenance required to keep a spell active after its Trigger.

### Termination Condition

The event, limit, command, failure, withdrawal, or completed purpose that ends a spell's active process.

### Abort Route

A prepared means of stopping or diverting formation before uncontrolled release, with its own requirements, limits, and consequences.

### Spell Instance

One actual formation and expression of a spell in a particular body, source, environment, target, and moment. The Instance is campaign state; the procedure is canonical or campaign-local knowledge.

### Spell Procedure

The documented or remembered structure needed to attempt a Spell Pattern, including assumptions, components, sequence or dependency, conditions, costs, limits, failure signs, and validation.

### Spell Profile

A qualitative external Campaign Record describing one spell procedure or relevant Spell Instance without treating it as a universal stat block.

## Formation Grammars Are Local

No single grammar governs all magic.

A Formation Grammar may organize spells through:

- spoken syntax and breath;
- gesture, stance, dance, or martial movement;
- runes, diagrams, knots, sculpture, music, or written notation;
- material transformations or prepared reagents;
- sensory images, emotion, memory, or bounded intent;
- mathematical or Conceptual relationships;
- species organs, colony behavior, web tension, song, scent, or color change;
- contracts, names, offerings, permissions, or source dialogue;
- environmental alignments, celestial timing, terrain, or Mana Currents;
- tools, focuses, constructed circuits, or Soul Weapon Forms;
- several of these working together.

A grammar establishes which relationships are meaningful, which combinations are coherent, how ambiguity is handled, what invalid structures do, and which world laws remain non-negotiable.

Shared notation does not prove shared grammar. Different notation does not prevent functional equivalence. Translation requires a scoped [Magical Interoperability Claim](../human/MAGICAL_SCHOOLS.md#school-equivalence-and-interoperability).

## The Spell Pattern

A Spell Pattern is the causal organization of a spell, not merely its visible notation.

Two casters may produce the same Pattern through different interfaces if the local system permits it. Two identical diagrams may produce different Patterns when source, orientation, material, intent, authority, or world law differs.

Every usable Pattern establishes the following functions where relevant.

### Source and Mana Relation

Identify what supplies or mediates magical change, how it reaches the procedure, which Mana State matters, who owns the source, and what drawing or conversion does to the world.

### Access and Authority

Identify the present route by which each actor participates and any permission required from a source, law, territory, institution, contract, deity, spirit, or other person-like being.

### Effect Specification

State the intended change precisely enough to constrain outcome and resistance. **Protect me**, **burn them**, or **reveal truth** is incomplete unless the local grammar itself supplies bounded meanings.

An Effect Specification should identify:

- the property or relationship affected;
- direction and type of change;
- permitted targets;
- scale and relevant precision;
- duration or completion condition;
- exclusions and protected subjects;
- dependencies and assumptions;
- evidence that the intended effect occurred;
- consequences and byproducts.

### Formation Components

Identify the components that represent, regulate, or support the Pattern and the function each performs. A missing decorative phrase may do nothing. A missing breath interval may destroy pressure regulation. Appearance alone cannot distinguish them.

### Targeting Relation

Identify how the Pattern finds or binds its target and why that route is valid. Targeting may depend on direct contact, sensed Mana Trace, a maintained line, a material sample, a true relationship, consent, a mapped location, a species sense, or another locally valid fact.

A target's name, blood, image, possession, or emotional importance grants no universal connection.

### Spell Boundary

Identify where the effect stops. A Boundary may constrain distance, volume, surface, duration, membership, jurisdiction, material, relationship, or concept. Poor boundaries may leak, exclude the wrong subject, follow moving matter, or fail under changed conditions.

### Trigger

Identify what begins release or transition. A Trigger may be deliberate, timed, conditional, source-initiated, contact-based, threshold-based, or automatic through a constructed mechanism.

The Pattern must distinguish a genuine Trigger from an observer's interpretation. **When danger approaches** requires a valid way to detect and define danger.

### Sustainment

Identify whether the effect is instantaneous, self-propagating, maintained by the caster, maintained by a source, held by an external structure, or dependent on continuing conditions.

Sustainment must account for interruption, moving targets, changing Mana State, fatigue, attention, consent, competing use, and environmental drift where relevant.

### Termination and Abort

Every persistent or hazardous procedure needs a bounded end. Identify normal completion, voluntary release, source withdrawal, condition failure, range or duration limit, dispelling route, collapse behavior, and safe or unsafe abort points.

An Abort Route is not guaranteed safety. It may dissipate load, redirect it into a Sink, preserve a target while injuring the caster, or merely reduce the worst failure.

### Trace and Evidence

Identify what the spell leaves behind: Mana Traces, material change, heat, sound, debt, source memory, legal evidence, biological strain, environmental displacement, or no easily observed sign.

Traces support investigation but do not reveal complete intent, identity, method, or capability automatically.

## Spell Formation Lifecycle

The following lifecycle is a claim-resolution sequence, not a required set of visible casting phases.

### 1. Establish Context

Confirm current world law, source, Mana State, environment, target, authority, embodiment, tools, and known interference.

### 2. Specify the Effect

Choose a bounded outcome and exclusions. If the intended effect cannot be distinguished from adjacent outcomes, the Pattern is not ready.

### 3. Select or Construct a Pattern

Use a known Spell Procedure, adapt one, or construct a new Pattern under a valid Formation Grammar. Identify assumptions rather than hiding them behind a spell name.

### 4. Establish the Route

Open or confirm Access, source response, Channels, tool interfaces, authority, target relation, and required affinities. Formation cannot manufacture a missing route merely by being correct on paper.

### 5. Assemble and Regulate

Perform the relevant components, organize the Mana Relation, constrain boundaries, monitor feedback, and hold the Pattern within present Capacity.

### 6. Validate Before Release

Where the system permits, compare expected signs against observed formation, test boundaries, confirm target and source, and choose whether to continue, adapt, or abort.

### 7. Trigger and Express

The Pattern attempts its effect. Resolve target interaction, resistance, opposition, environmental change, costs, byproducts, and uncertainty through their owners.

### 8. Sustain or Complete

Maintain required relationships until the Termination Condition occurs. Do not treat continued operation as free after initial release.

### 9. Terminate and Recover

Close Channels, release boundaries, settle source obligations, dissipate residue, assess strain, record traces, and address continuing consequences.

### 10. Review

Compare intention, procedure, evidence, and outcome. Learning belongs to the actual Skills and Development demonstrated, not to the spell label or output alone.

## Known, Adapted, and Novel Spells

### Known Procedure

A practitioner follows a Spell Procedure already tested for sufficiently similar conditions. Familiarity reduces some design uncertainty but does not guarantee current Access, supply, authority, target compatibility, or reliable execution.

### Adapted Procedure

A practitioner changes a known Pattern for a different body, source, target, environment, scale, tool, or law. Every changed assumption must be revalidated. Cosmetic substitutions may be harmless; a seemingly minor material or timing change may alter the Pattern completely.

### Novel Construction

A new spell requires Spell Construction: a learned capability for modelling, assembling, testing, revising, and validating a Pattern. Naming an effect is not construction.

Novel work commonly proceeds through bounded prototypes, inert or low-consequence tests, instrumentation, comparison, peer review, controlled variation, failure analysis, and gradual expansion. A single success proves only that one Instance occurred.

## Spell Construction and Casting Skills

The Pattern and the capability to use it are different facts.

The [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) owns learned capabilities such as:

- reading a Formation Grammar;
- executing components;
- controlling source flow;
- maintaining boundaries;
- targeting under variation;
- diagnosing malformed Patterns;
- aborting safely;
- adapting procedures;
- constructing new Patterns;
- coordinating with tools or other actors.

A written procedure can exist without anyone currently able to use it. A capable caster may improvise without a written procedure. An automated structure may execute a Pattern while its operator lacks direct casting capability.

### Adaptive Skills

[Adaptive Skills](../skills/ADAPTIVE_SKILLS.md) may emerge when practitioners repeatedly solve a bounded casting problem across meaningful variation and feedback. The Skill records the learned solution, not a new source, affinity, spell effect, or exemption from cost.

### Conceptual Spellcasting

A [Conceptual Skill](../skills/CONCEPTUAL_SKILLS.md) may organize formation, interpretation, targeting, diagnosis, or control when the local grammar supports that Conceptual relationship.

The Conceptual Skill does not define the magical effect by itself. The spell still requires valid world law, source, Mana Relation, Access, authority, embodiment, Pattern, cost, target interaction, resistance, termination, and failure.

## Prepared and Improvised Formation

Preparation may move work out of the moment of casting through memorized structures, pre-measured components, calibrated tools, mapped targets, arranged boundaries, negotiated permissions, stored Mana, or environmental positioning.

Prepared work remains dependent on what was prepared. A changed target, damaged tool, expired permission, depleted source, moved boundary, or altered law can invalidate it.

Improvisation exchanges preparation for live modelling, perception, control, uncertainty, time pressure, and risk. It is not inherently stronger or more advanced. A simple improvised effect may be safer than forcing an elaborate known Pattern into unsuitable conditions.

Neither mode creates spell slots. A culture may use prepared arrays, memorization limits, daily permissions, charged tokens, or cognitive partitions, but those are local dependencies rather than universal Magic rules.

## Costs and Consequences

Every Spell Instance accounts for relevant costs once through the systems that own them.

Costs may include:

- Mana draw, conversion loss, depletion, displacement, or Distortion;
- bodily strain, fatigue, pain, heat, tissue damage, or recovery time;
- attention, cognitive load, emotion regulation, sensory burden, or opportunity cost;
- consumed or degraded materials;
- tool wear, lost calibration, or infrastructure load;
- time, position, secrecy, or exposure;
- debt, obligation, consent, relationship change, or source refusal;
- environmental damage, residue, contamination, or ecological competition;
- legal, social, institutional, spiritual, or political consequences.

Costs do not need to be identical across casters or systems. They do need a causal route. Pain is not a universal fuel, and dramatic sacrifice does not validate an impossible Pattern.

Efficiency means achieving a scoped result with different waste, strain, setup, time, or risk. It never means free magic.

## Target Interaction and Resistance

A successfully formed spell has not automatically succeeded against its target.

Resolve separately:

1. whether the Pattern formed;
2. whether the Targeting Relation is valid and current;
3. whether the effect reaches the target;
4. how the target's body, material, Mana State, authority, agency, or environment responds;
5. which resistance, countermeasure, competing effect, or ordinary physical fact applies;
6. whether the outcome is full, partial, redirected, delayed, transformed, or absent;
7. what costs and traces remain despite failure.

Resistance must be scoped. Fire tolerance does not resist a flame used only as light. A ward against entry may not resist heat transfer. Magical resistance does not imply immunity to falling masonry produced by a spell.

## Countering and Interruption

Counterplay may act upon different layers:

- deny or exhaust the source;
- revoke authority or consent;
- alter the environment or Mana State;
- interrupt a Formation Component;
- break concentration, timing, position, or communication;
- damage or separate a tool, Channel, anchor, or target relation;
- introduce ambiguity into the grammar;
- exceed or evade a Boundary;
- trigger an Abort Route or premature release;
- oppose, absorb, redirect, or outlast the expressed effect;
- exploit recovery, trace, debt, or preparation requirements.

A counterspell is only one locally possible method. It is not a universal reaction, cancellation roll, or proof that larger output wins.

Countering also has costs, uncertainty, requirements, and consequences. Knowing a spell's public name does not reveal its current Pattern or weakest layer.

## Failure

Spell failure should identify the layer that failed.

### Formation Failure

The Pattern never becomes coherent because components, grammar, timing, attention, or control are inadequate.

### Route Failure

Source, Access, authority, Channel, affinity condition, tool, or environmental support is absent or incompatible.

### Targeting Failure

The target relation is false, stale, blocked, ambiguous, or directed at the wrong subject.

### Boundary Failure

The effect leaks, spreads, excludes the wrong subject, persists too long, or interacts beyond its intended scope.

### Capacity Failure

The route cannot safely receive, process, hold, or express the attempted Pattern.

### Control Failure

The Pattern forms but deviates under feedback, interference, movement, emotion, injury, or environmental change.

### Sustainment Failure

An active spell loses required attention, flow, authority, structure, condition, or source support.

### Termination Failure

The spell cannot end through its intended route, ends violently, leaves residue, or continues through a self-propagating process.

### Model Failure

The procedure's theory, source attribution, world-law assumption, equivalence claim, or predicted effect was wrong.

Failure may consume resources or cause consequences without producing the intended effect. It does not automatically grant Skill, Development, affinity, resistance, a stronger spell, or a safer next attempt.

## Safety and Validation

Responsible formation scales safeguards with uncertainty and consequence.

Useful safeguards include:

- bounded prototypes;
- inert, simulated, remote, or sacrificial test media where ethically appropriate;
- independent source and target confirmation;
- capacity margins rather than maximum loading;
- visible formation indicators and instruments;
- protected observers and affected-party consent;
- redundant Termination Conditions;
- tested Abort Routes and designated Sinks;
- quarantine, shielding, distance, or environmental containment;
- peer review and procedural checklists;
- after-action records and trace analysis;
- refusal to test when failure cannot be bounded.

Safety practice is real Skill and Professional Development. A reckless success does not prove a procedure safe, and secrecy does not excuse missing safeguards.

## Embodiment and Species

Spell Formation must be expressible through the current body or another valid interface.

Embodiment may affect:

- available senses and feedback;
- Channels and Capacity;
- timing, scale, mobility, and coordination;
- breath, voice, gesture, posture, memory, or attention;
- number and independence of limbs or distributed bodies;
- material composition and environmental survival;
- ability to form, perceive, or sustain particular Patterns.

A human hand-sign grammar is not a universal standard. A spider may form spatial Patterns in living silk, a slime colony through distributed pressure, a dragon through organ rhythm and breath, and a Weapon Soul through changes in its vessel.

Species-native magical expression remains owned by [Species Development](../progression/SPECIES_DEVELOPMENT.md), [Monster Skill Trees](../skills/MONSTER_SKILL_TREES.md), and [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/README.md) where appropriate. It becomes a spell only when it satisfies this document's procedural definition.

Evolution can create a new interface or remove an old one. It does not provide automatic knowledge, Spell Construction, translated procedures, or Practised Reliability.

## Affinities

[Magical Affinity](MAGICAL_AFFINITIES.md) may affect one exact part of formation, such as source response, Pattern stability, perception, conversion, environmental tolerance, or learning conditions.

Affinity does not:

- provide a Spell Procedure;
- make an invalid Pattern valid;
- replace Access, Skill, authority, cost, or target relation;
- grant every spell associated with its label;
- establish immunity or guaranteed success;
- remove incompatible components or world laws.

Record the exact Affinity Expression used by the Spell Instance.

## Soul-System Interactions

### Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may align a caster with a genuine source, history, place, concept, relationship, or Pattern within its Basis and Target. A receiving rule must state whether that alignment affects access, interpretation, recognition, or another bounded formation fact.

Resonance does not cast, power, validate, target, or sustain a spell by itself.

### Soul Echoes and Retained Instincts

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may remember former procedures, errors, sources, and casting contexts. [Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) may orient attention toward familiar timing or danger.

Neither performs components, supplies current feedback, proves that old laws still apply, or replaces present practice. An Echo advising a caster remains guidance rather than a second free caster.

### Soul Titles

A [Soul Title](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) influences formation only through a receiving source, law, target, institution, or other rule that recognizes its meaning. Recognition may create authority, opportunity, resistance, or narrative pressure without supplying a complete spell.

### Soul Depth and Soul Avatars

[Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) may support integration of complex magical experience without granting spell breadth or output. A [Soul Avatar](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) may synthesize accessible perspectives while still using one current route, body, attention budget, and action economy.

### Akashic Archive

An [Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) Record may preserve a procedure, Spell Instance, or failed theory as information. It does not supply source, Access, authority, current-law validity, embodiment, Skill, or reliability.

## Soul Weapons

A Weapon Soul may be a caster, participant, source negotiator, Pattern interface, tool user, target, or affected person according to its actual capabilities and consent.

For each spell involving a Soul Weapon, separate:

- the Weapon Soul's deliberate action and Skills;
- the bonded soul's action and Skills;
- the current Soul Weapon Form and vessel functions;
- the Mana Source and Mana Relation;
- affinities belonging to each participant or vessel;
- authority and consent;
- the Spell Pattern and effect;
- shared coordination under [Soul Weapon Compatibility](../soul-weapons/SOUL_WEAPON_COMPATIBILITY.md).

Awakening and Soul Intertwining do not install a spell list. A Weapon Form may support one Formation Component without granting the wielder the Pattern or Skill. A Weapon Soul may refuse participation, lose Access, make an error, or propose a different procedure.

Spell effects do not become Soul Weapon Forms merely because they pass through the weapon. Lasting change follows [Weapon Evolution](../soul-weapons/WEAPON_EVOLUTION.md), not repeated casting alone.

## Human Classes, Professions, and Schools

A Class, Profession, or [Magical School](../human/MAGICAL_SCHOOLS.md) may organize access to teachers, sources, procedures, notation, tools, testing, legal authority, roles, and recognition.

It does not make institutional doctrine a world law or grant every member the same spells. A school may call procedures **first circle** and **fifth circle**, but those are local curriculum or recognition labels, not universal spell tiers.

Noncasting roles remain real. A theorist may design Patterns, an instrument operator may validate formation, and a safety officer may control abort infrastructure without possessing direct casting Access.

Credentials and licences may establish social permission in a jurisdiction. They do not prove present capability or magical authority unless the relevant source or law explicitly recognizes them.

## Reincarnation

At Final Death:

- active Spell Instances, sustained effects dependent on the caster, prepared bodily states, Reserves, Channels, and Practised Reliability do not transfer;
- world-bound tools, sites, permissions, contracts, components, and procedures remain where causality leaves them;
- legitimate Spell Construction understanding may persist through Magical Imprints;
- bounded casting Skills may preserve Skill Imprints and Skill XP;
- memories, Soul Echoes, and Archive Records may preserve procedures and context;
- source relationships and Soul Titles persist only through their own rules.

In a new incarnation, old procedures must be revalidated against current body, source, grammar, materials, authority, targets, and world law. Retention can accelerate diagnosis and relearning. It cannot execute an old Pattern through absent hands, organs, senses, Channels, or permissions.

If an old spell remains functional, the caster still rebuilds present Access, control, reliability, and recovery. If only part translates, that part may become a Transferable Principle or basis for new Spell Construction. If no correspondence exists, the old procedure remains history.

## Information and Secrecy

Keep separate:

- the actual Spell Pattern and world truth;
- the practitioner's model;
- a written or oral procedure;
- observed components and traces;
- school doctrine and public names;
- deliberately false, incomplete, encoded, or obsolete records;
- player knowledge and character knowledge;
- unknown assumptions and hidden source behavior.

Observing a Spell Instance may reveal effects, timing, components, source signs, or failure. It does not automatically reveal the complete Pattern, valid Targeting Relation, hidden authority, safe capacity, or how to reproduce it.

Secrecy protects information, not causality. Secret spells still leave source-appropriate evidence and remain vulnerable to investigation, interruption, independent discovery, and changed laws.

## Spell Claim Procedure

When a spell matters:

1. determine whether the event is actually a spell or belongs to another owner;
2. state the Effect Specification and current objective;
3. establish world law, Formation Grammar, source, and Mana Relation;
4. identify Access, authority, consent, embodiment, tools, environment, and affinity;
5. identify the Spell Pattern, functional components, and current practitioner Skills;
6. establish the Targeting Relation, Boundary, Trigger, Sustainment, Termination Condition, and Abort Route;
7. account once for costs, conversion, strain, materials, obligations, and ecological effects;
8. resolve formation, route, target interaction, resistance, interference, and counterplay separately;
9. identify traces, information limits, and consequences;
10. apply Development only to capabilities actually exercised and integrated;
11. apply Reincarnation according to each component's owner;
12. store live procedures, prepared states, and Spell Instances outside the canonical repository.

## Safeguards

### No Universal Spell List

Worlds, Ages, sources, species, and grammars may support radically different procedures. One culture's spell name or catalog does not define universal capability.

### No Spell Slots by Default

Prepared arrays, daily permissions, charges, cognitive limits, and recovery cycles may exist locally. They are not a universal action currency or proof that every caster works the same way.

### No Spell Tiers as Total Power

Circles, ranks, grades, colours, complexity bands, danger classes, and licences are local labels for stated purposes. They do not create one cross-system ladder.

### No Name-to-Effect Casting

Speaking, thinking, remembering, or discovering a spell name does not establish its Pattern, source, Access, authority, cost, Skill, or effect.

### No Skill Replacement

A Spell Procedure is not casting Skill. Repeated use may provide meaningful practice, but output, success, danger, and repetition alone do not award mastery.

### No Soul Shortcut

Soul Resonance, Titles, Echoes, Depth, Avatars, the Archive, and Reincarnation cannot form or power spells outside their bounded receiving rules.

### No Weapon Shortcut

A Soul Weapon is a person and partner, not a spell inventory, universal focus, battery, or route for transferring capabilities between souls.

### No Affinity Shortcut

Affinity supplies only its exact Expression. It does not grant spells, authority, immunity, or effortless formation.

### No Conceptual Omnipotence

Conceptual labels cannot expand an effect through metaphor, override contradiction, or bypass source, target, cost, resistance, agency, and world law.

### No Automatic Copying

Observation, theft, ingestion, inheritance, Archive access, Soul Echoes, or receiving a written procedure does not grant compatible embodiment, interpretation, Access, Skill, or reliability.

### No Infinite Growth

Adding components, Mana, casters, preparation, sacrifice, complexity, or repeated incarnations does not produce unbounded output. Capacity, coherence, source rate, target interaction, environment, coordination, and consequence remain limiting.

### No Compulsory Magic

Spellcasting is optional. Noncasting magical roles and nonmagical paths remain viable, and magic does not automatically dominate preparation, tools, alliances, ecology, or ordinary causality.

### No Agency Bypass

A spell may affect perception, emotion, incentives, memory, movement, or body only through an explicit effect with resistance and consequences. A broad intention cannot seize deliberate choice, source consent, or Weapon Soul participation.

### No Campaign Data in Canon

Known spell lists, prepared spells, active effects, current sources, character capability, local secrets, discovered counters, and populated Spell Profiles belong outside this repository.

## Worked Examples

### Breath-Formed Heat Veil

A human practitioner uses breath rhythm to draw from a warm ambient Current. A hand arc sets a shallow Boundary while controlled exhalation forms a Pattern that redirects heat around the body for several moments.

The spell requires breathable air, an available Current, healthy respiratory Channels, and trained timing. It does not create fire, block projectiles, or grant heat immunity. Smoke disrupts feedback, breath loss ends Sustainment, and rapid movement deforms the Boundary.

### Rune-Web Restraint

A spider monster lays three tensioned silk lines whose intersection organizes spatial Mana. Vibration identifies a target crossing the web, and tension change triggers a brief constriction of space around the contacting limb.

The silk is a Formation Component and temporary Reservoir, not decoration. Cutting one line before the Trigger breaks the Pattern. A human who copies the diagram without compatible silk, tension sense, and learned web Skill has notation but no functioning spell.

### Conceptual Boundary Spell

A warder with a Conceptual Skill in operational boundaries defines **members carrying the hospice token may cross; hostile projectiles may not**. The local grammar recognizes physical tokens and directed momentum, not moral hostility.

The warder must translate the intention into valid categories, source the Boundary, and account for dropped tokens, thrown medicine, carried patients, and slow physical intrusion. Conceptual understanding improves specification; it does not let the word **hostile** read minds.

### Scholar and Caster

A noncasting scholar reconstructs the grammar of an old cooling spell and identifies that its final symbol controls dissipation rather than temperature. A caster with current Access executes the revised Pattern while the scholar monitors instruments.

The scholar contributes Spell Construction knowledge and diagnosis. The caster contributes formation and control. Neither receives the other's capability, and the resulting spell remains dependent on the local source.

### Reincarnated Master in a Different Body

A former six-armed caster Reincarnates as a two-armed human. The old Pattern requires four simultaneous gestures and two tactile feedback loops.

The soul remembers the procedure and recognizes errors quickly, but cannot cast it through absent limbs. It spends years designing a slower sequence with a calibrated focus and rebuilds reliability through current practice. Retention accelerates reconstruction without bypassing embodiment.

### Spell Through a Soul Weapon

A Weapon Soul shaped as a bell can hold one resonant Formation Component while its wielder maintains the target Boundary. Both consent and use separate Skills. A mountain spirit supplies the Mana under a current agreement.

The bell does not give the wielder its resonance affinity or spell knowledge. The wielder does not command the Weapon Soul's timing. If the spirit withdraws, correct coordination cannot supply the missing source.

### Obsolete Age Spell

An Archive Record preserves a spell that navigated by three moons. The current Age has one moon and a different spatial law.

Reciting the old procedure fails at the Targeting Relation. Researchers may extract principles of celestial measurement, but the spell is not restored until a new Pattern is constructed and validated under current law.

### Prepared Spell Disrupted

A messenger prepares a threshold alarm using a mapped doorway, a wax seal, and a signed permission from the house spirit. Overnight flooding shifts the doorframe and dissolves part of the seal.

The preparation remains partially recognizable but no longer has a valid Boundary. Choosing to trigger it anyway risks marking the entire flooded wall. Preparation saved time only while its dependencies remained true.
