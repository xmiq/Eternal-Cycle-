# Forbidden Magic

## Purpose

This document defines forbidden magic as a contextual classification applied to magical practices, knowledge, sources, methods, targets, effects, or infrastructure under explicit safety, ethical, legal, cultural, divine, ecological, or institutional restrictions. It establishes classification, evidence, scope, jurisdiction, hazards, protected interests, authority, review, enforcement, containment, research, reform, and safeguards without creating a separate dark-magic power source.

Forbidden magic integrates every completed Magic system, [Magical Development](../progression/MAGICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md), the [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md), Souls, embodiment, Classes, Professions, Monster Evolution, Soul Weapons, institutions, ecology, and the [Game Master Framework](../gm/GAME_MASTER_FRAMEWORK.md). It does not replace those owners.

## Core Rule

**Forbidden is a scoped judgment or restriction imposed on an otherwise defined magical claim; it does not describe a universal source, element, tier, morality, or capability.**

Before calling magic forbidden, identify:

- the exact practice or information being classified;
- what the underlying magic actually does;
- who or what restricts it;
- the Restriction Basis and evidence;
- the protected interests;
- the scope and jurisdiction;
- hazards independent of classification;
- permissions, exceptions, review, enforcement, and consequences;
- uncertainty, dissent, and change over time.

Forbidden magic is not:

- one skill tree, affinity, element, school, class, spell list, or Mana type;
- automatically evil, corruptive, secret, powerful, addictive, or sentient;
- automatically more effective because it is cruel, costly, illegal, or difficult;
- proof that the restricting authority is correct, legitimate, informed, or benevolent;
- permission to implement an effect prohibited or impossible under completed canon;
- a route around consent, embodiment, Souls, Skills, Development, Evolution, Soul Weapon personhood, or source ownership;
- a universal license for arbitrary GM punishment;
- a campaign list of banned spells stored in this repository.

## Canonical Terms

### Forbidden Magic

A contextual classification for a defined magical practice, method, source, target, effect, item, infrastructure, or body of knowledge subject to an explicit Magic Restriction within a stated scope and jurisdiction.

### Magic Restriction

A rule, boundary, prohibition, access condition, containment requirement, or dissemination limit applied to a defined magical claim by an identified authority, community, source, institution, relationship, or world law.

### Restricting Authority

The person, community, institution, Divine Source, spirit, territory, contract, law, world process, or other established actor or system that creates, recognizes, interprets, or enforces one Magic Restriction.

### Restriction Basis

The stated reason and supporting evidence for a Magic Restriction, such as direct harm, systemic risk, non-consent, ecological damage, source refusal, public safety, secrecy, monopoly, doctrine, prejudice, politics, or historical experience.

### Restriction Scope

The exact actions, knowledge, sources, methods, targets, effects, materials, tools, participants, circumstances, or forms of distribution to which a Magic Restriction applies.

### Restriction Jurisdiction

The people, identities, places, institutions, territories, relationships, sources, Ages, or conditions over which a Restricting Authority's rule can presently operate or be enforced.

### Restriction Status

The current legal, institutional, cultural, divine, contractual, ecological, or world-law condition of one Magic Restriction, including whether it is active, disputed, suspended, obsolete, unenforced, under review, or repealed.

### Intrinsic Hazard

A causal risk arising from the magical practice itself under stated conditions, independent of whether any authority recognizes or prohibits it.

### Hazard Profile

A qualitative account of one practice's failure modes, affected parties, exposure routes, severity, likelihood conditions, detectability, containment, recovery, uncertainty, and cascading consequences.

### Protected Interest

A person, agency right, Soul boundary, community, species, ecosystem, source, resource, place, infrastructure, body, relationship, public good, or other value a Magic Restriction is intended or claimed to protect.

### Controlled Practice

A restricted magical practice permitted only under defined competence, authority, consent, site, equipment, supervision, documentation, containment, purpose, or review conditions.

### Prohibited Practice

A defined magical practice that a Restricting Authority does not permit within its current scope and jurisdiction, subject to whatever exceptions, review, enforcement, and conflict rules actually exist.

### Taboo Classification

A cultural, sacred, relational, or identity-based restriction whose force arises primarily through shared meaning, community obligation, or spiritual relationship rather than ordinary state law.

### Restricted Knowledge

Information whose access, copying, teaching, publication, translation, possession, or use is limited under a defined Magic Restriction. Knowledge restriction does not erase what a knower already understands or grant capability to readers.

### Source Refusal

An established decision or condition by which a person-like magical source denies, limits, withdraws, or contests participation in a magical route. Source Refusal is distinct from law, taboo, technical failure, and inability.

### Containment Duty

A responsibility to prevent, limit, monitor, disclose, stabilize, recover from, or safely terminate a defined magical hazard under established capability, authority, and affected-party obligations.

### Review Route

An established process for challenging, testing, interpreting, narrowing, expanding, suspending, or repealing a Magic Restriction through evidence and accountable decision-making.

### Enforcement Route

The actual means by which a Magic Restriction is detected, communicated, prevented, interrupted, sanctioned, contained, or remedied, including its authority, limits, errors, costs, and accountability.

### Forbidden Magic Profile

A qualitative external Campaign Record describing one restricted magical claim, its underlying mechanics, classification, basis, authority, jurisdiction, hazards, protected interests, permissions, review, enforcement, history, and uncertainty.

## Classification Is Not Mechanism

The underlying magical effect remains owned by its actual system.

A forbidden spell remains a [Spell](SPELL_FORMATION.md). A prohibited rite remains a [Ritual](RITUALS.md). A controlled Enchantment remains a Host-bound [Enchantment](ENCHANTING.md). A restricted alchemical product remains an [Alchemical Batch](ALCHEMY.md). A source-refused miracle remains a claim under [Divine Magic](DIVINE_MAGIC.md).

Classification can change:

- who may access or perform the practice;
- where and when it may occur;
- required consent, supervision, tools, and containment;
- what information can be distributed;
- social, legal, institutional, divine, or relational consequences;
- how others investigate, resist, regulate, or respond.

Classification cannot change:

- an invalid source into a valid one;
- impossible targeting into a valid Targeting Relation;
- missing Skill into competence;
- absent embodiment into expression;
- non-consent into consent;
- a nonperson into a person or a person into property;
- one system's effect into another system's progression;
- an external source into soul-owned power;
- a harmful act into Development merely because it was prohibited.

## Distinct Restriction Categories

Several categories may apply to one practice. Keep them separate.

### Intrinsically Hazardous

The practice has established causal risks even without law or taboo. Hazard depends on source, scale, conditions, users, targets, failure, containment, and uncertainty.

### Legally Prohibited

A government or recognized legal authority prohibits defined conduct within a jurisdiction. The law may be protective, outdated, captured, discriminatory, impossible to enforce, or otherwise contested.

### Institutionally Restricted

An academy, guild, temple, military, profession, laboratory, archive, or other institution limits access or practice through its actual authority, property, membership, standards, or duties.

### Culturally or Religiously Taboo

A community treats a practice as violating sacred, ancestral, relational, identity, mourning, bodily, or ecological obligations. The taboo can be socially powerful without becoming universal world law.

### Source-Refused

A person-like source refuses a use, user, target, method, or condition. Refusal preserves source agency and may exist even where every government permits the practice.

### Contractually Restricted

A Covenant, Magical Contract, oath, licence, office, or agreement limits participants under its valid terms. It binds only through established parties, scope, authority, and law.

### Ecologically Restricted

Use is limited because harvesting, discharge, source draw, habitat alteration, disease, migration, reproduction, or cumulative effects threaten an ecosystem or shared resource.

### Ethically Restricted

The practice threatens consent, agency, privacy, bodily integrity, Soul boundaries, personhood, fair distribution, or other established duties even when no law currently addresses it.

### Information-Restricted

Knowledge is limited because disclosure itself creates a credible hazard, violates custody, exposes vulnerable people, or enables harmful use. Secrecy for monopoly, reputation, or political control is a different Basis and should be recorded honestly.

### World-Law Restricted

Current world law blocks or conditions a route. Distinguish a hard impossibility from a dangerous attempt, source refusal, environmental incompatibility, or a law that imposes consequences while still allowing the effect.

These are classifications, not moral alignments. A practice may be hazardous but legal, harmless but taboo, illegal because of prejudice, restricted for sound reasons but enforced abusively, or permitted despite severe exploitation.

## Underlying Practice

Every forbidden-magic claim begins by describing the practice without the word **forbidden**.

Identify:

- objective and effect;
- source and Mana Relations;
- method and owning procedure;
- practitioner and required Skills;
- receiver, target, affected parties, and consent;
- body, species, environment, site, tools, and materials;
- scale, duration, termination, failure, and aftermath;
- information requirements;
- external dependencies;
- actual hazards;
- possible nonmagical equivalents or substitutes.

**Forbidden resurrection**, **blood magic**, **mind magic**, **necromancy**, **holy prohibition**, and **black alchemy** are category labels, not complete mechanics. Define the exact act before judging it.

Two practices with the same social name may deserve different classifications. Preserving a corpse for identification, animating non-sapient tissue, coercing a deceased Soul, and fabricating a false ancestor message are not one mechanic merely because a culture calls all four necromancy.

## Restriction Basis and Evidence

A Restriction Basis may include:

- demonstrated direct harm;
- severe failure or uncertain catastrophic risk;
- violation of consent, personhood, Soul boundary, privacy, or bodily integrity;
- ecological depletion, contamination, disease, or cascading instability;
- source refusal or broken relationship;
- protection of vulnerable knowledge, sites, species, or infrastructure;
- professional safety and competence;
- historical catastrophe;
- conflict prevention or public order;
- sacred obligation or mourning practice;
- monopoly, secrecy, censorship, prejudice, fear, or political control;
- misinformation, mistaken attribution, or obsolete assumptions.

Record evidence separately from stated justification. An institution may cite public safety while protecting a monopoly. A taboo may preserve real ecological knowledge through an inaccurate myth. A ruler may prohibit safe healing used by a persecuted species.

Rarity, age, secrecy, spectacle, divine language, body horror, unfamiliar species, dark color, pain, and foreign origin do not establish hazard by themselves.

## Hazard Assessment

Assess an Intrinsic Hazard through:

- initiating conditions;
- exposed people, Souls, species, sources, ecosystems, and infrastructure;
- direct and indirect effects;
- scale, duration, spread, accumulation, and reversibility;
- detection and warning time;
- operator control and Practised Reliability;
- target resistance and consent;
- source behavior;
- ordinary and magical containment;
- termination and recovery;
- interaction with other systems;
- uncertainty and worst credible outcomes;
- how risk changes under different bodies, Ages, and environments.

Hazard is not one score. A procedure may be unlikely but catastrophic, frequent but recoverable, safe in one sealed site, dangerous only to one species, or socially damaging despite little physical risk.

Do not use suffering as evidence of productive cost. Injury, death, madness, corruption, Soul damage, mutation, and disaster remain harms under their owners rather than a price that proves potency.

## Protected Interests and Affected Parties

A restriction should identify who or what it protects and how.

Protected Interests may include:

- informed agency and consent;
- bodies, minds, Souls, memories, identities, and relationships;
- Weapon Soul personhood;
- children, prisoners, patients, dependants, or other vulnerable persons;
- species continuity and habitats;
- public health and shared Mana systems;
- food, water, shelter, infrastructure, and migration routes;
- source autonomy;
- graves, remains, mourning, and cultural heritage;
- privacy and Restricted Knowledge;
- future users exposed to persistent effects;
- communities bearing waste, risk, or enforcement costs.

Authorities may claim to protect a group while excluding its members from decisions. The GM should identify who defines the interest, who benefits, who bears costs, who can dissent, and who has a Review Route.

## Authority, Legitimacy, and Jurisdiction

A Restricting Authority must have an actual route to create or enforce its rule.

Possible authority comes from:

- law and government;
- community governance;
- affected-party consent and self-protection;
- source agency;
- Divine Mandate or Covenant;
- contract;
- professional duty;
- institutional property, membership, or custody;
- territorial relation;
- ecological stewardship;
- current world law.

Authority is not the same as legitimacy, competence, truth, morality, reach, or enforcement power.

A law may be valid in court and morally abusive. A village taboo may lack state recognition but accurately protect a spawning site. A god may refuse its own power without having authority to imprison a mortal who studies unrelated magic. An archive may control access to its copy without owning every independent copy.

Jurisdictions can overlap and conflict. Resolve actual relationships rather than selecting the authority with the highest title.

## Controlled Practice

A Controlled Practice may require:

- defined purpose and excluded uses;
- current competence and role-specific Skills;
- licence, Mandate, source permission, or affected-party consent;
- suitable site, tools, protective equipment, and containment;
- supervision or independent witness;
- limited scale, targets, duration, or frequency;
- provenance and chain of custody;
- logging, disclosure, monitoring, and follow-up;
- Abort Routes and emergency response;
- review, renewal, and accountability;
- cleanup, restitution, and long-term care.

Controls must address named hazards. Ceremonial paperwork cannot contain a Mana breach, while a useful containment procedure does not become illegitimate merely because one kingdom has not licensed it.

Exceptional permission is not general capability. A researcher authorized to observe a rite does not gain authority to perform it. A healer permitted to use one product does not own its source or formula.

## Prohibition

Prohibition states which conduct is not permitted in a scope.

A useful prohibition identifies:

- the prohibited act;
- authority and Jurisdiction;
- Restriction Basis and evidence;
- protected interests;
- whether possession, knowledge, teaching, preparation, attempt, performance, distribution, or aftermath is covered;
- exceptions and emergency powers;
- enforcement and due process;
- Review Route;
- sanctions, containment, remedy, and care;
- treatment of coerced participants and victims;
- historical version and current status.

Prohibition does not erase capability or knowledge. It changes choices, risk, access, and consequence. Characters may comply, resist, conceal, challenge, reform, violate, or flee under ordinary agency and world causality.

The GM must not use a prohibition as invisible punishment. Characters should have access to evidence appropriate to their knowledge, including uncertain or conflicting rules when that uncertainty is itself established.

## Restricted Knowledge

Knowledge can be hazardous without acting by itself. Distinguish:

- descriptive knowledge of an effect;
- historical records;
- diagnostic and defensive knowledge;
- component identities;
- complete operational procedures;
- source credentials or target data;
- live access keys;
- dangerous false instructions;
- knowledge that violates privacy or Soul boundaries;
- knowledge whose publication changes political or ecological conditions.

Restriction options include:

- redaction;
- staged access;
- supervised study;
- defensive-only extracts;
- split custody;
- delayed publication;
- authenticated copies;
- consent-based disclosure;
- quarantine pending review;
- preservation without current access;
- destruction only where ethically, legally, and practically justified.

Reading Restricted Knowledge does not grant Skill, Access, Mana, authority, embodiment, or reliability. Erasing a text does not erase every memory, copy, Echo, Archive Trace, or independently discovered principle.

## Research and Experimentation

Research on restricted magic must state:

- question and public or affected-party value;
- why safer knowledge is insufficient;
- source, method, scale, and subjects;
- competence and facilities;
- consent and vulnerable-party protections;
- stopping conditions;
- containment and emergency response;
- data custody and publication limits;
- independent oversight and conflicts of interest;
- cleanup, care, restitution, and long-term monitoring;
- Review Route.

Research approval does not make harm acceptable or results true. Prohibition does not make all defensive investigation immoral. A culture may preserve safe diagnosis of a banned curse while refusing to teach its creation.

Experimenting on prisoners, enemies, monsters, constructs, Weapon Souls, children, patients, deceased Souls, or communities does not remove personhood or consent requirements.

## Enforcement, Due Process, and Abuse

Enforcement may involve:

- education and warning;
- access control;
- inspection and testing;
- interruption or suppression;
- quarantine and containment;
- confiscation under actual authority;
- licence suspension;
- remediation and care;
- civil or community process;
- arrest, sanctions, exile, or force;
- source withdrawal;
- negotiated compliance;
- public disclosure.

Every route has limits, error rates, costs, incentives, and accountability. Detection magic can misidentify sources. Inspectors can be corrupt. Secret courts can suppress evidence. Public registries can endanger innocent practitioners. Confiscation can destabilize a Host or harm a Weapon Soul.

Preserve:

- presumption and evidence standards appropriate to the setting;
- distinction among suspicion, charge, finding, and world truth;
- ability to contest or appeal where established;
- protection of victims and coerced participants;
- proportionate response;
- safe handling by competent people;
- accountability for enforcers;
- uncertainty when institutions lack these safeguards.

An unjust restriction can be real and consequential without becoming canonical moral truth.

## Containment and Emergency Use

Containment should target actual causal dependencies.

It may involve:

- source isolation or negotiation;
- distance, shielding, grounding, filters, wards, or sealed Hosts;
- interrupted Supply;
- target protection;
- environmental stabilization;
- restricted communication or information;
- quarantine;
- antidote, medical care, repair, or purification under their owners;
- safe Termination or Abort Routes;
- monitored decay;
- evacuation;
- accepting a bounded lesser harm to prevent a larger credible one.

Containment does not prove evil, guilt, personhood, or permanent corruption. It must account for the rights and needs of contained persons or sources.

Emergency permission can authorize one bounded response when delay creates greater harm. It does not erase Skill requirements, victims, records, review, or consequences, and it does not become a standing exception by repetition.

## Reclassification, Reform, and Abolition

Restrictions can change through:

- better evidence;
- new containment or treatment;
- changed world law;
- source negotiation;
- institutional reform or capture;
- ecological recovery or collapse;
- public pressure;
- affected-community leadership;
- exposure of fraud or prejudice;
- catastrophe;
- loss of enforcement capacity;
- World Reset.

Possible outcomes include narrowing, expanding, replacing, suspending, repealing, decentralizing, or abolishing a restriction.

Reclassification does not retroactively make earlier evidence, harm, convictions, resistance, or social consequences vanish. Amnesty may change sanctions without erasing victims. Exoneration may correct a false finding without restoring lost years automatically.

## Interactions with Magic Systems

### Mana

[Mana](MANA.md) owns sources, state, flow, conversion, depletion, Distortion, ecology, and recovery. A restricted Mana Source remains finite and source-bound. Classification cannot create contamination or corruption without a causal process.

### Magical Affinities

[Magical Affinity](MAGICAL_AFFINITIES.md) may affect compatibility with a restricted source or method. Affinity is neither guilt nor permission, and persecution or repeated exposure does not create a beneficial affinity automatically.

### Spell Formation

[Spell Formation](SPELL_FORMATION.md) owns a prohibited Spell's Pattern, Targeting Relation, source, lifecycle, termination, failure, and counterplay. A legal ban cannot make an impossible spell form, while technical success cannot make its use lawful or ethical.

### Rituals

[Rituals](RITUALS.md) own a restricted Ritual's Structure, roles, components, integrity, interruption, and aftermath. Secrecy, sacrifice, and prohibition do not increase Ritual output. Containment and partial termination remain available where causally supported.

### Enchanting

[Enchanting](ENCHANTING.md) owns a controlled or prohibited Enchantment's Host, Functions, Patterns, Anchors, Interfaces, Supply, Layers, maintenance, Drift, and removal. Confiscation must consider Host damage, living-host consent, and Weapon Soul personhood.

### Alchemy

[Alchemy](ALCHEMY.md) owns restricted Reagents, Recipes, Batches, application, toxicity, storage, and waste. Ingredient rarity and legal status do not imply potency. Defensive testing and antidote work can be separated from harmful manufacture.

### Divine Magic

[Divine Magic](DIVINE_MAGIC.md) owns Source Refusal, Domain, Jurisdiction, Mandates, Covenants, responses, and withdrawal. Divine prohibition has only the scope its source can establish; it does not automatically become universal moral or state law.

## Skills, Development, Classes, and Professions

Restricted practice may involve genuine Skills in diagnosis, containment, defense, research, interpretation, performance, enforcement, advocacy, or remediation.

Development requires meaningful practice, feedback, integration, and changed capability. It does not arise automatically from:

- breaking a law;
- suffering punishment;
- killing or harming;
- using a prohibited source;
- surviving a curse or disaster;
- secrecy or danger;
- reading one manual;
- repeated failure;
- public fear;
- moral transgression.

A Class or Profession may specialize in regulation, investigation, containment, healing, advocacy, dangerous research, or prohibited practice. The social role neither grants underlying magic nor determines moral truth.

Licences and restrictions are current World Recognition and Access conditions. They are not Persistent Potential or soul-owned authority.

## Species, Monsters, and Evolution

One species' ordinary biology may be another culture's forbidden magic. Classification does not change the body's actual owner.

Assess:

- whether the effect is a Species Trait, Skill, spell, Ritual, Mutation, Hybrid Form, Evolution, tool, or social interpretation;
- affected species and cultural perspectives;
- whether a restriction criminalizes embodiment or only conduct;
- ecological and source risks;
- communication and accessibility;
- enforcement effects on migration, reproduction, food, care, and survival;
- whether safer adaptation or accommodation exists.

Forbidden status does not create a Mutation or Evolved Form. Persecution, vivisection, forced exposure, survival, and consumption do not grant evolution progress.

Monster communities may create valid restrictions through their own law, ecology, sacred relationships, and institutions. Human classifications do not default to universal jurisdiction.

## Soul-System Interactions

### Soul Boundaries

No forbidden-magic classification authorizes effects prohibited by [Soul Engine Safeguards](../soul/SOUL_ENGINE_SAFEGUARDS.md). Calling Soul theft, duplication, forced merging, Echo override, Avatar takeover, or Reincarnation capture **forbidden art** does not make it an available rare mechanic.

An exceptional Soul interaction must already have an explicit Soul-safe receiving rule, agency, resistance, non-duplication, failure, and consequence. Classification comes afterward.

### Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may align a soul with a restricted source, victim, event, place, or method under a valid Basis and Target. Resonance does not grant permission, guilt, Skill, immunity, or power.

### Soul Echoes and Retained Instincts

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may preserve knowledge of prohibited practice, enforcement, victimization, resistance, or containment. [Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) may orient attention toward familiar danger.

They do not restore tools, sources, licences, social identity, current-law validity, or Practised Reliability. An Echo's secrecy request does not override the current person's agency.

### Soul Titles

A [Soul Title](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may cause observers to associate a soul with a prohibited deed or resistance movement where its Basis is relevant. It does not establish guilt, legal identity, permission, immunity, or compelled behavior.

### Soul Depth and Soul Avatars

[Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) may support integration of difficult moral and historical experience without becoming tolerance for hazardous magic. A [Soul Avatar](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) may compare accessible perspectives but cannot bypass restrictions, consent, or present executive agency.

### Akashic Archive

[Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) Records may preserve restricted procedures, disasters, laws, victims, enforcement, and reform. An Archive Interface may limit access, but Archive presence does not make a Record true, safe, usable, lawful, or self-executing.

## Soul Weapons

A Weapon Soul may be practitioner, target, witness, protected person, source, custodian, or dissenter under a restriction.

Forbidden status does not:

- make a Weapon Soul property;
- authorize confiscation without accounting for personhood;
- permit forced Awakening, Bonding, Soul Intertwining, Evolution, Echo disclosure, Legacy Succession, or Manifestation;
- make vessel destruction harmless;
- turn abuse into loyalty or power;
- grant the wielder immunity from law;
- make an external curse part of Weapon Soul identity.

A prohibited Weapon Function remains owned by the Weapon Soul and its established source. Restriction, suppression, containment, removal, negotiation, and repair affect different layers and must preserve Vessel Continuity and agency.

## Reincarnation and World Resets

At Final Death:

- laws, taboos, institutions, evidence, victims, reputations, licences, sanctions, texts, tools, sources, and enforcement remain in the world;
- eligible knowledge and bounded Skills may persist through their established Imprints;
- no permission, immunity, warrant, conviction, legal identity, office, or enforcement authority follows the Soul automatically;
- Soul Titles and Archive Records persist only through their own rules;
- hazardous bodily effects remain with the body unless an explicit Soul effect owns persistence;
- Practised Reliability, embodiment, Access, and current-law knowledge must be rebuilt.

A later incarnation may be recognized, accused, recruited, protected, or targeted through valid evidence. Soul continuity does not itself establish current legal identity, inherited guilt, immunity, or authority.

World Resets can change magic law, hazards, institutions, culture, sources, ecology, and the feasibility of containment. A practice forbidden in one Age may be medicine in another; a former utility may become catastrophic under new world law.

## Information and Uncertainty

Keep separate:

- actual underlying mechanics;
- Hazard Profile;
- classification and stated Basis;
- Restricting Authority's private motive;
- law and enforcement practice;
- cultural taboo;
- source refusal;
- public reputation;
- accusation and finding;
- victim testimony;
- practitioner belief;
- player knowledge and character knowledge;
- unknown effects and disputed evidence.

The GM should communicate what a character can reasonably know: posted law, professional norms, rumors, observed enforcement, warning signs, source messages, or uncertainty. Hidden restrictions may create consequences only through actual causality, not retrospective surprise used to remove agency.

## Forbidden-Magic Claim Procedure

When forbidden magic matters:

1. describe the underlying magical practice without classification shorthand;
2. apply its owning Magic, Skill, Development, Species, Soul, and Weapon rules;
3. identify Intrinsic Hazards independently of law, taboo, secrecy, and morality;
4. identify each Restricting Authority, Basis, evidence, Protected Interest, Scope, Jurisdiction, and Status;
5. distinguish permission, competence, consent, Access, knowledge, and capability;
6. establish controls, exceptions, Containment Duties, Review Routes, and Enforcement Routes;
7. preserve accusation, institutional finding, public belief, and world truth as separate information;
8. resolve player choice, opposition, investigation, compliance, violation, and consequences causally;
9. prevent classification from validating impossible effects or bypassing safeguards;
10. award Development only for capability meaningfully practised and integrated;
11. propagate effects through communities, institutions, ecology, politics, sources, and history;
12. apply Final Death, Reincarnation, and World Reset according to each owner;
13. store current laws, bans, cases, practitioners, restricted texts, and profiles outside canon.

## Safeguards

### No Forbidden Power Source

Forbidden is a classification. It creates no Mana type, affinity, Skill tree, damage category, tier, or automatic capability.

### No Prohibition Bonus

Illegality, taboo, secrecy, sacrifice, cruelty, pain, risk, corruption, and social fear do not make magic stronger, faster to learn, or more compatible.

### No Canon Bypass

An effect impossible or prohibited under completed Soul, agency, embodiment, Evolution, or Soul Weapon safeguards cannot be introduced as rare forbidden magic.

### No Automatic Moral Truth

A ban may be wise, mistaken, obsolete, prejudiced, captured, or abusive. Permission may also be negligent or exploitative. Resolve evidence and affected parties.

### No Automatic Corruption

Use, knowledge, accusation, affinity, appearance, or proximity does not create Corruption. Any corruptive effect requires an established source and process.

### No Harm Dividend

Victimization, punishment, failed experiments, exposure, killing, survival, and disaster do not automatically grant Development, resistance, affinity, Mutation, Title, or mastery.

### No Knowledge-to-Capability Transfer

Reading, stealing, inheriting, remembering, or publishing Restricted Knowledge does not grant source Access, Skill, tools, authority, embodiment, or Practised Reliability.

### No Species Criminalization by Mechanic

Species Traits and bodies remain biological or magical facts even when societies prohibit their expression. Restrictions and persecution do not rewrite ownership or make a species evil.

### No Enforcement Omniscience

Detection, divination, testimony, records, institutions, gods, and investigators remain bounded and fallible. Suspicion and conviction are not world truth.

### No Player-Agency Trap

Restrictions create informed choices and causal consequences; they do not authorize invisible rules, forced transgression, predetermined guilt, compulsory corruption, or arbitrary punishment.

### No Reincarnated Guilt or Immunity

Soul continuity does not automatically transfer legal identity, conviction, licence, immunity, office, debt, or enforcement authority.

### No Campaign Prohibition List in Canon

Current laws, taboos, restricted spells, practitioners, cases, confiscated items, enforcement operations, and populated Forbidden Magic Profiles belong in external Campaign Records.

## Worked Examples

### Four Practices Called Necromancy

A kingdom bans **necromancy** after a war. Investigation separates four practices: preserving remains for identification, animating non-sapient tissue, fabricating ancestor messages, and coercing a deceased Soul.

The first is medically useful and low hazard under consent. The second presents disease and labor risks. The third is fraud. The fourth lacks a valid Soul-safe route and cannot be implemented merely because the law names it. One broad label hid four different owners and responses.

### A Safe Healing Method Prohibited by Prejudice

An insectoid minority uses a species-native secretion and Mana pulse to seal wounds. Human healers call it corrupt blood magic and outlaw possession of the secretion.

Evidence shows ordinary biological and magical healing with manageable allergy risk. The restriction remains legally real but its stated Basis is false and discriminatory. Reform requires law, evidence, training, and restitution rather than proving the technique powerful through persecution.

### Controlled Spatial Research

A university studies a Spell Pattern that can fold a hand-sized volume for an instant. Larger attempts can sever Targets or destabilize a local Field.

The practice is controlled through scale limits, remote instruments, independent shutdown, site containment, and staged publication. Authorization does not give researchers perfect Skill, and secrecy does not increase the spell's output.

### A Divine Source Refuses Weaponization

A spring spirit permits healing use of its water but refuses any poison delivery or siege application. The city permits weapon research, while the source does not.

Source Refusal blocks that source route regardless of city law. Researchers may seek another lawful mechanism, negotiate, or violate the relationship and face actual failure and consequence. The spirit's refusal does not prohibit unrelated chemistry everywhere.

### Monster Funeral Taboo

A carrion species consumes the dead as a sacred communal remembrance rite. A neighboring human state calls the rite forbidden corpse magic, though no magical effect occurs.

The state prohibition and species taboo around refusing the rite are both social facts. Neither can invent magic. Diplomacy must address remains, consent, jurisdiction, mourning, and minority rights rather than testing imaginary necromantic power.

### A Restricted Archive Manual

An Archive Interface reveals fragments of a containment manual for a historic Mana plague but withholds the section that cultured the agent. A healer petitions for defensive access during a new outbreak.

The Review Route can release diagnostic and containment sections under supervision without granting the missing culture process. Archive access does not make the record complete, current, or directly usable in the new Age.

### Confiscating a Soul Weapon

A government bans a Soul Weapon Function that opens prison locks and orders the vessel melted. The Weapon Soul did not choose the crime and offers to accept a bounded Interface restriction.

The Function, wielder's conduct, Weapon Soul agency, vessel continuity, evidence, and public risk are separate. Enforcement may restrict use or custody, but destruction is harm to a person and requires more than treating the weapon as contraband equipment.

### Forbidden in One Age, Necessary in Another

An Age with abundant ambient Mana bans bodily Reservoir cultivation because overloads once devastated cities. After a World Reset, ambient Mana nearly vanishes and carefully cultivated Reservoirs become essential for healing.

The old prohibition may survive in doctrine and institutions while its hazard assumptions change. Repeal still requires evidence, retraining, and new safeguards; the Reset does not make every cultivation method safe.

### The Glamour of the Ban

A novice assumes a forbidden flame spell must be stronger than academy fire magic. The actual Pattern is inefficient, easy to trace, and prohibited because its source consumes a protected nesting mineral.

The ban reflects ecology, not potency. Using the spell threatens habitat and legal consequences while offering no secret damage bonus.

### Emergency Use and Review

A quarantined town uses a prohibited weather Ritual to divert toxic ash. The emergency authorization defines target area, roles, abort conditions, evacuation, residue monitoring, and post-event review.

The Ritual succeeds but floods farmland downstream. Emergency necessity does not erase that consequence, the affected farmers, or the need for restitution and review.
