# Skill Consolidation and Historical Scope

## Purpose

This document owns routine consolidation of substantially redundant Skills and history-based interpretation of broad, merged, or Conceptual Skill scope. It implements FR-006 and FR-007 without restricting legitimate Adaptive Skill emergence, duplicating Skill Fusion, or replacing Skill Evolution.

## Document Control

- **Owner:** Skill Engine
- **Dependencies:** [Adaptive Skills](ADAPTIVE_SKILLS.md), [Skill Evolution](SKILL_EVOLUTION.md), [Skill Fusion](SKILL_FUSION.md), [Conceptual Skills](CONCEPTUAL_SKILLS.md), [Skill Engine Safeguards](SKILL_ENGINE_SAFEGUARDS.md), and [Skill Development](../progression/SKILL_DEVELOPMENT.md)
- **Extensions:** [Retained Cross-Life Development](../progression/RETAINED_CROSS_LIFE_DEVELOPMENT.md), [Reincarnation Skill Crossover](REINCARNATION_SKILL_CROSSOVER.md), and campaign persistence
- **Consumers:** GM adjudication, current Skill records, Life Archive references, migration, player views, and validation
- **Repository boundary:** no populated Skill, holder, campaign, or migration record belongs here

## Core Rules

Adaptive Skills may emerge whenever their evidence supports a distinct learned capability. Skill-list cleanliness must not suppress legitimate emergence.

Skills should consolidate regularly when accumulated play establishes that several records represent substantially the same underlying capability. Consolidation is routine representation maintenance, not a reward, a rare transformation, or a way to manufacture power.

A Skill name describes capability; it does not authorize capability. Acquisition, Development, applications, relevant failures, predecessors, consolidation, Evolution, embodiment-specific expressions, cross-Life translation, access, and supporting mechanics establish practical and conceptual scope.

## Capability Lifecycle

```text
experience
-> adaptive Skill emergence
-> further Development
-> overlap becomes clear
-> consolidation
-> one cleaner active Skill with traceable predecessors
```

Repeated equivalent challenges normally deepen an existing relevant Skill. A new Skill remains appropriate when the learner develops a mechanically distinct function, owner, mechanism, requirement, failure pattern, or meaningful gameplay choice.

## Consolidation Test

The central question is:

> Are these records meaningfully developing the same capability?

Names are candidate signals only. Review:

- acquisition and instruction history;
- recurring problem and mechanical function;
- methods, decisions, feedback, and correction;
- successful applications and relevant failures;
- underlying body, sense, cognition, tool, Mana, or other mechanism;
- Development evidence and historical peaks;
- embodiment-specific expressions and Receiving Routes;
- predecessor, successor, Evolution, and translation history;
- repeated overlap during actual play;
- whether keeping the records separate preserves a meaningful tactical, developmental, or ownership distinction.

Consolidate only when the evidence supports one coherent capability and separate records would duplicate current ownership or Development. Leave ambiguous candidates separate until evidence improves.

## Consolidation Outcomes

### New Coherent Identity

Use a new stable Skill identity when no predecessor truthfully represents the whole consolidated capability. Link every predecessor as `consolidated_into`, mark each predecessor superseded for current-state use, and retain its historical record.

### Existing Identity Absorbs Duplicate Representation

Use the established Skill identity when one record already owns the complete capability and later records are duplicate labels or needlessly separate narrow representations. Link absorbed records as predecessors and preserve their provenance. Absorption cannot retroactively claim applications absent from all evidence.

Neither outcome deletes history. The current Skill owner points to one active coherent identity; lineage records explain how it came to exist.

## Development Preservation

Consolidation preserves legitimate Development without adding overlapping evidence twice.

1. establish the Development and historical territory demonstrated by each predecessor;
2. identify evidence shared by several records, including the same exposure, training period, application, or adaptation;
3. count each meaningful adaptation once under its original owner;
4. preserve distinct non-overlapping evidence that genuinely broadened or deepened the shared capability;
5. derive the consolidated current profile from the strongest coherent combined evidence;
6. preserve peaks, limits, rust, embodiment expressions, and uncertainty through references rather than summing labels;
7. validate that the result grants no capability or Development unsupported by the predecessor history.

Two Level 10 predecessors do not automatically create Level 20. Local levels, XP records, qualitative mastery, or other Development representations follow their existing owner rules. Where exact overlap cannot be reconstructed, use the least overstated defensible result and retain an uncertainty note.

## Scope Union

The resulting Skill receives the coherent union of historically justified predecessor capability. It does not receive the linguistic union of predecessor names.

The union may preserve:

- established operations;
- demonstrated target and environmental ranges;
- distinct compatible expression routes;
- validated techniques and applications;
- known limitations and failure conditions;
- retained-development provenance from every qualifying source Life.

It does not invent an operation, route, scale, target, authority, immunity, magical effect, or conceptual relationship no predecessor established.

## Boundaries With Other Skill Operations

| Operation | Governing question | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ordinary Development** | Did one existing capability become more reliable, deep, efficient, or adaptable? | Update that Skill's Development. |
| **Skill Evolution** | Did one established Skill qualitatively transform through earned change? | Apply the Evolution lineage and tradeoffs. |
| **Skill Fusion** | Did distinct Component Skills develop a genuinely new integrated competency beyond separate use? | Create the bounded fusion identity and component relations. |
| **Skill Consolidation** | Did several records prove to be substantially redundant representations of one capability? | Supersede duplicates under one coherent active identity while retaining provenance. |
| **Scope adjudication** | Does a proposed application follow from established history and current mechanisms? | Classify the application without changing identity unless later Development warrants it. |

Skill Consolidation is not Skill Fusion. Consolidation does not satisfy Fusion's distinct-utility requirement, and Fusion does not exist merely to clean a list. Evolution does not require several predecessors. Renaming alone performs none of these operations.

## Repeated Exposure and Harm

Repeated equivalent adversity normally contributes to the already relevant capability and experiences ordinary diminishing novelty and returns. It does not continually produce differently named copies.

Deliberate meaningless harm provides no privileged Development route. Exposure must still involve usable feedback, adjustment, integration, meaningful consequence, and actual changed capability. Biological and magical limits, injury, recovery, maladaptation, and risk remain real.

Heat exposure might legitimately separate into thermal tissue adaptation, learned pain regulation, Mana heat dissipation, and fire-affinity manipulation when those effects have different owners and mechanics. Their shared theme does not make them duplicate Skills.

## Historical Scope Model

Interpret every broad, merged, ambiguous, or Conceptual Skill through the evidence chain:

```text
acquisition
-> Development activity
-> applications and relevant failures
-> predecessor and consolidation lineage
-> Skill Evolution
-> embodiment expressions and cross-Life translation
-> current access and enabling mechanics
-> proposed application
```

The GM need not store every trivial use. Preserve enough canonical evidence to answer: **Why can this Skill do this?**

## Scope Categories

### Established Application

The capability has been demonstrated or is mechanically equivalent to established use under sufficiently comparable conditions. Resolve it through ordinary adjudication; changed opposition or context can still alter effectiveness.

### Reasonable Extension

The proposed application follows naturally from established principles but reaches new material, scale, target, route, environment, precision, or conceptual relationship. It is not granted automatically. Experimentation, training, risk, Skill Development, or Skill Evolution may establish it.

### Related but Distinct Capability

The application shares a theme but uses a sufficiently different function, owner, mechanism, receiving route, or Development path. Represent it through the appropriate separate Skill, trait, spell, Evolution, or other owning system if earned.

### Unsupported Interpretation

The claim depends primarily on the wording or metaphor of the Skill name. The Skill does not grant it. A player may still pursue an in-world route to develop a legitimate capability.

These are adjudication categories, not mandatory stored states or an exhaustive application list.

## Conceptual Scope

A Conceptual Domain is a bounded field of interpretation, not universal ownership. Apply the complete [Conceptual Skill](CONCEPTUAL_SKILLS.md) requirements and its present access route.

`Boundary Cutting` learned through physical edge recognition, weapon alignment, material seams, and guarded openings can cut the physical boundaries it has actually developed to address. Its name does not automatically permit cutting walls, wards, space, dimensions, concepts, contracts, social divisions, or causality.

Breadth can be earned. If the practitioner later studies magical barrier structure, acquires a valid magical cutting route, tests varied wards, learns from failure, pays the route's costs, and completes appropriate Development or Skill Evolution, magical-barrier cutting may become a Reasonable Extension and then an Established Application. The abstract label did not cause the growth.

## Cross-Life and Embodiment Lineage

Consolidation must preserve:

- every predecessor Skill ID;
- acquisition and Development provenance;
- source Life and embodiment references;
- expression and Translation Bridge lineage;
- historical territory and peaks;
- Evolution and Fusion relations;
- relevant scope evidence and failures;
- the consolidation event and resulting active identity.

[Retained Cross-Life Development](../progression/RETAINED_CROSS_LIFE_DEVELOPMENT.md) follows those relations to locate qualifying Historical Contributions. A rename or consolidation cannot erase eligibility. It also cannot turn duplicate predecessor evidence into several contributions.

Current embodiment and Receiving Routes remain binding. Historical scope proves what was developed, not what the present body can express.

## Persistence Contract

Current Skill state remains owned by the canonical Skill/Development record. Consolidation and Evolution/Fusion relations are historical lineage. Life Archive and summaries may reference them but do not own current capability.

Use existing stable-ID and typed-reference structures to record:

- source Skill ID;
- destination Skill ID;
- relation type such as `consolidated_into`, `evolved_into`, or `fused_into`;
- effective event and time;
- disposition of the source record;
- evidence and review basis;
- Development reconciliation provenance;
- scope preserved, excluded, or uncertain;
- validation result.

The active current record describes purpose, included and excluded operations, current expression, and limits. Historical predecessors remain immutable evidence except through authorized correction.

No new physical SQL table is required by this clarification when an implementation already supports typed Skill references, versioned records, and supersession. A SQLite realization may normalize these relations into a lineage table with foreign keys to stable Skill IDs. It must not store comma-separated predecessor IDs or copy mutable Skill levels into lineage rows.

Derived summaries, player views, and Life summaries are non-authoritative. They may display the current label and selected predecessors but cannot redefine scope or Development.

## Existing-Campaign Adoption

1. create a complete backup under the migration protocol;
2. inventory active Skills and identify overlap candidates without changing them;
3. inspect acquisition, Development, function, applications, failures, mechanisms, embodiments, and lineage;
4. leave ambiguous or mechanically distinct Skills separate;
5. select a truthful active identity for each justified consolidation;
6. reconcile overlapping Development once and preserve distinct evidence;
7. write predecessor, supersession, event, and scope provenance;
8. update references to the active identity without deleting historical links;
9. validate scope, current ownership, cross-Life traceability, references, and numerical changes;
10. activate only after ordinary migration and read-back validation succeeds.

This repository performs no populated campaign migration.

## GM Operating Procedure

1. detect growing overlap, repeated narrow emergence, or a scope dispute;
2. inspect canonical Skill history and current capability evidence;
3. classify the issue as ordinary Development, Evolution, Fusion, consolidation, or scope ambiguity;
4. preserve separate Skills where meaningful mechanical distinctions remain;
5. consolidate redundant records when one capability is established;
6. reconcile Development without adding overlapping evidence twice;
7. preserve stable IDs, predecessor relations, embodiments, Lives, and application history;
8. classify proposed uses as Established, Reasonable Extension, Related but Distinct, or Unsupported;
9. reject name-only interpretations while leaving legitimate Development routes open;
10. persist only the affected current and lineage records and validate them.

Simple obvious cleanup may be routine. Ambiguous identity, Development, or scope changes require GM review. Automated metadata similarity may flag candidates but must not merge them.

## Player-Facing Legibility

An authorized player view should show enough to understand the current Skill:

- concise purpose;
- established applications relevant to current knowledge;
- notable known limitations;
- predecessor names where useful and discoverable;
- whether a proposed use is established or requires Development.

Do not expose GM Secrets, undiscovered applications, protected failures, or cross-Life history the player is not entitled to know. The description and history support interpretation; no exhaustive legal-application list is required.

## Canonical Cases

### A. Clear Redundant Merge

`Heat Resistance`, `Burn Tolerance`, and `Thermal Endurance` arose from substantially the same learned thermal-regulation history. They consolidate into `Thermal Resistance`. Shared burn events count once; distinct established regulation evidence remains. The predecessor records are superseded and traceable.

### B. Meaningful Distinction

`Swordsmanship` and `Spear Fighting` retain different reach, leverage, movement, recovery, tactics, and training. They remain separate even though both use weapons.

### C. Excessively Broad Merge

Poison resistance, fear regulation, thermal resistance, and impact bracing do not become universal `Resistance`. Their mechanisms and failures differ. A broad label cannot erase those distinctions.

### D. Conceptual-Name Abuse

Physical `Boundary Cutting` cannot sever a contract, social division, dimension, or causal relation merely because each may be called a boundary. Those claims are Unsupported Interpretations without relevant history and access.

### E. Earned Extension

The same practitioner develops magical-barrier analysis and a valid Mana cutting expression through varied ward training and failure. Barrier cutting becomes an earned application through Development or Evolution; it was not latent authority supplied by the name.

### F. Cross-Life Provenance

Life 1 developed `Heat Resistance`; Life 3 developed `Burn Tolerance`; Life 6 consolidated them as `Thermal Resistance`. A later Life follows predecessor relations to both source Lives. Overlapping exposure is counted once, while distinct Historical Contributions remain eligible under FR-001/FR-005.

### G. Repeated Injury

Repeated identical burns mostly deepen or test an existing thermal capability with diminishing novelty. Meaningless self-injury does not spawn `Scorch Resistance`, `Minor Burn Tolerance`, and further copies or bypass biological limits and consequences.

## Validation

A valid consolidation and scope implementation confirms:

- every active Skill identity and predecessor reference resolves;
- predecessor records remain historically traceable and are not competing current owners;
- overlapping evidence is not double-counted;
- legitimate Development, peaks, expressions, and provenance are preserved;
- meaningful distinctions remain separate;
- the resulting scope is the coherent evidence-supported union, not name-based expansion;
- Evolution and Fusion relations retain their own meanings;
- FR-001/FR-005 contribution and translation history remains traversable;
- current embodiment, access, costs, authority, and prerequisites remain necessary;
- repeated adversity cannot generate unlimited narrow Skills;
- player views respect knowledge boundaries;
- campaign migration is backed up, reviewed, and validated.

## Safeguards

- No suppression of justified Adaptive Skill emergence merely to keep a short list.
- No merge by name, tag, shared damage type, or automated similarity alone.
- No naive addition of predecessor levels, XP, or duplicated evidence.
- No deletion of predecessor history or cross-Life provenance.
- No universal `Resistance`, `Weapon Use`, or comparable breadth without earned coherent evidence.
- No name-based conceptual authority or metaphor-to-mechanic conversion.
- No replacement of Skill Evolution, Skill Fusion, traits, Magic, or another owner.
- No automatic campaign migration or silent correction.
- No injury, suffering, repetition, or crisis farming.
- No exhaustive static application catalog that prevents reasonable future Development.
