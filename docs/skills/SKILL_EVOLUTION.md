# Skill Evolution

## Purpose

This document defines how an established Skill can undergo a qualitative transformation through deeper integration, specialisation, generalisation, efficiency, changed embodiment, conceptual understanding, or sustained meaningful use. Skill Evolution changes how a learned capability is organised or what it can coherently do; it is not a routine increase in proficiency and is not biological [Evolution](../../design/TERMINOLOGY.md#evolution).

Skill Evolution remains part of multidimensional Development. It creates no universal rank, upgrade currency, guaranteed successor, or permanent measure of power.

## Core Rule

A **Skill Evolution** occurs when present-life Development produces a stable qualitative change in a Skill's identity, method, scope, requirements, relationships, or characteristic tradeoffs. The practitioner must integrate the change into usable capability and demonstrate it across relevant variation. A new name, repeated use, isolated breakthrough, institutional promotion, or temporary enhancement does not cause an evolution by itself.

An evolved Skill remains a bounded learned capability. It cannot supply missing anatomy, magical access, tools, authority, relationships, species traits, or Soul effects owned by another system.

## Skill Evolution and the Shared Model

Evolution must be recorded across the complete [Shared Skill Model](README.md#shared-skill-model):

| Dimension | Evolution question |
| --- | --- |
| **Skill Identity** | What coherent capability changed, and what now distinguishes it from its prior form? |
| **Origin** | Which human, monster, shared, professional, magical, species-shaped, or other route produced the change? |
| **Requirements** | Which foundations, body structures, senses, tools, resources, permissions, relationships, or environments remain necessary? |
| **Persistent Familiarity** | Which legitimately earned history supports future relearning without granting present expression? |
| **Current Access** | Which parts of the prior and evolved forms can this incarnation presently understand or invoke? |
| **Embodied Expression** | How does the current body and available system express, constrain, or translate the evolution? |
| **Practised Reliability** | Under which pressures and variations has the changed capability become dependable? |
| **Contextual Effectiveness** | Which objectives and conditions favour the evolved form, and which expose its tradeoffs? |
| **Evolution State** | Is the change an emerging route, stable evolution, alternative branch, internalised form, or currently inaccessible history? |
| **Visibility** | Who recognises, names, certifies, misunderstands, conceals, or cannot perceive the change? |

These dimensions are descriptive. They must not be added together as an evolution score or converted into a universal tier.

## Terms

### Skill Evolution

A stable qualitative transformation in an established Skill produced through legitimate Development and integration. It changes the capability's organisation, method, scope, requirements, or characteristic tradeoffs without becoming biological Evolution.

### Evolution Pattern

A description of the principal way a Skill changed, such as refinement, specialisation, broadening, compression, or embodiment translation. Patterns may overlap and are not fixed stages.

### Prior Form

The Skill expression from which an evolution developed. A Prior Form may remain available, become conditional, lose reliability, survive as a branch, or be partly reorganised; it is not automatically deleted or perfectly retained.

### Evolution Evidence

Observable support that a qualitative change has become integrated capability: changed problem solving, reliable expression, meaningful variation, corrected failure, understood requirements, and stable tradeoffs. One dramatic success may reveal an emerging route but does not alone prove completion.

## What Skill Evolution Is Not

| Related change | Distinction |
| --- | --- |
| **Improvement** | Better speed, precision, knowledge, endurance, judgment, or reliability within the same essential Skill identity. Improvement may prepare an evolution without constituting one. |
| **Mastery** | Deep, adaptable, reliable understanding within a bounded scope. A Skill may be mastered without evolving, and an evolved form may begin with low reliability. |
| **Skill Evolution** | A qualitative reorganisation or transformation of an established learned capability. |
| **Skill Fusion** | A later Phase 3 process in which two or more genuinely integrated Skills produce a coherent new competency. Evolution cannot be used to smuggle an undeveloped second Skill into one existing Skill. |
| **Mutation** | A change to a body's inherited or current structure through an established bodily route. Mutation may alter Skill requirements or expression but does not itself evolve the Skill. |
| **Biological Evolution** | A lasting transition into a distinct species form or stage. Phase 4 owns its routes and outcomes. |
| **Renaming** | A changed label, translation, fashion, or reinterpretation without a material capability change. Renaming records language, not Development. |
| **Institutional promotion** | A rank, licence, office, certification, or recognition granted by an authority. It may alter access and World Recognition without changing the Skill itself. |

## Conditions for Evolution

Skill Evolution normally requires all of the following in forms appropriate to the capability:

1. **Established foundation:** a real Skill or coherent Skill branch already exists; an isolated action cannot evolve.
2. **Developmental pressure or purpose:** the Prior Form encounters a recurring need, opportunity, limitation, responsibility, or line of inquiry that matters to its use.
3. **Active engagement:** the practitioner trains, experiments, studies, reflects, receives instruction, or acts meaningfully rather than merely enduring time or repetition.
4. **Variation and feedback:** the proposed change survives more than one protected script and produces information from success, failure, correction, and changed conditions.
5. **Qualitative distinction:** the changed form differs materially in method, organisation, scope, requirements, or tradeoffs rather than only degree or name.
6. **Integration:** perception, judgment, execution, correction, and consequences form one coherent capability.
7. **Current means:** the present body, mind, tools, resources, access, relationships, or metaphysical structures can actually support the expression being claimed.
8. **Practised Reliability:** the transformed form becomes dependable enough for its intended scope, including known failure conditions.
9. **Cost and tradeoff accounting:** demands, exclusions, lost transfer, risks, dependencies, and maintenance are recorded instead of hidden behind an upgrade label.

Meaningful use can occur in safe training, craft, research, service, cooperation, exploration, crisis, or conflict. Suffering, killing, danger, and deprivation are neither mandatory nor intrinsically more valuable.

## Evolution Patterns

Evolution Patterns describe what changed. They are analytical tools, not a menu or mandatory sequence.

### Refinement

The Skill reorganises around finer control, discrimination, timing, diagnosis, or error correction. Refinement may reduce waste while demanding greater attention or more exact conditions.

### Specialisation

The Skill narrows around a recurring purpose, opponent, environment, tool, or method. It may become exceptional within that scope while transferring poorly elsewhere.

### Broadening

The Skill integrates enough variation to address a wider family of related problems. Breadth requires evidence in those contexts and may trade peak precision for adaptable coverage.

### Compression

Many consciously managed steps become one coherent pattern that can be initiated and monitored as a whole. Compression reduces coordination burden but may hide errors or become difficult to modify.

### Efficiency

The Skill reaches comparable outcomes with less wasted movement, attention, material, mana, time, or coordination. Efficiency never makes a real cost vanish and may depend on tight assumptions.

### Reliability

The Skill transforms around recovery, fault tolerance, safe failure, consistency, or operation under disruption. It may sacrifice speed or peak output to remain dependable.

### Contextual Adaptation

The Skill becomes organised around a persistent environment, role, hazard, opponent class, or resource condition. The adaptation remains bounded by the contexts actually learned.

### Conceptual Elevation

The Skill begins operating through a coherent understanding of an underlying relationship or principle. This pattern requires the later Conceptual Skills rules before it can grant conceptual effects; until then it records only a constrained future route.

### Embodiment Translation

The Skill is rebuilt around materially different anatomy, senses, scale, cognition, magical structures, or movement. Translation creates a current expression rather than restoring a former body.

### Tool Integration

A recurring tool, weapon, interface, material, or prepared system becomes deeply integrated into execution and judgment. The Skill owns the learned coordination, while the tool and its effects remain external dependencies.

### Group Integration

The Skill evolves around roles, signals, trust, timing, redundancy, and shared correction among willing participants. It owns coordination, not every member's capabilities or agency.

### Ritualisation

The Skill becomes a repeatable structured process with preparation, roles, checks, symbolic or technical steps, and recovery procedures. Ritualisation can improve reproducibility while increasing setup and dependency costs.

### Internalisation

Parts of a once-deliberate process become stable perception, regulation, or response. Internalisation does not make the Skill free, infallible, always active, or immune to conscious override; full active and passive rules belong to the later Phase 3 task.

## Outcomes and Prior Forms

Skill Evolution does not always replace what came before. Record one of these relationships for each material function:

- **Layered:** the evolved form adds an integrated mode while the Prior Form remains reliable.
- **Branched:** the practitioner can sustain alternative forms with different assumptions and tradeoffs.
- **Conditional:** the Prior Form remains available only with particular embodiment, tools, environments, or access.
- **Reorganised:** many earlier techniques remain but are understood and executed through a changed structure.
- **Narrowed:** the evolution improves one scope while old breadth rusts or loses transfer.
- **Superseded:** an earlier method is abandoned because its habits conflict with the evolved form; its history persists, but current reliability does not.
- **Latent:** the evolved form is genuinely learned but currently inaccessible or inexpressible.

An evolution may split into alternatives when their habits, tools, objectives, or assumptions cannot be maintained together. Practice allocation matters: preserving several forms consumes time, attention, calibration, and suitable contexts.

## Costs, Limits, and Failure

An evolved Skill may:

- demand more concentration, stamina, mana, material, preparation, space, precision, or cooperation;
- lose breadth or become brittle outside its intended context;
- require a particular body, sense, tool, institution, relationship, environment, or metaphysical condition;
- introduce new failure modes or make old errors more consequential;
- conflict with ingrained timing, posture, priorities, or risk tolerance from the Prior Form;
- begin with lower Practised Reliability despite greater potential;
- create upkeep needs because uncommon methods receive little ordinary practice;
- become temporarily suppressed by injury, fatigue, altered embodiment, lost equipment, broken trust, changed world laws, or denied permission.

Attempted evolution can fail. The result may remain ordinary improvement, an unstable technique, an [Adaptive Skill Candidate](ADAPTIVE_SKILLS.md#adaptive-skill-candidate), a harmful Habit, a branch needing more evidence, or a lesson about incompatible requirements. Failure creates no consolation evolution and does not erase legitimate Development in the Prior Form.

## Reincarnation and Persistence

A completed evolution may contribute structure and Skill XP to the Skill's [Skill Imprint](../progression/SKILL_DEVELOPMENT.md#skill-imprint). What persists is developmental history, not guaranteed present ownership of every expression.

After Reincarnation:

- the Prior Form and evolved form may have different Current Access;
- a new body may support one form better than the other;
- Embodiment Translation may be required again because each receiving body is specific;
- retained familiarity can accelerate relearning but cannot bypass calibration or current practice;
- changed tools, cultures, magic, institutions, or world laws can make former assumptions obsolete;
- an inaccessible evolution remains part of Persistent Familiarity without current output;
- a translated current form belongs to its valid [Receiving Route](REINCARNATION_SKILL_CROSSOVER.md#receiving-route).

No reincarnation count, Soul Depth horizon, Resonance state, or volume of Skill XP automatically evolves a Skill.

## Interactions

- **Adaptive Skills:** an Adaptive Skill can later evolve once it has an established identity and sufficient Evolution Evidence. Novel emergence and transformation are separate claims.
- **Human and Monster Skill Trees:** evolution follows the practitioner's actual route and may create a branch without turning either tree into a universal ladder.
- **Skill Crossover:** crossover may supply retained principles or a Receiving Route, but present-life Development owns any evolved expression.
- **Development Tracks:** physical, magical, professional, social, and species Development may support or limit an evolution while retaining ownership of their effects.
- **Soul Depth:** may support integration of complex experience without granting a Skill or its evolution.
- **Soul Resonance:** may improve access to a genuine Skill history or relevant relationship without performing the transformation.
- **Soul Echoes:** may demonstrate, advise, or expose tradeoffs; they do not install their evolved reliability into the current incarnation.
- **Retained Instincts:** may cue learned attention or response, but an instinct is neither proof nor automatic execution of an evolved Skill.
- **Soul Titles:** may alter recognition and opportunities around an evolution without serving as its prerequisite unless a separate rule establishes a genuine contextual requirement.
- **Soul Avatars:** may synthesise relevant history under their own limits without turning every historical Skill evolution into present mastery.
- **Soul Weapons:** may act as tools, partners, teachers, or co-adapting dependencies. Weapon effects and Weapon Soul agency remain separately owned.
- **Akashic Archive:** may preserve evidence, methods, and failed alternatives without granting Current Access or Practised Reliability.
- **World Engine:** cultures, ecologies, institutions, technology, magic, scarcity, and conflict shape available pressures and recognition; the world need not evolve a Skill because a protagonist seeks it.

## Worked Examples

### Basic Sword Control to Duelling Specialisation

A swordswoman with reliable balance, edge control, recovery, and distance judgment spends years facing varied single opponents under rules where openings and commitment matter more than formation discipline. She develops a duelling specialisation centred on initiative traps, line denial, and reading one opponent's recovery.

The form is unusually effective in formal or close single combat but transfers poorly to shield formations, crowds, missile threats, and protecting several allies. Her basic sword control remains available, while maintaining both forms requires deliberate practice.

### Tracking to Multisensory Pursuit

A tracker learns to combine spoor, disturbed vegetation, scent movement, sound occlusion, heat traces, and prey decision patterns. The change is not "better tracking" alone: evidence from several senses is weighted, challenged, and revised as one pursuit model.

Smoke, magical masking, sensory injury, and unfamiliar ecologies can corrupt different inputs. The evolution improves resilience when channels agree but creates cognitive load and false synthesis when they do not.

### Healing to Battlefield Triage

A healer repeatedly works under limited time, unsafe terrain, scarce supplies, and many simultaneous casualties. Medical knowledge, rapid assessment, delegation, safe delay, resource allocation, and evacuation judgment become an integrated battlefield-triage Skill.

It does not make treatment stronger or guarantee morally comfortable outcomes. It trades exhaustive diagnosis for bounded prioritisation and depends on helpers, communication, transport, and current medical or magical access.

### Pack Coordination to Distributed Tactical Command

A monster pack develops signals, delegated roles, local initiative, fallback patterns, and error correction that continue when the leader cannot observe every member. Coordination evolves from following immediate cues into distributed tactical command.

The Skill belongs to the stable collective structure and participating roles as established by monster Skill ownership. It does not grant each member every role, compel obedience, or function with strangers who have not developed the shared protocol.

### Web-Building to Structural Engineering

A web-building creature studies load paths, material fatigue, redundancy, foundations, weather, and repair across structures larger than a hunting web. Through models, failed builds, instruction, and varied materials, its construction Skill evolves toward structural engineering.

Spinneret control and tension sense remain valuable, but architecture at settlement scale requires measurement, materials knowledge, tools, safety judgment, and social coordination. A later human incarnation may retain principles while needing current education and a new Receiving Route.

### Magical Perception to Conceptual Pattern Recognition

A mage who can perceive mana signatures studies how different spells preserve symmetry, exchange, boundary, and sequence despite different elements. The Skill begins evolving toward recognition of conceptual patterns rather than memorised spell forms.

Until Conceptual Skill requirements are defined and met, this is an emerging route, not permission to read every magic or rewrite reality. Unknown laws, deliberate noise, missing senses, and interpretive bias remain serious limits.

## GM Procedure

When adjudicating a proposed Skill Evolution:

1. identify the established Skill and its Prior Form;
2. state the proposed qualitative change in capability;
3. distinguish it from improvement, mastery, fusion, mutation, biological Evolution, renaming, and promotion;
4. identify the Evolution Pattern or overlapping patterns involved;
5. verify current requirements, meaningful engagement, variation, feedback, and integration;
6. record Evolution Evidence and unresolved failure conditions;
7. specify what happens to each important Prior Form use;
8. record new costs, dependencies, incompatibilities, and tradeoffs;
9. assess all ten Shared Skill Model dimensions without producing a score;
10. confirm that no later system or external effect has been silently implemented;
11. decide whether the claim is complete, emerging, merely improved, misclassified, latent, or failed;
12. communicate observable change and uncertainty rather than announcing a universal tier.

## Safeguards

- **No arbitrary upgrades:** narrative importance, a dramatic name, rarity, or GM convenience cannot substitute for Evolution Evidence.
- **No raw-repetition evolution:** repeating solved actions without meaningful variation, feedback, adjustment, or integration produces no automatic transformation.
- **No identical stage ladders:** different Skills, practitioners, bodies, cultures, and contexts may transform through different patterns and tradeoffs.
- **No automatic replacement:** an evolved form does not silently preserve, improve, or delete every use of the Prior Form; each material relationship is recorded.
- **No prerequisite bypass:** Skill Evolution cannot create missing anatomy, traits, magic, tools, knowledge, authority, relationships, consent, or other owned requirements.
- **No universal superiority:** a later or rarer form may be worse for a particular objective, environment, body, team, or risk tolerance.
- **No suffering reward:** injury, trauma, killing, deprivation, and near death are not privileged evolution currencies.
- **No external-effect capture:** a tool, ally, institution, spell, Soul effect, or environment remains a dependency rather than becoming an unconditional personal Skill effect.
- **No disguised fusion:** if the claim requires two or more independently developed Skills to become a new competency, it waits for the Skill Fusion rules.
- **No retroactive completion:** a surprising success may reveal a route or prior evidence, but it cannot invent years of integration that were never established.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- Skill Fusion;
- complete active and passive Skill modes;
- Hidden Skills or their discovery procedures;
- Conceptual Skill effects;
- monster species stages, biological Evolution branches, mutations, or hybrid forms;
- classes, professions, magical schools, institutional rank ladders, or complete magic rules;
- Soul Weapon evolution;
- campaign-specific Skills or characters.
