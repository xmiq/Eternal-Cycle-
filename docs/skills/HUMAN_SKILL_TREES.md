# Human Skill Trees

## Purpose

This document defines how human learned capabilities may be represented as structured relationships among competencies, techniques, Disciplines, traditions, Professions, and conceptual understanding.

It defines a design and adjudication structure. It does not prescribe one tree for every human, make trees universally visible in-world, or implement the Classes, institutions, and campaign-specific content owned by the [Human Classes and Professions](../human/README.md) framework.

## Core Rule

A **Human Skill Tree** is a contextual map of valid learning relationships. It shows how Skills can share foundations, require present conditions, branch into alternatives, develop specialisations, and reach comparable outcomes through different histories.

The map records possible routes. It does not grant Skills, waive prerequisites, force a sequence, or turn human learning into a universal level ladder.

## Shared Skill Model

Use the ten dimensions in the [Skill Engine index](README.md#shared-skill-model) for every Skill or branch:

| Dimension | Human-tree application |
| --- | --- |
| **Skill Identity** | Name the bounded function rather than a social label or vague field. |
| **Origin** | Record the culture, tradition, Profession, institution, mentor, self-taught route, or other provenance. |
| **Requirements** | State bodily, sensory, cognitive, material, social, legal, institutional, and magical conditions. |
| **Persistent Familiarity** | Preserve only legitimate Skill XP and Skill Imprints, not a whole tree or institution. |
| **Current Access** | Identify what the present incarnation can recall, interpret, practise, or be taught. |
| **Embodied Expression** | Check current anatomy, senses, health, tools, language, resources, and magical structures. |
| **Practised Reliability** | Assess performance across pressure, variation, fatigue, imperfect tools, and consequences. |
| **Contextual Effectiveness** | Compare the branch with the present objective, opposition, culture, environment, and support. |
| **Evolution State** | Record valid refinement, specialisation, broadening, translation, or later transformation separately. |
| **Visibility** | State whether practitioners, institutions, observers, or the user know or misclassify the route. |

No dimension may be summed into a universal Skill rating.

## Terms

### Human Skill Tree

A contextual analytical structure connecting human learning routes, prerequisites, alternatives, techniques, and mastery paths. It may describe one tradition, Profession, culture, institution, individual history, or comparison among several routes.

### Skill Root

The practical purpose, problem family, or sustained Discipline that gives a tree its identity. A root such as wound care, bladed combat, navigation, or mediation is narrower than all medicine, all combat, all travel, or all social interaction.

### Skill Branch

A coherent route of further Development distinguished by function, method, requirements, context, or tradeoffs. A branch may contain techniques, specialisations, and alternatives without being a universal tier.

### Skill Prerequisite

A current condition necessary for a particular route to be learned or expressed reliably. It may be a Foundational Skill, body structure, sense, tool, material, knowledge base, language, authority, relationship, environment, or metaphysical access.

### Alternative Branch

One of several valid routes toward a comparable purpose. Alternatives may coexist, compete for practice, encode contradictory habits, or become inaccessible under different conditions.

### Lateral Technique

A method connected across branches because it solves a shared narrow problem. Learning it does not grant either branch as a whole.

### Mastery Path

A pattern of deeper evidence within a bounded route, including refinement, judgment, adaptability, diagnosis, teaching, or pressure-tested reliability. It is not a universal sequence or guaranteed promotion.

### Cultural Variant

A form of a Skill shaped by a culture's tools, values, language, environment, institutions, bodies, and accumulated practice. Shared purpose does not make variants identical or automatically transferable.

## What a Human Skill Tree Is Not

A Human Skill Tree is not:

- a universal character level or power ladder;
- one mandatory path shared by every human;
- a rigid menu visible to every character;
- a list from which every node can eventually be purchased;
- proof that a named branch exists in every Age or culture;
- a replacement for teachers, communities, tools, bodies, institutions, or lived experience;
- a Profession, class, rank, licence, reputation, or social identity;
- a bundle that reincarnates as one unrestricted asset.

An in-world school may diagram its curriculum as a tree. Another tradition may teach through stories, imitation, oral lineages, ritual sequences, rotating apprenticeships, or private experiments without using tree language at all.

## Tree Architecture

### Roots

A root states the recurring function around which related learning is organized. Useful roots are broad enough to support meaningful alternatives but narrow enough to preserve ownership.

`Swordsmanship` may be a root for deliberate fighting with sword-like tools. `Combat` is usually too broad because it hides weapons, bodies, tactics, ranges, group roles, and contexts that develop differently.

`Medicine` may be a Discipline or Profession containing several trees, such as diagnosis, wound care, pharmacology, surgery, public health, or magical treatment. Calling all of them one Skill would erase distinct evidence and requirements.

### Foundations

Foundations are existing Foundational Skills and other current prerequisites that support several routes through actual function. Examples include:

- balance, distance judgment, and safe weapon handling for martial branches;
- anatomy, observation, sanitation, and record interpretation for medical branches;
- material recognition, heat judgment, and tool control for smithing branches;
- language, listening, cultural inquiry, and incentive analysis for diplomatic branches.

Possessing a foundation creates opportunity and may accelerate learning. It does not supply branch-specific decisions, practice, permissions, or reliability.

### Branches

A branch differs in at least one material way:

- objective;
- method or underlying principle;
- required body, sense, tool, or environment;
- knowledge or authority;
- risks and consequences;
- pressure-tested practice;
- tradeoff against another route.

Cosmetic naming, one preferred motion, or a single situation does not require a separate branch.

### Prerequisites

Prerequisites must be causal and current. Common kinds include:

- **capability:** a demonstrated Foundational Skill or technique;
- **embodiment:** hands, breath control, vision, balance, magical Channels, or another functional structure;
- **knowledge:** anatomy, mathematics, law, language, notation, or cultural context;
- **material:** tools, ingredients, workspace, records, or safe practice conditions;
- **social:** a willing teacher, patients, team, audience, counterpart, or community;
- **institutional:** admission, Licence, office, archive access, or legal permission;
- **metaphysical:** magical access, authority, affinity, contract, or compatible world law.

Meeting a prerequisite opens a route; it does not complete the Development that route requires.

### Alternatives and Incompatible Routes

Alternative Branches allow different solutions to related problems. They are not always mutually exclusive, but time, embodiment, risk, identity, resources, and contradictory habits may make simultaneous mastery impractical.

A duelling tradition built around committed forward pressure and one built around elastic retreat may both be effective. A practitioner can study both, but automatic responses may conflict until the distinction is deliberately integrated.

### Specialisations

A Specialisation narrows a branch toward a recurring context. It may gain precision, efficiency, diagnosis, or reliability while losing transfer.

A healer specialising in battlefield triage may outperform a broader physician during mass casualties while remaining less prepared for slow, ambiguous illness. Neither is universally higher.

### Lateral Techniques

Lateral Techniques cross branch boundaries when a narrow method genuinely applies in several routes. Breath pacing might support archery, public speaking, ritual chanting, and pain management, but each use requires different calibration and does not merge those Skills.

### Mastery Paths

Mastery paths describe deepening evidence rather than fixed tiers. They may emphasize:

- refined execution;
- adaptability under variation;
- diagnosis and correction;
- efficiency and conservation;
- strategic or ethical judgment;
- teaching and transmission;
- invention;
- integration with a body, tool, team, or environment.

No Skill must have the same number or order of mastery states as another.

## Human Learning Routes

### Cultural Variants

Culture influences what problems are prioritized, what evidence counts, which tools exist, who may learn, how failure is interpreted, and what names or ranks are used.

Comparable routes may encode different assumptions. A desert navigation tradition may organize movement around stars, water, wind, and oral landmarks; an oceanic tradition may emphasize currents, swell, weather, and collective watchkeeping. Neither automatically includes the other.

### Profession-Linked Branches

A Profession may organize several Skills, Professional Judgment, standards, tools, responsibilities, credentials, and institutional relationships. It creates access and a structured learning route without granting its Skills as a package.

A licensed physician may lack one specialist surgical Skill. An unlicensed field healer may possess excellent wound stabilization and limited diagnostic breadth. Use [Profession Development](../progression/PROFESSION_DEVELOPMENT.md) for the distinction.

### Martial Traditions

Martial traditions may connect weapon handling, movement, perception, tactics, maintenance, group coordination, and ethical or social practices. Membership does not grant every technique, and one tradition does not own all uses of a weapon.

The social framework, doctrine, curricula, Pressure Practice, and recognition of those traditions are defined in [Martial Traditions](../human/MARTIAL_TRADITIONS.md). This document continues to own the learned capabilities they reference.

### Magical Traditions

A magical tradition may connect perception, control, notation, ritual procedure, recovery, safety, and source-specific techniques. Its tree must state the actual magical source, access, embodiment, tools, authority, and world laws it assumes.

The social models, curricula, verification, safety, and recognition of these frameworks are defined in [Magical Schools](../human/MAGICAL_SCHOOLS.md). This document continues to own their learned capabilities. It does not define mana, universal spell tiers, affinities, or the full magic system; use [Magical Development](../progression/MAGICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md) for current boundaries.

### Social and Leadership Skills

Communication, negotiation, mediation, teaching, delegation, command, and governance may share foundations while remaining distinct Skills and relational practices.

No branch becomes mind control. Trust, Loyalty, reputation, Formal Authority, Cultural Legitimacy, relationships, and followers remain outside a personal Skill tree under [Social and Leadership Development](../progression/SOCIAL_AND_LEADERSHIP_DEVELOPMENT.md).

### Scholarly and Technical Skills

Research, archival criticism, mathematics, experimentation, drafting, measurement, diagnosis, and design may connect across scholarly and technical trees. Factual knowledge is evidence and material for these Skills, not proof of practical judgment or execution.

### Survival and Exploration Skills

Navigation, shelter building, foraging, hazard recognition, mapping, tracking, climbing, and expedition planning depend heavily on environment, tools, culture, and body. A forest route may transfer principles to a swamp while leaving important hazards and cues unknown.

### Self-Taught Paths

Self-teaching can create genuine Skill through observation, experimentation, feedback, correction, and meaningful use. It may also produce blind spots, unsafe habits, idiosyncratic notation, or poor ability to communicate the method.

Lack of institutional recognition does not erase capability. Lack of external correction may still limit it.

### Lost and Rediscovered Branches

A branch may become unavailable when its teachers, language, materials, tools, species, institutions, or magical laws disappear. Records, ruins, Soul Echoes, or the Akashic Archive may preserve knowledge without present execution.

Rediscovery requires interpretation, reconstruction, suitable conditions, testing, and current practice. A recovered name or diagram does not restore the lost branch automatically.

### Obsolete Techniques and Incompatible Habits

A once-reliable technique may become obsolete when tools, armor, medicine, law, language, ecology, or magical laws change. Its underlying principles may remain useful while its original execution becomes dangerous or ineffective.

Conflicting habits can reduce reliability even when both routes are valid. Integration requires deliberate cue separation, correction, and pressure testing rather than simply owning both labels.

## Comparable Outcomes Through Different Branches

Two humans may reach a comparable outcome without sharing a tree path.

A locked wound may be closed by conventional suturing, adhesive alchemy, controlled magical knitting, compression and staged healing, or a species-compatible graft. Each route has different owners, requirements, risks, aftercare, and failure states. Comparable immediate closure does not make the Skills identical or grant one route's wider capabilities to another.

The GM compares the outcome under a stated [Assessment Scope](../progression/CAPABILITY_ASSESSMENT.md#start-with-scope), not by searching for which branch has the higher universal tier.

## Local Names, Ranks, and Recognition

Cultures and institutions may use grades, belts, circles, examinations, lineage titles, guild stages, military qualifications, or poetic names. These are local evidence and World Recognition.

A local rank must state:

- issuer and jurisdiction;
- tested Skills and conditions;
- assumed tools, body, language, and doctrine;
- permissions or responsibilities granted;
- omissions and known error modes.

Rank does not create Skill, guarantee present reliability, transfer between institutions, or establish universal superiority.

## Reincarnation and Human Trees

At Final Death, a human tree is not preserved as one unlocked menu. Individual Skill Imprints, Skill XP, knowledge, Retained Instincts, and other properly owned history may persist.

A later human incarnation still needs:

- Current Access to relevant history;
- a valid receiving route;
- compatible embodiment and tools;
- current cultural and technical interpretation;
- present practice and feedback;
- renewed Practised Reliability.

Crossing from a monster or other nonhuman tree requires the dedicated Reincarnation crossover rules. This document establishes no automatic translation.

## Worked Examples

### Swordsmanship

One route begins with shield formation, safe edge control, and group timing before branching into close formation command. Another begins with unarmored distance, footwork, and single-opponent reading before specialising in duels. Both may produce excellent sword control under different conditions; neither grants the other's tactics, body assumptions, or reliability automatically.

### Medicine

An apprenticeship route joins anatomy, sanitation, observation, supervised care, and Professional Judgment. A battlefield route prioritizes triage, bleeding control, transport, and decisions under scarcity. Shared foundations accelerate exchange, but neither rank nor factual anatomy grants the other's pressure-tested practice.

### Smithing

Forge safety, heat judgment, hammer control, and material recognition support weapon, tool, armor, and precision-component branches. Mastery of one alloy or forge design does not grant unfamiliar materials, magical metallurgy, or every product family.

### Diplomacy

Language, listening, incentive analysis, cultural inquiry, and negotiation may branch toward treaty drafting, hostage mediation, trade bargaining, or monster-human contact. A court specialist may fail in a communal oral culture until current calibration replaces assumed etiquette.

### Command

Small-unit tactical command, logistics, training, morale coordination, and civil administration share planning and communication but demand different evidence. A decorated battlefield commander does not automatically possess governance or institutional legitimacy.

### Stealth

Urban blending, wilderness concealment, magical signature control, silent movement, and social misdirection can reach the outcome of avoiding notice through different owners. A practitioner trained against human vision may remain obvious to scent, vibration, or magical perception.

### Navigation

Celestial navigation, dead reckoning, landmark memory, current reading, magical orientation, and instrument use can support one another. Losing the sky, tool, sense, or stable magical law may suppress one branch without erasing its Skill Imprint.

### Ritual Practice

Notation, material preparation, timing, role coordination, source access, and error recovery form separate foundations. Knowing a script does not grant safe ritual execution, and one tradition's authority or metaphysics may not exist in another Age.

### Magical Control

Breath-led control, geometric visualization, sung modulation, organ-based regulation, and tool-mediated shaping may reach comparable precision through different embodiments. The routes remain bound to their sources, Channels, costs, and practice.

### Engineering

Measurement, material behavior, drafting, failure analysis, mathematics, fabrication, and project coordination support branches such as bridges, water systems, fortifications, or magical devices. A brilliant design Skill does not grant fabrication, institutional resources, or safe construction leadership.

## Safeguards

- **No giant umbrella Skills:** split a label when its functions, requirements, evidence, or failure states develop independently.
- **No automatic prerequisites:** a foundation opens a route but never grants the Dependent Skill.
- **No tradition bundles:** membership, initiation, inheritance, or a manual does not grant every associated technique.
- **No rank-equals-mastery:** institutional recognition remains scoped evidence and access.
- **No context-free culture transfer:** retained etiquette, language, law, or custom requires current verification.
- **No one-Skill-per-action:** transient actions, tactics, ordinary variations, and cosmetic names do not each become permanent Skills.
- **No universal completion:** breadth consumes time, attention, embodiment, tools, and opportunity; not every route is compatible or available.
- **No tree reincarnation bundle:** persistence follows each capability's Owning System.
- **No body bypass:** anatomy, senses, health, age, and physical calibration remain binding.
- **No full-Magic or Phase-5 shortcut:** human trees may reference a route without defining later systems in its place.

## GM Procedure

When placing or adjudicating a human Skill:

1. state the Skill Identity and bounded function;
2. record Origin and provenance;
3. identify its root, foundations, branch, and any lateral relationships;
4. name current prerequisites and who owns them;
5. separate Persistent Familiarity, Current Access, and Embodied Expression;
6. require evidence for Practised Reliability;
7. assess Contextual Effectiveness under the present objective;
8. record Evolution State under [Skill Evolution](SKILL_EVOLUTION.md) when qualitative transformation is claimed;
9. establish Visibility and World Recognition by observer;
10. reject duplicate labels, automatic bundles, and unsupported future-system claims.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- monster Skill trees;
- detailed Reincarnation crossover;
- adaptive Skill creation, Skill evolution, or Skill fusion;
- active, passive, hidden, or conceptual Skill rules;
- biological evolution, mutations, or species stages;
- canonical human classes, martial schools, magical schools, or institutions;
- the complete magic system;
- one universal catalog of human Skills.

Those systems may build on this structure only through their roadmap tasks.
