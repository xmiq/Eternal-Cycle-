# Reincarnation Skill Crossover

## Purpose

This document defines how a Skill developed in one incarnation may inform capability in another incarnation with different species, anatomy, senses, magical structure, culture, Profession, cognition, or environment.

It completes the bounded crossover framework established by [Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md), [Skill Development](../progression/SKILL_DEVELOPMENT.md), and [Species Development](../progression/SPECIES_DEVELOPMENT.md). It does not replace their persistence, embodiment, or Development rules.

## Core Rule

**Skill Crossover** is a present-life learning process in which legitimate retained history helps an incarnation recover, translate, or reconstruct a bounded capability through a valid current route.

[Retained Cross-Life Development](../progression/RETAINED_CROSS_LIFE_DEVELOPMENT.md) owns contribution stacking, Embodiment Relevance, Historical Territory, and the separation between Familiarity Unlock and Development Boost across multiple Lives. This document owns the resulting Skill Receiving Route and crossover expression.

Reincarnation preserves the source history. It does not perform the crossover. Every usable result must pass through Current Access, a receiving tree, compatible means, present experimentation, and Practised Reliability.

## Shared Skill Model

Use all ten dimensions in the [Skill Engine index](README.md#shared-skill-model):

| Dimension | Crossover question |
| --- | --- |
| **Skill Identity** | Is the result the same bounded Skill, one principle, a partial application, or a genuinely different translated Skill? |
| **Origin** | Which incarnation, tree, Discipline, ecology, Profession, culture, or magical system produced the source? |
| **Requirements** | Which source requirements persist, which current requirements replace them, and who owns each? |
| **Persistent Familiarity** | What Skill XP, Skill Imprint, integrated principle, or related Soul Imprint actually survived? |
| **Current Access** | What can this incarnation presently recall, interpret, reconstruct, or test? |
| **Embodied Expression** | What can the current body, senses, cognition, tools, magic, and environment execute? |
| **Practised Reliability** | What has been recalibrated and tested under current pressure and variation? |
| **Contextual Effectiveness** | How useful is the result for the current objective and conditions rather than its source context? |
| **Evolution State** | Is the current expression recovered, translated, adapted, evolved, or still only a candidate? |
| **Visibility** | Who recognizes the source and result, and who mistakes familiarity for mastery or novelty? |

No combination of these dimensions becomes a universal transfer percentage or crossover level.

## Terms

### Skill Crossover

The bounded use of legitimately retained Skill history to support recovery or new current-life Skill Development across a material change in embodiment, tree, culture, cognition, tools, environment, or metaphysical system.

### Receiving Route

The present Skill tree, body, toolset, magical structure, Profession, institution, or other valid pathway through which a crossover candidate may be learned and expressed now.

### Crossover Outcome

The adjudicated relationship between one retained source and one proposed present expression. Outcomes include direct, principle, partial, translated, latent, inaccessible, distorted, harmful, and impossible cases.

## Source and Result Remain Distinct

A crossover assessment identifies:

1. the source Skill and its provenance;
2. what actually persisted;
3. the proposed current function;
4. the Receiving Route;
5. the requirements and incompatibilities;
6. the present work needed to make the result reliable;
7. the final Skill Identity and ownership.

The source does not become a bag of detachable bonuses. A translated result may share principles while having different anatomy, techniques, risks, and limits. Record both provenance and current ownership so later rules do not award duplicate Skill XP for one adaptation.

## Crossover Outcomes

### Direct Transfer

The source and present expression retain the same Skill Identity because their function, method, embodiment, tools, senses, and rules correspond closely enough for literal recovery.

Direct does not mean instant. Current Access, physical or magical calibration, present materials, and pressure-tested practice remain necessary. A human swordsman reincarnated into a similarly proportioned human body may recover the same sword Discipline rapidly while still rebuilding strength, reach judgment, grip, and contemporary knowledge.

### Principle Transfer

One or more genuinely integrated Transferable Principles support a different Skill without transferring the source technique. Distance, rhythm, load paths, feedback, information control, or energy conservation may remain useful across forms.

Principle Transfer provides a better hypothesis, learning route, or error model. The receiving Skill earns its own present reliability and does not receive a duplicate copy of the source's mastery.

### Partial Transfer

Only a bounded part of the source corresponds. The incarnation may recover target selection without movement, rhythm without a sensory channel, or diagnosis without a treatment method.

The GM names what transfers and what does not. A partial result cannot be narrated as full access whenever convenient.

### Translated Transfer

The present incarnation develops a stable analogue that preserves a relevant function through materially different means. Translation may use new anatomy, senses, tools, magic, language, group structure, or culture.

The result normally has its own Skill Identity, requirements, limits, and evidence. Translation Loss remains real: strengths may narrow, new risks may appear, and former assumptions may become invalid.

### Latent Familiarity

The retained Skill history exists, but no valid present Receiving Route currently corresponds to it. It may influence dreams, curiosity, frustration, or future possibilities only through rules that actually permit those effects.

Latent Familiarity grants no present capability. A wingless body may retain aerial Skill XP while having no means to fly.

Latent or accessible familiarity may therefore coexist with a zero Development Boost. Zero means no current route can reuse practical calibration; it does not erase Skill identity or history. Unknown relevance remains distinct from zero.

### Inaccessible Familiarity

A potentially valid Receiving Route exists, but Current Access is blocked by memory conditions, Soul Strain, missing interpretation, suppression, an unrecognized Skill Imprint, or another established access barrier.

Removing the barrier reveals a learning opportunity, not automatic Practised Reliability.

### Distorted Transfer

Source familiarity reaches the present life through a materially mistaken correspondence. The incarnation applies a real pattern to the wrong signals, timing, anatomy, culture, or magical law.

Distortion can produce false confidence, unstable success, or a Maladaptation. Fair clues must expose the mismatch, and correction through current evidence remains possible.

### Harmful Carryover

Source habits, reflexes, assumptions, or emotional associations interfere with present action even when no useful transfer occurs. Harmful Carryover may cause phantom-limb responses, unsafe force, sensory fixation, instinct conflict, or social misreading.

It is a causal failure state, not permission to confiscate the Skill Imprint or dictate deliberate player action. Rehabilitation, deliberate override, accommodation, and retraining can matter.

### Impossible Transfer

No functional correspondence, Receiving Route, or permissible current expression exists under the established rules. The source history remains real but cannot support the proposed result.

Impossible for one body, world, or Age does not mean erased forever. A later incarnation may present a different assessment.

## Crossover Assessment

Evaluate one source-to-result claim at a time. Do not assign one permanent compatibility score between two species.

### 1. Verify Provenance

Confirm that the soul possesses a genuine Skill Imprint, integrated Transferable Principle, Retained Instinct, or other relevant history. Observation, an Echo's statement, Archive knowledge, a title, or species membership does not fabricate source mastery.

### 2. State the Proposed Function

Describe what the present incarnation is trying to do. “Use dragon Skills” is too broad. “Read rising air to anticipate movement on a glider” is assessable.

### 3. Identify the Receiving Route

Name the current human or monster tree, Skill root, body structure, tool, magical Channel, social practice, or other rule that can own the expression. If no owner exists, the result remains latent or impossible rather than floating between systems.

### 4. Compare Compatibility

Assess qualitatively:

- **Conceptual overlap:** do source and result solve a genuinely related problem?
- **Anatomical compatibility:** do scale, limbs, joints, balance, natural weapons, or materials correspond?
- **Sensory compatibility:** can the current incarnation detect and discriminate the necessary signals?
- **Cognitive compatibility:** can the present mind represent, sequence, attend to, and revise the method?
- **Environmental compatibility:** do terrain, medium, pressure, gravity, mana, and hazards support the route?
- **Metaphysical compatibility:** do current magical laws, Soul conditions, authorities, contracts, and Channels permit it?
- **Tool availability:** are necessary tools, materials, interfaces, and maintenance available?
- **Cultural interpretation:** are meanings, etiquette, institutions, language, and local assumptions still valid?
- **Current training:** has the incarnation practised the present expression with feedback and variation?

A strong match in one factor cannot silently erase a decisive mismatch in another.

### 5. Apply Retained Support

Different retained systems contribute only their own functions:

- **Skill XP and Skill Imprints** preserve source structure and learning familiarity.
- **Retained Instincts** may orient attention or response but do not supply a complete technique.

Query the full Life Archive expression lineage rather than comparing only the original embodiment with the present one. A legitimate translated expression may serve as a later Translation Bridge. Keep one stable Skill identity where bounded function remains continuous, record embodiment-specific expressions, and preserve predecessor or future merge provenance.

Familiarity Unlock never bypasses anatomy, Mana, senses, tools, tree access, Class, Evolution, environment, or another hard requirement. It does not automatically create Level 0 Instinct; Level 0 requires its own canonical provenance and receiving route.
- **Soul Echoes** may explain source context, diagnose old habits, or teach from one life's perspective.
- **Soul Resonance** may make a real Link easier to access or interpret without creating compatibility.
- **Soul Depth** may support integration of conflicting histories without granting the result.
- **Akashic Records** may verify old methods or changed laws without providing execution mastery.

No support source waives the Receiving Route or current practice.

### 6. Test in the Present Life

Use safe experimentation where possible, then meaningful variation. Calibration should reveal differences in timing, force, signal meaning, cost, fatigue, and failure. Present effort is not ceremonial: it creates the current expression.

Exceptional correspondence may make relearning very fast. It cannot make absent anatomy, untested reliability, or unknown modern conditions irrelevant.

### 7. Classify and Record the Outcome

Record the outcome category, current Skill Identity, requirements, translation loss, failure states, evidence of reliability, and Visibility. Reassess when embodiment, tools, laws, access, or training materially change.

## Changes in Species and Anatomy

The current species supplies its own body and tree. Former anatomy does not remain hidden inside it.

Body-shaped source habits may assist or interfere with current calibration:

- extra-limb timing may overload a two-limbed sequence;
- a former tail counterbalance may produce unsafe turns;
- claw commitment distances may misfit a sword or spear;
- distributed attention may exceed present working capacity;
- a former shell or regeneration trait may encourage reckless exposure.

Tools, transformations, magical substitutes, and accommodations can establish Receiving Routes only under their own costs and limits. They do not retroactively make the bodies identical.

## Changes in Senses and Cognition

A sensory Skill depends on both signal access and learned interpretation. Echolocation experience cannot interpret silence; human vision cannot reproduce polarization or pressure fields by memory.

A valid substitute may translate the function through audible clicks, instruments, magic, touch, a companion, or a constructed interface. The new signal requires calibration and may omit information the source once carried.

Cognitive translation follows the same rule. A hive's distributed consensus, a dragon's century-scale planning, an artificial being's exact logs, and a human's verbal analysis may offer useful principles while remaining different operating structures. No form is assumed to have perfect memory, unlimited parallel action, or universal social understanding.

## Changes in Culture and Profession

Social and professional Skills contain local assumptions. Laws, language, rank, markets, taboos, tools, standards, and institutions may change between lives or Ages.

Prior diplomacy can improve question choice, listening, preparation, and conflict diagnosis. It cannot identify unknown customs, restore authority, or guarantee that a behavior means the same thing. Prior Profession history can accelerate relearning while current credentials, materials, safety standards, and relationships remain separate.

## Changes in Magic and Environment

Magical Skill retains no automatic authority over a changed world's laws. A former ritualist may remember sequence, error control, symbolism, group timing, and research method while old names, materials, entities, Channels, or costs no longer function.

Environmental crossover likewise preserves only useful correspondence. Aquatic pursuit may inform three-dimensional interception, but air, water, vacuum, stone, dream, and magical currents each impose different sensing, movement, and survival requirements.

## Failure Cases

### Phantom-Limb Habits

A human reaches with a remembered tail or tries to stabilize with absent wings. The micro-response creates hesitation or imbalance. It may be retrained or translated through a tool; it cannot move a limb that does not exist.

### Incorrect Centre of Gravity

A former quadruped applies a low, forward commitment to a bipedal stance and repeatedly overextends. Source confidence increases danger until present balance and footwork are rebuilt.

### Sensory Overload

A newly accessible magical sense activates patterns associated with a former echolocating life. Treating every signal as spatially urgent causes fixation and false alarms until filtering is learned.

### Outdated Assumptions

A healer remembers a precise treatment from an Age before a pathogen changed. The reasoning is genuine, but uncritical execution is harmful. Contemporary evidence and revalidation decide the present method.

### Instinct Conflict

A pack-hunting Retained Instinct urges convergence while the current species' territorial response urges separation. The player chooses how to respond; reliable team coordination requires deliberate reconciliation and practice.

### Dangerous Automatic Response

A former armored monster habitually accepts a blow to gain position. The current fragile body begins the same movement. Warning, conscious override, protective training, and new defensive habits can correct it.

### Misapplied Social Behavior

A human diplomat addresses a hive's worker as an autonomous officeholder and mistakes delayed colony consultation for deceit. Diplomatic principles may help repair the error, but former etiquette is not universal.

### Memory Without Execution

A ritualist recalls every word and diagram while lacking the current world's Channel, materials, pronunciation, and group practice. This is clear knowledge with no reliable present spell.

## Worked Examples

### Slime Movement Through Human and Elf Bodies

A Slime Life develops Slime Movement through deformation and sliding. A later Human retains Familiarity Unlock but has no physiological boost. After developing applicable Mana capability and practising tendril vaulting and low-friction movement, the Human establishes a translated expression under the same Skill identity.

A later Elf can draw meaningful acceleration from the Human Translation Bridge because its body and magical route are more relevant than the original Slime mechanism. The Skill does not become universally applicable, and neither later Life receives the Slime's anatomy or former Skill level.

### Human Swordsmanship Informing Claw Combat

A reincarnated feline monster retains sword Skill XP. Human grip, guards, and blade alignment are incompatible. Distance judgment, initiative control, feint structure, and recovery planning support Principle Transfer. Through current claw conditioning and live practice, the monster develops a claw-fighting Skill owned by the monster tree. It does not receive a second copy of sword mastery.

### Spider Web-Building Informing Engineering

A human incarnation retains web-construction familiarity. Spinnerets and vibration sensing are absent, so Direct Transfer fails. Load distribution, redundancy, tension, and repair sequencing support Principle Transfer into structural engineering. Mathematics, tools, materials, codes, and human-scale fabrication require present study.

### Echolocation Informing Magical Spatial Perception

A former cave hunter learned active sensing through emitted sound. The current body has no echolocation organ but gains a magical pulse Channel. Timing, return comparison, and occlusion reasoning correspond. The magical signal has different noise, cost, and hazards, producing Translated Transfer only after safe calibration.

### Dragon Flight in a Wingless Body

Flight Skill remains Latent Familiarity for literal movement. Air-current judgment may partly transfer to weather reading, sailing, projectile prediction, or glider use when a valid tool and training route exist. Remembered wing corrections can also cause phantom responses near a fall.

### Aquatic Hunting in a Terrestrial Incarnation

Three-dimensional interception and patience transfer partially. Buoyancy, pressure sensing, propulsion, and underwater concealment do not. The terrestrial hunter must rebuild balance, footing, breathing, and terrain reading rather than treating land as thin water.

### Human Diplomacy Within a Hive Mind

Question design, incentive analysis, and de-escalation are useful principles. Assumptions about individual privacy, authority, response time, and representative consent may be wrong. The incarnation learns hive signals, decision topology, and who owns commitments before a translated diplomatic practice becomes reliable.

### Ritual Magic Under Changed Laws

A former ritualist enters an Age where names do not bind and circles conduct a different resource. Old procedures fail, but hazard isolation, role timing, symbolic comparison, and controlled experimentation remain useful. The result may become a new ritual Skill only through present magical access and testing; the old ritual remains historically real but currently impossible.

## Safeguards

- **No unrestricted mastery:** retained history accelerates only a bounded compatible learning route.
- **No free duplicate Skill:** a translated expression records one current result and does not duplicate XP or benefits merely because it has a new name.
- **No universal usefulness:** some past Skills remain latent, inaccessible, obsolete, harmful, or impossible in the current life.
- **No species bypass:** crossover cannot grant anatomy, traits, senses, organs, maturation, mutation, Evolution, or a former species stage.
- **No tree merger:** human and monster trees remain distinct even when one history informs the other.
- **No memory-equals-execution:** factual recall, Echo instruction, or Archive access does not establish calibration or reliability.
- **No Resonance shortcut:** Soul Resonance improves access or interpretation only through a genuine Link.
- **No harmful-compulsion shortcut:** source reflexes may create pressure or micro-responses but do not dictate deliberate action without an explicit contested rule.
- **No crossover farming:** repeated reincarnation, deliberate incompatibility, injury, failed translation, or superficial practice does not multiply Skill XP.
- **No consequence-free translation:** tools, magic, allies, institutions, and transformations retain their sources, costs, maintenance, permissions, and failure states.

## GM Procedure

For each crossover claim:

1. identify the source Skill, Origin, and legitimate Persistent Familiarity;
2. state the proposed present function narrowly;
3. identify the Receiving Route and Owning System;
4. compare conceptual, anatomical, sensory, cognitive, environmental, metaphysical, tool, and cultural compatibility;
5. identify Retained Instinct, Echo, Resonance, and Skill XP support without combining their functions;
6. name missing requirements, translation loss, and possible harmful carryover;
7. require current experimentation, feedback, calibration, and meaningful practice;
8. classify the Crossover Outcome;
9. record current Skill Identity, reliability, effectiveness, and Visibility;
10. check for duplicate rewards, body bypass, tree merger, and unsupported future-system access.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- how Skill Imprints form or persist;
- Reincarnation candidates or Soul Engine access states;
- biological Evolution, mutation, species stages, or hybrid evolution;
- the complete Adaptive Skill, Skill evolution, or Skill fusion systems;
- the complete magic system;
- human classes, monster species catalogs, or canonical crossover unlock lists;
- campaign-specific prior lives or abilities.
