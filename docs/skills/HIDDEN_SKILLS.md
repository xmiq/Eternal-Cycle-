# Hidden Skills

## Purpose

This document defines how a real Skill can remain unknown, unrecognised, misunderstood, concealed, dormant, suppressed, partially expressed, or otherwise hidden from one or more observers. Hidden status governs information and interpretation. It does not create a capability, expand its effects, or exempt it from ordinary Skill requirements.

Mystery must remain fair. A Hidden Skill cannot be invented when the GM needs a surprise, withheld as the only untelegraphed solution, or used to invalidate meaningful choices after the fact.

## Core Rule

A **Hidden Skill** is a real bounded learned competency whose existence, owner, requirements, effects, or full nature is not presently understood by one or more specified observers.

Before a Hidden Skill affects play, the GM's Factual View must establish enough of its identity, provenance, requirements, access, expression, limits, and evidence to distinguish discovery from retroactive invention. Hidden from one observer does not mean hidden from all observers, unknowable in principle, or absent from causal history.

## Hidden Skills and the Shared Model

Record the Skill itself through the [Shared Skill Model](README.md#shared-skill-model), then record who knows what:

| Dimension | Hidden-Skill question |
| --- | --- |
| **Skill Identity** | What real bounded capability exists beneath its unknown, mistaken, or concealed description? |
| **Origin** | Where, when, and through which valid learning route did it form? |
| **Requirements** | Which body, senses, knowledge, tools, resources, permissions, relationships, or metaphysical conditions does it actually need? |
| **Persistent Familiarity** | What legitimate Skill Imprint or Skill XP exists even if no current observer recognises it? |
| **Current Access** | What can the current incarnation reach, understand, initiate, or relearn now? |
| **Embodied Expression** | Which effects can the present body and systems actually express, including partial or suppressed forms? |
| **Practised Reliability** | What evidence supports dependable current use rather than isolated unexplained success? |
| **Contextual Effectiveness** | Where does the hidden capability matter, fail, or remain irrelevant? |
| **Evolution State** | Is its true form basic, evolved, fused, translated, dormant, or misidentified as another route? |
| **Visibility** | Which users, characters, societies, experts, systems, and observers know, suspect, deny, conceal, or misunderstand each part? |

Visibility is observer-relative and claim-specific. It is not a global hidden/revealed switch.

## Terms

### Hidden Skill

A real Skill whose existence, ownership, requirements, effects, or full nature is not presently understood by one or more specified observers.

### Skill Visibility Profile

A qualitative record of what relevant observers know, suspect, misclassify, conceal, or cannot access about one Skill. Different observers may hold different evidence and conclusions.

### Discovery Evidence

Causal signs that can support learning the existence or nature of a Hidden Skill, including recurring effects, discoverable patterns, consequences, clues, failed expectations, expert interpretation, Soul Echo recognition, Archive Records, and training breakthroughs.

### False Diagnosis

An evidence-based but incorrect conclusion about a Skill's identity, owner, source, requirements, effects, or limits. A fair False Diagnosis remains revisable and does not rewrite the underlying capability.

### Akashic Obscuration

An established condition that prevents, distorts, fragments, masks, or misindexes otherwise relevant Archive Traces or Records for a particular Archive Basis and Interface. It limits that reading and does not prove that the Skill or its history never existed.

## Required Factual Record

Before concealing a Skill from the user or using it as a consequential unknown, record at least:

1. **Identity:** the smallest truthful description of the learned capability.
2. **Owner:** the individual, group, institution, collective, or other valid owner.
3. **Provenance:** how it was learned, retained, translated, evolved, or fused.
4. **Requirements:** present access, embodiment, tools, resources, permissions, relationships, and other dependencies.
5. **Expression:** what it can and cannot currently do.
6. **Reliability:** evidence, pressure limits, failure modes, rust, suppression, and uncertainty.
7. **Costs and consequences:** attention, energy, risk, social exposure, maintenance, or other causal effects.
8. **Visibility Profile:** what each relevant observer knows or believes.
9. **Discovery Evidence:** clues that already exist or can arise through fair interaction.
10. **Reveal conditions:** plausible ways knowledge can change without guaranteeing discovery.

This record may be concise, but it must predate a reveal that materially affects choice or outcome. Later clarification may refine unknown details when it follows existing evidence; it cannot add a convenient unrelated power.

## Forms of Hiddenness

These forms can overlap. They are not ranks or a required sequence.

### Hidden From the User

The user is not yet told the capability's full factual definition. This is appropriate only when discovery is part of play and the mystery preserves meaningful choice.

Consequential effects need perceivable signs or fair uncertainty. Information necessary to understand a choice's basic stakes cannot be withheld merely to force a surprise. The GM may reveal mechanics sooner than the character learns them when that better protects agency and trust.

### Hidden From the Character

The current incarnation does not recognise the Skill, its source, or its full operation. The user may know this out of character while choosing only from information available to the character.

Lack of recognition does not imply unconscious mastery. Current Access, Embodied Expression, and Practised Reliability remain separate.

### Hidden From Society

A capability may be unknown, forgotten, taboo, dismissed, restricted, or outside a culture's concepts. Another culture, monster society, institution, god, spirit, or species may understand it differently.

Social ignorance changes World Recognition, instruction, danger, and opportunity. It does not make society's preferred classification metaphysically true.

### Deliberately Concealed

An owner or ally may hide signs, supply false explanations, avoid demonstrations, compartmentalise instruction, or control records. Concealment is an action with methods, dependencies, observers, and possible failure.

Concealment does not make the Skill undetectable, erase consequences, overwrite Archive Traces, or compel others to accept a cover story.

### Misclassified

Observers recognise a recurring capability but assign it to the wrong Skill, species trait, Profession, spell, blessing, item, person, or cause. Misclassification can be reasonable under limited evidence.

The wrong label grants no effects and cannot move ownership. Contradictions, failed predictions, and broader evidence must be allowed to challenge it.

### Dormant

A legitimate Skill Imprint or learned structure exists but lacks a present Trigger, accessible memory, receiving route, motivation, or relevant context. Dormancy is potential without current dependable output.

A crisis may make the history relevant, but it does not waive embodiment or practice.

### Suppressed

An established condition blocks or narrows Current Access or expression. Injury, seals, law, fear, trauma, magic, altered Channels, incompatible embodiment, lost tools, broken relationships, or hostile interference may suppress a Skill through their own rules.

Suppression does not erase the Skill Imprint, prove present reliability, or make recovery automatic when the suppressor ends.

### Unrecognised

The owner uses a real capability but interprets it as ordinary luck, talent, instinct, personality, assistance, or another Skill. Recurring correction and transfer distinguish the competency from coincidence.

Naming may improve instruction and deliberate development, but the Skill existed through its learned pattern before the name.

### Partially Expressed

Only part of the Skill has Current Access or compatible embodiment. Fragments may appear through a technique, passive filter, dream, reflex, translated principle, or narrow context.

Partial expression must be described at its actual scope. It cannot stand in for the whole Skill or imply hidden access to missing functions.

### Akashically Obscured

A valid Archive reading cannot presently locate or resolve relevant Skill history because Traces are absent from that source, fragmented, damaged, masked, mistranslated, deliberately misindexed, protected, or inaccessible through the reader's Basis or Interface.

Akashic Obscuration is scoped. Another Trace, Interface, Basis, Custodian, Annotation, participant, place, or consequence may preserve different evidence. "The Archive did not show it" is not universal proof of nonexistence.

## Fair Discovery Evidence

Hidden information should interact with the world before it becomes an answer.

### Recurring Effects

The same bounded outcome appears under related conditions, including its costs and limits. Recurrence supports investigation but does not prove one explanation.

### Discoverable Patterns

Timing, triggers, targets, errors, sensory emphasis, posture, resource use, dreams, records, or consequences form a pattern that deliberate observation can test.

### Consequences

A hidden capability leaves fatigue, changed habits, damaged tools, social reactions, missed stimuli, unusual preparation, altered mana, environmental traces, or other causal effects appropriate to its operation.

### Clues

Instruction fragments, scars, notes, warnings, taboos, architectural features, monster signals, institutional procedures, or inconsistent testimony can point toward the Skill without naming it.

### Failed Expectations

A predicted result repeatedly fails because the assumed model is incomplete. A "lucky" negotiator succeeds only after particular preparation, or an alleged immunity collapses when active regulation is disrupted.

### Expert Interpretation

A qualified observer may recognise structure, ask better questions, or propose tests. Expertise remains bounded by culture, evidence, access, bias, and unfamiliar systems.

### Soul Echo Recognition

A Soul Echo may remember the Skill's source, former use, or warning signs. Echo testimony is perspective and historical evidence, not automatic Current Access or unquestionable truth.

### Archive Records

An Archive Record may preserve provenance, a former technique, witness evidence, consequences, or contradictory classification. Archive access and resolution remain source-bound.

### Training Breakthroughs

Deliberate practice may expose an existing pattern when correction suddenly links formerly separate signs. The breakthrough reveals or restores understanding; it does not retroactively create unearned mastery.

Evidence should include limits and counterevidence where relevant. Clues need not identify the correct answer immediately, but they must make later revelation causally intelligible.

## Discovery, Naming, and Changing Knowledge

### Discovery

Discovery occurs when an observer gains usable evidence that a Skill or a material part of it exists. Discovery can concern identity, ownership, requirements, access, effects, limits, or history separately.

The observer may discover one claim while remaining wrong about another. Finding that a sensory filter exists does not reveal who trained it or every signal it suppresses.

### Naming

A name supports communication, teaching, comparison, and deliberate practice. Different cultures may name the same capability differently or use one term for several distinct Skills.

Naming does not create, upgrade, fully reveal, or standardise the Skill. The GM should preserve actual function beneath changing labels.

### Misunderstanding

Reasonable misunderstanding follows available evidence and produces testable expectations. New evidence can revise it without declaring earlier choices foolish or secretly impossible.

A reveal is unfair when the GM changes the explanation solely to defeat a successful inference.

### Deliberate Concealment

Concealment may itself require planning, social technique, magical masking, record control, equipment, allies, or a specialised Skill. Each method leaves possible signs and follows its own owner.

The concealed Skill does not automatically include the ability to conceal itself.

### Crisis Revelation

Pressure may expose an unnoticed cue, force an owner to reveal a capability, break suppression, or make a dormant Skill Imprint accessible. A crisis can reveal evidence or create an attempt; it cannot fabricate provenance, compatibility, resources, or Practised Reliability.

Unexpected success under crisis may be Fragmented Access. Repetition and present training are still needed before the expression is dependable.

### Loss Through Inaccessible Embodiment

A known Skill may become inexpressible after Reincarnation, transformation, injury, suppression, or loss of a dependency. The user or character may understand what existed while being unable to access or demonstrate it.

World observers may later doubt or forget it, creating new hiddenness in recognition without deleting the retained history.

### False Diagnosis

A false diagnosis should identify the evidence it explains, the evidence it misses, and what could revise it. It may cause real training, medical, legal, social, magical, or political consequences.

Correction changes knowledge and future choices. It does not retroactively transfer XP, excuse every prior failure, or create the correctly diagnosed Skill if it never existed.

## Ownership and Player Agency

Hiddenness never changes ownership. A hidden personal Skill belongs to its actual practitioner; a group Skill remains with its valid collective; a magical effect, item, ally, species trait, institution, or Soul system does not become a personal Skill because observers misattribute it.

The user retains authority over the current incarnation's deliberate choices. Hidden Skills may produce established passive cues, partial expressions, consequences, uncertainty, or involuntary effects owned by another explicit rule. They cannot be used to impose:

- unavoidable possession;
- secret allegiance, morality, intent, or consent;
- mandatory use of a discovered capability;
- an unannounced solution the user was required to guess;
- an action that bypasses [Conscious Override](ACTIVE_AND_PASSIVE_SKILLS.md#conscious-override);
- a missing prerequisite disguised as forgotten talent.

If possession, compulsion, Soul Intrusion, Echo Delegation, hostile magic, or another contested influence is actually involved, use that system's consent, resistance, scope, cost, and recovery rules. A Hidden Skill cannot conceal a rules bypass.

## Reincarnation and Soul-System Interactions

- **Skill Imprints and Skill XP:** may preserve genuine hidden history without providing present execution.
- **Retained Instincts:** may supply cues, urgency, valuation, or response bias that become Discovery Evidence rather than proof of a complete Skill.
- **Soul Echoes:** may recognise, explain, demonstrate, dispute, or misunderstand prior Skill history without controlling the current incarnation.
- **Soul Resonance:** may improve access to a genuine Link and relevant history without inventing a Skill.
- **Soul Depth:** may support integration of complex discovery without serving as a detection rank.
- **Soul Titles:** may cause observers to expect or overlook a capability; a title is neither the Skill nor proof of its current access.
- **Soul Avatars:** may synthesise relevant history under their own rules without making every hidden prior-life Skill available.
- **Akashic Archive:** may provide source-bound evidence or be subject to scoped Akashic Obscuration; absence from one reading is not proof.
- **Reincarnation Skill Crossover:** owns present translation when a former human or monster Skill seeks expression through a materially different route.

No Soul system exists to deliver a hidden GM-only solution. Every interaction retains provenance, requirements, present embodiment, current effort, uncertainty, and agency.

## Worked Examples

### An Unnoticed Sensory Filter

A cave guide learned in childhood to suppress constant stone echoes and prioritise deviations associated with movement. The guide calls this "good hearing" and does not notice the filter itself.

Recurring headaches in metal chambers, missed unfamiliar clicks, and unusually fast orientation in known caves provide Discovery Evidence. Training reveals a passive Skill expression with real Background Load. It does not create hearing beyond the current body's range.

### An Inherited Monster Hunting Method

A young pack monster follows nursery paths, watches elder demonstrations, and learns scent handoffs, silent spacing, and how to drive prey toward another hunter. The society calls the pattern "blood inheritance," but related monsters raised without the tradition do not perform it reliably.

The Skill is inherited culturally through a Monster Tradition, not automatically through species biology. Its owner and pack coordination remain explicit, and a solitary reincarnation does not receive the whole group method for free.

### A Social Technique Mistaken for Luck

A merchant consistently prevents bargaining disputes from escalating by mirroring tempo, leaving face-saving exits, and changing the order in which concessions are offered. Observers call the merchant lucky because no overt command is visible.

Success disappears in a culture with different status signals. An experienced mediator identifies the learned pattern and its limits. Discovery improves deliberate practice but grants no authority or guaranteed persuasion.

### A Suppressed Magical Control Skill

A former channeler retains a real Skill for balancing several mana flows, but a seal blocks Channel access. Residual correction movements, dreams of pressure imbalance, and an expert's tests suggest the Skill Imprint remains.

Breaking the seal may restore a receiving route, not safe mastery. Changed Channels, bodily injury, and years without practice can leave the Skill fragmented until present calibration and training rebuild reliability.

### A Prior-Life Skill Seen Through Dreams and Reflexes

A human incarnation dreams of reading aerial currents and reflexively leans before crosswinds. The prior-life flight Skill exists in Persistent Familiarity, but the human lacks wings and current aerial expression.

The dreams and directional bias are evidence of retained history, not a hidden current flight Skill. Through glider training, part of the principle may enter a valid crossover route; until then, claiming full flight mastery would bypass embodiment.

### A False Possession Diagnosis

A healer observes a patient speaking unfamiliar technical phrases during sleep and concludes that a hostile spirit is taking control. Recurring dreams, an Echo interview, and the absence of external Soul Intrusion signs instead support fragmented access to a former medical Skill.

The original diagnosis was plausible but incomplete. Correction removes neither the patient's agency nor the need to relearn the Skill; it also does not prove that every strange dream is a past-life capability.

## GM Procedure

When introducing or adjudicating a Hidden Skill:

1. create the Required Factual Record before a consequential reveal;
2. identify exactly which claim is hidden and from which observer;
3. establish the present Skill requirements, access, expression, reliability, costs, and ownership;
4. choose one or more forms of hiddenness without treating them as ranks;
5. place Discovery Evidence and fair consequences appropriate to the capability;
6. preserve clues needed for meaningful choice while keeping character and user knowledge distinct;
7. allow investigation, testing, expert interpretation, refusal, and reasonable error;
8. distinguish discovery from naming, access, expression, mastery, and World Recognition;
9. adjudicate crisis revelation as evidence or attempted access, not instant creation;
10. test alternative explanations and record False Diagnoses honestly;
11. preserve current-incarnation agency and route contested influence to its actual system;
12. update the Skill Visibility Profile when evidence changes.

## Safeguards

- **No retroactive convenience power:** identity, owner, provenance, requirements, limits, and causal evidence must predate a consequential reveal.
- **No secret GM-only solution:** a Hidden Skill cannot be the sole required answer to a problem when the user had no fair route to infer, seek, refuse, or replace it.
- **No unavoidable possession:** hidden capability cannot transfer executive control; possession and Soul Intrusion require explicit separate rules.
- **No choice-critical silence:** when hidden information materially changes understandable stakes, provide clues, visible uncertainty, or a fair opportunity to investigate.
- **No prerequisite bypass:** hidden status cannot supply anatomy, senses, magic, knowledge, tools, authority, relationships, consent, or reliability.
- **No discovery-equals-mastery:** learning that a Skill exists does not grant access, expression, Skill XP, or Practised Reliability.
- **No absence-as-proof:** failure to observe or read a Skill through one method does not establish universal nonexistence.
- **No concealment package:** owning a Skill does not automatically grant the ability to hide it.
- **No false-diagnosis trap:** reasonable conclusions remain revisable through evidence and are not changed solely to defeat user inference.
- **No observer collapse:** user, character, society, expert, institution, Echo, and Archive knowledge remain distinct.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- universal detection, secrecy, investigation, or revelation formulas;
- complete conceptual Skill rules;
- possession, compulsion, Soul Intrusion, or hostile memory procedures;
- complete stealth, divination, magic, institutional secrecy, or Akashic access systems;
- campaign-specific hidden capabilities, clues, or revelations.
