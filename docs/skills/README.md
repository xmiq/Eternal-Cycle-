# Skill Engine

The Skill Engine defines how bounded learned capabilities are structured, related, translated, transformed, expressed, discovered, and represented. It creates neither a universal character level nor a universal Skill currency, score, tree, or interface.

Repository-wide ownership, dependency, extension, and consumer metadata is maintained in the [Canonical Document Registry](../DOCUMENT_REGISTRY.md#skill-engine).

## Authority and Use

The files in this directory are the canonical Skill Engine. Read the Development foundations first, then the ten Skill documents in the order below. [Skill Engine Safeguards](SKILL_ENGINE_SAFEGUARDS.md) appears last because it consolidates the completed engine, but its ownership and representation rules apply throughout.

When two documents describe one event, assign each exact effect to one Owning System. A supporting rule may change access, cost, reliability, scale, timing, or Contextual Effectiveness without taking ownership of the effect it supports.

## Shared Skill Model

Every canonical Skill claim must keep these dimensions distinct:

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Skill Identity** | What bounded learned capability does the Skill actually represent? |
| **Origin** | Is its route human, monster, shared, profession-derived, species-derived, magical, social, conceptual, or otherwise established? |
| **Requirements** | Which body, senses, knowledge, tools, environment, authority, relationships, or metaphysical conditions are necessary? |
| **Persistent Familiarity** | What Skill XP, Skill Imprint, or learning advantage legitimately persists? |
| **Current Access** | What can the present incarnation recall, reconstruct, interpret, or invoke now? |
| **Embodied Expression** | What can the current body and receiving system actually perform? |
| **Practised Reliability** | What remains dependable under pressure, fatigue, variation, and disruption? |
| **Contextual Effectiveness** | How useful is the capability for the present objective, environment, opposition, tools, and allies? |
| **Evolution State** | Has the Skill refined, specialised, broadened, compressed, translated, fused, or conceptually transformed through a valid rule? |
| **Visibility** | Who knows, recognises, conceals, misidentifies, or only partly understands the Skill? |

These dimensions form a qualitative record, not a score. High Persistent Familiarity does not guarantee Current Access; Current Access does not guarantee Embodied Expression; reliable expression may still be a poor contextual match.

## Document Responsibilities

| Document | Owns | Does not own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Human Skill Trees** | relationships among human learned foundations, branches, techniques, traditions, and routes | universal human trees, classes, automatic Profession bundles, or institutional authority |
| **Monster Skill Trees** | monster-native learned routes and traditions | Species Traits, biological Evolution, identical species mastery, or renamed human trees |
| **Reincarnation Skill Crossover** | assessment and translation of one retained source into one present result | former anatomy, unrestricted transfer, free mastery, or merger of human and monster trees |
| **Adaptive Skills** | emergence of a stable distinct Skill from meaningful adaptation | action spam, bodily mutation, crisis rewards, or every useful Tactic |
| **Skill Evolution** | qualitative transformation of an established Skill | ordinary improvement, renaming, Skill Fusion, mutation, or biological Evolution |
| **Skill Fusion** | a coherent new competency formed through genuine component integration | routine combinations, Synergy, equipment effects, or pairwise fusion multiplication |
| **Active and Passive Skills** | expression modes, Background Load, disruption, and Conscious Override | duplicate Skills, free passive effects, infallible reflexes, or compelled deliberate action |
| **Hidden Skills** | Visibility, factual provenance, fair clues, discovery, and concealment | retroactive convenience powers, prerequisite bypass, possession, or arbitrary GM reversal |
| **Conceptual Skills** | bounded learned interpretation and application of a Conceptual Domain | automatic authority, unrestricted reality control, universal magic, or metaphor as effect |
| **Skill Engine Safeguards** | representation, ownership tests, anti-proliferation, and record cleanup | new species, Professions, spells, Soul powers, equipment powers, or numerical Skill formulas |

## Ownership Procedure

For any disputed capability:

1. state the exact effect rather than a broad label;
2. identify the system that directly makes that effect possible;
3. separate the owner from body, tools, magic, allies, institutions, relationships, Soul systems, and environmental support;
4. test what remains when each contributor is removed;
5. require meaningful learning before assigning any personal Skill;
6. use the smallest truthful Capability Representation;
7. count each effect and each legitimate Development change once;
8. preserve requirements, present embodiment, practice, failure, agency, and uncertainty.

The full procedure and cleanup rules are in [Skill Engine Safeguards](SKILL_ENGINE_SAFEGUARDS.md#ownership-test).

## Soul Continuity Inputs

Soul systems may preserve history or support access only within their own rules:

- [Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) owns the life transition, retained categories, and reset boundary.
- [Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) owns cue-shaped orientation and response without granting complete Skills.
- [Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) owns perspective-bearing prior-life imprints that may advise without performing current practice.
- [Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) and [Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may support integration or access without granting mastery.
- [Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md), [Soul Avatars](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md), and the [Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) provide only their defined recognition, continuity, synthesis, or record functions.
- [Soul System Interactions](../soul/SOUL_SYSTEM_INTERACTIONS.md) and [Soul Engine Safeguards](../soul/SOUL_ENGINE_SAFEGUARDS.md) resolve cross-Soul ownership and exploit limits.

Persistent Familiarity accelerates legitimate relearning. It does not restore present anatomy, access, calibration, resources, authority, or Practised Reliability.

## Later-System Boundaries

- **Monster Evolution** owns species stages, biological branches, mutations, hybrid forms, and evolved embodiment. Skill rules may supply learned prerequisites or evidence without declaring those outcomes.
- **Human Development** owns detailed classes, Profession structures, martial schools, magical schools, academies, and social advancement. Human Skill Trees may map learning relationships without granting those statuses.
- **Soul Weapons** owns Weapon Souls, awakening, intertwining, weapon evolution, manifestations, and item-specific Soul effects. Skill owns only learned use or coordination.
- **Magic** owns sources, affinities, spell effects, rituals, resistance, enchanting, alchemy, divine magic, and forbidden magic. Skill may own learned formation, control, diagnosis, or Conceptual Interpretation through valid access.
- **World Engine** owns population, ecology, institutions, history, recognition, and causal world consequences. Skill use may influence that state without absorbing it as personal capability.
- **GM Toolkit and templates** may record or adjudicate these rules but cannot create exceptions to their ownership, evidence, embodiment, or agency requirements.

## Phase Guarantees

The completed Skill Engine preserves all of the following:

- human and monster Skill Trees begin distinct;
- Reincarnation crossover is bounded and cannot erase species identity;
- knowledge and Persistent Familiarity do not equal current mastery;
- current embodiment, access, training, and Practised Reliability remain necessary for present expression;
- Skill Evolution and Skill Fusion are separate transformations;
- active, passive, hidden, and conceptual labels cannot waive ordinary requirements;
- one effect has one owner and cannot yield duplicate Development;
- no universal Skill level, score, currency, tree, list, or strongest route exists.

The [Roadmap](../../design/ROADMAP.md) remains authoritative for later implementation status.
