# Conceptual Skills

## Purpose

This document defines advanced Skills that operate through deeply integrated understanding of principles, relationships, abstractions, meanings, or metaphysical concepts rather than only fixed physical techniques. Conceptual Skills can support extraordinary perception, judgment, coordination, craft, or magical expression, but they remain bounded learned competencies.

A Conceptual Skill is never unrestricted permission to control reality, ownership of a concept, universal magic, automatic authority, or a replacement for ordinary training.

## Core Rule

A **Conceptual Skill** applies extensive foundational mastery and lived understanding to one bounded Conceptual Domain through a specific interpretation and valid present route. Every use must state what relationship the practitioner is attempting to perceive, predict, organise, preserve, alter, or express, and must remain limited by scope, access, embodiment, precision, cost, opposition, context, contradiction, authority, and consequence.

Understanding a concept, possessing access to act through it, holding authority over a target, and producing an effect are separate claims. No one of them automatically grants the others.

## Conceptual Skills and the Shared Model

Record every Conceptual Skill through the [Shared Skill Model](README.md#shared-skill-model):

| Dimension | Conceptual-Skill question |
| --- | --- |
| **Skill Identity** | Which bounded competency and Conceptual Domain does the Skill actually represent? |
| **Origin** | Which foundations, lives, practices, cultures, professions, species routes, or magical traditions produced the understanding? |
| **Requirements** | Which mastery, experience, embodiment, tools, relationships, access, authority, resources, and contexts are necessary? |
| **Persistent Familiarity** | What integrated understanding legitimately persists without preserving present access or timeless correctness? |
| **Current Access** | Which principles, interpretations, and expression routes can this incarnation presently reach? |
| **Embodied Expression** | How can the current body, senses, cognition, tools, magic, and environment act on the understanding? |
| **Practised Reliability** | Which Conceptual Claims remain dependable through ambiguity, contradiction, pressure, and changed context? |
| **Contextual Effectiveness** | Where does this interpretation apply, and where is another method or concept more useful? |
| **Evolution State** | Did the Skill arise through conceptual elevation, fusion, crossover, or another valid Development route? |
| **Visibility** | Who recognises the Skill, its domain, its method, or only an apparently impossible result? |

These dimensions cannot be combined into a conceptual rank, metaphysical level, or authority score.

## Terms

### Conceptual Skill

An advanced bounded Skill that applies deeply integrated understanding of a principle, relationship, abstraction, meaning, or metaphysical concept through a valid present expression route.

### Conceptual Domain

The specific concept or relationship around which a Conceptual Skill is organised, such as distance, momentum, boundaries, balance, memory, or connection. A domain is a field of interpretation, not property owned by the practitioner.

### Conceptual Claim

The exact result attempted through a Conceptual Skill in a stated context, including the target, relationship, method, scope, access, cost, limits, opposition, and intended consequence.

### Conceptual Access

A valid present route through which conceptual understanding can inform or produce an effect. Access may be perceptual, embodied, technical, social, professional, ritual, magical, divine, relational, territorial, tool-mediated, or otherwise established.

### Conceptual Interpretation

A coherent but bounded model of what a concept means and how it behaves within the practitioner's experience, culture, world laws, and expression route. Interpretations may overlap, conflict, or fail outside their evidence.

### Conceptual Spellcasting

A magical expression in which a spell's formation or control is organised through a Conceptual Skill. The Skill supplies learned interpretation and technique; the magic system retains ownership of source, access, authority, energy, effect, resistance, and failure.

## What Conceptual Skill Is Not

| Related thing | Distinction |
| --- | --- |
| **Abstract knowledge** | A theory or definition may be understood intellectually without adaptable, pressure-tested application. |
| **Metaphor** | Symbolic language can guide learning but does not become causally true merely because it is evocative. |
| **Foundational mastery** | Deep ordinary mastery is required but need not become conceptual; many masters remain focused on concrete methods. |
| **Skill Evolution** | Conceptual elevation is one possible Evolution Pattern. A completed Conceptual Skill must still meet this document's requirements. |
| **Skill Fusion** | Several foundations may contribute, but a conceptual label does not fuse them automatically. |
| **Magic** | A Conceptual Skill can be analytical, embodied, technical, social, or magical. Metaphysical effects require an actual magical or other valid access route. |
| **Species trait** | A body may perceive or interact with a domain naturally. Adaptable learned interpretation remains a Skill, while the trait stays body-owned. |
| **Soul system** | Soul Depth, Resonance, Titles, Echoes, and Avatars may support history or access under their rules without granting conceptual mastery. |
| **Authority** | Understanding names, boundaries, contracts, or identity does not grant jurisdiction, consent, ownership, or permission to alter them. |
| **Profession** | Professional expertise may develop conceptual depth, but standards, responsibility, tools, credentials, and institutional access remain profession-owned. |

## Prerequisites

A Conceptual Skill requires all of the following in forms appropriate to the domain:

1. **Extensive foundational mastery:** concrete Skills, knowledge, perception, and correction deep enough to distinguish the concept from a slogan.
2. **Lived experience:** meaningful encounters with variation, failure, tradeoffs, consequences, and cases that resist simple rules.
3. **Coherent understanding:** an integrated interpretation that can explain, predict, diagnose, and revise rather than merely repeat doctrine.
4. **Suitable access:** a valid route for the intended expression. Metaphysical claims specifically require metaphysical access; analysis alone does not move reality.
5. **Embodiment or tool support:** the present body, senses, cognition, magical structures, instruments, materials, allies, or institutions must support the action where relevant.
6. **Meaningful limitation:** the Skill has a bounded subject, scale, relationship, method, context, authority, cost, or tradeoff that excludes other claims.
7. **Contextual interpretation:** the practitioner can distinguish when the model applies, when it conflicts with evidence, and when another interpretation is necessary.
8. **Practised Reliability:** the capability has been tested across relevant ambiguity, opposition, disruption, and consequence.

Time, Soul Depth, intelligence, a title, revelation, one transcendent experience, or a concept-themed spell cannot substitute for these foundations.

## Expression Routes

### Analytical

The Skill detects, compares, predicts, or diagnoses relationships through observation and reasoning. It may appear extraordinary while remaining nonmagical and limited by information.

### Embodied

The practitioner organises movement, perception, timing, regulation, or tool use through a concept. A duellist can act through distance without changing the physical distance by supernatural means.

### Technical or Professional

The Skill guides design, medicine, law, craft, administration, research, or another responsibility. It remains subject to materials, standards, evidence, ethics, authority, and consequences.

### Social or Collective

The Skill interprets roles, incentives, communication, coordination, identity, boundaries, or obligations among people. It may change arrangements through valid action without overriding minds or consent.

### Magical or Metaphysical

A valid magical, divine, spiritual, territorial, Soul, artifact, ritual, or other rule permits some conceptual relationship to produce an effect. The access source defines what is possible, what it costs, who can resist, and what authority applies.

One Conceptual Skill may use several routes, but proficiency in one does not grant the others automatically.

## Possible Conceptual Domains

Domains are examples, not a universal catalog. Each must be narrowed by interpretation and claim.

### Distance

Distance may include reach, approach, separation, interval, route cost, social remoteness, or metaphysical adjacency. A duellist may control engagement distance; a portal mage still needs magical access to alter space. Understanding distance does not grant teleportation.

### Momentum

Momentum may describe physical motion, cascading decisions, institutional inertia, magical flow, or escalating conflict. A Skill may predict, redirect, preserve, or interrupt momentum through available means. It cannot create or erase arbitrary force without an owning effect.

### Boundaries

Boundaries may be physical edges, territories, roles, permissions, identities, wards, categories, or transitions. The Skill may detect, interpret, negotiate, test, mark, or maintain a relevant boundary. It does not make every boundary absolute or give the practitioner ownership of what lies within.

### Concealment

Concealment concerns the relationship among a thing, its traces, observers, expectations, and channels of detection. It may coordinate camouflage, misdirection, silence, record control, or magical masking. It does not grant universal invisibility or erase consequences.

### Names

Names may organise reference, identity, invocation, reputation, classification, memory, or contractual address. A Skill may identify naming structures or use a valid magical naming method. Knowing a name does not create consent, command, perfect knowledge, or ownership.

### Balance

Balance may concern posture, homeostasis, resource tradeoffs, competing forces, ecosystems, institutions, or magical stability. It can guide diagnosis and correction toward a stated range. It does not define one universally good equilibrium or heal every disorder.

### Rhythm

Rhythm may organise timing, recurrence, breathing, movement, work, ritual, communication, or cycles. The Skill can anticipate and alter participation in a pattern through valid means. It does not stop time or control every event with periodicity.

### Patterns

Patterns include recurrence, structure, symmetry, anomaly, dependency, and transformation. A Skill can compare known and emerging structure while remaining vulnerable to sparse data, noise, novelty, and false pattern recognition. It is not omniscience.

### Contracts

Contracts concern stated terms, mutual interpretation, obligations, permissions, exchange, enforcement, breach, and remedy. A conceptual Skill may draft, diagnose, negotiate, or magically express contracts through valid authority. It cannot bind an unwilling party merely by understanding obligation.

### Memory

Memory may concern encoding, retrieval, association, testimony, forgetting, identity, and reconstruction. A Skill may organise recall or interpret memory structure. Reading, restoring, suppressing, or changing another mind requires explicit access, consent or resistance, and an owning system.

### Identity

Identity may include continuity, self-concept, role, classification, embodiment, social recognition, or Soul persistence. The Skill may support interpretation and integration without defining another being's identity, rewriting a soul, or proving metaphysical truth by assertion.

### Decay

Decay concerns breakdown, fatigue, corrosion, entropy, forgetting, institutional collapse, magical dissipation, or ecological decomposition. A Skill may recognise stages, accelerate or slow a valid process through available methods, and plan around consequences. It does not command universal entropy.

### Connection

Connection may include material joins, causal dependencies, communication, relationships, networks, Soul Links, or magical conduits. A Skill may trace, strengthen, reroute, or sever only connections its access and authority can affect. It grants neither remote access to everything related nor control over connected people.

## Limits on Every Conceptual Claim

Every use must answer all of these constraints qualitatively:

| Limit | Required question |
| --- | --- |
| **Scope** | Which exact target, relation, scale, duration, range, and function are included and excluded? |
| **Interpretation** | Which understanding of the concept is being applied, and what assumptions does it make? |
| **Cost** | What attention, time, energy, mana, material, risk, obligation, preparation, or opportunity does the attempt consume? |
| **Precision** | How finely can the practitioner distinguish targets, degrees, timing, or collateral effects? |
| **Opposition** | Which beings, systems, conditions, counter-Skills, wards, evidence, or competing actions resist the claim? |
| **Context** | Which environment, culture, law, relationship, world rule, or history makes the interpretation relevant? |
| **Contradiction** | What happens when evidence, another interpretation, or a competing concept conflicts with the model? |
| **Embodiment** | Which present senses, structures, movements, cognition, tools, Channels, or interfaces are required? |
| **Authority** | What permission, jurisdiction, consent, ownership, bond, role, or magical recognition permits action on the target? |
| **Consequence** | What changes, who bears the outcome, what traces remain, and what new causal pressures follow? |

A vague claim such as "control distance" is invalid. A claim such as "use trained footwork and line pressure to keep one spear wielder outside effective thrust range in this corridor" is assessable. A magical version must additionally name the source, spatial law, range, cost, resistance, and failure.

## Interpretation, Contradiction, and Opposition

Concepts do not have one universally available interpretation. A healer may understand balance as dynamic regulation; a judge as equitable obligation; a monster as territorial equilibrium; a mage as conserved magical tension.

When interpretations conflict:

- neither label wins merely by sounding broader or more profound;
- compare the exact Conceptual Claims, access routes, evidence, authority, costs, precision, and context;
- permit partial compatibility, mutual interference, asymmetry, or Incomparability;
- let world laws and causal consequences matter;
- preserve uncertainty where evidence is genuinely incomplete;
- never resolve the dispute by assigning a universal conceptual tier.

Opposition can exploit a Skill's assumptions. Noise defeats a pattern reader; role ambiguity confuses a boundary model; nonreciprocal power undermines a contract interpretation; an unfamiliar body changes embodied distance; a being with distributed identity may not fit a human model of selfhood.

## Relationships With Other Systems

### Magic and Conceptual Spellcasting

Conceptual understanding can shape spell formation, targeting, efficiency, diagnosis, adaptation, or control. Magical Development owns Access, source, affinity, Channels, reserves, regulation, authority, and current capacity. The future magic system owns exact spell effects and resistance.

A nonmagical Conceptual Skill does not become magical through its name. Conceptual Spellcasting requires both the Skill and a valid magical route; losing either can leave knowledge without effect or magic without refined conceptual control.

### Soul Depth

Soul Depth may support integration of complex, contradictory, or identity-changing experience. It does not grant concepts, understanding, access, authority, output, or a conceptual rank.

### Soul Resonance

Soul Resonance may align a genuine history, relationship, place, object, or Soul Imprint relevant to a Conceptual Domain. It cannot create an absent Link or turn resonance with "connection" into universal access to all connections.

### Soul Titles

A Soul Title may create symbolic recognition, expectations, opportunities, resistance, or pressure around a Conceptual Skill. **Keeper of Boundaries** is not proof of boundary mastery or jurisdiction over every threshold.

### Soul Avatars

A Soul Avatar may synthesise conceptual perspectives from valid lives under Continuity Concordance. It does not own every concept associated with those lives, replace foundations, or provide infallible interpretation.

### Species Traits

A species trait may perceive, embody, or metaphysically interact with a domain, such as territorial edges, collective rhythm, decay, or connection. The trait provides capacity or access; meaningful learned interpretation and adaptable use remain Skill Development.

### Divine Authority

A deity, spirit, mandate, office, covenant, or magical law may grant scoped authority to act through a concept. Authority follows its source, jurisdiction, conditions, purpose, revocation, opposition, and consequences. Conceptual mastery does not create divinity, and divine status does not create mastery.

### Profession Expertise

Healers, jurists, architects, navigators, diplomats, archivists, ritualists, and other professionals may develop conceptual expertise through responsibility and difficult cases. The Conceptual Skill does not replace concrete procedure, safety, ethics, materials, teams, credentials, or current law.

### Skill Evolution and Fusion

Conceptual elevation can transform an established Skill when its qualitative change meets [Skill Evolution](SKILL_EVOLUTION.md) rules. Several developed Skills may support a Conceptual Skill through [Skill Fusion](SKILL_FUSION.md), but an abstract label cannot merge undeveloped components.

## Reincarnation and Changed Ages

A Conceptual Skill may form a Skill Imprint preserving legitimately integrated understanding and Skill XP. What persists is developmental history, not timeless ownership of a universal truth.

After Reincarnation or a World Reset:

- Current Access and embodiment must be re-established;
- metaphysical laws, magical sources, authorities, languages, professions, and institutions may differ;
- a former interpretation may remain insightful, become incomplete, or fail dangerously;
- retained familiarity can accelerate comparison and relearning without replacing present evidence;
- concepts expressed through a former species trait require a valid new Receiving Route;
- former divine, legal, territorial, contractual, or social authority remains behind unless a current rule restores it;
- Practised Reliability requires current testing against the new Age.

Living many lives may broaden available perspectives while also creating contradiction. Reincarnation does not make one soul the universal authority on memory, identity, death, connection, or any other recurring theme.

## Worked Examples

### A Duellist and Distance

A master duellist learns to understand distance as changing reach, commitment, recovery, attention, terrain, threat, and initiative rather than measurement alone. Through posture, line pressure, timing, and footwork, the duellist makes an opponent cross a disadvantageous interval.

The Skill neither teleports nor controls space. It is strongest against known bodies and weapons in readable terrain, and it can fail against several opponents, concealed reach, unfamiliar anatomy, missiles, or an opponent willing to accept the disadvantage.

### A Healer and Balance

A healer integrates anatomy, symptoms, reserves, magical flow, treatment burden, compensation, and recovery into a Conceptual Skill of dynamic balance. The healer can identify when suppressing one symptom would destabilise another system and choose a bounded intervention.

Balance is not an automatic cure or moral command to preserve the status quo. The healer still needs diagnosis, treatment access, consent, resources, and species knowledge, and may face several survivable but incompatible equilibria.

### A Tracker and Relationships

A tracker follows relationships among water, shelter, prey, predators, trails, fear, weather, and habitual decisions rather than relying only on footprints. Missing signs can themselves alter the model when the ecosystem should have produced them.

The tracker cannot locate a target with no relevant connection or infer private intent from one trace. Migration, deliberate deception, new species, stale assumptions, and damaged ecology can make the relational model wrong.

### A Leader and Coordination

A leader understands coordination through information flow, role clarity, trust, timing, redundancy, bottlenecks, and local initiative. By changing briefings, signals, incentives, delegation, and fallback authority, the leader can reshape how a willing group acts together.

This is not mind control. Members can refuse, misunderstand, defect, pursue different goals, lack needed Skills, or suffer overload. The leader owns coordination Skill, not the people or their capabilities.

### A Monster and Territorial Boundaries

A territorial monster species senses established markers and the pressure of crossings through a specialised organ. One elder develops a Conceptual Skill that distinguishes invitation, trespass, migration, shared hunting ground, sanctuary, challenge, and decaying claims.

The trait supplies perception; the Skill supplies interpretation and negotiated or ritual action. The elder does not own all land, erect an absolute barrier, or bind outsiders who lack a valid relationship to the territorial system. Changed markers, rival authority, environmental collapse, and unfamiliar boundary laws can defeat the method.

## GM Procedure

When adjudicating a proposed Conceptual Skill or use:

1. identify the bounded Conceptual Domain and exact Skill Identity;
2. verify extensive foundational mastery, lived experience, coherent understanding, and Practised Reliability;
3. state the Conceptual Interpretation rather than relying on a broad label;
4. identify the present Conceptual Access route and its Owning System;
5. state one exact Conceptual Claim with target, relation, method, and intended result;
6. resolve scope, interpretation, cost, precision, opposition, context, contradiction, embodiment, authority, and consequence;
7. separate analysis, embodiment, professional action, social action, magic, divine permission, species traits, and Soul support;
8. test alternatives, counters, invalid assumptions, and failure states;
9. assess the complete Shared Skill Model without a score or universal tier;
10. reject or narrow any wording that would grant undefined effects;
11. record changed-world and reincarnation compatibility separately from Persistent Familiarity;
12. communicate what the practitioner understands, attempts, risks, and observes without presenting the concept as omnipotence.

## Safeguards

- **No vague unlimited effect:** every use requires a specific Conceptual Claim and all ten limit questions.
- **No foundation replacement:** conceptual understanding cannot substitute for ordinary Skills, knowledge, embodiment, tools, resources, practice, or reliability.
- **No automatic metaphysical authority:** understanding a concept grants no ownership, jurisdiction, consent, permission, divine status, or right to alter a target.
- **No universal solver:** one Conceptual Domain cannot answer every physical, magical, social, professional, Soul, or world problem through metaphor.
- **No universal magic:** a concept-themed Skill produces metaphysical effects only through a valid owning system with source, access, cost, resistance, and failure.
- **No label supremacy:** broader names, rarer traditions, divine associations, and abstract language do not outrank narrower effective capabilities.
- **No trait capture:** species perception or anatomy remains body-owned and cannot be preserved as a free conceptual effect after Reincarnation.
- **No Soul shortcut:** Depth, Resonance, Titles, Echoes, Avatars, and Archive Records support only their established functions.
- **No contradiction erasure:** competing interpretations, world laws, evidence, opposition, and consequences remain capable of limiting or defeating the Skill.
- **No mind control by coordination:** conceptual leadership changes valid causal structures and communication, not another person's agency.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- the complete magic, divine magic, conceptual spell, ritual, or resistance systems;
- universal metaphysical laws or a closed catalog of concepts;
- gods, divine offices, magical schools, classes, professions, or monster evolution routes;
- formulas for conceptual power, range, cost, or opposition;
- campaign-specific Conceptual Skills or world laws.
