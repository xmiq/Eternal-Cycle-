# Skill Engine Safeguards

## Purpose

This document defines how to represent learned capability without creating a permanent Skill for every action, method, source, or descriptive label. It consolidates ownership, classification, anti-proliferation, and cleanup rules for the complete Skill Engine.

These safeguards protect earned Development. Their purpose is to make records truthful and playable, not to reduce a capable character by relabelling established evidence.

## Core Rule

For every claimed effect, identify one Owning System and use the smallest truthful Capability Representation that preserves the capability's actual function, evidence, requirements, dependencies, limits, persistence, and failure states.

An action, success, repeated motion, dramatic name, source of support, or useful combination is not automatically a Skill. A separate Skill exists only when a bounded learned capability has its own coherent function, meaningful Development, adaptable use, requirements, correction process, and failure behaviour that an existing representation does not already capture.

One scene may contain many effects with different owners. One effect cannot be counted several times because several systems helped produce it.

## Skill Safeguards and the Shared Model

Every proposed or retained Skill must still use the [Shared Skill Model](README.md#shared-skill-model):

| Dimension | Safeguard question |
| --- | --- |
| **Skill Identity** | Is this one distinct learned capability, an existing capability under another name, or something another system owns? |
| **Origin** | Which learning history produced it, and which sources merely enabled or supported that learning? |
| **Requirements** | Which body, senses, knowledge, tools, magic, relationships, authority, environment, or Soul access remain necessary? |
| **Persistent Familiarity** | What specific Skill XP or Skill Imprint can persist without duplicating another record? |
| **Current Access** | What can the present incarnation actually reconstruct, understand, or invoke now? |
| **Embodied Expression** | What learned function can the present body and available route perform, apart from external effects? |
| **Practised Reliability** | What evidence shows adaptable performance rather than one success, a script, or a supportive circumstance? |
| **Contextual Effectiveness** | Where is this representation useful, and where do its assumptions, dependencies, or counters make it ineffective? |
| **Evolution State** | Is the change ordinary improvement, a branch, a true evolution, a composite, a fusion, or only a renamed form? |
| **Visibility** | Is the capability known, hidden, misclassified, or represented by several local names without changing its identity? |

These dimensions are descriptive. They do not combine into a universal Skill rating, slot count, rarity tier, or currency.

## Terms

### Capability Representation

The smallest canonical record that truthfully describes a capability or effect, its owner, evidence, requirements, dependencies, persistence, and failure states. A representation may be transient, a tactic, a technique, a Habit, a branch, a Skill, or a record owned by another system.

### Ownership Test

The procedure used to identify which canonical system defines a claimed effect and which contributors only enable, modify, or support it.

### Representation Cleanup

The correction, merger, split, renaming, retirement, or reclassification of capability records so they match established evidence and ownership without duplicating or erasing legitimately earned Development.

### Tactic

A deliberate plan for achieving a particular objective with available capabilities, information, allies, tools, and circumstances. A tactic may be repeatable and teachable without becoming a separate Skill.

## Classification Model

The classifications below are not a ladder. A technique is not an inferior Skill, and a composite is not automatically better than its components. Choose the form that tells the truth with the least duplication.

### No Persistent Capability

Use this when an outcome came from luck, ordinary action, imitation without integration, one protected rehearsal, involuntary exposure, an isolated success, or a circumstance that produced no stable learned change.

Record consequences and useful evidence where appropriate. Do not create Skill XP, a Skill Imprint, or a permanent label merely because the moment was dramatic.

### Situational Ruling

Use a situational ruling when temporary facts resolve the current claim: terrain, surprise, weather, an opponent's mistake, a borrowed resource, a one-time opening, or a favorable interaction among existing capabilities.

The ruling describes what happened and why. It does not become a personal capability unless later meaningful learning independently qualifies under another classification.

### Tactic

Use a Tactic for a plan that selects, sequences, positions, or coordinates existing capabilities toward a stated objective. A Tactic may become familiar and may produce Development in the capabilities used, but it remains a plan unless it develops a distinct recurring learned function.

### Technique

Use a Technique for one bounded execution method inside an existing Skill. The parent Skill owns the broader perception, judgment, correction, and adaptation. A Technique may be named, taught, practised, hidden, or highly effective without requiring a separate Skill Imprint.

### Habit

Use a Habit for a repeated response that has become easy to initiate. A Habit may improve speed or reduce attention, but it may also be context-blind, harmful, or fragile. It supports or obstructs capability without automatically becoming a Skill or persisting through the Soul.

### Specialisation

Use a Specialisation when concentrated practice narrows an established Skill or Discipline around a recurring purpose, context, tool, opponent, or method. The narrower transfer and characteristic tradeoff must be real. A fashionable label or one preferred use is not enough.

### Skill Branch

Use a Skill Branch when a route remains part of a parent Skill identity but has sustained differences in function, method, requirements, context, evidence, or tradeoffs. Shared foundations do not grant the branch automatically, and the branch does not duplicate the parent capability.

### Full Skill

Use a full Skill only when all of the following are established in forms appropriate to the claim:

1. a bounded recurring function;
2. meaningful learning rather than source possession or repetition alone;
3. adaptable use across relevant variation;
4. its own perception, judgment, execution, or correction process;
5. identifiable requirements and dependencies;
6. characteristic failure states and limits;
7. Development evidence and sufficient Practised Reliability when the Skill was established, with current reliability tracked separately after rust, suppression, or Reincarnation;
8. a scope that is neither microscopic nor an umbrella for unrelated capabilities;
9. no adequate existing Skill, branch, technique, Profession, trait, effect, relationship, item, or Soul rule;
10. one clear owner for every claimed effect.

If evidence is promising but incomplete, use an [Adaptive Skill Candidate](ADAPTIVE_SKILLS.md#adaptive-skill-candidate) or the narrower current representation. Do not grant a provisional full Skill as a reward for uncertainty.

### Composite Skill

Use a Composite Skill when several developed Skills or Development Tracks are coordinated toward a recurring purpose while their operations and ownership remain clearly separable. The composite owns learned coordination and whole-task judgment, not duplicate copies of component effects.

### Skill Fusion

Use Skill Fusion only when Component Skills have crossed boundaries in perception, judgment, correction, and execution to form a coherent new competency under [Skill Fusion](SKILL_FUSION.md). Possession, simultaneous use, Synergy, or a named combination is insufficient.

### Evolved Skill

Use an evolved Skill only for a stable qualitative transformation of a Prior Form under [Skill Evolution](SKILL_EVOLUTION.md). A stronger result, a promotion, an additional Technique, a translation, or a new name does not create an evolution. Record the Prior Form relationship without inventing a chain of cosmetic stages.

### Species Trait

Use Species Trait when the capability is an anatomical, physiological, sensory, cognitive, magical, spiritual, material, or lifecycle property of a current form. Learned control or tactical use may be a Skill; the body-owned capacity and output remain Species Development.

### Professional Knowledge

Use Professional Knowledge or another Profession record when the claim concerns a field's standards, responsibilities, procedures, theory, institutions, credentials, or bodies of practice. Knowing one fact is not a Skill, membership grants no automatic bundle, and personal Skill remains distinct from Professional Judgment and authority.

### Magical Effect

Use a magical effect when magic supplies the actual source, energy, transformation, resistance, range, or supernatural result. A Skill may own learned formation, targeting, control, or diagnosis. It cannot absorb the spell or source as an unconditional personal effect.

### Social Relationship

Use a relationship record when trust, loyalty, fear, obligation, familiarity, shared history, consent, or access exists between particular parties. A social Skill may help form or navigate the relationship; it does not own the other party or convert the relationship into a portable personal bonus.

### Equipment Benefit

Use an equipment record when an item supplies material properties, reach, protection, storage, computation, a prepared interface, or another item-owned function. An [Enchantment](../magic/ENCHANTING.md) remains Magic-owned through its Host, Function, source, Layers, maintenance, and failure; a Weapon Soul or Soul Weapon effect remains Soul-owned. Skill owns learned use and adaptation. Removing the item removes its contribution unless another valid source replaces it.

### Soul-System Effect

Use the specific Soul system when Soul Depth, Soul Resonance, a Soul Echo, a Soul Title, a Soul Avatar, a Soul Weapon, an Akashic interface, Retained Instinct, or another established Soul rule supplies the effect. Skill may own present interpretation or practised use only where meaningful learning actually developed it.

## Ownership Test

Apply this test before creating, changing, or rewarding a Skill record.

### 1. State the Exact Effect

Replace labels such as better combat, dragon power, perfect stealth, or master crafting with the exact result being claimed. Different effects in the same action may have different owners.

### 2. Identify the Causal Source

Ask what directly makes the effect possible:

- learned perception, judgment, execution, or correction;
- current body or Species Trait;
- magical source, Channel, spell, contract, or authority;
- professional knowledge, standards, role, or institution;
- relationship, ally, group, or social permission;
- item, material, infrastructure, or environment;
- Soul system, title, Echo, resonance, or retained history;
- temporary circumstance or opponent error.

The direct source is the initial owner. Supporting contributors keep their own requirements and costs.

### 3. Apply the Removal Test

Consider what remains if one contributor is removed.

- If removing the item removes the claimed output, the item owns that output.
- If changing the body removes the natural capacity, Species Development owns it.
- If suppressing magic removes the supernatural effect, magic owns it.
- If the other person refuses or leaves, the relationship or group owned that access.
- If the Soul route closes, the Skill cannot claim the Soul effect as personal technique.

Learned methods that remain useful may still be Skills, but their external dependencies must stay explicit.

### 4. Apply the Learning Test

Ask whether the character meaningfully learned to recognise problems, choose among methods, correct errors, adapt across variation, and understand failure. Exposure, possession, inheritance, suffering, killing, elapsed time, and repetition alone fail this test.

### 5. Apply the Independence Test

Ask whether the proposed capability needs its own Development because it solves a coherent recurring problem that no existing representation handles adequately. If an existing Skill can improve, add a Technique, or branch truthfully, do that instead.

### 6. Apply the Scope Test

Reject both extremes:

- a micro-label that records one motion, target, trigger, body part, spell, tool, or successful action without independent Development;
- an umbrella label that hides capabilities with different teachers, requirements, feedback, reliability, and failures.

The representation should be broad enough to support meaningful variation and narrow enough to expose real requirements and tradeoffs.

### 7. Apply the Persistence Test

Identify what can persist and through which system. A Skill Imprint may preserve legitimate Skill structure and Skill XP. It does not preserve equipment, relationships, authority, species anatomy, magic sources, present reliability, or every situational method as additional Skills.

### 8. Apply the Duplication Test

Compare the proposed record with existing Skills, branches, techniques, forms, professions, traits, magic, items, relationships, and Soul effects. Synonyms, translations, cultural names, active and passive modes, hidden status, and changed embodiments do not automatically create separate owners or rewards.

### 9. Apply the Agency Test

No classification can grant control of another being, compel the current incarnation's deliberate choices, or turn automatic response into unavoidable action. Consent, authority, resistance, Conscious Override, and participant ownership remain in force.

### 10. Record Dependencies and Failure

A valid representation states what enables it, what it costs, how it can fail, and what happens when support disappears. A capability with no exposed dependency, counter, failure, or boundary is probably overbroad or owned elsewhere.

## Proliferation Safeguards

### No Skill Per Action

Actions are evidence and expressions, not automatic persistent assets. Repeated actions normally develop an existing Skill, Technique, Habit, or physical capacity. Create a new Skill only when a distinct capability passes every full-Skill test.

### No Duplicate Names or Rewards

Different cultures, interfaces, teachers, species, and Ages may use different names for one Skill Identity. Record aliases and variants instead of separate Skills unless their methods, requirements, Development, and failures are materially different.

One learning change awards one appropriate Development history. It cannot award duplicate Skill XP to a Skill, its alias, every Technique, an active mode, a passive mode, and a translated name.

### No Microscopic Fragmentation

Do not create independent Skills for every weapon motion, prey species, spell shape, tool, organ, terrain feature, emotion, or sensory cue. Use Techniques, branches, context tags, requirements, and evidence inside the owning capability.

A narrow capability may be a full Skill when it genuinely has independent learning, variation, correction, and transfer. Small scope alone neither proves nor disproves Skill status.

### No Giant Umbrella Skills

A label such as Combat, Magic, Survival, Leadership, Crafting, Knowledge, Monster Ability, or Concept Mastery is normally too broad to own unrelated functions. Split it when components require materially different training, embodiment, access, evidence, or failure handling.

Shared foundations and a Discipline may connect several Skills without merging them.

### No Fusion Explosion

Do not create a fusion for every pair or set of compatible Skills. Most combined use remains sequencing, Synergy, a Hybrid Technique, or a Composite Skill. One true fusion does not automatically fuse with each component's later evolution or every other Skill the practitioner knows.

### No Meaningless Evolution Chains

Each evolved form must show a qualitative change and a truthful relationship to the Prior Form. Collapse cosmetic ranks, numeric stages, repeated superlatives, promotions, and renamed improvements into one Skill history with relevant milestones.

### No Free Passive Accumulation

Active and passive are expression modes, not duplicate Skills or free slots. Internalisation preserves Background Load, resource use, triggers, suppression, unfamiliar-body error, opportunity costs, and Conscious Override. A former-life passive requires current access and recalibration.

### No Retroactive Hidden Skills

A Hidden Skill requires established provenance, ownership, requirements, evidence, and fair clues before it materially changes an outcome. Hiddenness changes who knows a capability; it cannot create an unrecorded solution after the fact.

### No Vague Conceptual Power

Every Conceptual Skill requires deep foundations, a bounded Conceptual Domain, a coherent interpretation, a valid access route, and a specific Conceptual Claim. Metaphor, a broad noun, or an abstract title grants no unspecified effect or authority.

### No Reincarnation Proliferation

Reincarnation preserves qualifying Skill Imprints and Skill XP, not one new Skill for every remembered act, Technique, Habit, body, or translated application. Crossover records source provenance and one current Receiving Route. Direct, principle, partial, translated, latent, inaccessible, distorted, harmful, and impossible outcomes do not duplicate the source benefit.

A receiving Skill becomes distinct only when current-life Development establishes a genuinely different bounded capability. Even then, shared evidence and XP are not counted twice for the same learning.

### No Trait Duplication

Species traits own natural weapons, organs, senses, secretion, inherited magic, body regulation, and other form-based capacities. Skill records learned control, interpretation, tactics, and adaptation without preserving the trait as a personal effect in an incompatible body.

### No Profession Bundles

Entering a Profession, earning a credential, holding an office, or accessing an institution grants no automatic bundle of Skills. Record actual knowledge, competence, judgment, standards, relationships, and access under their owners.

### No Item-to-Skill Conversion

Owning or repeatedly using equipment does not turn its material or magical powers into personal Skills. Record learned handling separately. Tool Integration may change Skill organisation while the item remains a dependency.

### No Multi-System Benefit Stacking

When several systems describe the same benefit, assign it once to the narrowest appropriate owner. Support may change access, cost, scale, reliability, timing, or Contextual Effectiveness only through its own rules. It does not reproduce the owned effect as another permanent gain.

## Representation Cleanup

Cleanup may occur during design review, rules migration, discovery of hidden facts, translation between Ages, or correction of an earlier classification. Explain material changes to affected participants; do not use cleanup as an invisible penalty or retroactive surprise.

### Merge Overlapping Skills

1. identify the shared Skill Identity and actual differences;
2. choose one surviving Skill or parent structure;
3. preserve meaningful variants as Techniques, Specialisations, branches, aliases, or evidence;
4. combine duplicate histories without multiplying Skill XP;
5. retain the strongest legitimately demonstrated scope only where its requirements and reliability remain supported;
6. record any incompatible habits or contexts rather than averaging them away.

### Retire Obsolete Labels

Mark an obsolete label as a historical alias, superseded local term, former diagnosis, or retired record. Retirement removes duplicate bookkeeping, not demonstrated capability. Current Access, rust, lost requirements, or changed meaning should be recorded separately.

### Rename Without Inventing Progression

A translation, corrected name, cultural title, clearer label, or stylistic change does not award Development, create a new Skill Imprint, restore reliability, or count as Skill Evolution. Preserve provenance and aliases where they matter.

### Split an Overbroad Skill

1. identify distinct functions, training routes, requirements, and failure states hidden by the umbrella;
2. assign shared foundations once;
3. allocate evidence and Skill XP qualitatively to the parts it actually supports;
4. do not copy the full former history into every new Skill;
5. leave unsupported parts undeveloped or unknown;
6. record remaining coordination as a Discipline, Composite Skill, or other truthful structure.

### Reclassify the Capability

Move a record to the system that actually owns it. A supposed Skill may become a Technique, Habit, branch, Profession record, Species Trait, magical effect, relationship, equipment benefit, Soul effect, or situational ruling.

Preserve any separately demonstrated learned coordination or control as a Skill component. Do not preserve the external output as personal mastery.

### Correct False Fusion or Evolution

Reclassify a False Fusion as coordination, combination, Synergy, Composite Skill, or Hybrid Technique while preserving genuine Component Skill Development. Collapse a false evolution into improvement, mastery, Specialisation, branch, renaming, or promotion while preserving valid evidence in the Prior Form.

### Preserve Legitimately Earned Capability

Representation Cleanup must preserve:

- demonstrated knowledge, judgment, execution, correction, and reliability;
- legitimate Skill XP and Skill Imprints once, under the correct identity;
- established Techniques, branches, Specialisations, and tradeoffs where useful;
- current requirements, dependencies, rust, suppression, and inaccessibility;
- source history, aliases, teachers, cultural variants, and prior classifications where relevant;
- uncertainty when evidence cannot support a more precise allocation.

Cleanup must not preserve duplicated rewards, unsupported scope, external-source effects, waived requirements, or claimed mastery that never had evidence.

## Worked Examples

### One Wall Kick

A fighter runs up a wall once to strike above a guard. Existing athletics, balance, footwork, terrain, surprise, and the wall's condition explain the success. Record a situational Tactic and possible evidence toward an acrobatic Technique, not Wall-Running Strike as a new Skill.

If later practice develops repeatable surface reading, approach adjustment, recovery, and use across varied structures, the method may become a Technique or branch within an existing movement Skill. It becomes a full Adaptive Skill only if that parent no longer represents its distinct recurring function adequately.

### Dragon Breath and Learned Control

A dragon's organ, fuel, ignition, heat tolerance, and natural output are Species Traits or magical structures. A learned Breath Shaping Skill may own aim, pulse length, mixture control, wind judgment, safe interruption, and ally spacing.

The Skill cannot preserve fire production in a human incarnation. Crossover may preserve principles useful to bellows control, spell shaping, or chemical safety through valid current routes without creating several copies of Dragon Breath.

### The Enchanted Smithing Hammer

An enchanted hammer maintains a precise temperature. Professional Knowledge and Professional Judgment select the alloy and process; the smith's tool-control Skill guides blows; the hammer's enchantment owns temperature regulation under the magic and item rules; the workshop owns its infrastructure.

The finished blade does not prove a personal Heat Command Skill. Removing the hammer removes its effect, though the smith may retain learned timing and diagnostic experience.

### Spider Memory to Human Engineering

A reincarnated spider retains web-tension familiarity. As a human, that history accelerates understanding of load paths, vibration, and distributed support. It does not grant silk glands, automatic architecture mastery, or duplicate Web Building under a human name.

Study, tools, mathematics, materials, and varied construction may eventually establish a structural Skill or branch. The record cites one retained source and one current Development route.

### A Coordinated Ambush

A leader, scouts, archers, terrain, trust, signals, and rehearsed roles produce a successful ambush. The leader does not gain every participant's Stealth and Archery Skills. Depending on evidence, the group may have a Composite coordination capability while each member retains individual Skills and the relationships retain trust.

One surprising success remains a Tactic and situational ruling. A true group fusion would require the much stricter fusion rules, stable ownership, repeated integration, and failure evidence.

### Cleaning Up Survival

A record called Survival claims navigation, hunting, shelter building, medicine, climbing, weather prediction, monster lore, and diplomacy. These functions have different teachers, tools, evidence, and failure states, so the umbrella is split.

Shared observation and field planning may remain foundations or a Discipline. Evidence is allocated only where demonstrated; the former label becomes a historical alias. The character loses no proven ability, but unsupported claims stop travelling under one convenient name.

### Cleaning Up Perfect Evasion

A hidden passive called Perfect Evasion has been used to explain every avoided attack. It has no bounded trigger, cost, evidence, embodiment, or counter and is therefore invalid.

Documented footwork, threat recognition, species senses, protective equipment, magical wards, and opponent errors return to their actual owners. Legitimate evasion Skill remains, but no retroactive hidden or conceptual label makes it infallible.

## Proposed New Skill Checklist

Before recognizing a new Skill, answer every item:

- [ ] What exact bounded learned function does the proposed Skill own?
- [ ] Which recurring problem or purpose makes the function coherent?
- [ ] What is its origin, and which sources are only support?
- [ ] Which current body, senses, knowledge, tools, magic, relationships, authority, environment, or Soul access does it require?
- [ ] What meaningful practice, feedback, variation, correction, and integration produced it?
- [ ] What evidence distinguishes it from one action, a Tactic, Technique, Habit, or lucky result?
- [ ] Why can no existing Skill, Specialisation, branch, Discipline, or Composite Skill represent it adequately?
- [ ] Does its scope avoid both microscopic fragmentation and umbrella inflation?
- [ ] What characteristic limits, costs, dependencies, counters, and failure states does it have?
- [ ] What does the current incarnation access, embody, and perform reliably now?
- [ ] What, if anything, persists as Skill XP or a Skill Imprint without duplicating another record?
- [ ] Does it duplicate a Species Trait, Profession, magical effect, relationship, equipment benefit, Soul effect, active or passive mode, hidden status, alias, fusion, or evolved form?
- [ ] If several systems contribute, has each exact effect been assigned to one owner?
- [ ] Does the classification preserve player agency, consent, resistance, and Conscious Override?
- [ ] Has the proposal been tested against changed contexts, unavailable support, and meaningful failure?
- [ ] Is a candidate or narrower representation more honest until further evidence exists?

If any ownership, duplication, scope, or evidence answer remains unresolved, do not create a completed full Skill. Use the narrowest supported representation and revisit it when meaningful evidence changes.

## GM Resolution Procedure

1. state the exact claimed effect and observer perspective;
2. list causal contributors without treating the list as a single capability;
3. run the Ownership, Removal, Learning, Independence, Scope, Persistence, Duplication, and Agency tests;
4. choose the smallest truthful classification;
5. apply the relevant owning document for requirements, costs, opposition, and failure;
6. record Skill Development only for distinct learned change;
7. preserve current embodiment, current practice, and Practised Reliability;
8. expose uncertainty honestly and use a candidate record when evidence is incomplete;
9. clean up overlaps before adding another label;
10. check the complete Shared Skill Model without generating a universal score.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- species stages, biological Evolution routes, mutations, or hybrid forms;
- human classes, complete Professions, institutions, or certifications;
- spells, magical sources, divine powers, enchantments, or item construction;
- equipment statistics, Soul Weapon powers, relationships, or campaign records;
- a universal Skill list, slot limit, rating, level, currency, formula, or interface;
- numerical conversion or redistribution of Skill XP during cleanup.

Later systems may define their owned capabilities. They must use the Ownership Test, preserve the Shared Skill Model, and cannot duplicate an effect already represented by the Skill Engine.
