# Skill Fusion

## Purpose

This document defines how two or more established Skills can become a coherent new competency through sustained integration. Skill Fusion is deliberately rare relative to combination, coordination, and ordinary composite practice. It exists to represent genuinely new ways of perceiving, deciding, and acting, not to generate a permanent Skill for every useful pairing.

## Core Rule

A **Skill Fusion** forms when two or more Component Skills are repeatedly used as one integrated capability until their interaction develops a distinct identity, conceptual synthesis, practical coherence, and utility beyond simple simultaneous or sequential use.

Possessing the Component Skills is not enough. Fusion requires present-life practice through a compatible embodiment, stable ownership of the result, evidence across meaningful variation, and a reason the result cannot be represented truthfully as coordination, combination, Synergy, a Composite Skill, or one Hybrid Technique.

The fusion owns only the new integrated competency. Component Skills, bodies, tools, magic, allies, institutions, relationships, and Soul effects retain their own requirements, costs, agency, and failure states.

## Skill Fusion and the Shared Model

Record every fusion claim across the [Shared Skill Model](README.md#shared-skill-model):

| Dimension | Fusion question |
| --- | --- |
| **Skill Identity** | What single bounded problem does the integrated competency solve that its components do not represent separately? |
| **Origin** | Which Component Skills and current learning route produced it? |
| **Requirements** | Which component capabilities, body structures, senses, tools, resources, permissions, allies, or metaphysical conditions remain necessary? |
| **Persistent Familiarity** | What legitimate Component Skill and fusion history persists without granting current expression? |
| **Current Access** | Which components and integrated principles can the present incarnation reach and interpret? |
| **Embodied Expression** | Can the current body and available systems coordinate the components as one capability? |
| **Practised Reliability** | Has the integrated result survived timing pressure, disruption, variation, and component failure? |
| **Contextual Effectiveness** | When is fusion more useful than separate use, and when do its costs or assumptions make it worse? |
| **Evolution State** | Is the result a candidate, stable fusion, evolved fusion, translated form, or inaccessible retained history? |
| **Visibility** | Who recognises the integrated competency, its components, or only its outward result? |

No dimension becomes a fusion rating, compatibility percentage, or universal tier.

## Terms

### Skill Fusion

A bounded learned competency formed from two or more genuinely integrated Component Skills. It has a distinct purpose, method, evidence, requirements, and failure behaviour beyond merely using the components together.

### Component Skill

An established Skill whose developed capability materially contributes to a proposed or completed fusion. A Component Skill retains its own identity, history, requirements, and independent Development unless explicitly affected by current practice.

### Fusion Candidate

A recurring integrated method that may become a Skill Fusion but has not yet demonstrated enough synthesis, distinction, ownership, or Practised Reliability. It may currently be a combination, Composite Skill, or Hybrid Technique.

### Fusion Evidence

Observable support that component coordination has become one coherent capability: integrated problem recognition, shared timing, conceptual synthesis, correction across component boundaries, distinct utility, and reliable response to meaningful variation.

### Hybrid Technique

A specific execution pattern that combines methods from multiple Skills. A Hybrid Technique may remain one useful method, support a Composite Skill, or provide evidence toward fusion; it is not automatically a new Skill.

### False Fusion

A result described as fusion even though its effect is better explained by temporary circumstances, simple sequencing, External Support, one dominant Skill, an equipment effect, or an unintegrated combination.

## Distinguishing Integrated Use

| Form | What occurs | What persists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Coordination** | Existing capabilities are timed, sequenced, or assigned so they do not obstruct one another. | The contributing Skills and coordination practice; no new personal Skill is assumed. |
| **Combination** | Two or more Skills are used in the same attempt or situation. | Each Skill supplies its own effect. Proximity does not create a new capability. |
| **Synergy** | Compatible contributors produce a result none could produce as effectively alone. | The causal interaction; Synergy does not itself create a persistent Skill. |
| **Composite Skill** | A recurring capability coordinates several developed Skills or Tracks toward one purpose while its components remain clearly separable. | The coordination competency and components remain distinct. |
| **Hybrid Technique** | One bounded method interleaves operations from several Skills. | The technique may be learned without representing the whole integrated domain. |
| **Skill Fusion** | Component boundaries are crossed in perception, judgment, correction, and execution to form a distinct bounded competency. | One fusion identity plus explicitly recorded Component Skill relationships. |

These forms are not ranks. A combination may be preferable to a fusion because it is easier to teach, maintain, substitute, or adapt.

## Requirements for Fusion

A completed Skill Fusion requires all of the following in forms appropriate to the claim:

1. **Established components:** every claimed Component Skill is real, sufficiently developed for its contribution, and currently accessible enough to train the integration.
2. **Meaningful overlap or complementary function:** the components address a genuine shared problem or supply necessary different parts of one recurring function.
3. **Repeated integrated use:** practice repeatedly crosses component boundaries rather than alternating unrelated actions.
4. **Conceptual synthesis:** the practitioner understands how information, timing, risks, and correction from one component change decisions in the others.
5. **Practical coherence:** the result can be trained, diagnosed, improved, and failed as one bounded capability.
6. **Compatible embodiment:** current anatomy, senses, cognition, tools, magic, environment, and throughput can coordinate the components.
7. **Stable ownership:** the rules identify whether the fusion belongs to an individual, a consenting group, a stable collective, an institution, or another valid owner.
8. **Distinct utility:** the fusion accomplishes or regulates something materially different from routine simultaneous use.
9. **Variation and correction:** the integrated method survives changed conditions and can correct failures that occur between components.
10. **Practised Reliability:** the whole remains dependable enough under relevant pressure, fatigue, disruption, and consequence.
11. **Explicit costs and dependencies:** integration load, upkeep, component bottlenecks, external support, and failure propagation are recorded.

No numerical threshold, number of repetitions, rarity label, or dramatic event can replace these requirements.

## Component Skill Relationships

Fusion does not prescribe one outcome for its components. Record the relationship of each Component Skill to the fusion.

### Independently Usable

The component retains useful current expression outside the fusion. Separate use may be broader, cheaper, safer, or better suited to another context.

### Partly Subsumed

Some component operations become internal steps of the fusion while other uses remain distinct. Subsumption reduces conscious coordination but does not transfer unrelated capabilities into the fusion.

### Reliability Shift

Heavy fused practice can improve timing inside the fusion while independent use rusts, or preserve independent mastery while the integrated form remains fragile. Reliability is recorded separately for each relevant expression.

### Parallel Evolution

A Component Skill may continue through its own [Skill Evolution](SKILL_EVOLUTION.md) route. The fusion does not inherit every later component evolution automatically; their compatibility must be retrained and reassessed.

### Conflicting Habits

Components may encode incompatible posture, timing, attention, ethics, risk tolerance, resource use, or assumptions. Fusion training must reconcile, switch, or deliberately preserve those conflicts rather than pretending they vanish.

### Conditional Component

A component may be accessible only with a body, tool, magical source, partner, permission, or environment. The fusion remains conditional and cannot absorb that source as an unconditional personal effect.

## Fusion Costs and Bottlenecks

Fusion commonly introduces costs that separate use avoids:

- simultaneous attention or rapid switching demands;
- higher energy, mana, material, or preparation use;
- stricter timing windows;
- dependence on the weakest current Component Skill;
- cascading failure when one component supplies bad information or misses its role;
- reduced ability to substitute another tool, ally, or method;
- maintenance practice across both components and their integration;
- difficulty teaching a competency whose foundations come from different traditions;
- social, legal, cultural, or institutional resistance to an unfamiliar synthesis;
- greater consequences when an integrated decision is wrong.

Fusion is therefore not automatically more powerful or efficient. It can be too specialised, too fragile, or too expensive for the present objective.

## Fusion Failure

An attempted fusion can remain useful without becoming a Skill Fusion.

### Incompatible Timing

One component requires patience and confirmation while another requires immediate commitment, or their movement and resource cycles cannot align. The result may remain a sequence with a vulnerable transition.

### Contradictory Principles

The components diagnose or value the same situation in incompatible ways. A stealth method built on avoiding all disturbance may conflict with a suppressive magic that necessarily announces itself.

### Excessive Cognitive Load

The practitioner cannot perceive, decide, regulate, and correct both components within available attention. Protected practice may work while surprise or fatigue causes collapse.

### Unsuitable Anatomy

The current body lacks the structures, scale, sensory channels, independent control, or movement needed to integrate the components, even if each has a narrow separate analogue.

### Unstable Magic

The magical contribution changes unpredictably, disrupts the mundane component, or cannot be regulated through current Channels. Magic remains its own owner and does not stabilise because fusion would be convenient.

### Unavailable Tools or Partners

Practice depends on a particular weapon, diagnostic instrument, material, formation, teacher, or willing group. Without that dependency the fusion may be latent, translated, or impossible rather than self-sufficient.

### False Fusion

One successful situation is credited to a new Skill even though terrain, surprise, equipment, an ally, luck, or a simple tactic explains the result. The honest representation remains with those causes.

### Partial Integration

The practitioner develops a Hybrid Technique or Composite Skill but cannot diagnose and adapt the whole across variation. Genuine progress remains recorded in that smaller form; failure to fuse grants no consolation Skill.

## Reincarnation and Crossover

A completed fusion may form its own Skill Imprint while retaining provenance from its Component Skills. Persistence does not merge the component Imprints or make their requirements interchangeable.

After Reincarnation:

- each Component Skill may have different Current Access and embodiment compatibility;
- the fusion cannot be expressed if an indispensable component has no valid Receiving Route;
- a compatible subset may support a Hybrid Technique or translated fusion candidate without restoring the whole;
- retained Fusion Evidence can accelerate reintegration but cannot replace current component practice;
- a changed body may resolve one old bottleneck and create another;
- human and monster Skills remain in their own trees unless bounded [Reincarnation Skill Crossover](REINCARNATION_SKILL_CROSSOVER.md) establishes present receiving routes;
- changed magic, tools, languages, institutions, ecologies, and world laws require revalidation;
- no soul system automatically combines all prior-life Skills into new fusions.

A fusion translated into a materially different route is a new present-life expression. It preserves source history without duplicating Skill XP or benefits under every former label.

## Group and Distributed Fusion

Some fusions belong to coordinated groups rather than one person. Ritual direction, pack pursuit, surgical teams, choirs, formations, and distributed monster cognition may develop integrated competencies across roles.

For a group fusion:

- identify the owner: stable group, role structure, institution, network, or other established collective;
- preserve each participant's agency and individual Skills;
- state which roles and relationships are indispensable;
- require shared practice, signals, correction, trust or enforceable protocol, and meaningful variation;
- do not grant the whole fusion to every participant;
- reassess reliability when membership, leadership, embodiment, communication, or objectives change.

One leader does not personally own every follower's contribution, and a member leaving the group does not carry the entire collective Skill automatically.

## Worked Examples

Illustrative names below describe possible outcomes; they do not require every campaign to register the same fusion.

### Swordsmanship and Footwork

Ordinary sword attacks while stepping are combination, not fusion. A duellist who learns to treat terrain, balance transfer, line control, blade threat, and recovery as one changing geometry may develop a fusion such as **Mobile Measure**.

Swordsmanship and movement remain independently useful. The fusion is vulnerable to cramped space, an unfamiliar body, unstable ground it has never trained on, or loss of the weapon whose reach defines its judgments.

### Medicine and Magical Sensing

A healer repeatedly correlates symptoms, anatomy, mana flow, false magical signals, treatment response, and diagnostic uncertainty. **Resonant Diagnosis** may emerge when magical perception changes medical hypotheses and treatment tests as one method.

It does not make every sensed anomaly a disease or let sensing replace medical knowledge. Suppression, unfamiliar species, deceptive magic, and absent treatment resources can still defeat it.

### Tracking and Aerial Movement

A flying hunter develops **Aerial Pursuit Mapping**, integrating altitude choice, wind, sight lines, spoor interruption, prey prediction, landing options, and energy reserves. Flying while looking for tracks was only a combination; the fusion continuously changes the pursuit plan through both capabilities.

Ground tracking remains broader in dense cover, while the fusion may fail in storms, enclosed spaces, sensory glare, or a body unable to sustain its trained flight pattern.

### Web-Building and Architecture

A web-builder and trained architect integrate live tension sensing, material placement, load redirection, occupancy, maintenance, and controlled failure into **Responsive Tensionworks**. The fusion can diagnose and alter a structure while it is under changing load.

It still needs valid materials, engineering knowledge, suitable attachment points, and often specialised anatomy or tools. It does not turn every web into a building or every architectural plan into an instinctive structure.

### Diplomacy and Cultural Analysis

A mediator develops **Contextual Mediation** by integrating language, institutional incentives, custom, status signals, historical grievance, face-saving, and live negotiation. Cultural analysis changes what offers are made and how reactions are interpreted.

The fusion creates no trust, authority, compliance, or perfect cultural knowledge. It can fail through misinformation, internal faction differences, bad faith, taboo error, or objectives no acceptable bargain can reconcile.

### Ritual Magic and Group Leadership

A ritual leader develops **Ritual Direction**, integrating participant roles, magical timing, consent checks, morale, error signals, substitutions, and safe abort decisions. The fusion belongs to the leader's coordination competency or, after sustained collective practice, to an established ritual group as explicitly recorded.

It neither supplies participants' magic nor compels obedience. Missing specialists, broken trust, altered ritual laws, fatigue, or communication failure can collapse the result.

### Stealth and Sensory Suppression

A scout learns to coordinate movement, cover, timing, attention prediction, and a bounded suppression effect so that each corrects the other's traces. **Occlusion Craft** may become a fusion when the scout can diagnose which channel exposes them and adapt the whole method.

The suppression source remains magical, technological, biological, or otherwise externally owned. The fusion does not create invisibility, suppress senses the source cannot affect, or guarantee concealment from unfamiliar observers.

## GM Procedure

When adjudicating a proposed Skill Fusion:

1. state the proposed bounded integrated competency;
2. list every Component Skill and verify its real current contribution;
3. test whether coordination, combination, Synergy, Composite Skill, or Hybrid Technique is the smaller truthful representation;
4. identify the meaningful overlap or complementary function;
5. verify repeated integrated use, conceptual synthesis, practical coherence, and cross-component correction;
6. test embodiment, tools, resources, magic, partners, permissions, and stable ownership;
7. identify distinct utility beyond simultaneous use;
8. record Fusion Evidence across meaningful variation and failure;
9. map each Component Skill as independent, partly subsumed, shifted in reliability, separately evolving, conflicting, or conditional;
10. record costs, bottlenecks, cascading failures, and maintenance;
11. assess all ten Shared Skill Model dimensions without a score;
12. classify the result as combination, Synergy, Hybrid Technique, Composite Skill, Fusion Candidate, completed fusion, latent history, or failed claim.

## Safeguards

- **No combinatorial explosion:** the existence of several Skills does not imply a fusion for every pair, trio, sequence, tool, target, or environment.
- **No pairwise completion:** learning two compatible Skills establishes opportunity, not integrated Development.
- **No duplicate benefits:** one causal contribution is recorded once under its Owning System; component and fusion labels cannot each claim the same output as separate rewards.
- **No routine-combination Skill:** using familiar capabilities together remains ordinary competent action unless a distinct integrated competency is evidenced.
- **No specialisation bypass:** fusion inherits component limitations and may add narrower conditions; it cannot provide all specialisations at once or erase their costs.
- **No missing-component fiction:** an unavailable, inaccessible, untrained, or incompatible Component Skill cannot be treated as present because the fusion once existed.
- **No external-effect capture:** tools, allies, magic, institutions, species traits, environments, and Soul effects remain dependencies and owners.
- **No forced group ownership:** coordination cannot transfer participants' Skills, consent, or agency to a leader or collective.
- **No instant past-life fusion:** retained histories from different lives require compatible present access and genuine current integration.
- **No universal superiority:** separate Skills, a simpler Composite Skill, or one Hybrid Technique may be safer, cheaper, broader, or more reliable.

## Scope Boundaries

Routine [Skill Consolidation](SKILL_CONSOLIDATION_AND_SCOPE.md) is not Skill Fusion. Consolidation removes redundant representations of one established capability; Fusion requires a genuinely new integrated competency with distinct utility across Component Skill boundaries.

This document does not define:

- active and passive Skill modes;
- Hidden Skills or discovery procedures;
- Conceptual Skill effects;
- biological Evolution, mutation, or hybrid species forms;
- complete classes, professions, magic, social authority, or monster evolution;
- Soul Weapon fusion or Weapon Soul intertwining;
- universal fusion recipes or campaign-specific fused Skills.
