# Class Evolution

## Status

This document defines the canonical transformation, continuity, divergence, synthesis, decline, succession, and reconstruction of Human Classes for Phase 5. Read [Human Class Philosophy](HUMAN_CLASS_PHILOSOPHY.md), [Human Classes](CLASSES.md), and [Institutions and Academies](INSTITUTIONS_AND_ACADEMIES.md) first.

Always use **Class Evolution** in full when referring to this social process. Unqualified [Evolution](../monster-evolution/MONSTER_EVOLUTION.md) remains a bodily species transition owned by Monster Evolution.

## Core Rule

Class Evolution is a sustained historical transformation in how a Human Class organizes, transmits, interprets, practises, or recognizes a path of expertise while retaining, contesting, dividing, combining, or relinquishing some social continuity with earlier forms.

It changes a social framework. It does not grant Skills, Development, bodily form, Profession, office, authority, membership, identity, or a universal advancement tier.

A class changes because people and societies change what they actually teach, practise, preserve, dispute, recognize, and depend upon. A decree, new name, promotion, dramatic event, founder, Skill change, or institutional reorganization may contribute, but none creates Class Evolution alone.

## Ownership Boundaries

| Change | Canonical owner |
| --- | --- |
| A capability changes qualitatively | [Skill Evolution](../skills/SKILL_EVOLUTION.md) |
| A distinct capability emerges from practice | [Adaptive Skills](../skills/ADAPTIVE_SKILLS.md) |
| A body transitions into another species form or stage | [Monster Evolution](../monster-evolution/MONSTER_EVOLUTION.md) |
| A Profession's work, standards, roles, or social function changes | [Professions](PROFESSIONS.md) and world causality |
| An Institution changes governance, resources, offices, or continuity | [Institutions and Academies](INSTITUTIONS_AND_ACADEMIES.md) |
| A person's affiliation, rank, access, recognition, or office changes | [Social Advancement](SOCIAL_ADVANCEMENT.md) |
| A Human Class changes its socially transmitted framework | This document |

Several changes may occur together while retaining separate records. A new tool may produce Skill Development, alter a Profession, prompt an Academy to reorganize, and eventually reshape a Class curriculum. Class Evolution owns only the change in the class framework.

## What Class Evolution Is Not

### Not Personal Advancement

A person does not cause Class Evolution by gaining rank, mastering a Skill, changing jobs, winning a duel, graduating, receiving a title, or becoming powerful. Individuals may influence social change, but Class Evolution requires a transmissible and socially sustained result beyond one person's capability record.

### Not a Class Upgrade

Classes do not advance through universal base, advanced, elite, legendary, or prestige tiers. A later form may be more useful in one context, less capable in another, politically dominant, ethically improved, specialized, broader, fragmented, or dependent on resources its predecessor did not need.

### Not Skill Evolution or Fusion

A Class can reorganize around Skills changed through [Skill Evolution](../skills/SKILL_EVOLUTION.md) or [Skill Fusion](../skills/SKILL_FUSION.md) without granting them to members. A synthesis of Classes does not perform Skill Fusion, and one Skill Evolution does not transform every Class that teaches the prior Skill.

### Not Monster Evolution

Class Evolution cannot change anatomy, Species Potential, Species Stage, lifecycle, Mutation, Evolution Route, or progression tree. A class may translate itself for a changed body only through capabilities and practices that body can currently access.

### Not Institutional Change Alone

An Academy may merge, split, move, collapse, or change leadership while its Classes remain substantially continuous. Conversely, a Class may transform across several stable Institutions. Organizational ownership and social-framework continuity are related but distinct.

### Not a Profession Change Alone

A Profession may gain a new market, legal scope, tool, standard, or work role while the Classes that prepare practitioners remain unchanged. A Class may also transform without creating a new Profession.

## Why Classes Change

Class Evolution may arise through interacting pressures such as:

- recurring failures or unmet needs;
- new Skills, tools, technologies, materials, or communication methods;
- changed bodies, species participation, or accessibility needs;
- contact among cultures, Classes, Professions, or monster traditions;
- changes in magic, ecology, law, economy, religion, warfare, or settlement;
- institutional patronage, competition, capture, reform, or collapse;
- changed professional responsibility or public need;
- migration, diaspora, conquest, persecution, secrecy, or displacement;
- discoveries, recovered records, Archive evidence, or lost context;
- ethical conflict, abuse, accountability, or refusal;
- generational reinterpretation and changed language;
- World Resets, Age transitions, or contact with another world;
- deliberate experimentation, criticism, preservation, revival, or synthesis.

These conditions create opportunities and pressures, not automatic results. The class changes only when people develop and sustain a meaningful altered framework through current social causality.

## Evidence of Class Evolution

A Class Evolution Claim needs evidence across the dimensions it says changed. Useful evidence may include:

- a revised or newly integrated interpretive core;
- curriculum changes adopted through repeated teaching and correction;
- changed practice, responsibilities, embodiment assumptions, or failure review;
- stable transmission among more than one participant or generation;
- changed recognition, language, symbols, ranks, obligations, or boundaries;
- new relationships to Institutions, Professions, sources, communities, or other Classes;
- records of disagreement, experimentation, failure, and adaptation;
- a durable branch, synthesis, successor, or reconstruction in present use;
- changed access requirements, tools, safety rules, or teaching media;
- clear continuity or discontinuity with earlier versions.

No universal number, duration, practitioner count, generation count, event count, or percentage establishes the change. Evidence must support the particular claim at its actual social scale.

## Class Change Profile

A Class Change Profile records the smallest useful account of one claimed transformation.

| Field | What it records |
| --- | --- |
| **Claim and purpose** | What changed, why the distinction matters, and which current decision requires classification. |
| **Prior framework** | The relevant Class version, period, domain, interpretive core, curriculum, practices, and recognition before the change. |
| **Pressure and opportunity** | Problems, contacts, discoveries, conflicts, losses, resources, or choices that made change relevant. |
| **Participants and agency** | Teachers, learners, practitioners, institutions, communities, opponents, sources, and affected parties who shaped or resisted it. |
| **Process and evidence** | Teaching, experimentation, debate, practice, correction, records, transmission, conflict, and consequences supporting the claim. |
| **Changed dimensions** | Domain, interpretation, curriculum, embodiment assumptions, practice, standards, access, recognition, obligations, naming, or related structures that actually changed. |
| **Continuities** | Provenance, purpose, knowledge, practice, people, institutions, relationships, recognition, or self-understanding that still connect the forms. |
| **Discontinuities and losses** | Methods, assumptions, responsibilities, access, meanings, groups, or claims rejected, forgotten, made obsolete, or rendered inaccessible. |
| **Capability ownership** | Referenced Skills, Development, tools, bodies, sources, and Collective Capabilities, none treated as class-granted benefits. |
| **Current form and branches** | Current Class, Class Variants, schisms, synthesis, successors, reconstructed forms, or disputed classifications. |
| **Audiences and contest** | Who treats the result as continuous, separate, legitimate, heretical, derivative, stolen, obsolete, or unknown. |
| **Consequences and dependencies** | Access, obligations, institutions, resources, risks, exclusions, maintenance, and world responses that sustain or constrain the result. |

The profile is a campaign-record interface. This repository defines its fields but stores no current Class history.

## Class Continuity

A Class Continuity Claim asks whether two historical or current frameworks should be treated as forms of the same Human Class for one stated purpose.

Assess:

1. shared provenance and causal transmission;
2. continuity of domain and social purpose;
3. continuity or intelligible revision of the interpretive core;
4. teaching, practice, correction, and curriculum links;
5. shared people, communities, Institutions, records, or acknowledged predecessors;
6. mutual recognition or meaningful contest over the same identity;
7. retained obligations, responsibilities, symbols, language, or public role;
8. differences large enough that one combined Class Profile would conceal how expertise is taught, interpreted, or recognized;
9. the purpose for which continuity matters.

No one factor decides every case. A shared name can conceal a complete replacement. Different names can describe a continuous diaspora tradition. A broken transmission may support reconstruction without creating literal uninterrupted continuity.

### Audience and Purpose

Class identity is socially interpreted. Several classifications may be simultaneously real:

- practitioners treat two branches as one Class;
- their Institutions treat them as separate for credentials;
- a government collapses both into one legal category;
- outsiders use an obsolete umbrella name;
- historians recognize a common predecessor;
- one branch denies the other's legitimacy.

The GM records the actual frameworks, provenance, and beliefs rather than declaring one metaphysical taxonomy. For a specific rule, use the classification that truthfully answers that rule's purpose.

## Scale of Change

Use the smallest truthful representation.

### Ordinary Revision

Routine lesson changes, corrected terminology, one new exercise, a new teacher, or a local policy can remain part of the current Class version when a separate record would not change adjudication.

### Class Variant

Use a [Class Variant](CLASSES.md#class-variants) when differences in curriculum, interpretation, embodiment, practice, or recognition have practical consequences while shared provenance, domain, continuity, or mutual recognition still makes one Class Profile useful.

### Class Evolution

Use a Class Evolution record when the framework has undergone sustained qualitative change and understanding the historical transition matters to current teaching, practice, recognition, access, identity, or consequence.

### Separate Class

Use a separate Human Class when combining the frameworks would hide meaningful differences in domain, interpretive core, transmission, social purpose, recognition, or continuity. Separation may emerge gradually and remain contested.

### New Class Emergence

A genuinely new Class requires the validity and emergence rules in [Human Classes](CLASSES.md). Novel capability or a founder's declaration can begin a candidate framework but cannot establish social continuity retroactively.

These representations do not form stages. A Variant is not waiting to become a full Class, and a separate Class is not more advanced than an ordinary revision.

## Class Evolution Patterns

Patterns describe what changed. They are not universal routes, ranks, or mutually exclusive categories.

### Refinement

Teaching, terminology, feedback, safety, or interpretation becomes more precise while domain and identity remain strongly continuous.

### Specialization

A framework narrows around a bounded problem, body, environment, responsibility, source, method, or audience. Specialization may become a Variant or separate Class depending on continuity and social practice.

### Broadening

A Class expands its domain, curricula, bodies, contexts, or responsibilities. Breadth can improve adaptability while increasing coordination, training, and coherence costs.

### Translation and Localization

A Class is re-expressed through another language, culture, body, species, tool set, magical system, or environment. Translation preserves only what current compatibility, practice, and social understanding can sustain.

### Synthesis

Two or more class frameworks contribute to a newly integrated social framework with a distinct domain, interpretation, transmission, and recognition.

Class Synthesis does not merge all member capabilities, award Component Skills, perform Skill Fusion, or make every source community recognize the result.

### Schism

A previously connected Class divides into branches that sustain incompatible interpretations, curricula, obligations, authority claims, or social identities.

A Class Schism may leave Variants, separate Classes, competing successors, or an unresolved mixed record. Capability known before the division is not duplicated as a reward.

### Convergence

Historically separate Classes become similar through shared problems, exchange, Institutions, tools, or social pressures. Similarity does not prove common origin or require merger.

### Standardization and Diversification

An Institution, Profession, state, network, or community may consolidate several curricula into shared standards, while local practice may also diversify around bodies, regions, values, or needs. Standardization can aid portability and still erase useful differences or entrench power.

### Institutionalization and Deinstitutionalization

An informal Class may gain stable organizational support, records, credentials, and governance. A formally controlled Class may survive institutional collapse through communities, lineages, independent teachers, or concealed networks.

Institutional change affects access and continuity without becoming the Class itself.

### Professionalization and Reorientation

A Class may become closely tied to a Profession, public duty, sacred role, military structure, research question, or social movement. The new relationship does not collapse Class and Profession or grant authority.

### Suppression and Concealment

Persecution, monopoly, conquest, safety, sacred trust, secrecy, or internal choice may change transmission, naming, practice, and recognition. Concealment is social and informational; it does not make associated Skills metaphysically Hidden Skills.

### Obsolescence and Decline

A Class may lose relevance, practitioners, teaching quality, access, recognition, or valid assumptions. Decline can preserve fragments, names, institutions, and capable individuals without sustaining the full framework.

### Revival and Reconstruction

Present people may rebuild from records, descendants, surviving practitioners, Soul memories, Archive evidence, related Classes, experiments, and current need. Reconstruction is new present-life work and may produce a faithful revival, altered successor, synthesis, or mistaken reconstruction.

### Succession and Replacement

A later Class may inherit some domain, obligations, practitioners, records, institutions, or public role from an earlier framework. Class Succession is a scoped continuity claim, not automatic inheritance of every identity, capability, right, debt, or recognition.

## Change, Power, and Institutions

Institutions can influence Class Evolution by:

- funding or withholding teaching;
- controlling facilities, records, credentials, names, or access;
- standardizing curriculum;
- bringing practitioners together;
- enforcing doctrine;
- preserving minority branches;
- suppressing rivals;
- rewarding research or conformity;
- absorbing, merging, or dividing departments;
- surviving after useful practice is lost;
- collapsing while practice survives elsewhere.

Institutional power can make a classification widely enforced without making it true for every audience. A council may rename a Class overnight, but the social framework changes only where people, curriculum, interpretation, practice, or recognition actually change.

A powerful Institution cannot copyright history into metaphysical ownership, declare every outsider incapable, or make its branch the sole causal successor without evidence.

## Class Synthesis

A proposed Class Synthesis should establish:

1. contributing frameworks and their actual provenance;
2. a recurring shared problem, purpose, or domain;
3. an integrated interpretation rather than a list of names;
4. transmissible curriculum and meaningful current practice;
5. explicit embodiment, tool, source, and access assumptions;
6. evidence of teaching, correction, and social continuity;
7. relationships to contributing communities and affected parties;
8. what remains separate, incompatible, disputed, or unavailable;
9. recognition, obligations, and consequences of the new framework.

Participants retain their own Class Affiliations and capabilities unless current causality changes them. A synthesis can exist even if its parent Classes reject it, but that rejection remains a social consequence.

## Schism and Branch Ownership

During a Class Schism, establish:

- the shared prior framework;
- the disputed question, pressure, or dependency;
- the actors and Institutions involved;
- what each branch preserves, rejects, changes, or claims;
- who controls records, names, sites, credentials, and teaching relationships;
- which practitioners, communities, and Professions follow each branch;
- what capabilities remain shared independently of class identity;
- whether communication, reconciliation, synthesis, or further division remains possible.

No branch automatically receives the original Class's total legitimacy, history, capability, or property. Those claims follow actual continuity, law, relationships, evidence, and power.

## Obsolescence, Loss, and Reconstruction

### Obsolescence

A Class becomes obsolete for a stated purpose when its assumptions, methods, responsibilities, or social function no longer answer current conditions reliably. It may remain useful elsewhere, preserve historical knowledge, retain symbolic importance, or adapt later.

### Lost Class

A Class is lost when no current social continuity can sustain its interpretive framework, transmission, and meaningful practice. Surviving records, names, institutions, isolated Skills, artifacts, or memories may remain without constituting the operating Class.

### Class Reconstruction Claim

To reconstruct a Class:

1. identify surviving sources and their provenance;
2. distinguish record, memory, doctrine, inference, and invention;
3. test assumptions against current bodies, tools, cultures, magic, law, and world conditions;
4. recover or create transmissible practice through current teaching and correction;
5. involve current participants and affected communities with their own agency;
6. record omissions, substitutions, uncertainty, and inaccessible components;
7. establish current recognition and continuity claims without rewriting the historical gap.

Reconstruction cannot restore former members, capabilities, institutions, property, authority, relationships, or cultural meaning automatically. A successful reconstruction may honestly be a successor rather than the original Class resumed unchanged.

## Embodiment and Cross-Species Change

Class Evolution may respond to new bodies and species by changing:

- teaching media and sensory assumptions;
- tools, geometry, timing, communication, or scale;
- practice roles and safety procedures;
- concepts and metaphors;
- accessibility and accommodation;
- group coordination;
- which capabilities are referenced;
- recognition and professional relationships.

The change must preserve human and monster progression ownership. A human Class learning from a monster tradition does not gain Species Traits, Monster Skills, Evolution Routes, or body-specific instincts. A monster participant does not acquire a Human Skill Tree merely through class affiliation.

[Reincarnation Skill Crossover](../skills/REINCARNATION_SKILL_CROSSOVER.md) may later support bounded individual translation where its own requirements are met. Class Evolution can create a social Receiving Route; it cannot perform crossover or waive present embodiment and practice.

## Founders, Communities, and Agency

Founders, reformers, teachers, patrons, rulers, institutions, and famous practitioners may exert strong influence. No one person owns every later meaning or can declare social continuity by intention alone.

A community may:

- accept, modify, reject, misunderstand, or appropriate a proposed change;
- preserve practice while rejecting a founder's identity claim;
- recognize several founders or none;
- split over ethics, methods, authority, or responsibility;
- continue a framework after its founders leave;
- refuse a synthesis or reconstruction even when technically possible.

Class Evolution cannot force affiliation, belief, loyalty, identity, teaching, or participation. Social pressure, law, secrecy, dependency, coercion, and consequence remain real through their actual owners.

## Reincarnation and Soul Interactions

### Reincarnation

[Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) may preserve relevant Skill history, retained Development, memories, and other Soul Imprints. It does not preserve Class Affiliation, rank, institutional standing, ownership, authority, reputation, or the right to define a living framework.

A reincarnated founder can contribute evidence, critique, or methods through current action. Present practitioners remain agents, and the former version may no longer be suitable or socially continuous.

### Soul Echoes and Retained Instincts

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may provide first-person perspectives on prior versions. [Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) may orient old routines. Neither supplies complete history, current compatibility, or public ownership.

### Soul Resonance and Soul Constellations

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may reopen a meaningful place, practice, or relationship. [Soul Constellations](../soul/SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md) may reconnect recurring participants. These create evidence and opportunity rather than predestined reconstruction or loyalty.

### Soul Titles and Soul Avatars

[Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may make a bearer symbolically relevant to a Class change. [Soul Avatars](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) may synthesize perspectives from several lives. Neither can make a community accept a new framework or grant every capability represented in its history.

### Akashic Archive

[Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) Records may preserve unusually strong evidence of prior Classes and transitions. They remain bounded records requiring interpretation, current testing, and present social transmission.

## Class Evolution Claim Resolution

When Class Evolution is proposed or asserted:

1. **Name the purpose.** Establish why distinguishing revision, Class Variant, Class Evolution, synthesis, succession, reconstruction, or separate Class matters now.
2. **Identify the prior framework.** Use a Class Profile and specify version, period, culture, institutions, and audience.
3. **Name the pressure and agents.** Identify current problems, opportunities, people, communities, Institutions, and affected parties.
4. **Describe the actual process.** Trace teaching, practice, experimentation, debate, correction, transmission, conflict, and consequence.
5. **Identify changed dimensions.** State exactly what changed in domain, interpretation, curriculum, embodiment, practice, recognition, access, obligations, or relationships.
6. **Test continuity.** Record retained provenance and social continuity as well as losses and disputes.
7. **Choose the smallest truthful representation.** Use ordinary revision, Variant, Class Evolution, separate Class, synthesis, successor, or reconstruction as supported.
8. **Resolve adjacent systems separately.** Skills, Development, Professions, Institutions, Social Position, bodily Evolution, and Soul effects retain their owners.
9. **Preserve agency and audience split.** Record who accepts, rejects, contests, or ignores the classification and what they can causally enforce.
10. **Apply dependencies and consequences.** Track resources, access, exclusion, obligations, maintenance, failure, and world response externally.
11. **Reassess over time.** Class identity and continuity may change as teaching, practice, communities, Institutions, and world conditions change.

Do not adjudicate through prestige, founder intent, age, practitioner count, institutional decree, skill count, victory, popularity, or a Class Evolution meter.

## Worked Examples

### Doctrine Revised After Repeated Failure

A river-warden Class treats fixed stone barriers as the ideal response to seasonal floods. Repeated failures in a changed climate lead practitioners to integrate wetland restoration, mobile warning teams, and negotiated evacuation into their interpretive core and curriculum.

Hydrology, construction, coordination, and negotiation capabilities still belong to their Skills and Development. The sustained change in what the Class teaches and regards as responsible practice supports Class Evolution without making the new form universally superior.

### A Rename Without Class Evolution

A royal Academy renames every licensed scout an "Imperial Horizon Knight" and issues new uniforms. Teaching, field practice, obligations, embodiment assumptions, and peer recognition remain unchanged outside ceremonial law.

The decree creates a title and institutional classification. It does not establish Class Evolution or retroactively affiliate every scout with a new framework.

### Magical School After an Age Transition

A Magical School's central theory becomes unreliable after world law changes. One branch preserves the old doctrine as historical method, tests present phenomena, rewrites safety practice, and builds a new curriculum around bounded observations.

Another branch insists the old law will return. The first may be a transformed successor or Class Variant; the second may remain continuous but obsolete for current casting. Magical capability and Access still follow current Magic and Development rules.

### Ethical Schism

A healing Class divides over whether memory alteration may be used without present consent during emergencies. Both branches share anatomy, diagnosis, and treatment traditions but develop incompatible ethics, supervision, documentation, and professional relationships.

The schism may produce competing Variants or separate Classes depending on sustained transmission and recognition. Neither branch receives new healing Skills from the dispute, and institutions cannot settle moral truth merely by controlling credentials.

### Human-Monster Synthesis

Human structural engineers and a spider society's web architects repeatedly cooperate on suspended settlements. They develop shared concepts, translated teaching media, joint safety roles, and a curriculum that neither prior framework contained.

The synthesis can become a new Class when social continuity and meaningful practice are established. Humans do not gain spinneret control, spiders do not gain human hand Skills, and shared structures remain Collective Capabilities with actual component ownership.

### Institution Changes, Class Does Not

Three Academies merge their administration, property, and credentials while allowing each resident Martial Tradition to keep its doctrine, teachers, curricula, and recognition.

The Institution changed organizationally; the Classes did not necessarily undergo Class Evolution. Shared facilities may create later opportunities, but merger paperwork is insufficient.

### Reincarnated Founder and Reconstruction

A former founder returns centuries after the Class became lost. Soul Echoes remember early doctrine, but records reveal that later practitioners corrected several dangerous assumptions before the collapse.

The founder can help reconstruct, oppose, or learn from the evidence. Present bodies, practice, criticism, and community choice determine the result. The founder's soul history does not restore authority or make the earliest version the authentic endpoint.

### Class Survives Professional Obsolescence

A navigators' Profession disappears after reliable portal travel replaces ocean shipping. Its Class survives among historians, explorers of unstable portal margins, and communities preserving celestial observation.

The social function and curricula may reorient without instantly creating a new Profession. Whether this is broadening, succession, or a separate Class depends on actual continuity and present practice.

### Suppressed Tradition Reappears

A persecuted Martial Tradition survives through family games, songs, tool-maintenance habits, and disguised festival roles. Generations later, descendants reconnect the fragments with surviving teachers and public practice.

The gap, adaptations, and uncertainty remain part of the Class Change Profile. Reappearance may be continuity through concealment, reconstruction, or both in different dimensions; a heroic origin story does not decide the claim alone.

## Safeguards

### No Class Evolution Ladder

Class Evolution Patterns are descriptions of change, not ranks. Refined, specialized, synthesized, ancient, successor, and reconstructed Classes are not inherently stronger or more advanced.

### No Class Evolution Rewards

Participating in reform, schism, synthesis, discovery, persecution, reconstruction, or an Age transition does not automatically grant Skills, Development, titles, authority, or progression.

### No Renaming Exploit

New names, uniforms, symbols, ranks, charters, departments, editions, founders, or institutional decrees do not create Class Evolution without changed social framework and evidence.

### No Class Inflation

Use ordinary revision or a Class Variant where one record remains truthful. Create a separate Class only when combining frameworks would conceal meaningful differences in domain, interpretation, transmission, purpose, recognition, or continuity.

### No Automatic Skill Evolution or Fusion

Curriculum change, Class Synthesis, schism, or reconstruction cannot award Adaptive Skills, perform Skill Evolution or Skill Fusion, duplicate components, or waive current practice.

### No Bodily Evolution

Class Evolution cannot provide anatomy, Species Traits, Monster Skills, Evolution Routes, Mutations, stages, or crossover. Embodiment translation remains present-life work.

### No Institutional Fiat

Law, monopoly, ownership, funding, credentials, or force can shape recognition and practice but cannot make doctrine true, create capability, or establish social continuity by declaration alone.

### No Founder Ownership

Founders and reincarnated predecessors may contribute history and influence. They do not own every successor meaning, present community, class name, institution, member, or future choice.

### No Retroactive Affiliation

A later Class cannot automatically classify every historical contributor, source culture, Skill user, ancestor, or related Profession as a former member.

### No Perfect Reconstruction

Archive evidence, Soul memories, records, relics, and surviving Skills do not restore missing context, bodies, institutions, relationships, consent, or practice automatically. Reconstruction records its present work and uncertainty.

### No Mandatory Change

Pressure, obsolescence, contact, magical change, institutional collapse, and World Resets create consequences and choices. They do not force one optimal Class route or erase communities that sustain a different viable framework.

### No Campaign Data

Class Change Profiles are external campaign records. This repository stores no current branches, founders, schisms, institutions, or Class history.

## Deferred Ownership

This document does not fully define:

- the final limits defined in [Limits of Human Progression](LIMITS_OF_HUMAN_PROGRESSION.md);
- transformations of Professions, cultures, religions, governments, economies, or Institutions except where they affect a Class claim;
- detailed magic, technology, legal, political, or World Engine procedures;
- the creation of particular Skills, bodies, species routes, credentials, or offices;
- a universal historical taxonomy or formula for deciding Class identity;
- live Class histories, branch rosters, disputes, or current recognition.

Use the [Provisional Rulings](../gm/PROVISIONAL_RULINGS.md) for narrow missing procedures while preserving these ownership boundaries.
