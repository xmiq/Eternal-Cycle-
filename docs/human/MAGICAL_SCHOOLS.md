# Magical Schools

## Status

This document defines the canonical social structure and adjudication of Magical Schools for Phase 5. Read [Human Class Philosophy](HUMAN_CLASS_PHILOSOPHY.md), [Human Classes](CLASSES.md), and [Magical Development](../progression/MAGICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md) first.

This document does not define the complete magic systems of Eternal Cycle. Phase 7 owns mana, affinities, spell formation, rituals, enchanting, alchemy, divine magic, forbidden magic, and detailed magical laws. This document defines how societies organize, teach, test, preserve, dispute, and recognize magical practice within whatever local magical systems actually exist.

## Core Rule

A Magical School is a socially sustained Human Class framework that organizes models, curriculum, practice, verification, safety, interpretation, and recognition around one or more bounded magical systems or questions.

Its central question is:

> How does this community understand and transmit reliable participation in these magical processes under these sources, bodies, permissions, tools, environments, and world laws?

A school can be coherent while its theories are incomplete or partly wrong. Social continuity makes it a school; world truth and demonstrated results determine whether its magical claims are accurate.

## Magical School, Magic System, and Academy

These terms must remain distinct.

| Fact | Meaning | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Magic system** | The actual sources, laws, access routes, costs, limits, interactions, and failure states through which magic functions. | Phase 7 and established world canon |
| **Magical School** | A class framework through which a society models, teaches, tests, interprets, and recognizes some magical practice. | This document |
| **Magical Academy** | An institution that organizes people, facilities, records, resources, admission, and governance around learning. | [Institutions and Academies](INSTITUTIONS_AND_ACADEMIES.md) |
| **Magical Skill** | A learned capability used to perceive, control, construct, perform, or respond to magic. | [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) |
| **Magical Development** | Change in bounded magical perception, access, regulation, capacity, reliability, and judgment. | [Magical Development](../progression/MAGICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md) |
| **Magical Profession** | Integrated magical work, standards, service relationships, responsibility, and accountability. | [Professions](PROFESSIONS.md) and [Profession Development](../progression/PROFESSION_DEVELOPMENT.md) |
| **Magical Authority** | Permission from a source, law, deity, spirit, institution, territory, or contract to invoke a defined effect. | The actual source and [Magical Development](../progression/MAGICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md) |

An academy may host several Magical Schools. One Magical School may span several academies, temples, lineages, correspondence circles, monster communities, and independent teachers. A school can survive without a building, and a building can survive after its school has lost useful knowledge.

## What a Magical School Owns

A Magical School may own:

- a Magical Epistemology;
- a Class Curriculum;
- a shared vocabulary and notation;
- teaching sequences and exercises;
- methods of observation, comparison, verification, and correction;
- safety and failure-containment practices;
- local interpretation of responsibility, consent, and acceptable use;
- peer relationships, lineages, recognition, and teaching permissions;
- records of successful, failed, disputed, and obsolete practice;
- characteristic ways of relating to sources, tools, spirits, environments, or authorities;
- known blind spots and competing theories.

It cannot make its account metaphysically true by decree. It does not grant Magical Access, organs, Channels, reserves, affinity, authority, spells, Skills, contracts, divine favor, or immunity.

## What a Magical School Is Not

### Not a Magic System

A school studies and teaches within magic's actual laws. Its doctrine cannot create a universal element chart, mana source, spell grammar, or conservation rule unless the owning world system establishes that fact.

### Not a Spell List

A school may preserve examples, formulas, rituals, and construction methods. Admission does not place those effects into a learner, and a curriculum is not a menu of automatically unlocked spells.

### Not an Affinity

School affiliation cannot create elemental, conceptual, divine, species, environmental, or source compatibility. A school may help discover, interpret, train, or work around an existing affinity under valid rules.

### Not Magical Access

Theory, memory, status, and desire do not create a current route to magic. A student may understand a system deeply while lacking a Channel, source, permission, tool, compatible environment, or other valid Access.

### Not an Academy or Institution

A school is a transmissible intellectual and practical framework. An academy is an organization. Institutional membership, employment, property, legal control, and governance are separate facts.

### Not a Profession

Study and magical practice need not be work. A magical Profession adds recurring service, responsibility, standards, affected parties, and accountability.

### Not a Caster Rank

Degrees, circles, robes, seals, examinations, offices, titles, and school reputation do not establish one universal measure of magical capability or threat.

## Magical School Validity Test

A proposed Magical School should answer:

1. **Magical subject:** Which bounded system, source, process, relationship, problem, or question does it study?
2. **World-law assumptions:** Which actual or believed laws, environments, costs, and failure conditions does it assume?
3. **Access assumptions:** Which bodies, organs, Channels, permissions, contracts, tools, sites, species, or group roles make participation possible?
4. **Epistemology:** How does the school distinguish observation, theory, authority, tradition, revelation, error, and uncertainty?
5. **Transmission:** How can another person learn its concepts, methods, safety, and judgment?
6. **Practice and feedback:** What meaningful present activity tests understanding and develops capability?
7. **Safety and responsibility:** How does it recognize, contain, disclose, recover from, and review failure?
8. **Social continuity:** Who preserves, recognizes, disputes, reforms, suppresses, or rejects the framework?

If the proposal is only one spell, element name, magical organ, bloodline, artifact, patron, Profession, academy, caster, title, or research question, it is not yet a distinct Magical School.

## Magical School Profile

A Magical School Profile extends the [Class Profile](CLASSES.md#class-profile) with magic-specific fields.

### Required Fields

| Field | What it records |
| --- | --- |
| **Name and scope** | Culture, lineage, region, institution, period, translations, disputed names, and who uses the classification. |
| **Magical subject** | The bounded sources, processes, relationships, practices, or questions organized by the school. |
| **Provenance** | How the framework formed and which practitioners, species, sources, events, or predecessor traditions shaped it. |
| **Magical Epistemology** | How claims are observed, interpreted, tested, authorized, recorded, challenged, and revised. |
| **World-law model** | The school's current account of relevant sources, laws, costs, limits, interactions, and failure; beliefs are distinguished from verified truth. |
| **Access assumptions** | Required Channels, organs, affinities, authority, contracts, environments, tools, materials, sites, roles, or group structures. |
| **Embodiment assumptions** | Bodies, senses, cognition, lifespan, recovery, species structures, and accommodations presupposed by teaching. |
| **Curriculum and practice** | Concepts, perception work, control exercises, supervised application, construction, ritual roles, maintenance, review, and progression of responsibility. |
| **Capability references** | Magical Development and Skill dependencies; never granted effects or bonuses. |
| **Verification methods** | Evidence, controls, comparison, replication, witnesses, source testimony, instruments, records, and uncertainty standards. |
| **Safety and containment** | Preparation, limits, supervision, consent, protective systems, abort conditions, recovery, reporting, and emergency response. |
| **Authority and source relations** | Permissions, contracts, divine or spirit relations, territorial rules, institutional claims, and revocation conditions. |
| **Ethics and affected parties** | Responsibilities toward subjects, participants, sources, communities, ecologies, bystanders, and future users. |
| **Tools and infrastructure** | Focuses, laboratories, archives, ritual sites, wards, supply, maintenance, assistants, and external dependencies. |
| **Recognition and evidence** | Audiences, examinations, demonstrations, teaching permissions, credentials, ranks, and what each actually claims. |
| **Interoperability and counters** | Known relationships with other systems, schools, sources, suppressions, resistances, and incompatible assumptions. |
| **Variants and current condition** | Branches, schisms, lost methods, changed laws, suppression, reconstruction, decline, or expansion. |

A profile can record a school's beliefs without declaring them world truth. When the truth is unknown, the GM must preserve the distinction among evidence, inference, doctrine, revelation, propaganda, and hidden fact.

## Magical Epistemology

Magical Epistemology is a school's method for deciding what it believes about magic and why.

Possible sources of knowledge include:

- direct magical perception;
- repeatable practical results;
- controlled comparison;
- historical records;
- mathematical or symbolic models;
- embodied tradition;
- source testimony;
- divine revelation;
- spirit negotiation;
- artifact behavior;
- species memory;
- Akashic Records;
- Soul Echo testimony;
- institutional authority;
- oral lineage;
- failure analysis;
- inference from environmental change.

Each source has limits. Repeatable results may be misinterpreted. Revelation may be scoped, symbolic, deceptive, or translated poorly. A spirit may know its territory but not universal law. An Archive Record preserves evidence without making the reader's interpretation correct. An old master may remember laws from another Age.

### Doctrine and World Truth

A school's doctrine can be:

- substantially accurate;
- useful but theoretically mistaken;
- accurate only in one environment;
- dependent on a hidden source;
- preserved after its laws changed;
- distorted by secrecy or politics;
- internally disputed;
- deliberately false in public and more accurate in private;
- unable to explain why an empirical method works.

The GM should let evidence and world causality expose these differences fairly. School prestige is not proof, and outsider status is not disproof.

## Curricula and Learning Roles

A Magical School may teach people with different access and responsibilities.

Possible learning roles include:

- source-sensitive observer;
- noncasting theorist;
- instrument operator;
- material preparer;
- ward or site maintainer;
- ritual participant;
- translator or negotiator;
- direct caster;
- safety monitor;
- recorder;
- examiner;
- researcher;
- teacher;
- professional practitioner.

These are local roles, not a universal ladder. A noncaster may possess exceptional theoretical or professional judgment. A powerful caster may lack the Skill to design, explain, verify, or safely maintain an effect.

### Teaching Sequence

A sound curriculum may provide:

1. vocabulary, provenance, and uncertainty;
2. source and Access identification;
3. safety, consent, and abort procedures;
4. perception and signal discrimination;
5. isolated control under recoverable conditions;
6. recordkeeping and error recognition;
7. supervised application;
8. variation and interference;
9. construction, adaptation, or role coordination;
10. independent judgment within a bounded scope;
11. collaboration and referral;
12. teaching or research where capability supports it.

This is a useful pattern, not a mandatory tier sequence. Different magical systems may require another order, and some learners can participate only in particular roles.

## Access, Affinity, Authority, and Technique

These facts must remain separate.

- **Magical Potential** describes possible developmental routes.
- **Magical Access** is a valid present route to participate in a system or source.
- **Magical Affinity** is a specific compatibility relationship.
- **Magical Authority** is scoped permission.
- **Magical Reserve and Capacity** concern available resources and safe throughput within one system.
- **Magical Skill** is a learned capability.
- **school affiliation** is a social and educational relationship.

A school can help a learner identify or use an existing route. It cannot award these facts as one package.

### Students Without Direct Access

A learner without direct casting Access may still develop valid knowledge, observation, tool use, safety, coordination, maintenance, negotiation, or professional capability. The profile must state which contribution is actually being learned.

Being unable to cast does not make the person unintelligent or useless. It also does not secretly grant casting through scholarship alone.

### Access Supplied by the School

An institution associated with a school may supply a ritual site, contract, focus, licensed source, artifact, spirit relationship, shared reserve, or supervised group role. That support remains External Empowerment or Institutional Access.

Loss of membership, location, maintenance, permission, consent, or source availability can remove it without erasing legitimately learned capability.

## Verification, Experimentation, and Research

Magical claims require methods proportionate to their risk and nature.

Verification may involve:

- repeated observation under stated conditions;
- independent practitioners;
- control samples or inactive forms;
- known instruments;
- records of setup, source, environment, participants, and outcome;
- comparison across species or bodies;
- adversarial review;
- source consent and testimony;
- prediction of failure signs;
- replication in another site;
- bounded attempts to disprove the claim;
- long-term ecological, bodily, Soul, or social observation.

Not every magical fact is repeatable on demand. Rare celestial events, divine choices, singular artifacts, person-like sources, and costly rituals may require historical inference rather than laboratory replication. Limited repeatability should increase uncertainty, not force the GM either to deny the event or accept every explanation.

### Magical Research

Research develops when practitioners form questions, gather evidence, test alternatives, record uncertainty, revise models, and accept consequences. Consuming materials, causing spectacle, risking corruption, or repeating an unchanged spell does not create insight automatically.

Research subjects with agency require consent and ethical treatment. Spirits, gods, monsters, Soul Weapons, familiars, bound entities, and sentient magical constructs are not inert components merely because a school labels them resources.

## Safety, Containment, and Recovery

A school's safety practice should identify:

- source and Access checks;
- current environmental compatibility;
- body, Channel, organ, and condition limits;
- safe reserve, capacity, and recovery assumptions where relevant;
- tool inspection and maintenance;
- participant roles and communication;
- consent and authority;
- containment boundaries;
- interruption and abort procedures;
- failure signs;
- evacuation and medical response;
- magical suppression or contamination response;
- records and reporting;
- post-use observation and recovery;
- review before repetition.

Safety does not make magic risk-free. It makes risk legible, proportionate, reviewable, and less dependent on preventable harm.

### Failure

Failure may result from incorrect theory, missing Access, changed laws, revoked authority, unsuitable embodiment, tool damage, environmental interference, insufficient Skill, poor coordination, deception, overload, inadequate recovery, or an honestly uncertain event.

The GM should identify the causal owner. Failure does not automatically create Magical Development, affinity, resistance, corruption powers, revelation, a better spell, or permission to try again without consequence.

## School Taxonomies

Societies may organize Magical Schools around:

- a source or patron;
- an element or material;
- a method of shaping;
- a family of effects;
- a profession or public responsibility;
- a region or environment;
- a species structure;
- a ritual lineage;
- a philosophical model;
- a tool or notation;
- a divine or spirit relationship;
- a research question;
- a historical founder or institution.

These are social taxonomies, not proof that magic itself is divided that way. Two schools may classify the same process differently. One school may place several actual systems under one misleading name. Another may split one system into competing disciplines.

No taxonomy is universal merely because it is elegant, ancient, dominant, or supported by an empire.

## School Equivalence and Interoperability

A Magical Interoperability Claim asserts that practices, roles, records, tools, or effects from two magical frameworks can function together for one stated purpose.

Assess:

1. actual sources and laws;
2. Access and authority;
3. body, Channel, and species compatibility;
4. terminology and model translation;
5. reserve, capacity, timing, and recovery assumptions;
6. tools, materials, environment, and setup;
7. target and effect ownership;
8. safety and abort procedures;
9. practitioner Skill and reliability;
10. known interference, resistance, and failure;
11. consent among person-like sources and participants;
12. the exact purpose for which compatibility is claimed.

Shared names do not establish interoperability. Different names do not prevent it. Compatibility for observation does not imply compatibility for casting, combination, cancellation, teaching credit, professional licence, or safe tool exchange.

### Equivalence of Study

A Class Equivalence Claim may recognize prior study for admission, examination, teaching, or professional eligibility. That social decision remains separate from magical interoperability and present capability.

## Recognition, Ranks, and Secrecy

A school may use circles, degrees, colours, robes, seals, names, ordeals, examinations, publications, licences, or teaching permissions. Every label must state:

- who recognizes it;
- what evidence it claims;
- which role, practice, or curriculum it covers;
- what access, obligation, or authority follows;
- where it is valid;
- how it can be challenged, revoked, or become obsolete.

None is a universal caster level or spell tier.

### Secrecy

Secrecy may protect dangerous procedures, person-like sources, communities, strategic knowledge, sacred trust, or institutional power. It may also conceal error, abuse, monopoly, exploitation, or fraud.

Secrecy creates information and access consequences. It does not make a Skill Hidden by itself, make independent discovery impossible, or turn forbidden knowledge into automatic power.

The GM should preserve discoverable evidence and fair uncertainty. "The school keeps it secret" is not an explanation of how the magic works.

## School Relationships With Professions and Institutions

A Magical School can prepare learners for several Professions. A magical Profession can recruit from several schools, monster traditions, apprenticeships, or independent paths.

For each relationship, identify:

- relevant parts of the curriculum;
- missing professional responsibilities;
- practical cases and present reliability required;
- credentials or licences actually recognized;
- current tools, teams, law, and affected parties;
- whether magical authority comes from the school, another institution, or an external source;
- accountability when doctrine and professional standards disagree.

An academy can provide facilities, peers, records, tools, sources, and review. Those are causal advantages, not proof that every graduate is capable or every outsider unsafe.

Detailed academy governance is defined in [Institutions and Academies](INSTITUTIONS_AND_ACADEMIES.md).

## Cross-Species and Monster Schools

Monster societies may sustain magical schools through nonhuman senses, organs, lifecycles, environments, collective bodies, song, scent, web geometry, territory, shared dream, metamorphosis, or source relationships.

Do not translate these into human spell lists by default.

Cross-species learning may require:

- sensory translation;
- alternate notation;
- tools that expose otherwise inaccessible signals;
- new safety limits;
- different recovery schedules;
- group roles that separate perception, authority, energy, and shaping;
- recognition of body-bound methods that cannot transfer;
- consent and cultural negotiation;
- a valid receiving Skill route.

A human may understand a monster school's theory without gaining its organ-based Access. A monster may use a human tool without receiving human affinity or every associated Technique.

## Reincarnation and Soul Interactions

### Reincarnation

[Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) may preserve Magical Imprints, Skill history, conceptual familiarity, judgment, and memories. It does not preserve former Channels, organs, reserves, affinity, authority, contracts, tools, environment, academy membership, school rank, licence, source relationships, or current-world validity.

The current incarnation must establish:

- a present magical route;
- compatibility with current laws and environment;
- embodied control and recovery;
- current practice and reliability;
- updated theory and safety;
- consent or permission from present sources;
- current social affiliation and recognition.

### Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may align a soul with a genuine former source, school, site, method, or relationship within its Basis and Target. It does not create Access, affinity, authority, a spell, or objective proof of doctrine.

### Soul Echoes

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may preserve old theory, case memory, warnings, and disagreements among prior schools. Their knowledge may be outdated, culturally narrow, or based on changed laws and requires present verification.

### Retained Instincts

[Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) may orient magical attention toward familiar cues. They do not cast, supply complete notation, distinguish every source, or bypass current control.

### Soul Titles

[Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may influence symbolic access, fear, expectation, or source response where an owning rule recognizes the title. They do not award school rank, affinity, authority, or magical capability.

### Soul Avatars

[Soul Avatars](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) may synthesize competing magical perspectives across lives. They do not resolve doctrine automatically, cast additional actions, or supply absent channels and sources.

### Akashic Archive

[Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) Records may preserve procedures, observations, and failures. Reading them supplies evidence, not current Access, Skill, authority, or infallible interpretation.

## Skill Interactions

### Human Skill Trees

[Human Skill Trees](../skills/HUMAN_SKILL_TREES.md) map learned perception, control, notation, ritual, recovery, construction, and other capability routes a school may teach. The school does not own those capabilities.

### Adaptive Skills

[Adaptive Skills](../skills/ADAPTIVE_SKILLS.md) may emerge when practitioners repeatedly solve a genuine magical problem through meaningful variation, feedback, adjustment, and integration. A new spell name, school decree, rare ingredient, or spectacular accident is insufficient.

### Skill Evolution and Fusion

[Skill Evolution](../skills/SKILL_EVOLUTION.md) and [Skill Fusion](../skills/SKILL_FUSION.md) require their own capability evidence. Graduation, rank, initiation, source contact, or combining school names cannot trigger them automatically.

### Hidden Skills

[Hidden Skills](../skills/HIDDEN_SKILLS.md) are observer-relative capability records. Institutional secrecy, private notation, or suppressed doctrine can hide evidence without making every associated Skill metaphysically hidden.

### Conceptual Skills

[Conceptual Skills](../skills/CONCEPTUAL_SKILLS.md) require bounded access, interpretation, Skill history, and counterplay. A school may teach conceptual models, but prestige, symbolism, and metaphysical vocabulary cannot create conceptual force.

## Magical School Claim Resolution

When a Magical School is invoked in play:

1. **Name the claim.** Is it about affiliation, theory, Access, Skill, affinity, authority, rank, profession, institution, source, or outcome?
2. **Identify the school version.** Establish culture, lineage, institution, period, branch, and relevant audience.
3. **Identify the magical system.** Name the actual or believed source, laws, environment, costs, and failure conditions.
4. **Separate belief from truth.** Record doctrine, evidence, inference, uncertainty, revelation, misinformation, and hidden world facts independently.
5. **Check present Access and embodiment.** Apply Channels, organs, tools, sites, contracts, permissions, species, environment, and condition.
6. **Check actual capability.** Resolve Development, Skills, preparation, reliability, recovery, and judgment through their owners.
7. **Check safety and affected parties.** Include consent, containment, authority, abort conditions, ecological effects, and professional responsibility.
8. **Check interoperability.** Test actual sources and dependencies rather than matching labels.
9. **Apply scoped recognition.** Resolve rank, credential, licence, office, reputation, and institutional access separately.
10. **Resolve outcome and consequence.** Preserve resources, strain, failure, source reaction, evidence, social response, and uncertainty.

Do not resolve the claim by school rank, spell count, robe, title, output, affinity count, or institutional prestige alone.

## Worked Examples

### Two Schools, One Visible Flame

One school produces flame by shaping an ambient thermal field. Another negotiates with hearth spirits for a bounded manifestation. Observers call both "fire magic."

Their visible results overlap, but their Access, authority, costs, failure, environment, consent, and cancellation methods differ. A shared name does not make their tools or spells interoperable.

### The Noncasting Theorist

A scholar lacks a Magical Channel but has exceptional Skill in comparing ward failures, maintaining instruments, and identifying flawed boundary models. The scholar cannot cast the wards and should not be represented as secretly having casting Access.

The school's curriculum and institution can still give the scholar a meaningful research and safety role. A powerful caster may depend on that judgment without transferring power in either direction.

### Reborn Archmage, Changed Laws

A former archmage reincarnates in an Age where the old geometric source no longer responds and local magic uses sung contracts with weather spirits.

Retained theory helps distinguish signals, ask better questions, and recognize unsafe assumptions. It does not recreate old Channels or authority. Present breath training, language, negotiation, source consent, and reliability are required, and the result may be a new school synthesis rather than recovered mastery.

### Rune-Web Translation

A spider monster school records magic through tension patterns in living silk. A human academy wants to teach the method.

Humans can study topology and use instrumented frames, but they lack spinneret perception and the silk's biological regulation. Translation creates a Class Variant with different tools, safety, and possible effects; it does not give humans the monster organ or guarantee full equivalence.

### Divine Authority Without Technique

A temple novice receives authority from a river god to perform one cleansing rite. The novice has little control and requires supervision.

The authority is real and scoped. It does not prove broad affinity, professional judgment, school mastery, or permission to improvise. An unordained expert may understand the ritual better while lacking the god's present consent.

### The Prestigious Obsolete School

An imperial school still teaches that moonlight stabilizes teleportation. A World Reset altered the relevant celestial law, but political prestige suppresses contradictory records.

Graduates can be sincere, highly trained, and dangerously wrong in this application. Investigation must distinguish obsolete doctrine, actual Skill, institutional pressure, and the current magical system.

### Small Caster, Large Collaborative Work

A student with limited personal reserve coordinates a ritual through prepared materials, a licensed site, several specialists, and a cooperative spirit.

The result belongs to the arrangement. The student's learning may lie in notation, timing, communication, control, and abort judgment. It does not imply a hidden increase in personal reserve or solo casting power.

## Safeguards

### No School-to-Power Conversion

Admission, study, graduation, rank, inheritance, reincarnation, credential, or reputation never grants Magical Access, affinity, authority, reserves, Channels, organs, spells, Skills, resistance, or output.

### No Universal Mana or Spell Tier

Schools may use local measures and curriculum labels only when their meaning and system are stated. They cannot establish one resource, level, circle, rarity, threat score, or spell hierarchy for all magic.

### No Spell-List Bundles

A school can preserve and teach procedures. Every current effect still requires its own Access, capability, preparation, cost, source, authority, and conditions.

### No Theory Bypass

Correct knowledge does not create embodiment, tools, permission, source, reserve, or Practised Reliability. Incorrect theory can coexist with useful empirical Skill.

### No Affinity Collection

Studying several schools does not stack their named elements or concepts as unrestricted affinities or bonuses.

### No Source Ownership by Institution

A school or academy may control access to a site, record, contract, or tool through real power. It cannot erase the agency of gods, spirits, Soul Weapons, monsters, constructs, or other person-like sources by declaring them property.

### No Experimentation Through Abuse

Exposure, overload, corruption, possession, sacrifice, Channel damage, subject harm, and environmental destruction do not create insight or Development merely because consequences are severe.

### No School Monopoly on Discovery

Political ownership of a name or curriculum does not make independent observation, parallel discovery, monster knowledge, or competing models impossible. Secrecy and suppression remain causal world facts.

### No Rank-as-Capability

School office, degree, robe, circle, title, publication, lineage, and fame are scoped recognition. Evaluate current capability and authority separately.

### No Reincarnated Access or Standing

Retained history may accelerate interpretation and relearning. It does not restore Channels, reserves, authority, contracts, source relationships, tools, school membership, rank, licence, or current-world validity.

### Preserve Mystery Fairly

Hidden magical truth must leave source-appropriate evidence and remain causally consistent. The GM should not change a school's correctness after the fact merely to defeat player investigation.

## Deferred Ownership

This document does not define:

- universal or local mana mechanics;
- complete affinities or elements;
- spell formation and grammar;
- ritual mechanics;
- enchanting or alchemy;
- divine or forbidden magic;
- exact magical costs, ranges, durations, damage, or counters;
- academy governance, funding, admission, property, and discipline defined in [Institutions and Academies](INSTITUTIONS_AND_ACADEMIES.md);
- final limits defined in [Limits of Human Progression](LIMITS_OF_HUMAN_PROGRESSION.md).

[Institutions and Academies](INSTITUTIONS_AND_ACADEMIES.md), [Limits of Human Progression](LIMITS_OF_HUMAN_PROGRESSION.md), and the later Magic phase own those subjects. Any provisional magical ruling must follow the [Alpha Playtest Rules](../gm/ALPHA_PLAYTEST_RULES.md).
