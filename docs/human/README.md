# Human Classes and Professions

Human Classes and Professions describe how societies organize, teach, preserve, apply, change, and recognize expertise. They do not create a universal character level, mandatory class progression, automatic Skill package, Profession XP bar, or metaphysical class lock.

Read the [Development System](../progression/README.md) and [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) before using these rules. Development owns changes in capability and reliability. Skills own bounded learned capabilities. This section owns the social frameworks, work structures, relationships, organizations, and constraints built around them.

## Canonical Reading Order

1. [Human Class Philosophy](HUMAN_CLASS_PHILOSOPHY.md) establishes the meaning, ownership boundaries, cultural variation, embodiment rules, and safeguards shared by every Human Class claim.
2. [Human Classes](CLASSES.md) defines Class Profiles, curricula, affiliations, local ranks, variants, equivalence, access, and class-claim adjudication.
3. [Professions](PROFESSIONS.md) defines social fields of work, roles, service relationships, standards, jurisdiction, accountability, formation, and profession-claim adjudication.
4. [Martial Traditions](MARTIAL_TRADITIONS.md) defines doctrine, Training Ecology, Pressure Practice, use of force, equipment relationships, matchups, counterplay, and martial-tradition adjudication.
5. [Magical Schools](MAGICAL_SCHOOLS.md) defines social models of magic, epistemology, curricula, Access boundaries, verification, safety, interoperability, and magical-school adjudication without defining the Magic system.
6. [Social Advancement](SOCIAL_ADVANCEMENT.md) defines multidimensional Social Position, Advancement Routes, opportunities, portability, gatekeeping, decline, agency, and social-claim adjudication.
7. [Institutions and Academies](INSTITUTIONS_AND_ACADEMIES.md) defines durable organization, governance, offices, authority, collective capability, institutional memory, academies, continuity, and institutional adjudication.
8. [Class Evolution](CLASS_EVOLUTION.md) defines historical transformation, continuity, variants, synthesis, schism, obsolescence, reconstruction, succession, and adjudication of class frameworks.
9. [Limits of Human Progression](LIMITS_OF_HUMAN_PROGRESSION.md) defines scoped constraints, embodiment, time, Practice Allocation, maintenance, access, tradeoffs, retained progression, crossover, assessment, and phase-wide safeguards.

The documents are cumulative. A specialist rule adds playable detail without turning a class label, Profession, rank, credential, office, institutional membership, or reputation into automatic capability.

## Human Claim Resolution

Use this sequence whenever a claim combines capability with a class, Profession, rank, school, tradition, office, credential, or Institution. Open only the specialist documents relevant to the claim.

1. **State the exact claim and purpose.** Name the person, group, framework, action, audience, objective, and consequence rather than asking whether someone simply "has a class."
2. **Classify each fact.** Separate capability, Development, class framework, Profession, Martial Tradition, Magical School, Social Position, Institution, historical class change, and current constraint.
3. **Identify the owner.** Assign every claimed effect to one canonical system. Supporting facts may change access, learning, reliability, recognition, opportunity, or consequence without duplicating the owned effect.
4. **Establish provenance and current form.** Identify the relevant culture, lineage, community, Institution, Profession, version, audience, and history. A familiar name or old record does not prove present equivalence or continuity.
5. **Test current capability.** Use the six capability layers, present embodiment, current access, practice, tools, relationships, support, and evidence. A social label never substitutes for this check.
6. **Establish current relationships.** Record affiliation, employment, membership, office, credential, rank, reputation, jurisdiction, rights, duties, and recognition separately and only where their current sources support them.
7. **Apply the specialist rule.** Use the relevant Class Profile, Profession Profile, Martial Tradition Profile, Magical School Profile, Social Position Profile, Institution Profile, Class Change Profile, or Progression Constraint Profile.
8. **Resolve access, agency, and cost.** Identify teaching, permission, exclusion, obligations, consent, maintenance, risk, tradeoffs, opposition, and valid alternatives. Social pressure creates consequences but does not choose for the character.
9. **Assess context and uncertainty.** State the actual objective, environment, preparation, opposition, information, and observer perspective. Preserve the difference between world truth, evidence, belief, recognition, and uncertainty.
10. **Propagate consequences once.** Update capability through its Development owner and update social or institutional facts through their owners. Store every live result in external campaign records rather than this repository.
11. **Run the safeguards.** Reject universal ranks, automatic bundles, inherited mastery, class or Profession inflation, rigid locking, authority by label, duplicate ownership, and progression farming.

## Soul and Reincarnation Boundary

Soul systems may preserve identity, history, perspective, familiarity, symbolic pressure, or relationships only through their own rules:

- [Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) preserves no class standing, Profession, office, membership, credential, reputation, property, or social authority.
- [Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) and [Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may support orientation or perspective without installing present capability.
- [Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may support a valid relationship or access route without proving recognition, mastery, or institutional right.
- [Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may create symbolic recognition, opportunity, affinity, or pressure without becoming a class, Profession, office, or capability bonus.
- [Soul Avatars](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) coordinate accessible soul continuity without restoring world-bound standing.
- [Soul Weapon Foundations](../soul/SOUL_WEAPON_FOUNDATIONS.md) preserve a distinct partner. A Weapon Soul's capability, consent, identity, and history do not become the wielder's class or Skill.

Retained history can accelerate valid relearning. Present embodiment, current-life effort, actual relationships, and the choices of living societies remain necessary.

## Human and Monster Boundary

[Intelligent Monster Societies](../monster-evolution/MONSTER_SOCIETIES.md) may organize expertise through their own traditions, Professions, institutions, and learning structures. Do not treat resemblance to a Human Class as proof that the human framework is universal or superior.

Cross-species participation is possible where bodies, communication, access, practice, culture, and agency permit it. Affiliation does not grant a human progression tree to a monster, grant monster-native embodiment to a human, or bypass [Reincarnation Skill Crossover](../skills/REINCARNATION_SKILL_CROSSOVER.md).

## Phase Guarantees

When these rules are applied together:

- no universal character level, class level, Profession level, social level, institutional rating, or human power score exists;
- Skills remain capabilities, Development remains change, Classes remain social learning frameworks, and Professions remain organized work and responsibility;
- Martial Traditions and Magical Schools remain specialized class frameworks rather than combat or spell packages;
- recognition, credentials, office, authority, legitimacy, reputation, and actual capability remain distinct;
- ranks, grades, stages, certifications, and mastery labels remain local and purpose-bound;
- current embodiment, current-life effort, practice, maintenance, access, and evidence remain necessary;
- several routes can support similar mastery, and no route, Institution, culture, or build is universally optimal;
- Human and monster progression remain distinct unless a bounded present-life receiving route permits crossover;
- Reincarnation preserves no automatic social or institutional standing;
- Class Evolution changes a social framework rather than upgrading individuals or performing bodily Evolution;
- constraints remain scoped and causal rather than universal caps or GM fiat;
- no campaign state is stored in the canonical repository.

## Later-System Boundaries

- **Soul Weapons** owns detailed Weapon Soul awakening, intertwining, trust, refusal, evolution, Weapon Echoes, Legacy Weapons, compatibility, and manifestations.
- **Magic** owns mana, affinity, spell formation, rituals, enchanting, alchemy, divine magic, forbidden magic, and exact magical effects.
- **World Engine** owns populations, economies, law, factions, conflict, migration, institutions in motion, and long-term causal simulation.
- **GM Toolkit and templates** may adjudicate or record applications but cannot create exceptions to capability ownership, embodiment, evidence, agency, or persistence.

Use the [NPC Generator](../gm/NPC_GENERATOR.md) to create campaign-local actors from these social frameworks. NPC generation records a person's actual relationships to Classes, Professions, Institutions, offices, credentials, and Social Position without converting labels into capability or storing live profiles here.

The [Roadmap](../../design/ROADMAP.md) remains authoritative for implementation status. Mentioning a later system here grants none of its unfinished capabilities.

## Authority and Repository Boundary

These documents contain playable canonical rules. Accepted governance remains in [Design Decisions](../../design/DECISIONS.md); neither source silently overrides the other. Resolve any conflict before treating the affected rule as internally consistent.

This section contains reusable rules only. Particular people, students, classes, schools, curricula, guild rosters, careers, appointments, reputations, institutions, disputes, and current social conditions belong in external campaign records under the [Game Master Framework](../gm/GAME_MASTER_FRAMEWORK.md).
