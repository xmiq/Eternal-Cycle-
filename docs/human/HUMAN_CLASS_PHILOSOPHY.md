# Human Class Philosophy

## Status

This document is the authoritative Phase 5 foundation for Human Classes. It defines what a Human Class means, what it can establish, and which claims remain owned by other systems. The specialist Phase 5 rules linked below add playable structures without changing these boundaries.

## Purpose

Eternal Cycle uses classes to describe how societies make expertise understandable and transmissible. A class can give a learner a vocabulary, curriculum, community, lineage, standard of practice, and recognizable place in a culture. It does not place a capability bundle inside the learner.

The governing rule is:

> A Human Class is a culturally situated framework through which a society organizes, teaches, interprets, and recognizes a path of expertise.

"Human" identifies the social-development family in which these frameworks are most commonly formed. It does not mean that every human must possess a class, that humans share one class system, or that a nonhuman person can never participate in a human class tradition.

## The Six Facts Around a Class

A class claim often sits beside several related facts. Adjudication must keep them separate.

| Fact | Governing question | Canonical owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present capability | What can this person actually do now? | [Skills](../skills/README.md) and [Development](../progression/README.md) |
| Learning framework | Through what organized language, method, and tradition is the expertise taught? | Human Class rules |
| Professional responsibility | What work, duty, and standard is the person expected to perform? | [Profession Development](../progression/PROFESSION_DEVELOPMENT.md) and [Professions](PROFESSIONS.md) |
| Institutional access | Which people, places, materials, offices, and permissions are available? | [Institutions and Academies](INSTITUTIONS_AND_ACADEMIES.md) and current world causality |
| World recognition | What does a particular society believe, acknowledge, or expect? | [Social and Leadership Development](../progression/SOCIAL_AND_LEADERSHIP_DEVELOPMENT.md) and [Social Advancement](SOCIAL_ADVANCEMENT.md) |
| Personal identification | Which traditions, names, and communities does the person accept as part of the current self? | Character agency and campaign state |

One fact may provide evidence for another, but it does not replace it. A recognized class member may be unskilled. A capable practitioner may be unrecognized. An institution may teach several classes. A profession may draw from several classes. One person may identify with a class without holding an office or practising its associated profession.

## What a Human Class Is Not

### Not a Universal Character Level

A class does not summarize overall power. Classes cannot place every being on one ladder, and class ranks cannot be compared as if they were universal levels. Contextual capability remains governed by the [Development System](../progression/DEVELOPMENT_SYSTEM.md).

### Not a Skill or Skill Bundle

A Skill records a capability. A class can teach, contextualize, sequence, test, or culturally interpret Skills, but class entry does not grant them. Graduation, naming, initiation, promotion, or public recognition likewise does not install Techniques, Habits, knowledge, or mastery.

Human Skill Trees remain plural analytical maps of capability relationships. A class may teach one route through part of a tree, combine parts of several trees, or preserve methods that no current tree representation captures neatly. It is not itself a Skill Tree or an MMORPG talent tree.

### Not a Profession

A Profession is organized and recognizable work carrying responsibilities, standards, and social or economic function. A class is a framework for understanding and transmitting expertise. A class may prepare people for several Professions; a Profession may recruit from several classes; and either may exist without the other.

### Not an Institution

An institution is an organized body with people, resources, procedures, memory, and access. It may preserve, regulate, suppress, reinterpret, or teach classes. The organization is not the class itself, even when it claims exclusive ownership of the name.

### Not a Credential, Office, or Authority

A certificate, licence, military commission, guild standing, religious office, or legal appointment may recognize a class relationship within a defined jurisdiction. It does not prove every relevant capability and does not create authority outside its actual source and scope.

### Not a Soul Identity

A class is not what a soul inherently is. It is neither a [Soul Title](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) nor a permanent metaphysical category. A soul can carry history shaped by a class without carrying the former membership, rank, office, or society that gave the class meaning.

## How Classes Emerge

Classes form when people repeatedly need to make a domain of expertise teachable, legible, and socially useful. Their origins may include:

- a culture preserving effective responses to recurring dangers;
- a tradition organizing accumulated craft or martial knowledge;
- an apprenticeship lineage transmitting judgment through supervised practice;
- a guild distinguishing acceptable work from harmful imitation;
- a school comparing and refining several methods;
- a religion placing a discipline inside ritual and ethical obligations;
- a military structure standardizing coordination and responsibility;
- an organization developing specialist roles around shared problems;
- social recognition giving a stable name to demonstrated capability;
- self-taught practitioners whose methods later become teachable by others.

No origin is universally required. A class may be formal or informal, centralized or distributed, prestigious or stigmatized, written or oral, ancient or recent. It becomes a meaningful class framework through sustained social use, transmission, interpretation, and recognition, not merely because someone invents a dramatic label.

## Culture Shapes Classification

There is no universal list of Human Classes.

Two cultures may:

- use different names for substantially overlapping practices;
- place the same Skill inside different ethical or religious interpretations;
- divide one broad tradition into several classes;
- combine several foreign classes into one local category;
- recognize a self-taught expert as classless, irregular, heretical, or the founder of a new path;
- disagree about whether a discipline is a class, Profession, office, sacred calling, or criminal practice.

These differences are world facts with consequences. They do not duplicate the underlying capability and do not make one taxonomy metaphysically correct.

## Affiliation Is Contextual and Nonexclusive

A person may participate in several class frameworks when time, access, embodiment, obligations, and actual learning support that breadth. A person may also develop substantial expertise without any class affiliation.

Class affiliation can describe different relationships:

- learning within a curriculum;
- inheriting a lineage of practice;
- accepting a code or interpretive tradition;
- being recognized by peers;
- being classified by outsiders;
- teaching or reforming the tradition;
- maintaining historical affiliation after ceasing active practice.

These relationships are not interchangeable. Outsiders may assign a label the person rejects. A former student may retain knowledge while losing membership. A respected teacher may lack legal credentials. The campaign record should state the relevant relationship rather than treating the class name as a complete character sheet.

Multiple affiliations do not multiply capability. If two classes teach the same underlying Skill, the learner still has one capability history with possibly richer instruction, contrast, and context. Overlap may improve understanding through genuine practice; it does not create duplicate bonuses.

## Entry, Study, Recognition, and Mastery

Entry into a class framework may require sponsorship, payment, birth status, proof of aptitude, an oath, examination, service, demonstrated capability, or nothing beyond finding a willing teacher. Those requirements express the society and institution; they do not define universal progression.

The following claims remain separate:

- **admitted** - allowed to begin or participate;
- **instructed** - exposed to the framework and its teaching;
- **practised** - has performed relevant work under real conditions;
- **capable** - can currently produce the claimed result;
- **reliable** - can do so consistently under the relevant pressures;
- **recognized** - acknowledged by a named audience;
- **authorized** - permitted by a valid office, licence, or jurisdiction;
- **masterful** - demonstrates deep, integrated capability under the owning Development and Skill rules.

A local tradition may use ranks, stages, grades, circles, colours, titles, certifications, or mastery labels to track some of these facts. Every such label must state what it measures and who recognizes it. None becomes a universal level.

## Embodiment and Current-Life Effort

Class instruction operates through a present body, mind, environment, and life.

A curriculum may assume particular senses, anatomy, lifespan, magical access, tools, language, or social conditions. A learner whose embodiment differs may need translated methods, accommodations, new equipment, alternate Techniques, or an entirely different route toward similar results. The class name cannot bypass those requirements.

The six capability layers remain distinct:

1. **Persistent Potential** may contain retained affinities and development history relevant to relearning.
2. **Current Access** depends on present routes, teachers, knowledge, tools, permissions, and conditions.
3. **Embodied Expression** depends on the current body's actual capacities and limitations.
4. **Practised Reliability** requires current-life use, correction, and integration.
5. **Contextual Effectiveness** depends on the problem, environment, opposition, preparation, and support.
6. **World Recognition** depends on what particular people and institutions know or accept.

No class label collapses these layers. Current-life training remains necessary even when retained history makes relearning unusually fast.

## Reincarnation and Soul Systems

### Reincarnation

[Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) may preserve relevant Soul Imprints, retained Development, and access to prior Skill history. It does not preserve enrollment, citizenship, rank, credentials, legal standing, reputation, teachers, institutional access, or the current existence of a former class.

A reincarnated practitioner may:

- relearn familiar methods faster when a valid present route exists;
- recognize assumptions embedded in a curriculum;
- compare a current tradition with an earlier version;
- reconstruct lost methods through evidence and present practice;
- discover that a new body or changed Age makes the former class partly unusable;
- be treated as an outsider, fraud, prodigy, founder, or dangerous anomaly.

None of those outcomes restores former mastery or authority automatically.

### Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may increase alignment with meaningful prior practice or help the soul recognize an authentic continuity. It does not enroll the person, reveal a complete curriculum, supply a Skill, or compel a society to recognize the claim.

### Soul Echoes

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may offer memories, interpretations, cautions, or contrasting perspectives from lives shaped by a class. An Echo remains a bounded person-like continuity, not a portable instructor who can overwrite present agency or current-life training.

### Retained Instincts

[Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) may provide cue-shaped orientation that makes some exercises or dangers feel familiar. They do not provide a complete technique, curriculum, professional judgment, or recognized class standing.

### Soul Titles

[Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may affect symbolic expectations around a class. A title such as Friend of Dragons might make a dragon-liaison tradition curious or suspicious, but it does not make the bearer a trained negotiator, grant membership, or force trust.

## Skill Engine Interactions

Classes depend on the Skill Engine without owning its effects.

- [Human Skill Trees](../skills/HUMAN_SKILL_TREES.md) can map capabilities commonly taught through one or more classes.
- [Adaptive Skills](../skills/ADAPTIVE_SKILLS.md) can emerge when learners repeatedly solve unmet problems through meaningful variation and integration; a class label does not create them.
- [Skill Evolution](../skills/SKILL_EVOLUTION.md) can transform established capability as practice and understanding change; promotion in a class does not trigger it automatically.
- [Conceptual Skills](../skills/CONCEPTUAL_SKILLS.md) may be studied through advanced traditions, but symbolic language or elite status cannot substitute for their requirements.
- [Reincarnation Skill Crossover](../skills/REINCARNATION_SKILL_CROSSOVER.md) requires a valid receiving route and present redevelopment; class membership is neither universal compatibility nor proof of translation.

If a class claims to teach something whose effect belongs to a Skill, Development Track, tool, Species Trait, Mutation, Soul system, institution, or relationship, the class record should link to that owner rather than restating the effect as a class benefit.

## Human and Monster Societies

[Intelligent Monster Societies](../monster-evolution/MONSTER_SOCIETIES.md) organize and transmit expertise through their own cultures, traditions, institutions, lifecycles, and embodiments. Human Classes are not the universal model against which those societies are measured.

A nonhuman person may enter a human class when communication, embodiment, access, social permission, and actual practice make participation possible. Likewise, humans may study within monster traditions where the tradition permits it. The receiving framework may need genuine translation, and capabilities remain governed by the participant's current progression routes.

Mixed societies may create shared classes that no longer fit a purely human taxonomy. Such frameworks still obey the same ownership safeguards: social organization can guide learning, but it cannot grant anatomy, Skills, Development, Soul traits, or authority by label.

## Class Consequences Without Class Bonuses

Classes matter because worlds respond to socially legible expertise.

A class relationship may influence:

- which teachers, archives, tools, exercises, and peer communities become accessible;
- what obligations, taboos, debts, rivalries, or ethical expectations apply;
- which methods a learner encounters and which blind spots a curriculum reproduces;
- how employers, officials, enemies, clients, and other practitioners interpret the person;
- what examinations, sponsorships, commissions, or accusations become possible;
- how knowledge survives disruption, migration, persecution, or institutional collapse;
- which collaborators can coordinate through shared language and assumptions.

These are causal opportunities and pressures, not flat numerical bonuses. They may help in one context and hinder in another. Recognition can be mistaken, propaganda can distort a class, and a prestigious tradition can preserve dangerous errors.

## GM Adjudication

When a class label matters, the GM should ask:

1. **Which class framework and which society are involved?** A name without cultural scope is incomplete.
2. **What relationship does the person have to it?** Distinguish admission, study, practice, recognition, authority, teaching, and self-identification.
3. **Which capability is actually being claimed?** Resolve it through the owning Skill and Development rules.
4. **What present embodiment and access support expression?** Include body, tools, language, teachers, time, and environment.
5. **Who recognizes the claim, and why?** Recognition must have an audience, evidence, and possible error.
6. **What consequence follows here?** Grant only the access, expectation, opportunity, obligation, or reaction established by current causality.

If canon does not yet define a detailed class structure, use a [provisional ruling](../gm/PROVISIONAL_RULINGS.md) rather than inventing a permanent class mechanic.

## Worked Examples

### Similar Capability, Different Classes

A republic's civic duelist and a mountain clan's oath-guard both possess highly developed timing, measure, and one-handed blade Skills. The republic teaches public challenge law, controlled first-blood forms, and witness procedure. The clan teaches formation protection, kin obligations, and fighting on steep ground.

Their capabilities overlap, but their class frameworks organize different methods, meanings, and social consequences. Neither receives a duplicate blade benefit for translating the other's terminology.

### Class Member Without Mastery

A noble cadet is admitted to a prestigious war college and may truthfully name the college's officer class. Poor practice and little field experience leave the cadet unreliable under pressure. The affiliation opens archives and creates social expectations; it does not make the cadet a capable commander.

### Expert Without Recognition

A self-taught village healer has spent decades observing local illnesses, testing remedies carefully, and maintaining excellent records. The royal physician class refuses recognition because the healer lacks its language and credential. Patients may still seek the healer because demonstrated capability and local trust are strong. The court's classification affects access and reputation, not the truth of the healer's Skills.

### Reincarnated Former Practitioner

A former imperial sword instructor reincarnates into a body with short reach, different balance, and no access to the extinct empire's training halls. Retained history helps identify errors and understand old manuscripts. Years of present practice are still required to rebuild control, adapt Techniques, and earn recognition in a culture that treats the imperial class as half myth and half enemy propaganda.

### One Class, Divergent Capability

Two members of a river-warden tradition share navigation language, emergency signals, and a duty to protect crossings. One develops exceptional rescue coordination and weather reading. The other specializes in structural inspection and defensive spearwork. The shared class explains coordination and identity; it does not make their capabilities identical.

### Nonhuman Participation

An intelligent cliff-dwelling monster joins a human courier tradition. Its natural climbing anatomy changes which exercises are useful, while its unfamiliar hands make seal work and human pack equipment difficult. The tradition supplies routes, peers, maps, and standards. Monster embodiment supplies current access to some movements, while present training must build every learned Skill. Neither species nor class grants the other's capabilities wholesale.

## Safeguards

### No Class Inflation

Do not create a new class merely because a character has a distinctive Technique, job, nickname, tool, office, costume, or combination of Skills. A separate class framework needs meaningful social transmission, interpretation, or recognition that cannot be represented more truthfully by an existing class, Profession, institution, or capability record.

### No Automatic Skill Packages

Admission, initiation, rank, title, credential, inheritance, reincarnation, and recognition never award a bundle of Skills. Teaching creates access and opportunity; capability still requires valid Development.

### No Rigid Locking

Class affiliation does not forbid learning elsewhere by metaphysical rule. Institutions may impose secrecy, law, scarcity, prejudice, oaths, or retaliation, but those are causal social constraints rather than invisible class locks.

### No Universal Ranking

Class grades remain local claims. Compare actual capability, preparation, embodiment, tools, allies, environment, and opposition rather than assigning a universal class power score.

### No Inherited Mastery

Family membership, a prior incarnation, Soul continuity, an inherited manual, or an honored name may create opportunity or retained potential. None supplies present embodied mastery without rebuilding.

### No Automatic Authority

Expertise, class recognition, credentials, reputation, Soul Titles, and office remain distinct. Authority must come from an actual relationship, institution, law, agreement, or coercive fact and remains limited to its scope.

### No Status Substitution

Prestige does not defeat a factual capability check. Stigma does not erase real competence. The GM should preserve both the social consequence and the underlying truth.

### No Human Default

Do not recast monster traditions as incomplete Human Classes. Use each society's actual transmission structures and progression ownership.
