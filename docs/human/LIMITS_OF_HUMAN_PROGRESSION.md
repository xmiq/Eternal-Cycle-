# Limits of Human Progression

## Status

This document defines the canonical boundaries, tradeoffs, constraints, and assessment rules for human progression in Phase 5. Read the [Development System](../progression/DEVELOPMENT_SYSTEM.md), [Human Skill Trees](../skills/HUMAN_SKILL_TREES.md), [Human Class Philosophy](HUMAN_CLASS_PHILOSOPHY.md), and the [Human Classes and Professions Index](README.md) first.

This document does not assign a universal human level, maximum statistic, Skill count, class slot, lifespan, power tier, or progression rate.

## Core Rule

Human progression is limited through specific current routes, bodies, time, attention, practice, access, resources, relationships, world laws, maintenance, tradeoffs, and consequences. It has no single universal ceiling or score.

A limit answers:

> Which capability layer or route is constrained, by what current cause, under which conditions, according to what evidence, and what could change it?

The current incarnation remains one embodied person in one world. Extensive retained history can improve options and redevelopment efficiency, but it cannot express every former capability at once, bypass current requirements, or turn accumulated lives into an unlimited multiplier.

## Scope of Human Progression

Human Progression is the family of capability and social-development routes commonly available through human embodiment, Human Skill Trees, Human Classes, Professions, Institutions, tools, relationships, and current-world opportunities.

It is not:

- one tree shared by every human;
- every form of progression a human could ever access;
- a biological claim that all humans have the same body or potential;
- a standard against which monster or other nonhuman progression is judged;
- proof that a route is exclusive to humans;
- a package acquired by being born human;
- a catalog whose every branch can eventually be mastered.

A monster may learn through a Human Class without acquiring a Human Skill Tree automatically. A human may access magic, transformation, Soul systems, tools, or other progression only through those systems' current routes. Human-native learning remains one family of routes among many.

## No Universal Human Cap

Eternal Cycle does not define one maximum for human strength, knowledge, Skill, magic, social influence, Profession, or mastery.

Different limits arise from different owners:

- anatomy and lifecycle limit one body's present route;
- Developmental Ceilings limit current adaptation;
- a Skill's requirements limit learned expression;
- a magical source or world law limits Magical Access;
- an Institution limits access, authority, or resources;
- a Profession limits responsibility through standards and relationships;
- time, attention, recovery, and maintenance limit what one person can sustain;
- compatibility limits retained redevelopment and crossover;
- current conditions limit Contextual Effectiveness;
- evidence limits what observers can know.

Some limits can change through training, accommodation, tools, relationships, reform, magic, transformation, or another valid route. Others remain binding for the present body or world. A later incarnation may face different limits without proving that every limit is temporary.

No rule may infer a universal human ceiling from one culture, Institution, body, Age, test, famous practitioner, or observed population.

## Limits and the Six Capability Layers

A Progression Constraint may bind one layer while leaving others intact.

| Capability layer | Typical constraint question |
| --- | --- |
| **Persistent Potential** | Was relevant Development genuinely integrated and preserved, or does only a story, label, or unearned claim exist? |
| **Current Access** | Can the incarnation reach the memory, Skill route, teacher, source, language, permission, or knowledge needed now? |
| **Embodied Expression** | Can the current body, senses, cognition, health, tools, and magical structures perform the function? |
| **Practised Reliability** | Has current expression been calibrated, varied, corrected, maintained, and demonstrated under relevant pressure? |
| **Contextual Effectiveness** | Does the capability fit this objective, environment, opposition, timeframe, support, and cost? |
| **World Recognition** | Which audiences acknowledge, permit, reward, misunderstand, restrict, or deny it? |

For example, a former master may retain deep Persistent Potential while an immature body blocks expression, an Academy blocks access, current practice remains unreliable, and a local guild refuses recognition. Those are four limits, not one reduced level.

## Progression Constraint Profile

Use a Progression Constraint Profile when a limit materially affects a claim. Record only relevant fields.

| Field | What it records |
| --- | --- |
| **Objective and route** | The exact capability, learning route, responsibility, or expression being attempted. |
| **Bound layer** | Persistent Potential, Current Access, Embodied Expression, Practised Reliability, Contextual Effectiveness, or World Recognition. |
| **Owning source** | Body, Skill, Development Track, Profession, Institution, magical system, relationship, Soul rule, environment, or other current cause. |
| **Constraint** | The specific absence, incompatibility, dependency, ceiling, conflict, cost, uncertainty, or condition limiting the route. |
| **Evidence state** | Demonstrated, corroborated, inferred, claimed, outdated, disputed, or unknown evidence supporting the limit. |
| **Current effect** | What is delayed, narrowed, unreliable, inaccessible, dangerous, dependent, prohibited, or impossible under present conditions. |
| **Change routes** | Training, rest, maturation, treatment, accommodation, tools, instruction, access, relationship, reform, transformation, another body, or investigation that could matter. |
| **Costs and tradeoffs** | Time, attention, resources, maintenance, risk, duties, conflicts, lost alternatives, and consequences. |
| **Persistence** | Whether the constraint ends with a condition, follows an external dependency, remains with the body, or must be reassessed in another incarnation. |
| **Unknowns** | Material facts that remain unresolved and how they might be investigated fairly. |

The profile belongs in an external Campaign Record when used for a current character. It is not a permanent penalty sheet or one total of limitations.

## Kinds of Constraint

These descriptions help locate ownership. They are not severity ranks or a mandatory checklist.

### Embodiment Constraint

The current anatomy, senses, cognition, size, material, health, disability, lifecycle, sex-linked form, or Species Potential does not support some expression as proposed.

Training cannot create absent anatomy. A valid accommodation, tool, magical route, transformation, or translated function may produce another route without pretending the body was never different.

### Maturation Constraint

The body or structure has not completed a required lifecycle, growth, construction, awakening, or recovery process. Retained history can improve safe choices and later training efficiency but cannot compress valid maturation into an instant.

### Access Constraint

A possible route exists, but current knowledge, memory, teacher, language, record, source, permission, relationship, Institution, location, or trigger is unavailable.

An access barrier is not proof of incapacity. Bypassing a gate also does not prove the gate's safety or accountability concerns were false.

### Developmental Constraint

The person lacks current adaptation, Skill, integration, judgment, calibration, or reliability required by the route. Valid current training may address it subject to diminishing returns, recovery, compatibility, and Developmental Ceilings.

### Resource and Tool Constraint

Materials, equipment, space, energy, food, medicine, information, maintenance, assistants, infrastructure, or supply are insufficient. External support can change current effectiveness while retaining its owner and dependency.

### Social and Institutional Constraint

Law, rank, licence, prejudice, secrecy, monopoly, reputation, obligation, office, culture, gatekeeping, or institutional procedure affects access, recognition, safety, employment, or authority.

Such a constraint can be materially powerful without becoming a metaphysical limit on capability. Reform, migration, resistance, accommodation, independent practice, or another Institution may create routes through actual consequences.

### Metaphysical and World-Law Constraint

Current magic, divine authority, Soul conditions, contracts, Channels, environmental laws, or other metaphysical structures do not permit a proposed route. Memory and effort cannot make a source or law exist.

### Compatibility Constraint

Retained history or an adjacent capability does not correspond closely enough to the current body, tree, function, environment, culture, or world law. A bounded principle may translate while literal expression remains latent or impossible.

### Coordination Constraint

The result requires partners, timing, leadership, communication, trust, consent, offices, or Collective Capability that the person cannot supply alone. Personal excellence does not replace missing participants or grant extra actions.

### Conflict Constraint

Several routes impose contradictory habits, values, duties, timing, source conditions, identities, or maintenance demands. Deliberate cue separation, prioritization, negotiation, integration, or relinquishment may be required. Possessing both histories does not resolve the conflict automatically.

### Uncertainty Constraint

Available evidence cannot establish whether a route exists, whether a ceiling is current or structural, or which cost applies. Uncertainty is a factual information condition, not permission for arbitrary denial or a hidden guarantee.

## Embodiment Is Binding

The present body supplies:

- available movement and structures;
- sensory signals and resolution;
- cognition, attention, memory, communication, and processing;
- age, lifecycle, maturation, recovery, and lifespan;
- health, pain, fatigue, illness, disability, and injury;
- size, mass, reach, leverage, and material composition;
- Species Traits, Current Instincts, and Magical Organs;
- vulnerabilities, needs, and environmental tolerances.

Human bodies vary. One anatomy, sex, age, health state, culture, or historical population is not the universal human baseline.

### Accommodation Is a Valid Route

Accommodation may change the body-environment relationship through tools, interfaces, scheduling, communication, architecture, assistance, altered technique, role design, treatment, or social procedure.

It does not fake capability or erase a disability. It changes the route and conditions through real support. A person using an accommodation can possess genuine mastery, while the accommodation retains its own availability, maintenance, and limits.

### Bodily Change Has an Owner

Mutation, transformation, artificial modification, divine alteration, corruption, purification, and bodily Evolution may alter available routes only through their dedicated systems. Training, Class Affiliation, Profession, rank, Soul Title, or desire cannot create anatomy by relabeling it.

## Time, Maturation, and Lifespan

Time enables change but does not award it.

Progression may require time for:

- maturation and structural growth;
- training cycles and recovery;
- varied cases and responsibility;
- trust and relationship formation;
- long observation or research;
- institutional access and teaching;
- correction of habits;
- maintenance and revalidation;
- consequences to become visible.

A short-lived body may face genuine opportunity constraints. A long-lived or immortal body may gain more opportunities while also facing maintenance, changed worlds, social loss, obsolete assumptions, boredom, injury, resource demands, and diminishing returns.

Elapsed time, seniority, time skips, meditation duration, membership years, and survival do not establish Development without relevant activity and integration.

## Finite Attention, Action, and Practice

One incarnation has finite attention, decision bandwidth, physical action, recovery, and opportunity.

### Practice Allocation

Practice Allocation is how the current incarnation distributes finite time, attention, effort, feedback, recovery, resources, and meaningful use among Development routes.

Learning many routes is allowed. There are no metaphysical class slots, profession slots, Skill slots, or affiliation limits. Practical constraints still create consequences:

- less practice per route;
- slower deepening;
- rust in neglected expressions;
- conflicting automatic responses;
- inadequate recovery;
- fragmented access;
- missed teachers, duties, relationships, or opportunities;
- inability to express several demanding capabilities simultaneously.

Retained familiarity can reduce rediscovery waste. It cannot create extra hours, bodies, actions, attention, or recovery.

### Breadth and Depth

Breadth supports flexibility, translation, diagnosis, and resilience. Depth supports precision, efficiency, adaptation, judgment, and reliability within a bounded domain.

Neither is universally superior. Breadth is not simultaneous throughput, and depth does not grant applicability outside its scope.

### Conflicting Habits

Two valid methods may interfere when they demand incompatible timing, posture, attention, ethics, source relationships, or automatic response. Integration requires current practice and reliable cues. Recording both Skills or Classes does not erase interference.

## Maintenance Burden

Maintenance Burden is the current time, practice, recovery, resources, relationships, calibration, and support required to preserve access or Practised Reliability in a capability.

Maintenance varies. A conceptual insight may remain accessible with little rehearsal while elite physical timing, delicate magic, professional standards, team coordination, and perishable technical knowledge may require frequent renewal.

Failure to maintain can cause rust, reduced reliability, outdated knowledge, lost access, weakened relationships, or institutional consequences without deleting legitimate Persistent Potential.

Powerful combinations may carry several independent Maintenance Burdens. Reincarnation does not merge them into one cost or make every former capability permanently ready.

## Opportunity Cost and Tradeoffs

Pursuing one route can reduce opportunities elsewhere through:

- time and attention;
- recovery and health;
- money, materials, tools, and travel;
- teachers and institutional access;
- legal, professional, sacred, or political duties;
- incompatible source relationships or vows;
- identity and community commitments;
- physical specialization or altered embodiment;
- secrecy, risk, reputation, or exclusion;
- forgone careers, relationships, locations, and life experiences.

Opportunity cost is causal, not a universal penalty. A wealthy Academy student and an isolated self-taught worker may face different costs. Retained progression changes some costs without removing choice or consequence.

No character must specialize, generalize, accept a Class, enter a Profession, join an Institution, or pursue maximum capability. Refusal and another life path remain valid.

## Access, Instruction, and Social Power

Human progression often depends on socially preserved knowledge, tools, teachers, peers, affected communities, Institutions, and Professions.

These structures can:

- expose learners to tested methods and varied feedback;
- preserve records and failure history;
- provide tools, sites, clients, teams, and supervision;
- create safety and accountability;
- exclude, exploit, indoctrinate, monopolize, or suppress;
- misclassify ability or preserve obsolete doctrine.

Institutional access improves opportunity rather than installing capability. Self-teaching can produce real mastery while retaining blind spots, resource limits, and recognition problems.

Social exclusion may prevent a person from entering a route that requires controlled resources or relationships. It does not make the exclusion a statement about the person's inherent potential. Conversely, opening access creates an opportunity, not a guaranteed outcome.

## Classes, Professions, and Social Advancement

### Human Classes

Human Classes organize and transmit expertise. A class curriculum can make Development more efficient, coherent, safe, or socially recognized, but Class Affiliation, rank, initiation, or Class Evolution cannot grant the referenced capabilities.

Multiple Class Affiliations are allowed. Their practical limits arise from Practice Allocation, access, obligations, conflicting doctrine, maintenance, embodiment, and current-life choice rather than class slots.

### Professions

A Profession integrates capability with work, judgment, standards, relationships, service, and accountability. Technical mastery alone may be insufficient for a professional responsibility, while a capable practitioner may remain outside formal recognition.

Professional breadth is limited by actual cases, tools, knowledge, relationships, law, and responsibility. Titles, licences, offices, employment, and seniority do not increase capability automatically.

### Social Advancement

Social Position can change access, resources, authority, obligations, reputation, and opportunity. It does not raise a human progression cap or prove Development.

Prestige can conceal weakness, and stigma can obstruct capable people. Both remain world consequences rather than objective capability measures.

### Institutions and Academies

Institutions can provide Collective Capability beyond any one member. That capability depends on roles, procedures, infrastructure, resources, communication, and relationships. It does not become the personal progression of a leader or participant.

## Diminishing Returns, Plateaus, and Ceilings

### Diminishing Returns

Repeated solved work increasingly supports maintenance, fluency, or reliability rather than new Development. Further growth normally requires changed precision, variation, resistance, context, feedback, integration, responsibility, or objective.

Efficiency does not abolish diminishing returns. An experienced soul may rebuild familiar foundations quickly and still need genuinely new problems for further growth.

### Plateaus

A Plateau means the current method, challenge, body, understanding, access, or environment is no longer producing meaningful change. It calls for diagnosis rather than more suffering.

Possible responses include altered practice, better feedback, rest, rehabilitation, new tools, instruction, experimentation, another environment, accommodation, resolving an access barrier, or accepting the current limit.

### Developmental Ceilings

A Developmental Ceiling is present and route-specific. It may depend on anatomy, lifecycle, health, species, materials, environment, magic, and established modifications.

Reaching one ceiling does not establish a universal personal maximum. Another capability, method, tool, role, team, body, or later incarnation may have a different route. No alternative is guaranteed, and a current ceiling cannot be ignored merely because the soul has greater Persistent Potential.

### Breakthroughs

A breakthrough is demonstrated by changed capability and later reliability. Danger, pain, dramatic importance, a Plateau, a teacher's approval, or a title does not guarantee one.

## Regression, Rust, Injury, and Suppression

Current performance can decline because:

- disuse weakens timing or recall;
- injury or illness changes expression;
- aging or lifecycle changes the body;
- tools, sources, laws, or environments disappear;
- habits conflict;
- professional knowledge becomes obsolete;
- magical or social suppression blocks access;
- relationships and group coordination decay;
- overtraining and burnout prevent integration.

Name what changed. Rust, lost access, bodily regression, revoked authority, and obsolete knowledge are different facts.

Recovery may restore prior expression without creating new retained progression. Rehabilitation can produce genuine new adaptation where the person develops a changed route, but repeated loss and recovery cannot be farmed.

## External Power and Support

Tools, artifacts, medicine, magic, transformations, allies, Institutions, Soul Weapons, territory, wealth, and infrastructure may greatly increase Contextual Effectiveness.

Every external contribution retains:

- source and owner;
- access and permission;
- compatibility and interface;
- cost and maintenance;
- throughput and availability;
- risk and failure;
- consequences when removed.

External support is not lesser or false. It is also not automatically internal Development. A human with a fortress, ritual team, and Soul Weapon may defeat a physically superior monster while remaining dependent on those supports.

## Current-Life Effort and Retained Progression

Reincarnation preserves legitimate soul-level progression according to its owners. It resets the body's statistics, condition, species traits, ordinary equipment, current social position, and world-bound access.

Retained progression may improve:

- recognition of useful principles and errors;
- choice of training and teachers;
- conceptual reacquisition;
- calibration where bodies correspond;
- Developmental Familiarity;
- ability to translate a function through a valid route.

It does not provide:

- mature anatomy, health, stamina, or current statistics;
- current tools, resources, Institutions, relationships, licences, or authority;
- automatic access to every memory or Skill;
- present calibration or Practised Reliability;
- compatible magic or world laws;
- simultaneous readiness across every former capability;
- a merged human-monster progression tree.

Current-life effort remains constitutive rather than ceremonial. The incarnation develops the present expression through training, experimentation, instruction, responsibility, feedback, recovery, and meaningful use.

## Stat XP and Redevelopment

Retained Stat XP records domain-specific Developmental Familiarity, not saved statistics.

It may provide bounded Redevelopment Acceleration when:

- provenance is genuine;
- Current Access exists;
- the present body has a compatible route;
- current effort creates useful adaptation;
- feedback and recovery support integration;
- diminishing returns and current ceilings are respected.

Stat XP cannot:

- set starting statistics;
- purchase unrelated capability;
- multiply through repeated rebuilding;
- earn interest across lives;
- make weak or damaged bodies produce bonus XP;
- preserve Species Traits or former forms;
- eliminate maturation, practice, or recovery;
- become one universal progression currency.

An ancient soul can redevelop exceptionally fast in a compatible route while taking years to mature, lacking current resources, or remaining unable to express incompatible history.

## Human and Monster Progression

Human and monster progression remain initially distinct. Neither is the complete or superior version of the other.

A Human Skill Tree does not contain monster anatomy, Species Traits, Current Instincts, Evolution Routes, or monster-native Skills. A Monster Skill Tree does not automatically contain Human Classes, Institutions, tools, or learned human routes.

### Bounded Crossover

[Reincarnation Skill Crossover](../skills/REINCARNATION_SKILL_CROSSOVER.md) requires:

1. genuine retained provenance;
2. Current Access to a bounded source principle;
3. a valid present Receiving Route;
4. functional and embodiment compatibility;
5. present experimentation, calibration, and practice;
6. a defined result with transfer loss, costs, and failure states;
7. protection against duplicated capability and tree merger.

Class contact, cultural adoption, memory, Soul Resonance, a hybrid identity, a changed name, or desire does not perform crossover. When no Receiving Route exists, history remains latent or inaccessible.

## Soul-System Interactions

### Soul Depth

[Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) may support capacity to integrate complex history. It does not raise bodily ceilings, grant Skills, add actions, or supply Practised Reliability.

### Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) may improve access to a genuine Link or retained history. It does not create compatibility, tools, anatomy, authority, or training.

### Soul Echoes

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) may teach, warn, compare methods, or lend bounded perspective. Echo performance is not the current incarnation's reliability, and multiple Echoes do not create parallel bodies or unlimited attention.

### Retained Instincts

[Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) may orient perception or response. They do not provide anatomy, a complete Skill, correct current assumptions, or compelled action.

### Soul Titles

[Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may create symbolic authority, pressure, opportunity, fear, or affinity. They do not raise capability ceilings or grant Class standing, Profession, office, or mastery.

### Soul Avatars

[Soul Avatars](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) may synthesize whole-soul perspectives under bounded Avatar Expression. They do not bypass the current body's action, access, embodiment, cost, or world consequences.

### Soul Weapons

[Soul Weapons](../soul-weapons/README.md) may provide a powerful external relationship, capability, tool, feedback source, and training partner. The Weapon Soul retains personhood, abilities, consent, form, maintenance, and limits. Its power does not become the wielder's human Development.

### Akashic Archive

[Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) Records may supply evidence and knowledge. Information does not become execution, current access, embodiment, permission, or reliability automatically.

## World Engine and Age Change

The World Engine may alter progression opportunities through:

- available resources and technologies;
- Institutions, cultures, laws, Professions, and teachers;
- ecology, migration, war, disease, and settlement;
- magical laws and source availability;
- lost or emerging knowledge;
- prejudice, reform, cooperation, and conflict;
- environmental compatibility and danger;
- time skips, Age transitions, World Resets, and world contact.

World change can open, close, translate, or obsolete routes. It does not award Development merely because circumstances became harder or more dramatic.

A World Reset can rebalance opportunities without deleting protected Soul progression or guaranteeing that former methods remain useful.

## Assessing Human Capability

Do not ask, "What level is this human?"

Ask:

1. What objective and timeframe matter?
2. Which capability layers and routes are relevant?
3. What Persistent Potential and current Development are evidenced?
4. What can be accessed, embodied, and expressed now?
5. What has Practised Reliability under comparable conditions?
6. What constraints, costs, maintenance, and dependencies bind the attempt?
7. What preparation, tools, allies, institutions, and information apply?
8. What counters, incompatibilities, environment, and opposition matter?
9. What social permission, responsibility, and consequences apply?
10. Which facts are demonstrated, inferred, disputed, outdated, hidden, or unknown?

Use the comparative findings in [Capability Assessment](../progression/CAPABILITY_ASSESSMENT.md): Clearly Superior, Generally Advantaged, Situationally Advantaged, Uncertain, Mismatched, or Incomparable.

Two humans may be impossible to rank meaningfully. One may possess extraordinary surgical judgment, another exceptional battlefield movement, and each may depend on different tools, contexts, and support.

## Communicating Limits and Uncertainty

The GM should distinguish:

- **demonstrated limit** - current evidence shows the proposed route failing under defined conditions;
- **known requirement** - canon or reliable evidence establishes a missing prerequisite;
- **credible risk** - evidence supports danger or instability without proving impossibility;
- **social barrier** - people or Institutions restrict the route through actual power;
- **inference** - observers have a reasoned but incomplete model;
- **unknown** - evidence cannot yet establish the route or ceiling;
- **false belief** - an audience treats a claim as true despite contrary world evidence.

Do not describe a route as impossible merely because an Institution rejects it, no known practitioner has succeeded, or a current method failed. Do not promise hidden potential merely because failure would be disappointing.

Where meaningful choice depends on a limit, provide fair signs, investigable evidence, and known consequences. Hidden facts must have prior causal existence and cannot be invented retroactively to block a valid plan.

## Human Progression Claim Resolution

When a claim says a human can learn, master, combine, restore, bypass, or exceed something:

1. **State the exact function.** Reject vague claims to maximize human potential or unlock a whole tree.
2. **Identify the owner and current route.** Name the Skill, Development Track, Profession, Institution, magic, body, Soul rule, or support involved.
3. **Separate the six layers.** Do not infer current expression from Persistent Potential or recognition.
4. **Identify constraints.** Name embodiment, maturation, access, practice, resource, social, metaphysical, compatibility, coordination, conflict, or uncertainty limits.
5. **Check present effort.** Verify meaningful engagement, feedback, adaptation, recovery, integration, and reliability.
6. **Apply time and Practice Allocation.** Include maintenance, competing routes, obligations, and opportunity costs.
7. **Apply diminishing returns and ceilings.** Diagnose the route rather than awarding a dramatic breakthrough.
8. **Separate external support.** Preserve tools, allies, Institutions, transformations, magic, and Soul Weapons as dependent owners.
9. **Audit persistence and crossover.** Reject duplicate gains, starting-stat restoration, tree merger, and recursive acceleration.
10. **Assess contextually.** Resolve the stated objective with evidence, preparation, opposition, and uncertainty.
11. **Apply consequences.** Record injury, fatigue, resource use, social response, duties, learning, and changed access under their owners.
12. **Reassess when conditions change.** A limit is not a permanent universal tag unless its Owning System makes it one.

## Worked Examples

### Ancient Swordsman in a Child Body

A soul with several lives of sword mastery reincarnates as a healthy human child. Spacing principles, error recognition, and Skill history are unusually accessible.

The body still requires maturation, strength, reach, bone development, recovery, and current calibration. Safe practice may advance quickly for the age, but retained history cannot make the child an adult body or guarantee pressure-tested reliability against present opponents.

### Broad Veteran and Narrow Specialist

One veteran has competent medicine, navigation, diplomacy, command, and sword use. Another has spent the same current-life opportunities developing exceptional close-quarters timing in one Martial Tradition.

The veteran has broader options and may lead a difficult expedition more effectively. The specialist is generally advantaged in the exact duel their practice fits. Neither is universally more progressed.

### Four Class Affiliations

A scholar participates in a legal Class, a Magical School, a ritual music tradition, and a civic mediator framework. No class-slot rule forbids this.

Travel, preparation, source obligations, conflicting terminology, professional duties, and maintenance make equal mastery impractical. The player may prioritize, integrate bounded principles, accept rust, or leave an affiliation. The labels themselves neither punish nor sustain capability.

### Disabled Engineer and Designed Access

An engineer cannot use a workshop built around standing foot controls after an injury. The Institution redesigns the station with seated hand interfaces and revised safety roles.

The accommodation restores a valid route and may support excellent work. It does not erase pain, maintenance, or interface dependencies, and the original inaccessible layout was not proof of lost engineering mastery.

### Excluded but Capable Healer

A healer develops strong present capability through community practice and peer correction but is barred from a royal Academy by caste law.

The social barrier blocks records, legal work, clients, and institutional tools. It does not cap the healer's inherent ability. Independent progress still faces real knowledge, resource, recognition, and accountability constraints rather than becoming unlimited because the gate is unjust.

### Human Rebuilding Monster Experience

A former spider monster reincarnates as a human and retains web-construction history. Tension, redundancy, and load-distribution principles are accessible; spinnerets and vibration organs are absent.

Engineering tools and current study create a Human Skill Tree Receiving Route for some principles. No amount of memory produces silk anatomy, and translated engineering does not copy the former Monster Skill.

### Academy and Ritual Team

A physically ordinary researcher coordinates an Academy's archive, calibrated instruments, ritual specialists, and evacuation plan to contain a stronger magical creature.

The result is a Collective Capability supported by preparation and compatibility. It does not prove the researcher personally possesses every ritual Skill or a higher power level than the creature.

### Long Life at a Plateau

An ageless human repeats the same safe sword drills for a century. Timing remains polished in that exercise, but unfamiliar terrain, modern weapons, and adaptive opponents expose narrow reliability.

Time preserved maintenance. It did not grant endless Development. Further growth requires meaningful variation, feedback, changed problems, or acceptance of the specialization.

### Transformation-Dependent Power

A human uses a lawful transformation to gain wings and learns reliable flight over several years. The capability is real while the transformation, its source, and maintenance are active.

The character's flight does not prove ordinary human shoulders can grow wings through training. If the transformation ends, retained principles may remain while literal expression becomes latent or requires another route.

### Two Incomparable Masters

One human is a master negotiator with broad cultural knowledge and fragile health. Another is a master builder with exceptional endurance and little diplomatic experience.

The negotiator is favored in a treaty crisis; the builder is favored in emergency reconstruction. A single human progression score would conceal the very capabilities that matter.

## Safeguards

### No Universal Level or Cap

Do not sum Skills, Development, ranks, Classes, Professions, magic, Soul history, equipment, or recognition into one level, potential score, or maximum.

### No Human Default

Human bodies, learning, institutions, and values are not the standard for intelligence, personhood, mastery, civilization, or viable progression.

### No Class, Profession, or Skill Slots

Plural routes are limited through current time, attention, access, embodiment, recovery, maintenance, obligations, interference, and opportunity cost rather than a metaphysical slot count.

### No Time, Pain, Kill, or Death Progression

Age, repetition, suffering, danger, kills, injuries, near-death events, Final Death, and Reincarnation create no automatic Development or breakthrough.

### No Reincarnation Snowball

Retained history does not earn interest, duplicate through rebuilding, restore starting statistics, multiply by life count, create parallel action, or waive current effort and ceilings.

### No Social Status as Capability

Rank, title, licence, class recognition, reputation, office, wealth, institutional prestige, and followers create only their actual social effects.

### No External-Power Internalization

Tools, transformations, magic, allies, Institutions, Soul Weapons, and infrastructure retain their owners, costs, permissions, maintenance, and failure states.

### No Tree Merger

Human and monster progression remain distinct. Crossover resolves one bounded source-to-result claim through a current Receiving Route.

### No Disability Stereotype

Do not turn disability into a universal penalty, virtue, inspiration reward, or proof of incapacity. Assess the actual body-environment relationship, capability, support, pain, fatigue, access, and choice.

### No Limit by Fiat

The GM must identify a canonical or causally established constraint and evidence. Unknown rules cannot be invented to prevent success, and lack of precedent is not proof of impossibility.

### No Guaranteed Hidden Potential

Uncertainty does not promise an unlock, transformation, breakthrough, or undiscovered route. Investigation may reveal opportunity, cost, impossibility, or continued uncertainty.

### No Universally Optimal Build

Specialization, generalization, magic, technology, institutions, social power, Soul history, and bodily strength all retain contexts, dependencies, counters, and opportunity costs.

### No Campaign Data

Progression Constraint Profiles and current character limits belong in external Campaign Records. This repository stores reusable rules only.

## Phase 5 Boundary

The Human Classes and Professions phase establishes social learning frameworks, integrated work, Martial Traditions, Magical Schools, Social Advancement, Institutions, Class Evolution, and the limits described here.

It does not define:

- one complete Human Skill Tree for a campaign;
- particular Classes, Professions, Academies, ranks, curricula, or characters;
- detailed Soul Weapon rules;
- the complete Magic system;
- World Engine economies, law, populations, factions, war, or long-term simulation;
- universal numerical growth curves, statistics, combat ratings, or encounter levels;
- live world state or campaign history.
