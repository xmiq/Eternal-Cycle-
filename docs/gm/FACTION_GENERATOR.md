# Faction Generator

## Purpose

This document defines how a Game Master creates, classifies, or revalidates a campaign-local faction through the completed [Faction Behaviour](../world-engine/FACTION_BEHAVIOUR.md) framework.

The generator produces an external Faction Profile from actual people, relationships, interests, information routes, procedures, resources, and continuity. It is not an alignment table, political plot, organization catalogue, automatic conflict source, or repository for live campaign factions.

## Core Rule

Generate coordination, not a collective personality.

A faction exists only where distinct actors coordinate around overlapping interests through recognizable relationships and action routes while retaining separate agency. Shared labels, conditions, enemies, or identities do not create that coordination by themselves.

## Ownership

This document owns:

- selecting a faction-generation scope;
- building and testing a Faction Basis;
- distinguishing members, participants, representatives, subjects, dependents, and audiences;
- assembling interests, information distribution, decision routes, capacity, cohesion, relationships, and continuity into an external Faction Profile;
- bounded faction-generation randomization;
- handoffs to Faction Behaviour, NPCs, Encounters, world simulation, and specialist owners.

It does not own:

- faction decisions or actions after generation;
- institutions, states, cultures, populations, religions, species, or settlements;
- NPC identity or individual capability;
- world-domain outcomes;
- war, unrest, disease, Economy, Ecology, Dungeons, Gates, Stability, Ages, or Resets;
- player allegiance or relationships;
- a universal faction power score or alignment;
- live campaign records in this repository.

## Key Terms

### Faction Generation Brief

A **Faction Generation Brief** is the external input for one generation pass, stating the coordination question, world context, scope, known participants and institutions, information views, preparation horizon, and canonical limits.

It does not prescribe a morality, enemy, leader, objective, or future action.

### Faction Basis

A **Faction Basis** is the validated scope, participants, overlapping interests, relationships, information and action routes, boundary conditions, and continuity that permit one faction claim.

It establishes coordination without claiming uniform belief, consent, loyalty, capability, or knowledge.

### Faction Seed

A **Faction Seed** is an unvalidated preparation possibility combining a possible Faction Basis, history, identity, participant relationships, internal differences, objectives, capacity, current activity, and world placement.

It is not established campaign fact until classification, causality, ownership, information, and continuity tests pass.

### Faction Version

A **Faction Version** is one dated configuration of a faction's participants, identity, interests, procedures, information routes, capacity, relationships, and continuity claims.

Material reform, capture, schism, merger, revival, or succession may require a new Version without automatically ending every continuity claim.

### Faction Generation Pass

A **Faction Generation Pass** is one bounded procedure that resolves only the faction facts needed for a stated issue and preparation horizon while preserving member agency, internal uncertainty, future decisions, and specialist ownership.

## Generation Scopes

### Classify Existing Coordination

Test whether an observed network, movement, bloc, order, household alliance, pack, guild chapter, council, patronage system, or other grouping currently meets the Faction definition.

The result may be:

- one valid faction;
- several overlapping factions;
- a Coalition of distinct factions;
- an Institution containing factions;
- a Population, culture, faith, Profession, settlement, species, or audience with no shared action route;
- a former faction whose coordination dissolved;
- a disputed classification used for political reasons.

Classification is allowed to fail.

### Generate a New Campaign-Local Faction

Create coordination from actual actors and world pressures. "New" means newly defined for the campaign; it does not mean appearing without formation history, relationships, or participants.

### Generate an Internal Faction

Define a bloc, caucus, reform network, rival office group, dissident circle, or patronage cluster inside a larger Institution, society, state, faction, or community.

Internal membership does not erase the larger relationships or grant every institutional resource.

### Generate a Coalition

Define coordination among otherwise distinct factions or parties around stated objectives, contributions, decision routes, and exit conditions.

A Coalition does not merge identities, knowledge, authority, obligations, or all interests.

### Revalidate a Changed Faction

After leadership change, death, Reincarnation, capture, reform, schism, merger, defeat, migration, resource crisis, Gate contact, Age transition, or other material change, create a new Faction Version from surviving continuity rather than restoring the old arrangement by name.

```markdown

- Resources, Economy, Ecology, Magic, Technology, conflict, and current pressures:
- known relationships, histories, and consequences:

- established NPCs and participant categories:
- offices, networks, households, communities, and other groups:
- affected nonparticipants and audiences:

- Factual View:
- relevant participant and outsider Observer Views:
- public claims and player-facing evidence:
- hidden, disputed, outdated, and unresolved claims:

- canonical and Provisional limits:
- facts and future choices that must remain open:
- later generators or owners not authorized in this pass:
```

The populated brief remains external.

## Faction Basis Requirements

### Scope

State the issue, place, time, or relationship within which coordination is being claimed.

A network may act as one faction around migration access and split into several factions around religious reform. The generator does not seek one permanent boundary for every question.

### Participants

Identify actual actors who can contribute to information, decision, support, mobilization, or execution.

Participants may be persons, households, offices, communities, organizations, spirits, Weapon Souls, distributed beings, or other agentive parties under their owners. A demographic category is not an actor by itself.

### Overlapping Interests

Establish why some participants have reason to coordinate. Interests may concern survival, resources, territory, work, care, ecology, law, faith, identity, magic, status, security, justice, autonomy, knowledge, or relationships.

Overlap need not mean identical motives or preferred methods.

### Relationships and Action Routes

Show how participants communicate, deliberate, delegate, bargain, command, contribute resources, coordinate behavior, enforce decisions, or recognize one another.

Shared sentiment without an action route may be an audience, tendency, culture, or movement potential rather than a current faction.

### Boundary Conditions

Define entry, exit, refusal, representation, membership, participation, secrecy, obligations, and how outsiders or affected subjects relate to the faction.

Coercion may produce participation or compliance without consent, loyalty, or durable cohesion.

### Continuity

Identify what lets the coordination remain recognizable through changing members, leaders, objectives, and conditions:

- relationships;
- identity and recognition;
- procedures and offices;
- records and memory;
- assets and infrastructure;
- obligations and promises;
- recurring interests;
- successor recognition;
- unresolved consequences.

A name or symbol alone is insufficient.

## Generation Procedure

### Step 1: State the Coordination Question

Name the exact issue and reason a faction profile is needed.

Good questions include:

- Which actors coordinate to preserve this migration corridor?
- Who inside the academy is organizing against the new licensing rule?
- Does the river delegation act as one Coalition for this treaty?
- What survived after the old military order fragmented?

"Create an evil cult," "the party needs enemies," and "every city needs three factions" are not valid foundations.

### Step 2: Search Established Campaign State

Review existing NPCs, Institutions, factions, Populations, cultures, professions, settlements, households, patronage, alliances, conflicts, and obligations.

Do not create a duplicate coordination when an existing faction could address the issue through its actual scope. Do not force reuse when that faction lacks interest, access, capacity, or authority.

### Step 3: Identify World-Side Pressures and Opportunities

Use established resource needs, ecological relations, laws, exclusions, beliefs, infrastructure, professions, threats, opportunities, disputes, and consequences that could motivate coordination.

Pressure makes coordination relevant. It does not create agreement, organization, leadership, or one inevitable response.

### Step 4: Identify Possible Participants

List actual actors and participant categories with routes to know, care, contribute, decide, or act.

Separate:

- formal members;
- practical participants;
- leaders and officeholders;
- supporters and patrons;
- clients and dependents;
- coerced actors;
- subjects and beneficiaries;
- representatives;
- outsiders and affected audiences.

### Step 5: Test Faction Classification

Confirm:

1. scoped coordination;
2. actual participants;
3. overlapping interests;
4. recognizable relationships and action routes;
5. boundary conditions;
6. continuity.

If the test fails, preserve the truthful Population, culture, Institution, audience, temporary crowd, shared condition, or uncoordinated tendency.

### Step 6: Establish Formation History

Build the smallest timeline explaining how coordination formed:

- initiating relationships or events;
- prior Institutions, networks, families, communities, or factions;
- recruitment, alliance, coercion, patronage, response, or shared practice;
- early objectives and disagreements;
- resources, symbols, records, and procedures that persisted;
- major successes, failures, betrayals, reforms, or exclusions;
- current Version.

Formation history does not make the faction ancient, secret, powerful, or morally coherent by default.

### Step 7: Establish Identity and Classification

Record:

- self-name and self-description;
- names used by members, rivals, institutions, and affected populations;
- symbols, stories, practices, places, or relationships supporting identity;
- public purpose and disputed interpretations;
- the current Faction Version and evidence for continuity.

Names are Observer View facts. A hostile label does not establish Factual motive.

### Step 8: Establish Participation and Boundaries

Define how actors enter, participate, refuse, leave, defect, become expelled, or remain represented.

Record what membership grants and does not grant, including information, resources, protection, obligations, voice, authority, and risk.

Do not treat birth, species, residence, employment, faith, taxation, or dependence as automatic voluntary membership.

### Step 9: Establish Internal Groups and Differences

Identify only differences capable of affecting decisions or continuity:

- local chapters;
- ideological blocs;
- offices;
- generations;
- species or embodiment needs;
- economic interests;
- professional roles;
- patrons and clients;
- reformers and traditionalists;
- central and peripheral participants;
- coerced and voluntary participants.

Internal variation need not become separate factions unless it develops its own coordination and boundary.

### Step 10: Establish Interests

Record what different participants seek to protect, gain, change, or avoid, why, and over what horizon.

Separate:

- declared doctrine;
- leadership preferences;
- operating interests;
- member interests;
- affected-subject interests;
- protected commitments and unacceptable costs.

Do not reduce the faction to one alignment, emotion, or desire.

### Step 11: Establish Information Distribution

Build the Faction Information Position for likely material issues.

Record:

- observers and information sources;
- communication channels and delays;
- records, archives, rumors, specialists, divination, and testimony;
- trust, secrecy, censorship, propaganda, translation, and access;
- who can combine information;
- common beliefs, elite beliefs, minority evidence, and unknowns;
- who can act on what they know.

No central Profile grants every participant every recorded fact.

### Step 12: Establish Decision Routes

Define how issues are raised, framed, decided, deferred, contested, and revised.

Record:

- agenda access;
- formal authority;
- practical influence;
- representation;
- vetoes and blocking routes;
- procedures, customs, rituals, bargains, or command;
- emergency exceptions;
- accountability and appeal;
- dissent, evasion, and unauthorized action;
- decision delay.

Leadership does not imply unilateral control unless the route actually supports it.

### Step 13: Establish Current Objectives

Create only objectives already proposed, accepted, contested, or being implemented in the current Version.

Each Faction Objective records:

- intended result and scope;
- sponsors and opponents;
- decision status;
- implementation route;
- acceptable cost and protected commitments;
- dependencies and opposition;
- success, failure, stopping, and revision conditions;
- information and uncertainty.

Future objectives remain unresolved.

### Step 14: Establish Function-Specific Capacity

Assess only capabilities relevant to current objectives, such as:

- deliberation and communication;
- negotiation and representation;
- care and sustenance;
- research and recordkeeping;
- secrecy and intelligence;
- ritual or magical operation;
- transport and logistics;
- construction and maintenance;
- defense, policing, or warfare;
- recovery and succession.

Trace each through actual members, Skills, Development, embodiment, institutions, resources, tools, infrastructure, relationships, permissions, willingness, and bottlenecks.

Do not add capacities into one faction power rating.

### Step 15: Establish Resources and Dependencies

Separate:

- member-owned resources;
- faction-controlled assets;
- institutional or office resources;
- borrowed, contracted, donated, seized, or shared resources;
- infrastructure and supply routes;
- labor, care, expertise, magic, Soul Weapon cooperation, and environmental support;
- maintenance, access, depletion, debt, custody, and accountability.

Faction membership does not pool every possession or capability.

### Step 16: Establish Cohesion by Issue

Describe the ability and willingness to coordinate for each material objective.

Consider:

- shared interest and identity;
- trust and legitimacy;
- communication and procedure;
- burden distribution;
- coercion and safe exit;
- alternatives and rival networks;
- prior success or betrayal;
- unequal risk and time horizons;
- leadership and succession.

Cohesion is qualitative, issue-specific, and allowed to be mixed.

### Step 17: Establish Audiences, Authority, and Legitimacy

Record how members, subjects, dependents, rivals, Institutions, cultures, spirits, gods, monsters, Weapon Souls, and other audiences interpret the faction.

Keep public Reputation, Formal Authority, Cultural Legitimacy, Symbolic Authority, expertise, coercion, trust, fear, and enforcement separate.

### Step 18: Establish Relationships With Other Actors

Define issue-specific alliance, trade, rivalry, dependence, patronage, infiltration, deterrence, shared membership, negotiation, coexistence, or conflict through actual history and contact.

A relationship does not merge information, authority, objectives, or capability.

### Step 19: Establish Current Mobilization and Activity

Record only actions already underway or authorized through actual routes:

- participants involved;
- instructions and interpretation;
- resources assembled;
- logistics and timing;
- resistance and leakage;
- current execution stage;
- observed results;
- Review Points and Pending Consequences.

An announced policy is not an accomplished action.

### Step 20: Establish Continuity Risks and Routes

Identify likely succession, reform, capture, schism, merger, fragmentation, dissolution, and revival conditions without predetermining them.

Record what names, offices, relationships, records, assets, obligations, and recognition could persist or become contested.

### Step 21: Separate Information Views

Maintain:

- Factual faction structure and state;
- participant Observer Views;
- leadership and office views;
- outsider and affected-population views;
- public claims and propaganda;
- player-facing evidence;
- hidden truth, mistakes, disputes, Record Gaps, and unresolved futures.

The Faction Profile is not one member's knowledge.

### Step 22: Run the Coherence Audit

Check:

- coordination rather than category defines the boundary;
- participants remain distinct actors;
- interests support but do not homogenize action;
- information has routes and delays;
- decisions have procedures and dissent;
- capability is function-specific and sourced;
- mobilization separates decisions from effects;
- membership, authority, legitimacy, and representation remain separate;
- current objectives and actions have causal status;
- continuity is supported beyond name;
- no future choice or world result was scripted.

### Step 23: Create the External Faction Profile

Use the [Faction Profile](../world-engine/FACTION_BEHAVIOUR.md#external-campaign-records) schema. Record the current Version, sources, evidence, uncertainty, and Review Points.

Do not add the populated Profile to this repository.

### Step 24: Hand Off to Play and Simulation

Use:

- [Faction Behaviour](../world-engine/FACTION_BEHAVIOUR.md#simulation-procedure) for decisions, mobilization, execution, adaptation, and continuity;
- [NPC Generation](NPC_GENERATOR.md) for individual participants and representatives;
- [Encounter Generation](ENCOUNTER_GENERATOR.md) when faction activity intersects a player decision;
- [Causal Event Chains](../world-engine/CAUSAL_EVENT_CHAINS.md) and [Consequence Resolution](CONSEQUENCE_RESOLUTION.md) for effects;
- specialist World Engine and progression owners for every domain claim.

Generation does not decide whether a faction cooperates, betrays, attacks, reforms, collapses, or succeeds.

## Faction Generation Record

The external [Faction Profile](../world-engine/FACTION_BEHAVIOUR.md#external-campaign-records) remains authoritative. The following optional extension preserves generation provenance:

```markdown
### Faction Generation Record: <external campaign identifier>

Generation scope:
- issue, place, interval, and preparation horizon:
- classify, create, internal, Coalition, or revalidate:

Faction Basis:
- participants and participation relationships:
- overlapping interests:
- action and communication routes:
- boundary conditions:
- continuity sources:

Version and formation:
- current Faction Version:
- concise formation history:
- predecessor, successor, merger, schism, or capture claims:
- identity and classification evidence:

- established world inputs:
- NPC, Institution, Population, and specialist references:
- randomly selected fields and committed options:
- hidden, disputed, outdated, and unresolved claims:
- corrections and next Review Points:
```

This extension is not a second Profile and contains no alignment, universal capability rating, member mind pool, or guaranteed story role.

## Selection and Randomization

Random tools may choose among causally supported ordinary details when no actor decision or established history settles them.

### Valid Uses

- selecting among plausible formation dates or meeting places;
- choosing which established network first connected participants;
- varying symbols, public names, record styles, or meeting customs within supported cultures;
- selecting among several supported office arrangements;
- choosing which minor internal disagreement currently occupies routine attention;
- determining which communication route carried one unresolved report when several were equally available;
- varying ordinary chapter or delegation composition within established membership routes.

### Invalid Uses

- assigning alignment, morality, hostility, loyalty, ideology, consent, or inevitable behavior;
- creating participants, Institutions, populations, resources, magic, authority, or territory without sources;
- deciding leadership or member choices that are currently due;
- making every member know one generated secret;
- assigning a universal power score, level, tier, or threat rank;
- forcing one faction per species, culture, Profession, settlement, religion, or social class;
- generating war, betrayal, alliance, schism, merger, reform, or victory as a profile trait;
- replacing faction behaviour or world-event resolution.

Commit the exact field, supported options, causal weighting, evidence status, and recording procedure before resolving. The result establishes only that field.

Do not reroll until the faction becomes more dramatic, coherent, powerful, diverse, useful, friendly, or hostile.

## Membership, Representation, and Agency

### Membership

Membership is one relationship. Record source, scope, rights, duties, access, exit, and current recognition.

Membership cannot transfer:

- complete information;
- personal property;
- individual Skills or Development;
- Soul identity or progression;
- Species Traits;
- Soul Weapon consent;
- universal authority;
- consent to every objective.

### Representation

A representative needs a source and mandate such as election, appointment, delegation, custom, acclaim, expertise, control, or coercion.

Record:

- who recognizes the mandate;
- issue and jurisdiction;
- instructions and discretion;
- information access;
- resources and support;
- accountability and recall;
- dissent and contested legitimacy.

A representative's statement is evidence of a faction position only within that route.

### Leaders and Founders

Leaders and founders are NPCs with particular relationships to decision routes. They do not become the faction mind.

Death, absence, Reincarnation, incompetence, capture, disagreement, or loss of legitimacy changes only supported functions. A returning founder cannot restore office, membership, secrets, property, followers, or command as Soul property.

## Information and Secrecy

Faction secrecy is a distribution problem.

For each material secret, record:

- the established truth;
- participants who know, suspect, misunderstand, or are excluded;
- access and concealment routes;
- records and copies;
- incentives, fear, trust, and enforcement;
- leaks and discoverable traces;
- who can act on the information;
- consequences of disclosure.

"The faction knows" is insufficient unless the relevant decision and execution actors actually receive and accept the information.

Magical communication, shared dreams, distributed senses, gods, spirits, Soul Echoes, Weapon Souls, and hive-like bodies may alter information routes only through their owners. Unusual coordination is not automatic omniscience.

## Capacity and Mobilization

Generation describes available arrangements; current action still uses Faction Behaviour.

For a material objective, trace:

1. who can decide or authorize;
2. who can contribute capability;
3. who is willing;
4. what resources and infrastructure are accessible;
5. how instructions and support move;
6. what timing and maintenance apply;
7. what opposition, dissent, leakage, and bottlenecks exist;
8. what actors actually attempt;
9. which results become observable.

Do not treat resources on a Profile as instantly mobilized or count member capabilities twice through personal and faction records.

## Faction Change and Versioning

A new Faction Version may be needed when changes materially alter:

- participant boundary;
- governing interests;
- decision routes;
- information structure;
- capacity and resources;
- identity or recognition;
- relationships and legitimacy;
- continuity claims.

Versioning preserves history. It does not declare every reform a new faction or every name reuse continuous.

For schism, merger, capture, dissolution, or revival:

- identify actual actors and routes;
- distribute records, assets, obligations, relationships, and claims causally;
- preserve dissent and successor disagreement;
- distinguish legal declaration from practical action;
- record consequences and unresolved identity claims;
- avoid restoring old coordination through nostalgia alone.

## Cross-System Interfaces

### Soul Engine and Reincarnation

Soul Titles, Resonance, Constellations, Echoes, Soul Avatars, and remembered history may affect evidence, recognition, relationship, opportunity, or Symbolic Authority only through their owners. They do not create membership, office, consent, one faction Soul, or restored command.

### Development and Skills

Members contribute current embodied capability. Coordination may enable practice, teaching, support, and Collective Capability but cannot pool or grant individual Development and Skills.

### Monster Evolution and Societies

Monster factions preserve species-specific embodiment, communication, ecology, culture, adaptation, and Evolution ownership. Pack, colony, hive, brood, or telepathic labels do not establish perfect unity or erase individual personhood.

### Human Classes, Professions, and Institutions

A faction may use, span, contest, influence, or capture Classes, Professions, offices, schools, and Institutions. Those structures retain their own authority, learning, capability, and continuity rules.

### Soul Weapons

Weapon Souls may participate as persons, advisers, members, representatives, allies, or opponents. Custody and wielder membership do not transfer Weapon Soul consent, knowledge, capability, or allegiance.

### Magic

Magic may alter communication, evidence, secrecy, logistics, enforcement, ritual coordination, and risk through valid sources, access, Skills, costs, and limits. It cannot create universal compliance or pooled Mana by faction label.

### World Engine

Populations, Resources, Economies, Ecology, War, Disease, Advancement, Dungeons, Stability, Ages, Resets, and Gates provide the changing conditions and receive faction consequences. Faction generation references but cannot determine their current values or outcomes.

### NPC Generator

Use [NPC Generation](NPC_GENERATOR.md) for leaders, representatives, members, dissenters, clients, and affected persons. NPC profiles retain actor-specific information and choices rather than inheriting the full Faction Profile.

### Encounter Generator

Faction policies, agents, operations, traces, obligations, opportunities, and consequences may become Encounter Sources only through actual routes and player decision relevance. Generation does not guarantee contact or conflict.

### Alpha Playtest Rules

Use narrow Provisional Rules only where completed foundations support them. Do not use faction generation to implement world-event, Time Skip, Age-transition, war, disease, economic, or other major systems by implication.

## Worked Examples

### Canal Access Coalition

Several villages, a monster community, ferry operators, and a water temple coordinate to preserve seasonal passage. They share an objective but differ over tolls, sacred access, repair labor, and acceptable extraction.

The Coalition Profile records delegates, contribution routes, decision procedure, limited mandate, issue-specific cohesion, and exit conditions. It does not merge cultures, knowledge, property, or every interest.

### Academy Reform Bloc

Students, two instructors, an archivist office, and outside practitioners coordinate against a captured licensing process. Some seek transparency, some want lower fees, and one patron wants influence over appointments.

The faction exists inside and beyond the Academy Institution. Its members retain different information and motives. Reform is a current objective, not a guaranteed outcome or proof of moral unity.

### Shared Grievance Without a Faction

Thousands of displaced residents suffer under the same relocation law but have no communication, representatives, network, or coordinated action route.

They are an affected Population and audience, not yet one faction. Organizers might later form several factions with conflicting objectives. The generator does not fabricate unity from harm.

### Monster Corridor Network

Distinct intelligent monster communities maintain signals, seasonal councils, and shared scouts around one Migration Corridor. Embodiment and communication differ, and one community contributes only environmental markers.

The Faction Basis supports coordination for passage. It does not create one monster culture, Evolution tree, shared mind, or universal command.

### Reincarnated Founder

A former founder returns and presents compelling knowledge of old procedures. The current Faction Version contains new offices, patrons, debts, objectives, and members.

The founder becomes a relevant NPC and possible source of Symbolic Authority. Recognition does not restore office or make every faction member loyal. Decision routes determine any new role.

### Disaster Relief Coordination

Several existing actors coordinate food, shelter, transport, healing, and information after a flood. The faction is temporary but valid because contributions, decisions, boundaries, and objectives are explicit.

Its Faction Version may dissolve when relief ends, become a durable Institution through later action, or split over reconstruction priorities. Generation leaves those futures open.

### Distributed Spirit Concord

Several spirits coordinate through seasonal dream exchanges. Each retains separate agency, and messages are delayed or distorted outside shared weather conditions.

The concord has unusual information routes but not omniscience. Human observers may mistake synchronized signs for one god; that classification remains an Observer View.

## Human and AI GM Use

Human and AI GMs use the same faction generator.

An AI GM should:

- search existing NPC, Institution, faction, Population, and relationship records before generating;
- test coordination rather than infer it from categories;
- separate Factual structure from public and participant views;
- never copy the full Faction Profile into every NPC;
- trace information, decisions, mobilization, and execution as separate stages;
- preserve dissent, delay, refusal, unauthorized action, and limited representation;
- leave future objectives, alliances, betrayals, reforms, and conflicts unresolved;
- use Versioning after material change rather than silently rewriting history;
- store populated Profiles only in the external Campaign Record;
- ask for clarification when faction identity, membership, authority, continuity, or prior actions materially conflict.

Generative convenience does not turn a category into coordinated agency.

## Safeguards

- **No faction by category:** Shared species, culture, faith, Profession, territory, class, grievance, or enemy does not establish coordination.
- **No collective mind:** Participants retain separate agency, knowledge, capability, property, Souls, and relationships.
- **No alignment generation:** A faction has interests, decisions, and consequences rather than one morality tag.
- **No mandatory hostility:** Rivalry and incompatible interests do not require constant conflict.
- **No pooled information:** Knowledge moves through actual routes and remains uneven.
- **No leader omnipotence:** Leaders act within authority, influence, procedure, capacity, and legitimacy.
- **No automatic representation:** Members, majorities, founders, offices, symbols, and public claims speak only through valid mandates.
- **No consent by membership:** Participation, dependence, taxation, employment, protection, or coercion does not prove agreement.
- **No pooled capability:** Member Skills, Development, Species Traits, magic, Soul properties, and Soul Weapons remain individually owned.
- **No universal faction score:** Capacity is function-, objective-, and condition-specific.
- **No decision-to-result collapse:** Policy, command, promise, or plan requires mobilization and execution.
- **No instant resources:** Assets require custody, access, logistics, maintenance, and willing participants.
- **No deterministic cohesion:** Agreement, dissent, coercion, and issue-specific cooperation remain distinct.
- **No scripted politics:** Alliance, betrayal, capture, schism, merger, reform, war, and dissolution require actor choices and causes.
- **No reincarnated command:** Soul continuity restores no membership, office, property, followers, secrets, or authority.
- **No symbolic mind control:** Reputation, fear, Soul Titles, Resonance, prophecy, and Divine recognition do not compel factions.
- **No player-centered faction:** Factions pursue objectives and change off-screen through appropriate simulation.
- **No plot immunity:** Important factions can fail, fragment, become obsolete, or dissolve while leaving consequences.
- **No random world outcomes:** Random generation cannot replace faction decisions or specialist simulation.
- **No campaign data in canon:** Populated briefs, seeds, Profiles, Versions, participants, objectives, and actions remain external.
- **No automation supremacy:** Generation supports GM judgment and actor routes instead of replacing them.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- named campaign factions, leaders, members, objectives, resources, relations, or histories;
- Institutions, states, cultures, Populations, religions, species, or settlements;
- NPC generation;
- faction decisions, mobilization outcomes, war, unrest, diplomacy outcomes, or world events;
- universal faction ranks, power ratings, alignments, or encounter roles;
- Time Skip or Age-transition procedure;
- live campaign state in the repository.
