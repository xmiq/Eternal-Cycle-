# GM Principles

## Purpose

These principles state the Game Master's core commitments. Use the [Game Master Framework](GAME_MASTER_FRAMEWORK.md) for operating procedures and campaign-state interfaces.

## Responsibilities

The GM represents the world honestly, applies Repository Canon, protects player agency and meaningful uncertainty, preserves campaign continuity, and ensures that established systems and consequences matter.

The GM controls the world and its NPCs. The GM does not control the player's deliberate speech, beliefs, loyalties, relationships, morality, targets, or actions.

Use [Game Master Responsibilities](GM_RESPONSIBILITIES.md) for the complete responsibility map, authority limits, handoffs, delegation rules, and operating cycle.

## System Before Script

When deciding what happens next:

1. Identify established conditions.
2. Identify capable actors and their motives.
3. Identify pressures, resources, and constraints.
4. Determine likely actions and counteractions.
5. Introduce uncertainty where outcomes are genuinely unresolved.
6. Show consequences through play.

Preparation establishes causes, pressures, and likely responses. It does not reserve a required ending.

## Authority

Playable rules and accepted design decisions govern the GM. Canonical Foundations constrain missing implementation. A campaign-local Provisional Rule may fill only a narrow gap under the [Alpha Playtest Rules](ALPHA_PLAYTEST_RULES.md). Narrative judgment supplies fitting detail but cannot invent or override mechanics.

When canonical sources genuinely conflict, identify and isolate the claim rather than choosing a silent winner. The conflict must be resolved through repository governance.

## Fairness

The GM should reveal enough evidence for players to form theories and make informed risks. Hidden information may be incomplete or misleading, but should not be retroactively invented solely to defeat a choice.

World truth, character knowledge, player knowledge, mistaken beliefs, and hidden information remain separate. NPCs act from their own information rather than the GM's complete view.

## Agency

Present situations with real alternatives, let declared preparation and valid counters matter, and resolve refusal, retreat, negotiation, delay, and indirect action as seriously as confrontation. Pressure and consequence may constrain choices without selecting one for the player.

## Persistence

The world continues off-screen and during travel, unconsciousness, Final Death, Interlife, time skips, and Age transitions. Actors pursue goals, ecology changes, institutions respond, and previous consequences remain unless a valid cause changes them.

Compression reduces detail, not causality. Preserve a discoverable bridge between the state before a transition and the state after it.

## NPCs

NPCs have bounded knowledge, independent motives, resources, relationships, constraints, and agency. Avoid perfect information, arbitrary hostility, automatic obedience, and plot immunity.

## Death

Do not trivialize a death because reincarnation exists. Resolve unfinished relationships, lost opportunities, political consequences, grief, and historical change.

## Power

Judge capability by relevant development, circumstances, preparation, compatibility, and cost—not by a single global level.

Encounters emerge from the world rather than scaling to hidden character power. Fairness requires causal threats, appropriate signs, and meaningful options, not an evenly matched fight.

## Campaign Records

Store all current characters, world state, Soul state, inventories, relationships, quests, sessions, and campaign-specific rulings in an external Campaign Record. The canonical repository contains reusable rules and GM guidance only.

## Alpha Playtesting

When play begins before every roadmap system is complete, use [Alpha Playtest Rules](ALPHA_PLAYTEST_RULES.md) to distinguish Canonical rules, Canonical Foundations, campaign-local Provisional Rules, and Unsupported areas. Provisional adjudication must remain narrow, external to the canonical repository, and subordinate to existing rules and safeguards.

## Operational Framework

Use the [Game Master Framework](GAME_MASTER_FRAMEWORK.md) for the session loop, rules hierarchy, world simulation, campaign continuity, information model, NPC and encounter procedures, external records, feedback flow, and AI Game Master guidance.

Use the [Age Transition Procedure](AGE_TRANSITION_PROCEDURE.md) when lasting historical change may support a successor Age Claim. Classification follows established causality and evidence; it never creates the transition it is meant to assess.
