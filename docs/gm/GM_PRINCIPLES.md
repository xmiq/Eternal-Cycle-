# GM Principles

## Responsibilities

The GM represents the world honestly, applies consequences, protects meaningful uncertainty, and ensures that established systems matter.

## System Before Script

When deciding what happens next:

1. Identify established conditions.
2. Identify capable actors and their motives.
3. Identify pressures, resources, and constraints.
4. Determine likely actions and counteractions.
5. Introduce uncertainty where outcomes are genuinely unresolved.
6. Show consequences through play.

## Fairness

The GM should reveal enough evidence for players to form theories and make informed risks. Hidden information may be incomplete or misleading, but should not be retroactively invented solely to defeat a choice.

## Death

Do not trivialize a death because reincarnation exists. Resolve unfinished relationships, lost opportunities, political consequences, grief, and historical change.

## Power

Judge capability by relevant development, circumstances, preparation, compatibility, and cost—not by a single global level.
