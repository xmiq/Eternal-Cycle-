# Reincarnation Generation

## Purpose

This document defines how a Game Master generates, validates, presents, and revalidates incarnation candidates for step 5 of the canonical [Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) transition.

It is a candidate-generation tool, not an independent Reincarnation system. It does not determine Final Death, perform Life Reconciliation, set Interlife awareness, select a Reincarnation Mode, embody the soul, grant starting memory, or decide retained progression. Those claims remain with their existing owners.

## Core Rule

**Generate candidates from real embodiment opportunities in the world after Interlife, then filter them through reachability, viability, soul compatibility, earned access, causal placement, personhood, and the active Reincarnation Mode.**

A Reincarnation Candidate is not a reward package, character build, species unlock menu, or promise of comfort. It is one specific valid route by which the continuing soul could become a new current incarnation.

The generator proposes only candidates that survive these owners. It cannot make an invalid body valid by labeling it rare, thematic, balanced, or random.

## Preconditions

Begin candidate generation only after the transition has established:

1. one completed Final Death;
2. severance of the former incarnation;
3. completed Life Reconciliation within the current scope;
4. the relevant Interlife interval or target embodiment window;
5. current world truth at that time;
6. the active Reincarnation Mode;
7. soul-persistent conditions that can affect compatibility or access;
8. any applicable domain reachability.

If Final Death remains uncertain, a revival route is still active, Life Reconciliation is incomplete, or another incarnation remains active, stop. Candidate generation cannot settle those questions by implication.

## Candidate Model

### Embodiment Opportunity

An **Embodiment Opportunity** is a world-side process capable of producing a new viable life or person-like embodiment at a particular time and place.

Possible routes include:

- birth within a living lineage;
- hatching from a viable egg;
- spawning, budding, division, seeding, or colony differentiation;
- metamorphic emergence where the new person begins under established species rules;
- spirit condensation, elemental formation, or local manifestation under an actual world law;
- construction, cultivation, inscription, assembly, or awakening of a person-capable body;
- another species-appropriate route already supported by canon and campaign truth.

An opportunity is not yet a candidate. It may fail reachability, soul compatibility, earned access, timing, personhood, or placement tests.

### Reincarnation Candidate

A **Reincarnation Candidate** is one Embodiment Opportunity that has passed every currently applicable eligibility test for the specific soul and transition.

It must identify:

- embodiment route and provenance;
- expected species, form, lineage, or body type;
- starting life stage and developmental history;
- time and location;
- ecological and material conditions;
- social, kin, institutional, or solitary context where applicable;
- ordinary innate capabilities, needs, and limitations known at generation time;
- candidate-affecting rare or exceptional requirements;
- soul and world compatibility findings;
- relevant uncertainty and hidden properties;
- dependencies that require revalidation before embodiment.

### Candidate Seed

A **Candidate Seed** is an unvalidated draft built from one Embodiment Opportunity. It lets the GM explore variety without treating every idea as eligible.

Candidate Seeds may be numerous, discarded silently during preparation, or retained as world possibilities. Only passed candidates may enter selection.

### Candidate Pool

The **Candidate Pool** is the bounded set of currently valid Reincarnation Candidates considered under one transition and Reincarnation Mode.

It is not every possible body in existence. Its boundary follows:

- the target time and reachable domains;
- actual Embodiment Opportunities;
- the specific soul's compatibility and earned access;
- current campaign focus and agreed mode;
- reasonable preparation scope.

The pool need not have a fixed size. Emergent mode may require one candidate. Constrained Choice requires enough meaningful alternatives for the promised choice. Directed mode may search narrowly around a valid request.

### Candidate Profile

A **Candidate Profile** is the external record used to evaluate and, when appropriate, present one candidate. It separates established facts, player-facing facts, hidden facts, uncertainty, dependencies, and selection status.

Profiles are campaign state and never belong in this repository.

## Embodiment Sources

### Living Lineages

For birth, hatching, spawning, or similar routes, establish:

- viable progenitors or reproductive process;
- lineage and variation supported by the population;
- timing and developmental environment;
- care, abandonment, parasitism, nesting, brood, colony, or other actual social ecology;
- hazards and dependencies affecting survival;
- whether a new person or distributed embodiment begins at this stage.

Population presence alone does not guarantee an available candidate. Fertility, life cycle, season, scarcity, policy, disease, migration, and local conditions may matter.

### Asexual, Colonial, and Distributed Life

Do not force human assumptions onto budding, fission, swarms, fungal networks, colonies, animated ecosystems, or distributed persons.

Establish:

- whether the route creates a new person, extends an existing person, divides one continuity, or creates a component;
- how identity and personhood work locally;
- what counts as one body or embodiment;
- whether another Soul is already present;
- what consent or transition rules apply.

Reincarnation cannot take over a mere component while pretending it is an independent incarnation.

### Constructed and Awakened Bodies

A constructed candidate needs:

- actual designers, builders, sources, materials, tools, and process;
- a body capable of personhood under established rules;
- a valid point at which the new incarnation begins;
- no existing person displaced by the placement;
- current maintenance, environment, ownership claims, and social consequences;
- compatibility with Soul and Magic rules.

An empty shell, enchanted object, automaton, Soul Weapon Vessel, or Dungeon Core is not a candidate merely because it resembles a body. Existing personhood and system ownership remain decisive.

### Spirits and Nonmaterial Embodiments

A spirit, elemental, dream-being, field intelligence, or other nonordinary form requires an established formation law and an actual bounded embodiment.

The candidate must still have:

- a beginning route;
- scope and boundaries;
- current access and needs;
- relations to place, source, Mana, belief, host, or environment;
- vulnerability and consequence;
- compatibility with one-active-incarnation continuity.

“Disembodied” does not mean unlimited, omnipresent, or exempt from embodiment.

### Rare, Ancient, Hybrid, and Unique Forms

Exceptional labels do not establish candidates.

Check:

- whether the form still exists or can currently be formed;
- whether it is a species, stage, Evolution, Mutation, Hybridization outcome, title, class, office, or unique person;
- whether the soul has earned any required access;
- whether the route begins at an appropriate stage;
- whether uniqueness means no second embodiment can exist;
- which costs, dependencies, incompatibilities, or world responses apply.

A soul that once became an apex monster does not automatically reincarnate at apex stage. Prior history may influence eligibility or relearning, but the new embodiment needs its own valid route.

## Personhood and Occupancy

### No Default Displacement

Reincarnation normally enters a newly beginning embodiment. It does not overwrite, evict, consume, merge with, suppress, or secretly replace an existing person or Soul.

Any exception requires an explicit canonical mechanism defining:

- every participant and their continuity;
- knowledge and consent;
- refusal and withdrawal where possible;
- body and Soul ownership;
- memory and agency;
- failure, separation, harm, and consequence;
- one-active-incarnation compliance.

The generator cannot create such a mechanism provisionally merely to make a desired body available.

### Apparent Vacancy

Unconsciousness, infancy, silence, low intelligence, incomplete awakening, magical suppression, coma, dormancy, or unfamiliar communication does not prove that a body is unoccupied.

Establish personhood and Soul state through valid evidence before treating a body as available.

### Bodies Made for the Soul

A culture, god, spirit, institution, or machine may deliberately prepare an embodiment for a known Reincarnation only through actual capability, information, materials, authority, consent, time, and compatibility.

Preparation does not guarantee arrival, ownership, obedience, memory, or gratitude. The new incarnation remains the current self.

## Candidate Axes

Use these axes to create meaningful variety without treating them as point-buy categories.

### Body and Life Cycle

- size, structure, senses, mobility, metabolism, cognition, and communication;
- infancy, larval, juvenile, mature, cyclical, colonial, or stranger stages;
- lifespan, maturation, metamorphosis, reproduction, renewal, and senescence;
- innate capabilities and current limitations;
- care and environmental needs.

### Ecology and Place

- habitat, climate, Mana conditions, food and resource routes;
- predators, prey, competitors, symbionts, disease, and migration;
- Dungeon, settlement, wilderness, oceanic, subterranean, aerial, planar, or Gate-contact context;
- local pressures and viable support;
- how the birth or formation belongs there.

### Society and Relationship

- kin, brood, pack, hive, community, institution, creator, patron, or solitude;
- culture, language, expectations, danger, care, prejudice, privilege, and obligations;
- whether the species is recognized as person, monster, property, spirit, citizen, threat, or something contested;
- current conflict and faction context without predetermining allegiance.

### Access and Potential

- compatibility with retained Imprints;
- likely ease or difficulty of current access;
- body structures that support or constrain prior methods;
- candidate-specific routes for new development;
- Soul Weapon relationship and possible Vessel expression;
- persistent strain, wound, curse, bond, or title interactions.

These are not starting powers. Actual expression is set after embodiment by its owners.

### Risk and Support

- immediate survival conditions;
- available caregivers, resources, shelter, instruction, or community;
- known hazards and uncertainty;
- social and ecological dependencies;
- room for preparation, refusal, escape, negotiation, or adaptation once play begins.

Danger is not value. A harsher candidate does not grant compensating power unless a valid rule creates it.

### Thematic Direction

Campaign focus may favor monster survival, civilization, exploration, craft, magic, politics, horror, diplomacy, or another agreed direction. Theme narrows search and presentation; it does not bypass eligibility or script the life.

Monster reincarnation is a major project focus. Candidate preparation should regularly include genuinely distinct monster embodiments when valid, without making monster selection mandatory or treating monsters as combat-only bodies.

## Eligibility Passes

Apply the passes in order. A candidate failing one pass is removed or revised before presentation.

### Pass 1: Transition Validity

Confirm the same soul is in Interlife after one Final Death and has no active incarnation or unresolved revival completing in parallel.

### Pass 2: Temporal Validity

Confirm the Embodiment Opportunity exists during the selected window. The world at death and the world after Interlife may differ.

### Pass 3: Domain Reachability

Confirm the opportunity lies within the scope of ordinary Reincarnation law or a valid current Cross-Domain Reincarnation Route.

Seeing, contacting, visiting, or opening a World Gate to a domain does not by itself make it reachable in Interlife.

### Pass 4: World Viability

Confirm that the lineage, production process, ecology, material supply, social context, and placement can actually produce and sustain the starting embodiment long enough for its declared entry point.

Viability need not guarantee safety or adulthood. It must establish a real beginning rather than an unsupported appearance.

### Pass 5: Occupancy and Personhood

Confirm no existing person or Soul is displaced, duplicated, or silently merged. Apply any explicit exception mechanism in full.

### Pass 6: Soul Compatibility

Check current Soul integrity, capacity, persistent bonds, Soul Weapon relationship, wounds, curses, Imprints, Depth, Resonance, Titles, and other relevant conditions.

Compatibility may be partial, conditional, or costly. It does not imply immediate access to everything compatible.

### Pass 7: Earned Access

Check route-specific requirements for rare, ancient, hybrid, constructed, spiritual, or exceptional forms. Prior species experience may support a condition without replacing current world availability.

### Pass 8: Causal Placement

Confirm the candidate has a real history leading to its starting moment. A mature body requires prior development, relationships, resource use, witnesses, and consequences appropriate to that life.

Do not fabricate years of mastery, allegiance, authority, or possessions as a free entry package.

### Pass 9: Mode Compatibility

Confirm the candidate belongs within the active Emergent, Constrained Choice, or Directed search and disclosure rules.

Mode compatibility is applied last because a mode cannot rescue a mechanically invalid candidate.

## Generation Procedure

### Step 1: Freeze the Transition Brief

Record externally:

- completed life and transition identity;
- target time or open Interlife window;
- active Reincarnation Mode;
- reachable domains;
- relevant Soul conditions and candidate influences;
- campaign focus and participant boundaries;
- required degree of player-facing choice;
- facts that must remain hidden.

This **Reincarnation Brief** is not a new Soul state. It is the GM's bounded input to generation.

### Step 2: Advance the World to the Candidate Window

Use the World Engine and Simulation Abstraction to establish current conditions after Interlife. Preserve extinctions, migrations, births, construction, wars, institutions, World Resets, Gate changes, and other causal developments.

Do not select a body from the death-era world and assume it still exists.

### Step 3: Map Reachable Opportunity Sources

Identify actual populations, formation processes, workshops, spirit ecologies, magical sources, institutions, and other routes capable of producing new embodiments.

Map enough scope to satisfy the Reincarnation Mode. Do not enumerate the whole world when a smaller search produces valid variety.

### Step 4: Draft Candidate Seeds

Create Candidate Seeds across meaningfully different combinations of body, ecology, society, access, limitation, and future opportunity.

Begin from world causes, not a list of powers. Ask:

- what life is beginning here;
- why now;
- what produced it;
- what it needs;
- who or what is affected;
- which parts of the former soul fit or strain against it;
- what kind of present-life work it invites without guaranteeing.

### Step 5: Apply Eligibility Passes

Reject or revise every Seed that fails. Record material reasons when they affect hidden rules, Directed requests, future unlocks, or continuity.

Candidate rejection is not punishment. It states that this route is not presently valid.

### Step 6: Build Candidate Profiles

For each surviving candidate, separate:

- established Factual View;
- player-facing description;
- hidden established facts;
- genuinely unresolved conditions;
- relevant dependencies and revalidation triggers;
- known strengths, needs, limits, and starting context;
- excluded assumptions such as inherited mastery or property.

### Step 7: Test Pool Integrity

For Constrained Choice, ask:

- Are the candidates genuinely distinct in lived experience rather than cosmetic variants?
- Is each candidate valid and playable?
- Does any option appear superior only because its costs, needs, stage, or context were omitted?
- Is any candidate a disguised trap whose basic risk the soul or player should know?
- Does the set support campaign focus without forcing one route?
- Are monster forms represented when world and mode support them?

The pool need not be balanced by equal combat power. It should support meaningful, informed differences.

### Step 8: Apply Disclosure Rules

Present only what the active mode and current Soul access permit, while preserving meaningful choice.

The presentation may include:

- broad form or lineage;
- life stage;
- ecological and social setting;
- obvious needs and limitations;
- known compatibility or strain;
- major foreseeable stakes;
- clearly marked uncertainty.

Hidden details need prior existence and fair discoverability. The GM cannot conceal an obvious lethal dependency solely to make a candidate undesirable after selection.

### Step 9: Apply the Reincarnation Mode

- **Emergent:** resolve one candidate through established causes and authorized uncertainty. The player does not choose among a hidden menu.
- **Constrained Choice:** present a bounded valid set and receive the player's selection. Do not include an invalid decoy.
- **Directed:** translate the player's desired direction into search constraints, disclose whether a valid result exists, and present the qualifying candidate or nearest permitted decision point without granting the request by fiat.

The mode determines who chooses among valid possibilities. It does not change eligibility.

### Step 10: Revalidate Immediately Before Embodiment

Recheck every material dependency:

- opportunity still exists;
- time and location remain valid;
- route remains reachable;
- body remains unoccupied;
- world and Soul compatibility remain supported;
- exceptional access remains satisfied;
- selection and consent remain current;
- no competing revival or incarnation completed.

If a selected candidate becomes invalid, explain the transition-level reason at the appropriate information view and return to the active mode using remaining or newly generated valid candidates. Do not force an invalid body or treat a GM correction as an in-world penalty.

### Step 11: Hand Off to Embodiment

Pass the selected candidate to step 7 of Reincarnation with:

- validated embodiment route;
- species, form, stage, and starting history;
- location, time, ecology, and social context;
- established innate capabilities and needs;
- Soul compatibility conditions;
- persistent bonds and conditions requiring expression review;
- player-facing and hidden information views;
- first playable situation.

Reincarnation then owns embodiment and awakening. Development, Skill, Monster Evolution, Human, Magic, and Soul Weapon owners set current access and expression.

## Contextual Candidate Adjudication

The GM generates and selects among valid possibilities through bounded judgement rather than a mandatory weight equation. Relevant inputs may include prior lives and species, Final Death, Soul condition, elapsed Interlife, current world and Age, embodiment opportunities, cosmology, consequences, themes, and established player wishes. Inputs inform the search; they neither create eligibility nor guarantee a candidate.

Use this procedure:

1. determine current Soul and Reincarnation state;
2. establish elapsed Interlife and relevant world changes;
3. identify valid Embodiment Opportunities and domain reachability;
4. consider established consequences and player wishes;
5. consider known or contacted worlds only where a valid route makes them relevant;
6. generate candidates through GM judgement within the active Reincarnation Mode;
7. verify every candidate against Canon, compatibility, world state, exceptional unlocks, and prerequisites;
8. present and revalidate candidates through the ordinary procedure.

The GM cannot use discretion as arbitrary reward, punishment, or contradiction of Canon. No reusable formula maps a chosen death circumstance to a guaranteed species or destination.

### Player Preference

A request for a particular species is meaningful input. If that species has no valid body, route, compatibility, or world presence, the request cannot create one. The GM may present a valid related possibility, wait where the mode permits it, or adjudicate another valid candidate.

### Final Death Context

Dying while defending a volcanic settlement may leave consequences relevant to an ember-associated opportunity, local relationships, or Soul Resonance. It does not invoke a universal rule that weights fire species or guarantees an ember incarnation.

## Reincarnation Modes in Detail

### Emergent Mode

Emergent mode emphasizes discovery and world causality.

Safeguards:

- selection follows a committed causal or uncertainty method;
- the GM cannot choose a punitive body because the player died inconveniently;
- the result remains valid and playable;
- hidden rules are established before use;
- thematic focus may weight search without guaranteeing power or suffering.

### Constrained Choice

Constrained Choice emphasizes informed agency among limited possibilities.

Safeguards:

- every displayed candidate is eligible;
- omitted material facts do not turn one option into a gotcha;
- the player may compare lived implications, not universal ratings;
- selection is final after valid embodiment begins;
- declining all options does not automatically create new ones unless the mode permits deferral, redirection, or another genuine transition cost.

### Directed Mode

Directed mode permits a requested direction, known form, lineage, habitat, role, or bounded quality.

The request may:

- narrow search;
- activate an earned route;
- express a preference among valid candidates;
- justify waiting for a future opportunity when the mode and costs allow.

It cannot:

- create an extinct or nonexistent lineage;
- skip a species stage or Evolution;
- demand another person's body;
- import a prior identity, class, office, property, inventory, or authority;
- guarantee optimal compatibility;
- waive world contact or domain reachability;
- reopen selection after embodiment.

## Rerolls, Rejection, and Delay

### No Candidate Farming

Repeated death, self-destruction, short lives, or deliberate rejection does not expand the pool, improve rarity, or accumulate selection currency by itself.

### Invalid Candidate Correction

Removing a candidate that was never valid is a correction, not a reroll. Restore the mode's promised agency without charging an in-world cost for the GM's error.

### Player Rejection

Rejection exists only where the active mode or another rule allows it. It may cause continued Interlife, a narrowed search, a later opportunity window, or a meaningful transition consequence if established in advance.

It never permits unlimited unseen rerolls until an optimal body appears.

### No Immediate Candidate

If no candidate is valid now, Interlife continues or another established Reincarnation condition acts. The generator does not conjure a body.

Record why the pool is empty and the next review condition, such as:

- a viable birth season;
- completion of a constructed body;
- restoration of domain reachability;
- Soul recovery or compatibility change;
- a relevant world migration;
- an authorized mode change.

Reincarnation's reliability concerns continuity, not immediate scheduling or comfort.

## Starting Context Integrity

### Earliest Playable Stage

The default entry is the earliest species-appropriate stage at which meaningful play can begin. Summarized gestation, incubation, infancy, formation, or dormancy grants no free Development.

### Mature Entry

A mature candidate requires a real prior history. Distinguish:

- development actually possessed by the body or new incarnation;
- education and relationships the prior history supports;
- imposed memories or roles, if any valid mechanism exists;
- what the continuing Soul can access;
- which past choices were not made by the current player.

Avoid mature entry when it would require inventing years of player-character deliberate decisions. Use bounded standing intentions, collaborative setup, or a younger playable stage instead.

### First Situation

The first situation should arise from the candidate's actual life:

- hunger, hatching, care, migration, threat, awakening, construction, ceremony, work, social expectation, or discovery;
- current ecological and social pressures;
- immediate perceptions of the new embodiment;
- enough room for the player to establish current-incarnation intent.

Do not begin by forcing a predetermined allegiance, personality, or moral choice.

## Cross-System Interfaces

### Soul Depth, Resonance, Titles, Echoes, and Retained Instincts

These systems may influence compatibility, candidate visibility, Directed scope, memory access, attraction, recognition, or later expression only through their own rules. Candidate generation treats their established outputs as inputs; it grants none of them.

### Development and Stat XP

Persistent Potential may make some bodies easier or harder to develop, but no candidate begins with prior bodily statistics. Retained Stat XP supports future rebuilding under current embodiment and present-life effort.

### Skills

Retained Skills remain Imprints. Candidate anatomy, senses, Mana relations, tools, culture, and practice determine current access and possible translation. Do not rank candidates solely by how many old Skills they can express immediately.

### Monster Evolution

Candidate generation normally selects a starting species form and stage, not a future Evolution. Hidden routes, pressures, Mutations, Hybridization, apex status, and Evolution costs remain future owned processes unless an actual starting form independently qualifies.

### Human Classes and Professions

A human or comparable social candidate does not inherit a Class, Profession, office, licence, school membership, certification, or social position from a prior life. Starting context may offer later access through real institutions.

### Soul Weapons

The Weapon Soul and Bond persist as their owners permit. Candidate generation checks compatibility and starting manifestation conditions but does not turn the weapon into ordinary inventory, force a Vessel form, or guarantee immediate expression.

### Magic

Candidate Mana relations, sources, organs, affinities, environments, and institutions are current-world facts. They may enable later magic while preserving source agency, access, Skill, cost, and embodiment.

### World Engine

Populations, ecology, resources, disease, factions, conflict, advancement, Dungeons, Ages, World Resets, and World Gates create or close Embodiment Opportunities through their own causal rules. They cannot allocate Souls or guarantee candidates by demand alone.

## Worked Examples

### Monster-Focused Constrained Choice

After a long Interlife, the frontier supports three valid opportunities: a cave-spider hatching in a stressed brood, a juvenile marsh mimic budding from a colonial parent, and a newly formed ember spirit near a recovering volcanic shrine.

Each profile explains body structure, life stage, immediate needs, ecology, social context, known Soul compatibility, and major uncertainty. None promises an Evolution or carries old statistics. The player chooses among three distinct lives rather than three combat loadouts.

### Requested Extinct Dragon

A Directed request asks for an ancient dragon species that vanished during the prior Age.

The generator finds no living lineage, viable egg, construction route, formation law, or earned restoration process. Prior dragon experience does not create one. It may report no current candidate, search related valid lineages within the request's permitted breadth, or continue Interlife if the mode allows waiting.

### Constructed Body

An artificer order is building person-capable stone guardians and has a lawful, consensual practice for inviting reincarnating Souls into newly awakened bodies.

A specific unfinished guardian may become a Candidate Seed. It still needs materials, completion timing, soul compatibility, absence of an existing person, domain reachability, and a real starting social context. The order's expectations do not compel the new incarnation's obedience.

### Gate Closure Before Embodiment

A Cross-Domain Reincarnation Route depends on an active World Gate channel. A valid candidate is selected, but the channel closes before embodiment.

Revalidation removes that candidate if no broader route remains. A completed embodiment would persist, but this one has not begun. The active mode determines whether another valid candidate is selected or Interlife continues. The soul is not duplicated or stranded in an illegal half-body.

### Mature Wolf Body Rejected

A GM drafts a mature dire wolf with years of hunting mastery but cannot establish how the current player character made those prior choices or why the body is unoccupied.

The Seed fails causal placement and personhood. The GM may instead use a newborn or juvenile opportunity, or establish a canonical non-displacing mature-formation route with bounded history. Calling the body convenient does not validate it.

### No Immediate Opportunity

A rare aquatic spirit route is compatible and earned, but local formation occurs only during a conjunction expected years later.

The candidate is not current. Directed mode may allow a declared wait, making the conjunction a review condition while the world continues. The generator does not move the conjunction earlier or fabricate another spirit for convenience.

## Safeguards

- Candidate generation begins only at step 5 of one valid Reincarnation transition.
- The generator cannot confirm Final Death, perform Life Reconciliation, choose a mode, or embody a soul.
- Every candidate begins from an actual Embodiment Opportunity.
- Existing persons and Souls are not displaced, overwritten, merged, or suppressed by default.
- Population demand, fertility, institutions, gods, and Gates cannot allocate a Soul by implication.
- A Reincarnation Mode governs selection among valid candidates, never eligibility.
- Candidate variety is qualitative and contextual, not a point-buy balance exercise.
- Monster candidates receive meaningful focus without becoming mandatory or combat-only.
- Rare, ancient, hybrid, unique, apex, or mature forms retain every owned requirement.
- Prior lives influence compatibility and access only through established Soul rules.
- No candidate carries old statistics, inventory, property, office, class, reputation, legal identity, or automatic mastery.
- Candidate rejection and repeated death do not create reroll currency.
- Hidden candidate facts require prior truth and fair disclosure where meaningful choice depends on them.
- Every selected candidate is revalidated before embodiment.
- Completed embodiment cannot be reopened because another candidate later appears.
- Current pools, profiles, selections, and incarnation facts remain outside this repository.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- new species, Evolution trees, Human Classes, Skills, spells, Soul Weapon forms, or world populations;
- a universal candidate count, rarity table, point budget, power score, random table, or optimality metric;
- candidate-specific live campaign content;
- encounter, monster, NPC, Dungeon, faction, or world-event generation;
- Time Skip or Age-transition procedure;
- software implementation or campaign templates.
