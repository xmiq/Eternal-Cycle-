# World-Event Generator

## Purpose

This document defines how a Game Master identifies, prepares, resolves, and records a campaign-local World Event from established causes, current world state, autonomous actors, and supported uncertainty.

It applies the completed [World Engine](../world-engine/README.md). It is not a random-event table, plot-beat generator, prophecy engine, disaster quota, timeline script, or repository for live events.

## Core Rule

Generate the next material occurrence from causes already able to produce it.

A World Event becomes true only when an established process, actor, interaction, threshold, or external input supports the bounded occurrence under its canonical owner. Dramatic usefulness, genre expectation, player absence, elapsed time, symbolic symmetry, and random selection cannot create the missing cause.

## Ownership

This document owns:

- selecting the exact world-event question and preparation scope;
- building and testing a World-Event Basis;
- distinguishing a possible event from one that is due, occurring, established, misidentified, prevented, or still unresolved;
- setting the direct Event Boundary, footprint, timing, evidence, and uncertainty;
- resolving only supported event-generation uncertainty;
- creating an external World-Event Record;
- handing the established event to consequence, chain, domain, Encounter, and simulation owners.

It does not own:

- specialist mechanics that make an event possible or determine its direct effect;
- consequences beyond the direct Event Boundary;
- World-State Claims or live values;
- actor identity, information, decisions, or capability;
- Encounters, quests, scenes, rewards, or player objectives;
- Time Skip or Age-transition procedures;
- World Resets, World Gates, war, disease, economies, ecology, Dungeons, magic, or any other specialist domain;
- campaign history stored in this repository.

## Key Terms

### World Event

A **World Event** is a bounded material occurrence or transition in world truth, established for a stated subject, scope, and time through valid causes and canonical ownership.

It may be sudden or extended, public or hidden, local or widespread, deliberate or emergent. It need not involve the player, create an Encounter, alter an Age, or become historically famous.

### World-Event Generation Brief

A **World-Event Generation Brief** is the external input for one generation pass, stating the event question, relevant scope and horizon, established state and causes, possible receivers, information views, simulation resolution, and canonical limits.

It does not prescribe that an event must occur or what its consequences must become.

### World-Event Basis

A **World-Event Basis** is the established initiating condition or action, receiving subject, valid transition route, enabling and inhibiting conditions, relevant timing, canonical owner, and evidence that together support one possible bounded event.

A Basis supports consideration. It is not proof that an uncertain event occurred.

### World-Event Seed

A **World-Event Seed** is an unvalidated preparation possibility combining a possible Basis, direct transition, footprint, timing, information views, uncertainty, and owner handoffs.

It is not campaign truth until its causes, ownership, event boundary, and resolution are validated.

### World-Event Generation Pass

A **World-Event Generation Pass** is one bounded procedure that identifies and resolves only the event facts needed for a stated Simulation Question and Causal Horizon while preserving autonomous agency, unresolved branches, specialist ownership, and future consequences.

### Event Boundary

The **Event Boundary** states exactly what occurrence or transition becomes true through the generation pass and what remains a possible consequence, actor response, interpretation, or future branch.

The boundary prevents one event result from silently deciding an entire Causal Event Chain.

## Distinguishing Related Structures

| Structure | What it owns | Why it is not the World Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **World-State Claim** | A scoped current condition, relation, or trend | State can persist without one newly resolved occurrence |
| **Process** | Continuing change through time | A process can produce several events and continue after them |
| **Causal Node** | Any established event, action, decision, state change, discovery, or input with possible consequences | World Events are one kind of material Causal Node, not every node |
| **Causal Event Chain** | Connections among causes, responses, delays, branches, and consequences | A World Event is one bounded occurrence within or entering that network |
| **Immediate Outcome** | The bounded result of an attempt or adjudication | An outcome may be personal or local; it becomes a World Event only when materially world-facing within a stated scope |
| **Consequence** | A supported change following an established outcome or event | Consequences require their own links and may become later events |
| **Encounter** | A decision-relevant situation involving player agency | A World Event may occur off-screen or never intersect player choice |
| **Simulation Delta** | The changes produced by one Simulation Pass | A Delta can contain several events, trends, continuities, and unresolved branches |
| **Time Skip** | Narrative compression between established moments | It reveals or summarizes events but cannot cause them |
| **Age Transition** | A causal change in the historical pattern used to distinguish Ages | Most World Events do not alter an Age, and the classification requires its own procedure |

## Generation Scopes

### Identify a Due Event

Review Active Causes, Pending Consequences, thresholds, scheduled actions, arrivals, completions, failures, and Review Points whose requirements may now be met.

The pass may establish the event, find it delayed, find it prevented, redirect it, or leave occurrence unresolved.

### Prepare a Supported Emerging Event

When current pressures or interactions make several near-horizon events possible, prepare the smallest set whose resolution could change a meaningful decision, dependency, or world condition.

Preparation does not make every Seed inevitable.

### Resolve an Actor-Initiated World Event

An autonomous actor may attempt an action with a world-facing direct effect. Resolve the actor's decision and attempt under their owners first, then use this generator to establish the bounded event if the effect occurs.

### Classify a Reported Event

Reports, legends, omens, rumors, records, divinations, and observations may describe an event inaccurately. The generator can separate:

- established occurrence;
- observer interpretation;
- uncertain timing or footprint;
- false attribution;
- several events conflated into one story;
- one event incorrectly divided into several reports;
- no supported event.

### Revalidate a Changing Event

An extended occurrence may change while underway. Revalidate its active causes, receiving subjects, footprint, timing, Counterforces, owner, and Event Boundary without rewriting what already happened.

### Receive an Established External Input

A campaign may receive an event established by an authorized campaign premise, scenario boundary, owner-approved intervention, or compatible external world. Record provenance and direct facts, then apply normal exposure and consequence rules.

An external input is not permission to invent its history, hidden mechanism, future effects, or importance.

## World-Event Basis Requirements

### Established Initiating Input

Identify at least one established condition, action, process, interaction, threshold approach, Pending Consequence, or authorized external input capable of contributing to the event.

The initiating input must already be world truth or be resolved under its owner during this pass. A desired event cannot serve as its own cause.

### Receiving Subject

Identify what can actually undergo the transition:

- body, object, structure, or site;
- person, group, Population, or faction;
- route, habitat, resource, service, or network;
- Institution, Economy, ecology, magical field, or other process;
- bounded region or contact interface.

An abstract theme, genre, moral, prophecy, or campaign mood is not a receiving subject.

### Valid Transition Route

State how the initiating input reaches and changes the receiver. Include required access, exposure, compatibility, information, authority, capability, supply, sequence, or transmission.

Physical proximity alone does not establish social, magical, ecological, informational, or spiritual transmission.

### Enabling and Inhibiting Conditions

Record necessary, contributory, triggering, and inhibiting conditions plus relevant Counterforces.

Counterforces are not merely modifiers to a predetermined result. They may prevent, narrow, delay, redirect, or transform the event.

### Timing

Establish the earliest possible occurrence, expected process interval where knowable, ordering dependencies, and the Review Point at which the question becomes material.

Elapsed time cannot complete an event whose route, resources, participants, maintenance, or other necessary conditions fail.

### Canonical Owner

Name the rule owner that can establish the direct transition. Cross-domain events may require several owners, but each fact is resolved once.

The World-Event Generator coordinates those results; it does not absorb their mechanics.

### Evidence and Uncertainty

Separate:

- established world truth;
- direct observations;
- estimates and forecasts;
- beliefs and interpretations;
- genuinely unresolved occurrence;
- missing or conflicting records.

Ignorance about an established event is not permission to randomize whether it happened. An unresolved event is not secretly established because the GM expects it.

## Candidate Sources

Valid Seeds usually begin from one or more of the following.

### Due Pending Consequences

A delay expires or review condition arrives for a Pending Consequence whose causal requirements remain met.

Recheck those requirements. A forecast is not a scheduled certainty.

### Threshold Crossings

A specialist rule establishes that a scoped threshold condition has been crossed. Resolve only the transition owned by that threshold; do not infer a universal crisis or automatic escalation.

### Autonomous Actor Actions

NPCs, factions, Institutions, spirits, gods, Weapon Souls, monsters, and other actors make choices from their own information and agency. Their resolved attempts can become world-event inputs.

### Continuing Processes

Weather, erosion, reproduction, depletion, recovery, construction, disease, ecological succession, magical currents, infrastructure, and other processes may produce material transitions when their supported conditions mature.

### Causal Convergence

Several pressures or actions may combine to support an event that none could produce alone. Preserve each material contributor and its scope.

### Feedback

A prior consequence may return as an input to an active process. Recheck current stocks, capacity, actors, and Counterforces rather than repeating the previous result automatically.

### Cross-System Interaction

Established outputs from Soul, Development, Skills, Monster Evolution, Human structures, Soul Weapons, Magic, or a World Engine domain may meet a world receiver and create a valid event candidate.

The interaction inherits every source's access, agency, cost, compatibility, and ownership limits.

### Authorized External Input

An owner-approved campaign premise may establish a bounded occurrence. Record exactly which facts arrive established and which remain unresolved.

## Invalid Candidate Sources

Do not generate a World Event because:

- play has been quiet;
- a genre convention expects one;
- a region has not appeared recently;
- the player needs a quest, threat, reward, or motivation;
- a random table says something dramatic occurs;
- a campaign anniversary or round count has arrived;
- a prophecy would be aesthetically satisfying;
- an NPC or faction needs to become relevant;
- a desired Age Transition needs retroactive causes;
- the GM wants to rebalance capability;
- an unused canonical system deserves screen time.

Any of these may prompt the GM to inspect existing causes. None creates a Basis.

## Generation Procedure

### Step 1: State the Event Question

Ask one bounded question, such as:

- Does the damaged reservoir fail before repairs restore safe pressure?
- Which supported transition becomes due when the migration reaches the closed pass?
- What directly occurs when the ritual network loses its final maintained Anchor?
- Did the reported comet strike happen, and what evidence actually survives?

"What exciting thing happens next?" is not a sufficient event question.

### Step 2: Establish the Simulation Frame

Use [Simulation Abstraction](../world-engine/SIMULATION_ABSTRACTION.md) to set:

- scope;
- interval or Causal Horizon;
- resolution;
- subjects and owners;
- Resolution Anchors;
- Material Exceptions;
- Simulation Review Point.

Event magnitude does not determine resolution by itself. Resolve detail where agency, uncertainty, ownership, or distribution can change the result.

### Step 3: Load Established State

Read the external Campaign Record for:

- current World-State Claims;
- relevant histories and legacies;
- Active Causes and Counterforces;
- Pending Consequences and Review Points;
- actor locations, plans, knowledge, and commitments;
- available resources, routes, maintenance, and capacities;
- unresolved branches and record gaps.

Do not fill an absent material fact merely to make a Seed viable.

### Step 4: Collect Candidate Sources

List only sources capable of becoming material within the current Frame and horizon.

Include quiet outcomes. Maintenance completed, a pressure contained, an expected arrival delayed, a rumor disproved, or a route remaining closed can be more causally important than a spectacle.

### Step 5: Form World-Event Seeds

For each material possibility, state:

- initiating input;
- receiving subject;
- direct transition;
- transition route;
- timing;
- possible footprint;
- canonical owner;
- evidence and uncertainty;
- conditions that would prevent or redirect it.

Do not generate consequences, scenes, rewards, or historical importance as Seed properties.

### Step 6: Test the World-Event Basis

Reject or revise a Seed if it lacks:

- an established initiating input;
- a valid receiver;
- a supported route;
- required conditions;
- plausible ordering and timing;
- an owner able to establish the transition;
- a resolvable evidence or uncertainty status.

Failure to validate is a useful result. Preserve the real pressure, failed route, or unresolved question.

### Step 7: Separate Existing Truth From Unresolved Occurrence

Classify the candidate:

- **established:** the event already occurred under valid authority;
- **occurring:** its direct process is underway and current facts are known;
- **due:** requirements appear met and the occurrence must now be resolved;
- **supported:** occurrence remains possible but not yet due;
- **stalled:** a required route or condition is absent;
- **prevented:** a Counterforce has closed the supported occurrence within the relevant scope;
- **redirected:** the same pressure now supports a different event;
- **misidentified:** evidence refers to a different occurrence or no supported event;
- **obscured:** available world truth or records are insufficient for classification.

These are preparation findings, not universal event ranks.

### Step 8: Identify Specialist Owners

Map every direct fact to its owner before resolution.

Examples:

- structural collapse uses established physical and technical conditions;
- disease transmission and agent change use Disease Evolution;
- faction decisions and mobilization use Faction Behaviour;
- World Gate channel changes use World Gates;
- magical effects use their Magic owner;
- death and bodily harm use current embodiment and consequence rules;
- Soul, Skill, Evolution, or Weapon changes return to those systems.

If the required owner is incomplete, use the narrowest valid [Provisional Rule](ALPHA_PLAYTEST_RULES.md#provisional) or leave the claim unresolved.

### Step 9: Define the Direct Event Boundary

Write one sentence stating what would become true if the event is established.

For example:

> The north retaining wall breaches between the second and third watch, releasing the water physically connected to that section into the lower channel.

This does not yet establish deaths, evacuation, crop loss, political blame, migration, disease, repairs, or historical significance.

### Step 10: Map Direct Footprint and Exposure

Identify the subjects and spaces that the direct event can reach through immediate physical, magical, social, informational, ecological, or institutional routes.

Preserve:

- uneven distribution;
- barriers and exclusions;
- timing differences;
- embodiment and compatibility;
- Material Exceptions;
- unknown or disputed exposure.

Large scale does not imply uniform exposure. Small scale does not imply low significance.

### Step 11: Resolve Actor-Mediated Preconditions

For every actor whose choice is necessary:

- identify their Observer View;
- establish that the decision point has arrived;
- apply commitments, procedures, values, interests, relationships, capability, and constraints;
- preserve refusal, error, disagreement, delegation, and delay;
- resolve only the current choice.

The generator cannot make someone act because the event would be better if they did.

### Step 12: Apply Counterforces

Test prevention, containment, substitution, repair, resistance, adaptation, evacuation, warning, redundancy, and other supported responses.

Record what each Counterforce can actually affect. Preventing one event may create a different cost, delay, burden, or dependency.

### Step 13: Resolve Timing and Sequence

Compare:

- earliest and latest supported occurrence;
- travel, communication, coordination, construction, biological, magical, and institutional delays;
- competing actions and processes;
- maintenance and resource exhaustion;
- interruption windows;
- Review Points.

If sequence is decisive and genuinely uncertain, frame that exact uncertainty rather than rolling for the entire event.

### Step 14: Resolve Material Uncertainty

Use [Uncertainty Handling](UNCERTAINTY_HANDLING.md):

1. state the exact unresolved question;
2. remove impossible and already-settled branches;
3. identify evidence and causal weighting;
4. use deterministic resolution where facts settle the issue;
5. use qualitative judgment for ordinary uncertainty;
6. use a declared campaign method only among supported material branches;
7. commit inputs and method before seeing the result;
8. record the result and scope externally.

One result settles only the framed event fact.

### Step 15: Establish or Decline the Event

If supported resolution establishes the event, record the direct Event Boundary, timing, footprint, provenance, and uncertainty.

Otherwise record the truthful result:

- delayed;
- stalled;
- prevented;
- redirected;
- misidentified;
- still unresolved.

The generator is successful when it preserves truth, even when no event occurs.

### Step 16: Separate Information Views

Record independently:

- Factual event truth;
- direct observations and traces;
- participant Observer Views;
- outsider and institutional reports;
- public narratives and propaganda;
- player-facing information;
- hidden, disputed, mistaken, outdated, and unknown claims.

An event may be real without public knowledge. A famous report may be false without being causally irrelevant.

### Step 17: Create Immediate Consequence Handoffs

Use [Consequence Resolution](CONSEQUENCE_RESOLUTION.md) to identify direct affected subjects, costs, traces, relationship changes, response opportunities, and owner handoffs.

Do not include those consequences inside the event result unless they are part of the direct transition itself.

### Step 18: Extend Causal Event Chains

Use [Causal Event Chains](../world-engine/CAUSAL_EVENT_CHAINS.md) to:

- add the established event as a Causal Node;
- connect supported incoming causes;
- propose but not preselect downstream branches;
- identify Counterforces and feedback;
- create Pending Consequences;
- set the next Causal Horizon and Review Points.

The generator does not finish the chain.

### Step 19: Update World State Once

Apply each established state change through [World-State Variables](../world-engine/WORLD_STATE_VARIABLES.md) and the specialist owner.

Do not count the same transition as event impact, consequence, domain update, and narrative importance four times.

### Step 20: Test Encounter Relevance

Use [Encounter Generation](ENCOUNTER_GENERATOR.md) only if the event or its traces create a material player-controlled decision.

An off-screen event remains valid without a scene. A visible event remains description rather than an Encounter if no material choice is available.

### Step 21: Record the External Event

Store the World-Event Record, related state changes, Pending Consequences, information views, and Review Points outside the repository.

Preserve source references so later corrections can distinguish an invalid event from a mistaken interpretation.

### Step 22: Communicate Proportionately

Reveal what current characters can perceive or learn, including fair warnings where a meaningful decision depends on them.

Do not narrate hidden Factual View, manufacture spectacle, or conceal the basic category of an observed danger merely to force uncertainty.

### Step 23: Stop at the Causal Horizon

Stop when the direct event is established or declined, immediate handoffs are recorded, and further resolution cannot yet change a meaningful decision or preserved dependency.

Do not simulate to a preferred ending.

## Selection and Randomization

Random tools may resolve only a genuinely unsettled field among supported possibilities after causes and constraints are established.

### Valid Uses

- choosing which of several equally supported minor material failures occurs first;
- resolving uncertain timing within a causally supported window;
- selecting a weather variation from established local possibilities when the exact state is open;
- resolving which of several exposed ordinary sites receives one variable effect;
- selecting an unknown but bounded non-agentic detail of an authorized external input;
- breaking a material tie after actor procedures and current conditions leave it unresolved.

### Invalid Uses

- rolling periodically to see whether a dramatic event appears;
- selecting catastrophe, war, plague, Gate opening, Reset, miracle, betrayal, or discovery without a Basis;
- inventing actor decisions, consent, information, capability, or coordination;
- deciding a specialist outcome without its owner;
- increasing event magnitude until it feels important;
- choosing a target because the player is nearby or emotionally attached;
- rerolling a quiet, prevented, or inconvenient result;
- using rarity as causal support;
- generating a reward, quest, prophecy fulfillment, or Age Transition.

Record the exact field, supported options, weighting, method, and result before continuing.

## Scale, Significance, and Visibility

Keep these independent:

- **footprint:** which subjects and places directly receive the event;
- **magnitude:** how much the direct condition changes within that footprint;
- **duration:** how long the direct occurrence or altered condition persists;
- **systemic reach:** which dependencies may transmit consequences;
- **historical significance:** what later actors preserve, interpret, or use;
- **narrative attention:** how much play focuses on it;
- **visibility:** who can observe or infer it.

A private agreement can redirect a war. A spectacular meteor can land harmlessly and remain a curiosity. Neither significance nor attention can be generated as an intrinsic event score.

## Concurrent and Compound Events

Several events may overlap in time or space.

Do not merge them merely because they are simultaneous. For each event preserve:

- initiating causes;
- Event Boundary;
- receiver and footprint;
- owner;
- timing;
- evidence;
- direct result.

Merge preparation only when the same transition genuinely has converging causes or one Event Boundary cannot be truthfully separated. Even then, record source contributions and Material Exceptions.

Coincidence can be real. Coincidence is not automatically a Causal Link.

## Event Continuity and Correction

An established event remains part of campaign history. Later evidence may correct:

- timing;
- footprint;
- mechanism;
- attribution;
- observer interpretation;
- affected subjects;
- whether several reports describe the same occurrence.

Correction preserves every fact still supported. It does not erase downstream actions taken from sincere mistaken beliefs.

If the direct occurrence itself lacked authority or causal support, use [Uncertainty Handling](UNCERTAINTY_HANDLING.md#corrections) and campaign continuity procedures to repair affected decisions and records.

## Cross-System Interfaces

### Soul Engine and Reincarnation

World Events may establish death context, witnesses, relationships, loss, opportunity, historical pressure, and environmental conditions. Soul owners alone resolve persistence, Echoes, Titles, Depth, Resonance, Constellations, Soul Avatars, Archive access, and Reincarnation.

An event cannot grant Soul progression or restore world-bound standing.

### Development and Skills

Events may provide access, instruction, hardship, practice conditions, evidence, and meaningful use. Development and Skill owners decide whether any learning occurs.

Exposure, survival, repetition, suffering, killing, elapsed time, and event importance grant no automatic progression.

### Monster Evolution

Events may alter habitat, resources, competition, threats, duration, contact, and Evolutionary Pressure. Monster Evolution resolves Adaptation, Mutation, Hybridization, eligibility, transitions, and extinction claims.

The event generator cannot award a form or invent a hidden route.

### Human Classes, Professions, and Institutions

Events may change access, offices, demand, legitimacy, resources, law, curricula, labor, and recognition. Human systems retain ownership of Classes, Professions, Martial Traditions, Magical Schools, Social Advancement, Institutions, and Class Evolution.

Historical importance does not grant a rank, credential, office, or Profession.

### Soul Weapons

Events may establish custody, danger, shared experience, emotional pressure, damage, loss, witnesses, and opportunity. Soul Weapon owners resolve Awakening, Intertwining, compatibility, Evolution, Echoes, Legacies, and Manifestations with Weapon Soul agency intact.

An intense event never guarantees Awakening.

### Magic

Magic owners establish sources, Mana, access, Affinity, formation, Functions, costs, failures, and direct magical effects. The generator receives those effects as bounded inputs and traces only valid world-facing occurrence.

Magic does not turn an unsupported event into a possible one.

### World Engine Domains

Every world-domain event retains its specialist owner:

- Populations own demographic change;
- Resources and Food own supply, access, renewal, depletion, and food conditions;
- Economies own production, allocation, exchange, Price, obligations, and adaptation;
- Ecology and Migration own ecosystem and movement processes;
- Faction Behaviour owns faction decisions, mobilization, and adaptation;
- War and Unrest owns conflict states and operations;
- Disease Evolution owns disease processes, outbreaks, and agent change;
- Technology and Magical Advancement owns discovery through implementation and decline;
- Dungeon Activity owns Dungeon processes and transitions;
- World Stability owns scoped continuity and systemic strain;
- Ages and World Resets owns Age and Reset classification;
- World Gates owns contact interfaces and channel events;
- Simulation Abstraction owns resolution changes.

The generator coordinates event preparation but resolves no owner by summary.

### Existing GM Generators

Monster, NPC, Dungeon, and Faction Profiles may supply actors, sites, relationships, capacities, and current conditions. They do not make their associated events occur.

An established World Event may later become an Encounter Source, require new NPC or Monster detail, revalidate a Dungeon or faction, or affect Reincarnation candidates through those generators' existing procedures.

### Alpha Playtest Rules

Use Provisional Rules only for narrow unresolved mechanics supported by completed foundations. Event generation cannot use provisional authority to implement Time Skip, Age-transition, Phase 11 templates, or a missing specialist system wholesale.

## Worked Examples

### Reservoir Breach Prevented

Cracks, high water, deferred maintenance, and one blocked outlet support a possible breach. Engineers have materials for one reinforcement, and residents have opened a secondary spill route.

The generation pass resolves timing and repair under their owners. The spill route lowers pressure before the wall fails. The candidate breach is recorded as prevented within this interval. Water loss, repair debt, and downstream flooding from the spill route become separate consequences.

No catastrophe is inserted merely because the Seed was dramatic.

### Migration Meets a Closed Pass

A monster Population follows an established Migration Corridor toward a pass closed by an early storm. The event question asks what directly occurs when the leading groups reach the blocked route.

The Event Boundary establishes arrival and loss of access. It does not establish attack, starvation, Evolution, settlement, or conflict. Group decisions, local observers, resource pressure, alternate routes, and later Encounters remain separate handoffs.

### Faction Proclamation Without Mobilization

A council validly adopts an embargo. That decision is a Causal Node and may be recorded as a public World Event within its political scope.

The Event Boundary is adoption and authorized communication, not successful enforcement. Messengers, border offices, traders, dissenting members, stockpiles, and foreign responses determine later implementation. The generator does not turn policy into result.

### Hidden Mana-Current Diversion

An established Ritual diverts part of a Mana Current underground. Magic owns the direct effect. Few surface observers can detect it.

The diversion is a real hidden World Event. Changed magical access, ecological response, failed Enchantments, investigation, and political blame require separate links and information routes. Lack of public witnesses does not make the event unreal.

### False Report of a Fallen City

Refugees report that a city was destroyed, while surviving records show one district burned and the government evacuated.

The generator classifies the district fire and evacuation as established events, city destruction as a mistaken belief, and several casualties as uncertain. The false report still changes markets and military choices through valid information links.

### Concurrent Eclipse and Assassination

An eclipse and a ruler's assassination occur during the same hour. The eclipse was predictable; the assassins used its darkness, but their plan and action remain separate causes.

The events are connected only where actual timing, visibility, and preparation provide a link. Symbolic interpretation may become a later informational cause. The generator does not declare cosmic intent.

### Gate Closure as an Input

World Gate rules establish closure of one transfer channel. The event generator records the bounded closure and current direct footprint.

Stranded travelers, interrupted supply, foreign ecology, Soul questions, faction blame, and later Gate Legacies go to their owners. This is not the queued Gate-event template and does not generate a new Gate.

### Quiet Institutional Failure

A licensing office misses its renewal cycle because records, staff, and legal authority no longer align. No building explodes and few people notice immediately.

The lapse is a World Event because a material authority relation changes. Delayed recognition, invalid credentials, opportunistic rivals, reform, or replacement are possible consequences rather than guaranteed plot beats.

## Human and AI GM Use

Human and AI GMs use the same generator, evidence duties, and ownership boundaries.

An AI GM should:

- search established state, Active Causes, Pending Consequences, actor plans, and specialist rules before proposing an event;
- state the World-Event Basis and Event Boundary explicitly in its working record;
- distinguish established events, reports, predictions, and unresolved occurrence;
- reject event requests that lack a cause while identifying what preparation could validly establish;
- preserve quiet, prevented, delayed, and redirected outcomes;
- trace actor information and decisions instead of using collective or omniscient responses;
- use randomness only after committing a bounded supported question;
- keep direct events separate from consequences, Encounters, Time Skips, and Age Transitions;
- preserve source attribution, uncertainty, Material Exceptions, and Review Points;
- store populated records only in the external Campaign Record;
- ask for clarification when established history, timing, ownership, or event identity materially conflicts.

Generative fluency does not establish causality.

## Safeguards

- **No event quota:** Quiet periods and contained pressures are valid world states.
- **No plot-first causality:** Desired scenes, twists, themes, and endings cannot supply a Basis.
- **No random spectacle:** Randomness resolves supported uncertainty rather than creating causes.
- **No automatic escalation:** Pressure may be contained, redirected, adapted to, or remain unresolved.
- **No outcome bundle:** One Event Boundary cannot decide every downstream consequence.
- **No actor puppetry:** Event need cannot choose an actor's consent, belief, loyalty, or action.
- **No collective omniscience:** Factions, Institutions, Populations, and species receive information through actual routes.
- **No uniform footprint:** Distribution, access, barriers, embodiment, and Material Exceptions remain visible.
- **No scale score:** Footprint, magnitude, duration, significance, visibility, and narrative attention stay separate.
- **No specialist theft:** Each direct effect and state claim remains with its owner.
- **No progression reward:** Events grant no automatic Development, Skill, Soul growth, Evolution, Class, office, or Weapon change.
- **No encounter conversion:** A World Event needs no scene, hostility, or player involvement.
- **No time shortcut:** Elapsed time cannot cause an unsupported event.
- **No Age shortcut:** Historical importance does not automatically establish an Age Transition or Reset.
- **No retroactive secret:** Hidden events require prior truth, causes, timing, and traces appropriate to their footprint.
- **No target convenience:** Events do not select places or actors because the player cares about them.
- **No reroll for drama:** A valid quiet or prevented result stands.
- **No duplicate state:** Apply each established transition once under its owner.
- **No campaign data in canon:** Briefs, Seeds, records, events, timelines, and state remain external.
- **No automation supremacy:** The procedure supports causal judgment rather than replacing it.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- a random-event table, event frequency, event rarity, threat rank, or universal event scale;
- campaign-specific events, histories, timelines, rumors, prophecies, or future branches;
- immediate outcome mechanics or downstream consequence mechanics;
- specialist world-domain mechanics;
- Encounters, quests, rewards, or narrative arcs;
- Time Skip or Age-transition procedure;
- World Reset or Gate-event generation;
- live campaign state in this repository.
