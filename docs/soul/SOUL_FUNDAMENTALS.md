# Soul Fundamentals

## Purpose

This document defines what a soul is, what belongs to it, how it relates to an incarnation, and what limits persistent progression. Detailed rules for individual soul systems belong in their own documents.

## Core Rule

A soul is the continuous metaphysical identity that connects all of its incarnations. It can preserve changes that become deeply integrated into that identity, but it is neither a perfect copy of every life nor a way to ignore the limits of the current body.

The current incarnation is the soul's active self. Previous lives inform that self; they do not own it.

## Fundamental Properties

- **Continuity:** The soul remains the same continuing identity across its incarnations even as that identity changes.
- **Individuality:** A soul is distinct from other souls. Intertwining, relationships, copied memories, and shared power do not erase that distinction.
- **Singularity:** One soul has one active incarnation by default. Exceptions require an explicit rule and cannot be inferred from duplication, possession, or Soul Echoes.
- **Embodiment:** The current body provides real capabilities and constraints that soul progression cannot casually ignore.
- **Integration:** Only changes meaningfully integrated into identity become persistent Soul Imprints.
- **Consequence:** Persistence preserves growth, not lost time, relationships, opportunities, or worldly position.

## Terms

### Incarnation

One embodied life of a soul, including its body, instincts, memories, relationships, culture, and historical circumstances.

### Soul Imprint

A durable change engraved into the soul by meaningful development or transformation. Skills, retained experience, instincts, Soul Titles, and bonds may use Soul Imprints according to their detailed rules.

An imprint preserves potential or structure. It does not guarantee conscious memory, immediate access, or identical expression in another body.

### Soul Integrity

The coherence that allows a soul to remain one continuing identity while holding many lives, imprints, and bonds. Integrity is a condition, not a general power score.

### Soul Strain

Temporary instability caused when a soul channels more memory, power, conflict, or incompatible development than it can presently integrate.

### Soul Wound

A lasting injury to identity, access, memory, or spiritual bonds. A Soul Wound persists until its cause is meaningfully treated or resolved.

## The Soul and the Incarnation

Every incarnation is a genuine life, not a disposable shell.

The body contributes:

- anatomy and physical capability;
- senses and biological needs;
- innate species instincts;
- health, age, and developmental limits;
- access to body-dependent skills;
- vulnerability to the environment.

The soul contributes:

- continuity of identity;
- Soul Imprints carried from prior lives;
- capacity for soul-level bonds and systems;
- retained potential recognized by other rules;
- the possibility of recovering prior experience.

Influence runs in both directions. A soul shapes how an incarnation develops, while every incarnation can change what the soul becomes. A new species, upbringing, or relationship may therefore produce a meaningfully different person without breaking continuity.

## Formation of Soul Imprints

Experience becomes a Soul Imprint only when the relevant system recognizes lasting integration. Common causes include:

- sustained mastery that changes instinct or understanding;
- adaptation under genuine pressure;
- an identity-defining choice, sacrifice, or relationship;
- reconciliation of a serious internal conflict;
- transformation that remains meaningful beyond its immediate reward.

Routine repetition, manufactured danger, and death by themselves do not create Soul Imprints. An event may be intense without becoming permanent if it does not change the soul.

Detailed systems decide what is imprinted and how. For example, the Development System governs retained Stat XP, while [Soul Title](SOUL_TITLES.md) rules govern persistent identity-bearing titles.

## Persistence and Access

Persistence means that a Soul Imprint survives the end of an incarnation. It does not mean the next incarnation can immediately perceive or use it.

Access may depend on:

- compatibility with the current body or species;
- relevant training and present-life experience;
- sufficient Soul Depth or Soul Resonance;
- a suitable emotional, environmental, or relational trigger;
- the condition of the soul and its bonds;
- restrictions defined by the originating system.

An inaccessible imprint remains part of the soul unless a rule explicitly damages, transforms, seals, or removes it. Access should return through play, discovery, and development rather than appearing solely for convenience.

## Identity and Agency

The current incarnation makes present decisions and bears their consequences.

- A Soul Echo may advise or influence but does not automatically control the incarnation.
- A Weapon Soul remains a distinct person even after intertwining.
- A [Soul Constellation](SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md) creates connection, not ownership or compulsory affection.
- Memories of an old relationship do not restore its former legal, social, or emotional claims.
- Copying memories, mannerisms, or skills does not by itself duplicate a soul.

Conflict among current desires, bodily instincts, imprints, and prior identities is possible. Such conflict is part of character development, not proof that one former life is the only authentic self.

## Integrity, Harm, and Recovery

Soul Integrity can be pressured by incompatible imprints, forced access, hostile spiritual effects, broken bonds, identity rejection, or contact with powers beyond the soul's capacity.

Soul harm requires an established cause, fair warning appropriate to the threat, and an opportunity for resistance or response. It must not be introduced retroactively merely to remove inconvenient progression.

Consequences may include:

- temporary Soul Strain;
- blocked or distorted access to an imprint;
- unreliable memory or instinct;
- damage to Soul Resonance or Soul Space;
- a Soul Wound tied to a specific cause;
- severe fragmentation under extraordinary circumstances.

Fragmentation does not automatically create independent souls, grant extra turns, or duplicate abilities. Any true division of a soul requires an explicit future rule or exceptional campaign premise.

Recovery must address the kind of harm suffered. Rest may relieve Strain, but a Soul Wound may require reconciliation, renewed development, repair of a bond, confrontation with its cause, or a meaningful change in identity. Ordinary healing does not automatically repair soul harm.

Total soul annihilation is outside the default consequence model. Introducing it requires an explicit campaign-level rule because it can end the continuity on which Eternal Cycle is built.

## Scope Boundaries

These fundamentals do not determine:

- where souls originate or what creates them;
- whether every soul naturally reincarnates;
- the exact operation of Soul Depth, Soul Resonance, Soul Echoes, or Soul Space;
- how a next incarnation is selected;
- numerical measures for Soul Integrity, Strain, or Wounds;
- the cosmological destination of souls outside Reincarnation.

Those questions belong to later Soul Engine rules, Reincarnation rules, or setting cosmology. Until then, no answer should be assumed from these fundamentals alone.

## Exploit Limits

- Repeated suicide, disposable incarnations, and manufactured near-death experiences do not create automatic soul growth.
- Killing or consuming another being does not automatically transfer its Soul Imprints, skills, identity, or Soul Depth.
- A large collection of shallow experiences does not substitute for meaningful integration.
- Persistent development cannot bypass incompatible anatomy or the need to develop the current body.
- Forced access can create Strain or Soul Wounds instead of reliable power.
- No soul mechanic grants authority over another soul without a specific rule, valid cause, and meaningful resistance.

## System Interactions

- **Reincarnation** ends one incarnation, reconciles persistent imprints, and begins another.
- **Development** determines how practice and experience become retained potential.
- **[Soul Depth](SOUL_DEPTH.md)** governs existential capacity and the integration of complexity.
- **[Soul Resonance](SOUL_RESONANCE.md)** governs alignment and access among identity, history, and potential.
- **[Soul Echoes](SOUL_ECHOES.md)** preserve perspectives from completed incarnations without replacing current agency.
- **[Soul Space](SOUL_SPACE.md)** provides the internal metaphysical environment in which parts of the soul may be encountered.
- **[Retained Instincts](RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md)** preserve integrated, body-shaped responses without carrying former anatomy or replacing current agency.
- **[Soul Titles](SOUL_TITLES.md)** preserve identity-bearing recognition rather than simple numerical bonuses.
- **[Soul Constellations](SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md)** preserve recurring relational patterns without compelling present relationships or replacing world causality.
- **Soul Weapons** persist through a bond between distinct souls.
- **[The Akashic Archive](AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md)** may preserve or reveal source-bound Records beyond one soul's personal history.
- **[Soul Avatars](SOUL_AVATARS.md)** permit exceptional whole-soul synthesis while the current incarnation remains the executive self.
- **The World Engine** receives the external consequences of each life even when the soul continues.

## Examples

### Incompatible Expression

A sword master reincarnates as a spider. The soul retains the relevant Skill XP and technique imprints, but the spider cannot simply wield a human sword. With present-life experimentation, the old understanding might eventually support a compatible limb technique, thread-cutting discipline, or later crossover skill.

### Experience Without Engraving

A reincarnator repeatedly enters trivial fights hoping to accumulate permanent combat growth. Once those fights demand no adaptation or meaningful decision, they produce little development and no new Soul Imprint.

### Lasting Injury

A hostile ritual tears at the bond between an incarnation and a deeply held Soul Title. Physical healing may end the ritual's bodily damage, but the resulting Soul Wound remains until the character restores, transforms, or relinquishes the identity the title represents.
