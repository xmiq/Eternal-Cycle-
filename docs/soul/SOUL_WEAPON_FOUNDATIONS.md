# Soul Weapon Foundations

## Weapon Souls

Weapons and meaningful tools may possess dormant Weapon Souls. Most remain quiet throughout their existence.

## Awakening

A Weapon Soul may awaken when wielder and weapon endure an intense, identity-defining struggle together. Typical catalysts include:

- a fight to the death;
- survival at the edge of death;
- impossible resistance;
- sacrifice;
- a vow upheld under extreme pressure;
- a moment in which both weapon and wielder are permanently changed.

Routine use, manufactured danger, or repetitive farming is insufficient.

## Intertwining

When awakening and resonance coincide, the wielder's soul and Weapon Soul may intertwine. Neither identity is erased. The bond allows the weapon to accompany the soul through reincarnation.

## Personality

A Weapon Soul's personality grows from origin, use, treatment, defining battles, and relationships. A knightly blade may value honour; a survival dagger may become pragmatic or sardonic; a scholar's book may become curious.

## Evolution

A Soul Weapon evolves through meaningful use and identity. Evolution paths should reflect history rather than generic rarity tiers.

Example:

> Iron Sword → Knight Blade → Hero Sword → Living Blade → Astral Edge → Conceptual Blade

An executioner's history might instead produce:

> Iron Sword → Executioner's Edge → Blood Fang → Tyrant's Judgment

## Legacy Weapons

If intertwining is incomplete, the awakened weapon may remain in the world. Future wielders must earn trust rather than automatically gaining ownership.
