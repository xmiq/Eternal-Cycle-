# Soul System Interactions

## Purpose

This document defines how Soul Engine systems combine, which system owns each outcome, and how to resolve cross-system claims without duplicating benefits or allowing one rule to waive another.

## Core Rule

Every cross-system effect has one Owning System that defines whether the effect exists and what it can do. Supporting Systems may provide history, access, capacity, interpretation, compatibility, cooperation, or consequences, but they cannot create an effect that the Owning System does not permit.

When several rules apply, satisfy all relevant requirements. Use the narrowest explicit rule for its own domain, preserve current-incarnation agency and other souls' consent, respect present embodiment and world causality, and resolve uncertainty toward a bounded result rather than an invented exception.

After resolving the claim, apply [Soul Engine Safeguards](SOUL_ENGINE_SAFEGUARDS.md) to test farming, duplicate throughput, agency, soul harm, and consequence continuity.

## Terms

### Interaction Claim

The exact outcome a character, being, rule, or GM is attempting to produce by combining two or more systems.

### Owning System

The canonical system that defines the claimed outcome, its requirements, scope, limits, persistence, and failure states.

### Supporting System

A system that supplies a relevant prerequisite or context without owning the resulting effect.

### Soul Engine Resolution

The ordered process used to test an Interaction Claim against agency, provenance, permission, access, capacity, embodiment, consent, cost, and world consequence.

## Five Distinct Questions

Most cross-system mistakes come from treating five different questions as one.

1. **Persistence:** Does the relevant history, Imprint, bond, title, instinct, Attunement, or capacity survive?
2. **Access:** Can the current incarnation approach or perceive it now?
3. **Interpretation:** Can the source be understood accurately enough for the intended use?
4. **Expression:** Can the current body, skill set, resources, relationships, and circumstances produce the claimed effect?
5. **Consequence:** What changes in the soul, body, relationships, and world after the attempt?

A Soul Imprint may persist while all four later answers remain uncertain. Clear access may still provide poor interpretation. Correct understanding may still be impossible to express. Successful expression may create severe external consequences.

## Soul Engine Resolution

Resolve an Interaction Claim in this order. A later step cannot repair a failed earlier step unless an explicit rule directly addresses that failure.

### 1. State the Claim

Describe the exact requested result rather than naming several systems.

Good: "Use a spider life's tension instinct to notice movement through a held thread."

Too broad: "Use Resonance, Echoes, and Avatar power to gain spider senses."

### 2. Identify Agency

Identify the current incarnation making the attempt and every other soul or person affected.

- The current incarnation remains the active self.
- An Echo is not another active incarnation.
- A Weapon Soul and every Constellation member remain distinct persons.
- No past relationship restores current consent or authority.

If the claim replaces the current self or controls another soul, it requires an explicit hostile or consensual rule before ordinary resolution can continue.

### 3. Establish Provenance

Name the genuine history supporting the claim.

- Which incarnation learned, suffered, chose, or adapted?
- Which Echo, Imprint, Instinct Origin, Title Basis, Constellation Thread, Archive Trace, or Weapon Soul carries the source?
- What could that source actually know or perceive?

Similarity, a convenient name, or a vivid symbol is not provenance.

### 4. Select the Owning System

Choose the system that defines the requested result.

- remembering a former perspective belongs to Soul Echoes;
- translating an old response belongs to Retained Instincts and Development according to the exact result;
- identifying a recurring relationship belongs to Soul Constellations;
- reading world history belongs to the Akashic Archive;
- selecting a new body belongs to Reincarnation;
- whole-soul synthesis belongs to Soul Avatars.
- establishing whether world contact supplies a valid cross-domain placement handoff belongs to World Gate Soul Interactions, while Reincarnation still owns the candidate and embodiment.

If no existing system owns the effect, the effect is not available merely because several Supporting Systems sound related. Record a design question instead of improvising canon.

### 5. Check Owning-System Permission

Apply every eligibility, formation, scope, consent, resistance, and unlock rule imposed by the owner. A Supporting System cannot substitute for a missing Title Basis, Archive Interface, valid incarnation candidate, Constellation recurrence, Weapon Soul consent, or Soul Avatar emergence.

### 6. Check Access and Interpretation

Apply target-specific Resonance, Echo Presence, Archive Reading Mode, Instinct Access, Soul Space Thresholds, memory limits, and source translation as relevant.

Access to one part does not expose the whole:

- one Echo does not reveal every life;
- one Soul Gate does not expose every Region;
- one Archive Trace does not answer every historical question;
- one Title Expression does not reveal the entire Title Basis;
- one recognized Constellation Thread does not reveal every member or role.

### 7. Check Capacity and Integrity

Soul Depth determines whether the soul can integrate the attempted complexity. Soul Integrity determines how coherently it can function now.

Depth never supplies missing permission, knowledge, skill, consent, or anatomy. When capacity is insufficient, narrow the scope, make access fragmentary, or apply justified Strain rather than deleting protected history or granting unstable full success.

### 8. Check Embodiment and Development

Confirm the present body, senses, cognition, skills, statistics, tools, resources, time, and environment can express the result.

- Retained sword mastery does not create hands.
- Dragon memory does not create breath organs.
- Archive instructions do not create profession mastery.
- Avatar Expression does not add actions.
- Soul Space rehearsal does not develop muscles.

When literal expression is impossible, use a compatible translation only if the Owning System and Development rules support it.

### 9. Check Other Participants

Apply consent, resistance, privacy, trust, and distinct identity for every affected soul.

- Relational Resonance may be one-sided, while communication is not.
- A Constellation creates no compulsory reunion.
- A Weapon Soul may refuse cooperation.
- An active Echo may withhold voluntary context.
- Archive access to protected first-person material needs permission or an explicit contested method.

Power, historical importance, and emergency do not erase this step.

### 10. Apply Time, Cost, and Risk

Account for attention, bodily fatigue, external time, resources, Dissonance, Soul Strain, visibility, obligation, and established danger. Combining systems does not produce free parallel actions or allow one cost to be paid once for unrelated effects.

### 11. Resolve the External Effect

Apply the result through the body and world as they presently exist. Institutions, enemies, ecosystems, witnesses, gods, and ordinary people respond through their information and incentives. Symbolic importance does not suspend causality.

### 12. Route Persistence

If the event changes the soul or reaches Final Death, use Soul Imprint formation and Life Reconciliation to determine what ends, remains in the world, and persists. Success does not make every part of an event permanent.

## Interaction Rules

### No Silent Overrides

A system grants only what its rules say it grants.

- Soul Depth provides capacity, not access.
- Soul Resonance provides alignment, not history or mastery.
- Soul Space provides an interface, not capability.
- Soul Echoes provide perspective, not objective truth.
- Soul Titles provide contextual recognition, not obedience.
- Soul Constellations preserve relationships, not destiny.
- Retained Instincts provide orientation, not anatomy.
- Archive Records provide evidence, not development.
- Soul Avatars provide synthesis, not unrestricted power.
- Reincarnation provides continuity, not recovery of worldly assets.

### No Circular Qualification

An effect cannot create its own prerequisite.

- A claimed Soul Title cannot provide the only evidence for its Title Basis.
- Resonance with an alleged relationship cannot by itself prove a Soul Constellation.
- An imagined Archive Interface cannot grant the access used to make it functional.
- A proposed Avatar Association cannot justify the lives required to form that Association.
- A desired Reincarnation candidate cannot be its own embodiment route.

Independent history or an already valid rule must begin the chain.

### No Duplicate Benefit

Several systems may describe different parts of one history without multiplying the same benefit. An Echo memory, Retained Instinct, Skill Imprint, and Archive Record from one sword fight can each contribute their own function. They do not award four copies of the fight's mastery, development, or action.

### Narrow Rules Own Their Domain

The narrowest explicit rule controls its named effect while remaining subordinate to foundational agency, identity, harm, and repository governance.

For example, Echo Delegation defines bounded control transfer. Soul Avatar rules determine that full Delegation suspends whole-soul Expression. Neither rule can erase the current-incarnation agency safeguards established by Soul Fundamentals.

### Conflict Between Canonical Documents

If two playable documents, or a playable document and an authoritative design decision, make incompatible claims that interpretation cannot reconcile, neither silently overrides the other. Mark the affected roadmap task blocked, record the conflict in [Unresolved Questions](../../design/UNRESOLVED_QUESTIONS.md), and resolve the playable rules and decisions log before treating the interaction as consistent.

### Examples Do Not Create Exceptions

An example illustrates rules already established. It cannot introduce a new ability, waive a requirement, or become a universal precedent merely because its scene was memorable.

## Lifecycle Across Incarnations

### During an Incarnation

- Development creates present capability and may form Soul Imprints.
- Resonance activates genuine Links under relevant Triggers.
- Soul Echoes, Retained Instincts, Soul Titles, bonds, Archive access, and Avatar Expression remain conditional.
- World consequences accumulate outside the soul.

### At Final Death

- Reincarnation confirms that ordinary revival no longer applies.
- The incarnation's body and body-bound capabilities end.
- The world's consequences continue.

### During Life Reconciliation

- one Soul Echo normally forms from the completed incarnation;
- Development rules preserve legitimate Skill XP and Stat XP;
- qualifying habits or adaptations may be recognized as Retained Instincts;
- Soul Titles, Constellations, Resonance Links, Archive Attunements, Avatar Associations, Soul Wounds, and Weapon Soul bonds change only when their own rules recognize a cause;
- death itself grants no completion bonus.

### During Interlife

- the World Engine continues;
- existing soul structures persist subject to access;
- Depth may provide capacity for awareness, while Reincarnation provides the actual transition route;
- access to Soul Space, Echoes, the Archive, and Avatar Expression remains limited by time, access, and Integrity;
- no system provides unlimited training or consequence-free repair.

### At New Embodiment

- Reincarnation supplies one valid current body and context;
- the body supplies present anatomy, senses, instincts, statistics, needs, and developmental limits;
- persistent structures begin at whatever access their detailed rules support;
- Resonance and present practice may reopen or translate old potential;
- former social identity, property, relationships, and authority do not return automatically.

## Common Workflows

### Recover Prior-Life Knowledge

1. Use a Soul Echo for the former life's personal perspective or the Archive for external historical evidence.
2. Use Soul Resonance to connect a valid Target, Basis, and Trigger.
3. Use Soul Depth and Integrity to bound safe access.
4. Preserve source limitations and translation loss.
5. Apply current reasoning and external corroboration before treating the result as fact.

### Express a Skill in a New Species

1. Confirm that the Skill Imprint and Skill XP persist.
2. Identify any supporting Retained Instinct or Echo context.
3. Use Resonance to approach the relevant history.
4. Check current anatomy and progression-tree rules.
5. Develop a compatible expression through present experimentation and practice.
6. Keep the original skill dormant where no valid translation exists.

### Respond to a Retained Instinct

1. Identify the Instinct Origin and present Cue.
2. Determine whether expression is direct, analogical, or incompatible.
3. Use Resonance or Echo context only when genuine access exists.
4. Present urgency or attention without choosing deliberate action.
5. Use Development for any reliable technique built from the response.

### Recognize a Recurring Soul

1. Establish a genuine Constellation Thread or other Relational Link rather than resemblance.
2. Use a valid Trigger and Resonance State for the recognizing soul.
3. Keep recognition one-sided unless the other soul independently recognizes or consents.
4. Require world causality for meeting and communication.
5. Restore no former role, intimacy, authority, or obligation automatically.

### Read an Archive Record

1. Define the Archive Target and bounded question.
2. Establish an Archive Basis and Interface.
3. Apply Resonance, Depth, Integrity, permission, and Reading Mode.
4. Preserve Trace provenance, omissions, conflicts, and Annotations.
5. Use Development and embodiment for any practical application.

### Enter Avatar Expression

1. Confirm genuine Soul Avatar emergence and the current Avatar Lead.
2. Name the Trigger, present responsibility, and relevant histories.
3. Apply Echo Presence, contributor consent, Resonance, Depth, Integrity, Soul Anchor, and embodiment.
4. Define scope, costs, visibility, and exit.
5. Keep every contribution bounded by its Owning System.
6. Resolve world response without making the Soul Avatar automatically central.

### Resolve a World-Gate Soul Claim

1. Establish the World Gate's Contact Domains, Basis, channels, operation, compatibility, and current evidence.
2. Distinguish a living incarnation, possible death, Final Death, Interlife, candidate generation, embodiment, and Avatar Expression.
3. Resolve ordinary Gate travel and exposure before inferring a Soul effect.
4. For cross-domain candidate placement, establish a Cross-Domain Reincarnation Route where one is required and then apply every ordinary candidate condition.
5. For Avatar Expression, establish a genuine Trigger, provenance, present responsibility, contributors, costs, and exit without granting Gate operation or universal interpretation.
6. Preserve one active incarnation, current embodiment, source provenance, other persons' agency, and external world consequences.

### Cooperate With a Soul Weapon

1. Treat the Weapon Soul as a distinct person.
2. Identify the shared history and relevant mutual Resonance.
3. Obtain cooperation for the specific form, technique, memory, or internal contact.
4. Apply current weapon form, evolution, body, skill, and resource rules.
5. Do not infer a Weapon Manifestation, Constellation, or Soul Avatar benefit from intertwining alone.

### Resolve Final Death and Reincarnation

1. Confirm Final Death and external consequences.
2. Classify what ends, remains in the world, and persists.
3. Apply each system's own formation or change rules during Life Reconciliation.
4. Advance the world through Interlife.
5. Generate only valid incarnation candidates.
6. Establish starting access separately from persistence.
7. Begin one current incarnation with its own agency and embodiment.

## Failure and Partial Outcomes

When a claim cannot fully resolve, choose the consequence that matches the failed layer.

| Failed layer | Typical result |
| --- | --- |
| No genuine provenance | no Link, title, relationship, Record access, or retained source |
| Owning System permission absent | claimed effect is unavailable; related systems may still produce their own narrower effects |
| Access absent | persistent structure remains Dormant, Silent, sealed, or unrecognized |
| Interpretation weak | fragmentary, biased, symbolic, or mistranslated information |
| Capacity or Integrity insufficient | narrowed scope, Dissonance, fatigue, Soul Strain, or established Wound risk |
| Embodiment incompatible | dormant capability or a present-life translation project rather than literal expression |
| Consent absent | no cooperative effect; a contested attempt requires its own hostile rule |
| Time or resources absent | delayed, interrupted, reduced, or failed external action |
| World route absent | no meeting, candidate, intervention, or consequence merely for narrative convenience |

Failure at one layer should not erase legitimate success at another. An Archive reading may fail while the investigation still uncovers a mundane clue. A skill may be physically unusable while its history remains accessible. A Constellation member may refuse contact while the recognizing soul's own memories remain valid.

## Composite Examples

### The Spider Swordsman

A former human sword master reincarnates as a spider. The Swordsmanship Imprint persists through Reincarnation. A Retained Instinct preserves spacing and threat attention, while the sword-master Echo remembers formal techniques. Resonance opens access, but the spider lacks hands and a sword.

Development owns any new capability. Through present practice with reinforced thread and leg movement, the spider may develop a cutting-web discipline. Depth supports integrating the alien body with old history; none of the supporting systems awards the finished skill or human anatomy.

### The Recognized Enemy

A current goblin recognizes a human diplomat as a recurring rival. Soul Constellation rules own that recognition, while Relational Resonance provides present access to the Thread. A former warlord Echo explains one prior conflict, and an Archive Record corroborates a treaty, but neither reveals the diplomat's current thoughts.

The human remembers nothing and refuses private contact. The Constellation creates no right to pursue or command them. World circumstances still allow a public negotiation, where the goblin may use the history it legitimately knows and accept the political consequences.

### The Title at the Archive

A bearer of **The One Who Stayed** approaches an Interface connected to an ancient seal. The Soul Title can provide a relevant Archive Basis because its real history involved that place, while Resonance and an Archive Attunement support access. The Archive remains the Owning System for the reading.

The title does not force the Interface open, prove every legend about the vigil, or grant defensive statistics. A bounded Record may corroborate pressure on the seal and reveal later consequences while leaving private intention to the Echo's testimony.

### The Soul Avatar and the Weapon

During a political crisis, a Soul Avatar enters Expression with several willing Echoes and an intertwined spear. Soul Avatar rules own the synthesis; Echo rules own each perspective; the Weapon Soul owns its consent and testimony; Resonance aligns the relevant Links; Depth and Integrity bound complexity; the World Engine owns the public consequences.

The spear may warn that an oath resembles one that once ended badly. It does not become an extra combatant or automatically gain a Weapon Manifestation. The Avatar Lead chooses whether to reveal the warning, and institutions respond according to what they witness and believe.

## GM Procedure

For any cross-system claim:

1. state the exact Interaction Claim;
2. identify the current actor and every affected soul;
3. identify provenance and the Owning System;
4. list each Supporting System and the one prerequisite it contributes;
5. reject circular qualification and duplicate benefits;
6. apply permission, access, interpretation, capacity, embodiment, consent, time, and cost in order;
7. use partial outcomes that match the layer that failed;
8. route the external effect through World Engine causality;
9. route persistent change through Soul Imprint and Life Reconciliation rules;
10. record a design conflict instead of inventing an exception when canonical owners disagree.

Track only interactions that matter to play. The purpose of this procedure is predictable rulings, not additional bookkeeping or a universal formula.
