# Reincarnation

## Purpose

Reincarnation allows one soul to continue through radically different bodies, species, cultures, and Ages.

## Core Transition

At death:

1. The current life ends and its immediate worldly claims are severed.
2. The soul enters or awakens within Soul Space.
3. Soul Echoes and the Soul Weapon may react to the completed life.
4. Persistent development is reconciled.
5. The next incarnation is selected, discovered, assigned, or earned according to the campaign's mode.
6. A new body begins with its own capacities, limitations, instincts, and social context.

## What Resets

- body;
- ordinary statistics;
- wealth and inventory;
- current reputation and office;
- temporary titles;
- relationships that depend on the previous identity.

## What Persists

- the continuity of the soul;
- skills and Skill XP, though access may be limited by the new body;
- retained Stat XP;
- Soul Depth and Soul Resonance;
- Soul Echoes and instincts;
- Soul Titles and Constellations;
- Soul Weapons.

## Crossover

Humans and monsters normally use different skill and development trees. Advanced Reincarnation may permit crossover, but access should require compatibility, soul capacity, and meaningful experience. Crossover does not erase anatomical or metaphysical constraints.

## Consequence Rule

Reincarnation cannot retroactively save the previous life. A dead ruler loses the throne, a dead parent loses years with a family, and a destroyed civilization may remain gone.
