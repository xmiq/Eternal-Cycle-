# Soul-Bound Companion Fate and Reincarnation Continuity

## Purpose

This document defines a persistent fate bond between two distinct Souls. It guarantees eventual reunion while preserving world causality, valid Reincarnation adjudication, independent identity, uncertain recognition, and current-life agency.

## Document Control

- **Owner:** Soul-Bound Companion Bond identity, membership, formation provenance, active convergence obligation, and bond-level continuity
- **Dependencies:** [Soul Fundamentals](SOUL_FUNDAMENTALS.md), [Reincarnation](REINCARNATION.md), [Memory Continuity](MEMORY_CONTINUITY.md), [Relationship Memory Engine](../persistence/RELATIONSHIP_MEMORY_ENGINE.md), and [Canonical Data Ownership](../persistence/CANONICAL_DATA_OWNERSHIP.md)
- **Extensions:** campaign schemas and setting-specific effects may implement bounded formation, interference, release, or manifestation without changing these invariants
- **Consumers:** Reincarnation adjudication, World Engine simulation, Life Archive, Timeline, Campaign History, Context Assembly, Relationships, and GMs

## Core Rule

> Fate eventually draws the bound Souls back into one another's lives.

A **Soul-Bound Companion Bond** joins exactly two distinct continuing Souls. While the bond remains active, sufficient continuation of both Souls and valid cosmological circumstances guarantee an eventual meaningful encounter.

The guarantee is stronger than probability. It is not a percentage, encounter bonus, fortunate tendency, or ordinary Relationship persistence.

Eventual does not mean immediate. Reunion may occur in the next incarnation, late in a Life, several Lives later, after migration or travel, across worlds, after conflict, or following major historical change. No universal timetable applies.

## What the Bond Is Not

The bond is not inherently:

- ownership, servitude, command, or custody;
- romance, friendship, family, loyalty, or party membership;
- approval, trust, affection, attraction, compatibility, or moral alignment;
- physical proximity, tracking, communication, or shared senses;
- a current-life Relationship Record;
- a Reincarnation destination selector;
- a prophecy that scripts every intermediate event.

Calling one Soul a **companion** describes the persistent fate relation, not a compulsory social role in every Life.

## Distinct Souls and Current Persons

The participants remain two Souls with separate identities, histories, memories, Echoes, Titles, Weapons, bodies, choices, and consequences. The bond never merges Souls or creates shared ownership of either participant's internal state.

Each incarnation remains a distinct current person. A reunion may place the Souls in unfamiliar species, ages, cultures, factions, families, social positions, or moral circumstances. Former roles grant no present entitlement.

The player, Controller, player-character Entity, current incarnation, and Soul remain distinct under the [Simulation Architecture](../core/SIMULATION_ARCHITECTURE_AND_PERSPECTIVE.md). Binding a companion does not make that companion another player-controlled body.

## Formation

A Soul-Bound Companion Bond forms only through an explicit canonical formation route that establishes:

1. two stable Soul identities;
2. a meaningful shared basis capable of reaching both Souls;
3. a declared or discoverable fate-binding mechanism;
4. valid participation, consent, or contested resistance under that mechanism;
5. costs, witnesses, consequences, and formation provenance;
6. successful persistence and validation.

Deep attachment, repeated travel, rescue, romance, family, ownership claims, oaths, Soul Resonance, a Soul Constellation, or player preference does not form the bond by implication.

Ordinary formation should involve informed mutual participation. A setting-specific coercive attempt requires an explicit contested rule with warning, resistance, limits, consequences, and a valid outcome. Unilateral obsession, violence, captivity, or suffering cannot manufacture a bond merely by intensity.

This document defines the resulting bond, not one universal ritual or acquisition method.

## Convergence Intervals

A **Convergence Interval** begins when active bound Souls have materially separated after their latest meaningful encounter, including separation by Final Death, Reincarnation, migration, inaccessible worlds, or ordinary life circumstances.

Its state is qualitative:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | The active guarantee has not yet produced the next meaningful encounter. |
| **Approaching** | Established causal developments make a viable encounter materially nearer; exact timing may remain unknown. |
| **Fulfilled** | The Souls entered one another's lives through a meaningful causal encounter during this interval. |
| **Obstructed** | No currently valid route is established, or an established obstacle prevents convergence. The guarantee remains pending. |
| **Suspended** | A canonical effect temporarily prevents the bond from operating; cause and recovery conditions are recorded. |
| **Disputed** | Identity, bond status, or alleged fulfillment lacks sufficient evidence. |

These states are not a countdown or power scale. Fulfillment closes one interval, not the bond. A later material separation may begin another interval.

## Meaningful Reunion

A **Reunion Manifestation** is a meaningful causal encounter in which the current incarnations enter one another's lives. It may be cooperative, hostile, brief, indirect but consequential, mistaken, or initially unrecognized.

Mere simultaneous existence, unnoticed proximity, seeing an anonymous crowd, sharing a world, or appearing in the same summary does not necessarily fulfill the interval. The encounter must create a real opportunity for interaction, consequence, recognition, choice, or later discoverable connection.

Reunion does not require:

- conscious recognition;
- autobiographical recall;
- disclosure of the bond;
- restoration of an old Relationship;
- cooperation or continued proximity;
- a pleasant outcome.

## Fate Constrains the Outcome, Not Every Step

The GM must preserve both the long-term guarantee and the causal integrity of the route. Fate operates through established world possibilities such as:

- valid births and Reincarnation candidates;
- migration, institutions, trade, conflict, ecology, or shared Projects;
- lawful travel and World Gates;
- third parties acting from their own motives and Knowledge;
- converging consequences of prior choices;
- world changes that create a route which did not previously exist.

Fate does not authorize arbitrary teleportation, retroactive coincidence, impossible births, invalid candidates, NPC mind control, fabricated Knowledge, premature death, or protection from consequences.

The GM need not pre-script the route. Preserve a Pending Convergence, simulate the world independently, and select or recognize a valid causal route when one emerges. The guarantee is a long-term constraint on eventual campaign history, not a script for each scene.

## Reincarnation Candidate Boundary

The bond is relevant context during candidate adjudication, but [Reincarnation Generation](../gm/REINCARNATION_GENERATION.md) remains authoritative.

- The bond may inform the GM's contextual evaluation of otherwise-valid candidates.
- It cannot make an invalid body, extinct species, impossible birth, inaccessible world, or causally unsupported placement valid.
- It cannot bypass Soul compatibility, embodiment requirements, world state, the active Reincarnation Mode, or player choices available under that mode.
- It cannot guarantee that the next incarnation is near the other Soul.
- It cannot be farmed through engineered death or candidate rerolls.

If no candidate can support convergence now, the bond remains Pending or Obstructed. Later Lives, travel, migration, or world change may satisfy it.

## World Contact and Cross-World Reunion

World Knowledge, World Contact, Travel Routes, and Reincarnation Possibility remain separate under [World Gate Soul Interactions](../world-engine/WORLD_GATE_SOUL_INTERACTIONS.md).

A bond does not create a World Gate, Travel Route, Cross-Domain Reincarnation Route, receiving law, population, or valid embodiment. When the Souls occupy disconnected domains, the GM records the obstruction and allows established world processes to create, reveal, or restore a valid route over time.

The eventual guarantee may survive Gate Closure, World Reset, extinction, and Age change. Those events can transform the route and delay reunion; they do not justify pretending a route already exists.

## Asynchronous Death and Interlife

Bound Souls need not die, reincarnate, or awaken together.

- One Soul's Final Death does not kill, recall, or reincarnate the other.
- One Soul's Interlife does not suspend the other's embodied life.
- The bond grants no resurrection, life extension, shared death, body reservation, or immunity.
- An embodied Soul is not removed from its current responsibilities merely to synchronize the pair.
- Interlife contact requires a separate valid interface and does not by itself satisfy embodied reunion unless the formation route explicitly defines otherwise.

Long asynchronous intervals remain part of history. [Long-Horizon Summaries](../persistence/LONG_HORIZON_SUMMARIES.md) preserve material causes, obstacles, and continuity hooks without determining the reunion route.

## Recognition and Memory

[Memory Continuity](MEMORY_CONTINUITY.md) owns autobiographical recall. Soul Resonance may provide a clue or Memory Cue where its own requirements are met.

The bond alone grants no:

- recognition of a current incarnation;
- memory of former meetings;
- knowledge of the other Soul's identity, location, condition, thoughts, or history;
- proof that a suspected person is the bound Soul;
- protection against mistaken identity.

One participant may recognize the connection while the other does not. Both may fail to recognize it. Recognition can occur before, during, long after, or never within a particular Reunion Manifestation.

## Current-Life Relationships and Agency

[Relationships](../persistence/RELATIONSHIP_MEMORY_ENGINE.md) owns present trust, hostility, affection, commitments, communication, and unresolved issues. Every reunion begins from the current incarnations' actual circumstances and inherited world consequences, not a restored social state.

The bond cannot compel either participant to:

- like, trust, forgive, obey, protect, follow, love, remain near, or agree with the other;
- resume marriage, family, command, debt, service, ownership, or companionship;
- disclose memories, secrets, plans, identity, or the bond;
- accept continued contact after the encounter.

The current Relationship develops through current actions. A participant may leave, oppose the other, establish boundaries, or choose no ongoing relationship without invalidating the historical reunion.

## Long-Term Positive Direction

The bond's long arc should create a net-positive opportunity for both Souls: growth, understanding, repair, rescue, challenge that enables later flourishing, shared creation, release from a harmful pattern, or another meaningful benefit.

Net-positive does not mean immediate comfort, equal benefit in every encounter, moral approval, guaranteed friendship, immunity from tragedy, or a conflict-free path. The GM must not use this direction to excuse abuse, remove consequences, force reconciliation, or declare suffering beneficial without evidence.

The opportunity may be refused. Fate guarantees convergence, not a chosen response or predetermined lesson.

## Multiple Bonds and Non-Transitivity

A Soul may hold more than one Soul-Bound Companion Bond when each has its own valid formation route and the Soul can sustain the resulting complexity.

Every bond remains pair-specific:

- A bonded to B and A bonded to C does not bind B to C.
- One reunion does not fulfill another pair's interval unless those Souls also meaningfully encounter one another under their own bonds.
- A group, party, family, or constellation does not become one shared bond automatically.
- Scheduling pressure from several bonds cannot override causality, agency, or embodiment.

Soul Depth may support integrating complex bonds, but it does not create them or turn quantity into power.

## Soul Constellation Boundary

A [Soul Constellation](SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md) is recurring relational history formed through reciprocal transformation in multiple incarnational contexts. Its Convergence is only an opportunity and may never occur.

A Soul-Bound Companion Bond is an explicit pairwise fate relation whose active invariant guarantees eventual reunion. The systems may coexist, but neither implies the other:

- a bond may exist before the Souls have enough recurring history to form a Constellation;
- a Constellation may persist forever without a fate bond;
- reunion through a bond may later contribute to Constellation formation only through genuine reciprocal transformation;
- Constellation recognition and Commons access do not arise from the bond automatically.

## Resonance, Depth, Wounds, and Interference

- **Soul Resonance** may express the bond through bounded relational alignment, cues, Dissonance, or recognition signs. It grants no tracking or command.
- **Soul Depth** supports integration of the bond's complexity and contradictions. It is not a bond rank or reunion timer.
- **Soul Wounds** may damage access, expression, trust, or the route without silently deleting the bond.
- **Interference** may delay, obscure, redirect, or temporarily suspend operation only through an established cause with scope, resistance, consequences, provenance, and recovery conditions.

Ordinary distance, disagreement, forgetting, or refusal of a current Relationship does not sever the Soul-level bond. This document defines no routine unilateral severance method. Any future effect capable of release, transformation, or destruction must explicitly preserve identity, history, agency, and consequences and requires owner-authorized Canon.

## Logical Persistence and Ownership

The Soul/fate domain owns one stable bond record for the unordered pair of Soul IDs. The record contains:

- stable Bond ID;
- the two distinct Soul IDs in canonical pair order;
- formation basis, event, mechanism, and provenance;
- status and any established interference;
- current Convergence Interval ID and state;
- latest fulfilled Reunion Manifestation reference;
- unresolved obstacles, uncertainty, and required source recovery;
- revision, migration, and validation history.

Other owners remain separate:

- Entities and Lives own current incarnations and embodiments;
- Relationships owns current-life relationship state;
- Memory Continuity and Knowledge own recall and recognition access;
- Timeline and Campaign History own events;
- Life Archive indexes each Life's historical manifestations;
- World and Gate owners own routes and current world state;
- Context Packets and summaries are Derived or Cache views.

A normalized implementation may use logical structures equivalent to `soul_fate_bonds`, `convergence_intervals`, `reunion_manifestations`, `bond_interference`, and typed bond references. These names are guidance, not a mandatory physical schema.

Enforce one active bond identity per Soul pair. Reversing participant order must not create a duplicate. A Reunion Manifestation references the bond, involved Life and Entity IDs, Timeline event, and Relationship records without copying their mutable state.

This repository contains no populated campaign schema, so FR-016 requires no campaign-specific migration.

## Save and Context Procedure

When a bond is formed, obstructed, expressed, fulfilled, damaged, or otherwise materially changed:

1. load the authoritative Bond, both Soul identities, relevant current Lives and Entities, world routes, Relationships, Memory records, Timeline, and candidate context;
2. distinguish established facts from Unknown route, timing, recognition, or outcome;
3. adjudicate through the specialist owner for each effect;
4. determine the complete Affected Set;
5. update the Bond and Convergence Interval once through their owner;
6. update current Relationships or Character Knowledge only when their own facts changed;
7. append significant Timeline and Campaign History events;
8. update Life Archive references or historical summaries only where their scope warrants it;
9. persist, validate, read back, and activate the Save Point before durable narration closes.

[Context Assembly](../ai/CONTEXT_ASSEMBLY_AND_TURN_PERSISTENCE.md) loads the bond when identity, Reincarnation, encounter causality, separation, recognition, or a materially relevant historical callback requires it. Hidden route plans and GM-only bond facts remain protected.

## Migration

Existing campaigns adopt the subsystem through [Migration and Versioning](../persistence/MIGRATION_AND_VERSIONING.md):

1. back up the active campaign;
2. audit only established Soul bindings, alleged reunions, participant identities, and source evidence;
3. preserve Unknown and Disputed cases rather than inferring a bond from closeness or repeated encounters;
4. merge one stable pair record and referenced historical manifestations;
5. validate pair uniqueness, Soul identity, chronology, ownership, Knowledge, Relationships, routes, and provenance;
6. activate only after successful read-back.

Migration never grants a bond retroactively because two characters were companions, and it never fabricates a past Reunion Manifestation to satisfy the guarantee.

## Validation

Validate that:

- exactly two distinct valid Souls participate;
- canonical pair ordering prevents duplicate reversed bonds;
- formation has an explicit route and provenance;
- one active Convergence Interval exists at most per active bond;
- a Fulfilled interval references a meaningful causal encounter and valid participants;
- current Relationship state remains with Relationships;
- recognition and recall remain with Memory Continuity and Character Knowledge;
- candidate, world, travel, and Gate validity remain with their owners;
- pending or obstructed routes remain Unknown rather than fabricated;
- no bond causes shared death, resurrection, tracking, command, or automatic control;
- multiple bonds remain independent and non-transitive;
- summaries, Life Archive entries, and Context Packets do not become competing owners;
- no campaign-specific facts entered this repository.

## Safeguards

- Do not reduce eventual reunion to probability or quietly abandon a valid pending bond.
- Do not force immediate, next-Life, nearby, or same-world reunion.
- Do not invalidate world causality, embodiment, candidate rules, or player choices to manufacture a scene.
- Do not use engineered death, repeated Reincarnation, rerolls, or self-harm to accelerate convergence.
- Do not confuse reunion with recognition, memory, consent, trust, loyalty, affection, or alliance.
- Do not use the bond as tracking, telepathy, shared inventory, pooled capability, resurrection, or Soul Space access.
- Do not duplicate the bond per Life, Entity, Relationship, direction, or summary.
- Do not make several pair bonds transitive or turn them into a compulsory group.
- Do not expose hidden identity, route, or timing without a valid information path.
- Do not script a fixed story when several causal routes remain valid.

## GM Procedure

1. confirm the stable Bond ID and both Soul IDs;
2. load the current Convergence Interval and latest validated reunion;
3. establish each Soul's current Life, embodiment, location, world access, and uncertainty;
4. identify material obstacles and possible causal paths without declaring an unsupported route;
5. during Reincarnation, consider the bond only among otherwise-valid candidates;
6. advance independent world processes and participant choices normally;
7. recognize a viable convergence route when Canon supports it;
8. preserve current agency, information boundaries, and Relationship ownership;
9. determine whether an encounter meaningfully fulfills the interval;
10. persist the Bond, interval, manifestations, and every specialist consequence atomically;
11. keep the next route and timing Unknown until established.

The GM must neither force every nearby opportunity nor defer reunion indefinitely without genuine obstruction. For long-running Pending intervals, periodically review whether existing Canon now supports a fair causal path.

## Worked Cases

### Asynchronous Lives

One companion dies while the other has decades left. The survivor continues their life. The dead Soul reincarnates elsewhere, and no synchronization occurs. After the survivor eventually reincarnates, migration and shared institutional consequences create a meeting two Lives later.

### Reunion Without Recognition

Two bound Souls meet as rival monster scouts. Their encounter fulfills the interval because each materially enters the other's life, but neither recognizes the bond. Later cues may produce recall under FR-015; hostility and trust develop only through Relationships.

### Worlds Without a Route

The Souls occupy disconnected worlds. The interval is Obstructed, not failed. The GM does not invent a Gate or impossible candidate. Centuries later, independent world contact creates a valid route, and subsequent travel allows convergence.

### Refused Companionship

A current incarnation recognizes the other Soul and refuses further contact after one consequential meeting. The interval is fulfilled. The bond does not override the refusal or restore their former friendship. A later separation may begin another interval.

### Several Bonds

Soul A is bound separately to Souls B and C. A meets B during a war. That reunion says nothing about A's pending interval with C and creates no bond between B and C.

### Immortal and Reincarnator

An immortal Soul remains embodied while its companion passes through several Lives. Fate does not kill or relocate the immortal. A later incarnation reaches the immortal through ordinary exploration and political consequence.

### A False Identification

A prophecy and familiar appearance suggest that an NPC is the bound companion. Bond and Soul evidence remain inconclusive, so the claim is Disputed. The GM preserves the mystery rather than rewriting the NPC's identity or marking the interval Fulfilled.
