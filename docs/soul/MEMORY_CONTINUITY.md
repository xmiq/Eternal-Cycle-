# Memory Continuity, Fading, and Recall

## Purpose

This document defines autobiographical memory continuity across incarnations. It preserves one continuing Soul without treating every new life as either a perfect archive or a blank unrelated person.

## Document Control

- **Owner:** cross-incarnation autobiographical memory identity, persistence, fading, dormancy, reinforcement, and recall eligibility
- **Dependencies:** [Reincarnation](REINCARNATION.md), [Life Archive](../persistence/LIFE_ARCHIVE.md), [Truth Layers](../persistence/TRUTH_LAYERS.md), and [Canonical Data Ownership](../persistence/CANONICAL_DATA_OWNERSHIP.md)
- **Extensions:** storage schemas and campaign implementations may represent these records without changing their meaning
- **Consumers:** Character Knowledge, Context Assembly, Relationships, Timeline, retained development, Soul systems, GMs, and player-facing views

## Core Rule

> The Soul continues, but continuity does not mean perfect memory.

Autobiographical memories may persist, fade, become Dormant, return as fragments, strengthen through meaningful reinforcement, or become consciously accessible through relevant cues. Ordinary forgetting remains possible within one life and across Reincarnation.

Total amnesia is not the default consequence of Reincarnation. Total recall is not the default benefit of Soul continuity.

## A New Life Is Still New

Each incarnation is a distinct person shaped by its current body, cognition, senses, upbringing, language, culture, relationships, choices, and circumstances. Previous-life memory may influence that person without replacing them.

Remembering that one once lived as someone else does not require adopting that Life's name, loyalties, morality, profession, relationships, or unfinished goals. The current incarnation interprets recalled history from a new present and retains agency over what it means.

## Information Boundaries

```text
GM Canon
!=
Player historical access
!=
Current Character recall
```

- **GM Canon** includes the authoritative historical facts needed to adjudicate continuity.
- **Player historical access** may include authorized Life Archive views and prior-session knowledge.
- **Current Character recall** is only the autobiographical content the current incarnation can consciously access through Character Knowledge.

The GM loading complete history through [Context Assembly](../ai/CONTEXT_ASSEMBLY_AND_TURN_PERSISTENCE.md) does not grant it to any character. A player inspecting the [Life Archive](../persistence/LIFE_ARCHIVE.md) does not create Character Knowledge. Conversation memory is never evidence that recall occurred.

## Memory Record and Recall Manifestation

One **Autobiographical Memory Record** identifies a bounded remembered experience or coherent cluster from a source Life. It references the source Life, relevant historical events, participants, places, sensory conditions, provenance, and uncertainty without copying their authoritative facts.

The memory-continuity domain owns:

- stable Memory identity;
- source Soul and Life references;
- autobiographical content scope;
- significance and reinforcement history;
- cross-incarnation continuity and accessibility condition;
- associations capable of acting as cues;
- recall-manifestation history and provenance.

The Knowledge domain owns what a particular incarnation currently recalls, believes, misunderstands, or can consciously access. A **Recall Manifestation** therefore references the Memory ID and records the bounded representation available to that incarnation. It does not rewrite the source event or create a second historical truth.

Timeline and Campaign History own events. Relationships owns current relationship state. Life Archive owns Life indexing and finalized Life Summaries. Memory records reference those owners.

## Accessibility Conditions

Accessibility is qualitative and may vary by incarnation, context, and time.

| Condition | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Accessible** | The incarnation can deliberately recall a bounded representation under ordinary relevant conditions. |
| **Fading** | Access or detail is weakening through time, interference, disuse, or changed context. |
| **Dormant** | The memory persists but is not consciously available without a valid recovery route. |
| **Cued** | A relevant association has made some portion temporarily or newly accessible. |
| **Fragmentary** | Only partial sensory, emotional, conceptual, spatial, linguistic, or sequential content is accessible. |
| **Conflicted** | Accessible fragments or interpretations disagree, and the conflict remains unresolved. |
| **Suppressed** | An established bodily, magical, psychological, Soul-level, or information effect restricts access. |
| **Integrated** | The incarnation has repeatedly and meaningfully incorporated the memory into current identity while preserving its source. |

These are adjudication states, not a mandatory linear ladder. A memory may move among them. Integrated does not mean perfectly detailed or permanently immune to fading.

## Significance and Reinforcement

Persistence is favored when an experience materially shaped identity, attachment, survival, understanding, responsibility, sacrifice, creation, loss, or a major choice. Repetition can reinforce memory when it renews meaning or use; mere repeated exposure is not enough.

Relevant factors include:

- emotional and identity significance;
- deliberate reflection, rehearsal, commemoration, or retelling;
- repeated meaningful use of the remembered knowledge;
- enduring relationships and promises;
- unusual sensory or Soul-level associations;
- later events that reinterpret or reinforce the experience;
- support from compatible Soul Resonance, an Echo, or a trusted record;
- present embodiment's ability to represent the original experience.

Suffering, death, fear, violence, or repetition alone does not guarantee a strong memory. Trauma may intensify, fragment, distort, suppress, or sever access; it is not a memory-farming route.

## Fading and Ordinary Forgetting

Fading may result from elapsed time, lack of reinforcement, competing memories, changed language or cognition, radically different senses, embodiment mismatch, injury, magical interference, Soul harm, suppression, or many intervening Lives.

Fading can affect different parts independently. A name may vanish while affection remains; a route may persist without its location's meaning; a death may remain as fear without a reliable sequence. Missing detail remains Unknown rather than being completed from genre expectation or player knowledge.

Fading does not normally delete the source history. It changes current accessibility. True destruction or alteration of a Soul-level memory requires an established effect with authority, cost, provenance, and consequences.

## Cue-Triggered Recall

A **Memory Cue** is a present stimulus or condition meaningfully associated with a Memory Record. It may be sensory, emotional, relational, linguistic, spatial, magical, symbolic, procedural, or Soul-resonant.

When a possible cue appears, the GM:

1. identifies the current observer and relevant Memory ID or source history;
2. verifies that the cue has a defensible association rather than relying on superficial similarity;
3. considers significance, reinforcement, fading, embodiment, interference, current context, and access routes;
4. determines whether nothing, familiarity, emotion, a fragment, or bounded conscious recall emerges;
5. preserves uncertainty, distortion, and source limits;
6. updates the memory record and Character Knowledge only when access actually changes;
7. persists the complete Affected Set before durable narration closes.

A cue creates an opportunity, not a guaranteed retrieval command. Repeating a cue without new meaning has diminishing value and may produce no further recall.

## Fragmentary and Uncertain Recall

Recall reports subjective access, not objective proof. A fragment may preserve:

- a sensation without chronology;
- emotion without identity;
- a face without a name;
- a name without present recognition;
- a technique's context without current capability;
- a remembered belief that was false in the source Life;
- several incompatible interpretations.

The GM records accessible representation, confidence, source, and known limitations. Investigation may compare recall with historical evidence, but later confirmation updates Knowledge through a valid route rather than retroactively making the original memory perfect.

## Strong Persistent Memories

Some memories remain unusually accessible because they are repeatedly reinforced, deeply identity-forming, supported by Soul structures, or repeatedly encountered across Lives. Strength increases durability and retrieval likelihood; it does not guarantee exact wording, perfect chronology, unrestricted access at birth, or immunity to embodiment and interference.

No memory automatically grants omniscience about everyone involved. A remembered promise reveals the rememberer's experience, not the other person's hidden motives or current wishes.

## System Boundaries and Interactions

- **Reincarnation:** carries the same Soul and its qualifying memory continuity into a new embodiment; it does not expose every Memory Record immediately.
- **Life Archive:** indexes established Life history for authorized players and GMs. It is not the character's mind and does not determine recall.
- **Soul Echoes:** preserve a completed Life's imprint and perspective. Echo testimony or Overlay may cue or contextualize memory, but an Echo is not a Memory Record and does not make its whole Echo Core current Character Knowledge.
- **Retained Instincts:** carry body-shaped orientation without narrated recollection. An instinct may operate while its source memory is Dormant.
- **Retained Development and Skill crossover:** familiarity and redevelopment support may operate without autobiographical recall. Remembering mastery neither restores anatomy nor grants current Skill access or reliability.
- **Soul Resonance:** may provide a valid access condition or cue when alignment exists; it neither fabricates a memory nor guarantees accuracy.
- **Soul Depth:** may support integration and complexity but is not a memory-capacity meter or immunity to forgetting.
- **Soul Titles and Soul Weapons:** may preserve associations and supply cues through established relationships. They do not grant general historical access.
- **Relationships:** owns present trust, hostility, promises, and other current dimensions. Remembering an old relationship does not restore it, compel recognition, or bind another incarnation.
- **Timeline and Campaign History:** own what occurred. Memory preserves a subject's autobiographical access to it.
- **Long-Horizon Summaries:** aid historical retrieval but remain non-authoritative for recall and do not bypass Knowledge filtering.
- **Context Assembly:** reads relevant memory and Knowledge records when recall could matter, but GM access stays backstage and perspective-filtered.
- **[Soul-bound companion continuity](SOUL_BOUND_COMPANIONS.md):** the persistent bond may create relevant cues, but it guarantees only eventual reunion, not recognition, memory, trust, or a current Relationship.

## Logical Persistence Contract

Storage technology is not canonical. A normalized implementation should be able to represent:

- stable Memory ID, Soul ID, source Life ID, and optional source-event references;
- bounded autobiographical content and protected source references;
- significance basis and reinforcement events;
- accessibility condition by receiving incarnation;
- cue associations and their provenance;
- Recall Manifestations with form, scope, confidence, time, and Knowledge reference;
- suppression, damage, recovery, revision, and validation history;
- uncertainty and source-recovery requirements.

Stable references should connect memories to Entities, Lives, places, Relationships, and events. Mutable facts must not be copied into the memory record as a competing authority.

An implementation may use structures equivalent to `autobiographical_memories`, `memory_associations`, `memory_reinforcements`, and `recall_manifestations`. These are logical names, not a required migration. This reusable repository contains no populated campaign database, so FR-015 requires no campaign-specific schema migration.

## Save and Migration Rules

When recall accessibility changes, the Affected Set includes the Memory Record, the receiving incarnation's Character Knowledge, relevant source references, and any material Timeline or Relationship consequence. Unchanged history is not rewritten.

Migration preserves stable identity, source Life, provenance, accessibility by incarnation, uncertainty, cue history, and Knowledge separation. Missing memory data remains `Unknown`, `Not Yet Verified`, or `Requires Source Recovery`; it is never inferred from player access or a Life Summary.

## Safeguards

- Do not convert Life Archive access, GM context, transcripts, summaries, or player memory into Character Knowledge.
- Do not treat Reincarnation as perfect recall or total amnesia by default.
- Do not let recalled information erase current-life identity or decide the character's response.
- Do not grant Skills, attributes, anatomy, authority, or relationship standing through memory alone.
- Do not farm memory through repeated death, trauma, cue spam, disposable Lives, or rote exposure.
- Do not treat subjective recall as objective proof or a route around GM Secrets.
- Do not invent missing detail to make a fragment narratively smooth.
- Do not duplicate event, Relationship, location, or current-state ownership inside memory records.
- Do not guarantee recognition of a reincarnated Soul, companion, enemy, place, or object without a valid access route.
- Do not implement an exhaustive belief, testimony, or misinformation engine through this subsystem.

## Worked Cases

### The Forge Smell

A former smith reincarnates in a body with unfamiliar hands. Hot scale and charcoal smoke cue a Fragmentary memory of judging metal color. The recollection may guide a question or experiment, but retained development and present practice determine whether smithing competence returns.

### The Unrecognized Friend

The character meets a Soul once loved in another Life. No automatic recognition occurs. A shared phrase later cues warmth and an uncertain image, while Relationships still begins from the current meeting and develops through current choices.

### The Player Reads the Archive

The player opens an authorized Life Summary naming an old betrayal. The current character has no recall route and cannot act on the betrayer's identity. The GM keeps the fact available for adjudication without leaking it through narration.

### Familiar Hands Without a Scene

A swordsman feels that a guard position is familiar but remembers no former teacher or duel. Retained Skill familiarity may support redevelopment; FR-015 records no conscious autobiographical recall until a valid cue produces one.

### A Memory That Changed Meaning

An old monarch strongly remembers ordering a retreat as cowardice. Current-life historical evidence reveals that the withdrawal saved civilians. The original shame remains authentic autobiographical content, while Character Knowledge gains new evidence and interpretation.

### Centuries of Fading

A childhood song survives across many Lives only as rhythm and safety. Hearing the original language produces a name fragment, not the entire childhood. Continued investigation and meaningful rehearsal may reinforce access without guaranteeing complete recovery.

## Validation

Validate that:

- every Memory Record has one stable Soul and source Life;
- current conscious recall belongs to the receiving incarnation's Knowledge view;
- source events and mutable facts remain references to their owners;
- player and GM access do not create Character Knowledge;
- recall changes have provenance and a valid route;
- accessibility and confidence are not treated as objective truth;
- missing detail remains unknown;
- retained familiarity remains distinct from recollection;
- current Relationships and current-life agency are preserved;
- no FR-016 bond mechanics have been implemented.
