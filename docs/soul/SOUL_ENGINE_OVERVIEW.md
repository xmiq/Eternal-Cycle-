# Soul Engine Overview

The Soul Engine governs identity and progression that can persist across death.

Its governing rules are defined in [Soul Fundamentals](SOUL_FUNDAMENTALS.md). Persistence listed here means survival across lives, not guaranteed immediate access.

## Persistent Elements

Subject to their detailed rules, the following may persist:

- skills and Skill XP;
- retained Stat XP;
- Soul Resonance;
- Soul Depth;
- Soul Echoes;
- [Soul Titles](SOUL_TITLES.md);
- [Retained Instincts](RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md);
- [Soul Constellations](SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md);
- Soul Weapons and Weapon Echoes;
- [Akashic Archive](AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) Attunements and established access;
- genuine [Soul Avatar](SOUL_AVATARS.md) emergence and the conditional capacity for Avatar Expression.

## Temporary Elements

A normal death ends or removes:

- the current body;
- bodily statistics;
- ordinary inventory and money;
- current legal and social identity;
- temporary titles;
- most reputation;
- uncompleted plans and obligations.

## Design Constraint

Persistence must create continuity, not immunity from consequence. Every death should close possibilities while opening others.

## Interaction Rule

When several soul systems contribute to one outcome, use [Soul System Interactions](SOUL_SYSTEM_INTERACTIONS.md) to identify the Owning System and resolve agency, provenance, permission, access, capacity, embodiment, consent, cost, and world consequence without duplicate benefits or silent overrides.

## Safeguard Rule

Persistent breadth never creates unlimited present throughput. Use [Soul Engine Safeguards](SOUL_ENGINE_SAFEGUARDS.md) to resist farming, stacking, coercion, unfair soul harm, and transitions that erase causal consequences while preserving genuinely earned synergy.
