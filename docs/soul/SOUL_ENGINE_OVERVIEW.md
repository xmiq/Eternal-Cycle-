# Soul Engine Overview

The Soul Engine governs identity and progression that can persist across death.

Its governing rules are defined in [Soul Fundamentals](SOUL_FUNDAMENTALS.md). Persistence listed here means survival across lives, not guaranteed immediate access.

## Persistent Elements

Subject to their detailed rules, the following may persist:

- skills and Skill XP;
- retained Stat XP;
- Soul Resonance;
- Soul Depth;
- Soul Echoes;
- Soul Titles;
- retained instincts;
- Soul Constellations;
- Soul Weapons and Weapon Echoes;
- access to the Akashic Archive.

## Temporary Elements

A normal death ends or removes:

- the current body;
- bodily statistics;
- ordinary inventory and money;
- current legal and social identity;
- temporary titles;
- most reputation;
- uncompleted plans and obligations.

## Design Constraint

Persistence must create continuity, not immunity from consequence. Every death should close possibilities while opening others.
