# Soul Engine Safeguards

## Purpose

This document defines the shared exploit-resistance and narrative-consequence rules for the Soul Engine. It protects earned progression without allowing reincarnation, accumulated history, internal systems, or symbolic authority to erase present embodiment, agency, risk, or world causality.

## Core Rule

Persistent soul progression expands what may become relevant in future lives. It does not grant unlimited present access, simultaneous expression, automatic success, immunity from loss, control over other souls, or freedom from consequences.

When an interaction appears abusable, identify its [Owning System](SOUL_SYSTEM_INTERACTIONS.md#system-ownership-map), preserve every genuinely earned element, and enforce that system's formation, access, embodiment, consent, cost, and consequence rules. Do not answer a rules gap with an unrelated punishment or retroactive confiscation.

## Shared Safeguard Principles

### Earned Basis

Every persistent benefit needs the genuine history required by its Owning System. Repetition, danger, suffering, death, fame, possession, consumption, imitation, or a dramatic name is not a substitute for meaningful development and integration.

One event may legitimately affect several systems when it satisfies each system's distinct requirements. It is not counted several times to multiply the same mastery, action, authority, or reward.

### Persistence Is Not Access

An earned Soul Imprint, Echo, Title, Link, Attunement, instinct, bond, or Avatar Association may survive while remaining inaccessible, unclear, incompatible, or irrelevant in the current life.

Loss of access does not erase protected progression. Persistence does not waive the route back to expression.

### Breadth Is Not Throughput

Many lives can create broad potential, diverse counsel, and more possible responses. They do not create unlimited active capacity.

Present expression remains bounded by:

- the current body's anatomy, senses, statistics, health, and species;
- developed skills and compatible progression paths;
- Soul Depth and current Soul Integrity;
- target-specific Soul Resonance and valid access;
- attention, time, resources, tools, and environmental opportunity;
- consent and cooperation from distinct souls;
- the ordinary action economy of the scene.

An ancient soul may have more relevant options than a young one while still being able to choose and perform only a bounded number of them now.

### Embodiment Is Real

A new body is not a cosmetic wrapper. Its needs, instincts, limitations, mortality, culture, and developmental opportunities are part of the current incarnation.

Past experience may guide translation. It cannot create missing anatomy, skip current calibration, merge human and monster progression trees, or make every former technique equally effective in every form.

### Agency and Consent Persist

The current incarnation chooses deliberate present action. Echoes, instincts, Titles, Associations, prophecies, institutions, gods, and internal majorities may offer pressure, information, emotion, or consequence without becoming the controlling self.

Weapon Souls, Constellation members, visitors, and every other distinct soul retain their own consent. Past intimacy, ownership, command, worship, or hostility creates no present claim.

### Costs Are Consequences, Not Currency

Death, sacrifice, Soul Strain, Dissonance, Soul Wounds, overload, and loss do not award progress merely because they were endured. Meaningful response and integration may later qualify under an Owning System, but the cost itself is not spendable proof of growth.

Repeating a known cost cannot repeatedly purchase the same transformation.

### The World Keeps Its History

Final Death, Interlife, time skips, and World Resets transform circumstances without rolling the world back to a preferred state. Survivors, institutions, ecosystems, ruins, myths, debts, injuries, and causal chains continue or change through established causes.

The soul's continuity does not make the world wait for it.

## Exploit-Resistance Tests

### Death and Reincarnation Loops

Repeated suicide, disposable incarnations, engineered Final Death, or deliberately weak lives do not improve Reincarnation, deepen the soul, strengthen Resonance, mature Titles, or produce useful Echoes and instincts by themselves.

Every completed incarnation normally forms an Echo, but a shallow or brief life produces a correspondingly sparse perspective. Quantity cannot be converted into wisdom, active advisers, extra actions, or a Soul Avatar qualification.

A character may still choose death for a legitimate strategic, moral, or personal reason. The GM should respect the choice while resolving bodily loss, unfinished relationships, worldly reaction, Interlife uncertainty, and the next valid embodiment. Denying an automatic reward is not the same as denying the choice.

### Shallow Collection

Collecting species, skills, Titles, Echoes, instincts, Archive Records, relationships, or weapon forms creates no completion bonus, set bonus, universal rank, or guaranteed evolution.

Broad history matters when a present claim has valid provenance, access, relevance, and expression. Irrelevant or weakly integrated history may remain dormant. Repeated collection without changed meaning receives diminishing narrative attention and no automatic Deepening.

### Circular Qualification

No system may create the sole prerequisite used to establish itself.

- a proposed Title cannot prove its own Title Basis;
- a desired Constellation cannot create the recurrence needed to form it;
- imagined Soul Space architecture cannot create its functional source;
- claimed Archive authority cannot grant the access that proves the claim;
- a proposed Avatar Association cannot manufacture the histories it is said to integrate;
- a desired incarnation cannot create its own valid embodiment route.

An independent history or already-valid rule must start the chain.

### Duplicate Benefits and Stacking

Several systems may contribute different functions to one result. They cannot multiply one underlying benefit.

An Echo may provide perspective, a Retained Instinct may orient attention, Resonance may provide access, a Title may create contextual recognition, and an Archive Record may corroborate history. If the requested outcome is a developed technique, Development still owns that capability and awards it once.

Different names, sources, symbols, or presentation layers do not create separate action economies, repeated mastery, cumulative authority, or several copies of one resistance. Independent benefits may combine only when each has its own Basis, owner, expression, and cost.

### Parallel Action and Internal Time

Soul Echoes, an Echo Chorus, Soul Space, a Constellation Commons, Archive contact, Avatar Expression, and a Weapon Soul do not create free parallel turns, passive labor, duplicated concentration, stopped external time, or an invisible workforce.

Internal activity consumes real attention and meaningful time. Divided attention can narrow awareness or performance. A supporting perspective may improve one action without becoming an additional action.

### Embodiment and Progression-Tree Bypass

Retained Skill XP and Stat XP support redevelopment under their own rules. They do not reproduce former bodily statistics at birth or ignore the current species' structure.

Cross-species expression requires a compatible route and present development. Translation may produce a new technique rather than a literal copy. Where no route exists, the old potential remains dormant.

Human and monster progression remain distinct until a later explicit crossover rule permits a specific bridge. Reincarnation, Resonance, an instinct, an Echo, an Archive Record, or Avatar Expression cannot invent that bridge by implication.

### Information and Omniscience

Every memory, warning, instinct, prophecy, Record, and recognition retains provenance.

- Echoes know their source lives and later context they genuinely received.
- Retained Instincts carry response patterns rather than factual explanations.
- Resonance follows a specific Target and Basis.
- Archive Records preserve source-bound evidence, not automatic objective truth.
- Titles and Constellations reveal only what a valid recognition method can perceive.
- Soul Avatars synthesize bounded contributors rather than the whole soul at once.

No combination grants an unbounded request for every useful fact, another soul's private interior, or a settled future. Corroboration improves confidence without erasing source limitations.

### Agency, Possession, and Compulsion

Internal influence may shift attention, emotion, posture, urgency, memory, or interpretation. It cannot select the player's deliberate speech, values, allegiance, or action unless an explicit hostile or consensual rule owns that transfer.

Calling control an instinct, destiny, true self, Avatar state, former bond, divine command, or majority decision does not bypass agency. Hostile control needs a defined source, route, scope, warning, resistance, duration, ending condition, and aftermath.

The same protection applies to nonplayer characters. Recognition or former intimacy does not convert another incarnation into an ally, enemy, reward, dependent, or romantic claim.

### Symbolic Authority and Destiny

Soul Titles, Avatar Associations, Archive recognition, prophecies, and Constellations may make a soul socially or historically important. They do not grant obedience, legal office, moral correctness, universal relevance, or a predetermined role.

Narrative pressure must enter through actors and systems with information, beliefs, incentives, and capabilities. A temple may demand service, a monster nation may request judgment, or a rival may issue a challenge. The soul may refuse, and the world may then react through ordinary causality.

### Harm Farming

Provoking Dissonance, overload, Soul Intrusion, title conflict, damaged Echo contact, dangerous Archive readings, or Soul Wounds does not guarantee Depth, evolution, fusion, mutation, or improved resistance.

Recovery can create meaningful development when it changes and integrates the soul. Reopening the same injury to repeat its reward is repetition, not a new transformation.

### World Reset and Time-Skip Escape

A reset or time skip cannot be used to erase an inconvenient war, debt, ecological collapse, relationship, institution, Soul Wound, Archive Trace, or historical responsibility without an established cause that actually changes it.

Compression may reduce detail, not causality. The GM must preserve a discoverable bridge between the former state and the new Age, including what survived, transformed, was forgotten, or was destroyed and why.

## Pressure Without Coercion

Soul systems should make choices harder and richer, not choose for the player.

The GM may present:

- a Retained Instinct's urgent cue;
- an Echo's demand, warning, memory, or refusal;
- several incompatible internal perspectives;
- a Title's social expectations;
- a Constellation's recognition and emotional history;
- a Soul Avatar's involuntary onset before stabilization;
- bodily preparation or another micro-response explicitly permitted by a rule;
- clear consequences attached to accepting or refusing an opportunity.

The GM must not:

- declare which internal voice is the character's only true self;
- resolve a deliberate choice through an internal vote;
- dictate speech, morality, intimacy, loyalty, or identity because a soul mechanic is active;
- confiscate unrelated progression because the player rejects an Echo, Title, bond, or institution;
- treat refusal as system failure when refusal is a protected option;
- use hidden canon to invalidate a reasonable choice retroactively.

An involuntary effect can still be dangerous. Its rule must distinguish the initial sensation or bodily response from deliberate conduct and provide the resistance and recovery appropriate to its scope.

## Soul Harm Review

Before applying lasting soul harm, establish:

1. **source:** the being, event, system, or condition capable of causing it;
2. **route:** how that source reaches the soul and why ordinary defenses do not simply prevent contact;
3. **target:** the specific Link, Echo, Title, Region, bond, Imprint, access channel, or part of Integrity at risk;
4. **warning:** signs that make the danger discoverable before irreversible loss;
5. **resistance:** meaningful choices, defenses, escape, mitigation, or response;
6. **scope:** what suppression, distortion, injury, severance, erasure, or fragmentation can actually occur;
7. **aftermath:** evidence, worldly consequences, and a response or recovery path appropriate to the harm;
8. **persistence:** what remains protected, what becomes inaccessible, and what has genuinely changed.

Fair warning does not require a complete rules explanation. It requires consistent signs proportionate to the danger. The more permanent the threatened loss, the clearer and earlier the opportunity to understand and respond should be.

Use increasing scrutiny for increasing severity:

- temporary fatigue, Dissonance, or Soul Strain follows an established overload or conflict;
- a Soul Wound requires a named cause, bounded injury, warning, resistance, and appropriate recovery;
- permanent severance, erasure, or destruction of protected progression requires a narrow exceptional rule and consequences proportionate to the loss;
- total soul annihilation requires an explicitly adopted campaign premise and is never inferred from ordinary danger.

Recovery need not be quick, painless, or guaranteed to restore the former expression exactly. It must address the cause and leave the player able to make meaningful choices about response. Soul harm cannot appear retroactively merely to remove a capability that became inconvenient for the GM.

## Narrative Consequences

### Final Death

Final Death should close real possibilities. The body, biological growth, active position, immediate plans, ordinary possessions, and many forms of contact end. Survivors grieve, adapt, exploit the absence, continue the work, or change course according to their own lives.

Persistent soul growth makes another life possible; it does not refund the life that ended.

### A New Incarnation

Every new incarnation begins as the current active self in a real body and context. Former memories and potential may return unevenly. Local upbringing, needs, species, relationships, and development must have room to matter before old history dominates the life.

Former allies may be dead, changed, unreachable, skeptical, or unaware. Former enemies may have won, collapsed, become institutions, or survived only as myths. Property, rank, consent, reputation, and unfinished authority remain with the former identity and world unless a valid current route changes them.

### Accumulated Significance

Greater soul history may attract attention, responsibility, fear, worship, regulation, predation, or requests for help when an actor has a valid way to notice it. These are causal consequences, not automatic encounter scaling.

The GM should not spawn stronger enemies merely because the soul grew. Threats should arise because institutions adapt, predators detect a useful target, rivals learn, old actions persist, or wider responsibilities expose the incarnation to greater stakes.

An ancient soul is not automatically central to every crisis. Events outside its knowledge and reach continue without waiting for it.

### Relationships Across Lives

Continuity can preserve grief, recognition, obligation, resentment, and hope while every current person remains free to redefine the relationship. Reconnection requires a world route and present choices.

Separation is a valid consequence. Some relationships end permanently in practice even if a Constellation Thread or memory persists.

### Knowledge Across Ages

Old knowledge can be valuable, obsolete, misunderstood, politically dangerous, or impossible to express under new natural laws. Access creates investigation and responsibility rather than an automatic solution.

Public proof of past-life or Archive knowledge may alter religion, law, scholarship, diplomacy, and security. Those changes occur through witnesses and institutions, not because the information entered a character sheet.

### Time Skips and World Resets

When compressing years or changing Ages, establish:

- the pressures that caused the transition;
- what major actors attempted;
- which causal chains continued, changed direction, or ended;
- what survived materially, socially, ecologically, spiritually, and in the Archive;
- what was lost and by what cause;
- how later cultures remember or misinterpret the former Age;
- which consequences now create playable opportunities and threats.

A new genre, magic law, technology, species order, or geography should transform old advantages rather than merely delete inconvenient history.

## Stress Tests and Rulings

### Disposable Monster Lives

A reincarnator repeatedly chooses short spider lives and engineers early death to collect web instincts and Echoes.

Each valid life may leave a sparse Echo, but death awards no instinct, Depth, Resonance, Avatar qualification, or candidate advantage. A Retained Instinct forms only from meaningful embodied adaptation and integration. Near-identical shallow Origins do not combine into perfect web sense, and every death leaves ordinary world consequences.

### The Stacked Crisis

A Soul Avatar bearing **Monster King** consults a warlord Echo, enters Soul Space, reads an Archive Fragment, and cooperates with a Soul Weapon during one battle.

Each system contributes only its own function. The Title creates relevant recognition, the Echo offers perspective, Soul Space organizes contact, the Archive provides bounded evidence, and the Weapon Soul may cooperate. Avatar Expression synthesizes them, but the current body still performs one bounded sequence of actions with its present skills, resources, fatigue, and risks. The same battle history does not grant repeated mastery or authority.

### The Endless Council

A character enters Soul Space with hundreds of Echoes and claims centuries of private planning before an enemy's next strike.

External time continues. Only a manageable relevant set of Echoes can participate clearly, internal work consumes attention, and no Chorus creates parallel labor. The GM may allow one brief warning or already-prepared response if the timing supports it, not unlimited deliberation.

### The Returning Beloved

A soul recognizes a former partner and repeatedly follows the new incarnation to force a Constellation reunion.

Recognition reveals only the pursuer's side. Stalking cannot create reciprocal transformation, strengthen a Thread, restore intimacy, or grant location knowledge. The other incarnation may refuse contact, and social or legal consequences proceed normally.

### The Manufactured Awakening

A wielder arranges repeated lethal duels with healers ready in order to force a weapon to awaken.

Risk alone is insufficient. Without genuine mutual transformation and an emerging Weapon Soul, no awakening or intertwining occurs. The weapon may be damaged, witnesses may intervene, and the wielder bears the consequences of engineered violence.

### Resetting an Empire

A reincarnator destabilizes the world hoping a Reset will erase an enemy empire and all personal debts.

World Stability and Reset rules, not player desire, own the transition. If a Reset occurs, the empire may fall, adapt, migrate, persist through successor institutions, or leave Archive and cultural consequences. The former life's debts and enemies remain world facts and affect a new incarnation only through valid causal routes; the Reset is not retroactive absolution.

## GM Safeguard Procedure

When a Soul Engine result appears unusually strong or exploitable:

1. state the exact result being claimed;
2. identify the genuine history supporting it;
3. identify one Owning System and each Supporting System;
4. remove circular prerequisites and duplicated benefits;
5. separate persistence, access, interpretation, expression, and consequence;
6. test current embodiment, Development, attention, time, resources, and action economy;
7. test current-incarnation agency and every distinct soul's consent;
8. establish the cost, risk, failure state, and world route;
9. ask whether repeating the setup would create automatic permanent growth;
10. ask whether the result erases a species, body, relationship, death, or Age as a meaningful constraint;
11. allow a strong result when all valid requirements are genuinely satisfied;
12. if a real loophole remains, clarify the Owning System rather than adding unrelated punishment.

An unusual combination is not an exploit merely because it is effective. Earned synergy should work. The safeguard test rejects unearned scope, duplication, coercion, and consequence avoidance while preserving creative play.
