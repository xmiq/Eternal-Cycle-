# Akashic Archive

## Purpose

The Akashic Archive preserves world-deep traces of lived events, consequences, identities, relationships, creations, and metaphysical change so that history can be discovered without becoming perfect omniscience or a replacement for investigation.

## Core Rule

The Akashic Archive is a persistent metaphysical record structure formed from traces left by reality as events occur and consequences unfold. An Archive reading presents bounded evidence with a source, perspective, resolution, and access path. It is not automatically complete, objective, correctly interpreted, or available to every soul.

Access always requires a specific target, a genuine basis for reaching it, a valid interface, compatible Soul Resonance, sufficient Soul Depth and Integrity, and any applicable permission or resistance. The Archive can preserve or reveal history; it cannot rewrite the past, invent missing knowledge, copy a soul, grant mastery, or make a reader the owner of what was recorded.

## Terms

### Akashic Archive

The world-deep metaphysical structure containing Archive Traces. Use **Akashic Archive** on first reference and **Archive** thereafter.

### Archive Trace

A persistent, source-bound imprint left by an event, participant, consequence, creation, relationship, place, system, or world law. A Trace is evidence from a limited origin rather than a complete account.

### Archive Record

A coherent reading assembled from one or more related Archive Traces through a valid interface. A Record is the result made available to a reader, not proof that every relevant Trace was found or understood.

### Archive Target

The specific event, person, soul history, object, place, relationship, technique, Age, world law, or pattern being approached.

### Archive Basis

The genuine connection that justifies approaching an Archive Target, such as direct participation, a Resonance Link, a traced object or place, a recognized Soul Title, an authorized role, an established research path, or permission from an applicable Archive system.

### Archive Interface

A place, artifact, ritual, being, institution, Soul Construct, or other explicit method capable of locating and translating some Archive Traces. Every Interface has a scope, method, limits, and possible bias.

### Archive Attunement

A persistent relationship with a specific Archive Interface, Record family, or access method. Attunement may survive reincarnation but does not guarantee present access, safe reading, or broader permission.

### Archive Annotation

Testimony, interpretation, classification, or cross-reference deliberately attached to a Trace or Record through an authorized Interface. An Annotation does not overwrite the underlying history or become true merely because it was admitted.

### Archive Custodian

A being, intelligence, institution, or system responsible for maintaining, interpreting, protecting, or granting access to part of the Archive. A Custodian has bounded jurisdiction and is not automatically omniscient, neutral, or infallible.

## What the Archive Records

Reality may leave Archive Traces through several sources:

- **experiential traces:** what a participant sensed, attempted, understood, feared, chose, or endured;
- **consequential traces:** changes left in bodies, places, ecosystems, institutions, relationships, objects, and later events;
- **witness traces:** what another being or established observing system perceived;
- **created traces:** works, techniques, oaths, Soul Weapons, rituals, writings, structures, and inventions carrying their making and use;
- **relational traces:** interactions that altered bonds, conflicts, communities, or recurring soul relationships;
- **systemic traces:** Reincarnation transitions, World Resets, changes in world law, divine action, or other explicit metaphysical events;
- **annotated traces:** later testimony and interpretation attached through a valid Interface.

Several sources may describe the same event differently. A soldier, prey animal, fortress wall, healing relic, and weather system may each leave a different Trace of one battle. Their disagreement is information, not a defect to be silently flattened.

The Archive need not privilege fame, violence, or public history. A private choice may leave a strong Trace through the soul that made it and the consequences it caused. A famous but heavily mythologized event may be surrounded by clear public Annotations while its direct experiential Traces remain sparse or inaccessible.

Not every ordinary moment needs a playable Record. Trivial events may leave traces too faint, common, disconnected, or low-resolution to retrieve usefully. The GM should track only Archive material that matters to play and should not invent perfect detail merely because the Archive exists.

## Trace Provenance and Resolution

Every useful Trace has provenance. The GM should be able to identify:

- what left the Trace;
- when and under what conditions it formed;
- what that source could perceive or know;
- whether it records experience, consequence, testimony, interpretation, or system activity;
- what has obscured, translated, annotated, damaged, or connected it since formation.

Resolution describes the kind and amount of detail a Trace can support. Resolution is qualitative and source-specific.

- A footprint may preserve direction, pressure, and material disturbance without preserving motive.
- A Soul Echo may supply personal testimony but is not itself an Archive Trace or objective record.
- A ritual observer may record magical structure while missing the participants' private intentions.
- A World Reset may leave a broad systemic Trace while erasing the interfaces needed to interpret local lives.

Combining sources may improve context but does not guarantee certainty. Missing Traces, incompatible categories, translation failure, deliberate deception, and different points of view can leave a Record unresolved.

## Trace Formation and Historical Importance

Historical importance can strengthen discoverability when an event creates broad, enduring, or system-level consequences, but importance is not a points threshold and does not determine truth.

Meaning, participation, witness, consequence, repetition, creation, destruction, and metaphysical significance may all affect what Traces exist and how they connect. None functions as a universal requirement. A deed can be well recorded without changing the participating soul, and a soul can be transformed by a deed that the outside world barely notices.

Manufacturing spectacle does not guarantee a useful Record. A staged battle may create genuine Traces of staging, fear, injury, and deception rather than the heroic history its organizers intended. Repetition may create a pattern Record when the repeated acts have real causal relation, but copying the same harmless action does not manufacture deep Archive authority.

## Access Requirements

An Archive reading requires all of the following:

1. **Target:** The reader approaches something specific enough to distinguish from unrelated history.
2. **Basis:** A genuine Archive Basis connects the reader or Interface to that Target.
3. **Interface:** An established method can locate and translate the relevant kind of Trace.
4. **Scope:** The requested time, perspective, question, or Record family is bounded.
5. **Resonance:** A valid Resonance Link and present Trigger support contact where the method requires them.
6. **Capacity:** Soul Depth and current Soul Integrity can contain the attempted reading.
7. **Permission:** Any Custodian, participant, protected source, or system-level access condition is satisfied or engaged through an explicit contested rule.
8. **Consequence:** The time, attention, strain, exposure, obligation, cost, or risk of the method is accepted.

Desire, curiosity, resemblance, possession of an unrelated relic, or knowing a name does not supply a Basis. An Interface may help discover a narrower valid Target from available evidence, but it cannot answer an unrestricted request for every useful secret.

Physical and scholarly investigation can establish Archive Bases. Recovering a weapon from a battlefield, comparing several inscriptions, interviewing a surviving witness, and identifying a matching Resonance Link may together justify a reading that none could support alone. The Archive should reward engagement with the world rather than replace it.

## Reading Modes

Reading Modes describe the scope and method of one contact, not permanent ranks or a universal Archive power level.

### Trace Contact

The reader detects that a relevant Trace exists and may receive a quality, direction, symbol, emotion, or identifying feature. Contact does not reveal a coherent event.

### Fragment Reading

The Interface translates a bounded portion of one Trace, preserving its source limitations. A Fragment may be vivid while lacking context.

### Record Reading

Several compatible Traces are assembled into a coherent account of a defined Target and scope. Gaps, conflicts, and provenance remain visible or discoverable.

### Comparative Reading

The reader examines contradictory Records or Trace families together, testing provenance and consequences without forcing one answer. This mode often requires time, expertise, several Bases, or cooperation among Interfaces.

### World-Bearing Reading

The reader engages a Trace tied to an Age, World Reset, world law, or comparable world-scale structure. It requires explicit access, World-Bearing Resonance, sufficient Soul Depth, and severe scope discipline. It does not grant control of the structure being read.

A reader may qualify for Comparative Reading of one familiar event while being incapable of Trace Contact with an unrelated one. Repeated readings do not automatically advance a soul through these modes.

## Interpretation and Truth

An Archive Record is evidence. Its reliability depends on provenance, resolution, Interface quality, translation, completeness, and the question asked.

A Record may be:

- accurate within a narrow perspective;
- incomplete because relevant Traces are absent or inaccessible;
- contradictory because participants experienced the event differently;
- mistranslated across species, Ages, magic systems, or forms of cognition;
- distorted by a damaged Link, Soul Wound, hostile Interface, or corrupted index;
- surrounded by false or biased Annotations;
- precise about consequence while silent about intention;
- authentic but irrelevant to the conclusion a reader wants to draw.

Vividness, age, divine presentation, institutional certification, or emotional force does not guarantee truth. The GM must not invent hidden falsity solely to invalidate a reasonable decision. Every distortion needs an established source and consistent, discoverable signs.

The Archive does not record the unrealized future as settled fact. It may preserve plans, prophecies, causal patterns, simulations, or predictions as Traces of those things. Readers may infer likely outcomes, but the prediction remains an interpretation and cannot remove player or world agency.

## Privacy, Souls, and Personal History

Worldly actions and consequences may leave Archive Traces even when a participant would prefer secrecy. This does not make every private thought, memory, or soul structure publicly readable.

An Archive reading cannot by default:

- enter another soul's private Soul Space or Echo Core;
- read thoughts that left no applicable Trace;
- copy a soul, Soul Echo, consciousness, or identity;
- compel testimony from a participant;
- restore consent, intimacy, authority, or ownership from a former relationship;
- expose every incarnation associated with a soul merely by identifying one life.

Access to protected first-person material requires the participant's permission, a legitimate posthumous or system-level rule, or an explicit contested method with meaningful resistance and proportionate consequences. A Trace of what someone did is not unrestricted access to who they are.

Archive recognition of a soul establishes evidence of continuity only within the method's scope. It does not make the reader that soul's judge, owner, heir, or destined companion.

## Knowledge, Techniques, and Development

An Archive Record may preserve demonstrations, principles, failures, instructions, or the experience of creating a technique. Reading that material supplies information only to the degree the Record and reader can support.

Archive access does not automatically grant:

- Skill XP, Stat XP, bodily development, or Soul Depth;
- the sensory categories or cognition of the original practitioner;
- missing anatomy, mana, tools, materials, or species traits;
- the tacit understanding earned through practice;
- a human, monster, magical, or professional progression-tree unlock;
- safe use of a dangerous method.

A reader may use a Record as instruction and then develop through real practice, experimentation, embodiment, and consequence. An inaccessible technique can remain historically understood yet presently unusable.

## Interfaces and Custodians

Archive Interfaces may take many forms, including ruins, world trees, divine courts, memory wells, observatories, living libraries, monster organs, ritual lineages, Soul Constructs, Weapon Souls, or constructed intelligences.

Every Interface must define:

- the kinds of Traces it can locate;
- the Basis it accepts;
- how it translates nonhuman or obsolete experience;
- what scope and Reading Modes it supports;
- who controls or maintains it;
- what it costs or risks;
- how errors, omissions, and tampering can be detected;
- how access is ended or revoked.

A Custodian may protect privacy, demand service, enforce law, ration dangerous knowledge, favor a culture, misunderstand an alien Trace, or pursue its own purpose. Custodian authority applies only where an established Archive rule gives it jurisdiction. No Custodian receives universal knowledge or moral correctness by title alone.

An Interface inside Soul Space is still an Interface. Its symbolic form cannot create Traces, permission, safety, or truth that the underlying connection does not possess.

## Annotation, Tampering, and Damage

Authorized participants may attach Archive Annotations to preserve testimony, dispute an interpretation, classify a Record, link related Traces, or warn future readers. The Annotation retains its author and method as provenance.

Annotation cannot:

- rewrite the event that occurred;
- erase an underlying Trace merely by contradicting it;
- make a false claim true;
- alter a Soul Echo, Soul Title, relationship, or world consequence;
- change world law or the past by editing a Record.

Tampering most often affects an Interface, index, translation, access route, or Annotation rather than reality's underlying Trace. A regime may hide Records, a cult may add false interpretations, or a damaged Interface may merge unrelated symbols.

Sealing, corrupting, severing, or erasing an underlying Archive Trace requires an explicit world-deep or soul-level rule proportionate to that loss. It must identify the affected Trace, provide fair warning and meaningful resistance where participants can respond, and leave consequences and evidence. Destroying one library or Interface does not automatically destroy the Archive material it once reached.

## Attunement, Reincarnation, and World Resets

Archive Attunements and legitimate Resonance Links may persist through Final Death, Life Reconciliation, Reincarnation, Interlife, and World Resets as soul-level relationships. Persistence does not guarantee that the next body can perceive the Interface, that the Interface still exists, or that its former permission remains socially valid.

During Reincarnation:

- a known Record family may remain a valid Target while its Link falls Silent;
- an Archive role or oath may persist only when its explicit rule binds the soul rather than the former office;
- an Interface tied to a destroyed body, institution, or location may become inaccessible;
- memory of a reading may return separately from the Attunement that made it possible;
- Archive contact cannot select an invalid incarnation or bypass Reincarnation Mode.

World Resets normally create powerful systemic Traces and preserve earlier Archive strata. A Reset may change indexing, language, metaphysical law, available Interfaces, and the compatibility required to read old Records. It does not automatically erase prior Ages or make all of their history accessible.

Interlife contact remains subject to Reincarnation, Resonance, Depth, Integrity, Interface, time, and access rules. It cannot provide unlimited research outside the continuing world.

## Exposure, Failure, and Recovery

Archive contact can fail or harm without making every reading punitive.

Possible consequences include:

- sensory overload or incomplete translation;
- memory bleed between Record and personal experience;
- mistaken identification with a recorded participant;
- Dissonance among contradictory Traces;
- false certainty produced by a persuasive Interface;
- attention from a Custodian, god, institution, soul predator, or rival reader;
- temporary loss of access;
- Soul Strain;
- a Soul Wound when severe exposure or hostile interference has been properly established.

Recovery may require rest, grounding in the current body, comparison with worldly evidence, repair of a Link or Interface, help from an Echo or Weapon Soul, removal of a hostile Annotation, reconciliation of a destabilizing truth, or treatment appropriate to a Soul Wound.

High Soul Depth increases capacity but does not make a reading harmless or correct. A world-deep soul can misunderstand a narrow human life, and a modest soul may safely read one intimately connected Fragment.

## System Interactions

- **[Soul Fundamentals](SOUL_FUNDAMENTALS.md):** The Archive records traces without duplicating souls, replacing current agency, or waiving Soul Integrity and harm rules.
- **[Reincarnation](REINCARNATION.md):** Archive Attunement may persist as a soul-level relationship while embodiment, world state, and access remain conditional.
- **[Soul Depth](SOUL_DEPTH.md):** Depth governs how much complexity a reader can integrate, not which Records it may access or whether they are true.
- **[Soul Resonance](SOUL_RESONANCE.md):** Resonance supplies target-specific alignment and may support World-Bearing contact; it cannot create an Archive Basis or Interface by itself.
- **[Soul Echoes](SOUL_ECHOES.md):** An Echo offers bounded personal testimony. It may help interpret a Record but is not an objective Archive source or a replacement for provenance.
- **[Soul Space](SOUL_SPACE.md):** A valid Archive Interface may appear within Soul Space without turning imagined architecture into access or reality editing.
- **[Soul Constellations](SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md):** Records may corroborate recurring intersections but cannot create a Constellation, force recognition, or expose another member's private soul.
- **[Soul Titles](SOUL_TITLES.md):** Records may corroborate a Title Basis or preserve prior names; recording, fame, or Archive recognition does not form or grant a title.
- **[Retained Instincts](RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md):** Records may contextualize an Instinct Origin, while instinct alone cannot retrieve objective history.
- **[Soul Avatars](SOUL_AVATARS.md):** Avatar Expression may compare relevant Records with lived testimony, but still needs a Target, Basis, Interface, scope, permission, and sufficient Integrity.
- **Soul Weapons:** A Weapon Soul may contribute its own testimony, serve as a limited Interface, or share an Attunement according to its bond; it remains a distinct person rather than an Archive device by default.
- **Development and skills:** Archive information can guide real development but cannot award retained experience, mastery, or progression access.
- **World Engine:** Records arise from causal events and consequences. Archive knowledge enters politics, religion, war, ecology, and institutions through actors rather than automatically changing the world.

## Exploit Limits

- The Archive cannot answer an unbounded request for every useful fact, weakness, treasure, identity, or future event.
- Repeating a query does not improve its Basis, resolution, truth, or Reading Mode indefinitely.
- Manufactured disasters, staged sacrifice, mass observation, rapid reincarnation, and public fame do not guarantee privileged access or high-resolution Records.
- Possessing, consuming, copying, or naming a person or object does not grant access to all Traces connected with it.
- A Record cannot provide private memories, unrelated knowledge, a complete soul history, or objective intent without an applicable source and access rule.
- Archive reading cannot grant skills, statistics, anatomy, evolution, Soul Depth, Soul Titles, authority, ownership, resurrection, or extra actions.
- An Annotation, destroyed Interface, or official interpretation cannot silently rewrite history or erase protected progression.
- World-Bearing Reading does not permit editing world laws, causing a World Reset, controlling Reincarnation, or becoming the world's sole narrator.
- Archive contact cannot bypass consent or resistance merely because the desired information is historically important.
- Deliberate overload, conflicting readings, or exposure to dangerous Records does not guarantee Deepening or new powers.

## GM Procedure

When the Archive may be consulted:

1. name the Archive Target and bound the question or scope;
2. identify the Archive Basis and any supporting worldly investigation;
3. establish the Interface, its Custodian, jurisdiction, and limits;
4. identify the available Traces and the provenance of each;
5. check Resonance, Soul Depth, Soul Integrity, permission, and resistance;
6. choose the narrowest Reading Mode that the method supports;
7. present source limitations, gaps, conflicts, and translation signs alongside useful evidence;
8. apply time, attention, obligation, exposure, Strain, or other established consequences;
9. distinguish the Record from interpretation and from external corroboration;
10. record new Attunement, Annotation, damage, or access changes without creating a universal Archive score.

The GM may keep provenance or missing Traces partly concealed when investigation requires it, but later revelations must remain consistent with the sources and Interface. Archive access should deepen a mystery by grounding it in history, not end every mystery by declaration.

## Examples

### The Unwitnessed Vigil

An explorer died alone maintaining a seal and formed the Soul Title **The One Who Stayed**. Centuries later, a damaged sealstone provides an Archive Basis and a temple well serves as an Interface. Trace Contact reveals sustained pressure and one continuing presence; Fragment Reading later shows the explorer's hands and exhaustion from the sealstone's consequential Trace. It does not expose every private thought or create the title retroactively.

### Five Histories of One Battle

A kingdom teaches that its saint ended a monster siege alone. Comparative Reading finds a terrified soldier's experiential Trace, a dragon's view from above, damage recorded by the city wall, a healer's Annotation, and a divine instrument's record of the final spell. Together they show that monsters and civilians also broke the siege. The saint's contribution was real, but the official account mistook one vivid source for the whole event.

### The Lost Technique

A goblin scholar finds a Record of a human glassmaker from a vanished Age. The Record preserves furnace timing, several failed mixtures, and the maker's visual judgments. The goblin lacks the original materials and sees heat differently. It can begin experiments from better information, but gains no profession rank or human hands from the reading.

### The False Index

An imperial Archive Interface has not erased evidence of an old rebellion. Its Custodians changed the index and attached loyalist Annotations so that ordinary queries return only records of banditry. Contradictory place Traces and a hidden participant Basis expose the manipulation. Destroying the imperial catalogue would remove an Interface, not the underlying rebellion.

### Reading Across a Reset

A World Reset changed magic from spoken formulae to embodied signs. A surviving observatory remains Attuned to pre-Reset spell Traces, but its translations now appear as impossible geometry. World-Bearing Resonance permits limited contact with the Reset stratum; reconstructing any old spell still requires translation, present magical development, and compatibility with the new Age.

### A Custodian's Price

A World Spirit offers Record Reading of a poisoned river's origin in exchange for repairing one of its forgotten shrines. The spirit can reach ecological and ritual Traces within its watershed but knows little of a foreign alchemist's motives. Accepting the bargain grants scoped access and an obligation, not proof that the Custodian's preferred interpretation is morally correct.
