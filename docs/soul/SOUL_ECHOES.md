# Soul Echoes

## Purpose

Soul Echoes preserve the bounded first-person perspectives of completed incarnations so that former lives can remain meaningful without replacing the current incarnation.

## Core Rule

A Soul Echo is a persistent soul structure formed when one incarnation is reconciled after Final Death. It preserves how that incarnation experienced, understood, and responded to its life.

An Echo is part of the continuing soul, but it is not the whole soul, a separate soul, a second active incarnation, or an objective record of history. It may remember, advise, disagree, and cooperate according to its Presence, while the current incarnation retains primary agency.

## Terms

### Soul Echo

The persistent, bounded perspective left by one completed incarnation. Use **Soul Echo** on first reference and **Echo** thereafter.

### Echo Core

The historically rooted identity, memories, values, emotions, habits, and modes of thought preserved from the source incarnation during Life Reconciliation.

### Echo Presence

The current degree to which an Echo can be perceived and can participate: Dormant, Faint, Awakened, Advisor, Manifest, or Delegate.

### Echo Chorus

A coordinated presentation of several Echoes for communication or play. A Chorus does not merge its members, erase their differences, or create a new soul.

### Echo Overlay

A cooperative state in which an Echo lends perspective, memory context, or practiced timing while the current incarnation retains executive control.

### Echo Delegation

A bounded, mutually agreed transfer of executive control from the current incarnation to an Echo at Delegate Presence. Delegation is not possession and does not make the Echo the active incarnation.

## Formation During Life Reconciliation

Every completed incarnation normally forms one Soul Echo during Life Reconciliation.

- Formation occurs only after Final Death. A revival that returns the same incarnation does not create an Echo.
- One incarnation normally creates one Echo, even when that life contained conflicting roles, identities, or periods of radical change.
- Multiple Echoes from one incarnation require an explicit fragmentation rule; they cannot be inferred from trauma, disguise, transformation, or ordinary internal conflict.
- A short or cognitively limited life still forms an Echo, but the result may preserve only sparse sensation, attachment, fear, comfort, or instinctive understanding.
- An Echo is formed from the reconciled life as a whole, not from a literal scan of the final instant.

The circumstances of death may strongly shape an Echo when they transformed the source incarnation or revealed what it had become. Violence, suffering, sacrifice, or dramatic last words do not automatically make an Echo clearer or more powerful.

Echo formation grants no death bonus, skill increase, Soul Depth, or special authority. It preserves a perspective that was genuinely lived.

## What an Echo Preserves

An Echo Core may preserve:

- first-person memories available to the source incarnation;
- its self-image, values, loyalties, fears, desires, and unresolved questions;
- emotional associations with people, places, objects, and events;
- habits of attention and modes of reasoning;
- species-specific or body-shaped ways of perceiving the world;
- practical context surrounding skills and Soul Imprints developed in that life;
- the meaning it assigned to relationships, achievements, failures, and Soul Titles;
- memories and perspectives shaped by Soul Wounds, distortions, or sealed regions that genuinely affected its identity.

An Echo does not automatically preserve:

- perfect sensory records or a complete timeline;
- facts the source incarnation never learned;
- objective truth about disputed events;
- events occurring after its Final Death;
- bodily statistics, anatomy, mutations, or physical reflexes as usable capabilities;
- ordinary possessions, social authority, legal identity, or ownership;
- every skill used during the life when no persistent Soul Imprint was formed;
- another person's private memories merely because the source incarnation knew that person.

Skills and progression belong to their own persistent systems. An Echo may supply context for accessing a legitimate Soul Imprint, but it cannot preserve development that the soul never earned.

## Historical Memory and Continuing Context

The Echo Core preserves the source incarnation as reconciled. Later conversation may change how an Echo interprets its history, relates to the current incarnation, or responds to an old regret, but it does not retroactively replace what the source life knew, believed, or experienced.

An active Echo can learn current context when that context is shared through Resonance, Soul Space, an Overlay, or another explicit channel. The GM must distinguish:

- **historical memory:** knowledge and perspective originating in the completed life;
- **later context:** information learned after the Echo formed;
- **current inference:** conclusions the Echo draws from what it presently knows.

An Echo does not passively observe every thought or event of later incarnations. Its access depends on its current Presence and the channel connecting it to the active self.

Memories may be incomplete, mistaken, emotionally biased, or damaged. Deliberate deception by an Echo must follow its established perspective and provide discoverable signs. The GM cannot invent a hidden falsehood solely to invalidate a reasonable choice.

## Personality and Identity

An Echo should be portrayed as a coherent perspective shaped by a real life, not as a database wearing a former face.

It may:

- retain loyalties, grudges, humor, fears, ambitions, prejudices, and regrets;
- disagree with the current incarnation or other Echoes;
- reconsider its interpretation when given new context;
- refuse advice, Overlay, Delegation, or another voluntary form of cooperation;
- care about people or purposes that no longer exist in the same form.

The current incarnation is not required to obey, forgive, imitate, or become an Echo. A former monarch Echo has no present claim to a throne. A former parent Echo cannot demand that a reincarnated relative resume an old relationship. A former monster Echo is not less authentic than a former human Echo.

An Echo is a meaningful part of the same continuing soul, but no single Echo is the one true self. The current incarnation decides how prior perspectives contribute to present identity.

## Echo Presence

Presence describes an Echo's current expression, not its combat power, truthfulness, moral authority, or value.

| Presence | Available expression | Default limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dormant** | The Echo persists without reliable awareness or communication. | It produces no deliberate guidance. |
| **Faint** | Emotion, dreams, sensations, impulses, images, or fragments emerge through a Trigger. | It cannot sustain a coherent exchange. |
| **Awakened** | The current incarnation can recognize a specific former perspective and receive brief memories or responses. | Contact remains intermittent and may not reveal the Echo's full identity. |
| **Advisor** | The Echo can sustain dialogue, offer bounded analysis, and deliberately approach accessible memories. | It has no direct authority over the body or world. |
| **Manifest** | The Echo can hold a stable internal form within accessible Soul Space and interact with permitted internal structures or other Echoes. | Manifestation is internal and grants no body control or external projection. |
| **Delegate** | The Echo can maintain advanced cooperation through an Overlay and may qualify for consent-based Delegation. | It remains constrained by the current body, soul systems, consent, and available development. |

Presence is conditional. A previously Manifest or Delegate Echo may become Dormant after Reincarnation, injury, a changed identity, or loss of a relevant Trigger without losing its history. A powerful event may also produce a brief higher Presence that cannot yet be sustained.

Echo Presence and Resonance State answer different questions. Presence describes how directly the Echo can participate; Resonance State describes how coherently its Link aligns in the present. Clear communication does not guarantee agreement, and an Echo with highly developed potential may still be inaccessible when its Link is Silent or damaged.

There is no fixed number of lives, conversations, or Soul Depth events required for a Presence. The stages describe demonstrated access and stability rather than a universal advancement track.

## Accessing and Strengthening an Echo

Formation establishes a potential Resonance Link because the soul directly lived the source incarnation: that history supplies the Basis, and the Echo is the Target. Activation still requires a present Trigger, compatible access, enough Soul Depth, and sufficient Soul Integrity for the attempted expression.

An Echo may become clearer through:

- encountering a person, place, object, species, language, or problem important to its source life;
- deliberate Resonance attunement using meaningful sensory or emotional anchors;
- present-life practice related to an accessible Soul Imprint;
- honest exchange between the Echo and current incarnation;
- resolving Dissonance tied to the source life;
- restoring a damaged memory, bond, or region of Soul Space;
- integrating the Echo's perspective without surrendering current agency;
- accepting new context that changes an old interpretation.

Repeatedly summoning an Echo under safe, identical conditions does not raise its Presence indefinitely. Growth requires improved access, trust, understanding, integration, or capacity.

Higher Presence is not always desirable. An incarnation may reasonably limit contact with an overwhelming Echo, and an Echo may choose quiet when its intervention would be harmful.

## Communication

Echo communication may appear as:

- a recognizable internal voice;
- emotion or bodily urgency;
- sensory replay;
- dreams or symbolic scenes;
- a sequence of remembered actions;
- a nonverbal model of space, prey, danger, motion, or social hierarchy;
- direct conversation or manifestation within Soul Space.

Communication reflects the source incarnation's cognition. A spider Echo may communicate through vibration, geometry, hunger, and threat patterns rather than fluent human speech. Resonance may translate those patterns into concepts the current incarnation can understand, but it cannot fabricate knowledge or categories the source life never possessed.

An Echo's advice is limited by what it knew then, what it has learned since, and how it interprets the current situation. Expertise does not make it universally wise.

The GM should make an Echo's identity and information source understandable without turning every appearance into complete exposition. Ambiguity may create discovery; arbitrary obscurity should not block information the Echo can clearly communicate.

## Bounded Agency

An active Echo may:

- initiate an internal signal when a valid Link and Trigger permit it;
- offer memories, advice, warnings, questions, or disagreement;
- choose whether to cooperate in an Overlay or Delegation;
- interact with accessible internal symbols, Echoes, and structures at Manifest Presence;
- preserve its own established perspective during internal reconciliation or conflict.

An Echo cannot by default:

- take control of the current body without informed consent;
- perform an additional world action or grant an extra turn;
- spend resources, cast abilities, or make external contracts independently;
- reincarnate, possess a separate body, or become another active incarnation;
- bind another soul based on a former relationship;
- erase, imprison, or rewrite another Echo through ordinary disagreement;
- confiscate Soul Imprints that belong to the continuing soul.

An Echo may withhold its contextual assistance or refuse voluntary cooperation. That refusal does not erase legitimately retained skills, Skill XP, Stat XP, or other protected progression.

The current incarnation may reject an Echo's advice and remains responsible for present decisions. An internal disagreement never transfers worldly responsibility to the Echo.

## Echo Overlay

During an Echo Overlay, the current incarnation retains executive control while an Echo supplies some combination of:

- practiced timing;
- recalled sensory patterns;
- emotional recognition;
- a former problem-solving approach;
- access cues for a compatible Soul Imprint;
- active commentary or warning.

An Overlay does not grant missing anatomy, bodily statistics, unavailable skills, or knowledge outside the Echo Core and later shared context. It may help translate old mastery into a compatible expression when the Development, skill, body, and Resonance rules all permit it.

Overlay effort rises with cognitive distance, bodily incompatibility, emotional conflict, the complexity of the borrowed perspective, and the number of simultaneous Echoes. An unstable Overlay may cause distraction, memory bleed, Dissonance, or Soul Strain.

## Delegate Presence and Echo Delegation

Delegate Presence makes bounded Delegation possible; it does not make Delegation automatic.

Echo Delegation requires:

1. informed agreement from both the current incarnation and the Echo;
2. a defined purpose, scope, duration, or ending condition;
3. Harmonic or Concordant Resonance appropriate to the action;
4. enough Soul Depth and current Integrity to preserve both perspectives;
5. a body and set of accessible Soul Imprints capable of the intended action;
6. a safe way to return executive control.

The current incarnation remains aware by default and may revoke consent while capable of doing so. The Echo may also end Delegation. Loss of awareness ends Delegation unless a narrow emergency continuation was explicitly agreed beforehand and another rule supports it.

Delegation does not:

- make the Echo a separate soul or current incarnation;
- create an external body or projection;
- bypass anatomy, resources, cooldowns, conditions, or system access;
- grant more actions than the current incarnation could take;
- restore former legal or relational authority;
- excuse the current incarnation from consequences.

Repeated Delegation does not automatically develop the current incarnation's skills. Development requires real participation, practice, understanding, and integration rather than allowing an Echo to perform every difficult task.

Forced takeover, suppressed consent, or inability to regain control is not ordinary Echo behavior. It requires an explicit hostile effect, curse, Soul Wound, or exceptional rule with fair warning and meaningful resistance.

## Multiple Echoes and the Echo Chorus

A soul may preserve an Echo from every completed incarnation, but only a manageable portion can normally hold clear Presence at once. Soul Depth, Integrity, Resonance, compatibility, and current relevance determine active complexity without creating a fixed Echo-slot count.

Several Echoes may form an Echo Chorus when coordinated communication is useful. A Chorus may:

- compare perspectives from different species or Ages;
- recognize a repeated pattern across lives;
- coordinate a stable Overlay when their contributions are compatible;
- present many low-intensity responses without requiring separate dialogue for each one.

A Chorus does not decide by majority vote. The current incarnation remains the active self, and important disagreement must not be hidden behind a collective voice. Individual Echoes remain distinct and may emerge from the Chorus when their source life becomes relevant.

Echoes at sufficient Presence may converse or act within accessible Soul Space, but this does not create unlimited parallel research, training, crafting, spellcasting, or world actions. Internal dialogue still consumes attention, capacity, and meaningful time under the relevant rules.

The GM should activate Echoes because they matter to the present situation, not merely because they exist. An endless history should enrich play rather than turn every decision into a crowded council scene.

## Conflict, Reconciliation, and Integration

Echo conflict may arise from opposed values, incompatible species instincts, incomplete memories, old guilt, unresolved bonds, or different interpretations of the current incarnation.

Conflict can produce Dissonance, but disagreement is not itself damage. The current incarnation may respond by:

- hearing an Echo without obeying it;
- investigating disputed memories;
- setting boundaries on contact or Delegation;
- finding a present expression that respects several perspectives;
- rejecting a former belief while preserving what was learned from it;
- repairing, transforming, or releasing an unresolved bond;
- allowing an Echo to become quieter after its concern has been understood.

Integration does not require obedience, fusion, deletion, or treating every former self as equally correct. It means the current soul can hold the Echo's existence and meaning without surrendering its own agency or denying genuine history.

Reconciliation may support a Deepening Event when it creates lasting transformation under the Soul Depth rules. An Echo cannot perform that integration on behalf of the current incarnation, and resolving the same conflict repeatedly grants no automatic growth.

## Effort, Risk, and Recovery

Echo Presence is not a spendable resource pool. Sustained contact may still create fatigue or Soul Strain according to:

- Presence and duration;
- emotional intensity or trauma;
- contradiction between the Echo and current identity;
- cognitive or species distance;
- number of active Echoes;
- damage to the Echo, Link, Soul Space, or Soul Integrity;
- use of Overlay or Delegation under pressure.

Recovery may require rest, reduced contact, deliberate attunement, present-life practice, Soul Space work, repaired trust, investigation of memory, or treatment of a relevant Soul Wound.

Strain should create meaningful limits and consequences without making all Echo contact punitive. Cooperative, familiar contact may become easier as the relationship stabilizes.

## Persistence, Damage, and Erasure

Soul Echoes and their Echo Cores normally persist through Reincarnation, Interlife, long time skips, and World Resets.

An Echo may become Dormant, sealed, distorted, fragmented in expression, or difficult to reach because of a valid soul-level cause. Such harm must identify the affected Echo or channel, provide fair warning and meaningful resistance, and leave discoverable evidence.

Ordinary disagreement, rejection, reconciliation, or silence does not erase an Echo. Permanent erasure is an exceptional loss of persistent soul structure and requires an explicit rule proportionate to that consequence. An Echo does not die independently under the default rules.

Fragmented expression does not create extra souls, extra Echoes, duplicated skills, or additional actions. Recovery must address the cause of the damage rather than merely demanding that the Echo speak again.

## Interlife and Reincarnation

Existing Echoes accompany the soul through Interlife, subject to their access rules. Interlife contact does not provide infinite subjective time, effortless training, or automatic reconciliation.

Echoes may advise about a Reincarnation candidate only when Resonance and Reincarnation rules provide access. They do not choose the next body by vote, and a former species Echo does not automatically unlock that species.

The newly embodied incarnation is the active self. Echoes may awaken immediately, gradually, or only under later Triggers.

If an incarnation reaches Final Death during Echo Delegation, Life Reconciliation forms one Echo for the completed current incarnation. The delegated Echo is not copied, reborn, or used to generate an Echo of an Echo.

## System Interactions

- **Soul Fundamentals:** An Echo preserves one former perspective without becoming another soul or active incarnation.
- **Reincarnation:** Life Reconciliation forms the new Echo and carries existing Echoes onward.
- **Soul Depth:** Depth determines how much Echo complexity and contradiction can be integrated safely.
- **Soul Resonance:** Each Echo is a Target whose Presence depends on a valid Link, Trigger, access, and compatibility.
- **Soul Integrity:** Integrity governs whether communication, Overlay, and Delegation remain coherent now.
- **[Soul Space](SOUL_SPACE.md):** Manifest Echoes require an accessible internal environment and remain subject to its rules.
- **[Retained Instincts](RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md):** An Echo may explain or contextualize an instinct, but does not replace the instinct system.
- **[Soul Titles](SOUL_TITLES.md):** An Echo may remember the identity behind a title without restoring its former worldly authority.
- **[Soul Constellations](SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md):** Echo memories may reveal recurring relationships but cannot prove ownership, establish the other soul's perspective, or compel recognition.
- **Soul Weapons:** A Soul Echo belongs to the reincarnating soul; a Weapon Echo belongs to the distinct Weapon Soul. They are not interchangeable.
- **[Akashic Archive](AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md):** An Echo is personal testimony, not an Archive Record or objective historical source.
- **[Soul Avatars](SOUL_AVATARS.md):** Echoes may contribute according to Presence, consent, and access, but Soul Avatar status cannot promote them or make a Chorus the controlling self.
- **Development and skills:** Echoes can supply context and cooperation but cannot replace present practice, access requirements, or compatible embodiment.

## Exploit Limits

- Repeated suicide or accumulation of trivial lives produces additional sparse Echoes, not automatic power, wisdom, or Soul Depth.
- An Echo cannot reveal facts its source never knew or function as a perfect historical encyclopedia.
- Multiple Echoes do not grant parallel turns, passive training, unlimited planning, or multiplied resource use.
- Overlay and Delegation cannot bypass anatomy, skill access, statistics, evolution requirements, or other system limits.
- Letting an expert Echo act does not automatically grant the current incarnation equivalent Development.
- A former ruler, lover, parent, owner, or enemy Echo creates no binding claim over present people or property.
- Copying, consuming, stealing, or receiving another being's memories does not create that being's Soul Echo.
- The current incarnation cannot erase an inconvenient Echo through ordinary rejection, nor must it obey an Echo to preserve progression.
- Conflict farming does not guarantee Deepening, Presence, fusion, mutation, or a new skill.
- Echoes cannot recursively generate Echoes, reincarnate separately, or become extra active incarnations by repetition.

## GM Procedure

When forming or activating a Soul Echo:

1. identify the completed source incarnation;
2. define the Echo Core from what that life genuinely experienced and integrated;
3. separate historical memory, later shared context, and present inference;
4. identify the Resonance Link, Trigger, and purpose of the contact;
5. check Soul Depth, Integrity, compatibility, and any Soul Space access;
6. determine the Echo's current Presence and communication form;
7. preserve the Echo's perspective without displacing current-incarnation agency;
8. apply consent requirements before Overlay or Delegation;
9. resolve Dissonance, fatigue, relationship change, or damage according to established causes;
10. record lasting changes to Presence, context, trust, or injury without rewriting the Echo Core retroactively.

The GM should reveal Echo information through a source-appropriate perspective and keep its limitations discoverable. An Echo should complicate or enrich a decision before it solves one automatically.

## Examples

### The Spider Hunter

A human incarnation notices ordinary floor vibrations, and a Faint spider Echo gives them the remembered shape of nearby movement. The Echo does not speak in sentences; it expresses distance, web tension, hunger, and threat. Later, an Overlay helps the human time a thread trap, but it cannot provide spinnerets or bypass the need to learn suitable tools.

### The Saint and the Tyrant

Two Advisor Echoes disagree over captured enemies. Their argument provides memories of mercy, betrayal, fear, and rule, but it does not become a vote. The current incarnation must choose, bear the outcome, and decide what both lives mean now.

### The Borrowed Sword Hand

A Delegate Echo of a former sword master accepts Delegation during a duel. The current body remains slower and weaker than the old one, and only accessible techniques can be expressed. Afterward, the current incarnation gains no automatic mastery from having watched; learning requires practice and integration.

### A Sparse Life

An incarnation that died in early infancy leaves a Dormant or Faint Echo of warmth, hunger, pain, scent, and attachment. The Echo is real, but it cannot become an expert Advisor by inventing a personality or knowledge the life never developed.

### The Former King

An Awakened Echo recognizes the ruins of its capital and demands that surviving descendants obey. Its memories may reveal genuine history, but its authority ended with the former identity. The current incarnation may investigate the ruins without accepting the Echo's claim.
