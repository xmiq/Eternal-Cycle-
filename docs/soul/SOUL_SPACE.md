# Soul Space

## Purpose

Soul Space provides a playable internal environment through which a soul may encounter its own identity, memories, Soul Imprints, Soul Echoes, bonds, damage, and soul-level systems.

## Core Rule

Every soul possesses a latent Soul Space. Conscious access reveals an adaptive metaphysical interface to structures that genuinely belong to or connect with that soul.

Soul Space is not a physical pocket dimension, private inventory, stopped-time chamber, perfect memory archive, or reality editor. Its appearance may be symbolic and partly shapeable, but changing an image does not create, erase, transfer, unlock, or heal the soul-level reality represented by that image.

## Terms

### Soul Space

The persistent internal metaphysical environment through which one soul's structures, connections, and condition may be perceived or approached.

### Soul Anchor

The central orientation through which the active self recognizes itself, maintains continuity, and returns safely to embodied awareness or Interlife consciousness.

### Soul Region

A differentiated part of Soul Space associated with a history, identity, Soul Imprint, Echo, bond, injury, system, or present concern.

### Soul Construct

A symbolic structure formed within Soul Space. A Construct has system-level function only when another rule, genuine soul structure, or earned connection supports that function.

### Soul Threshold

A boundary governing discovery, entry, communication, or influence between Soul Regions or between a Region and a visitor.

### Soul Gate

A scoped connection that permits another being, soul, or external system to contact or enter part of a Soul Space under explicit access rules.

### Soul Intrusion

A contested attempt to enter, observe, alter, or remain within another soul's Space without its current consent. Intrusion requires an explicit rule and meaningful resistance.

## Latent Space and First Access

Possessing a Soul Space does not grant conscious awareness of it. For many souls it remains latent throughout life and may influence dreams, instincts, memory organization, or spiritual reactions without becoming a place they can deliberately enter.

First conscious access requires an established route, such as:

- a strong Reflexive Resonance event;
- deliberate meditation or a culturally developed soul practice;
- contact with a Soul Echo or intertwined Weapon Soul;
- a Deepening Event that changes self-understanding;
- a soul-level ritual, blessing, curse, or injury;
- Final Death, Life Reconciliation, or Interlife awareness;
- another system that explicitly opens access.

The attempted access must also fit current Soul Depth, Soul Integrity, Resonance, embodiment, and any system-specific requirements. Depth supplies capacity rather than entry by itself.

First access may reveal a dark void, ember, den, nest, room, path, shoreline, web, rhythm, scent field, machine, or another form suited to the soul's cognition. A minimal first Space is not weak, and a spectacular one is not automatically deep or stable.

Opening Soul Space is not itself a Deepening Event, evolution, skill, or power increase. It creates an opportunity to perceive and work with what is genuinely present.

## The Soul Anchor

Every consciously accessible Soul Space reveals or develops a Soul Anchor.

The Anchor may appear as:

- a flame, heartbeat, star, name, reflection, or voice;
- a den, throne, tree, pool, forge, web nexus, or central room;
- a recurring sensation, rhythm, direction, scent, or point of gravity;
- any other form through which the active self recognizes continuity.

The Anchor is not the entire soul and is not a numerical health bar. It provides orientation, supports a return from internal attention, and represents the current incarnation's primary agency within its own Space.

While embodied, the Anchor connects internal awareness to the living body. During Interlife it instead maintains continuity under the Reincarnation rules. Its appearance may change radically between species or Ages while its underlying function persists.

Ordinary confusion, disagreement, navigation failure, or cosmetic alteration cannot destroy, possess, or replace the Anchor. Obscuring, displacing, or damaging it requires an explicit soul-level cause. When safe orientation fails without such damage, the default consequence is interrupted access, ejection, confusion, or Soul Strain rather than loss of the soul.

## Architecture and Symbolism

Soul Space has no mandatory architecture or fixed sequence of forms.

A soul might experience:

- a void becoming organized around a campfire;
- a library whose shelves hold accessible memories and questions;
- a castle containing Echo chambers and guarded doors;
- an infinite forest whose paths follow relationships;
- a floating island chain shaped by separate lives;
- a living web that communicates through tension rather than distance;
- a forge shared with a Weapon Soul;
- a city, ocean, song, machine, constellation, or shifting abstract pattern.

These are expressions, not ranks. Soul Depth may support greater complexity and stability, but physical size, ornament, brightness, number of rooms, or human-like architecture does not measure power or maturity.

Spatial distance is symbolic. A door may represent emotional resistance, a mountain may represent incompatible identity, and an unreachable island may represent a dormant Link. Crossing that distance requires the condition represented by it, not merely imagined walking speed.

Architecture may translate when the current body or cognition changes. A spider may experience a memory as a vibrating route through a web, while a later human incarnation experiences the same underlying connection as a corridor of taut threads. The presentation changed; the history did not.

The GM should preserve recurring symbols when they remain meaningful while allowing the Space to change as the soul changes. No visual symbol has one universal interpretation across all souls.

## Soul Regions and Thresholds

A Soul Region organizes one meaningful part of the Space without isolating it from the continuing soul.

Common Regions may be associated with:

- the current incarnation and its active concerns;
- one Soul Echo or an Echo Chorus;
- a group of compatible Soul Imprints;
- an intertwined Weapon Soul and shared bond;
- a Soul Title, [Retained Instinct](RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md), or recurring relationship;
- a Soul Wound, sealed memory, or damaged Resonance Link;
- a Gate to an external system such as the [Akashic Archive](AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md);
- a place for reflection, rehearsal, communication, or reconciliation.

Regions are optional organizational structures, not mandatory containers. One Soul Space may use distinct rooms, while another presents every connection through changes in one landscape or melody.

Soul Thresholds describe access to a Region. Common conditions include:

| Condition | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | The Region can be approached by the actor under current conditions. |
| **Veiled** | Its existence, location, or meaning is obscured but can be discovered through consistent clues. |
| **Guarded** | Entry requires consent, proof, reconciliation, a Trigger, resistance, or another established condition. |
| **Sealed** | Access is blocked by a specific rule, injury, oath, system, or protective act until its cause is addressed. |
| **Breached** | A damaged boundary permits leakage, distortion, or unwanted access and requires attention to its cause. |

A Threshold may differ by actor. The current incarnation, an Echo, a Weapon Soul, and an invited guest may each possess different access to the same Region.

The current incarnation has primary agency but not automatic omniscience. It cannot open every sealed memory, command every Echo, inspect a Weapon Soul's private identity, or bypass system requirements merely because the Space belongs to its soul.

Deliberate boundaries may protect attention and privacy where the soul has authority to establish them. They cannot be used to erase inconvenient progression, permanently imprison an Echo, enslave a Weapon Soul, or invalidate another being's agency without an explicit rule.

## Soul Constructs and Shaping

Soul Constructs give comprehensible form to existing soul structures and deliberate internal organization.

Shaping operates at three broad levels:

1. **Cosmetic shaping:** Changes presentation, comfort, color, arrangement, or metaphor without creating mechanical effects.
2. **Interpretive shaping:** Organizes already accessible memories, Links, Echo relationships, or internal questions for clearer understanding without changing access or capability.
3. **Functional shaping:** Creates a stable interface recognized by another soul system and therefore requires earned access, compatible structure, and that system's rules.

A current incarnation may often alter cosmetic details within an accessible Region through focus or habit. Interpretive and functional changes require more than imagination.

Examples:

- A library can organize accessible memories but cannot write facts the soul never learned.
- A forge can support communication and cooperation with a Weapon Soul but cannot create physical materials or award a Soul Weapon evolution.
- A council chamber can help Echoes communicate but cannot grant extra actions or force agreement.
- A sealed vault can represent inaccessible Soul Imprints but cannot make their access conditions disappear.
- An evolution altar may display valid choices only when the evolution system has already made those choices available.
- An Archive Interface represented by a gate may present an established connection but cannot invent permission to read the Akashic Archive.

Persistent structural change normally follows meaningful integration, repaired harm, a changed bond, a Deepening Event, developed Resonance, or another explicit system event. Repetitive construction, elaborate visualization, or decorative effort does not manufacture permanent soul growth.

## What Soul Space Can Do

Subject to the relevant systems, Soul Space may:

- provide a stable setting for meeting Manifest Soul Echoes;
- permit communication with an intertwined Weapon Soul;
- make Soul Imprints, Resonance Links, Soul Titles, instincts, or injuries perceptible through symbols;
- support deliberate attunement, reflection, memory investigation, and internal reconciliation;
- expose contradictions that are causing Dissonance or Soul Strain;
- host a valid evolution, Reincarnation, Archive, or soul-repair interface;
- create scoped meeting ground for invited visitors;
- preserve meaningful internal structures across lives and World Resets.

Soul Space reveals and organizes possibilities. Another rule must still provide capability, access, permission, development, and external effect.

## What Soul Space Cannot Do

Soul Space does not by default:

- store ordinary items, bodies, food, money, mana, loot, or physical resources;
- transport worldly possessions through Reincarnation;
- stop, reverse, or multiply external time;
- provide a second body, turn, workforce, or stream of unrestricted thought;
- create knowledge, memories, skills, Soul Imprints, Soul Titles, or Resonance Links from imagination;
- replace physical training, bodily recovery, sleep, nutrition, crafting, or social action;
- unlock an evolution, spell, class, candidate, or Archive Record without its own requirements;
- copy, summon, imprison, or reconstruct another soul merely by representing it;
- heal a Soul Wound by hiding or repainting its symbolic appearance;
- alter the outside world unless another established ability creates that effect.

An ordinary object represented in Soul Space is a Construct, memory, or symbol rather than the physical object. Soul Weapons and other soul-bound structures follow their own explicit persistence rules and are not evidence that ordinary storage is possible.

## Entry, External Time, and the Body

Conscious entry may occur during sleep, meditation, ritual, crisis, Soul Resonance, unconsciousness, or Interlife. The method determines what attention and control remain available outside.

Unless an explicit ability states otherwise:

- external time continues normally;
- the body remains where it was and remains vulnerable;
- deep internal attention reduces awareness and action in the world;
- entering, navigating, or shaping the Space consumes meaningful attention and time;
- internal events do not grant additional turns during combat or other time-sensitive scenes;
- fatigue, injury, conditions, and resource costs remain in effect.

Partial attention may allow a brief internal signal, Echo warning, or familiar Construct to be perceived while acting. It does not permit full external performance and unrestricted internal activity simultaneously.

The GM may summarize long reflection when no external pressure exists, but the summary does not create more training time than actually passes. During Interlife, subjective access remains bounded by Reincarnation, Depth, Resonance, and world-time rules.

## Internal Residents and Manifestations

Soul Space can host manifestations of structures belonging to the soul without turning all of them into people.

- **Soul Echoes** may hold Manifest Presence in accessible Regions. Their agency and knowledge remain governed by the Soul Echo rules.
- **Weapon Souls** remain distinct persons. An intertwined Weapon Soul may enter, appear, or maintain a shared Region according to its bond, consent, and Soul Weapon rules.
- **Soul Imprints** may appear as books, tools, paths, instincts, teachers, monsters, or abstract patterns without becoming independent beings.
- **Soul Titles** may shape symbols, clothing, architecture, weather, inhabitants, or reactions without automatically creating an independent manifestation or Soul Avatar.
- **[Retained Instincts](RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md)** may appear as urges, creatures, senses, trails, or environmental forces without becoming Soul Echoes.
- **Memories of other people** may appear as Constructs, but those representations are not the actual people or their souls.

The current incarnation cannot treat a manifestation of someone it remembers as a substitute for that person's consent, current identity, or continued existence.

Internal residents do not automatically perceive every Region. Echoes and Weapon Souls retain the access and privacy granted by their own rules, Resonance Links, relationships, and Thresholds.

## Soul Gates and Visitors

A Soul Gate creates a limited route between a Soul Space and another soul, being, or external system.

A valid Gate requires:

1. a specific host and visitor or external system;
2. a genuine Resonance Basis and applicable Link;
3. a present Trigger and method capable of opening the connection;
4. informed consent from affected participants, unless a contested rule explicitly permits Intrusion;
5. a defined destination, scope, duration, and ending condition;
6. compatible awareness and enough Soul Depth and Integrity to sustain contact;
7. a safe route for each participant to withdraw or return.

Permission to enter one Region does not grant access to the entire Soul Space, private memories, Echo Cores, Soul Imprints, or the Soul Anchor. A visitor perceives only what the Gate, Thresholds, Resonance, and host permission make available.

An embodied visitor's body remains in the outside world, external time continues, and divided attention follows the normal entry rules. A Gate transports awareness or permitted soul-level contact rather than a physical body or ordinary possessions.

The current incarnation may normally narrow or revoke voluntary access. A visitor may also leave. Persistent Gates require an enduring bond or system-level cause and may fall Silent when their Resonance conditions no longer apply.

Gods, World Spirits, Archive custodians, Weapon Souls, recurring souls, and other powerful beings receive no automatic exception. Power may support a valid method, but it does not create consent, a Resonance Basis, or unrestricted scope.

A Gate does not merge souls, establish ownership, or create a permanent shared Soul Space. A [Constellation Commons](SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md#constellation-commons-rules) is the defined shared meeting structure for recurring souls and requires its own formation rules and the continuing agency of every participant.

## Soul Intrusion

Soul Intrusion is never a casual extension of ordinary perception or magic.

A rule permitting Intrusion must define:

- the target and route of entry;
- what the intruder is attempting to observe, alter, steal, suppress, or occupy;
- which Threshold or Region is at risk;
- how the target detects, resists, redirects, or ends the attempt;
- what happens to the intruder if the attempt fails;
- the limits on duration, information, and lasting harm.

Breaching one Threshold does not expose the entire soul. An intruder cannot assume access to the Soul Anchor, all memories, every Echo, or protected progression.

Possible consequences include temporary contact, false or partial impressions, Dissonance, a Breached Threshold, Soul Strain, expulsion, a trace that can be followed, or a properly established Soul Wound. Permanent theft, rewriting, imprisonment, or destruction requires a separate explicit rule proportionate to that loss.

The GM must provide fair warning appropriate to the threat and meaningful resistance. Soul Intrusion cannot be introduced retroactively merely to reveal secrets, remove abilities, or force a preferred decision.

## Memory, Training, and Evolution

Soul Space may make memory or practice easier to approach, but it does not change the origin or limits of development.

- Replaying a memory remains bounded by what was genuinely preserved and accessed.
- Mental rehearsal may contribute only when the Development System recognizes the attention, time, understanding, and present-life integration involved.
- Internal practice does not directly develop muscles, organs, species traits, bodily statistics, or unavailable senses.
- Echoes may advise or Overlay under their rules but cannot train in parallel on behalf of the current incarnation.
- A simulated opponent behaves only from knowledge genuinely available to the soul or connected system.
- Repeating safe internal drills produces diminishing returns like comparable external practice.

Evolution branches, hidden conditions, Reincarnation candidates, and crossover possibilities may appear symbolically when their own systems and Resonance make them discoverable. Appearance is information or interface, not automatic qualification or selection.

## Damage, Collapse, and Repair

Soul Strain, Soul Wounds, damaged Resonance, hostile effects, broken bonds, identity conflict, or unsafe contact may alter Soul Space.

Possible signs include:

- unstable terrain, storms, loops, or shifting gravity;
- missing paths, distorted Constructs, or inaccessible Regions;
- leaking memories, voices, instincts, or emotions;
- Guarded Thresholds becoming Sealed;
- protective Thresholds becoming Breached;
- a dim, distant, divided, or obscured Soul Anchor;
- an invading pattern that does not belong to the soul.

Symbols are evidence, not universal diagnoses. A cracked wall may represent a damaged bond in one soul and a false self-image breaking open in another. The cause must be established through consistent clues and relevant rules.

Repair must address that cause. Rest may stabilize temporary Strain. A Soul Wound may require confrontation, reconciliation, renewed development, repair of a bond, removal of an intruder, or another proportionate response. Repainting a wall, locking a door, or imagining the Space whole does not by itself heal the underlying condition.

A Soul Space collapse normally means loss of conscious access, disorganized Regions, failed Gates, forced ejection, or severe Strain. It does not automatically annihilate the soul, erase all progression, or kill internal residents.

Closing, revoking, or allowing a Gate to fall Silent follows its bond and Resonance rules and is not automatically damage. Permanent destruction of a Region, a Construct tied to protected progression, or the Soul Anchor, as well as forced severance of an enduring Gate, requires an explicit soul-harm rule, fair warning, meaningful resistance, and consequences proportionate to permanent loss.

## Reincarnation, Interlife, and World Resets

The latent Soul Space and genuinely persistent Regions normally survive Final Death, Life Reconciliation, Reincarnation, long Interlife, and World Resets.

At Life Reconciliation:

- structures tied only to the former body or temporary identity may dissolve or change;
- the new Soul Echo may gain a Region or symbolic place when access permits;
- persistent Soul Imprints, bonds, Titles, Wounds, and established Gates retain appropriate representation;
- ordinary possessions and physical resources remain in the world;
- the Soul Anchor transitions from embodiment to Interlife continuity.

On new embodiment, the Soul Anchor and accessible surface may adopt forms compatible with the new body, species, culture, and cognition. Persistent deeper structures are translated rather than automatically erased.

Interlife may make parts of Soul Space easier or harder to reach according to Reincarnation, Depth, Resonance, and Integrity. It does not suspend the World Engine or grant infinite internal work.

## Assessment and Interpretation

Soul Space provides discoverable evidence about internal condition, but it is not an infallible status screen.

Assessment may identify:

- which Regions are currently accessible;
- which Thresholds are Open, Veiled, Guarded, Sealed, or Breached;
- which Echoes, Links, bonds, Titles, or injuries appear relevant;
- whether a change seems cosmetic, interpretive, functional, or imposed;
- what Trigger, relationship, practice, repair, or outside rule may be involved.

Interpretation can be mistaken because symbols are personal, memories are limited, and outside effects may deceive. A false or distorted sign requires an established cause and discoverable inconsistencies.

The GM should track only Regions and Constructs that matter to play. Soul Space should make a complex soul understandable without requiring a complete map of every memory or skill.

## System Interactions

- **Soul Fundamentals:** Soul Space presents parts of one continuing soul without duplicating identity or bypassing embodiment.
- **Soul Depth:** Depth supports the complexity and stability a soul can integrate within its Space but does not determine appearance or access.
- **Soul Resonance:** Resonance reveals Regions, activates Constructs, opens Gates, and connects the current incarnation to valid internal Targets.
- **Soul Integrity:** Integrity determines how coherently the Space functions now.
- **Soul Echoes:** Manifest Echoes may inhabit accessible Regions while retaining bounded agency and knowledge.
- **Reincarnation:** Persistent structure survives transition while current-life presentation and embodiment-linked features may change.
- **[Soul Titles](SOUL_TITLES.md):** Titles may shape symbols and reactions without becoming simple bonuses or automatic inhabitants.
- **[Retained Instincts](RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md):** Instincts may be perceived and reconciled without being mistaken for Echoes or independent souls.
- **[Soul Constellations](SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md):** Recurring relationships may support Gates or a consensual Constellation Commons but do not compel entry, disclosure, or intimacy.
- **Soul Weapons:** An intertwined Weapon Soul may maintain a consensual Region while remaining a distinct person.
- **[Akashic Archive](AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md):** Soul Space may host an established Archive Interface, but it cannot create access, provenance, or objective truth.
- **Development and evolution:** Internal practice and interfaces remain subject to present-life effort, embodiment, and system-specific requirements.

## Exploit Limits

- Soul Space cannot function as an inventory, shelter for physical bodies, resource generator, or way to smuggle items through Reincarnation.
- Internal time does not provide unlimited training, planning, recovery, crafting, or additional actions.
- Cosmetic shaping cannot create functional Constructs, erase injuries, open sealed memories, or manufacture soul progression.
- A large, beautiful, ancient-looking, or complex Space is not proof of power, Soul Depth, truth, or superiority.
- Representing a person, monster, item, spell, or opponent does not copy its soul, abilities, resources, or unknown information.
- A Gate does not grant unrestricted mind reading, memory access, ownership, or permanent residence.
- Hostile entry requires an explicit Intrusion rule and cannot bypass warning, scope, resistance, or consequences.
- A current incarnation cannot permanently imprison Echoes or dominate a Weapon Soul merely by changing architecture.
- An internal evolution display cannot waive hidden conditions, embodiment, species rules, or valid candidate requirements.
- Destroying symbolic scenery does not automatically destroy the soul structure represented by it or generate growth through repeated repair.

## GM Procedure

When Soul Space becomes relevant:

1. identify who is entering or perceiving the Space and by what route;
2. establish the external time, body state, attention cost, and return condition;
3. identify the Soul Anchor and the current incarnation's orientation;
4. select only the Regions, Constructs, residents, and Thresholds relevant to the scene;
5. choose symbols grounded in the soul's actual species, lives, identity, and present conflict;
6. connect every functional effect to an established Soul Imprint, Link, bond, injury, or system rule;
7. define visitor consent, Gate scope, privacy, and resistance before outside entry;
8. distinguish cosmetic change from interpretive or structural change;
9. resolve Strain, Intrusion, damage, repair, or persistence through their actual causes;
10. record meaningful changes without treating the Space as a complete inventory or status screen.

The GM may keep a Region's full meaning hidden, but its symbols and access conditions must remain consistent enough to investigate. Soul Space should externalize an internal truth without pretending that every interpretation is objective.

## Examples

### The Ember in the Burrow

A goblin first reaches Soul Space as a dark burrow organized around one small ember. The ember is its Soul Anchor, not a measure of magical strength. After the goblin builds a community and integrates responsibility for others, a meeting chamber emerges around the same flame. The change reflects identity and relationship; it was not purchased by decorating the burrow.

### The Living Web

A spider incarnation experiences Soul Space as tension across an endless web. Echoes occupy patterns of vibration rather than rooms, and a Sealed memory appears as a strand that cannot carry a signal. A later human incarnation may perceive the same structure as corridors of thread without changing what it represents.

### The Painted Door

A Soul Wound appears as a sealed library door. The current incarnation paints the door open, creating a convincing image, but the Threshold remains Sealed. Access changes only after the character confronts the betrayal and identity rejection that caused the Wound.

### The Shared Forge

An intertwined Weapon Soul maintains a forge Region by mutual agreement. The forge lets wielder and weapon examine memories of prior battles and discuss possible forms. It does not generate metal, upgrade the weapon, or unlock an evolution until the Soul Weapon rules recognize the required change.

### The God at the Gate

A god opens a valid Gate through an established oath and is invited into one audience chamber. The god can speak there but cannot inspect Echo Regions or approach the Soul Anchor. Greater power does not expand the agreed scope, and forcing the next Threshold would become Soul Intrusion.

### The Evolution Altar

A monster sees three forms reflected in an internal pool. The Soul Space is displaying candidates already made discoverable by the evolution system and Resonance. Touching a reflection does not waive hidden conditions or guarantee that the outside transformation can occur.
