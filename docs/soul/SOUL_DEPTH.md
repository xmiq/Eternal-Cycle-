# Soul Depth

## Purpose

Soul Depth measures a soul's qualitative capacity to integrate lives, Soul Imprints, bonds, contradictions, and world-changing experience without losing its continuing identity.

## Core Rule

Soul Depth grows when meaningful experience transforms the soul and that transformation becomes integrated into how the soul understands and expresses itself.

Depth is not a statistic, currency, moral score, age counter, or universal measure of power. It persists across reincarnation, is not spent to activate abilities, and cannot be increased through repetition alone.

## Terms

### Deepening Event

An experience or connected sequence of experiences that permanently expands the soul's perspective, identity, or capacity after meaningful integration.

### Depth Horizon

A broad qualitative description of what complexity a soul can coherently integrate. Horizons are developmental landmarks, not numerical levels or guaranteed power packages.

## What Soul Depth Governs

Soul Depth contributes to a soul's ability to:

- integrate many different Soul Imprints without losing coherence;
- hold conflicting instincts, identities, values, and memories in productive tension;
- support increasingly complex Soul Echoes, Soul Titles, Soul Constellations, and Soul Weapon bonds;
- endure radical changes of body, species, culture, and Age;
- approach demanding soul systems without immediate overload;
- remain aware through difficult Reincarnation transitions;
- survive contact with deep Soul Space or the Akashic Archive;
- qualify for rare evolutions, hybrid expressions, or world-scale interactions when their other requirements are met.

Depth provides capacity. It never supplies the missing knowledge, anatomy, skill, compatibility, permission, or Soul Resonance required to use that capacity.

## What Soul Depth Does Not Govern

Soul Depth does not directly determine:

- bodily statistics or combat power;
- skill proficiency or magical output;
- intelligence, wisdom, morality, courage, or social authority;
- conscious access to every retained memory or Soul Imprint;
- control over Soul Echoes, Weapon Souls, or other people;
- immunity to Soul Strain, Soul Wounds, corruption, or deception;
- the value or importance of one life compared with another.

A physically weak child, obscure craftsperson, or small monster may possess a deep soul. A godlike combatant may possess enormous power without comparable Depth.

## Depth, Integrity, and Resonance

These three qualities answer different questions:

| Quality | Governing question | Does not guarantee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Soul Depth** | How much meaning and complexity can the soul integrate? | Present access or control |
| **Soul Integrity** | How coherently is the soul functioning now? | Great capacity or maturity |
| **Soul Resonance** | How strongly does the soul align with a history, bond, identity, or potential? | Safe integration or sufficient capacity |

A deep soul can suffer low Integrity after a Soul Wound. A shallow soul can experience powerful Resonance and be overwhelmed because it lacks capacity. High Integrity means stability, not Depth.

## Becoming Deeper

A possible Deepening Event should satisfy four conditions:

1. **Meaningful pressure:** The experience matters to the soul and carries real uncertainty, cost, responsibility, or consequence.
2. **Transformation:** The soul gains or changes a perspective, commitment, relationship, identity, or way of understanding the world.
3. **Integration:** The change is accepted, reconciled, embodied through later choices, or otherwise made part of the continuing self.
4. **Persistence:** The transformation remains meaningful after the immediate reward, danger, or emotion has passed.

An event does not need to be violent, tragic, heroic, or historically important. Raising a child, mastering a craft, accepting an enemy's humanity, building a monster community, abandoning a cherished false belief, or learning to live within an unfamiliar body may deepen a soul.

## Common Sources of Deepening

- living seriously within a species or culture unlike prior incarnations;
- reconciling Soul Echoes with genuinely incompatible values;
- accepting responsibility for lasting consequences;
- forming, transforming, or releasing a profound bond;
- mastering a discipline until it changes perception or identity;
- creating something that remains meaningful beyond the incarnation;
- choosing under pressure when every available answer carries a real cost;
- confronting a repeated failure and responding in a fundamentally new way;
- surviving contact with a world-deep truth and integrating its implications;
- changing the course of a community, species, institution, or Age while being changed in return.

## What Does Not Create Depth by Itself

- the passage of time;
- gaining ordinary power, wealth, status, or fame;
- killing large numbers of enemies;
- suffering without reflection or transformation;
- dying, reincarnating, or accumulating short lives;
- repeating a lesson the soul has already integrated;
- copying memories or borrowing an Echo's expertise;
- consuming another being or stealing its power;
- collecting titles, skills, relationships, or species forms as trophies;
- performing a dramatic act solely to trigger a reward.

These experiences may create the circumstances for Deepening, but the soul must still be transformed and integrate what occurred.

## Diminishing Returns

Depth follows meaning rather than novelty alone, but repeating the same solution to the same kind of problem usually produces little further Deepening.

A soul that repeatedly conquers weaker settlements is demonstrating an established pattern. Another conquest is unlikely to deepen it. If the same soul later governs those settlements, becomes responsible for former enemies, fails under the weight of its own methods, and changes its understanding of rule, that connected experience may become a Deepening Event.

Returning to a familiar species, profession, or relationship role can still deepen the soul when the new life creates a genuinely different transformation. Variety helps, but difference without engagement is not enough.

## Depth Horizons

Horizons describe demonstrated capacity. They do not require a fixed number of lives or Deepening Events, and crossing one does not automatically unlock every possibility associated with it.

An ordinary soul begins within the Nascent Horizon unless an explicit origin rule establishes otherwise. Depth can grow substantially within one life and does not require Reincarnation; many lives provide opportunities, not automatic advancement.

### Nascent

The soul is shaped primarily by its current incarnation and a limited range of integrated imprints. It can form meaningful bonds and develop profoundly, but large conflicts among lives or soul systems are difficult to hold consciously.

### Rooted

The soul has a stable continuing identity beneath changing circumstances. It can coherently integrate important imprints, sustain soul-level bonds, and examine prior experience when another system provides access without being defined entirely by one life.

### Layered

The soul can integrate substantially different lives, species, values, and instincts while preserving current-incarnation agency. Contradictory Soul Echoes and early cross-tree possibilities can be approached without automatically collapsing into identity confusion.

### Vast

The soul can coherently support extensive history, several complex bonds, demanding Soul Space structures, and the strain of stable Interlife awareness when Reincarnation and Resonance provide it. It may qualify for rare hybrid or ancient paths, but must still earn every other requirement.

### Mythic

The soul can preserve identity through radical transformations, long Ages, and World Resets. It can survive limited contact with world-scale beings, laws, or Archive records that would overwhelm ordinary souls.

### World-Deep

The soul has capacity to survive direct engagement with structures that shape reincarnation, the Akashic Archive, or the laws of an Age when another system provides access. World-Deep capacity does not grant omniscience, invulnerability, ownership of the world, or freedom from consequence.

## Crossing a Horizon

Horizons are crossed through demonstrated integration, not through filling a hidden counter.

The GM should consider:

- whether the soul now holds complexity that its previous Horizon could not;
- whether this capacity appears consistently in choices rather than in one dramatic moment;
- whether important contradictions have been integrated instead of ignored;
- whether the change persists across pressure, loss, or reincarnation;
- whether the new Horizon creates meaningful possibilities and responsibilities.

A singular transformative event can reveal that a soul has crossed a Horizon, but the event does not award a predetermined amount of Depth. The Horizon describes the soul after the transformation; it is not payment for the event.

## Active and Dormant Imprints

Legitimately acquired skills, Skill XP, Stat XP, and other protected Soul Imprints persist regardless of current Depth.

Depth affects how much complexity can be coherently active or combined at once. Imprints that exceed present integration capacity become dormant, fragmented in expression, or difficult to access rather than being deleted. Soul Resonance, compatible embodiment, practice, and later Deepening may restore useful access.

There is no fixed skill-slot count. The GM should judge active complexity by compatibility, contradiction, current embodiment, and the soul's demonstrated Horizon. Closely integrated skills may impose less strain than unrelated or mutually hostile identities and systems.

## Persistence, Harm, and Suppression

Soul Depth normally persists through Final Death, Life Reconciliation, Reincarnation, long Interlife, and World Resets.

Depth is not spent, traded, transferred, consumed, or divided to pay for abilities. A teacher can create conditions for another soul to deepen but cannot donate personal Depth.

Soul Strain or a Soul Wound may suppress the expression of Depth, block access to integrated structures, or make the soul function below its demonstrated Horizon. This does not normally erase the Depth itself. True erosion requires an explicit soul-damage rule, fair warning, meaningful resistance, and consequences proportionate to permanent progression loss.

Greater Depth can also create risk. Deep souls may attract gods, Archive custodians, soul predators, ancient systems, or responsibilities that would never notice a Nascent soul.

## Interlife and Reincarnation

Soul Depth may provide the capacity to remain aware, preserve coherence, or endure deliberate movement during Interlife. It does not grant Interlife navigation by itself.

Choosing timing, destination, or incarnation direction still requires Reincarnation development, sufficient Soul Resonance, a valid candidate, and any relevant world or cosmological permission. Depth makes advanced navigation survivable; another rule must make it possible.

## System Interactions

- **Soul Fundamentals:** Depth expands the complexity that can become integrated without replacing Soul Integrity.
- **Reincarnation:** Depth persists and may support memory, stability, candidate compatibility, and Interlife awareness.
- **Soul Resonance:** Resonance accesses and aligns potential that Depth can safely contain.
- **[Soul Echoes](SOUL_ECHOES.md):** Depth supports clearer, more contradictory, and more cooperative Echo relationships.
- **[Soul Space](SOUL_SPACE.md):** Depth supports greater stability and complexity within the internal metaphysical world.
- **[Soul Titles](SOUL_TITLES.md):** Depth may support titles carrying broader or conflicting identity without turning them into bonuses.
- **Retained instincts:** Depth helps distinguish, reconcile, and adapt instincts from different bodies.
- **[Soul Constellations](SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md):** Depth supports the complexity of relationships that change form across many lives without creating recognition, reunion, or consent.
- **Soul Weapons:** Depth can sustain more complex intertwining while never replacing mutual trust or compatibility.
- **Akashic Archive:** Depth limits how much world-deep truth can be integrated without severe harm.
- **Development and skills:** Depth may qualify a soul for advanced combinations, but practice and system-specific requirements remain necessary.

## Exploit Limits

- Deepening cannot be awarded for repetitive danger, suicide, or disposable incarnations.
- Trauma does not become Depth merely because it caused suffering.
- A Soul Echo cannot perform another life's integration on behalf of the current incarnation.
- World-changing power does not create Depth when the soul remains unchanged.
- Repeating one species, build, or moral choice eventually yields little Depth unless its meaning changes.
- Depth cannot be converted directly into statistics, damage, mana, skill ranks, or guaranteed evolution.
- A Deepening Event cannot be manufactured by arranging a known checklist while avoiding genuine uncertainty and consequence.
- Killing, consuming, copying, or absorbing another being cannot transfer its Depth under the default rules.

## GM Procedure

When a possible Deepening Event occurs:

1. identify the pressure, choice, relationship, mastery, or truth involved;
2. state what changed within the soul, not only what happened externally;
3. look for integration through later choices and consequences;
4. compare the demonstrated capacity with the current Depth Horizon;
5. record the evidence for Deepening without assigning numerical points;
6. apply any related opportunity, responsibility, internal conflict, or Soul Strain through the appropriate system;
7. reveal the change through behavior, Soul Space, Resonance, Echo reactions, or world response as appropriate.

The GM may keep the formal Horizon hidden, but the evidence for Deepening and its consequences must be discoverable. A Horizon should never be raised or denied solely to force a preferred plot outcome.

## Examples

### Repeated Survival

A spider incarnation repeatedly escapes stronger predators using the same ambush pattern. The victories improve relevant skills but do not continue deepening the soul. When the spider later protects a colony, learns cooperation, accepts responsibility for weaker creatures, and changes how it understands survival, that longer transformation may become a Deepening Event.

### Conflicting Lives

A former saint and a former monster tyrant leave opposed Soul Echoes. Silencing one Echo does not create Depth. Building a present identity that understands both mercy and ruthless survival, then acting consistently under real pressure, may demonstrate a Layered soul.

### Power Without Depth

A dragon inherits extraordinary natural power and dominates its territory without meaningful challenge or reflection. Its body and species development may be exceptional while its Soul Depth remains Nascent or Rooted.
