# Soul Resonance

## Purpose

Soul Resonance governs how a soul aligns with its own history, Soul Imprints, identities, bonds, places, objects, and world-deep patterns strongly enough for them to affect the present.

## Core Rule

Resonance connects the current incarnation to something with which the soul has a genuine Resonance Basis. It can reveal, align, translate, or activate existing potential; it cannot invent history, create an unearned Soul Imprint, bypass incompatible embodiment, or compel another soul.

Soul Resonance is not one global score. Each soul develops a profile of different Links, and every Link may be quiet, active, harmonious, conflicted, damaged, or inaccessible independently.

## Terms

### Resonance Link

A persistent potential connection between a soul and a valid Resonance Target, justified by a Resonance Basis.

### Resonance Basis

The genuine shared cause that justifies a Link, such as lived identity, direct participation, integrated mastery, meaningful relationship, transformative creation, or inheritance explicitly recognized by another rule.

### Resonance Target

The specific history, Soul Imprint, Soul Echo, Weapon Soul, soul, relationship, place, object, species pattern, Soul Title, Archive Record, or world structure with which alignment is occurring.

### Resonance Trigger

A present condition that activates or strengthens a Link, such as a sensation, emotion, action, relationship, location, symbol, bodily state, repeated choice, Reincarnation transition, or World Reset.

### Resonance Profile

The complete pattern of a soul's developed Links and attunements. A Profile persists through reincarnation even though its active expression changes with each incarnation.

### Dissonance

Instability produced when active Links, identities, participants, or forms of expression cannot align coherently.

## Forms of Resonance

### Reflexive Resonance

Alignment with something already within the soul, such as a Soul Imprint, [Retained Instinct](RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md), Soul Echo, Soul Title, or memory.

### Relational Resonance

Alignment involving another soul or distinct person, including a Weapon Soul, recurring soul, bonded companion, rival, or member of a Soul Constellation.

### Environmental Resonance

Alignment with a place, object, ruin, species territory, work of creation, or other part of the world carrying a valid connection to the soul.

### Systemic Resonance

Alignment with Reincarnation, the [Akashic Archive](AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md), an Age, a World Reset, a world law, or another structure larger than an individual life.

These forms may overlap. A Soul Weapon awakening may combine reflexive, relational, and environmental Resonance in one event.

## Requirements for Activation

A valid Resonance event requires:

1. **Target:** Something specific is being aligned with.
2. **Basis:** The soul has a genuine Resonance Basis for a Link to that Target.
3. **Trigger:** A present event or condition makes the Link relevant now.
4. **Compatibility:** The current body, identity, circumstances, and intended expression can support the result.
5. **Capacity:** Soul Depth and Soul Integrity can contain the attempted alignment safely enough for it to occur.
6. **Access:** Any system-specific permission, development, consent, or unlock requirement is satisfied.

Similarity alone does not establish a Link. Finding a sword like one from a prior life, meeting someone who resembles an old friend, or visiting an unrelated ruin may create associations without genuine Resonance.

## Resonance States

States describe one Link or one event, not the soul's overall power. They are not mandatory numerical ranks, and different Links may occupy different states at the same time.

### Silent

The Link exists but produces no present awareness or usable alignment. A Silent Link is not lost.

### Stirring

The Link appears involuntarily through emotion, dreams, instinct, sensory fragments, unease, recognition, or brief changes in behavior.

### Attuned

The soul can deliberately notice, approach, or invoke the Link under suitable conditions. Results remain limited by memory, embodiment, skill, and system-specific rules.

### Harmonic

The Link aligns stably with the current incarnation. Past and present can reinforce one another without one replacing the other, allowing reliable expression or translation when all other requirements are met.

### Concordant

Several Links or willing participants align toward one purpose while preserving their distinct identities. Concordance may support joint techniques, shared perception, Soul Weapon cooperation, or stable reconciliation among Soul Echoes.

### World-Bearing

The soul sustains alignment with an Archive Record, Age, World Spirit, world law, or comparable structure. This state requires sufficient Depth and explicit access; it never follows from ordinary Resonance development alone.

## Establishing a Link

A Link may form when a soul:

- lives deeply enough within a species, culture, discipline, or identity for it to become a Soul Imprint;
- forms a relationship that changes one or more participants;
- creates, protects, destroys, or transforms something with lasting personal meaning;
- awakens or intertwines with a Weapon Soul;
- participates directly in an event remembered by a Soul Echo or recorded by the Archive;
- accepts a Soul Title that genuinely describes its identity;
- repeatedly responds to a place, oath, symbol, or purpose until it becomes integrated;
- undergoes Reincarnation, a World Reset, or another explicit soul-level process.

Contact, possession, imitation, or desire alone is insufficient. A stolen relic does not grant the thief its maker's Resonance, and consuming a creature does not automatically establish Links to its history or Soul Imprints.

## Deliberate Attunement

Resonance can be trained through both shared traditions and highly personal practices. Cultures may develop rituals, meditations, mnemonic arts, pilgrimages, paired exercises, or Soul Space disciplines, while individuals may rely on familiar movements, sensory anchors, conversation with an Echo or Weapon Soul, or careful reenactment under safe conditions.

Attunement practice may improve:

- recognition of a Link and its Trigger;
- deliberate entry into an accessible State;
- stability, clarity, and recovery;
- translation into a compatible present-life expression;
- distinction between Resonance, ordinary association, and outside manipulation.

Training cannot create a Resonance Basis, force an unwilling Target, reveal unrelated knowledge, or remove another system's requirements. A method works only when it engages a genuine Link, and no practice is universally effective for every soul or Target.

## Strengthening and Changing a Link

A Link becomes clearer and more stable through meaningful alignment rather than repeated activation alone.

Useful development may include:

- understanding the experience that formed the Link;
- expressing old knowledge through the current incarnation's real constraints;
- renewing a relationship through present consent and action;
- confronting a contradiction instead of suppressing it;
- practicing an accessible imprint under relevant pressure;
- repairing a bond or Soul Wound tied to the Link;
- discovering accurate context for an incomplete memory;
- integrating the Link into current identity without surrendering agency.

A Link's meaning or expression may change, but its Target does not silently become something else. Resonance with an old throne may evolve from a claim to rule into a commitment to protect the people who survived it. If that transformation creates a genuine connection to a different Target, the soul develops a new Link with its own Basis. Transformation is valid when the soul's relationship to the underlying history genuinely changes.

## Resonance Triggers

Common triggers include:

- encountering a former location, object, language, enemy, or creation;
- performing an action once mastered in another body;
- meeting a soul connected through a prior relationship;
- repeating or breaking an important oath;
- experiencing an emotion central to a Soul Echo or Soul Title;
- entering danger resembling a defining past event;
- touching a Soul Weapon or relic carrying a valid shared history;
- undergoing Final Death, Life Reconciliation, Reincarnation, or Interlife;
- witnessing a pattern associated with an Archive Record or World Reset.

The strongest events usually combine several triggers with current emotional or practical importance. A trigger should matter to the present incarnation, not merely resemble a detail from the past.

## Resonance Outcomes

When requirements are satisfied, Resonance may:

- produce emotion, instinct, sensory memory, dreams, or conscious recollection;
- reveal that a dormant Soul Imprint exists;
- support access to a retained skill or practiced memory;
- help translate a skill into a body-compatible expression;
- permit communication with a Soul Echo or Weapon Soul;
- stabilize cooperation with a willing bonded soul;
- reveal clues toward a hidden evolution, Soul Title, or Reincarnation candidate;
- identify a meaningful connection to a place, object, event, or soul;
- open an accessible region of Soul Space;
- permit limited contact with a valid Archive Record;
- support deliberate movement or awareness during Interlife when Reincarnation rules allow it.

An outcome should follow from the Target and Basis. Resonance with a former smithing life may support craft memory; it does not reveal an unrelated assassination merely because that knowledge would be useful.

## Memory and Truth

Resonance retrieves perspective, not perfect objective history.

A memory accessed through Resonance may be:

- incomplete;
- shaped by the former incarnation's beliefs and senses;
- emotionally intensified or muted;
- missing context learned by other people;
- distorted by Soul Strain, a Soul Wound, Dissonance, or outside interference.

Strong feeling does not prove factual accuracy. Contradictory memories should be treated as evidence to investigate rather than errors to erase.

The GM must not fabricate a false memory retroactively merely to invalidate a reasonable decision. Distortion requires an established cause and discoverable signs.

## Relational Resonance and Consent

One soul may feel its own side of a Relational Link without the other participant noticing or reciprocating.

Resonance never grants automatic:

- mind reading;
- control or possession;
- trust, loyalty, love, forgiveness, or obedience;
- recognition by the other participant;
- ownership based on a former relationship;
- access to another soul's private memories or Soul Space.

Mutual perception, communication, shared techniques, or entry into another Soul Space require willing participation or an explicit contested rule with meaningful resistance. A Soul Constellation preserves a pattern; it does not predetermine the present relationship.

## Dissonance

Dissonance occurs when Resonance activates without coherent alignment.

Common causes include:

- incompatible Soul Imprints or instincts activating together;
- a prior identity conflicting with present values;
- a current body being forced to express an unsuitable imprint;
- several Soul Echoes competing for attention;
- one participant resisting a relational alignment;
- an incomplete, false, or externally manipulated trigger;
- active Resonance exceeding Soul Depth or current Integrity;
- attempting World-Bearing contact without sufficient preparation.

Possible consequences include:

- intrusive emotion or memory bleed;
- unreliable skill expression;
- instinctive actions that conflict with intent;
- communication failure or hostility;
- temporary loss of access to a Link;
- Soul Strain;
- mutation of an attempted technique;
- a Soul Wound when the cause is severe and properly established.

Dissonance is not automatically failure or punishment. Understanding and integrating its contradiction may repair a Link, create a compatible expression, or contribute to a Deepening Event. The benefit comes from resolution, not from provoking Dissonance repeatedly.

## Effort, Fatigue, and Recovery

Soul Resonance is not a spendable resource pool. Deliberate activation creates effort and may cause fatigue or Strain according to:

- distance between present identity and the Target;
- incompatibility with the current body;
- emotional intensity;
- number and complexity of simultaneous Links;
- damage to Soul Integrity;
- resistance from another participant or system;
- the scale of the attempted outcome.

Recovery may require rest, present-life practice, reflection, Soul Space work, reconciliation, repair of a relevant bond, or treatment of a Soul Wound. Repeating an activation while exhausted increases risk without guaranteeing stronger Resonance.

## Persistence and Damage

The Resonance Profile and established Links normally persist through Final Death, Life Reconciliation, Reincarnation, Interlife, and World Resets.

Active Resonance does not remain equally strong in every incarnation. A new body, identity, Age, or environment may render a Link Silent until a relevant Trigger and compatible expression arise.

A Link may be obscured, distorted, suppressed, redirected, or damaged by a valid soul-level cause. Permanent severance is not a default consequence and requires an explicit rule, fair warning, meaningful resistance, and consequences appropriate to the Link's importance.

No ordinary action automatically causes permanent Resonance damage. Any rule capable of lasting damage must name the affected Link or part of the Profile, explain the soul-level cause, and distinguish suppression, distortion, injury, and severance.

## Assessment

Resonance is assessed per Link rather than as one total rating.

Characters may infer it through:

- recurring sensations, dreams, memories, or instincts;
- changes within Soul Space;
- reactions from Soul Echoes, Weapon Souls, Soul Titles, or bonded souls;
- repeated success or Dissonance under known triggers;
- specialized magic, artifacts, gods, World Spirits, or Archive Interfaces.

Assessments may identify a Target, State, Trigger, or obstruction, but should not expose every hidden requirement automatically. Powerful evaluators can still be mistaken, limited, or biased unless another rule makes them authoritative.

## Interactions

- **Soul Fundamentals:** Resonance activates genuine Links without creating new history or overriding current-incarnation agency.
- **Soul Depth:** Depth determines how much active alignment the soul can safely integrate.
- **Soul Integrity:** Integrity determines how coherently Resonance functions now.
- **Reincarnation:** Resonance influences memory access, candidate compatibility, and advanced Interlife navigation.
- **[Soul Echoes](SOUL_ECHOES.md):** Resonance governs when an Echo can be perceived, consulted, or expressed.
- **[Soul Space](SOUL_SPACE.md):** Resonance reveals pathways, Regions, Constructs, visitors, and conflicts within the internal world.
- **[Soul Titles](SOUL_TITLES.md):** Resonance determines when identity-bearing recognition becomes active or relevant.
- **[Retained Instincts](RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md):** Resonance can awaken, distinguish, or adapt instincts from prior bodies.
- **[Soul Constellations](SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md):** Resonance permits recognition and voluntary interaction across recurring relationships without determining present roles.
- **Soul Weapons:** Mutual Resonance supports awakening, intertwining, communication, and coordinated expression.
- **[Akashic Archive](AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md):** Resonance supplies target-specific alignment while an Archive Basis, Interface, permission, and Depth still govern reading.
- **[Soul Avatars](SOUL_AVATARS.md):** Relevant Links and an Avatar Trigger support Continuity Concordance without granting every history or skill access.
- **Skills and evolution:** Resonance may reveal or support dormant, crossover, hybrid, or hidden possibilities but never replaces their other requirements.

## Exploit Limits

- Repeating a safe Trigger does not strengthen a Link indefinitely.
- Manufactured emotion, danger, death, or ritual does not create a genuine Basis by itself.
- Resonance cannot reveal unrelated information outside its Target and history.
- Resemblance does not prove a recurring soul, past-life location, or destined relationship.
- One-sided Relational Resonance cannot bypass another person's consent or resistance.
- Resonance does not grant anatomy, statistics, skill mastery, evolution, or Archive access by itself.
- Stolen objects, consumed beings, copied memories, and impersonated titles do not automatically transfer Links.
- Deliberately provoking Dissonance does not guarantee Deepening, fusion, mutation, or power.
- A World-Bearing State cannot be reached by accumulating many weak Links.

## GM Procedure

When Resonance may occur:

1. name the Resonance Target;
2. identify the genuine Resonance Basis for the Link;
3. identify the present Trigger;
4. check body, identity, system, and consent compatibility;
5. compare the attempted outcome with Soul Depth and current Integrity;
6. determine the Link's present State or whether Dissonance occurs;
7. choose an outcome that follows from the Target and Basis;
8. apply fatigue, Strain, relationship effects, or world consequences when appropriate;
9. reveal enough sensory or causal evidence for the event to be understood and investigated;
10. record changes to the Link without assigning a universal Resonance score.

The GM may conceal the full Target or Basis when mystery requires it, but clues must remain consistent. Resonance should answer a connection before it answers a plot question.

## Examples

### A Place Remembered

A monster enters a ruined fortress built over a battlefield where one of its prior incarnations died. Familiar stonework alone is not enough. The soul's participation in that death establishes the Link, and the smell of the same ritual incense becomes the Trigger. Stirring Resonance produces fear, a fragment of a marching song, and the sense that a sealed stairwell matters; it does not provide a complete map.

### A Skill in Another Body

A human incarnation retains a spider life's web techniques. Handling enchanted thread triggers Reflexive Resonance. With practice and compatible tools, the Link becomes Harmonic enough to support a thread-based trap discipline. Resonance translates understanding; it does not give the human spinnerets.

### A Recurring Soul

The soul meets someone connected to several former lives. Relational Resonance creates familiarity and an emotional reaction, but the other person does not remember and owes nothing. Recognition becomes a choice and a mystery rather than proof of trust.

### Weapon Concordance

During a defining battle, wielder and awakening Weapon Soul willingly align around the same purpose. Their Concordant Resonance may permit intertwining if the Soul Weapon rules' other conditions are met. Intensity alone cannot substitute for mutual transformation.
