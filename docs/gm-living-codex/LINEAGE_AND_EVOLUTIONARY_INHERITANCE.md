# Lineage, Hybridization, and Evolutionary Inheritance

## Purpose

This document defines Step 13 of the GM Living Codex: the reusable developmental architecture that may follow successful formation under [Reproductive Compatibility](REPRODUCTIVE_COMPATIBILITY.md). It owns Lineage Templates, Inheritance Profiles, Species and Evolution Expression, Mana reconciliation and equalization, inherited Level 0 Instincts, Ancestral Echoes, and lineage stabilization.

It does not define attraction, consent, courtship, anatomy of reproduction, pregnancy, gestation, birth, childcare, marriage, family culture, or demographic simulation.

## Document Control

- **Owner:** reusable lineage architecture and inheritance-outcome design
- **Primary authorities:** [GM Living Codex](GM_LIVING_CODEX.md), [Species Registry](SPECIES_REGISTRY.md), [Hybridization](../monster-evolution/HYBRIDIZATION.md), [Mana](../magic/MANA.md), and [Level 0 Instinct](../skills/LEVEL_ZERO_INSTINCT.md)
- **Dependencies:** successful formation, contributing Lineage Templates, Evolution graph references, current conditions, and any valid assistance
- **Extensions:** species-specific profiles, reusable variants, stabilization methods, and later bounded reproduction procedures
- **Consumers:** GMs, World Engine population abstraction, campaign persistence, AI runtimes, and Living Codex tooling
- **Repository boundary:** no genealogy, actual parent, individual descendant, live lineage state, campaign probability, or populated Codex record belongs here

## Core Sequence

```text
Reproductive Compatibility
  -> Successful Formation
  -> Lineage Templates
  -> Mana Reconciliation and Equalization
  -> Species Expression
  -> Evolution Expression
  -> Inherited Level 0 Instincts
  -> Individual Development
```

Step 12 determines whether formation succeeds under stated conditions. Step 13 begins only after success and determines possible developmental outcomes. Compatibility percentages never become inheritance weights.

## Lineage Templates

A **Lineage Template** is a reusable inherited biological, magical, developmental, and metaphysical architecture. It may encode:

- body architecture and developmental pathways;
- Mana architecture, Channels, Magical Organs, elemental tendencies, and maintenance demands;
- Species Traits, sensory structures, regulation, lifecycle, and characteristic processes;
- Soul-body interfaces without carrying a soul or Soul progression;
- Evolution relations and Evolutionary Potential;
- possible Level 0 Instincts and Ancestral Echo definitions;
- provenance, design basis, and revision history.

A template is not an ancestry percentage, a character sheet, a soul, or a package of mastered Skills. Several templates may remain present without blending into one average body.

### Stable Identity

Every reusable template has an immutable Codex Stable ID. Species, reusable variants, and independently indexed Evolved Forms may reference one or more templates. Renaming, revising, splitting, merging, deprecating, or replacing a template preserves typed history under ordinary Living Codex governance.

### Carried Lineage

A **Carried Lineage** is a Lineage Template hereditarily present in an individual even when it does not substantially organize the current form. Carrying does not guarantee visibility, expression, future transmission, Evolution access, or player knowledge.

### Expressed Lineage

An **Expressed Lineage** materially contributes to the individual's current developmental architecture. Expression may be broad, localized, conditional, stage-dependent, suppressed, or supported by another template.

### Dominant Expression

**Dominant Expression** means one template overwhelmingly organizes current Species Expression. It does not erase other Carried Lineages or prove pure ancestry.

## Species Expression

**Species Expression** is the species-level body and developmental architecture currently expressed by an individual. Valid profile outcomes may include:

- predominantly one contributing species;
- Mixed Lineage;
- a stable hybrid or recognized hybrid species;
- a Pure-Line Throwback;
- a reusable variant;
- Born-Evolved expression;
- another explicitly defined viable result.

Appearance is evidence, not a complete lineage assay. A being may resemble one source while carrying or expressing less visible structures from others. Humanoid shape alone establishes neither low Reproductive Distance nor close lineage architecture.

### Mixed Lineage

A **Mixed Lineage** carries and materially expresses more than one Lineage Template through one coherent developmental architecture. It does not stack complete source bodies, action economies, Skills, or best traits. Whole-form integration, tradeoffs, and Hybrid Stability remain governed by [Hybridization](../monster-evolution/HYBRIDIZATION.md).

### Pure-Line Throwback

A **Pure-Line Throwback** occurs when a carried ancestral template becomes overwhelmingly expressed after one or more generations of weaker or mixed expression. Exact fractional genealogy is unnecessary. The applicable Inheritance Profile must establish that the template remained carried and that current conditions permit renewed expression.

## Evolution Expression and Potential

**Evolution Expression** is the Evolved Form architecture currently embodied. **Evolutionary Potential** is the set of Evolution branches that remain causally available under their own conditions.

A descendant may inherit:

- an earlier stable form with later branch potential;
- a Mixed Lineage with a bounded route set;
- an Evolved Form directly;
- a base form plus an Evolution-linked Level 0 Instinct;
- a variant or new branch defined by the profile.

Potential is not readiness, qualification, or destiny. Evolution graphs remain branching and non-exhaustive. An evolved form normally retains meaningful lineage relations to earlier and sibling forms, but compatibility and inheritance values are never copied automatically across those relations.

### Born-Evolved

A **Born-Evolved** individual begins life expressing an architecture normally reached later through an Evolution Route. Born-Evolved status grants the body, structural access, dependencies, vulnerabilities, and potential defined for that form. It grants no inherited memory, XP, mastery, learned technique, accomplishment, Soul Title, Soul Echo, Soul Weapon relationship, or personal history.

## Mana Reconciliation

**Mana Reconciliation** is the developmental process by which Mana patches, translates, coordinates, or stabilizes otherwise conflicting lineage architectures. It may address biological regulation, elemental structure, developmental timing, Magical Organs, Channels, Soul-body interfaces, or Evolution-linked architecture.

Mana does not ignore incompatibility. Reconciliation needs a valid source, route, capacity, timing, environment, and cost under the [Mana rules](../magic/MANA.md). Failure may suppress an outcome, reduce viability already established by Step 12, produce instability within a permitted outcome, or require a different profile; it cannot revise a completed compatibility result retroactively without an authorized correction.

## Mana Equalization

**Mana Equalization** is the tendency of competing inherited architectures to settle toward a viable developmental Mana equilibrium. Architectures with denser Channels, more specialized organs, tighter Soul-body interfaces, or greater ongoing energetic demands are generally harder to express naturally.

Equalization therefore often favors earlier, less demanding, or more stable lineage forms while preserving compatible potential and bounded instincts. It is not a universal suppression effect, moral judgement, or fixed formula.

### Evolutionary Attenuation

**Evolutionary Attenuation** is the lineage-specific reduction in natural full-form expression as inherited Evolution architecture becomes more demanding. It preserves common earlier forms, uncommon advanced forms, rare apex forms, ecological differentiation, and meaningful individual Evolution.

There is no universal halving rule. A profile may preserve advanced forms strongly, attenuate them sharply, favor intermediate forms, open a novel branch, or depend on environment. Two advanced contributors may increase advanced-expression likelihood without guaranteeing it.

## Inheritance Profiles

An **Inheritance Profile** is a sparse, reusable outcome model for a defined set of contributing lineage or form states under stated conditions. Profiles are created only when narratively or procedurally relevant, required for reusable design or population abstraction, or explicitly expanded by the GM.

An absent profile means **Not Yet Defined**. It does not authorize an invented distribution.

### Sources

A profile uses an ordered set of one or more contribution records rather than hardcoding exactly two parents. Each source record identifies:

- source position or role where direction matters;
- Lineage Template and current species or Evolution expression;
- carried lineages relevant to this profile;
- contribution requirements and provenance.

Two contributors are common. Multi-source magical, symbiotic, hive, artificial, or Soul-mediated additions remain possible only where an explicit origin rule permits them. Soul mediation never contributes another person's Soul facts.

### Conditions and Assistance

Profiles may reference environment, developmental conditions, Mana context, institutions, Skills, materials, symbionts, divine authority, technology, or stabilization methods. These references preserve their owners and do not grant access automatically.

### Outcomes

Each outcome may define:

- Species Expression and optional Evolution Expression;
- Carried and Expressed Lineage Templates;
- Evolutionary Potential;
- inherited Species Traits where the outcome architecture supports them;
- Level 0 Instincts under the Skill rules;
- Ancestral Echo definitions;
- dependencies, vulnerabilities, instability, and conditions;
- a weight and weight basis when a complete distribution is established.

When a profile claims a complete outcome table, active outcome weights under the same condition set must be nonnegative and sum to the profile's declared normalized total, normally 100. Partial or unknown distributions remain explicitly incomplete and cannot be sampled as complete.

### Separate Probability Stages

```text
Step 12: successful formation chance
Step 13, conditional on success: inheritance outcome distribution
```

No multiplication, substitution, or reuse occurs unless an external resolution procedure explicitly combines the separately resolved stages for a stated purpose.

### Illustrative Profile

```text
Forgekeeper + Gatherer

Gatherer                         50%
Gatherer with Level 0 instinct  25%
Forgekeeper                     25%
```

This illustrates schema semantics only. It creates no actual Codex species, universal weights, campaign genealogy, or result.

## Level 0 Instinct

An inherited capability uses the existing [Level 0 Instinct](../skills/LEVEL_ZERO_INSTINCT.md) representation. A profile may grant Level 0 access to an Evolution Skill associated with an ancestral form without granting that form or mature capability.

Instinct may support primitive expression, attention, intuition, behavior, pattern recognition, or easier learning where the Skill defines it. It never transfers XP, practiced reliability, memories, judgement, accomplishments, or normal Level 1 capability.

## Ancestral Echoes

An **Ancestral Echo** is a reusable hereditary pattern carried by lineage. It may influence instinct, affinity, behavior, dreamlike imagery, body development, Mana response, Skill acquisition, or Evolution tendency within its defined scope.

An Ancestral Echo is not a [Soul Echo](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md), consciousness, reincarnation memory, testimony, objective history, or complete ancestral memory. It has no independent agency. Similar imagery does not prove identity or historical accuracy.

## Lineage Stabilization

**Lineage Stabilization** is deliberate support that increases the reliability of a defined inherited architecture or outcome. Methods may include ritual, Mana-rich development, controlled environments, Soul-pattern support, alchemy, artificial incubation, selective breeding, lineage engineering, magical technology, symbiosis, divine intervention, or long-term adaptation.

Every method identifies source, mechanism, conditions, materials, Skills, institutions, risks, affected outcomes, and limits. Stabilization changes only the profile claims it owns. It does not change the natural baseline, guarantee success, grant mastery, provide consent, or make advanced forms universally sustainable.

Ancient or sophisticated lineages may maintain advanced forms through reliable stabilization. Their infrastructure and knowledge remain causal dependencies rather than proof that the same outcome is naturally common elsewhere.

## Natural and Assisted Profile Variants

Natural and stabilized inheritance use distinct condition sets or profile variants. A stabilized profile references its natural baseline and records the exact changed outcomes or weights. Several stabilization methods may coexist without being compressed into one unexplained bonus.

## GM Procedure

1. Resolve Reproductive Compatibility; stop if formation fails.
2. Identify every contributing Lineage Template and relevant species or Evolution expression.
3. Search the latest configured Living Codex for an applicable Inheritance Profile.
4. Apply any explicit Campaign Divergence without changing the base Codex.
5. If no profile exists, decide whether reusable or campaign-local definition is required now; otherwise preserve Not Yet Defined.
6. Evaluate source forms, carried lineages, actual developmental conditions, and origin roles.
7. Apply Mana Reconciliation, then Mana Equalization.
8. Apply valid environmental or artificial Lineage Stabilization.
9. Select only among active outcomes in one complete applicable distribution, or make a bounded qualitative ruling when canon permits and exact weights remain undefined.
10. Resolve Species Expression, Evolution Expression, Carried Lineages, and Evolutionary Potential.
11. Resolve only explicitly defined inherited Level 0 Instincts and Ancestral Echoes.
12. Persist reusable design through a complete Living Codex migration only after GM approval.
13. Persist actual individuals, genealogy, contributors, and results only in campaign state.
14. Validate the appropriate store and continue only after any required save-before-delivery boundary succeeds.

Hidden templates, weights, echoes, and stabilization methods remain GM Secrets or other protected knowledge until valid observation, Research, Magic, genealogy, experimentation, cultural knowledge, or capability reveals them.

## Population and Procedural Use

The World Engine may apply complete profiles in aggregate when generating populations, settlements, mixed communities, evolved populations, and ancient lineages. It need not simulate every formation. Aggregate use preserves conditions, uncertainty, stabilization infrastructure, ecological limits, and advanced-form rarity; it cannot turn a profile into a deterministic demographic forecast.

## Living Codex and Campaign Boundary

The Living Codex may store reusable templates, default profiles, Evolution inheritance relations, Ancestral Echo definitions, and stabilization methods. Campaign state stores actual contributors, Carried Lineages, Species and Evolution Expression, individual instincts, genealogy, mutations, Soul state, descendants, and campaign divergences.

Promoting a reusable campaign discovery requires separate GM approval and a Living Codex migration. A campaign Save Transaction and a Codex migration remain separate transactions.

## Safeguards

- Never derive inheritance from appearance or ancestry fractions alone.
- Never infer a missing profile or complete unknown weights.
- Never treat Step 12 percentages as Step 13 weights.
- Never guarantee advanced expression from advanced contributors.
- Never apply a universal attenuation formula.
- Never copy parent, variant, or Evolved Form profiles automatically.
- Never grant memories, XP, mastery, Soul facts, titles, relationships, or authority through lineage.
- Never make Level 0 a hidden Skill Seed subsystem or normal Level 1 capability.
- Never confuse Ancestral Echoes with Soul Echoes.
- Never let stabilization erase dependencies, risks, or natural baselines.
- Never store populated lineage or genealogy records in this repository.
