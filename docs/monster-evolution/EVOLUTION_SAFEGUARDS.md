# Monster Evolution Safeguards

## Purpose

This document consolidates exploit resistance, representation cleanup, agency protection, and consistency checks for the complete Monster Evolution framework. It prevents grinding, infinite branching, adaptation inflation, duplicate forms, universal apex species, mandatory chains, Skill duplication, embodiment bypass, and Soul bypass without denying unusual Evolution that satisfies the actual rules.

These safeguards preserve earned change. They are not permission to erase a valid form, conceal arbitrary limits, punish experimentation, or reject an effective route merely because the GM did not anticipate it.

## Core Rule

Every Evolution Claim must identify one bodily result, one valid Evolution owner, causal route provenance, a qualifying current form, meaningful current conditions, an actual transition, a distinct sustainable result, and its consequences.

Repetition, labels, dramatic events, accumulated history, external output, or similarity to a desired form cannot substitute for those elements. One event may support several systems, but one effect is recorded once under its smallest truthful owner.

## Terms

### Evolution Claim

A claim that a condition, action, history, resource, route, transition, or relationship creates, reveals, grants access to, completes, alters, preserves, or transmits Monster Evolution.

### Evolution Representation

The smallest truthful record that distinguishes a current form, variation, stage, branch, lineage, or route while preserving its provenance, Species Potential, lifecycle, requirements, tradeoffs, ecology, and consequences.

### Form Distinction Test

The qualitative test used to decide whether a proposed result requires a distinct Evolved Form or species record rather than an existing form, variation, Mutation, Hybrid Form, Trait Expression, adaptation, Skill, title, social category, or cosmetic name.

### Route Consolidation

The merger or reclassification of duplicate or needlessly separate Evolution Routes into one truthful route with explicit conditions, variants, or outcomes, without erasing established history or valid differences.

### Evolution Safeguard Audit

The ordered review used to test an Evolution Claim for ownership, provenance, current embodiment, meaningful evidence, transition integrity, distinct result, consequence, agency, duplication, and repeatability abuse.

## Safeguard Model

An Evolution Claim is valid only when each applicable layer succeeds.

| Layer | Safeguard question |
| --- | --- |
| **Claim** | What exact bodily change, access, or persistence is requested? |
| **Ownership** | Does Monster Evolution own the result, and are supporting systems limited to their own functions? |
| **Provenance** | What established source makes this route and transition possible? |
| **Current form** | Can the present body enter the route under its anatomy, lifecycle, health, and Species Potential? |
| **Current causality** | What pressure, Response Evidence, relationship, practice, or other current condition makes the route relevant? |
| **Transition** | What process changes the body, and what time, material, vulnerability, consent, and failure remain? |
| **Result** | Is the Evolved Form coherent, distinct, regulated, and sustainable? |
| **Ownership after change** | Which effects are Species Traits, Skills, Development, magic, Soul facts, tools, relationships, or institutions? |
| **Consequences** | What is gained, lost, needed, risked, closed, inherited, and changed in ecology or society? |
| **Agency and information** | Were meaningful choices, clues, stakes, and resistance handled fairly? |
| **Repeatability** | Would repeating or manufacturing the setup create unlimited progress without new causal change? |
| **Representation** | Is this the smallest record that tells the truth without duplication? |

A failed layer does not require punitive failure. Reclassify the claim under the correct owner, preserve valid evidence, and state what remains possible.

## Ownership Before Reward

Classify the requested effect before deciding whether it is desirable.

| Effect | Normal owner |
| --- | --- |
| Anatomy, lifecycle, integrated Species Traits, and Evolved Form | Monster Evolution and Species Development |
| Bodily conditioning, recovery, tolerance, and current regulation | Physical Development |
| Learned detection, control, timing, tactics, and techniques | Skill Engine |
| Magical source, affinity, Channel, Spell, Ritual, or effect | [Magical Development](../progression/MAGICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md) and the [Magic rules](../magic/README.md) |
| Culture, office, law, coordination, and institutional support | Social, Profession, and society rules |
| Mutation caused outside ordinary form organization | Mutations |
| Multi-source whole-body integration | Hybridization |
| Soul history, access, identity, and bounded transition support | Soul Engine and Soul Interaction with Evolution |
| Current population, habitat, pressure, resources, and consequences | Monster Ecology and World Engine |

An Evolved Form may interact with every row. It does not absorb every row. New anatomy may create a route to learn flight control; it does not grant a fully developed Flight Skill. A ritual may cause a valid transition; the resulting form does not own the ritual's knowledge.

## Anti-Grinding and Anti-Farming

[Grinding](../progression/DEVELOPMENT_SAFEGUARDS.md#grinding) repeats solved or low-variation activity without materially extending capability. [Farming](../progression/DEVELOPMENT_SAFEGUARDS.md#farming) manufactures or resets conditions to claim a reward without satisfying its real requirements. Neither creates Evolution.

### Kills and Consumption

Kill counts, defeated threat categories, consumed bodies, absorbed residue, and collected trophies are not Evolution currency.

Violence or consumption may matter through:

- actual nutrition, material, exposure, ecology, or danger;
- a valid assimilation, Mutation, magical, symbiotic, or ritual source defined by another rule;
- meaningful behavior and Response Evidence;
- relationships, reputation, law, and world consequences.

Even then, the route must specify what transfers, why it is compatible, how it is integrated, and what does not transfer. Killing a thousand flame creatures does not grant fire anatomy. An explicit digestive assimilation route may use bounded material from one, but it remains limited by source, body, regulation, transition, and consequence.

### Repeated Exposure and Survival

Exposure can provide pressure, feedback, conditioning, injury, disease, Adaptation Lag, or Response Evidence. It cannot be converted into a hidden exposure total.

Once a being repeats a solved response under unchanged conditions, further repetition provides little new evidence unless difficulty, understanding, body, stakes, or integrated response changes meaningfully. Survival proves that survival occurred; it does not prove that a better form has become available.

### Time, Age, and Waiting

Time may permit maturation, recovery, seasonal windows, resource accumulation, social preparation, or gradual change. Time alone does not buy Evolution.

A species may have a mandatory lifecycle metamorphosis, but that is a specific body process with stages, requirements, and failure rather than a universal reward for reaching an age.

### Pain, Injury, Suffering, and Death

Harm can close routes, reduce compatibility, impair transition, create maladaptation, or cause Final Death. It is not proof of growth.

Near-death events, sacrifice, coercion, repeated injury, and engineered danger matter only through their actual meaning, response, source, and consequence. They do not guarantee a hidden branch, Mutation, apex form, Soul condition, or stronger failure outcome.

### Manufactured Conditions and Retries

Arranging the surface appearance of a condition does not reproduce its causal meaning. Staged worship does not create a Soul Title, captive prey does not create ecological mastery, and a controlled crisis does not necessarily produce the uncertainty or responsibility of the original route.

A failed transition cannot be rerolled unchanged until it succeeds. Each attempt consumes time and resources, changes the body and world where appropriate, and produces only the consequences supported by its source. New preparation may justify a later attempt; repetition alone does not.

### Breeding, Construction, and Candidate Farming

Repeated births, eggs, spawnings, artificial bodies, hybrids, Mutations, or Reincarnation candidates do not produce selectable trait rolls or rarity chances. Every origin follows its actual inheritance, variation, compatibility, personhood, resource, and world rules.

Persons cannot be treated as disposable progression materials merely because a route involves lineage, symbiosis, mentorship, or Soul compatibility.

## Preventing Infinite Branching

A separate branch exists because a distinct causal pathway leads to a meaningfully distinct form, not because a designer can name another combination.

Before adding a branch, identify:

1. route provenance different from or meaningfully variant within existing routes;
2. conditions and transition that change play;
3. organised Species Potential that differs materially;
4. gains, losses, dependencies, or ecology not represented already;
5. why a variant, Mutation, Hybrid Form, Technique, adaptation, or local label is insufficient;
6. how the branch relates to, converges with, diverges from, or closes other routes.

Do not create a separate branch for:

- every color, size, sex, caste, profession, culture, habitat, diet, element, weapon, title, Skill, tactic, mood, or reputation;
- every minor adjustment of one trait;
- every pairwise combination of existing branches;
- every temporary, borrowed, artificial, divine, corruptive, or symbiotic effect;
- every observer's name for the same form;
- hypothetical future possibilities with no current provenance.

Use Route Consolidation when several entries share the same bodily result and differ only by approach, clue, local name, or support. One route may have several valid sources or condition variants where those differences do not create distinct forms.

Consolidation must not erase meaningful provenance. Convergent forms can remain distinct lineages when their origins, structures, lifecycle, regulation, ecology, or future routes genuinely differ even if their function looks similar.

## Adaptation Is Not Evolution

[Monster Adaptation](MONSTER_ADAPTATION.md) describes a functional response across existing owners. It becomes Monster Evolution only when a valid Evolution Route completes a lasting reorganisation of current Species Potential into a distinct form.

None of these facts is sufficient by itself:

- the response is useful;
- it has lasted a long time;
- it is dramatic or visible;
- it changes behavior or performance;
- it has become reliable;
- it can be taught or socially transmitted;
- it is a Stable Mutation;
- it is inherited under an existing form;
- several adaptations share one theme;
- the player wants the change to become permanent.

Adaptation may provide Response Evidence or Route Readiness. It does not accumulate into an Evolution meter. If no distinct transition and Evolved Form exist, preserve the valid bodily Development, Skill, Current Instinct, institution, ecological response, Mutation, or other actual owner.

### Adaptation Boundary Test

Ask:

1. Did organized Species Potential change, or did the current form learn, condition, regulate, express, or receive support differently?
2. Is there a transition with provenance, opportunity, cost, and failure?
3. Does the result require a new whole-form description?
4. Did lifecycle, regulation, structures, needs, or future route access change coherently?
5. Would removing training, equipment, spell, institution, symbiont, exposure, or maintenance return the same underlying form?

The fifth question is evidence, not an absolute test. A source-dependent Evolved Form can remain Evolution, and a permanent injury can remain injury rather than Evolution.

## The Form Distinction Test

Use a distinct Evolved Form or species representation only when it tells a materially different bodily truth.

Assess:

- **provenance and origin:** where the form comes from and how bodies enter it;
- **organisation:** how anatomy, materials, Magical Organs, regulation, cognition, and action economy work together;
- **Species Potential:** which traits, expressions, limits, and body-owned access routes are current;
- **lifecycle and renewal:** maturation, maintenance, reproduction, construction, awakening, dormancy, or replacement;
- **ecology:** habitat, niche, resources, waste, relationships, pressures, and sustainability;
- **tradeoffs:** meaningful gains, losses, vulnerabilities, incompatibilities, and dependencies;
- **route structure:** what future transitions open, close, converge, or diverge;
- **inheritance and variation:** what can recur in later bodies and by which process.

No fixed number of differences is required. The combined distinction must be causally coherent and useful in play.

### Usually Not a Separate Form

- cosmetic appearance without functional change;
- a current-life Skill, Profession, office, culture, reputation, or title;
- temporary transformation or equipment;
- one isolated Mutation not integrated into Species Potential;
- ordinary Trait Expression within an existing form;
- individual injury, conditioning, illness, or magical status;
- a local name for an already represented lineage;
- an apex reputation without a distinct Evolved Form.

### May Require a Separate Form

- a completed route that reorganises several structures and their regulation;
- a stable Hybrid Form with a coherent repeatable origin;
- a lineage with distinct renewal, lifecycle, ecology, and future routes;
- a convergent body whose separate provenance and organisation matter;
- a specialised or regressed form with real losses and changed needs;
- an individual Evolved Form not yet inherited but bodily distinct.

A distinct individual form is not automatically a new species. Stable lineage status still requires repeatable coherent origin and ecological persistence.

## Preventing Duplicate Species and Forms

Before adding a species, form, or branch:

1. compare it with all known representations in the relevant family;
2. run the Form Distinction Test;
3. separate social taxonomy from bodily truth;
4. preserve convergent provenance without duplicating identical mechanics;
5. classify one-off Mutations, hybrids, transformed states, and variants correctly;
6. choose the smallest useful representation;
7. use aliases or regional names rather than duplicate records where appropriate;
8. record unresolved taxonomy as world belief, not several simultaneous canonical truths.

Two societies may dispute whether forms are one species. That disagreement can be meaningful without requiring the rules to duplicate one body. Conversely, two forms can be mechanically similar yet remain distinct when incompatible origins, lifecycles, Souls, or ecologies make the difference consequential.

## No Universal Apex

Every [Apex Monster](APEX_MONSTERS.md) claim names an Apex Domain, context, scale, dependencies, and Counter-Ecology.

An apex form is never:

- the universally strongest species;
- the final rung of all routes;
- immune to ordinary consequence;
- entitled to leadership, worship, or obedience;
- automatically rare, unique, or legendary;
- superior outside its stated domain;
- guaranteed to remain apex after ecology changes;
- a required answer to another apex form;
- protected by encounter scaling.

Greater capability usually creates larger needs, visibility, specialization, opposition, or systemic consequence. These are causal tradeoffs, not a balancing tax added solely because the form is strong.

Do not solve apex loss by inventing a higher apex tier. A form may decline, migrate, adapt, branch, simplify, cooperate, become irrelevant, or go extinct.

## No Mandatory Evolution Chain

Evolution Route Maps describe supported possibilities. They neither command a being to evolve nor establish one correct order for all members.

Valid outcomes under pressure include:

- remaining in the current form;
- adapting within that form;
- learning Skills or using tools;
- receiving social, magical, or ecological support;
- migrating, negotiating, hiding, or changing behavior;
- attempting one of several routes;
- failing, declining, or becoming extinct.

A route may close because anatomy, lifecycle, another transition, lost ecology, or deliberate commitment changed its requirements. Closure needs cause and fair information where choices are irreversible.

Species-specific maturation or metamorphosis may be unavoidable when its lifecycle establishes that truth. It is not a universal power ladder, and individuals still retain agency over whatever preparation, timing, resistance, interpretation, or alternatives their actual situation permits.

Involuntary Evolution or transformation requires a defined source, route, warning, resistance, scope, failure, and aftermath. It cannot be introduced merely to force a preferred build or plot.

## Skill and Development Integrity

Evolution grants the Evolved Form's body and body-owned access. It does not grant learned control.

After transition:

- new anatomy is recorded as Species Potential or Species Traits;
- deliberate control, timing, interpretation, and adaptable use remain Skills;
- current Physical, Magical, Profession, Social, and other Development remain separate;
- retained Skill XP may accelerate valid redevelopment without becoming immediate reliability;
- inaccessible former Skills remain latent rather than being converted into anatomy;
- a bodily effect and learned method are resolved once each under their own owner;
- a species name does not bundle every culturally common Skill;
- collective capability remains with institutions and groups;
- access to a tree or branch is not mastery of its contents.

If one claimed effect appears in both a Species Trait and Skill, split body capacity from learned use or choose the single truthful owner. Do not award duplicate range, damage, resistance, actions, or reliability because both labels sound applicable.

## Embodiment and Transition Integrity

The current body is the transition substrate. Every route must respect:

- current anatomy, Species Potential, lifecycle, maturity, health, injury, and existing Mutations;
- material, energy, mana, environmental, social, and constructed resources;
- transition duration, vulnerability, location, and interruption;
- Integration Bridges, symbionts, rituals, makers, institutions, and other dependencies;
- one current action economy and one coherent resulting body;
- losses, inaccessible capabilities, calibration, and recovery after change.

No route may:

- stack every prior body beneath the new one;
- use Retained Development as missing tissue or maturation;
- call equipment, a spell, a Soul memory, or social authority anatomy;
- skip incompatible intermediate work without another explicit route;
- create resources from the fact that a transition is narratively important;
- turn failed regulation into unlimited trait slots;
- preserve every old advantage while ignoring new needs.

Substitution is valid when another source genuinely performs the required function. The substitute keeps its own cost, limits, vulnerability, owner, and failure behavior.

## Soul Integrity

Apply [Soul Interaction with Evolution](SOUL_INTERACTIONS.md) before accepting any Soul-based Evolution Claim.

A Soul system must have one named role: pressure, discovery, condition, compatibility, transition support, or interpretation. It cannot silently take all six.

Soul Depth, Resonance, Echoes, Retained Instincts, Titles, Soul Avatars, Constellations, Soul Space, Soul Weapons, Archive access, prior species lives, and Reincarnation do not provide:

- route provenance;
- current Species Potential;
- current Response Evidence by default;
- missing anatomy or transition materials;
- a former form as a saved loadout;
- unrestricted candidate or branch choice;
- automatic Skill or Development access;
- extra actions, merged souls, or inherited Soul history;
- a guaranteed rare, hybrid, ancient, or apex form.

Persistent history can make a valid route richer and more accessible in the exact ways its owning systems permit. Reject unearned scope, not legitimate trans-incarnational meaning.

## Mutation and Hybridization Integrity

A Mutation is not Evolution merely because it is useful, stable, inherited, or dramatic. It must enter a valid route and become coherently integrated before Monster Evolution owns a new form.

A Hybrid Form is not a trait list. It requires a valid Hybrid Origin, compatibility across the whole body, one regulatory and material budget, explicit progression ownership, dependencies, failure, and Soul arrangement.

Neither system permits:

- repeated consumption or exposure to copy desired traits;
- unlimited Mutation stacking;
- best-of-every-source inheritance;
- automatic dual-tree access;
- one cosmetic change becoming a new species;
- prior lives serving as an anatomical library.

## Ecology and Population Integrity

An Evolved Form must be able to persist under its stated conditions. Define resources, waste, habitat, lifecycle, predators, competitors, symbionts, maintenance, and social support relevant to its form.

Strong individual capability does not guarantee population viability. A form may be individually formidable and unable to renew without rare sites, long care, makers, or vulnerable partners.

One transition does not automatically:

- rewrite a whole population;
- become heritable;
- create a stable lineage;
- fill an empty niche;
- avoid Counter-Ecology;
- rescue a threatened species;
- survive a magical or environmental change.

Inheritance, spread, replacement, invasion, and extinction follow their own causal processes. Current numbers and outcomes remain external campaign state.

## Hidden Information and Fairness

Hidden routes and conditions are valid only with prior Condition Provenance, proportionate Route Clues, several reasonable inquiry paths where circumstances allow, and meaningful information before irreversible commitment.

The GM must not:

- add a requirement after seeing the player's plan;
- hide a known fatal tradeoff solely to force failure;
- use secret kills, pain, time, consumption, or repetition counters;
- deny information justified by established Skills or knowledge;
- make obscurity imply superiority;
- invent a surprise branch solely to rescue or punish a character;
- turn every mystery into a menu of exact outcomes.

Uncertainty may remain honest. Players need fair evidence and agency, not omniscience.

## Failure and Partial Outcomes

Failure follows the attempted route's actual source and transition.

Possible outcomes include:

- no transition and retained preparation;
- partial resource loss or delay;
- bodily injury, exhaustion, illness, or instability;
- route closure or changed conditions;
- a source-consistent Mutation or partial transformation;
- successful transition with a misunderstood cost;
- interruption that preserves some current-life evidence;
- Final Death where the circumstances genuinely warrant it.

Failure does not automatically produce a stronger secret form, Evolution progress, Soul Depth, a useful Mutation, or immunity to the same problem. It also does not erase valid Skills, Development, Soul Imprints, knowledge, or Response Evidence unless the relevant owning rule says the event changed them.

Consequences should be proportionate, discoverable where possible, and tied to the route rather than added as punishment for player creativity.

## Representation Cleanup

Evolution records may become redundant as the world, rules, or understanding changes. Clean them up when:

- two branches describe the same form under different names;
- a supposed form is actually a Skill, Mutation, Hybrid Form, transformation, adaptation, title, caste, or social category;
- a route duplicates another route's source, conditions, transition, and result;
- an apex tier exists only to outrank a previous tier;
- every small variation has become a separate branch;
- inherited and individual forms have been conflated;
- a Soul, Skill, and Species effect has been recorded several times.

Cleanup may merge, split, rename, reclassify, retire, or clarify records. Preserve:

- what actually happened in the world;
- legitimately earned Development and Soul persistence;
- meaningful bodily differences;
- known aliases and mistaken classifications;
- consequences of choices made under prior understanding.

Cleanup corrects representation. It does not retroactively change bodies, confiscate valid capability, or pretend a former belief never existed.

## Evolution Safeguard Audit

Before approving or retaining an Evolution Claim:

1. state the exact claimed bodily effect or route access;
2. identify one Evolution owner and every bounded supporting owner;
3. establish route and Condition Provenance before outcome resolution;
4. verify current Body Compatibility and Species Potential;
5. identify meaningful current pressure, Response Evidence, and non-evolutionary alternatives where applicable;
6. preserve source, opportunity, transition, material, time, vulnerability, consent, cost, and failure;
7. define the Evolved Form as one coherent body with gains, losses, needs, and dependencies;
8. run the Form Distinction Test and compare existing representations;
9. separate Species Traits from Skills, Development, magic, tools, society, and Soul effects;
10. test Soul involvement under one named permitted role;
11. test ecology, renewal, inheritance, Counter-Ecology, and population consequences;
12. test player agency, fair clues, irreversible choice, and involuntary effects;
13. ask whether repetition, counting, collection, retries, or manufactured conditions would grant unlimited progress;
14. consolidate or reclassify duplicate and incidental records;
15. allow the claim when every necessary layer holds, even if the combination is unusually effective;
16. record only reusable rules in canon and current outcomes in the external Campaign Record.

### Audit Outcomes

- **Valid Evolution:** the route and distinct bodily result satisfy the complete audit.
- **Valid Variant:** the change fits an existing form and needs only bounded variation or Trait Expression.
- **Supporting Evidence:** real preparation or history exists, but Route Readiness or transition is incomplete.
- **Different Owner:** the result belongs to Skill, Development, Mutation, Hybridization, magic, Soul, society, equipment, or world state.
- **Consolidate:** an existing route or form already represents the result.
- **Invalid Claim:** provenance, compatibility, transition, consequence, or agency fails and no narrower truthful result remains.
- **Provisional Ruling:** canon is insufficient during alpha play; use [Alpha Playtest Rules](../gm/ALPHA_PLAYTEST_RULES.md) without making the ruling permanent automatically.

## Stress Tests

### Kill-Count Evolution

A hunter claims a horned tyrant form after killing one thousand rivals. The count is not currency. Evaluate ecology, behavior, current Development, Response Evidence, route provenance, transition, and form costs. Rivalry may have changed the world without creating the route.

### Exposure Ladder

A monster repeatedly enters a controlled acid bath to unlock sequential resistance forms. Exposure may develop tolerance, injury, knowledge, or a valid adaptive response. It creates no branch ladder unless distinct routes and transitions independently exist.

### Every Adaptation Becomes a Branch

A climber improves grip through conditioning, learns safer placement, and uses resin-coated tools. These are Physical Development, Skill, and equipment. Their useful combination may support a future route, but none should be relabeled as three Evolved Forms.

### Cosmetic Species Multiplication

Three regions use different names for color variants of one coherent form. Unless origin, Species Potential, lifecycle, ecology, or route structure materially differs, use one form with regional variation and aliases.

### The Universal Apex

A proposed “Omega Dragon” is described as strongest in every habitat, immune to all counters, self-sufficient, and the final branch of every draconic route. It fails the Apex Domain, dependency, ecology, route, and Form Distinction safeguards. Narrow it into a sourced form with real tradeoffs or reject it.

### Mandatory Upgrade Chain

A route map lists larva, warrior, champion, king, emperor, and god as compulsory upgrades. Replace rank assumptions with actual lifecycle and route causality. Mandatory metamorphosis may remain where species biology supports it; political roles and universal superiority do not become body stages.

### Skill Bundle

A winged Evolved Form is said to grant expert flight, aerial tactics, navigation, and weather prediction. Wings and body-owned senses belong to the form. Control, tactics, navigation, and prediction require their own current learning and Development.

### Soul Loadout

A reincarnator combines dragon Resonance, a spider instinct, **Monster King**, and an apex Echo to request a flying web-breath sovereign form. Each Soul fact may perform one permitted role for a pre-existing compatible route. Together they do not assemble anatomy or invent a Hybrid Form.

### Infinite Hybrid Branches

A designer proposes a branch for every pair and triple of known species. Hybridization requires an actual origin and whole-body compatibility. Hypothetical combinations with no causal route are not branches, and similar coherent outcomes should be consolidated.

### Failure Fishing

A character repeats an unstable transition hoping failure will produce a rare Mutation. Failure follows the source and changes later conditions. It cannot be sampled as a reward table, and repeated self-harm does not guarantee a useful outcome.

## Phase-Wide Safeguards

- **No universal level, rarity tier, power score, or final form.**
- **No kill, consumption, exposure, age, pain, death, or repetition currency.**
- **No automatic Evolution from pressure, survival, adaptation, Mutation, inheritance, or permanence.**
- **No infinite branches, cosmetic stages, duplicate forms, or pairwise-combination catalogs.**
- **No mandatory Evolution chain outside a causally defined lifecycle process.**
- **No universal apex, immunity, guaranteed rescue, or escalation treadmill.**
- **No free anatomy, body stacking, action duplication, or transition-resource bypass.**
- **No automatic Skills, Development, control, or tree mastery from a form.**
- **No Soul anatomy, saved-form loadout, universal branch key, or current-life bypass.**
- **No retroactive route, hidden counter, arbitrary surprise, or player-targeted punishment.**
- **No automatic inheritance, species redefinition, ecological viability, or population spread.**
- **No cleanup that erases valid capability, history, agency, or consequence.**

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define species, branches, live forms, route availability, campaign outcomes, numerical caps, encounter scaling, or a universal balance formula. It supplies qualitative tests for applying and maintaining the canonical Monster Evolution rules.
