# Hidden Evolution Conditions

## Purpose

This document defines how an Evolution Route may depend on conditions that characters, cultures, players, or observers do not fully know. It makes hidden routes discoverable and fair without converting them into public checklists or allowing the GM to invent requirements after the outcome.

## Core Rule

A **Hidden Evolution Condition** is a real, route-specific condition whose existence or exact function is not yet reliably known to the relevant observer.

Hidden means unknown, obscured, misunderstood, forgotten, protected, or difficult to interpret. It does not mean causeless. Every hidden condition must:

1. exist before it determines an outcome;
2. follow established world and system rules;
3. leave clues proportionate to its importance and observability;
4. relate meaningfully to the resulting form;
5. preserve more than one reasonable path of inquiry or response where circumstances permit;
6. avoid secret farming counters and arbitrary punishment;
7. retain the ownership of every Development, Skill, Soul, magical, social, or ecological contribution.

## Terms

### Hidden Evolution Condition

A real condition of a specific Evolution Route whose existence, relevance, or exact function is not yet reliably known to a relevant observer.

### Route Clue

Discoverable evidence that indicates a route, condition, transition risk, or resulting tradeoff without necessarily revealing the complete answer.

### Condition Provenance

The causal origin explaining why a Hidden Evolution Condition belongs to its route and how it affects transition.

### False Theory

An in-world interpretation of route evidence that is incomplete or mistaken. A False Theory is a belief held by an observer, not permission for the GM to change world truth retroactively.

### Route Discovery

The process by which observation, records, experiment, instruction, Soul access, or lived experience turns unknown route facts into justified knowledge.

## Information Layers

Keep these facts separate:

| Layer | Question |
| --- | --- |
| World truth | What conditions actually govern the route? |
| Available evidence | What signs, records, outcomes, and patterns can be discovered? |
| Character knowledge | What does this character justifiably know or suspect? |
| Cultural theory | What does a community, institution, or tradition teach? |
| Player knowledge | What has the GM fairly communicated to the player? |

A culture can be wrong while the route remains stable. A player may recognise a pattern their character does not. A character may possess specialist knowledge the player needs the GM to explain. Resolve these differences through the [Game Master Framework](../gm/GAME_MASTER_FRAMEWORK.md), not by silently moving facts between layers.

## Valid Condition Categories

### Structural Conditions

The current body must possess, lose, align, or stabilise specific structures. Clues may appear through failed moults, anatomy, healing behavior, related species, or known incompatibilities.

### Lifecycle Conditions

A route depends on maturity, dormancy, spawning, renewal, seasonal form, or a Transition Window. Important windows should have biological, magical, cultural, or ecological signs.

### Environmental Conditions

A route requires a habitat, pressure pattern, resource, magical field, host, symbiont, or world event. The condition must have a defined source and access route.

### Developmental Conditions

The current being must demonstrate embodied control, Physical Development, Skill integration, magical regulation, social practice, or other current-life capability relevant to surviving or using the resulting form.

Developmental conditions test actual capability. They do not consume Development as currency or transfer mastery into Species Traits.

### Behavioral Integration Conditions

A route depends on a meaningful, repeated way of solving problems or inhabiting a niche. Repetition matters only when it includes feedback, variation, correction, consequence, and integration. Empty drills, staged danger, or repeated trivial acts do not qualify.

### Relational Conditions

A route depends on a genuine symbiosis, bond, social role, collective structure, negotiated relationship, or sustained opposition. The relationship's own agency and costs remain real. Another being cannot be reduced to an unlock object.

### Sacrifice and Tradeoff Conditions

A route requires relinquishing a structure, opportunity, resource, bond, status, alternative branch, or other meaningful possibility because the resulting form cannot support both.

A sacrifice is not measured by pain alone. It must be causally connected to the route and cannot be satisfied through disposable substitutes that preserve the supposed cost.

### Historical and Meaning Conditions

A route may depend on deeds, identity, tradition, a Soul Title, Resonance, or another history only where an explicit receiving rule permits it. The condition must use the meaning and consequences of that history rather than a label as a password.

### Transition Conditions

The route needs a catalyst, protected site, ritual, material, magical state, assistance, or onset sequence. Possessing the ingredient does not replace all other readiness requirements.

## Fair Clues

The more consequential and less reversible a hidden condition is, the stronger its clue structure should be.

Useful Route Clues include:

- recurring anatomical signs;
- environmental or magical reactions;
- related forms displaying partial structures;
- reliable historical records or oral traditions;
- ecological patterns and missing dependencies;
- failed or interrupted transitions with consistent consequences;
- specialist observation, dissection, healing, divination, or experimentation under its own rules;
- Soul Echo testimony, Resonance, Archive access, or Titles where those systems permit information;
- institutions protecting a Transition Window or resource for reasons characters can investigate;
- visible tradeoffs in existing Evolved Forms.

Clues can be incomplete, culturally framed, or contested. They must not all point away from the truth solely to preserve surprise.

## Discovery States

These are descriptions of knowledge, not route progression ranks:

- **unknown:** no relevant observer has connected the available evidence;
- **suspected:** evidence supports a possibility but important alternatives remain;
- **modelled:** an observer can explain likely conditions and risks;
- **verified:** repeated evidence or a valid transition strongly confirms the condition;
- **institutionalised:** reliable knowledge has become teachable practice, tradition, or procedure.

Different observers can occupy different states. Verification of one condition does not reveal every branch.

## Experimentation

Characters may investigate routes through observation, controlled exposure, comparative anatomy, records, ritual, magic, social inquiry, and cautious trials.

Experiments require:

- a question or theory;
- an intervention that could produce informative variation;
- observable outcomes;
- meaningful cost, time, uncertainty, or risk appropriate to the method;
- respect for consent and harm where other beings are involved;
- interpretation through relevant Skills and knowledge.

Repeatedly feeding random materials, killing categories of beings, inflicting injuries, or attempting every action is not valid discovery unless an established causal theory and evidence support the method. The GM should reward better understanding with clearer predictions, not guaranteed success.

## False Theories and Cultural Knowledge

Traditions may encode a valid procedure while misunderstanding why it works. Scholars may identify one condition but miss another. Institutions may hide knowledge, exaggerate danger, or claim exclusive authority.

A False Theory must arise from understandable evidence, bias, history, or misinformation. It cannot exist solely to make a player fail after reasonable investigation.

When a false procedure works sometimes, identify the hidden variable explaining the pattern. When it never could work, provide evidence that careful investigation can uncover before irreversible commitment where possible.

## Secret Conditions and Player Agency

Hidden conditions create uncertainty; they do not authorize loss of agency.

The GM should communicate:

- observable bodily and environmental signs;
- confidence and uncertainty in available explanations;
- known risks, missing information, and irreversible choices;
- what further inquiry could reasonably reveal;
- when a character's expertise supplies information beyond the player's wording.

The player need not solve a puzzle exactly as the GM imagined. A sound alternative investigation can reveal equivalent evidence. Refusing a mysterious route remains a valid choice.

An involuntary transition may still occur where canon establishes it, but it must follow prior causes, clues, and opportunities for meaningful response. Surprise alone is not fairness.

## Hidden Conditions and Soul Systems

Soul Resonance, Echoes, Titles, Retained Instincts, Avatars, and the Akashic Archive may reveal clues or provide route-specific evidence only through their own access rules.

They cannot:

- expose every hidden condition on demand;
- replace current Body Compatibility;
- make remembered anatomy current;
- convert a former route into a guaranteed option;
- turn a title name into a complete requirement;
- remove uncertainty that the Soul system itself preserves.

[Soul Interaction with Evolution](SOUL_INTERACTIONS.md) defines complete Soul interaction.

## Preventing Requirement Farming

A hidden condition should describe integrated reality, not a counter.

Use:

- sustained protection of a territory through changing threats;
- reliable regulation of an existing organ under real variation;
- a genuine symbiosis that survives conflict and renegotiation;
- adaptation to recurring scarcity while maintaining the population's future;
- relinquishing an incompatible structure during transition.

Avoid:

- kill a fixed number of targets;
- consume a list of unrelated materials;
- suffer the same injury repeatedly;
- wait an arbitrary number of days;
- repeat a trivial motion until a secret counter fills;
- collect titles or relationships without living their meaning;
- exploit reincarnation to replay one condition without current-life integration.

Qualitative requirements can still be strict. Their evidence comes from demonstrated relationship, capability, consequence, and change.

## GM Procedure

Before using a Hidden Evolution Condition:

1. write the route's complete world truth and Condition Provenance outside the narrative presentation;
2. identify which part is structural, lifecycle, environmental, developmental, behavioral, relational, sacrificial, historical, or transitional;
3. define why the condition matters to the resulting form;
4. place several proportionate Route Clues in anatomy, ecology, history, behavior, magic, or relationships;
5. identify who knows, suspects, misunderstands, or conceals each clue;
6. establish what observation or experiment can distinguish competing theories;
7. define failure evidence and avoid all-or-nothing opacity;
8. preserve agency, especially before irreversible commitment;
9. test the condition for farming, counters, Soul bypass, and retroactive invention;
10. record campaign-specific truth and discoveries only in the external Campaign Record.

## Worked Examples

### Hidden Environmental Dependency

A plated branch appears associated with a rare mineral. The actual condition is not consumption of the mineral but completing a moult in water where the mineral forms a stable lattice. Clues include malformed dry moults, mineral traces in old nesting pools, and healthy plates in related populations.

Randomly eating ore does not fill a hidden counter. Investigation can identify the real mechanism.

### Meaningful Protection Rather Than Kill Count

A guardian form requires an integrated territorial bond and demonstrated regulation of an existing warning organ under genuine threat. Killing intruders is neither necessary nor sufficient. Negotiation, evacuation, deterrence, and restraint can provide stronger Response Evidence than violence.

### A Cultural Procedure With an Incorrect Theory

A tradition teaches that a transition requires moonlight. In fact, the relevant condition is a predictable low-mana interval that often coincides with one lunar phase. Records from eclipses and sheltered chambers provide evidence that timing, not light, is causal.

### Soul Clue Without Soul Bypass

An Echo remembers entering a heat-resistant form near volcanic vents. The memory reveals a transition sensation and an overlooked breathing pattern. The current species has a related but not identical route. The Echo supplies a clue; current anatomy, pressure, practice, and transition conditions remain necessary.

### Failed Attempt That Teaches

A monster attempts a cocoon transition with sufficient material but in the wrong humidity. The cocoon begins forming, then becomes brittle before deep reorganisation. The failure causes recovery costs but also provides observable evidence. It does not secretly delete unrelated progress.

## Safeguards

- **No retroactive requirements:** world truth exists before outcome adjudication.
- **No arbitrary surprise:** important conditions have proportionate, discoverable clues.
- **No secret counters:** kills, pain, consumption, time, and repetition are not hidden currency.
- **No single-solution investigation:** sound alternative methods can reveal relevant evidence.
- **No expertise denial:** characters receive information justified by their established knowledge and Skills.
- **No consequence-free brute force:** random experimentation carries ordinary cost and cannot guarantee discovery.
- **No relationship objects:** bonds, symbionts, and communities retain agency and cannot be collected as keys.
- **No Soul omniscience:** persistent systems reveal only what their own access and uncertainty rules permit.
- **No irreversible gotcha:** known or discoverable stakes are communicated before commitment where the fiction allows.
- **No hidden superiority:** obscurity does not make a route stronger, rarer, or more desirable.

## Scope Boundaries

This document defines hidden route knowledge and fairness. It does not create specific secret forms, in-world prophecies, transition sites, institutions, or campaign mysteries.
