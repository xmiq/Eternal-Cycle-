# Monster Evolution

## Purpose

This document defines Monster Evolution as an emergent, lasting reorganisation of species form. It explains how valid routes arise, how transitions occur, what changes and persists, and how gradual change, convergence, divergence, specialisation, generalisation, regression, and evolutionary traps operate without universal levels or deterministic trees.

## Core Rule

**Evolution** is a lasting transition into a distinct species form or [Species Stage](SPECIES_STAGES.md) through a valid Evolution Route.

Evolution changes organised **Species Potential**: the current body's anatomy, material, lifecycle, Species Traits, Magical Organs, senses, movement, needs, dependencies, and possible later routes may be reconfigured. Evolution does not automatically grant the conditioning, Skills, knowledge, magical control, social authority, or practical reliability needed to use the resulting form well.

An Evolution is valid only when all of the following are established:

1. a current monster form with a defined origin and Species Potential;
2. a causally supported Evolution Route with an explicit source;
3. Body Compatibility or a transition capable of creating the required structures at real cost;
4. relevant pressure, Development, behaviour, relationship, resource, lifecycle, or other route evidence;
5. a valid transition opportunity, process, and timescale;
6. gains, losses, dependencies, vulnerabilities, and failure states;
7. consequences for identity, ecology, society, and later development;
8. clear ownership for every supporting effect.

No kill count, universal level, consumed mass, age, rarity, suffering total, Soul rank, or time served can replace this causal structure.

## Terms

### Evolution Route

A causally supported pathway by which a current monster form may undergo a lasting transition into a distinct form or Species Stage under defined sources, conditions, tradeoffs, and consequences.

### Evolution Transition

The bounded process that reorganises a current form into the resulting evolved form. It has a source, onset, duration, resource demands, vulnerabilities, possible interruption, and outcome.

### Evolved Form

The current species form after a completed Evolution Transition. It is a new embodiment with its own Species Potential, not a package of rewards added on top of the prior body.

### Route Readiness

A qualitative assessment that the current form, evidence, conditions, resources, and transition opportunity are sufficiently aligned for a specific Evolution Route to be attempted or occur. Route Readiness is route-specific and is not a meter.

### Form Stability

The degree to which an evolved form can maintain its organised Species Potential under its current health, resources, environment, regulation, and dependencies.

### Evolution Cost

A meaningful loss, demand, dependency, risk, incompatibility, opportunity cost, or consequence created by access to, transition through, or life within an Evolved Form.

### Convergent Evolution

Independent Evolution through which different origins develop similar functions in response to overlapping problems while retaining distinct anatomy, provenance, tradeoffs, and progression ownership.

### Divergent Evolution

Evolution through which related origins develop meaningfully different forms because their pressures, resources, behaviour, relationships, values, or route histories differ.

### Evolutionary Regression

A valid Evolution that reduces, simplifies, suppresses, or abandons costly structures or functions as part of a sustainable new form. It is not automatically failure or loss of Soul progression.

### Evolutionary Trap

A form, dependency, or route that was advantageous under earlier conditions but becomes harmful when its environment or relationships change faster than the form can respond.

## Ownership Boundaries

Monster Evolution owns routes, transitions, resulting monster forms, and the changes those forms make to Species Potential.

| Claim | Owning system |
| --- | --- |
| What the current anatomy, lifecycle, traits, and Species Potential can support | [Species Development](../progression/SPECIES_DEVELOPMENT.md) |
| Why a recurring problem or opportunity makes a route relevant | [Evolutionary Pressures](EVOLUTIONARY_PRESSURES.md) |
| Learned methods before or after transition | [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) |
| Conditioning, calibration, recovery, and bodily reliability | [Physical Development](../progression/PHYSICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md) |
| Magical Access, Channels, affinity, control, and learned use | [Magical Development](../progression/MAGICAL_DEVELOPMENT.md) and later Magic rules |

Evolution can open or close access for these systems. It cannot award their developed outcomes by relabelling them Species Traits.

## What Evolution Is Not

### Not Maturation

Maturation expresses the expected lifecycle of a current form. Growth in size, organ activation, ageing, or ordinary seasonal change remains maturation unless a species rule establishes a distinct Species Stage or Evolution Route.

### Not Metamorphosis by Default

Metamorphosis is a structured lifecycle reorganisation. It may also be an Evolution Transition when it leads to a distinct evolved form under the species' Evolution rules. A metamorphosis that every healthy member undergoes as ordinary maturation need not be Evolution.

### Not Physical Development

Conditioning, acclimation, recovery, regulation, and present bodily adaptation improve expression within current Species Potential. They can support Route Readiness but do not create a new form on their own.

### Not Skill Evolution

Skill Evolution reorganises a learned capability. Monster Evolution reorganises species form. A monster may evolve wings without knowing how to fly well, or evolve a new sensory organ without knowing how to interpret complex signals.

### Not Mutation by Default

A Mutation changes inherited or current structure through its own source. It may remain an individual alteration, become inherited through a valid origin route, contribute evidence, destabilise a form, or be integrated by a later Evolution. Mutation is not a synonym for Evolution.

### Not Transformation by Default

A temporary form, borrowed body, shapeshift, possession, equipment-dependent configuration, divine mantle, corruption, or artificial modification retains its source and duration. It becomes Evolution only if an explicit route performs a lasting reorganisation into an independently defined species form.

## Route Provenance

Every Evolution Route needs an origin that explains why the transition is possible. Valid provenance may include:

- an inherited species pathway;
- a lifecycle route capable of divergence;
- a stable population history under recurring pressure;
- a biological, magical, spiritual, elemental, artificial, symbiotic, or constructed transition system;
- integration of a compatible Mutation;
- a bounded hybrid origin;
- a social, ritual, or technical process that actually changes Species Potential;
- a Soul interaction explicitly permitted by [Soul Interaction with Evolution](SOUL_INTERACTIONS.md).

Provenance does not guarantee access. A species may possess a dormant route whose required habitat no longer exists. A ritual may be known but unavailable. A body may be too damaged, too immature, or incompatible. A route may close after another branch changes necessary structures.

The GM cannot invent provenance retroactively after a desired outcome is chosen. Unknown provenance may be hidden, but it must leave fair evidence and obey [Hidden Evolution Conditions](HIDDEN_EVOLUTION_CONDITIONS.md).

## Route Readiness

Assess Route Readiness qualitatively for one route and one current form.

### Current Form

Identify species, current form, Species Stage, lifecycle, health, alterations, prior Evolutions, and relevant structures. A route begins from the body that exists now, not an idealised species average.

### Causal Evidence

Identify meaningful current-life evidence relevant to the route:

- recurring Evolutionary Pressure and integrated response;
- developed use of current Species Traits;
- Skills or Physical Development that demonstrate safe control without being converted into anatomy;
- ecological role and sustainable access to necessary resources;
- social, ritual, magical, symbiotic, or technological relationships;
- valid Mutations or prior branch history;
- permitted Soul evidence.

Evidence is not a checklist currency. It explains why this transition fits this history.

### Compatibility

Determine whether the current body can support the transition, whether the transition itself can create missing structures, and what makes that reorganisation possible. A route requiring wings, a distributed mind, or a mana core must explain where the material, regulation, and integration come from.

### Opportunity

Identify when and where transition can occur. Opportunities may depend on:

- a lifecycle window;
- safe or dangerous environmental conditions;
- a nest, cocoon, forge, host, ritual, symbiont, structure, or magical field;
- resource accumulation or deliberate sacrifice;
- social assistance or isolation;
- deathlike dormancy without Final Death;
- a rare but causally established world event.

### Sustainability

An Evolved Form needs a viable way to persist after transition. A route that creates immense mana demand in a depleted habitat may be possible but unstable. Sustainability does not require comfort or safety; it requires explicit needs and consequences.

## The Evolution Transition

An Evolution Transition is an event or process, not a menu selection detached from the world.

Define:

1. **onset:** what initiates transition and whether it can be delayed, refused, interrupted, or misunderstood;
2. **reorganisation:** which structures dissolve, grow, migrate, fuse, divide, awaken, or change state;
3. **resources:** what matter, energy, mana, care, hosts, catalysts, or protected conditions are required;
4. **duration:** whether transition is sudden, staged, cyclic, dormant, or gradual;
5. **vulnerability:** what functions are impaired and what can go wrong;
6. **agency:** who can choose, consent, contest, assist, coerce, or interfere;
7. **failure:** what partial change, injury, instability, Mutation, route closure, or death may result;
8. **completion:** what evidence distinguishes an Evolved Form from an incomplete transition;
9. **aftermath:** which needs, losses, calibration, social responses, and ecological consequences begin immediately.

For non-sapient beings, agency may be instinctive rather than reflective. Natural onset still follows causality. Sapient beings should receive fair evidence about known stakes and retain meaningful choice where the route itself allows choice. Coercive evolution remains coercion and cannot be reframed as consent because the result is powerful.

## Gradual Evolution

Evolution may be gradual in three different senses:

### Gradual Preparation

Development, pressure, behaviour, symbiosis, and resource accumulation build Route Readiness over time. The body remains its current form until transition begins.

### Gradual Transition

The body's reorganisation unfolds over days, seasons, moults, cycles, or generations. The route must define intermediate vulnerability and when the new form becomes current.

### Gradual Population Change

Different members or descendants enter related routes over many renewal cycles. Population composition changes without requiring every individual to transform.

Incremental change does not erase system boundaries. A long acclimation remains Physical Development; inherited variation remains an origin mechanism; learned conduct remains Skill or culture. Call the result Evolution only when organised Species Potential has become a distinct form or Stage.

## Individual and Population Evolution

Eternal Cycle permits Evolution to operate at several scales where a species' origin supports them:

- **individual transition:** one current body evolves during its life;
- **renewal-linked transition:** offspring, spawned forms, constructed successors, or other valid renewals inherit a changed origin;
- **population divergence:** subpopulations stabilise different forms over time;
- **collective transition:** a genuinely collective species reorganises through its established shared-body rules.

Do not move consequences between scales without a rule. One individual's Evolution does not rewrite every member of the species. A population trend does not transform an isolated individual. A hive, colony, swarm, or network is one actor only if its species rules establish shared personhood and action ownership.

## Branching Without Determinism

[Branching Evolution](BRANCHING_EVOLUTION.md) represents several causally supported ways a form might change. A branch map records known possibilities; it is not a universal menu, destiny, or exhaustive metaphysical tree.

Route availability may differ because of:

- actual exposure and response history;
- current Development and Body Compatibility;
- resources, habitat, relationships, and institutions;
- prior branches, Mutations, injuries, or alterations;
- lifecycle timing and transition opportunities;
- individual values and choices;
- hidden but discoverable conditions;
- permitted Soul history.

Several routes can remain available. None may be available. A new route may be discovered. A known route may close. The GM must not change those facts solely to force a preferred story outcome.

## Convergent Evolution

Different species may solve similar problems through similar functions:

- armor through shell, scale, mineral growth, magical field, distributed sacrifice, or symbiotic cover;
- flight through wings, buoyancy, levitation organs, controlled webbing, or elemental propulsion;
- cold survival through insulation, dormancy, heat exchange, antifreeze chemistry, mana regulation, or collective shelter bodies.

Convergence does not make the forms identical. Each retains origin, structures, Skills, needs, vulnerabilities, and progression ownership. Similar appearance does not prove shared ancestry or route.

## Divergent Evolution

Related forms may diverge when different populations or individuals experience different pressures, make different choices, access different resources, or integrate different relationships.

Divergence can arise even in the same habitat when social role, life stage, exposure, or values differ. Conversely, physical separation alone does not guarantee divergent forms if conditions and routes remain similar.

Divergence may create compatible species, reproductively or metaphysically isolated species, castes, seasonal forms, or merely separate branches within one form family. The route must define the resulting relationship rather than assume every branch creates a new species.

## Specialisation and Generalisation

### Specialisation

An evolved form concentrates Species Potential around a narrower set of conditions, resources, functions, or relationships.

Specialisation may improve efficiency, precision, scale, reliability, or niche fit. It also creates dependence, reduced flexibility, inaccessible prior routes, or greater vulnerability to Pressure Release and environmental change.

### Generalisation

An evolved form broadens tolerances, diets, movement options, sensory interpretation, or viable habitats without becoming universally competent.

Generalisation trades peak efficiency, scale, or refinement for flexibility. It does not grant every specialised trait at reduced strength, and it does not bypass Skill practice.

Neither is inherently superior. Context determines effectiveness.

## Evolutionary Regression and Simplification

Evolution can validly reduce or abandon:

- an expensive organ;
- a dangerous magical affinity;
- extreme size;
- a vulnerable lifecycle stage;
- a narrow diet;
- an unused sense;
- a structure maintained only by a vanished symbiont or habitat.

Regression is descriptive, not a moral judgment or universal descent. A smaller, simpler, or less spectacular form may be more sustainable and effective. Lost anatomy makes body-dependent Skills latent or inexpressible but does not delete legitimate Skill Imprints or Soul history.

Restoring a former structure later requires a valid route; it is not free because the form once possessed it.

## Evolutionary Traps

An Evolutionary Trap occurs when a once-useful form or dependency becomes harmful after conditions shift.

Examples include:

- specialisation around a resource that disappears;
- attraction to a magical signal that becomes artificial bait;
- armor that protects against predators but prevents migration through a new barrier;
- synchronised reproduction disrupted by climate or World Reset;
- dependence on an institution, host, or symbiont that collapses;
- an apex form whose energy demands exceed reduced Carrying Capacity.

A trap does not guarantee extinction or immediate corrective Evolution. Escape may require behavior, Skills, social action, migration, technology, magic, another route, simplification, or outside help. Evolution does not exist to rescue every form from consequences.

## Route Closure, Reopening, and Further Evolution

A route may close when:

- required structures are lost or reorganised;
- a lifecycle window passes;
- a necessary source, habitat, relationship, or institution disappears;
- an incompatible branch is completed;
- health or instability makes transition unsurvivable;
- the route's meaning or permitted Soul alignment is rejected where that route explicitly depends on it.

Closure is route-specific. It does not imply that the being can never evolve again.

A route may reopen only when its actual cause is restored. Memory, regret, a diagram, or prior access does not recreate anatomy or ecology by itself. Further Evolution begins from the current Evolved Form and includes all accumulated gains, losses, and dependencies.

There is no final universal form. Even a highly developed or [Apex Form](APEX_MONSTERS.md) remains contextual, embodied, and subject to change.

## What Changes at Completion

For each Evolved Form, record:

- current species identity and form family;
- anatomy, material, scale, senses, movement, organs, Channels, and lifecycle;
- Species Traits gained, changed, suppressed, or lost;
- current instincts and bodily orientation;
- new Skill access routes and old Skills made latent by embodiment;
- current Physical and Magical calibration needs;
- resource, habitat, social, symbiotic, and maintenance dependencies;
- vulnerabilities, incompatibilities, and reduced options;
- ecological niche and Carrying Capacity consequences;
- social, cultural, institutional, religious, and political recognition;
- later routes and routes now closed;
- Form Stability and known failure behavior.

Do not preserve every prior form as a stack of passive bonuses. A new form replaces the old current embodiment. Prior Skills, Development, knowledge, and Soul Imprints persist or become latent according to their own systems.

## Post-Transition Development

Evolution creates access, not mastery.

After transition, a being may need to:

- learn balance, movement, feeding, rest, communication, and sensory interpretation;
- condition new structures and recover from transition;
- recalibrate old Skills to changed reach, mass, timing, senses, or action economy;
- develop control of Magical Organs or Channels;
- understand new instincts without surrendering agency;
- renegotiate relationships, law, identity, role, and social recognition;
- secure resources and habitat appropriate to new needs.

Retained Development may accelerate valid relearning where compatibility exists. It cannot make an unpractised new body fully reliable at onset.

## Evolution and Death

Final Death ends the current Evolved Form. Reincarnation does not preserve its anatomy, Species Traits, current Stage, Mutation, branch access, or route completion.

Legitimate Skill XP, Stat XP, Retained Instincts, memory, Soul Titles, Echoes, Resonance, Depth, and other protected Soul Imprints persist only through their own rules. Prior Evolution may become history or permitted evidence for a future compatible route; it is not a portable body blueprint.

An Evolution Transition that resembles death must distinguish suspended life, body dissolution, Soul separation, and Final Death. A route cannot evade Reincarnation rules by calling Final Death a moult.

## GM Procedure

When adjudicating a proposed or emerging Monster Evolution:

1. name the current form, Stage, origin, lifecycle, health, alterations, and Species Potential;
2. identify the proposed Evolution Route and its causal provenance;
3. verify actual pressures, Response Evidence, Development, relationships, resources, and route-specific conditions;
4. separate Species Traits, Skills, Physical Development, magic, Soul effects, equipment, and institutions by owner;
5. assess Body Compatibility and explain how missing structures would be created;
6. establish the transition opportunity, onset, duration, resources, vulnerability, agency, and failure states;
7. define the Evolved Form's gains, losses, needs, dependencies, incompatibilities, and closed routes;
8. test ecological sustainability and world consequences;
9. require post-transition calibration and current-life Development;
10. check hidden conditions for provenance, fair clues, and non-farmable meaning;
11. reject universal thresholds, deterministic branches, stacked bodies, duplicated Skills, and Soul bypasses;
12. record world-specific facts only in the external Campaign Record.

## Worked Examples

### Gradual Preparation, Discrete Transition

A tunnel-dwelling monster spends years under falling-rock pressure. It develops route memory, learns bracing behavior, conditions its limbs, and relies on communal supports. Those are Skills, Physical Development, and social responses.

A valid inherited route later permits a protected moult that reorganises its shell, stance, and vibration organs. The transition is Evolution because organised Species Potential changes. The years of preparation explain Route Readiness but do not become evolution points.

### Convergent Defense

One desert species develops reflective plates through mineral integration. An unrelated magical species develops a heat-shedding aura organ. Both reduce thermal load, but their anatomy, resources, vulnerabilities, magical ownership, and later routes remain different.

### Divergence Through Social Choice

Two populations share a coastal origin. One maintains migratory traditions and develops a generalist amphibious route. The other builds permanent reef institutions and stabilises a specialised sessile guardian form supported by collective care.

Neither branch is more evolved. One gains flexibility; the other gains local efficiency and social dependence.

### Useful Regression

A former aerial hunter lives for many renewal cycles in sealed caverns. A valid route reduces wings and hollow structures while strengthening compact movement and low-frequency sensing. Flight Skills become latent, but the new form spends less energy and survives narrow passages.

### Evolutionary Trap

A mana-feeding form specialises around a stable magical spring. Its new organ is efficient but cannot process ordinary food. When the spring is diverted, the form faces migration, negotiated access, artificial support, another compatible route, or collapse. Evolution does not produce a rescue branch on demand.

### Failed Transition

A monster attempts a route during poor health without enough transition material. Reorganisation begins but cannot stabilise. The result is injury and a source-owned Mutation rather than a partial collection of the route's best traits. Recovery and future access depend on the actual damage and route rules.

## Safeguards

- **No universal level:** overall character level never grants Evolution.
- **No kill or consumption threshold:** violence and feeding matter only through their real ecology, pressure, Development, and consequences.
- **No deterministic tree:** maps describe supported possibilities; they do not compel or exhaust future forms.
- **No free anatomy:** every new structure needs provenance, material, regulation, integration, and cost.
- **No access-as-mastery:** an Evolved Form still requires calibration, practice, and current-life Development.
- **No stacked bodies:** the new form replaces current embodiment rather than adding every prior trait permanently.
- **No costless upgrade:** every route states losses, needs, vulnerabilities, dependencies, or meaningful opportunity costs.
- **No forced superiority:** specialisation, generalisation, regression, stasis, and non-evolutionary solutions can all be valid.
- **No automatic inheritance:** an individual's route does not rewrite offspring or population origins without an explicit mechanism.
- **No retroactive condition:** hidden routes require prior provenance and fair evidence.
- **No Soul bypass:** persistent soul history cannot manufacture Body Compatibility, Species Potential, or transition resources.
- **No guaranteed rescue:** pressure, danger, and impending extinction do not force a useful route to appear.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not define:

- a catalog of species or fixed Evolution trees;
- universal stages, grades, rarity tiers, or power rankings;
- exact population inheritance or genetics;
- complete Mutation, Hybridization, adaptation, society, extinction, or Soul-interaction rules;
- numerical transition formulas;
- live forms, route readiness, populations, or campaign outcomes.
