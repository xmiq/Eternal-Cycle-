# Soul Interaction with Evolution

## Purpose

This document defines how established Soul systems can influence Monster Evolution without transferring ownership of bodies, routes, Skills, or Development to the Soul Engine. It integrates Soul Resonance, Soul Echoes, Soul Depth, Reincarnation, Retained Instincts, Soul Titles, Soul Avatars, and related Soul systems with Evolution while preserving current embodiment and current-life consequence.

## Core Rule

**Monster Evolution owns every lasting transition from one current monster form into another.** A Soul system may contribute only the specific history, identity, access, orientation, information, capacity, relationship, or support that its own rules permit.

A Soul fact never supplies missing anatomy, Species Potential, route provenance, current Response Evidence, transition resources, Skill mastery, or a sustainable Evolved Form by implication.

When a Soul system matters to Evolution, identify its exact role:

1. **Pressure:** an established Soul phenomenon creates a meaningful current condition;
2. **Discovery:** a Soul source reveals or contextualizes Route Clues;
3. **Condition:** a valid route explicitly recognizes one bounded Soul fact;
4. **Compatibility:** a Soul fact affects whether the soul can inhabit or integrate the proposed form;
5. **Transition support:** a Soul system assists awareness, coherence, consent, or stability during a valid transition;
6. **Interpretation:** a Soul source helps choose, understand, or live with a resulting form.

Naming one role does not grant the others. The Evolution Route must independently establish current form compatibility, provenance, pressure or other causal reason, conditions, costs, transition opportunity, gains, losses, dependencies, and post-transition ecology.

## Terms

### Soul-Conditioned Evolution Route

An otherwise valid Evolution Route that explicitly recognizes one or more established Soul-system facts as bounded pressure, evidence, condition, compatibility, transition support, or interpretation.

### Evolution-Relevant Soul Evidence

A source-bounded Soul fact whose owning rule permits it to inform one named Evolution claim. It is not currency, current Response Evidence by default, or proof that a route exists or must complete.

## Authority and Ownership

Soul-Evolution interactions use the ownership rules in [Soul System Interactions](../soul/SOUL_SYSTEM_INTERACTIONS.md).

| Claim | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Whether a Soul Imprint, Link, Echo, instinct, Title, Avatar, Attunement, or other Soul fact exists and is accessible | The relevant [Soul Engine](../soul/README.md) document |
| Whether an Evolution Route exists, what it requires, and which form results | [Monster Evolution](MONSTER_EVOLUTION.md) and its narrower route documents |
| Current anatomy, lifecycle, Species Potential, and body compatibility | [Species Development](../progression/SPECIES_DEVELOPMENT.md) and the current species rules |
| Whether a condition qualifies as Evolutionary Pressure and what current Response Evidence exists | [Evolutionary Pressures](EVOLUTIONARY_PRESSURES.md) |
| Learned methods, retained Skill Imprints, expression, and crossover | [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) |
| Current capability, practice, reliability, and Development | [Development System](../progression/README.md) |
| Current populations, institutions, resources, disasters, and available origins | [World Engine](../world-engine/WORLD_ENGINE_OVERVIEW.md) and the external Campaign Record |

The Soul Engine answers whether a Soul fact is real and accessible. Monster Evolution answers whether that fact is relevant to one route and what bodily transition can follow. Neither silently overrides the other.

## The Interaction Test

For every proposed Soul-Evolution interaction, answer these questions in order.

### 1. What Is the Exact Claim?

State the requested result. “My old dragon life helps” is incomplete. Useful claims include:

- an Echo recalls a Route Clue;
- a Resonance Link makes a known route perceptible;
- a Soul Title satisfies one declared symbolic condition;
- Soul Depth provides capacity to remain coherent during a radical transition;
- a Retained Instinct helps interpret the sensory demands of a possible form;
- Avatar Expression compares several already accessible routes.

### 2. Which Soul System Owns the Evidence?

Identify the Soul fact, its provenance, present access, and limits. A memory is not automatically an Echo, an intense feeling is not automatically Resonance, and a prestigious name is not automatically a Soul Title.

### 3. Which Evolution Rule Owns the Result?

Name the specific Evolution Route, hidden condition, Species Stage, branch, Mutation integration, Hybrid Form, or other current bodily claim. If no Evolution owner exists, Soul history cannot create one merely because the interaction is dramatic.

### 4. Was the Soul Role Declared Before Resolution?

A Soul-Conditioned Evolution Route must state what kind of Soul fact it recognizes and why that fact matters to the resulting form. The GM cannot add “requires a deep soul” after a choice has been made or treat any convenient Title as interchangeable with the intended history.

### 5. Does the Current Body Still Qualify?

Test current Species Potential, anatomy, lifecycle, health, materials, Magical Organs, action economy, and route-specific compatibility. Former bodies cannot answer these requirements.

### 6. Is Current-Life Causality Present?

Establish current pressure, behavior, practice, relationships, opportunity, or other route conditions. Prior lives may orient a response; they do not automatically become the present body's Response Evidence.

### 7. Are Source, Cost, and Transition Intact?

Soul support cannot replace a cocoon, ritual, habitat, symbiont, magical source, construction process, vulnerable interval, resource expenditure, consent requirement, or other established transition element.

### 8. What Changes Afterward?

Define the Evolved Form, losses, dependencies, current instincts, Skill access, Development needs, Soul access changes, ecology, and social consequences. Reassess rather than assuming that every prior Soul fact becomes easier to express.

## Permitted Roles of Soul Systems

### Soul Phenomena as Pressure

A Soul phenomenon may become a Pressure Source when it produces a sustained or meaningfully recurring current condition with real stakes and unequal consequences.

Examples include:

- an established Resonance field disrupting incompatible forms;
- a region saturated with Archive Traces that overloads certain cognition;
- recurring soul migration changing hosts or lifecycle opportunities;
- a divine soul-work process altering current renewal;
- Dissonance between a current form and a genuinely active Soul bond;
- a World Reset changing how souls can inhabit local bodies.

The phenomenon remains owned by its Soul or world source. Evolutionary Pressure owns only its current consequences for possible forms.

Possessing Soul Depth, many Echoes, a Title, or a former monster life is not pressure by itself. Deliberately provoking Soul harm, Dissonance, overload, Final Death, or conflict does not create a useful route or reward.

### Route Discovery

Soul systems can reveal Route Clues when their provenance supports the information:

- Resonance can make a genuinely linked form, place, symbol, or history feel relevant;
- an Echo can remember what its incarnation observed or believed;
- a Retained Instinct can direct attention toward a familiar pattern;
- a Soul Title can prompt recognition by a valid observer or institution;
- Avatar Expression can compare bounded perspectives;
- an Archive Record can preserve evidence under its own access rules;
- Soul Space may represent known possibilities through symbolic interfaces.

Discovery does not establish truth beyond the source. Echoes can be mistaken, instincts are not explanations, Titles do not reveal full requirements, and Archive Records remain provenance-bound. Hidden Evolution Conditions still require prior truth, fair Route Clues, and meaningful inquiry.

### Route Conditions

A route may use a Soul fact as one condition when the connection is specific and causal.

Valid patterns include:

- a form whose metaphysical structure requires enough Soul Depth to remain coherent;
- a species-bound rite that recognizes a relevant Soul Title before permitting access to a transition source;
- a route requiring Harmonic Resonance with an established species pattern or location;
- a transformation that can integrate one named Retained Instinct because the resulting senses and lifecycle express that pattern;
- a form that requires reconciliation among specific Echo histories because its identity architecture would otherwise fail.

Invalid patterns include:

- “high Soul power” as a universal key;
- any Soul Title substituting for any rare route;
- counting Echoes, lives, deaths, instincts, or species collected;
- using the desired route to create the Resonance, Title, or history that qualifies for it;
- treating a Soul Avatar as permission to select any associated species.

A valid Soul condition may be necessary without being sufficient. All other route requirements remain.

### Compatibility and Transition Support

Soul systems may affect whether a soul can remain coherent, recognize consent, integrate identity change, or use an established transition safely.

They may support:

- maintaining continuity through radical sensory or cognitive change;
- recognizing a familiar form pattern without reproducing it;
- reconciling conflicting prior identities before a route that would amplify them;
- communicating with an explicit Soul-bearing transition source;
- staying oriented during a cocoon, metamorphosis, ritual, construction, or magical reorganisation;
- interpreting a new body's instincts after transition.

Support is not material. Soul Depth does not become flesh, Resonance does not become a Magical Organ, an Echo does not perform a second transition task, and an Avatar does not create extra actions.

### Choice and Interpretation

Soul history may make different branches meaningful. It can help the current incarnation understand likely identity pressure, recognize old mistakes, anticipate sensory change, or choose a route consistent with present values.

The current incarnation chooses deliberate action. An instinct, Echo, Title, Constellation, Avatar Association, prophecy, god, institution, or internal majority cannot force Evolution under ordinary rules. Involuntary transformation requires its own source, resistance, warning, and consequence rules.

## Soul Resonance

[Soul Resonance](../soul/SOUL_RESONANCE.md) can reveal, align, translate, or activate existing potential through a genuine Resonance Link. In Evolution it may:

- reveal a linked Route Clue;
- support access to a route already connected to the soul's lived history;
- establish one explicit compatibility factor;
- stabilize communication or identity alignment during a valid transition;
- help translate prior experience into understanding of the resulting form.

Every use still needs a Resonance Target, Basis, Trigger, compatibility, capacity, and access. Similarity to a former species does not establish a Link. Resonance with dragons does not grant dragon anatomy, and Resonance with a desired route cannot prove that the route exists.

Dissonance can complicate or prevent an attempted transition when the route genuinely depends on alignment. Deliberately causing Dissonance does not guarantee Mutation, Evolution, fusion, or Soul Depth.

## Soul Echoes

[Soul Echoes](../soul/SOUL_ECHOES.md) preserve bounded former perspectives. In Evolution an Echo may:

- remember an observed form, ritual, habitat, failure, or clue;
- explain how a former body perceived a pressure;
- advise about tradeoffs the source incarnation experienced;
- provide perspective during branch choice;
- cooperate through Overlay while the current incarnation performs accessible transition tasks;
- help interpret the Evolved Form afterward.

An Echo's confidence is not objective truth. It does not retain former anatomy as current capability, unlock its former species, vote a route into existence, supply an extra action, or take control without the established Delegation rules.

An Echo from a former apex or Evolved Form does not make that form the soul's default. It preserves experience; the current body must qualify independently.

## Soul Depth

[Soul Depth](../soul/SOUL_DEPTH.md) is capacity to integrate complexity and transformation without losing continuity. It may be relevant when a route would:

- radically change cognition, senses, identity, or metaphysical structure;
- activate several legitimately retained but conflicting histories;
- bind the body to a large-scale magical or Soul pattern;
- require coherent interaction with ancient, hybrid, or world-deep structures;
- expose the soul to transition strain beyond ordinary embodiment.

Soul Depth may be one compatibility or capacity condition. It does not create the route, determine its morality, pay its costs, guarantee survival, grant its anatomy, or replace current-life integration.

Completing a dramatic Evolution does not automatically deepen the soul. The experience may later contribute to a Deepening Event only when the soul genuinely integrates meaningful complexity under Soul Depth's own rules.

## Reincarnation

[Reincarnation](../soul/REINCARNATION.md) and Monster Evolution are distinct transitions.

- Reincarnation begins after Final Death and places the soul into one valid new embodiment.
- Evolution changes a living current monster form through one valid Evolution Route.
- Final Death ends current Species Potential, Evolved Form, Species Stage, body-bound Mutation, and current ecology.
- The soul carries only those records its persistence rules protect.

A Reincarnation candidate may belong to a species capable of later Evolution. It may correspond to an unusual or advanced form only when a plausible origin, world state, Soul compatibility, earned access, causal placement, and appropriate starting history make that body valid. Reincarnation does not count as completing the body's in-life Evolution Transition and cannot manufacture an adult history or free Development.

Prior Evolved Forms may influence candidates or later routes only through explicit Soul systems. They cannot be reselected as saved loadouts. An extinct form is unavailable as an ordinary candidate while no viable embodiment route exists.

## Retained Instincts

[Retained Instincts](../soul/RETAINED_INSTINCTS.md) preserve integrated patterns of attention, urgency, valuation, and response from prior embodiment. They are especially relevant to monster Evolution because they can bridge how radically different bodies encounter the world without carrying the former body itself.

### What an Instinct May Contribute

A Retained Instinct may:

- direct attention toward a relevant environmental, bodily, social, or magical pattern;
- make a Route Clue noticeable through a compatible current Instinct Cue;
- help the current incarnation compare the demands of a possible form with lived prior experience;
- guide present behavior that may, through meaningful current use, become part of Response Evidence;
- serve as one explicit condition for a route designed to embody that pattern;
- assist calibration after an Evolution Transition when the new body supplies compatible senses or structures.

### What It Cannot Contribute

A Retained Instinct does not provide:

- the former body's anatomy, Species Traits, Species Stage, Mutation, statistics, or progression tree;
- a complete Skill, factual explanation, or perfect hazard detector;
- current Response Evidence merely because the Origin life once faced similar pressure;
- automatic compatibility with a route sharing the instinct's theme;
- compulsory behavior or authority over the player's choice;
- an Evolution unlock, transition, or Evolved Form by itself.

### Current-Life Integration

Prior instinct can orient current action, but Response Evidence records what the current being actually encounters, attempts, pays, and integrates. A former spider instinct may draw attention to vibration and spacing. Only present use through current senses, tools, relationships, and consequences can become current evidence.

A route explicitly built around trans-incarnational identity may recognize the Instinct Origin as Evolution-Relevant Soul Evidence. It must still explain why that history matters to this body and how the current transition can express it.

### After Evolution

Reassess each relevant instinct after transition:

- a new body may provide a closer Instinct Cue and make translation easier;
- changed senses may distort or suppress an old pattern;
- new Current Instincts may cooperate or conflict with retained ones;
- a Naturalized instinct may become Cued or Quiescent when expression changes;
- improved anatomical fit does not turn the Retained Instinct into a Species Trait or Skill;
- the Instinct Origin remains the former life even when the current body develops its own similar instinct.

Evolution neither consumes nor upgrades a Retained Instinct automatically. Its Access follows the current body, Resonance, practice, context, and Soul condition.

## Soul Titles

[Soul Titles](../soul/SOUL_TITLES.md) may influence Evolution through contextual recognition, affinity, symbolic authority, opportunity, and identity-bearing conditions.

A relevant title may:

- cause a species, spirit, institution, place, or transition system to recognize the bearer;
- provide access to a trial, teacher, habitat, rite, or source that still has its own conditions;
- serve as one explicit requirement for a route whose meaning genuinely matches the Title Basis;
- create Narrative Pressure around accepting, rejecting, or redefining a possible form;
- help interpret how a branch would express a continuing identity.

The title does not provide flat statistics, command a species, grant anatomy, or force a thematic form. **Monster King** does not automatically unlock a sovereign branch. **Friend of Dragons** does not turn every body into a dragon candidate.

Soul Title evolution is a change in persistent identity meaning under the Title rules. It is not a bodily Monster Evolution unless a separate valid route also occurs.

## Soul Avatars

[Soul Avatars](../soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md) synthesize relevant histories under the current incarnation's leadership. Avatar Expression may:

- compare how several lives responded to related pressures;
- expose disagreement about branch costs or identity consequences;
- help interpret a transition that crosses former species assumptions;
- coordinate bounded Echo, Resonance, Title, Archive, and Weapon Soul contributions;
- preserve provenance and current agency during a complex valid transition.

Soul Avatar status is not an Evolution rank, species category, rare-form entitlement, or combat transformation. An Avatar Association can make one route symbolically relevant without granting it. Expression remains subject to current anatomy, Skills, Development, resources, time, action economy, and Evolution conditions.

The current Avatar Lead makes the choice. Former incarnations cannot vote the body into a branch, and Avatar Expression cannot bypass an involuntary-transformation resistance rule.

## Other Soul Systems

### Soul Space

[Soul Space](../soul/SOUL_SPACE.md) may represent routes, conditions, conflicts, or transition preparation through symbolic Regions and Constructs. An evolution altar, tree, cocoon, mirror, or map inside Soul Space is an interface. It cannot create a route, complete a transition, provide stopped-time training, or make imagined anatomy real.

### Soul Constellations

[Soul Constellations](../soul/SOUL_CONSTELLATIONS.md) may supply recurring relationship history, voluntary cooperation, social access, or a specific condition where a route genuinely concerns those relationships. They do not pool Species Potential, share Evolution, inherit one member's route, or bind another soul's consent.

### Akashic Archive

The [Akashic Archive](../soul/AKASHIC_ARCHIVE.md) may preserve route history, failures, form descriptions, or evidence. Reading supplies bounded information, not anatomy, Development, Skill mastery, objective certainty, access, or Evolution. A Record of an extinct route does not recreate its missing source.

### Soul Weapons

A Weapon Soul remains a distinct person. It may provide testimony, consent, Resonance, protection, or a route-specific relationship when a valid transition recognizes the bond. A Soul Weapon does not fuse with the body, donate weapon evolution, or become a monster organ unless a later explicit rule defines that exceptional interaction.

## Individual and Population Evolution

Soul-conditioned routes most often apply to individuals because Soul facts belong to particular continuing identities.

One individual's Evolved Form does not redefine an entire species. For the form to become a stable lineage or population route, establish:

- a repeatable origin or renewal process;
- whether descendants require their own Soul condition;
- which bodily traits are inherited independently of the initiating soul;
- whether the route remains available to unrelated souls;
- ecology, variation, lifecycle, and social transmission;
- how the original Soul fact remains historically relevant without being copied.

A Soul Title, Echo, Resonance Profile, Soul Depth, Avatar Association, or Retained Instinct is not inherited with anatomy. Descendants may inherit a body while lacking the founder's Soul history, or they may need another valid route entirely.

## After an Evolution Transition

When a Soul-conditioned transition completes:

1. the new Evolved Form becomes the only current body;
2. former anatomy and the prior form do not stack beneath it;
3. current Species Potential, needs, lifecycle, instincts, and progression access are updated;
4. Skills and Development are re-evaluated for bodily compatibility and practised reliability;
5. each Soul system retains its own provenance and access rules;
6. Resonance Links may shift state without being replaced;
7. Echoes remain former perspectives rather than becoming current bodies;
8. Retained Instinct Access may improve, distort, conflict, or become Quiescent;
9. Titles and Avatar Associations may gain new interpretation without being automatically transformed;
10. costs, vulnerabilities, ecology, relationships, and world reactions take effect.

Evolution does not duplicate a Soul benefit into both body and soul records. If the new form now possesses a vibration-sensing Species Trait, a former spider Retained Instinct may help interpret it, but the anatomy and instinct remain separate owners.

## GM Procedure

When a Soul system is claimed to affect Evolution:

1. state the exact Evolution result being requested;
2. identify the Soul fact, its owning document, provenance, current access, and limits;
3. assign pressure, discovery, condition, compatibility, transition support, or interpretation as its exact role;
4. identify the independent Evolution Route and its Condition Provenance;
5. reject circular qualification and retroactive Soul requirements;
6. test current Species Potential, embodiment, pressure, Response Evidence, Development, and Skills;
7. preserve the route's source, materials, opportunity, costs, risks, losses, and ecology;
8. preserve current-incarnation choice and every distinct soul's consent;
9. resolve information according to Echo, instinct, Resonance, Title, Avatar, Archive, and observer limits;
10. complete or fail the transition under Monster Evolution rules;
11. re-evaluate bodily and Soul access in the Evolved Form without duplication;
12. record current Soul and world state only in the external Campaign Record.

## Worked Examples

### The Former Web Hunter

A shelled climbing monster carries a Retained Instinct from a spider life. The instinct draws attention to minute surface vibrations and helps it experiment with current shell contact. This current behavior may become Response Evidence for a route emphasizing distributed vibration sensing. It does not provide spinnerets, web Skills, or the route itself.

### Resonance With a Dragon Pattern

A former dragon soul has a genuine Resonance Link to an ancient nesting ground. A hidden route for the current scaled body recognizes that pattern and becomes perceptible there. The body still needs compatible heat regulation, current fire exposure, transition material, and a sustainable post-transition habitat. Resonance reveals and aligns; it does not make the body a dragon.

### Friend of Dragons

The bearer of **Friend of Dragons** is admitted to a guarded rite because the resident dragons recognize the title. The rite provides a real transition source for one compatible route. The title grants the opportunity and symbolic context, not success, obedience, anatomy, or a universal dragon branch.

### Depth Without a Route

A World-Deep soul could remain coherent through a radical many-minded metamorphosis, but its current species lacks the required distributed organs and no valid Hybrid Origin or transition source exists. Soul Depth establishes capacity and nothing more. The route remains unavailable.

### The Apex Echo

An Echo remembers ruling a former habitat in an Apex Form and can explain that form's dependencies. The current juvenile body lacks the stage, ecology, and route conditions. The Echo offers useful warning but cannot restore the form or turn remembered authority into current progression.

### Avatar Expression at a Branch

A Soul Avatar enters Expression before choosing between a solitary sensory branch and a cooperative sovereign branch. Relevant Echoes expose prior successes and harms, a Title creates social expectations, and Retained Instincts reveal bodily unease. The current incarnation weighs the information and chooses. Expression adds no third branch and cannot pay either transition's costs.

### Archive of an Extinct Form

An Archive Record describes a lost species' Evolution Route in detail. The current reader can identify why the route failed and perhaps adapt principles through future research. Because the required symbiont is globally extinct, the Record cannot complete or recreate the route.

### Soul Phenomenon as Population Pressure

A stable Resonance field disrupts the renewal of bodies whose Soul Anchors cannot align with it. This is a current Pressure Source affecting a population. Some migrate, some use social or magical buffers, some decline, and a compatible route may become relevant. No individual receives Evolution merely for possessing a soul.

## Safeguards

- **One owner per effect:** Soul systems own their facts; Monster Evolution owns the bodily route and result.
- **No anatomy from history:** prior bodies, Echo memories, instincts, Titles, Avatar Associations, and Archive Records do not become current structures.
- **No route invention:** a Soul fact cannot create its own Evolution Route, provenance, or hidden condition.
- **No circular condition:** the desired form cannot produce the Soul fact used to qualify for that same transition.
- **No current-life bypass:** prior Response Evidence does not replace present embodiment, pressure, action, practice, and consequence unless a route explicitly and narrowly spans lives.
- **No Soul currency:** Depth, Resonance, Echoes, instincts, Titles, lives, deaths, and Associations are not points spent on forms.
- **No collection reward:** sampling many species or shallow lives grants no universal branch access or hybrid form.
- **No forced theme:** Soul history may create pressure and opportunities but cannot choose the player's body or morality.
- **No doubled benefit:** the same history cannot become duplicate Species Traits, Skills, instincts, actions, or resistances.
- **No transition erasure:** Soul support does not replace source, materials, time, vulnerability, failure, loss, or ecology.
- **No automatic inheritance:** an individual's Soul-conditioned result does not transfer Soul history to descendants.
- **No harm farming:** Dissonance, Soul Wounds, overload, sacrifice, and dangerous transitions do not guarantee Evolution or Deepening.
- **No extinction bypass:** souls and records do not make extinct bodies available without a valid current origin.
- **No universal Soul branch:** no single Soul state, Title, Avatar, or depth of history unlocks every rare or apex form.

## Scope Boundaries

This document does not create new Soul Imprints, Resonance states, Echo Presence levels, Depth Horizons, Soul Titles, Avatar Associations, Reincarnation modes, species, branches, transformations, or live character records. It defines only how existing Soul facts may support Monster Evolution claims.
