# Monster Evolution Rules

Monster Evolution describes how monster forms, populations, and societies respond to ecology, embodiment, meaningful pressure, and world change. Evolution is an emergent bodily transition, not a universal level system, kill-count reward, rarity ladder, mandatory tree, or substitute for Skills, Development, or Soul progression.

Repository-wide ownership, dependency, extension, and consumer metadata is maintained in the [Canonical Document Registry](../DOCUMENT_REGISTRY.md#monster-evolution).

## Evolution Claim Resolution

Use this sequence when a proposed change might be Evolution. Open only the linked specialist rules relevant to the claim; the sequence is an ownership map, not a requirement to apply every subsystem every time.

1. **State the claim.** Name the proposed current-body change, its subject, its scope, and whether it concerns an individual, lineage, population, or society.
2. **Establish causal context.** Use [Monster Ecology](MONSTER_ECOLOGY.md) and [Evolutionary Pressures](EVOLUTIONARY_PRESSURES.md) to identify the environment, resources, relationships, exposure, stakes, buffers, and Response Evidence that already exist.
3. **Classify the response.** Use [Monster Adaptation](MONSTER_ADAPTATION.md) to decide whether the change is better owned by current condition, behavior, Physical Development, a Skill, a Species Trait, a Mutation, social practice, technology, or ecology. Stop the Evolution procedure when another system fully owns the truth.
4. **Establish route provenance.** Under [Monster Evolution](MONSTER_EVOLUTION.md), identify why this form is possible for this lineage or body. Pressure makes a route relevant but does not create one by itself.
5. **Apply the relevant route rules.** Consult [Species Stages](SPECIES_STAGES.md), [Branching Evolution](BRANCHING_EVOLUTION.md), [Hidden Evolution Conditions](HIDDEN_EVOLUTION_CONDITIONS.md), [Mutations](MUTATIONS.md), [Hybridization](HYBRIDIZATION.md), or [Apex Monsters](APEX_MONSTERS.md) only when the claim invokes that structure.
6. **Check present readiness.** Confirm current embodiment, current-life causal evidence, compatibility, opportunity, sustainability, agency, and any established conditions. Prior lives may support interpretation or reacquisition but do not replace this check.
7. **Resolve the transition.** Apply the route's process, costs, vulnerability, failure possibilities, and irreversible or difficult commitments. A valid attempt does not guarantee the desired form.
8. **Define the resulting form.** Record new bodily access, altered needs, lost access, dependencies, and immediate instability. New anatomy grants access to expression and training, not automatic Skill mastery or Development.
9. **Propagate consequences.** Apply effects on ecology, society, reproduction, lineage viability, Counter-Ecology, migration, and extinction risk at the scale the world supports. One individual transition does not prove population success.
10. **Run the safeguard audit.** Use [Monster Evolution Safeguards](EVOLUTION_SAFEGUARDS.md) to verify ownership, provenance, form distinction, embodiment, cost, agency, ecological consequence, and exploit resistance.

## Ownership Map

| Claim | Primary owner | Evolution boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Habitat, niche, resources, population relationships, and carrying capacity | [Monster Ecology](MONSTER_ECOLOGY.md), with the [World Engine](../world-engine/WORLD_ENGINE_OVERVIEW.md) at broader scale | Ecology creates context and consequences, not automatic forms. |
| Recurring source, exposure, stakes, buffers, and Response Evidence | [Evolutionary Pressures](EVOLUTIONARY_PRESSURES.md) | Pressure makes routes relevant; it does not select or complete one. |
| Change within the current form | [Monster Adaptation](MONSTER_ADAPTATION.md) and the smallest truthful source system | Adaptation does not accumulate into Evolution merely through repetition. |
| Persistent species capacity and bodily Development | [Species Development](../progression/SPECIES_DEVELOPMENT.md) | Development can support readiness but does not reorganise the form. |
| Learned competency, method, or reliable technique | [Skill Engine](../skills/README.md) | New anatomy may create a receiving route but grants no mastery. |
| Whole-form reorganisation | [Monster Evolution](MONSTER_EVOLUTION.md) | Evolution owns the transition and resulting embodied form. |
| Lifecycle form or transition window | [Species Stages](SPECIES_STAGES.md) | Stages are local structures, not universal ranks. |
| Route alternatives and commitments | [Branching Evolution](BRANCHING_EVOLUTION.md) | Route maps describe possibilities rather than prescribe upgrades. |
| Unknown route requirement | [Hidden Evolution Conditions](HIDDEN_EVOLUTION_CONDITIONS.md) | Hidden truth must pre-exist resolution and leave fair evidence. |
| Source-caused structural variation | [Mutations](MUTATIONS.md) | Mutation retains its source and is not Evolution by default. |
| Integrated multi-lineage or multi-origin body | [Hybridization](HYBRIDIZATION.md) | A Hybrid Form is one coherent current body, not a bundle of trees or Souls. |
| Contextual ecological dominance | [Apex Monsters](APEX_MONSTERS.md) | Apex always names a bounded domain, dependency, and counterplay. |
| Culture, institutions, collective action, and social inheritance | [Intelligent Monster Societies](MONSTER_SOCIETIES.md) | Collective capability belongs to its actual people, roles, and infrastructure. |
| Population loss, replacement, invasion, and recovery | [Extinction and Replacement](EXTINCTION_AND_REPLACEMENT.md) | Replacement does not recreate a lost lineage or erase consequences. |
| Soul evidence or bounded support | The originating [Soul rule](../soul/README.md), interfaced through [Soul Interaction with Evolution](SOUL_INTERACTIONS.md) | Soul systems never manufacture anatomy, Skills, Development, or route completion. |
| Final representation and exploit review | [Monster Evolution Safeguards](EVOLUTION_SAFEGUARDS.md) | Use the smallest truthful representation and preserve earned history during cleanup. |

## Phase Guarantees

When these rules are applied together:

- ecology and world causality determine which problems and opportunities matter;
- pressure creates several possible responses, including migration, adaptation, social change, stasis, decline, and Evolution;
- Evolution requires a valid route, present readiness, an actual transition, and consequences;
- current embodiment and current-life effort remain necessary;
- evolved anatomy changes access but does not award Skills, mastery, or universal power;
- Soul history may inform or condition a route only through an explicit bounded interface;
- hidden routes are discoverable through prior truth and fair evidence;
- stages, branches, mutations, hybrids, and apex outcomes remain contextual rather than hierarchical rewards;
- individual success does not automatically establish inheritance, a stable lineage, ecological viability, or social acceptance;
- failure produces causal outcomes rather than a retryable reward table;
- no kill count, consumption tally, suffering quota, elapsed-time threshold, universal level, rarity tier, mandatory tree, or final species guarantees Evolution;
- no species, form, route, or strategy is universally optimal.

## Authority and Boundary

These documents contain playable canonical rules. Accepted governance remains in [Design Decisions](../../design/DECISIONS.md); neither source silently overrides the other. Resolve any conflict before treating the affected rule as internally consistent.

This section contains no species catalog, named population, current ecology, campaign creature, settlement, faction, inventory, quest, or live world state. Campaign-specific applications and provisional rulings remain outside the repository under the [Game Master Framework](../gm/GAME_MASTER_FRAMEWORK.md).
