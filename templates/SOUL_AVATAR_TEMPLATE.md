# Soul Avatar Profile Template

Use this storage-neutral template to record one established Soul Avatar emergence, its integrated history, and any current Avatar Expression without treating the phenomenon as a class, transformation, inherited power package, or replacement identity.

The repository keeps this template blank. A populated Avatar Profile belongs in an external Campaign Record. Completing this form does not create a Soul Avatar, prove Continuity Concordance, awaken an Echo, grant access to prior mastery, or establish a world fact.

## Document Control

- **Template owner:** Souls and Incarnations records in the [Campaign Persistence Engine](../docs/persistence/STRUCTURED_PERSISTENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md)
- **Primary mechanical owner:** [Soul Avatars](../docs/soul/SOUL_AVATARS.md)
- **Dependencies:** current Incarnation, Soul identity and Integrity, Reincarnation, Soul Depth, Soul Resonance, Soul Echoes, Retained Instincts, Soul Titles, Soul Space, embodiment, Development, Skills, and every specialist source named by a claim
- **Extensions:** Avatar Associations, Echo participation, Soul Weapon cooperation, Soul Constellations, Akashic Archive contact, and World-Contact Events
- **Consumers:** Character records, reincarnation adjudication, Soul Space, capability adjudication, Relationships, Timeline, Campaign History, Research, world simulation, migration, and validation
- **Repository boundary:** no named Soul, Incarnation, Echo, Avatar Association, Trigger, expression event, relationship, current access, or campaign history belongs in this blank file

This template organizes references. The Soul, Incarnation, Echo, Title, Resonance Link, Skill, Soul Weapon, Archive Record, World Gate, and other source-owned records retain distinct identities and owners.

## Usage Guidance

1. Establish the continuing Soul and current Incarnation before creating an Avatar Profile.
2. Record Soul Avatar emergence only after the canonical qualitative requirements are demonstrated through actual history.
3. Keep the current Incarnation as the Avatar Lead; previous lives contribute without replacing the present self.
4. Link each contribution to its actual source and access route instead of copying capabilities into the Avatar Profile.
5. Distinguish persistent Soul Avatar status, current access, and a particular Avatar Expression event.
6. Reassess embodiment, access, consent, Integrity, Trigger, purpose, cost, and exit for every Expression.
7. Preserve uncertainty and provenance. Echo testimony, Archive Records, public legend, and current observation are not interchangeable truth.
8. Validate populated campaign records before use and reusable proposals through repository governance.

## Required Field Policy

**Required** fields must be present or carry an explicit `Unknown`, `Not Yet Verified`, `Disputed`, or `Requires Source Recovery` state. **Conditional** fields become required only when their feature exists. **Optional** fields grant nothing when omitted.

## Validation Checklist

Before activating or updating a populated Avatar Profile, verify:

- [ ] The continuing Soul and current Incarnation have distinct stable IDs.
- [ ] The current Incarnation remains the Avatar Lead.
- [ ] Emergence is supported by integrated history, Depth, Resonance, Integrity, consequence, present responsibility, and transformation.
- [ ] Longevity, life count, prestige, suffering, prophecy, ritual, title, or combat need was not treated as sufficient.
- [ ] Persistent status, current access, and each Expression event remain separate.
- [ ] Every contributor has an actual source, relevance, access route, consent state, and provenance.
- [ ] Echo Presence is not treated as truth, authority, or control.
- [ ] Concordance preserves disagreement and does not decide by vote.
- [ ] Associations arise from lived patterns and grant no package of powers.
- [ ] Current embodiment, Development, Skills, Magic, resources, environment, and action economy still apply.
- [ ] No missing anatomy, inherited mastery, extra action, universal translation, objective history, or world authority was granted.
- [ ] Entry, scope, costs, visibility, exit, and recovery are established for every Expression.
- [ ] Soul Space symbols and manifestations remain internal unless another explicit method owns external expression.
- [ ] Soul Weapons, Constellation members, visitors, gods, institutions, and Echoes retain distinct identity and agency.
- [ ] Reincarnation follows all seven ordinary stages and reassesses access after embodiment.
- [ ] World Gates remain distinct from Soul Gates and Avatar Expression grants no Gate operation.
- [ ] External recognition remains observer-specific and causally grounded.
- [ ] Truth Layers preserve mistakes, secrets, uncertainty, and provenance.
- [ ] Timeline, Campaign History, affected records, and validation results are updated externally.
- [ ] No populated campaign data has entered the canonical repository.

## Consumers and Handoffs

- **Character records** reference the Avatar Profile and current expression access without copying its history.
- **Reincarnation procedures** preserve Soul continuity and reassess post-embodiment access through all seven stages.
- **Soul Space records** provide the internal environment, Anchor, Regions, Thresholds, and visitor permissions.
- **Capability adjudication** follows Development, Skills, embodiment, Magic, equipment, and source-owned limits.
- **Relationships and world simulation** resolve recognition, obligations, fear, worship, regulation, and consequence causally.
- **Timeline and Campaign History** preserve emergence, Expression events, damage, integration, and public consequences.
- **Save Updates, migration, and validation** update only affected records and preserve authority, provenance, uncertainty, and typed references.

This template records continuity. It does not make the accumulated past more authoritative than the person living now.
