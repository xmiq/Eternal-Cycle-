# Development Profile Template

Use this storage-neutral template to assess one subject's Development for a bounded objective without creating a universal level, power score, or interchangeable progression currency.

A populated profile belongs in an external Campaign Record. This blank template grants no capability, Stat XP, mastery, rank, or progression.

## Document Control

- **Template owner:** the campaign module owning the subject; Player State or Actors commonly owns the view
- **Primary mechanical owner:** [Development System](../docs/progression/README.md)
- **Dependencies:** current embodiment, exact Development Tracks, Skills, tools, environment, evidence, and specialist owners
- **Extensions:** Physical, Skill, Profession, Magical, Social and Leadership, Species, retained Development, and Capability Assessment
- **Consumers:** Character and NPC records, Skills, Classes, Magic, Monster Evolution, encounter adjudication, Projects, and validation
- **Repository boundary:** no current character rating, statistic, Stat XP total, training history, injury, or live capability belongs here

## Usage Guidance

1. State the subject, objective, scope, and evidence before comparing capability.
2. Create separate entries for each Development Track that materially affects the objective.
3. Preserve all six capability layers even when some are unknown.
4. Attribute growth to meaningful training, use, reflection, instruction, experimentation, or hardship rather than elapsed time alone.
5. Record retained potential as bounded relearning support, never automatic mastery.
6. Use local ranks only with their issuing framework and purpose.

## Validation Notes

- [ ] No universal level, power score, or shared progression currency is present.
- [ ] Development, power, knowledge, access, potential, reputation, and Soul persistence remain distinct.
- [ ] Tracks interact without becoming interchangeable.
- [ ] Current embodiment and current-life effort remain necessary.
- [ ] Suffering, killing, death, repetition, or time alone did not grant growth.
- [ ] Retained progression accelerates relearning without bypassing training or Transfer Loss.
- [ ] Regression, rust, injury, suppression, Plateaus, and uncertainty remain possible.
- [ ] Comparison is purpose-specific and preserves tactical, environmental, relational, and informational advantages.
