# Project Record Template

Use this storage-neutral template for one intentional or emergent undertaking without treating elapsed time, resource expenditure, a task list, or GM preference as automatic completion.

A populated Project belongs outside the repository. This blank template creates no objective, participant, authority, resource, progress, output, completion, or consequence.

## Document Control

- **Template owner:** Projects in the [Structured Persistence Architecture](../docs/persistence/STRUCTURED_PERSISTENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md)
- **Primary mechanical owners:** the specialist systems governing the undertaking's methods and outputs
- **Dependencies:** objective, participants, authority, methods, resources, dependencies, milestones, risks, Timeline, and evidence
- **Extensions:** training, Research, construction, travel, reform, recovery, investigation, institution-building, and maintenance
- **Consumers:** Character and NPC records, Factions, Infrastructure, Research, Mysteries, world simulation, Timeline, Save Updates, and validation
- **Repository boundary:** no current undertaking, objective, milestone, resource allocation, participant, output, or progress state belongs here

## Usage Guidance

1. State a bounded objective and success conditions.
2. Identify participants, decision authority, consent, and specialist method owners.
3. Link resources, Inventory, Infrastructure, knowledge, Skills, and relationships.
4. Resolve milestones through actual events and evidence.
5. Preserve interruption, failure, redesign, abandonment, and unintended consequences.

- **Alternative plans:** `<branches and tradeoffs>`

## Validation Notes

- [ ] Objective, scope, participants, authority, methods, and success conditions are explicit.
- [ ] Every output is owned and justified by a specialist system.
- [ ] Time, repetition, resources, or a checked box alone does not create progress.
- [ ] Participants retain agency and may withdraw, refuse, fail, or renegotiate.
- [ ] Inputs, custody, dependencies, risks, and externalities remain current.
- [ ] Milestones have evidence and do not duplicate other records.
- [ ] Completion, failure, pause, and abandonment preserve consequences.
