# Research Record Template

Use this storage-neutral template to preserve one research question and its observations, hypotheses, experiments, evidence, theories, confidence, and knowledge handoffs without making speculation true.

A populated Research Record belongs outside the repository. This blank template creates no discovery, evidence, theory, confirmed fact, Skill, Development, or world truth.

## Document Control

- **Template owner:** Research in the [Structured Persistence Architecture](../docs/persistence/STRUCTURED_PERSISTENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md)
- **Primary semantic owner:** [Research Engine](../docs/persistence/RESEARCH_ENGINE.md)
- **Dependencies:** question identity, researchers, methods, evidence, ethics, Infrastructure, Projects, Knowledge, and specialist facts
- **Extensions:** competing, obsolete, forgotten, disproved, or rediscovered theories; publication; secrecy; and cross-system Research
- **Consumers:** Knowledge, Mysteries, Projects, Magic, Species, world simulation, advancement, Timeline, and validation
- **Repository boundary:** no campaign question, observation, experiment, discovery, theory, confidence, or current Research state belongs here

## Usage Guidance

1. Start with a bounded Research Question.
2. Keep observation, interpretation, hypothesis, experiment, evidence, theory, and confirmed knowledge distinct.
3. Record negative and failed results rather than deleting them.
4. Preserve competing theories, bias, fraud, ethics, affected parties, and uncertainty.
5. Promote a claim to Campaign Canon only through in-world confirmation by its factual owner.
6. Link Skills, Development, Infrastructure, and Projects without granting progress merely for recordkeeping.

## Validation Notes

- [ ] Research and world truth remain separate until in-world confirmation.
- [ ] Every observation, experiment, evidence item, and theory has provenance.
- [ ] Competing, obsolete, forgotten, disproved, and rediscovered theories remain traceable.
- [ ] Confidence uses canonical qualitative states and follows evidence.
- [ ] Failed and negative results are retained.
- [ ] Ethics, consent, affected parties, fraud, bias, and error are represented.
- [ ] Theories do not silently leak into Character Knowledge or Campaign Canon.
- [ ] Research grants no automatic Skill, Development, invention, or successful outcome.
