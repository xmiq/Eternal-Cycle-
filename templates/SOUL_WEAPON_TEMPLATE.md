# Soul Weapon Record Template

Use this storage-neutral template to record one Weapon Soul, its vessel continuity, and any Soul Weapon relationship without treating a person as equipment, collapsing consent into compatibility, or turning persistence into present access.

The repository keeps this template blank. A populated Soul Weapon record belongs in an external Campaign Record. A reusable reference may enter `docs/` only through ordinary canonical governance; completing this form does not establish a Weapon Soul, bond, capability, or world fact.

## Document Control

- **Template owner:** Souls and Incarnations records in the [Campaign Persistence Engine](../docs/persistence/STRUCTURED_PERSISTENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md#souls-and-incarnations), with custody and world state retained by their own modules
- **Primary mechanical owner:** [Soul Weapons](../docs/soul-weapons/README.md)
- **Dependencies:** Souls, Relationships, embodiment, Development, Skills, current vessel state, world causality, and every specialist source named by a claim
- **Extensions:** Awakening, Soul Intertwining, personality, Evolution, Weapon Echoes, Legacy Status, Compatibility Profiles, Manifestation Profiles, and unconventional Vessel Topology
- **Consumers:** Character records, Relationship records, Inventory and custody, Reincarnation, capability adjudication, world simulation, institutions, Timeline, Research, migration, and validation
- **Repository boundary:** no named Weapon Soul, vessel, bond, current consent, custody, form, location, damage, wielder, or campaign history belongs in this blank file

This template organizes related records. The Weapon Soul, vessel, Bonded Soul, bond, Relationship, custody, Skill, and source-owned capabilities retain distinct identities and owners.

## Usage Guidance

1. Establish the Weapon Soul and vessel identities independently before recording any relationship.
2. Use one stable ID for each person, vessel, bond, form, Echo, route, and Profile; link rather than duplicate them.
3. Record formation, dormancy, Awakening, Soul Intertwining, Evolution, and Manifestation only when their separate canonical conditions are met.
4. Reassess current consent, compatibility, embodiment, access, and custody whenever circumstances materially change.
5. Assign every capability once to the Weapon Soul, user, vessel, Magic, Skill, or other actual owner.
6. Keep legal ownership, physical custody, cooperation, trust, bond status, and metaphysical personhood separate.
7. Preserve unknown, private, disputed, inaccessible, and concealed facts through Truth Layers instead of inventing or leaking them.
8. Validate populated campaign records before use and reusable proposals through repository governance.

## Required Field Policy

**Required** fields must be present or carry an explicit `Unknown`, `Not Yet Verified`, `Disputed`, or `Requires Source Recovery` state. **Conditional** fields become required only when their feature exists. **Optional** fields grant nothing when omitted.
