# Human Framework Profile Template

Use this storage-neutral template for a Human Class, Profession, Martial Tradition, Magical School, Institution, Social Position framework, Class Change, or Progression Constraint without turning a social label into capability.

A populated campaign instance belongs outside the repository. Reusable framework proposals require ordinary canonical governance; completing this form does not create a Class, grant Skills, issue a credential, establish an Institution, or confer authority.

## Document Control

- **Template owner:** Factions and Institutions or the campaign module owning the framework instance
- **Primary mechanical owner:** [Human Classes and Professions](../docs/human/README.md)
- **Dependencies:** culture, history, participants, Development, Skills, Relationships, access, and current institutions
- **Extensions:** Class, Profession, Martial Tradition, Magical School, Social Position, Institution, Class Evolution, and Progression Constraint Profiles
- **Consumers:** Character and NPC records, Factions, Institutions, education, employment, Projects, world simulation, and validation
- **Repository boundary:** no named student, employee, officeholder, roster, current curriculum, credential, reputation, or institution state belongs here

## Usage Guidance

1. Select the exact framework type and its specialist owner.
2. Preserve culture, version, lineage, audience, and current institutional context.
3. Link Skills and Development rather than granting them through a label.
4. Record affiliation, membership, employment, office, credential, rank, reputation, authority, and legitimacy separately.
5. Treat local equivalence and portability as evidence-based findings, not universal tiers.
6. Preserve participant agency, access barriers, obligations, alternatives, and change over time.

## Validation Notes

- [ ] No class level, Profession level, social level, institutional rating, or human power score exists.
- [ ] Labels, curricula, credentials, offices, reputation, authority, legitimacy, and capability remain distinct.
- [ ] No automatic Skill package, mastery, Magic, or bodily trait is granted.
- [ ] Local ranks retain their issuer, purpose, scope, and portability limits.
- [ ] Reincarnation restores no office, membership, credential, property, or social standing.
- [ ] Human frameworks do not overwrite monster progression or present embodiment.
- [ ] Participants and institutions retain agency and can change, refuse, exclude, or fail.
