# Soul Continuity Record Template

Use this storage-neutral template to preserve one continuing Soul and its distinct Incarnations without treating persistence as present access, merging completed lives, or moving world-bound state between bodies.

A populated record belongs in an external Campaign Record. This blank template creates no Soul, Reincarnation, Echo, Title, bond, access, or campaign fact.

## Document Control

- **Template owner:** Souls and Incarnations in the [Structured Persistence Architecture](../docs/persistence/STRUCTURED_PERSISTENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md)
- **Primary mechanical owner:** [Soul Engine](../docs/soul/README.md)
- **Dependencies:** Soul identity, Incarnations, Reincarnation events, embodiment, Truth Layers, Timeline, and source-owned Soul structures
- **Extensions:** [Soul Avatar Profile](SOUL_AVATAR_TEMPLATE.md), [Soul Weapon Record](SOUL_WEAPON_TEMPLATE.md), Development, Skills, Relationships, and world consequences
- **Consumers:** Character records, Reincarnation, Soul adjudication, Timeline, Campaign History, migration, continuity resolution, and validation
- **Repository boundary:** no named Soul, life, death, body, Echo, Title, bond, memory, or current access belongs in this blank file

## Usage Guidance

1. Give the continuing Soul and every Incarnation separate stable IDs.
2. Append lives and transition events; do not overwrite one life with another.
3. Record persistent structures, current access, and embodied expression separately.
4. Link every Soul claim to its specialist owner and source evidence.
5. Keep ordinary possessions, social standing, bodies, and worldly consequences outside Soul persistence.
6. Preserve uncertainty rather than inventing missing past-life data.

## Validation Notes

- [ ] One continuing Soul and no more than one active Incarnation are recorded.
- [ ] Every Incarnation, Final Death, and Life Reconciliation has a distinct identity.
- [ ] Persistent structures do not imply current access or bodily expression.
- [ ] Echoes, Titles, Avatars, Constellations, and Weapon Souls remain distinct.
- [ ] Reincarnation transfers no ordinary inventory, statistics, office, reputation, or social identity.
- [ ] Current-life embodiment, practice, consent, and world causality still apply.
- [ ] Missing information remains unknown and Truth Layers prevent leakage.
- [ ] No populated campaign data is stored in the repository.
