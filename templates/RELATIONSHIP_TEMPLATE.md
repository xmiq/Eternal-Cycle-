# Relationship Record Template

Use this storage-neutral template to preserve one evolving relationship among distinct participants without reducing it to a score, script, ownership claim, or permanent attitude.

A populated Relationship Record belongs outside the repository. This blank template creates no meeting, trust, hostility, promise, betrayal, debt, family tie, recognition, or current feeling.

## Document Control

- **Template owner:** Relationships in the [Structured Persistence Architecture](../docs/persistence/STRUCTURED_PERSISTENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md)
- **Primary semantic owner:** [Relationship Memory Engine](../docs/persistence/RELATIONSHIP_MEMORY_ENGINE.md)
- **Dependencies:** stable participant identities, Timeline events, observer perspectives, commitments, communication, and evidence
- **Extensions:** family, organizations, Soul Constellations, Weapon Soul bonds, employment, alliances, rivalry, and shared work
- **Consumers:** Character and NPC records, Companions, Factions, Soul systems, Projects, Knowledge, continuity resolution, and validation
- **Repository boundary:** no named participants, meeting, trust state, promise, debt, conflict, or live relationship belongs here

## Usage Guidance

1. Give the relationship a stable identity separate from its participants and current state.
2. Record direction and perspective where feelings, knowledge, or obligations differ.
3. Append important encounters; do not replace the relationship with its latest scene.
4. Keep trust, hostility, affection, fear, dependence, authority, and recognition independent.
5. Preserve promises, betrayals, debts, gifts, family, organizations, discoveries, and unresolved issues with provenance.
6. Never treat a known character as a stranger without an in-world explanation or authorized correction.

## Validation Notes

- [ ] Every participant has one stable identity and typed references.
- [ ] First and latest meetings exist or are explicitly unknown.
- [ ] Trust, hostility, communication, promises, debts, gifts, kinship, and recognition remain distinct.
- [ ] Directional perspectives and Truth Layers prevent mind reading or knowledge leakage.
- [ ] The latest interaction does not erase earlier history.
- [ ] Death or Reincarnation does not silently reset or restore the relationship.
- [ ] Player and NPC agency remain intact; the record predicts no mandatory choice.
- [ ] Duplicate relationships are merged only through authorized identity repair.
