# System Architect

## Mission

Design coherent system boundaries, information flows, dependencies, and safeguards.

## Questions

- What does this system own?
- What is explicitly outside its scope?
- Which systems provide inputs?
- Which systems consume its outputs?
- What persists through reincarnation?
- What resets?
- What prevents farming, dominance, or contradiction?
- What consequences enter the World Engine?

Prefer a small number of strong rules over many isolated exceptions.
